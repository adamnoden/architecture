# 1. Decision

F06 is **FROZEN**.

The adversarial pass materially narrows the residual Ovid-delta hypotheses left by ILA-01B-F06.

The most important correction is historical and unexpectedly close.

In June 1902, A. S. Wainwright's contemporary profile of Birmingham architect **W. H. Bidlake** described three `lamps of domestic architecture`. Wainwright called the first the **`Lamp of Repose`**. In his account of Bidlake's teaching:

- the house should be a haven of rest;
- domestic rather than heroic virtues belong to home life;
- self-assertive architectural `features` are suspect;
- decoration should not seize or rivet the occupant's **unwilling attention**.

Source:

- A. S. Wainwright, `A Birmingham architect: W. H. Bidlake`, *International Studio*, no. 64, June 1902. Heidelberg digitisation / article DOI: https://doi.org/10.11588/diglit.22773.37

This is close enough to Ovid's:

> **A good home should minimise unnecessary vigilance.**

that no meaningful priority claim is available for the underlying domestic-attention idea.

A crucial source-discipline correction follows:

> **The phrase `Lamp of Repose` is Wainwright's contemporary summary of Bidlake's teaching unless a direct Bidlake text using that exact phrase is later found.**

ILA must not silently convert a reporter's formulation into a Bidlake quotation or documented self-description.

Other adversarial tests narrow F06 further:

1. **Rybczynski already treats domestic comfort as a compound cultural idea** including privacy, intimacy, domesticity, ease, light/air, efficiency and well-being rather than one mechanical variable.
2. **Alexander's domestic pattern cluster operationalises much of regulated exposure and retreat** through entrance transition, intimacy gradient, common areas, private retreat and graded sitting spaces.
3. **Aalto already makes technical functionalism subordinate to human life** rather than a complete architectural end.
4. **Altman owns the privacy-as-dynamic-boundary-regulation concept** far more directly than Ovid does.
5. **Weiser/Brown's Calm Technology supplies a close cross-domain antecedent** to technical systems receding from focal attention rather than demanding continuous interaction.
6. **London housing guidance has used `Home as a Place of Retreat` since the 2010 London Housing Design Guide**, combining privacy, daylight, noise and other domestic environmental qualities; current London and national guidance make the combination even more explicit and include personal control / ease of use.
7. **Bachelard and Evans prevent the refuge/privacy story becoming universal or sentimental**: domestic intimacy is a bounded philosophical idea, and greater separation can encode social hierarchy or reduce encounter.

The frozen F06 conclusion is therefore:

> **Ovid's House does not originate repose as an architectural value, the house as a place of rest, the avoidance of attention-seizing domestic features, humane architecture beyond technical functionalism, compound domestic comfort, privacy as regulated access, graded privacy, personal retreat, or technology receding from attention. Its plausible contribution is narrower: a contemporary domestic governance criterion that integrates established concerns across physical performance, embodied use, privacy/retreat, spatial comprehensibility, local agency, perceptual settlement and technical subordination, while explicitly separating measured evidence from briefing judgement and architectural hypothesis.**

That is a candidate **synthesis / operationalisation / epistemic contract**, not an originality claim.

# 2. Repose has an older architectural vocabulary

## 2.1 Ruskin — aesthetic / metaphysical repose, not Ovid's domestic criterion

John Ruskin's *Modern Painters*, volume II, contains a chapter `Of Repose, or the Type of Divine Permanence`.

Available text:

- Project Gutenberg: https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/29906/pg29906-images.html

Ruskin treats repose as a quality of artistic greatness opposed to visible agitation, strain or restless exertion.

Relationship to C12:

- `EARLIER_ANTECEDENT` to architectural/artistic use of the word `repose`;
- **not** a close source for domestic privacy, local control or ordinary occupied comfort.

Freeze consequence:

- Ovid cannot present `Repose` as a newly coined architectural value;
- Ruskin does not own Ovid's actual domestic bundle.

## 2.2 Frank Lloyd Wright — simplicity and repose as architectural value

Wright's early propositions, later published in `In the Cause of Architecture`, include the claim that simplicity and repose measure the value of a work of art and connect domestic planning to comfort, utility and beauty.

Reference text:

- *Architectural Record*, `In the Cause of Architecture`: https://www.architecturalrecord.com/articles/11469-in-the-cause-of-architecture

Relationship:

- supporting architectural term/history;
- not the closest C12 antecedent.

## 2.3 Bidlake/Wainwright — the close domestic antecedent

Wainwright's 1902 article is materially different from the broader aesthetic lineage because it is explicitly about **domestic architecture in relation to the owner**.

The reported principles include:

- the house as a rest condition after ordinary work;
- domestic rather than heroic values;
- restraint of self-assertive architectural novelty;
- avoidance of features that demand unwilling attention.

This directly anticipates two of Ovid's strongest reader-facing ideas:

- ordinary rooms should not require continuous perceptual monitoring;
- novelty / technical display should not become the permanent background condition of domestic life.

### Relationship

`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT`.

No project influence is established.

### Attribution caution

The article reports Bidlake's teaching, but `Lamp of Repose` appears in Wainwright's phrasing (`may be called`).

Later ILA must distinguish:

- **Bidlake's reported domestic posture**;
- **Wainwright's formulation of that posture**.

Do not create a false direct quotation genealogy.

# 3. Rybczynski collapses most of the compound-home novelty hypothesis

Source:

- Witold Rybczynski, *Home: A Short History of an Idea* (1986).
- Canadian Centre for Architecture bibliographic/summary record: https://www.cca.qc.ca/en/search/details/library/publication/14966304

The CCA record gives the chapter structure:

- `Intimacy and privacy`;
- `Domesticity`;
- `Ease`;
- `Light and air`;
- `Efficiency`;
- `Comfort and well-being`.

Its summary describes the book as a history of comfort and the cultural variables that shape **psychologically comfortable homes**.

This is unusually close to Ovid's refusal to reduce Repose to:

- temperature;
- sparse composition;
- technical efficiency;
- one plan type;
- one historical style.

### Adversarial result

The proposition:

> **domestic comfort is a compound of physical conditions, privacy, intimacy, ease and cultural habits rather than a single technical metric**

is established prior intellectual territory.

### Relationship

`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT`.

No formative project contact is currently established.

### Remaining difference

Rybczynski is historical and interpretive. Ovid turns a comparable compound idea into a current **review / governance criterion** linked to technical architecture and evidence boundaries.

That is operationalisation, not conceptual priority.

# 4. Alexander's pattern cluster owns much of the spatial side

F06 discovery initially tested `Intimacy Gradient` and `A Room of One's Own`. The adversarial pass widened only enough to determine whether the **cluster** already approaches Repose as an architectural system.

It does.

## 4.1 Entrance Transition — explicit tranquility

Pattern 112 states that buildings, especially houses, with a graceful transition between street and interior are **more tranquil** than those opening directly off the street.

Source:

- https://patternlanguage.cc/Patterns/Entrance-Transition-%28112%29

This anticipates the idea that domestic repose can begin with a spatial transition from public world to protected interior.

## 4.2 Intimacy Gradient — graded exposure

Pattern 127 organises movement from public through increasingly private domains.

Source:

- https://patternlanguage.cc/Patterns/Intimacy-Gradient-%28127%29

This is direct prior art for Ovid's graded public/shared/private relationships.

## 4.3 A Room of One's Own — retreat without leaving the household

Pattern 141 argues that closeness to others requires opportunities to be alone and provides each household member with a place to retire.

Source:

- https://patternlanguage.cc/Patterns/A-Room-of-One%27s-Own-%28141%29

This is extremely close to Ovid's review question:

> is there somewhere to withdraw without leaving the household altogether?

## 4.4 Common Areas at the Heart — the counterweight to retreat

Pattern 129 makes informal contact central rather than allowing privacy to fragment the social group.

Source:

- https://patternlanguage.cc/Patterns/Common-Areas-at-the-Heart-%28129%29

## 4.5 Sequence of Sitting Spaces — different degrees of enclosure

Pattern 142 distributes sitting places along the intimacy gradient with differing degrees of enclosure and connection.

Source:

- https://patternlanguage.cc/Patterns/Sequence-of-Sitting-Spaces-%28142%29

### Adversarial result

Alexander already supplies a surprisingly complete architectural topology of:

- arrival transition;
- public/private gradient;
- sociable centre;
- personal retreat;
- graded enclosure.

Ovid therefore cannot claim that it invented **regulated exposure as a plan/section problem**.

### Relationship

`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT` with project-source caveat.

Alexander is already an explicit project source generally. ILA has not yet established whether these specific patterns materially influenced Repose's formulation; that remains a later contact/attribution question.

# 5. Aalto collapses the broad technical-subordination claim

Source:

- Alvar Aalto, `The Humanizing of Architecture`, 1940: https://www.fondazionebrunozevi.it/it/alvar-aalto-the-humanizing-of-architecture/
- Aalto Foundation Paimio conservation study: https://www.alvaraalto.fi/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/PaimioSanatoriumConservationManagementPlan.pdf

Aalto argues that modern architecture had been rationalised primarily from technical/economic standpoints and that real functional architecture must become functional **from the human point of view**. Technology is an aid, not an independent architectural end.

Paimio is the built context for this argument: long-duration occupation, sound, light, colour, furniture and room detail are considered from the patient's position.

### Adversarial result

Ovid cannot claim novelty for:

> **technical intelligence must remain subordinate to human occupation.**

### Remaining difference

Ovid applies this as a specific veto inside a long-life technical platform:

- a demountable wall that rattles can fail;
- access hatches can dominate a corridor;
- automation can become opaque;
- maintenance visibility can make a home feel provisional.

This is a project-specific application of an older humanising principle.

# 6. Altman owns the privacy-as-regulation concept

Sources:

- Irwin Altman, *The Environment and Social Behavior* (1975): https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Environment_and_Social_Behavior.html?id=GLBPAAAAMAAJ
- Irwin Altman, `Privacy: A Conceptual Analysis` (1976), ERIC EJ143439: https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ143439

Altman's privacy model is explicitly dynamic: privacy is an interpersonal boundary-control process regulating access to the self rather than a fixed maximum-seclusion condition.

That is the conceptual core of Ovid's `regulated exposure`.

### Relationship

`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT / CONCEPTUAL SUPPORT`.

### Freeze consequence

Ovid must not imply priority for:

- privacy as choice over exposure;
- privacy as adjustment between connection and withdrawal;
- the idea that maximum enclosure is not the goal.

Architecture supplies the physical mechanisms; the conceptual model is older.

# 7. Evans prevents privacy becoming a one-way virtue

Source:

- Robin Evans, `Figures, Doors and Passages`, *Architectural Design* 48 (1978), pp. 267–278. Bibliographic record: https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1573950400279292800?lang=en

Evans shows that room connection, doors and corridors materially construct social relations. The historical move toward corridor-based plans increased privacy and separation while also reducing incidental contact and reorganising household relations.

### Relationship

`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT + COUNTERPOINT`.

### Freeze consequence

Repose must remain about **regulable exposure**, not privacy maximalism.

More private architecture can also:

- segregate;
- encode hierarchy;
- reduce encounter;
- atomise shared life.

This supports Ovid's current balance rather than a new claim.

# 8. Calm Technology is a close cross-domain antecedent to low-attention technicality

Sources:

- Mark Weiser, `The Computer for the 21st Century` (1991): https://calmtech.com/papers/computer-for-the-21st-century
- Mark Weiser, `The World is Not a Desktop` (1993): https://calmtech.com/papers/the-world-is-not-a-desktop
- Mark Weiser & John Seely Brown, `Designing Calm Technology` (1995): https://calmtech.com/papers/designing-calm-technology
- Mark Weiser & John Seely Brown, `The Coming Age of Calm Technology` (1996): https://calmtech.com/papers/coming-age-calm-technology

The relevant proposition is not about architecture generally. It is about **attention**.

Weiser's ubiquitous-computing argument says profound technology can recede into everyday life rather than continually occupying focal consciousness. Weiser/Brown then frame calm technology around fitting technology to human life and moving information between centre and periphery of attention.

### Relationship

`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT / CROSS-DOMAIN ANALOGUE` to the technical-attention part of C12.

There is no evidence of project influence.

### Why it earns inclusion

Ovid explicitly rejects homes where:

- controls are opaque;
- technical systems require constant monitoring;
- automation becomes the focus of ordinary life;
- serviceability means continuous technical display.

Calm Technology materially anticipates the **attention relationship**, even though it does not supply architectural privacy, comfort or plan topology.

### Freeze consequence

`minimise unnecessary vigilance` remains plausible project wording, but its technical-attention principle is not conceptually isolated or unprecedented.

# 9. Professional housing guidance already assembles much of the bundle

This is not thinker lineage, but it matters to novelty discipline.

## 9.1 London Housing Design Guide — `Home as a Place of Retreat`

The 2010 London Housing Design Guide contains a whole section:

> **Home as a Place of Retreat**

Source:

- Interim London Housing Design Guide: https://www.london.gov.uk/sites/default/files/interim_london_housing_design_guide.pdf

Its standards include:

- privacy;
- dual aspect;
- noise;
- daylight/sunlight;
- spatial quality and related domestic environmental concerns.

Current London guidance explicitly says homes should provide comfortable places of **retreat and privacy** and treats privacy, aspect, daylight, indoor air quality, noise and thermal comfort as a holistic design problem.

Source:

- London City Hall, Housing Design / Module C: https://www.london.gov.uk/publications/housing-design

## 9.2 National Design Guide

Current national guidance joins:

- ease of use;
- comfort;
- safety/security;
- privacy;
- accessibility/adaptability;
- ventilation / overheating / noise / air quality;
- **personal control**.

Source:

- GOV.UK, National Design Guide: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-design-guide/national-design-guide-accessible-version

### Relationship

`CONTEMPORARY PROFESSIONAL CONVERGENCE / EVIDENTIAL SUPPORT`.

Not historical ancestry.

### Freeze consequence

Even Ovid's combined domestic package is not unprecedented in contemporary professional practice.

The residual project question is therefore not:

> **Did Ovid discover that homes need privacy + comfort + control + retreat?**

It is:

> **Does Ovid make that familiar bundle operationally useful inside its unusually technical long-life architecture, and does it police evidence boundaries better than typical doctrine?**

# 14. Residual Ovid-delta hypotheses after saturation

## 14.1 Repose as a single bounded governance criterion

No one source found in bounded discovery exactly matches the current Ovid review contract:

- measured physical environmental performance;
- embodied ease in repeated actions;
- spatial comprehensibility;
- regulated exposure / retreat;
- effective local control;
- bounded perceptual settlement;
- technical subordination;
- explicit distinction among measurement, briefing and architectural judgement.

But nearly every ingredient has strong prior art.

Disposition:

- candidate `SYNTHESIS / OPERATIONALISATION`;
- no claim of conceptual invention.

## 14.2 `Minimise unnecessary vigilance`

Bounded discovery did not find the exact phrase in an earlier architectural source.

However Wainwright's 1902 Bidlake profile is semantically very close through:

- home as haven of rest;
- avoidance of self-assertive features;
- avoidance of architecture that rivets **unwilling attention**.

Weiser/Brown supply a parallel technical-attention lineage.

Disposition:

- **project wording / compression** may be original to the project;
- the underlying attention concept is not.

## 14.3 Occupation-side veto over the technical platform

Ovid's strongest useful move may be procedural:

> **a technically successful long-life solution can still be rejected if it makes ordinary domestic occupation too noisy, visually technical, cognitively demanding, awkward or dependent on opaque control.**

Aalto strongly anticipates the human-over-technical principle; current housing guidance anticipates the multi-factor domestic evaluation.

What may remain project-specific is the explicit cross-family role of Repose as a veto against over-engineered serviceability / adaptability.

Disposition:

- candidate `CROSS-FAMILY GOVERNANCE / DOMESTIC APPLICATION`.

## 14.4 Explicit epistemic boundary

The project repeatedly separates:

- what should be measured;
- what should be briefed because it depends on occupants/programme;
- what remains architectural judgement;
- what current evidence does **not** justify.

This is especially visible around:

- structural appearance;
- prospect/refuge;
- curvature;
- neuroarchitecture;
- claims of stress / health.

Disposition:

- candidate `PROJECT EPISTEMIC CONTRACT`;
- not a claim that evidence hierarchy itself is new.

## 14.5 Richness without compulsory stimulation

Ovid explicitly rejects the equation:

`repose = minimalism`.

It permits:

- ornament;
- books;
- texture;
- material richness;
- local intensity;
- surprise;

provided the whole remains intelligible and the intensity is not an unavoidable background demand.

This has obvious precedents and should be treated as project positioning, not invention.
