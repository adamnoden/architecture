# 1. Decision

ILA-01 broad discovery is complete.

The project now knows enough about the intellectual field to stop asking:

> `Who else might be relevant?`

and begin asking the narrower question:

> **Which primary sources can still change a material attribution, genealogy, scope, Ovid-delta or publication-treatment decision?**

ILA-02 therefore begins with a deliberately small dossier programme.

This packet admits **ten primary dossier clusters**.

It does **not** assign final `FOUNDATIONAL` / `MAJOR` importance classes. Admission means only:

> **deeper primary-source reading can still materially change one or more ILA decisions.**

The ten admitted clusters are sufficient to test the intellectual spine exposed by F01–F08. Other discovery sources remain supporting, conditional or matrix-only unless a later dossier creates a concrete reason to promote them.

The governing posture is:

> **Deep-read edges, not reputations.**

and:

> **A dossier exists to resolve an ILA decision, not to honour a thinker.**

# 2. Admission test

A source or tightly connected source cluster earns an ILA-02 dossier only when primary-source reading can still materially change at least one of the following.

## A — relationship type

Examples:

- `EARLIER_ANTECEDENT` versus `DOCUMENTED_DERIVATION`;
- `CONVERGENT_PARALLEL` versus a real source-to-source lineage;
- `COUNTERPOINT` versus merely contextual difference.

## B — project-contact classification

Examples:

- current acknowledgement only;
- documented adoption;
- documented formative use;
- no documented pre-ILA contact.

A dossier must not manufacture formative influence from conceptual similarity.

## C — source-to-source genealogy

Examples:

- whether Brand's relevant rate/coupling account explicitly derives from Duffy / O'Neill;
- whether a later source actually credits an earlier one for the same proposition;
- whether an attractive chronological chain is merely convergent.

## D — scope / transfer limit

Examples:

- office / institutional architecture transferred into detached domestic architecture;
- ecological hierarchy used as an analogy rather than architectural authority;
- temporary / industrialised systems used as precedent rather than universal doctrine.

## E — Ovid delta

A source earns deep reading when its exact proposition may collapse, narrow or substantiate a proposed Ovid distinction such as:

- domestic operationalisation;
- proportionality;
- interface / service / maintenance consequence;
- governance / verification;
- quiet architectural integration.

## F — attribution finding

A dossier is warranted if the source could materially change a later finding among:

- `ADEQUATE`;
- `UNDER_ATTRIBUTED`;
- `MISATTRIBUTED`;
- `OVER_CREDITED`;
- `LINEAGE_UNCERTAIN`;
- `NO_PUBLIC_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT_REQUIRED`;
- `SUBSTANTIVE_QUALIFICATION_REQUIRED`.

## G — reader-facing treatment

A source earns deep reading if the difference between T0/T1/T2/T3/T4/T5 cannot responsibly be decided from discovery material alone.

# 3. Non-admission rules

The following do **not** by themselves earn a dossier.

- canonical reputation;
- architectural fame;
- appearing in `Sources and further reading`;
- being an interesting precedent;
- having one striking quotation;
- supporting an established professional fact;
- repeating a proposition already settled by stronger admitted sources;
- being necessary for technical adequacy rather than intellectual lineage;
- being useful merely to make the bibliography look complete.

A source should remain matrix-only when:

- its proposition is already sufficiently clear;
- its relationship type is stable;
- its project-contact status is stable;
- deeper reading is unlikely to change Ovid delta or treatment;
- a stronger cluster already carries the same intellectual burden.

---

# 4. Dossier form

Every admitted dossier must expand the ILA-00C source contract rather than inventing a new format.

At minimum record:

1. exact bibliographic identity;
2. first relevant publication / presentation date;
3. edition actually consulted;
4. exact relevant proposition with page / section / figure where recoverable;
5. original problem / building type / institutional context;
6. actor / decision-maker assumed by the source;
7. time horizon;
8. important limitations / counterevidence inside the source itself;
9. documented source-to-source edges;
10. project-contact chronology;
11. exact overlap with ILA claim IDs;
12. exact difference from Ovid;
13. transfer limits;
14. provisional importance recommendation;
15. provisional Ovid-delta recommendation;
16. current Ovid treatment;
17. candidate publication-treatment range;
18. contradictory / negative evidence;
19. unresolved questions;
20. explicit dossier result.

## Dossier result vocabulary

Use one or more of:

- `CONFIRMS_DISCOVERY` — primary source supports the frozen ILA-01 model;
- `NARROWS_DISCOVERY` — source overlap is weaker / more bounded than discovery suggested;
- `STRENGTHENS_ANTECEDENT` — source is closer to Ovid than discovery could safely establish;
- `ESTABLISHES_DERIVATION` — primary evidence upgrades a source-to-source edge;
- `BREAKS_GENEALOGY` — expected lineage is not supported;
- `CHANGES_PROJECT_CONTACT` — durable repo chronology changes contact classification;
- `CHANGES_OVID_DELTA` — source materially changes the defensible Ovid contribution;
- `CHANGES_TREATMENT_PRESSURE` — source materially changes likely public treatment;
- `NO_FURTHER_EFFECT` — deep reading confirms the source does not deserve further programme weight.

These are dossier outcomes, not public taxonomy.

# 10. Dossier stop conditions

Each dossier should stop when the following are resolved well enough for ILA-03/04.

- exact relevant proposition found in primary source;
- source chronology sufficiently stable;
- project-contact status sufficiently evidenced;
- source-to-source edge typed or explicitly unresolved;
- transfer limit stated;
- overlap with Ovid stated;
- Ovid delta narrowed honestly;
- contradictory / limiting evidence recorded;
- likely importance range stated;
- likely public-treatment range stated;
- additional reading would add context rather than change the decision.

A dossier does **not** need:

- complete intellectual biography;
- exhaustive bibliography;
- reception history;
- every built project by the author;
- every later derivative tradition;
- proof of universal historical priority.

---

# 11. Dossier source discipline

Once admitted, a dossier should prefer:

1. Tier A primary source;
2. Tier B authoritative scholarship for chronology / disputed interpretation;
3. Tier C orientation only where primary material is unavailable or to locate it.

A potentially `FOUNDATIONAL` / `MAJOR` recommendation must not rest on search snippets or tertiary summaries.

For books, cite exact pages / sections / figures where recoverable.

For office / collective work, distinguish authorship carefully:

- individual author;
- office / research group;
- project report;
- later retrospective attribution.

Do not collapse DEGW into Duffy, Open Building into Habraken, or CHS into one individual merely for narrative neatness.

---

# 12. Provenance edge discipline

ILA-02 is where source-to-source arrows may finally be upgraded — but only with evidence.

Examples:

- **Alexander → Duffy** currently means documented contact only, not derivation.
- **Duffy → Brand** may become `DOCUMENTED_DERIVATION` only for the proposition Brand actually credits.
- **O'Neill → Brand** may become `DOCUMENTED_DERIVATION` only for the slow/fast hierarchy Brand actually takes from the ecology source.
- **Habraken → later Open Building** may be a documented lineage but specific technical replacement claims may belong to later sources rather than Habraken's original *Supports*.
- **SCAG ↔ CHS** remains convergent / independent unless evidence proves a transfer.
- **Weeks ↔ Hertzberger** should not become a genealogy merely because both answer uncertainty / flexibility.

No dossier may draw a picturesque chain first and search for evidence second.

---

# 13. Public-treatment restraint

ILA-02 may recommend a **range** of likely reader-facing treatment, but it may not edit the manuscript.

The default posture remains:

> **large internally, small externally.**

Likely outcomes include:

- a FOUNDATIONAL source staying T2 because one endnote restores honesty;
- a MAJOR source requiring T3 because the prose otherwise reads as project-originated;
- a famous source remaining T0/T1 because naming it adds no value;
- a source requiring T5 because current prose attributes the wrong lineage.

Publication changes belong to ILA-05 after ILA-03/04 have integrated the dossiers.
