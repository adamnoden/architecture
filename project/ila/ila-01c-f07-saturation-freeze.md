# 1. Decision

F07 is **FROZEN** at discovery stage.

The adversarial pass materially narrows the already modest novelty space left by ILA-01B-F07.

The strongest bounded conclusion is:

> **Ovid's House does not originate bioclimatic design, passive-first environmental ordering, building fabric as primary environmental control, active systems as supplementary correction, low energy joined to long life, passive provision as fallback during service loss, future-climate scenario testing, or the warning that fabric-first simplicity can become harmful dogma. All have substantial architectural, engineering or professional prior art. Ovid's remaining candidate contribution is narrower: making passive-first a proportional cross-family governance rule inside a century-scale house platform, where permanent geometry is asked to earn environmental value before shorter-lived plant is added, while health, measured performance, resilience and future climate retain authority to override simplicity.**

That remaining proposition is a **synthesis / governance hypothesis**, not an originality claim.

F07 should therefore not be narrated as a history of `passive architecture`, nor as a contest between natural and mechanical systems. The correct intellectual object is an **ordering principle under evidence**:

1. ask what durable form/fabric/geometry can do;
2. measure what it actually achieves;
3. add active systems where the remaining requirement earns them;
4. retain direct user control where useful;
5. test the permanent decisions against plausible future conditions;
6. reject simplicity when it damages health, fabric, comfort or resilience.

Every generic component of that sequence has prior art. The possible Ovid delta lies only in how the sequence is tied to the project's wider temporal, failure, maintenance and governance machinery.

## 2.1 Olgyay's relevant lineage begins before the 1963 synthesis

ILA-01B correctly used Victor Olgyay's *Design with Climate* (1963) as the strongest foundational source found for the architectural ordering of environmental correction.

The freeze must not let the date `1963` imply that the proposition begins there.

The Olgyay brothers' bioclimatic programme was already substantially developed in the early 1950s.

Relevant chronology:

- Victor Olgyay, `The Temperate House`, *Architectural Forum* 94, 1951;
- Victor Olgyay, `Bioclimatic Approach to Architecture`, Building Research Advisory Board conference work, 1953;
- Victor and Aladár Olgyay / US Housing and Home Finance Agency, *Application of Climatic Data to House Design*, 1954;
- Victor and Aladár Olgyay, *Solar Control and Shading Devices*, 1957;
- Victor Olgyay, *Design with Climate*, 1963.

Primary / strong bibliographic sources used in the adversarial pass:

- US Housing and Home Finance Agency, *Application of Climatic Data to House Design* (1954): https://books.google.com/books?id=oABQAAAAMAAJ
- David Leatherbarrow & Richard Wesley, `Performance and style in the work of Olgyay and Olgyay`, *arq* 18(2), 2014: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/arq-architectural-research-quarterly/article/performance-and-style-in-the-work-of-olgyay-and-olgyay/747BA7C900B47AA0907E803E2FA97C91

The Cambridge account explicitly places the Olgyays' bioclimatic research from the early 1950s onward and records the 1954 and 1957 books before the 1963 synthesis.

### Consequence

The frozen F07 lineage should treat:

- **early-1950s Olgyay work** as the beginning of the documented modern bioclimatic programme relevant here;
- **1963 *Design with Climate*** as the mature and portable synthesis, not a magical origin date.

This does not require a larger public history. It merely prevents a false priority chronology.

### Relationship

Olgyay remains:

- `EARLIER_ANTECEDENT`
- likely later `FOUNDATIONAL` or `MAJOR`, subject to ILA-02/04 rather than discovery

No documented formative project contact before ILA-F07 has been established.

## 2.2 `Passive-first but not passive-only` is itself inherited

A tempting residual claim after ILA-01B was that Ovid might at least own the anti-dogmatic formulation:

> **`First` does not mean `only`.**

The adversarial pass rejects a broad novelty reading of that boundary.

### Olgyay

Olgyay's bioclimatic framework is not a rejection of technology.

The relevant ordering moves through climate / microclimate / structural response and then mechanical correction where necessary. The purpose is to reduce environmental load through architecture before relying on plant, not to outlaw plant.

### Hawkes

Dean Hawkes is even closer to the current C13 wording.

His selective-environment work distinguishes selective from exclusive environmental control and gives:

- primary environmental control to built form / fabric and ambient sources;
- mechanical heating, cooling, ventilation and lighting a **supplementary** role;
- occupant control a deliberate place.

Primary article:

- Dean Hawkes, `The theoretical basis of comfort in the 'selective' control of environments`, *Energy and Buildings* 5(2), 1982: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0378778882900081

### Banham

Reyner Banham remains the essential counterpoint.

His environmental history prevents the project from treating mechanical environmental control as an architectural embarrassment. Conservative, selective and regenerative modes are all legitimate ways architecture has produced environmental conditions.

### Consequence

Ovid must not claim novelty for:

- architecture before plant;
- fabric as primary control;
- ambient sources before powered correction;
- active systems as supplements rather than enemies;
- occupant control inside a selective system;
- combining passive and active environmental modes pragmatically.

The project may retain its own concise wording, but the underlying intellectual position is inherited.

## 2.3 Passive provision as failure fallback is prior art

ILA-01B left one potentially distinctive cross-family rationale:

> durable passive provision may be valuable not only for energy demand, but because it provides fallback when active systems or utilities fail.

The adversarial pass found a direct prior formulation.

Alex Wilson's **passive survivability** work from 2005–06 explicitly argues that houses, apartments and other buildings should maintain critical / habitable conditions during extended loss of power, heating fuel or other services.

Sources:

- Alex Wilson, `Passive Survivability`, *Environmental Building News*, December 2005: https://www.buildinggreen.com/op-ed/passive-survivability
- Alex Wilson, `Passive Survivability: A New Design Criterion for Buildings`, *Environmental Building News*, May 2006: https://www.buildinggreen.com/feature/passive-survivability-new-design-criterion-buildings

Relevant strategies include:

- high-performance envelope;
- passive-solar provision;
- natural ventilation;
- reduced dependence on powered environmental systems.

### Consequence

Ovid does **not** originate the proposition that passive environmental measures can provide useful resilience when active services disappear.

Wilson should be retained as:

- `EARLIER_ANTECEDENT` to the passive-fallback / failure-resilience rationale;
- probably `CONTEXTUAL` rather than a central F07 foundational source, unless ILA-02 shows the failure-link to be more important to C13 than currently expected.

The remaining project distinction is narrower:

- F04's explicit failure/recovery machinery is joined to F07's passive-first discipline inside the same house system;
- the project can test whether a permanent architectural move serves both normal environmental demand and abnormal fallback.

That is integration, not priority.

## 2.4 Future-climate scenario testing is established professional practice

ILA-01B left `future-climate scenario testing of permanent geometry with rewind authority` as one of the strongest residual Ovid hypotheses.

The **scenario testing** part does not survive as novelty.

CIBSE already provides climate-projection weather files specifically for design-stage building-performance analysis.

Current CIBSE weather data includes UKCP18-based projections for:

- 2030s;
- 2050s;
- 2080s;

and multiple scenarios / probability percentiles.

CIBSE explicitly describes the future files as suitable for:

- overheating stress testing;
- evaluating future energy requirements;
- climate-resilience assessment.

Sources:

- CIBSE Weather Data: https://www.cibse.org/knowledge-research/weather-data/
- CIBSE Overheating Position Statement: https://www.cibse.org/policy-advocacy/key-policy-areas/health-and-wellbeing/overheating-position-statement/

The position statement explicitly recommends future timelines such as the 2050s and 2080s where appropriate to test mitigation under future / extreme conditions.

### Consequence

Ovid must not claim novelty for:

- testing buildings against future climate scenarios;
- using future weather files to test overheating / energy performance;
- stress-testing environmental strategies beyond present-day weather.

### Residual project hypothesis

What may remain project-specific is the **governance consequence**:

> if future-climate testing shows that a permanent geometry or envelope decision creates an avoidable long-term liability, the design sequence is authorised to rewind upstream rather than simply adding more active correction downstream.

The testing method is prior art.

The explicit relationship between test result and **architectural rewind authority** may be a project process formulation.

That remains to be judged later; it is not an originality claim at discovery stage.

## 2.5 Contemporary practice already rejects fabric-first dogma

Ovid currently protects C13 with a strong limit:

> measured performance, health and resilience outrank ideological simplicity.

The adversarial pass tested whether this anti-dogmatic boundary is itself distinctive.

It is not.

Historic England's current retrofit guidance gives a particularly clear building-specific counterexample.

It warns that a simplistic `fabric first` approach can be:

- impracticable;
- technically unsound;
- ineffective;
- damaging to moisture behaviour / fabric;
- harmful to indoor-air quality or overheating performance.

It requires a whole-building approach and explicitly says ventilation may need to be:

- natural;
- mechanical;
- hybrid.

Sources:

- Historic England, `Improving Energy Efficiency Through Mitigation`: https://historicengland.org.uk/advice/technical-advice/retrofit-and-energy-efficiency-in-historic-buildings/improving-energy-efficiency-through-mitigation/
- Historic England, `Risks of Energy Efficiency Interventions in Buildings of Traditional Construction`: https://historicengland.org.uk/advice/technical-advice/retrofit-and-energy-efficiency-in-historic-buildings/improving-energy-efficiency-through-mitigation/risks-of-energy-efficiency-interventions/

Passivhaus supplies a different counterexample: extremely low environmental demand can coexist with deliberate reliance on controlled mechanical ventilation and other engineered systems.

### Consequence

Ovid does not originate:

- the rejection of passive-only ideology;
- the rule that health overrides formal simplicity;
- the need to choose natural / mechanical / hybrid ventilation based on performance;
- the warning that energy interventions can produce moisture, IAQ or overheating harm;
- the idea that low demand and sophisticated active systems can coexist.

The project can still make this boundary constitutionally explicit. That is governance value, not conceptual priority.

# 4. Claim-by-claim frozen discovery disposition

F07 has one primary claim.

## ILA-C13 — Passive architecture does the first work

### Current proposition

> **Use form, envelope, orientation, shading, mass, controllable openings, drainage and gravity to reduce demands on active systems before adding mechanical or electronic correction.**

With the explicit limit:

> **`First` does not mean `only`. Measured performance, health and resilience outrank ideological simplicity.**

### Strongest prior-art families

- Olgyay — bioclimatic architecture and architecture-before-mechanical sequence;
- Hawkes — built form / ambient sources as primary control, active systems supplementary;
- Banham — active environmental systems as legitimate architecture / counterpoint;
- Gordon — low energy joined to long life / loose fit;
- Wilson — passive survivability / service-loss fallback;
- CIBSE — future-climate scenario testing as professional practice;
- Historic England / Passivhaus — anti-dogmatic professional counterexamples;
- Illich / autonomous-house lineage — technical dependence / autonomy as bounded adjacent questions.

### Discovery-stage conclusion

**The underlying C13 principle is inherited architectural discipline.**

There is no defensible priority claim for the generic environmental ordering rule.

### Residual candidate Ovid delta

Potentially:

1. **cross-family lifespan rationale**  
   Passive provision is valued not only for energy but because permanent geometry often outlives active equipment and therefore deserves a different burden of proof.

2. **cross-family failure integration**  
   Passive environmental capability is reviewed alongside F04 failure/fallback logic rather than as a separate sustainability score. Wilson proves the idea of passive fallback; Ovid may still differ in whole-house integration.

3. **proportional technical-dependence objective**  
   Reduced dependence is secondary and conditional rather than autonomy-maximalist.

4. **architectural rewind governance**  
   Future-climate / performance testing may force an upstream change to permanent geometry before accepting a downstream plant solution.

5. **shared evidence machinery**  
   The same project hierarchy distinguishes doctrine, project judgement, regulatory requirement, empirical evidence and Reference House occurrence.

These are **synthesis / governance hypotheses** only.

Nothing in discovery establishes them as unprecedented.

# 7. Leads rejected or deferred at saturation

## 7.1 A complete history of solar / passive architecture

Rejected.

Ancient solar orientation, vernacular climate responses, solar-house experiments and energy-crisis architecture can all push chronology earlier, but no longer change the judgement that C13 is established prior art.

Further history would improve completeness, not the decision.

## 7.2 Full adaptive-comfort genealogy

Deferred / out of scope.

Adaptive comfort may matter to specific environmental targets and occupant control, but F07 is not a comfort-standard history and HFI already owns the empirical performance boundary.

## 7.3 Full Passivhaus genealogy

Rejected.

The standard is useful as a counterpoint, not as the intellectual origin of passive-first architecture.

## 7.4 Full autonomous-house / off-grid history

Rejected.

The limit case is established. More self-sufficiency examples would not change Ovid's proportional position.

## 7.5 General sustainability canon

Rejected.

No LEED/BREEAM/carbon-accounting/sustainable-architecture survey is earned by the F07 question.

## 7.6 Mechanical-ventilation mechanism research

Out of scope.

Whether a particular house requires MVHR, MEV, natural ventilation or hybrid ventilation is a building-science / project-specific evidence question, not an intellectual-lineage finding.
