# 1. Decision

F03 is **FROZEN** at discovery stage.

The adversarial pass materially revises the discovery model, especially for ILA-C10.

The strongest bounded conclusion is:

> **Ovid's House does not originate the architectural idea that components from different producers should be coordinated through stable interfaces, that closed proprietary systems create harmful dependence, that later substitution should be possible, that joints can carry architectural meaning, or that technical systems should be given deliberate architectural order. All of those propositions have substantial prior architectural lineage. Ovid's remaining candidate contribution is narrower: the synthesis of lifecycle-boundary accounting, weak-stewardship assumptions, replacement-sequence verification, workmanship robustness and quiet domestic architectural resolution into one whole-house interface discipline.**

The family should therefore not be narrated as a genealogy of `tectonic honesty` or as a history of prefabrication. It contains three different intellectual streams:

1. **functional interface discipline** — movement, tolerance, boundary continuity, access and replacement;
2. **open-system interoperability** — ordinary or heterogeneous components coordinated by stable interface rules rather than a closed total system;
3. **tectonic / spatial resolution of technology** — joints and services becoming architectural order rather than residue or spectacle.

The adversarial search found earlier and stronger sources for each stream. Additional broad searching now adds names and examples but no longer changes the core relationship model, counterpoints, residual Ovid delta or later dossier priorities.

# 2. What changed after ILA-01B-F03

ILA-01B-F03 already established SCSD, Open Building, Wachsmann, Prouvé, Pye, Frascari, Kahn and Banham as material sources or counterpoints.

ILA-01C changes that model in four important ways.

## 2.1 The British Modular Society predates SCSD as a close C10 antecedent

The discovery packet treated SCSD as the closest architectural antecedent to Ovid's `ordinary parts / stable interfaces / varied architecture` proposition.

That is too late.

British modular-coordination work in the mid-1950s already framed the problem explicitly as the opening of **closed systems**.

A 1956 *Architects' Journal* report on W. E. Tatton-Brown's Modular Society position records the demand for:

- opening closed systems through interchangeability;
- jointing conventions analogous to those of ordinary construction;
- coordination that would allow a component to be exchanged for one outside the original system.

Sources:

- *The Architects' Journal*, 16 February 1956: https://www.usmodernist.org/AJUK/AJUK-1956-02-16.pdf
- *The Architects' Journal*, 12 April 1956: https://www.usmodernist.org/AJUK/AJUK-1956-04-12.pdf
- *The Architects' Journal*, 22 November 1956, discussing the unresolved need to free components from closed systems and achieve interchangeability: https://www.usmodernist.org/AJUK/AJUK-1956-11-22.pdf

The Modular Society's experimental **Modular Assembly**, completed in 1958, then tested an open approach using many market-available components from many manufacturers.

Christine Wall's archival history reconstructs the project as:

- 80 different modular components;
- 36 manufacturers;
- as many components as possible bought `off the shelf`;
- explicitly intended to demonstrate an **open** alternative to closed proprietary systems.

Secondary reconstruction:

- Christine Wall, *An Architecture of Parts: Architects, Building Workers and Industrialisation in Britain 1940–1970* (Routledge, 2013), chapter on the Modular Society and Modular Assemblies.

### Consequence

SCSD remains important because it operationalised subsystem performance/interface specifications at institutional scale, but it is **not the earliest close antecedent** to C10.

The Modular Society is a strong `EARLIER_ANTECEDENT` candidate for:

- ordinary market components;
- open rather than closed systems;
- interchangeability;
- joint/interface conventions;
- architecture retaining design responsibility while components come from multiple suppliers.

There is no documented project contact before ILA.

This materially narrows any claim that Ovid's House newly combines ordinary parts with stable interfaces.

## 2.2 Open Building extends interoperability from procurement into later replacement

The discovery packet left an important uncertainty: did SCSD/open systems establish only **initial multi-manufacturer compatibility**, or did they also establish **future in-use substitution**?

The answer is that later Open Building theory explicitly does the latter.

Stephen Kendall's 2004 account lists as a core Open Building idea:

> the interface between technical systems allows the replacement of one system with another performing the same function.

Source:

- Stephen Kendall, `An Open Building Strategy for Achieving Dwelling Unit Autonomy in Multi-Unit Housing`, *Housing and Society* 31(1), 2004: https://drstephenkendall.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/12/An-OB-Strategy-for-Achieving-Dwelling-Unit-Autonomy-in-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Housing-Society-2004-copy.pdf

Kendall and Teicher's *Residential Open Building* similarly develops:

- interchangeability between products from different manufacturers;
- technically distinct subsystems;
- reduction of interface entanglement;
- replacement and upgrading with less disturbance;
- a deliberate counter-position to `total systems integration and unified design control`.

Source:

- Stephen Kendall and Jonathan Teicher, *Residential Open Building* (E & FN Spon, 2000): https://www.uceb.eu/DATA/CivBook/43.%20Residential%20Open%20Building%2C%20Stephen%20Kendall%20and%20Jonathan%20Teicher.pdf

### Consequence

Future substitution is **not** an Ovid-originated extension of SCSD.

For C10, Open Building is a strong `EARLIER_ANTECEDENT` to:

- subsystem disentanglement;
- replacement without disturbing adjacent systems;
- substitution by later products performing the same function;
- multi-manufacturer interchangeability.

The residual Ovid question is therefore not `can another supplier's component replace this one?` in the abstract. It is narrower and more operational:

- does the building remain understandable under weak stewardship;
- can ordinary future trades identify the system;
- are ordinary tools sufficient;
- are fixings still physically reachable;
- can bespoke architectural covers/interfaces be reproduced after suppliers disappear;
- does the complete removal/replacement/reinstatement sequence preserve every relevant boundary;
- is that capability actually verified rather than merely stated.

Those remain candidate project deltas only, not originality claims.

## 2.3 Contemporary professional guidance now owns much of generic C06/C10

The adversarial pass also tested whether current professional guidance has already normalised the interface/interoperability logic.

It has.

### RIBA DfMA Overlay

The RIBA *DfMA Overlay to the RIBA Plan of Work* (2nd ed., 2021) states that:

- standardising interfaces facilitates interoperability and market competition;
- proprietary building systems create long-term supplier dependency and close down options;
- performance specification and standardised interfaces can reduce that dependency;
- interoperability means interfaces are understood well enough to work with other products, assemblies or systems **at present or in the future**.

Source:

- RIBA, *DfMA Overlay to the RIBA Plan of Work*, 2nd edition, 2021: https://www.riba.org/media/t3dpnww2/design-for-manufacture-and-assembly-overlay-to-the-riba-plan-of-work-2020-2nd-editionpdf.pdf

### ISO 20887:2020

ISO 20887 establishes, among other things:

- independence between systems/layers;
- access to components and services;
- removal/replacement without disturbing adjacent components;
- reversible connections;
- standardisation;
- explicit pre-construction disassembly methods;
- decision-making under service life, cost, obsolescence, ownership, maintenance responsibility and trade-offs.

Source:

- ISO 20887:2020, *Sustainability in buildings and civil engineering works — Design for disassembly and adaptability — Principles, requirements and guidance*.

### Consequence

Ovid must not imply novelty for generic:

- system independence;
- accessible/reversible connections;
- future substitution;
- anti-proprietary interoperability;
- standardising interfaces rather than entire products;
- designing replacement/disassembly in advance.

These are established professional prior art.

What may remain distinctive is the way Ovid binds those established mechanics to its **architectural and stewardship constraints**, especially where a single interface is required to preserve structure, fire, sound, air, vapour, bulk water, pests, security, finish, tolerance, movement and future replacement sequence together.

## 2.4 Kahn is a closer C14 antecedent than technological exhibition

ILA-01B correctly retained Banham as a broad technology/architecture antecedent and counterpoint, but the adversarial pass strengthens **Louis Kahn** as the closer source for Ovid's actual C14 posture.

In `Architecture Is the Thoughtful Making of Spaces` (1957), Kahn argues that mechanical requirements can `push forward and obscure form in structure`; his answer is not denial or decorative exposure, but a space order that **harbours** services and gives servant spaces their own structure.

Primary source:

- Louis I. Kahn, `Architecture Is the Thoughtful Making of Spaces`, *Perspecta* 4 (1957), pp. 2–3.

Kahn therefore matters to C14 not because Ovid adopts his formal language, but because he articulates the closer architectural principle:

> technical necessity should receive deliberate spatial/structural place so that it belongs to the architecture rather than invading it accidentally.

This is a stronger antecedent to Ovid's `quiet integration` than a High-Tech lineage based on exposure.

Frascari remains valuable for the **joint/detail as architectural locus**. Banham remains useful precisely because he demonstrates a broader range of possible architecture/technology relationships and therefore prevents Ovid's quiet domestic treatment from masquerading as the only honest form of technical expression.

# 3. Frozen F03 topology

F03 is frozen as three intersecting streams.

## Stream A — functional interface discipline

Question:

> What physical jobs must the junction reconcile, and how are incompatibility, movement, tolerance and replacement prevented from becoming failure?

### Established prior art

Conventional and professional practice already owns:

- movement joints and bearings;
- tolerance allocation and adjustment zones;
- accessible/reversible connections;
- environmental-boundary continuity;
- maintenance access;
- designed disassembly/replacement;
- independence of systems/layers.

Relevant sources include:

- ISO 20887:2020;
- professional facade/window/joint guidance;
- British tolerance practice;
- service-life and maintainability literature;
- current Ovid evidence sources already inventoried under ILA-00A.

### F03 significance

The project cannot claim to have invented the **designed interface** as a technical necessity.

The unresolved candidate delta is the particular **interface ledger** that forces multiple boundary/performance/lifecycle obligations to be reconciled in one architectural object and then verified through a complete future work sequence.

That hypothesis must be tested later; it is not established by discovery.

---

## Stream B — open-system interoperability

Question:

> Can components or subsystems from different or later suppliers participate through stable rules without forcing the building into one proprietary total system?

### Historical sequence — typed, not genealogically forced

**British modular-coordination movement / Modular Society — 1950s**  
`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT` to open interchangeability and jointing conventions.

The relevant proposition is not merely dimensional modularity. It is the attempt to free components from closed systems so they may be interchanged across suppliers.

**Modular Assembly — 1958**  
`PRECEDENT / EXEMPLAR` + `EARLIER_ANTECEDENT` to ordinary/off-the-shelf multi-manufacturer component assembly.

**Wachsmann / General Panel — 1940s–50s**  
`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT` + `COUNTERPOINT`.

Wachsmann's universal connector shows the ambition to make many configurations from repeatable parts, but General Panel remained a much more closed, bespoke and commercially fragile total-system proposition. It is therefore useful precisely as contrast with Ovid's preference for ordinary components and few architectural interfaces.

**SCSD / Ehrenkrantz — 1962 onward**  
`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT`.

SCSD operationalises common modular/performance specifications across independent subsystem manufacturers and demonstrates that standard components need not produce standard buildings.

Primary/authoritative background:

- National Academies, *Fourth Dimension in Building: Strategies for Avoiding Obsolescence* (1993), SCSD discussion: https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/2124/chapter/5

**Open Building / Kendall / Teicher — 1990s–2000s**  
`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT` to in-use substitution and subsystem disentanglement.

Open Building explicitly carries interface openness into later replacement and upgrade rather than only initial procurement.

**RIBA DfMA / platform guidance — contemporary**  
`EVIDENTIAL_SUPPORT` / professional prior art for future-facing interoperability, anti-lock-in and standardised interfaces.

### F03 significance

There is no defensible Ovid originality claim for:

- open systems;
- multi-manufacturer components;
- standard interfaces;
- standard components producing varied architecture;
- interchangeability;
- future supplier substitution;
- anti-proprietary interface strategy.

The only residual candidate delta is an unusually explicit **century-scale domestic weak-stewardship discipline**, combining supplier independence with physical access, replacement path, remanufacturability, ordinary tools/trades, legibility and boundary reinstatement.

---

## Stream C — technical necessity as architecture

Question:

> Once the technical condition is real and necessary, how should it enter architecture without becoming either concealed accident or gratuitous technical display?

### Kahn

`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT`.

Kahn's served/servant and `hollow stone` thinking gives services deliberate spatial/structural place. His relevant F03 value is **integration through order**, not aesthetic exposure for its own sake.

### Frascari / Scarpa

`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT` / theoretical articulation.

Frascari's `Tell-the-Tale Detail` makes the detail/joint a locus where construction and architectural meaning meet. Scarpa is the principal exemplar through which that argument is developed.

The overlap with Ovid is real at the level of `the joint is not residue`; it does not establish Ovid's lifecycle or interoperability logic.

### Banham

`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT` + `COUNTERPOINT`.

Banham establishes that architecture's relationship to environmental/technical systems is a first-order architectural question. His broader technology-positive field also prevents the project from equating honesty with quiet concealment.

### Prouvé

`PRECEDENT / EXEMPLAR`.

Prouvé demonstrates demountable/repeatable assembly with genuine architectural quality, but should not be inflated into the universal conceptual source for C06/C10/C14.

### F03 significance

Ovid cannot claim to have discovered that:

- joints are architectural;
- construction can be legible without being literal exhibition;
- services should be given spatial order;
- technical systems can shape architecture;
- demountable/repeatable assemblies can be architecturally resolved.

The residual Ovid hypothesis is **quiet tectonic integration under domestic-Repose constraints**, combined with lifecycle and maintenance obligations. That combination may be project-specific in formulation, but each ingredient has prior art.

# 5. Counterpoints retained

## 5.1 Closed total systems

Wachsmann / General Panel and many proprietary systems show that elegant integration can still create:

- supplier dependence;
- high bespoke content;
- brittle commercial ecosystems;
- difficult future substitution;
- failure if the total system disappears.

This is the strongest counterpoint to fetishising system elegance.

## 5.2 Protocol reductionism

Stephen Kendall's criticism of some open-system traditions remains important: an architecture reduced to interface/performance protocols can lose architectural content.

Ovid's interface discipline therefore cannot close merely because components interoperate.

## 5.3 Universal demountability

ISO 20887 itself is contextual rather than absolutist. F02 already established the proportionality boundary.

F03 must therefore reject the inference that every joint should be reversible or every assembly demountable.

## 5.4 Technical-display aesthetics

Banham / later High-Tech traditions demonstrate that technical legibility can legitimately become overt architectural expression.

Ovid's quieter domestic posture is a **project choice bounded by Repose**, not the universal meaning of tectonic honesty.

## 5.5 Craft dependence

Pye prevents `reproducible assembly` from becoming a crude anti-craft doctrine.

The project should preserve the distinction:

- eliminate avoidable skilled correction at unresolved interfaces;
- retain workmanship where skilled judgement creates architectural value.

## 5.6 Standards are not architecture

RIBA / ISO can establish interoperability, access and reversibility principles without deciding how a threshold, architrave, reveal, panel or service cover should become good architecture.

The standards therefore close novelty space for mechanics while leaving architectural judgement genuinely open.

# 9. Residual Ovid-delta hypotheses after saturation

The following are the only F03 deltas worth carrying forward for serious testing.

## 9.1 Lifecycle interface ledger

Ovid may differ in requiring one interface record to reconcile, where relevant:

- load / restraint;
- movement;
- tolerance;
- fire;
- smoke;
- sound;
- air;
- vapour;
- bulk water;
- thermal continuity;
- pests;
- security;
- finish;
- access;
- fixing reachability;
- removal path;
- replacement path;
- reinstatement;
- future inspection.

The novelty question is not whether each item is established — they are — but whether this **whole lifecycle boundary accounting** is a meaningful project synthesis.

## 9.2 Weak-stewardship interoperability

Ovid may sharpen interoperability by asking whether the interface still works when:

- the original manufacturer is gone;
- the original installer is gone;
- proprietary information is unavailable;
- only ordinary competent trades are available;
- digital records are incomplete;
- a cover, trim or backplane must be remade locally;
- the replacement product is not identical.

Open Building and current RIBA guidance already own much of future substitution; this hypothesis survives only at the **weak-stewardship implementation standard**.

## 9.3 Complete work-sequence verification

Ovid may be distinctive in refusing nominal access/reversibility unless the complete sequence is executable:

`identify → isolate → expose → reach → disconnect → withdraw → replace → reconnect → restore boundaries → close → verify`.

This overlaps OCG and F05 and must later be tested against maintainability literature before being treated as a project delta.

## 9.4 Quiet tectonic integration under domestic Repose

Ovid's position may be distinctive only as a particular synthesis:

- real technical function;
- concealment permitted;
- falsification rejected;
- overt technical expression not required;
- ordinary domestic architectural elements may perform genuine interface work;
- visual resolution judged partly through Repose.

This is clearly not a universal architectural law.

## 9.5 Enforcement through project machinery

The most plausible project-specific contribution may ultimately be **methodological** rather than conceptual:

- doctrine;
- strategy;
- patterns;
- replacement sequences;
- evidence state;
- Reference House integration;
- compiler checks where a relationship is modelable;
- rewind when interface failure invalidates upstream architecture.

ILA-04 must later determine whether this is enough to count as a meaningful Ovid delta or merely a thorough implementation of established ideas.
