# 1. Decision

F05 is **FROZEN**.

The adversarial pass collapses most of the residual novelty space left by ILA-01B-F05.

- **VA Hospital Building System (VAHBS)** already integrates functional planning, structural bay dimensions, service bays, horizontal interstitial zones, reserved service subzones, standing maintenance access and future modification from the initial design stage. Services are therefore an upstream planning / structural determinant rather than residual routing.
- **John Weeks** was already described as using `geography rather than design` to generate hospital form, with clear principal routes, separated service/communication networks and independently expandable parts.
- **Established maintainability practice** already designs approach, galleries/platforms, work position, lifting, heavy-plant routes, removal and replacement clearances.
- **Ignaz Strebel's 2011 paper is literally titled _The living building: towards a geography of maintenance work_.** The current manuscript already cites it. This removes any defensible naming-priority story around the phrase-family `maintenance geography`.
- **Physical identifiers tied to drawings/O&M**, site verification of stale records and lifecycle information handover are established practice; C11's remaining distinction is at most its explicit weak-stewardship framing.

The strongest bounded conclusion is:

> **Ovid's House does not originate service hierarchy, service-driven planning, dedicated service zones, maintenance-work geography, physical service identification, record/field correspondence or lifecycle information continuity. The remaining candidate contribution is a proportional domestic synthesis that binds where a system lives, how foreseeable work is executed, how failure and occupied-change consequences are bounded, and how future people identify and understand the system under weak stewardship — then gives that synthesis explicit governance and rewind authority through the project's pattern / Reference House / verification machinery.**

That is a candidate **synthesis / operationalisation**, not an originality claim.

# 2. Four source streams

F05 is best understood as the intersection of four established traditions:

1. **architectural service order** — services shape plan, section, structure and hierarchy;
2. **maintenance-work geography** — approach, access, work position, handling, lifting, withdrawal, replacement and reinstatement;
3. **maintenance / repair as constitutive work** — maintenance belongs to the life of architecture rather than its afterlife;
4. **stewardship / information continuity** — future people must identify and safely work on systems after teams, owners, suppliers and records change.

Ovid's internal shorthand remains useful:

`where → work → know`

but is classified only as a **project coordination model / mnemonic**.

# 3. VA Hospital Building System — decisive C08/C09 antecedent

Sources:

- VA, *VA Hospital Building System*: https://www.cfm.va.gov/til/studies/VAHBS.pdf
- VA/WBDG, *VA Hospital Building System Development Study Supplement*: https://www.wbdg.org/FFC/VA/VASTUDIES/hospital_bldg_system_supp.pdf

VAHBS defines a **service module** containing structural bays plus a service bay. Structural-bay dimensions respond to functional layout, service-zone clearances and structural-system choice. Major plant and vertical distribution occupy the service bay; horizontal distribution occupies an interstitial service zone.

The source calls the service module the most significant planning component because it coordinates:

- function;
- structure;
- service distribution;
- fire safety.

The service zone is further divided into reserved subzones / channels intended to:

- give trades clear routes;
- minimise crossovers;
- permit future extensions;
- keep component locations predictable;
- support maintenance and modification.

Interstitial access is by **industrial stair**, not ladder, and gives standing work space. Surveyed remodel work could proceed without disrupting occupied space below.

### Relationship

`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT + PRECEDENT / EXEMPLAR`.

No project influence is claimed.

### Ovid consequence

Ovid cannot claim invention of:

- services affecting planning / structural modules;
- service geography established at initial design;
- dedicated vertical/horizontal service zones;
- reserved channels for service families;
- standing maintenance access;
- future modification enabled by service-zone organisation.

The valid difference is **domestic proportional transfer**: a hospital may justify an interstitial floor; a house usually cannot.

# 4. John Weeks — geography as generator already exists

Source:

- Architects for Health, `John Weeks`: https://www.architectsforhealth.com/2005/06/john-weeks/

The Llewelyn-Davies Weeks retrospective states that Weeks introduced **`geography` rather than a fixed `design` generating a hospital form**.

Northwick Park / `Indeterminate Architecture` used:

- clear principal routes;
- separated major service/communication networks;
- expandable services;
- independently adaptable parts;
- defined directions of growth without a fixed final form.

Relationship:

`EARLIER_ANTECEDENT / CONVERGENT`.

There is no evidence that Ovid's terminology descends from Weeks.

Freeze consequence:

> `maintenance geography` cannot be defended as conceptually novel merely because it is project vocabulary.

# 5. Ignaz Strebel — explicit `geography of maintenance work`

Source:

- Ignaz Strebel, `The living building: towards a geography of maintenance work`, *Social & Cultural Geography* 12(3), 2011, pp. 243–262, DOI 10.1080/14649365.2011.564732.

The paper follows concierge `block checks` at Red Road, Glasgow: workers move systematically through lifts, stairs and floors, inspect the building, coordinate routes and perform minor maintenance.

Its importance to F05 is conceptual and terminological:

- maintenance is spatial practice;
- the building is animated through repeated care / inspection / problem-solving;
- a **geography of maintenance work** is explicit pre-Ovid language.

Relationship:

`EXPLICIT_PROJECT_SOURCE / INTELLECTUAL CONTEXT` because the current manuscript already cites it.

The current manuscript correctly distinguishes:

- Strebel — descriptive / ethnographic maintenance geography;
- Ovid — prospective architectural organisation of maintenance-intensive systems, work spaces, routes and replacement paths.

This is a **prospective operationalisation** distinction, not naming priority.

# 6. Established maintainability practice collapses broad C08 novelty

Source:

- IStructE, `Designing for maintenance` (2022): https://www.istructe.org/resources/guidance/designing-for-maintenance/

The guidance explicitly treats maintenance access as a design responsibility and includes:

- access galleries for inspection and replacement;
- platforms in shafts;
- access hatches;
- safe cladding removal/replacement;
- routes to plant rooms capable of carrying replacement plant;
- winch points / crane beams;
- overhead lifting gear;
- safe routes for removing and replacing heavy plant.

This already represents:

`approach → stand/work → inspect/manipulate → lift/support → remove → replace`

as a design problem.

Related CIRIA / CDM / CIBSE practice gives this a longer professional lineage.

### Ovid consequence

The underlying `maintenance geography` task is established. Ovid's useful move is cross-scale coordination and proportional domestic application, not invention.

# 7. C11 — physical plus documentary redundancy is established

## Physical identifiers

Ordinary M&E specifications already require:

- permanent plant / valve / pipe labels;
- unique valve numbers;
- valve charts / schematics;
- labels corresponding to record drawings;
- cross-reference to O&M information.

## Records are not trusted blindly

Source:

- Historic England, `Installing New Services`: https://historicengland.org.uk/advice/technical-advice/building-services-engineering/installing-new-services/

Historic England requires desk-based records to be verified on site and notes that record drawings may be old, out of date or omit later services.

## Lifecycle information

Source:

- GOV.UK, `Keeping information about a higher-risk building: the golden thread`: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/keeping-information-about-a-higher-risk-building-the-golden-thread

The golden-thread regime requires building information to be accurate, intelligible, change controlled, handed over and maintained during occupation.

### Ovid consequence

Ovid cannot claim novelty for:

- durable field identifiers;
- identifiers tied to records;
- lifecycle handover;
- change control;
- recognising stale drawings;
- physical verification against records.

The surviving project framing is to treat **degraded stewardship as an expected long-life condition** and prefer geometry / identifiers / isolation that remain partly intelligible when records are imperfect.

That is a synthesis / narrowing, not a priority claim.

---

# 8. Counterpoint — topology can decay

Richards Laboratories shows that a strong original service hierarchy is not self-enforcing. Later interventions can congest routes, create ad hoc crossings and erode legibility.

The lesson is:

> **geometry without stewardship is not a permanent guarantee of legibility.**

This supports combining physical geography with identifiers, records and change control, but does not establish novelty.

# 13. Saturation test

Discovery is saturated because further searching no longer changes the following:

- service hierarchy predates Ovid;
- geography as a change-supporting framework predates Ovid;
- `geography of maintenance work` is explicit pre-Ovid scholarly language;
- service zones affecting structure/planning predate Ovid;
- accessible service geography predates Ovid;
- complete access/lifting/removal/replacement planning is established;
- field identification + records is established;
- lifecycle information continuity is established.

Stable counterpoints are:

- institutional provision can be disproportionate for houses;
- initial topology can decay;
- records and labels can become stale;
- maintainability provision itself creates area/material burdens;
- serviceability can become institutionally expressive;
- technical optimisation must not override architectural quality / Repose.

Any plausible surviving F05 delta is now limited to:

- proportional domestic transfer;
- cross-family integration;
- explicit rewind / governance;
- degraded-stewardship design case;
- internal mnemonic / operational compression.

**Disposition: `FROZEN FOR DISCOVERY`.**
