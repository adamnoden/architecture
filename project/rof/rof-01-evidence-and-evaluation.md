## Question and answer

Which comparison dimensions prevent a visually simple or nominally durable roof from winning on unearned assumptions?

The [roof failure and recovery evaluation](../../docs/research/roof-failure-evaluation.md) establishes the common candidate record: thirteen separate dimensions, seven located challenge cases, evidence/unknown labels and five counterexamples. It preserves the differences between water entry and destructive hidden wetting, covering life and assembly renewal, joint count and interface consequence, and apparent redundancy and independent recovery.

Primary evidence includes England moisture/fire guidance, HSE roof-work guidance, Historic England's lead-corrosion research and identified NHBC/copper-industry material. The evidence table records editions, locators, access limitations and applicability. Indexed excerpts do not become a claim to have read the inaccessible full document. No current standard detail, service-life number, product selection or regulatory approval is asserted.

## Acceptance and red-team review

All ROF-01 programme dimensions are mapped to a candidate question and evidence requirement. The calibration cases reject scalar joint-count/material-longevity answers while allowing well-detailed conventional construction to win. Unknowns remain explicit and essential unresolved performance/fit conditions cannot pass selection merely by being assigned to a later stream.

The comparison is falsifiable: it fails if a later candidate's decisive mechanism cannot be expressed, if an “independent” backup shares the initiating failure, or if a preferred conclusion depends on unsupported lifespan, access or hidden-layer claims. Later evidence may revise the frame explicitly; “frozen” means a common recoverable baseline for parallel research, not immunity from correction.

No geometry, no-flat-roof rule, shared roof/water contract, maturity or claim ceiling changed. The research adds no compiler schema or synthetic physical test. ROF-02, ROF-03 and ROF-04 can now use the same frame; their aliases remain one canonical lease per packet, as defined in PCO.
