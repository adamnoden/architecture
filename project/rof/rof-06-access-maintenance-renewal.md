## Decision and adverse evidence

Reject the tested ordinary BoSS 850/1.8 m high mobile tower in the 3500 court: required SP10 footprint 4072×4768 exceeds it by 572/1268. Lower SP7 nominally fits, but its useful level is too low; the represented −150 grade makes the advertised low working level 1250 below the +6800 eave. The manufacturer also forbids using that working platform to access another structure. The separately documented LiftShaft700 is indoor-only, so its smaller body and props/ties do not rescue an open-court proposal.

The aligned 2400 nominal garden/court doors admit a conditional 950×2100×300 authored component-carrying envelope in the drawing. This is not a measured packed size or a human-action/clear-doorway acceptance; actual hardware, handlers, furniture and 150 level transition remain unresolved. An erected tower is not wheeled through this route.

A conventional purpose-designed court scaffold remains a candidate, with an authored 700 perimeter band, 2100 central remainder and 1000×1800 access reservation. These are spatial questions only. Bases, actual ladder/landings or stair, loading bay, ties and safe roof transfer require design. A located nominal spandrel patch above the south door earns no anchor in the 417.5 layered envelope. Inspection, receiver work and roof-slope travel have different working positions and burdens.

The existing shallow roof storage space is not a standing route. Inner receiver removal requires an independently supported temporary weathering/catchment state; common primary/secondary discharge through the disabled receiver cannot count as recovery. Ridge/valley working extents and whole replacement-object handling remain explicit. No permanent hatch, anchor, plant, selected scaffold or house geometry change is introduced.

Source arithmetic, retained-artifact check, A3 visual review, repository checks and exact-head CI belong in the PR. These validate a repeatable investigation, not safe execution, temporary works, physical handling, structural adequacy, statutory compliance or construction readiness. Completing ROF-06 unlocks synthesis; it does not close the access finding.
