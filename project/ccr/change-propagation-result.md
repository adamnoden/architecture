## Roof input and evidence boundary

The [roof-pitch adapter](../../compiler/reference-house/current-design/roof-pitch.ts) binds the [native MAT method](../../project/mat/studies/ring-roof-pitch-case.py), its catalogue, the exact reference geometry and the owner's 25° minimum pitch before running. The native program reuses `course_layout` and reproduces the existing 25° roof field ridge and both exposure schedules before computing the requested pitch. Its geometry holds the outer wall level and the recorded eave, ridge and court plan axes. Changed-pitch stock uses the 25° catalogue lap as a comparison. The product still needs pitch-specific installation evidence and resolved caps, hips, valleys, support and drainage geometry.

The suite moves presentation labels in paper space and confirms that geometry primitives, source identities and the recalculated evidence comparison stay equal. It rejects changed method bytes and a changed catalogue slate dimension by exact input identity, as well as stale case bindings, non-finite pitch values and missing or renamed object IDs. Existing reference roof evidence remains available at its original inputs and is withheld for the changed case.

Save/load and exact undo/redo reproduce the nominal draft and its plan in both specimens while advancing revisions. Delayed results from an old edit cannot populate a newer view. The suite also independently compiles the historical RH-C fixture before and after the edits and rejects it at the current entry point.

## Ownership and remaining integration

The named roof, service, structural and assembly cases remain separate versioned input contracts. The shared draft editor currently contains nominal plan geometry and assembly depth. A future editable technical candidate needs an explicit case-aware composition carrying every applicable native input and its object/field dependencies. Domain owners retain the selected roof/catchment, pressure, installed product and loaded manipulation proofs. CCR-CURRENT-RELEASE still audits their final selected entry points and controlled outputs after convergence. ADE-BROWSER-EDITOR consumes the same headless edit contract and owns the browser interactions.
