## 3. Does profile-disposition uncertainty block all aggregation?

### Hold argument

A strict reading could require every family to be classified definitively as due-now/future/not-applicable before any whole-site result is emitted.

That would avoid any chance that an unresolved acceleration question changes the current blocking set.

### Counter-argument from DPL-03

DPL-03 already says the deployment-level middle state is correct when:

- applicability of a blocking rule is unknown;
- required judgement is pending;
- required evidence is absent;
- the method is unsupported;
- a site-interface route is unresolved;
- another admitted route remains untested.

Those are not preconditions that must be cleared before `UNRESOLVED`. They are canonical reasons **for** `UNRESOLVED`.

### Decision

> **Profile-disposition uncertainty may participate in an `UNRESOLVED @ SCREENING` aggregate, provided it is represented explicitly as a blocking classification uncertainty and is not mistaken for a substantive failure or pass.**

Therefore `PROFILE_DISPOSITION_UNRESOLVED` does not force a new owner-research wave before first conservative aggregation.

It does, however, prohibit `COMPATIBLE @ SCREENING`.

## 4. Canonical acceleration-review disposition

DPL-13B currently raises acceleration-review references only where:

- the owner entry has an acceleration trigger recorded by DPL-04/owner authority; and
- a referenced canonical site claim is `UNKNOWN` or synthetic-assumed.

That mechanism is deliberately conservative. It does **not** say the trigger has fired.

DPL-I classifies every such canonical reference as:

```text
owner acceleration question: represented
native trigger result: absent
profile due-classification: unresolved
substantive failure: not established
positive discharge: not established
aggregation role: blocking uncertainty for UNRESOLVED-only aggregation
```

No owner-side repair is required merely to produce `UNRESOLVED`, because unresolved applicability/due classification is itself within DPL-03's middle-state semantics.

A future positive compatibility result **will** require those questions to be resolved sufficiently to know the complete due-now positive set.

DPL-13C must preserve owner identity on every acceleration blocker and must not collapse several trigger questions into a generic `unknown` count.

## 6. DPL-13C aggregation rule

DPL-I admits one deliberately asymmetric result implementation.

### DPL-13C may emit `UNRESOLVED @ SCREENING` only when all are true

1. exact deployment case identity is recorded;
2. exact DPL-13B readiness identity is recorded;
3. profile owner universe has **zero uncovered required claims/families/authority edges**;
4. every due-now or due-classification-unresolved blocking coverage entry has a corresponding aggregation-ledger record;
5. each aggregation record preserves owner authority and provenance;
6. actual DPL-13A/DPL-12B findings are used where available rather than replaced by coverage metadata;
7. a due blocker without an implemented evaluator/evidence determination is represented explicitly as `UNRESOLVED` or `UNSUPPORTED`, never omitted;
8. unresolved applicability/acceleration classification is represented separately from substantive unresolved results;
9. at least one current blocking record is unresolved/open/unsupported or has unresolved due classification;
10. no controlled proof establishes whole-site incompatibility;
11. route exhaustion remains absent/unproved and is stated as such;
12. the output is explicitly qualified `@ SCREENING` and carries future-due obligations separately;
13. conclusion provenance/fingerprint includes case, readiness, aggregation records and evidence state.

### If scope is incomplete

DPL-13C must **not** emit `UNRESOLVED`.

It must return a non-conclusion readiness state such as:

```text
WHOLE_SITE_CONCLUSION_NOT_ADMITTED
reason: PROFILE_UNIVERSE_INCOMPLETE
```

or equivalent.

### If every represented blocker happens to resolve positively

DPL-13C must still **not** emit `COMPATIBLE`.

It must stop at a non-conclusion state such as:

```text
POSITIVE_CONCLUSION_NOT_ADMITTED
```

because DPL-I authorises only the conservative middle conclusion.

### If a hard failure appears

DPL-13C must still **not** emit `INCOMPATIBLE` unless separately authorised route-exhaustion/invariant-blocker machinery exists.

The safe output remains `UNRESOLVED` while admitted routes remain or exhaustion is unproved.

## 7. Coverage uncertainty versus substantive uncertainty

DPL-13C must keep at least two blocker classes separate in provenance:

### `SUBSTANTIVE_UNRESOLVED`

Examples:

- actual obligation result `UNRESOLVED`;
- actual obligation result `UNSUPPORTED`;
- candidate conformance `OPEN`;
- absent external determination/evidence on an understood due route.

### `DUE_CLASSIFICATION_UNRESOLVED`

Examples:

- an opaque acceleration question cannot yet be resolved;
- applicability/timing cannot be determined from current controlled facts.

Both may support whole-site `UNRESOLVED @ SCREENING`.

Neither is a pass or failure.

Their separation must be identity-bearing and reader-visible in machine output.

## 8. Positive compatibility boundary

`COMPATIBLE @ SCREENING` remains **unauthorised**.

Admission of the conservative middle result does not imply the positive result.

Positive compatibility will require, at minimum:

- complete profile scope;
- resolved due/applicability/acceleration classification;
- every applicable due-now blocking result positively `PASS` or `EXTERNALLY_DISCHARGED`;
- candidate conformance positively resolved at required scope;
- current accepted evidence;
- no contradictory evidence;
- any local planning target required by a real site correctly composed;
- a separately authorised positive aggregation gate.

The synthetic DPL-RS-01 result cannot shortcut those requirements.

---

## 9. Incompatibility boundary

`INCOMPATIBLE` remains **unauthorised**.

DPL-03 still requires:

- invariant site-level blocking proof; or
- admitted route exhaustion.

DPL-I does not authorise route search, route optimisation or route exhaustion.

A `FAIL` inside DPL-13C therefore remains compatible with whole-site `UNRESOLVED` while a conformance-preserving route may remain or exhaustion is unproved.
