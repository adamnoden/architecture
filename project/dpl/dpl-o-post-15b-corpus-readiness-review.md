## 2. Candidate-authoring readiness is not compatibility

Freeze the following distinction.

`CANDIDATE_AUTHORING_READY` means only that the site evidence is sufficiently concrete to author one controlled SCREENING placement proposition for the unchanged 12.0 m × 10.8 m Reference House.

It does **not** mean:

- the house fits after all obligations are evaluated;
- planning policy is satisfied;
- title/control is proved;
- access rights are sufficient;
- ground/drainage/utilities are suitable;
- construction is feasible;
- `COMPATIBLE` is available.

Likewise `NOT_READY` is an evidence state, not `INCOMPATIBLE`.

## 3. Frozen SCREENING readiness contract

A real site may be considered candidate-authoring-ready only if the admitted evidence establishes all of the following at SCREENING scope.

### R1 — controlled spatial identity

There must be a sufficiently definite real parcel/application boundary or other controlled spatial extent for the placement proposition.

Approximate address points, host-property identity and approximate marketing area are not enough.

### R2 — footprint-test geometry

Shape/dimensions must be explicit enough to test a 12.0 m × 10.8 m footprint without reverse-engineering pixels, scaling screenshots or inventing a rectangle from gross area.

A controlled dimensioned planning drawing may satisfy this at SCREENING scope without becoming title/survey authority.

### R3 — real orientation/frame

The placement must have a real orientation/reference relationship rather than inheriting the synthetic Reference-Site XY/cardinal frame.

### R4 — datum / level evidence

There must be enough level/topographic information to author the house-ground relationship without inventing a datum.

This need not be construction-grade topography, but pure absence of levels is insufficient.

### R5 — physical access / street relationship

The evidence must establish enough actual approach/street/access geometry to place the house relative to the real approach condition.

This is distinct from legal access entitlement.

### R6 — site identity coherence

There must not be an unresolved identity edge that makes it unclear which land, sub-parcel or planning red line is being tested.

### Held separately

The following may remain unresolved at SCREENING candidate-authoring stage if explicitly carried forward:

- legal title/control;
- covenants/easements/private rights;
- planning judgement;
- geotechnics/groundwater/contamination;
- drainage/sewers/utilities;
- trees/ecology/flood;
- structural design;
- construction logistics.

Those matters block later conclusions, not necessarily the act of authoring a screening placement.

Jumping immediately from RP-003 to deeper Croydon document hunting would optimise one site's evidence path before knowing whether another admitted site already has a shorter path.
