## Exact decision question

For the unchanged T3/T4 Reference House placement, what are the current physical east and west side clearances and usable passages, with enough declared accuracy to apply the already-frozen Policy 8 and RH-PROT-14 decision rules?

## Minimum acquisition

Use the T9 base brief unchanged except that the public material above should be supplied as orientation/context so field time is not spent rediscovering already-known site form.

The competent return must still establish directly:

1. a repeatable survey control / coordinate basis suitable for centimetre-scale plan measurement;
2. the current physical east and west control features adjacent to the full Reference House side elevations, with continuation beyond the ends sufficient to remove endpoint ambiguity;
3. wall/fence/hedge thickness, overhangs and fixed obstructions that materially affect usable passage width;

6. usable physical passage widths where different from facade-to-control distance;
7. measurement accuracy / uncertainty at the controlling locations;
8. a simple plan extract showing the minima and relevant RH-08 maintenance access/task geography.

## Decision rules

### Bromley Policy 8 numeric floor

Use **1.000 m** as the already-frozen numeric floor for each relevant side:

- lower confidence bound **>= 1.000 m** → numeric floor supported on the measured/control basis; qualitative policy/context remains separate;
- upper confidence bound **< 1.000 m** → measured numeric conflict indication; escalate the planning/boundary basis, not whole-site incompatibility;
- interval straddles 1.000 m or relevant control line remains ambiguous → keep only the ambiguous portion unresolved.

### RH-PROT-14 maintenance geography

Apply the measured usable widths to the existing RH-08 maintenance task geography. Report affected task(s) and whether ordinary ground-supported access remains credible, constrained-but-manageable or not credibly available. Do not invent a universal maintenance-corridor minimum.

### T10 planning-context replay

Once the measured return is controlled, replay the T10 planning-context judgement. The measurement does not automatically produce planning acceptability. It supplies the missing factual side/context hinge.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not procure by default:

- a whole-site topographic survey;
- a determined legal boundary or title opinion;
- neighbour measured-building surveys;
- drainage / utility tracing;
- tree survey;
- ground investigation;
- Party Wall advice;
- planning consultant judgement;
- redesign, alternative siting or route search.

If field evidence exposes a genuine discrepancy that makes one of those disciplines decision-critical, accelerate only that precise issue afterwards.
