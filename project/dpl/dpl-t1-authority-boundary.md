`CONTROLLED_SCREENING_GEOMETRY_DOES_NOT_DISPROVE_PLACEMENT` means only that the exact controlled HMLR index polygon used at SCREENING does not eliminate the unchanged Reference House on geometric containment grounds.

It does not mean:

- the legal title boundary is proved;
- planning permission for Ovid's House is likely or obtained;
- setbacks, amenity, trees, drainage, rights, utilities or access are satisfied;
- full R1–R6 admission has passed;
- an authored deployment candidate exists;
- the site is `COMPATIBLE`.

The constructive rectangle is a witness that the geometry-first gate survives. It is not architecture authored by the compiler.
