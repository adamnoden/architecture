## Decision

Missing measurements need not prevent an explicit design hypothesis. They do prevent that hypothesis from becoming a verified site fact. The previous proposed DPL-15J blanket pause is superseded for exploration only.

`trials.json` owns the assumptions. `results.json` is derived by `scripts/dpl-exploration.ts`. The existing DPL-15C/F/H/I readiness records and Lennard control records retain their own evidence authority and are not rewritten.

There are two distinct questions:

| Question | Result authority |

This is an additional research mode, not a new regulatory assurance profile. Neither a conditional success nor a conditional failure becomes whole-site COMPATIBLE/INCOMPATIBLE.

## Assumption register

Each site has three explicit width, depth, slope and access-width probes. Every probe family carries its basis and the evidence that could replace it. No probability, confidence interval, dimensional accuracy or representativeness is assigned.

- RP-001, RP-006 and RP-008 retain their reported area magnitudes as third-party anchors. Rectangular aspect ratios are uncalibrated hypotheses. Their width/depth cross-products deliberately include different areas; those combinations are stress probes, not reconstructions consistent with an exact area constraint.
- RP-002/003/005/007 have no usable numerical parcel dimensions in the retained record. Their broad rectangular alternatives are typological experiments, not educated estimates of those actual boundaries.
- RP-004 uses the openly synthetic 17.4 × 30 m Reference Site as its nominal street-front hypothesis, with 12/22 m width and 18/40 m depth alternatives. No claim is made that Nelmes Way measures 17.4 × 30 m.
- RP-005 remains a corner-subparcel hypothesis while host/subparcel identity is unresolved. Its runtime ledger is a conditional obligation inventory, not admission of a real deployment case.
- RP-007's steep context motivates 5/10/15 degree probes, in both longitudinal slope directions. Those are not recovered site gradients.

All candidates retain one authored placement: front-centre X = 0, front Y = 5.2 m, no rotation relative to the assumed rectangular parcel, FFL 0.3 m above assumed front ground. The 5.2 m setback comes from the synthetic Reference Site; the 0.3 m raise is an exploratory design choice also used in Lennard's authored datum. They are not site measurements. The placement stays fixed as the plot changes; there is no search, optimisation or automatic recentring. Geographic north is unknown for these hypotheses; daylight, solar and townscape orientation are not evaluated.

The four project test choices are 1 m side working space, 3 m rear working space, a 1:20 illustrative approach gradient, and 2.5 m illustrative logistics width. These are neither statutory limits nor claims of adequate maintenance, access, fire or construction provision. An access-width check omits turning, route length, overhead constraints, vehicle characteristics and rights.

## Conditional envelope

For the fixed, unrotated 12 × 10.8 m Reference House placement:

- bare rectangular containment needs width at least 12 m and depth at least 16 m, because the front is fixed 5.2 m into the plot;
- adding the chosen 1 m side / 3 m rear working-space probes needs width at least 14 m and depth at least 19 m;
- these are exact conditional rectangular thresholds, not a minimum acceptable London plot specification;
- the 0.3 m entrance rise needs 6 m of sloping approach at the chosen 1:20 test; allowing the tested ±0.3 m front-level errors raises the required sloping length to 12 m. Landings, turns, crossfall, entrance geometry and usable route width are additional design work;
- a hypothetical 15-degree longitudinal slope changes ground level by about 2.89 m over the 10.8 m house depth. Both slope directions demand an explicit support, grading and water response; unchanged internal floors do not resolve those interfaces;
- the minimum bare footprint area is 129.6 m². If RP-008's reported 97 m² were the complete available extent, it would be too small regardless of shape or rotation. The actual extent would need to be over 33% larger merely to remove that area obstruction, before any external territory;
- Lennard's fixed placement has about 0.64 m minimum clearance to its indicative index polygon. A hypothetical uniform inward boundary shift of 0.5 m leaves about 0.14 m; 0.75 m loses containment. Those offsets are sensitivity probes, not statements of HMLR accuracy or legal boundary position.

## Provisional ordinary street-front interface brief

For RP-004's **assumed** 17.4 × 30 m rectangle, the fixed placement leaves 2.7 m each side, 5.2 m front and 14 m rear. This is a useful provisional layout, not a measured Nelmes Way plan.

| Interface | Conditional design response | Assumptions and falsification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Support / ground | Preserve the house's internal support order; design the foundation/site connection separately | Suitable ground and a competent structural solution are hypotheses. Weak bearing, trees, groundwater or ground movement can change the foundation concept and cost; no capacity is calculated here |
| Datum / approach | Retain the internal section; use the front approach zone for the level transition. Reserve at least 6 m sloping route for the nominal rise and test 12 m for the level-error case | This is a length requirement, not proof that a complete accessible route fits. A detailed route must account for landings, width, turning and the actual entrance |
| Site water | Keep roof/site-water routes inspectable and separate from protected fabric; reserve external storage/connection territory | Outfall, groundwater, infiltration and flood conditions remain hypotheses. No assumed sewer capacity or discharge permission enters the ledger |
| Utilities | Bring services through the replaceable site-interface routes, with isolation/access before entry to the house | Road connection points and capacity are assumed possible for the concept only; rights, apparatus and actual routes remain unknown |
| Maintenance / construction | Test the 2.7 m nominal side strips as potential working territory and keep rear access connected | This is not proof that equipment, scaffolding, deliveries or boundary works fit. The 1 m probe is only a sensitivity threshold |
| Context / environment | Keep the canonical front legible to the street and the rear territory usable | North, neighbour windows, trees, noise and policy judgement are unresolved. No approval likelihood or environmental adequacy is asserted |

This brief lets architectural coordination continue without waiting for every external answer. Where a premise is false, the corresponding site interface must be reconsidered; the house is not silently resized or split-levelled.

## Evidence firewall and validation

Assumed site geometry never enters the existing verified obligation ledger. That ledger receives the unchanged house's authored facts and known two-storey proposal only; no assumed structural opinion, flood clearance, rights, boundary or planning finding is submitted as evidence. Existing local target plug-ins are used only for Bromley/Croydon. The other five boroughs explicitly retain unsupported local coverage.

Automated checks reproduce the retained result and test:

1. rejection of false authority, missing provenance, non-finite inputs, invalid ranges and corpus substitution;
2. exact threshold containment, off-centre placement and rotated rectangles;
3. preservation of unknown professional obligations, open conformance clauses and 0/8 evidence-backed readiness;
4. changed assumptions changing the exploratory fingerprint while leaving the evidence-led runtime fingerprint unchanged;
5. small-area failure, footpath-width consequences, steep-slope effects and Lennard boundary sensitivity.

The nine rectangle samples support claims only about those samples. The closed-form thresholds above hold for the specifically stated fixed unrotated rectangular model. Neither establishes continuous robustness for irregular real parcels, correlated uncertainty, terrain surfaces, construction tolerances or all possible design routes. Joint checks exclude slope adequacy: slope calculations expose a design dependency rather than inventing an acceptable engineering limit.

## Genuine remaining boundary

Further uncalibrated rectangles would repeat a sensitivity experiment. They would not make an exact real parcel less uncertain. For the next real-site placement, select one corpus parcel and replace the assumptions with (1) a controlled extent tied to that exact parcel and (2) metric boundary/orientation/levels/access information adequate for the question. A coherent screening package suffices; determined legal title, GI, full utility investigations and a full technical-design survey are not blanket prerequisites to exploratory candidate authoring.

Professional structural, water, rights and planning questions remain stage-specific dependencies for stronger deployment claims. No external contact or spend has been made. Those routes are deferred verification tasks under the revised brief, rather than active blockers to the conditional programme closeout.
