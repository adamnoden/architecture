## What was actually exhausted

Six bounded recovery attempts were made.

### Planning Data / HMLR title-index geometry

The official title-boundary dataset and coordinate-query route are established, and RP-006 already has an exact real BNG/WGS84 point from DPL-15E. The custom spatial request could not be returned/retained through the current tool environment, so no exact intersecting title entity or polygon bytes were acquired.

The route therefore remains **known but unacquired**. It cannot satisfy R1 or R2.

Any future HMLR index polygon remains indicative registered-property extent rather than a determined legal boundary.

### Exact planning drawing

The exact-site technical report proves that Enclosure Architects `(P)02 Rev A — Site Plan & Landscape` exists at `1:100 @ A3`; the public council index separately records current location/site-arrangement drawings.

The current environment can recover indexed text but not the inspectable original/vector drawing geometry needed to reconstruct the application extent. Screenshot/raster-pixel measurement is deliberately not used.

The applicant house is approximately 9.4 m × 5.25 m in the indexed plan text. Those dimensions belong to the applicant proposal and are **not transferred into the 12.0 m × 10.8 m Reference House placement question**.

### Environment Agency terrain

The official Environment Agency LiDAR Composite DTM 1 m WCS route, coverage identity, EPSG:27700 frame and ODN semantics are established. A concrete GetCoverage subset around BNG `544654,179561` was attempted, but the exact raster bytes could not be retained through the current web/container transport.

Service discovery is not a site datum.

### Approximate site level

The exact-site contaminated-land report contains a useful but weak numeric clue:

```text
approximate site elevation ≈ 5.0 mAOD
```

The same report explicitly says this general level is **according to Google Earth**, and states that no site walkover was undertaken; its photographs were obtained from Google Maps.

DPL-15F therefore refuses to convert the `~5.0 mAOD` number into R4 satisfaction. It narrows the expected datum but does not meet the existing controlled level/topography threshold.

### Physical access

The exact-site Design & Access Statement materially improves the approach picture:

- level front-door access is proposed via a gently sloping `1:100` garden path;
- an eight-space parking area is described immediately adjacent at the Camelot Close turning head;
- the site is stated not to carry a public right of way.

But these proposal/context facts are still not bound to controlled placement geometry, and the auction evidence expressly warns that some or all of the marketed plot may comprise adopted highway.

R5 therefore remains unresolved. Physical access context is not private-right proof and is not silently promoted into controlled street/access geometry.

### Auction legal pack

The October 2026 auction lot publicly confirms that a legal pack exists. The pack bytes were not recovered through the current public search path. The auction particulars themselves explicitly say the depicted site area/measurements are indicative and warn of possible adopted-highway overlap.

Marketing/legal-pack route discovery therefore cannot be used as placement geometry.
