# DPL — owner-house plot and site successor

**Tracking:** [#1009](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/1009). Follows the [owner-house charter](../owner-house-charter.md) and [DEL route](../del/owner-house-delivery-plan.md).

The [earlier deployment programme](deployment-programme.md) and #299 remain complete at their conditional research scope. Their older geometry, assumptions, test plots and target coverage do not establish actual suitability for the current house. RSI stays closed. No property search, site purchase, external outreach or spend is authorised here.

## DPL-SITE-BRIEF — evidence and plot requirements

After DEL-BRIEF and HRS-BASELINE, produce `project/dpl/owner-site-brief.md`. Derive the plot questions from the accepted house and owner priorities: footprint/context, orientation, external working space, access, garden relationships, levels, drainage/utilities, ground and applicable planning/design constraints. Separate chosen preferences, derived geometric needs, assumptions, professionally assessed constraints and unknowns. Do not promote a convenient assumed setback into a legal minimum.

State what can be screened from available evidence and what needs survey, professional work or owner decisions before a stronger claim. Define adaptation choices the owner may later consider without silently reopening the accepted 12 × 12 m baseline. Exit: a useful shortlist/assessment brief and evidence request for a future real plot, not a fabricated compatible London site or a renewed corpus search.

## DPL-COMMISSION — manual activation

This gate requires a real owner-nominated plot, explicit study scope, available evidence references and a record of missing information/responsible parties. Record `project/dpl/commission-activation.md`. It does not require purchase or claim that all due diligence is complete. An agent cannot choose a hypothetical plot and satisfy the gate itself.

Protect private evidence appropriately. Establish the project's identity, evidence authority and permission to proceed with the defined assessment. No automatic appointment, spending or disclosure follows. Earlier contact with advisers is allowed only when separately authorised; this gate does not postpone it until software completion.

## DPL-SITE-ASSESSMENT — evidence-linked adaptation brief

After activation, produce `project/dpl/site-assessment-result.md` and its controlled project/site input records. Evaluate the current house against the actual available parcel, orientation, levels, access and contextual evidence. Map applicable target requirements and unsupported questions at the assessment's declared scope. Use tools only where they support the required geometry/checks; compiler completion is not a prerequisite for competent site appraisal.

Distinguish verified facts, assumptions and unavailable evidence. State retain/adapt/reject options and the concrete architectural consequences. Do not automatically resize/reposition selected geometry or declare planning acceptance. Route proposed design changes to the owning design streams, with owner/professional decisions explicit. Unknowns can remain in an assessment result, but suitability cannot be claimed through them.

Exit: a traceable site-specific assessment/adaptation brief with no unsupported suitability claim, all material next decisions and evidence responsibilities, and inputs for DEL-COMMISSION-PLAN. Real planning/design adoption, detailed engineering, formal applications, purchase and construction remain separately controlled activities.
