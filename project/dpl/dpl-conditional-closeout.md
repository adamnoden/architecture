## What is assumed

The JSON register states eight unverified premises with their replacement evidence: spatial extent; control/rights; support/ground; water/environment; drainage/utilities; context/planning; access/logistics; and remaining technical completion.

For this study, each is assumed to admit a viable solution without mutation of the protected house. The conditional case is therefore not an engineering proof produced by assigning favourable inputs. Only the specifically checked geometry is derived. Adequacy of the other disciplines remains stipulated, and every relevant evidence-backed runtime obligation stays unresolved rather than receiving an invented PASS.

The register makes no claim that these premises are probable at Nelmes Way, shared by all eight plots, or jointly true at any actual site. A contrary measurement or professional finding invalidates the relevant premise and reopens that part of the design. In particular, the small-area RP-008 obstruction is retained; assuming good ground or approval cannot make a 97 m² extent contain a 129.6 m² gross footprint.
