# HFI-10 — Adversarial Closeout

**Packet:** HFI-10  
**Programme:** `docs/development/human-factors-low-friction-inhabitation-programme.md`  
**Execution plan:** `docs/development/human-factors-low-friction-inhabitation-execution-plan.md`  
**Owning issue:** #195  
**Baseline `main`:** `c81e54bdfa5b0fc41cd13d1fd6f1f93d53df58ba`  
**Prior gate:** HFI-09D complete; 40/41 packets integrated

## Question

Does the integrated HFI result survive a final programme-wide adversarial attack for spatial bloat, median-body bias, false universality, standards worship, ergonomic pseudo-precision, institutional character, hidden domestic-labour assumptions, multidisciplinary conflict, cost/carbon disproportion, Reference House overfit, doctrine leakage, stale publication/drawing residue, compiler-scope inflation and novelty seeking; and does every surviving proposition have an explicit evidence basis, owner, maturity/proof boundary and downstream disposition?

## Verdict

**PASS — HFI may close.**

The programme materially changed Ovid’s House, but in a narrower and more defensible way than its opening hypotheses might have suggested:

- foreseeable human variance now enters Principle 1 before design targets are frozen;
- repeated bodily/cognitive compensation and complete-action review now sharpen Principle 8;
- the Reference House principal stair changed from 280 mm to 300 mm going after an explicit full-system proportionality comparison;
- N17 carries complete-action / finished-interface verification into delivery;
- RH-11 now has a bounded human-factors / inclusive-design specialist-review handoff;
- no new numbered principle, named strategy, canonical pattern, universal accessibility geometry, compiler proof family or standing prototype programme was created.

The strongest evidence that HFI did not overrun its authority is what it refused to do. It did not turn accessibility guidance into doctrine, did not make 300 mm a statutory or universal minimum, did not convert wheelchair or assistance geometry into whole-house defaults, did not solve laundry by decree, did not reserve a lift without a bounded case, did not fabricate missing finished-interface facts, and did not create RH-C6 merely because the research programme was large.

Programme completion does **not** mean every human-factors question in a future real house is answered. It means the research, architectural integration, Reference House correction, delivery handoff, external-review boundary, publication reconciliation, computational admission and regression-control questions owned by HFI are now closed or explicitly handed off.

# 1. Attack — spatial bloat

## Failure mode

A human-factors programme can become a one-way ratchet: wider corridors, larger bathrooms, larger kitchens, larger landings, continuous two-person clearance and specialist turning space everywhere.

## Result

**Survives.**

HFI repeatedly rejected blanket enlargement and instead retained local causal correction:

- role/manoeuvre-specific clearance rather than one generous corridor number;
- conflict-node space rather than continuous passing width;
- actual assistance geometry rather than generic carer space;
- task-specific staging rather than permanent landing shelves everywhere;
- category-specific M4 geometry rather than universal M4(3) planning;
- complete swept-path/open-state testing before adding floor area.

The one accepted architectural enlargement is the principal stair going, 280 → 300 mm. Its burden was not hidden: five goings per flight add 100 mm run per flight. HFI-08B selected it only after proving that the existing stair reservation could absorb the change without enlarging the shell, reducing the courtyard, displacing the service hub, changing the room programme or introducing winders.

**Closeout lock:** local HFI improvements are not additive entitlements to area. A future design must still identify the actual causal bottleneck and compare lower-burden corrections first.

---

# 2. Attack — median-body / capability bias

## Failure mode

“Ordinary occupant” could silently mean an able-bodied median adult, with everyone else treated as an exception.

## Result

**Survives.**

HFI-01C froze scenarios before target derivation and explicitly separated larger/smaller bodies, older occupants without bundled impairment assumptions, children, wheelchair users, mobility-aid users, assisted dyads, pregnancy, temporary limb impairment, occupied hands, reduced dexterity/balance, fatigue, low light, visual/processing variation and unfamiliar use.

The matrix also forbids combining those attributes into a fictional “vulnerable user”. Wheelchair use does not imply weak upper-body function; age does not imply disability; pregnancy is not pathology; assistance is not permanent dependence.

The opposite failure is also controlled. Human variance does not require every room or interface to satisfy every specialist scenario simultaneously. Category-specific claims remain category-specific.

**Closeout lock:** no single percentile, body, device or diagnostic proxy may stand in for the scenario/task actually being tested.

---

# 3. Attack — false universality and standards worship

## Failure mode

A project could launder external guidance or specialist-access dimensions into universal Ovid’s House truths.

## Result

**Survives strongly.**

The target architecture maintains distinct authority classes:

- law / statutory guidance;
- standards / professional guidance;
- empirical evidence;
- Ovid’s House target or preference;
- project/site/product-specific fact.

Approved Document K remains a bounded external numeric-method correspondence in RH-C5. M4(2)/M4(3) geometry remains category-specific context. Government force observations remain evidence, not universal limits. The selected 300 mm stair going remains an Ovid’s House / Reference House preference, not a rewritten external rule.

HFI-08A promoted mechanisms into Principles 1 and 8 while keeping numerical targets below doctrine.

**Closeout lock:** passing one authority layer never silently proves another.

---

# 4. Attack — ergonomic pseudo-precision

## Failure mode

A large evidence programme can create false confidence through exact millimetres, universal force values or composite “low-friction” scores.

## Result

**Survives.**

HFI rejected:

- a weighted friction score;
- one 95th-percentile person;
- universal corridor/turning/control-height numbers;
- direct laboratory force observations as project limits;
- route distance as a proxy for domestic-task burden;
- bathroom or kitchen size as a proxy for task usability.

The 300 mm stair preference was explicitly red-teamed as the highest-risk scalar. It remains a rounded preferred design centre, not a 299-fails/300-passes cliff. Nearby geometry is not declared defective solely by arithmetic.

Most stronger results remain relational: approach + opening + turn; control + operating position + sequence + feedback; threshold + drainage/weathering + operation; appliance open state + user position; task chain + set-down/regrip events.

**Closeout lock:** preserve the form of the evidence. A relational mechanism must not be collapsed into a magic number merely because software prefers scalars.

---

# 5. Attack — clinical / institutional character

## Failure mode

Inclusive design can become visibly medicalised or convert the home into permanent specialist equipment for hypothetical future users.

## Result

**Survives.**

HFI deliberately separates durable enabling architecture from demand-led equipment:

- support-capable sanitary zones may be enabled early; actual rails/seats remain user-specific unless presently required;
- a credible future level-access shower may be enabled without making the room a permanent specialist bathroom;
- entrance-level fallback living is dual-use ordinary accommodation, not a dedicated institutional room;
- stairlift architecture may preserve future possibility without installing machinery;
- motorisation/adjustable kitchens/lifts are not universal defaults.

No new reader-facing “accessibility aesthetic” or HFI taxonomy was introduced. HFI conclusions entered the constitution as ordinary architecture: time, repose, useful geometry and proportional enabling capacity.

**Closeout lock:** specialist appearance, mechanism and maintenance burden require a present brief or independently earned future case; neutral enabling capacity is preferred where proportionate.

# 6. Attack — equivalent / dignified use

## Failure mode

A technically passable solution could create a conspicuously inferior second route or segregated mode of participation.

## Result

**Survives at the correct scope.**

HFI-01C includes `E — equivalent/dignified use` as a distinct scenario class and tests, among other cases, whether a wheelchair user can participate through the same principal arrival/social system and reach outdoor destinations without a conspicuously inferior route.

HFI does not turn “equivalent” into a universal same-geometry rule where site, safety or technical conditions differ. The test remains architectural: unnecessary segregation must be justified rather than normalised.

**Downstream route:** real-site and finished-route equivalence remains a project/RH-11/N17 question where current virtual geometry is insufficient.

---

# 7. Attack — hidden housework, childcare and care assumptions

## Failure mode

The house could be optimised around an unstated household role or treat domestic labour as invisible.

## Result

**Survives.**

The frozen scenario architecture treats groceries, laundry, waste/recycling, cleaning, garden-serving, childcare/care and object movement as tasks rather than assigning them to a gender or archetype. Assistance is represented as a two-person interaction rather than a permanent dependent/carer identity.

The laundry finding is particularly important because HFI did not convert a genuine repeated cross-floor burden into an upstairs-laundry ideology. Moisture, leak containment, noise/vibration, services, replacement, structure and garden/dirty-work relationships remain part of the decision.

**Closeout lock:** do not cherry-pick the easiest domestic route to represent “household usability”; audit the tasks material to the actual household/project.

---

# 8. Attack — moisture / fire / structure / services / security / privacy conflict

## Failure mode

“Human factors” could become an excuse to weaken hard technical duties.

## Result

**Survives.**

HFI-05C establishes the controlling rule:

> Preserve the human need; preserve the hard technical duty; change the means until both can coexist, or qualify/reject the proposition explicitly.

Examples retained through later packets:

- level/traversable thresholds do not override drainage, weathering or doorset robustness;
- low-force openings do not override fire closure, security, acoustics or weather seals;
- future shower capability must have technically real drainage/moisture/service support;
- sanitary support zones must coordinate with concealed services;
- stair preferences remain subject to complete stair, structure, headroom and fire resolution;
- lift and laundry questions remain unresolved where cross-system consequences are not yet bounded.

N17 carries this into delivery rather than pretending the virtual Reference House has proved finished assemblies.

**Closeout lock:** “HFI required it” is never a valid reason to violate an applicable technical duty.

---

# 9. Attack — cost, carbon and complexity disproportion

## Failure mode

Small ergonomic benefits could justify permanent area, materials, mechanisms and future maintenance burden without proportionality.

## Result

**Survives.**

HFI-01D/05B require frequency, consequence, evidence confidence and retrofit asymmetry to be weighed against area, initial/whole-life cost, embodied/operational carbon, technical complexity, maintenance, workmanship and displaced friction. There is no weighted master score that can average away a hard conflict.

The programme repeatedly prefers:

- local geometry over whole-house enlargement;
- passive enabling capacity over installed specialist equipment;
- manual/intelligible operation over unnecessary automation;
- established evidence/detailing over prototype theatre;
- demand-led equipment over speculative product lock-in.

**Residual reality:** project-specific cost/carbon quantities are not established by HFI and must not be implied. HFI proves a proportionality method and bounded architectural decisions, not a whole-life carbon calculation.

# 10. Attack — Reference House overfit / incumbency

## Failure mode

Targets could be selected to protect the current house, or Reference House convenience could become general doctrine.

## Result

**Survives.**

The programme selected the 300 mm stair preference before auditing the house, then allowed that target to reopen RH-03 when the current 280 mm stair returned `IMPROVE`. Existing drawing/publication maturity did not protect the geometry.

Conversely, the Reference House did not create universal doctrine from its particular layout. HFI-08A explicitly prevents promotion of:

- the three-flight stair arrangement;
- 300 mm as a universal rule;
- the current courtyard/gallery topology;
- the current laundry topology;
- nominal ≈1000 mm stair width;
- current room sizes.

The programme changed the house once where a bounded comparison earned change and declined to manufacture further rewinds from `NOT-MODELLED` facts.

# 11. Attack — implementation detail promoted into doctrine

## Failure mode

A 41-packet programme could leave behind too much taxonomy and turn workflow artefacts into architecture.

## Result

**Survives strongly.**

HFI-05D found only two principle-level propositions and HFI-08A integrated both into existing Principles 1 and 8. HFI created:

- no Principle 12;
- no new named strategy;
- no new canonical pattern;
- no universal HFI dimension schedule.

HFI-S1 remains a review method; HFI-S2 remains an application of Principle 7/proportionality; HFI-S3 remains an interpretation of Principle 1. `HFI-*` remains workflow provenance rather than reader-facing identity.

This is the correct compression outcome.

# 12. Attack — stale publication / drawing residue

## Failure mode

Accepted HFI changes could exist in research while the book or drawings still show superseded architecture.

## Result

**Survives at the audited baseline.**

HFI-09A found no accepted-but-unpropagated publication change. Principles 1/8, N17, the review summary and the 300 mm stair are current in their controlled surfaces.

HFI-09B found no HFI-specific stale DRW plate. The stair change was made at source, regenerated through deterministic successors and physically re-proved; unresolved lift/laundry/interface questions were not cosmetically drawn as if solved.

Open research questions are not freeze breaks until they become accepted architectural changes.

**Closeout lock:** any future accepted correction before physical freeze reopens its source owner and affected publication/drawing dependencies; drawings are never hand-patched around stale architecture.

---

# 13. Attack — compiler proof-boundary inflation

## Failure mode

The programme could mistake machine representation for human-use proof, or create a new proof family to make qualitative conclusions look stronger.

## Result

**Survives strongly.**

HFI-09C admits only facts actually controlled by the semantic model:

- selected stair source geometry;
- deterministic derived arithmetic;
- bounded RH-C5 external-guidance numeric correspondence;
- source/output propagation.

It explicitly refuses machine proof for complete-action adequacy, finished stair usability, stairlift compatibility, through-floor lift reservation, laundry topology, real-site access and general HFI principles.

HFI-09D then correctly adds **no HFI-specific regression code**. Existing independent controls already protect the accepted 300 mm stair/source-output state. A constant such as `HFI_MIN_GOING = 300` would wrongly convert a project preference into a universal invariant.

**No RH-C6 is earned.**

---

# 14. Attack — novelty seeking

## Failure mode

A large research programme may feel compelled to invent proprietary answers even when established practice is sufficient.

## Result

**Survives.**

Several HFI outcomes are intentionally negative or conservative:

- no stronger universal headroom target;
- no universal turning circle;
- no universal corridor width;
- no universal control height or operating-force scalar;
- no general prototype programme;
- no new HFI compiler family;
- no new canonical pattern;
- no automatic lift/laundry solution.

The principal new scalar, 300 mm stair going, is not exotic; it lies within the evidence-supported/external-guidance landscape and was selected only because the Reference House could absorb its bounded benefit proportionately.

HFI therefore earns distinctiveness through disciplined synthesis and application, not novelty for its own sake.

# 16. What HFI does not claim

Programme completion does **not** establish that:

- Ovid’s House is universally accessible;
- the Reference House is M4(2), M4(3) or wheelchair-usable throughout;
- every future occupant/state is accommodated simultaneously;
- 300 mm is a statutory, ergonomic or universal minimum stair going;
- RH-C5 proves stair comfort, accessibility, fire safety, headroom, guarding, handrails or whole-stair adequacy;
- nominal ≈1000 mm stair width proves stairlift compatibility;
- a future through-floor lift route is required, unnecessary or already reserved;
- upstairs laundry is preferable, or the current ground utility is finally vindicated;
- real-site entrance/access conditions are proved by the site-agnostic Reference House or a synthetic setting drawing;
- currently unmodelled finished door, threshold, sanitary, control, kitchen or object-route conditions pass;
- N17 is a compiler proof;
- a standing human-factors prototype programme exists;
- an independent human-factors / inclusive-design review has occurred;
- inclusive-design certification or accessibility compliance has been externally established;
- HFI created a new architectural maturity level merely because the research was extensive.

These non-claims are part of the result, not caveats to be forgotten after closeout.

# 17. Programme completion condition

HFI is complete because the programme now has:

1. a frozen authority/evidence/scenario architecture;
2. domain research across anthropometry, stairs, routes, thresholds, domestic tasks, controls and fixed interfaces;
3. causality, proportionality and multidisciplinary conflict synthesis;
4. the smallest defensible principle integration rather than a new doctrine branch;
5. target architecture with explicit authority classes and red-team;
6. a Reference House audit with one bounded rewind, now resolved at RH-03;
7. delivery and external-review handoffs for facts that legitimately belong later;
8. publication and DRW reconciliation with no accepted stale HFI delta;
9. a bounded computational admission with no proof-scope inflation;
10. regression-control review that adds no duplicate/false HFI invariant;
11. explicit ownership and disposition for every material unresolved/not-modelled question.

No further HFI packet is justified merely to make the programme look more complete.

Future evidence may reopen **the owning architectural, delivery, review, site or computational authority**. It should not resurrect HFI as a permanent parallel authority layer unless a genuinely new cross-cutting research question first earns a new programme.
