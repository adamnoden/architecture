**Question:** which HFI conclusions are genuinely general architectural knowledge, and what is the narrowest existing upstream authority that should own them?  
**Boundary:** upstream integration only. This packet does not execute `HFI-RW-01`, change Reference House geometry, relocate laundry, promote HFI numerical targets into doctrine, create a pattern, add compiler proof or change maturity.

---

## 1. Result

HFI has produced real upstream architectural knowledge, but **not a new constitutional branch**.

Two conclusions earn canonical public integration:

1. **Foreseeable human variance is a normal design input, not a late accessibility check.**  
   Narrowest owner: **Principle 1 — Build for time**.

2. **Repeated bodily or cognitive compensation imposed by ordinary use is one form of unnecessary vigilance. Complete actions matter more than isolated dimensions.**  
   Narrowest owner: **Principle 8 — Design for repose**.

The existing constitution already contains the parent logic for the remaining HFI strategy candidates. After applying the architectural-specificity and duplication tests, HFI-08A therefore creates:

- **no Principle 12**;
- **no new canonical pattern**;
- **no new named strategy**;
- **no general 300 mm stair rule**;
- **no universal level-access / wheelchair / lift / laundry doctrine**.

This is deliberate compression, not loss. The HFI programme has sharpened the meaning and testability of existing architecture rather than proving that the project needs more categories.

---

## 2. Promotion test

A HFI proposition moves upstream only if it:

1. survives materially different domestic situations;
2. states a durable reason rather than a Reference House remedy;
3. remains meaningful across architectural grammars/styles;
4. survives the HFI proportionality and technical-conflict gates;
5. does not duplicate an existing constitutional or strategy provision;
6. does not require an HFI numerical target to sound true;
7. remains useful if the Reference House is replaced by a different project;
8. does not convert one scenario category into a universal claim.

Reference-House evidence may expose the importance of a proposition, but **the Reference House cannot prove its generality by itself**.

## 3. HFI-P2 → Principle 1

### Accepted general conclusion

HFI-05D hardened the original statement “human variance is a normal design input” into a bounded proposition:

- do not design around a fictional average person;
- consider representative bodies, capabilities and ordinary temporary states before freezing geometry;
- treat ageing, temporary injury, occupied hands, care and similar foreseeable states as normal design inputs where relevant;
- do not infer that every space must satisfy every specialist scenario;
- early provision remains subject to the existing proportionality test.

### Why Principle 1 owns it

Principle 1 already treats the building as something that must survive change in occupants as well as systems. HFI adds one missing precision: **human variation should enter before design targets are fixed, not merely appear later as a compliance/accessibility check**.

The accepted wording therefore strengthens the existing life-course paragraph rather than creating a separate accessibility principle.

### Canonical change

`docs/manuscript/governing-principles.md` is hardened to say, in substance:

> Treat foreseeable variation in bodies, capability and ordinary temporary states as normal design input rather than a late accessibility check; do not design around a fictional average person.

It also makes the existing proportionality split more explicit: durable enabling capacity may be worth providing early; specialist equipment remains demand-led unless the brief requires it.

### Rejected upstream promotions

This does **not** establish:

- universal M4(2) or M4(3) geometry;
- wheelchair usability everywhere;
- a permanent through-floor-lift reservation;
- installed stairlift/lift equipment;
- one percentile person;
- one universal reach or force envelope.

## 4. HFI-P1 → Principle 8

### Accepted general conclusion

HFI-05D established that ordinary repeated use can impose a form of vigilance through bodily/cognitive compensation:

- careful foot placement;
- unnecessary turns/regrips;
- awkward approach or reach;
- avoidable contortion;
- unnecessary force;
- having to enter a wet/hot/noisy condition before controlling it;
- repeatedly remembering a building-specific workaround.

The durable principle is not “remove every inconvenience”. It is:

> where ordinary repeated use demands material compensation, test the building-side cause before normalising the workaround.

### Why Principle 8 owns it

Principle 8 already owns unnecessary vigilance, physical comfort, comprehensible space and intelligible local control. HFI makes “vigilance” less visually/atmospherically narrow by showing that **embodied operational compensation** belongs to the same occupation criterion.

### Canonical change

Both:

- `docs/manuscript/governing-principles.md`; and
- `docs/manuscript/principle-08-repose.md`

are hardened so that ordinary routes, controls and interfaces are judged as **complete actions in their occupied states**, not as isolated dimensions.

The qualification remains explicit: trivial, rare or technically necessary inconvenience does not automatically justify permanent area, mechanism or complexity.

## 5. HFI-S1 — sequence, not isolated dimension

**05D classification:** `STRATEGY-CANDIDATE`  
**08A disposition:** **METHOD / REVIEW RULE — INTEGRATED UNDER PRINCIPLE 8; NO NEW NAMED STRATEGY**

HFI-S1 says:

> Judge interfaces and routes as complete sequences under the states in which they are actually used.

This survived the later audit strongly. HFI-07 repeatedly showed why a room label, nominal width or adjacency cannot prove the complete task.

However, after re-reading the pattern-system strategy definition, HFI-S1 is more procedural than architectural. It tells the designer **how to inspect a use condition**; it does not itself describe a broad architectural approach realised through several physical pattern families.

Creating a canonical strategy merely to give this method a name would add taxonomy without adding architectural ownership.

**Route:** Principle 8 public explanation + HFI evaluation method.

---

## 6. HFI-S2 — spend generosity at causal bottlenecks

**05D classification:** `STRATEGY-CANDIDATE`  
**08A disposition:** **DERIVED APPLICATION OF PRINCIPLE 7 + GOVERNING PROPORTIONALITY; NO NEW NAMED STRATEGY**

The rule remains valid:

> Use permanent space or geometry where a demonstrated manoeuvre, sequence or future adaptation need earns it; prefer local causal correction over blanket enlargement.

But the constitution already says:

- useful dimensions and generous circulation can support many lives;
- permanent architectural relationships must earn the future constraints they create;
- serviceability is pursued proportionately.

HFI-06D and HFI-07 strengthened this by repeatedly rejecting area inflation and false universal dimensions. They did not expose a missing upstream category.

**Route:** retain as the HFI human-factors application of Principle 7/proportionality. No separate canonical strategy is added.

---

## 7. HFI-S3 — durable capability early; specialist equipment demand-led

**05D classification:** `STRATEGY-CANDIDATE`  
**08A disposition:** **INTEGRATED INTERPRETATION OF PRINCIPLE 1; NO NEW NAMED STRATEGY**

The distinction is important:

- durable, low-burden enabling capacity can deserve early provision when retrofit would be destructive;
- proprietary, user-specific or maintained equipment should normally remain demand-led unless a present brief requires it.

But Principle 1 already owns exactly the parent problem: cheap passive provision for foreseeable change versus expensive provision for speculative futures.

The HFI wording therefore sharpens Principle 1 rather than duplicating it one layer below.

Examples remain examples, not doctrine: support-capable sanitary zones, future level-access shower capability and genuine stairlift-compatible architecture may be worthwhile when earned; installed specialist equipment and universal lift reservations are not implied.

---

## 8. Numerical targets stay below doctrine

HFI-06 established a layered target architecture. HFI-08A preserves it.

The following do **not** move into the governing constitution:

- preferred 300 mm principal-stair going;
- route/corridor target ranges;
- door clear-opening targets;
- turning/manoeuvre envelopes;
- threshold/gradient values;
- reach/operating-force values;
- category-specific sanitary or kitchen clearances.

These values are useful only with their authority, scenario, evidence strength and target class attached. A number stripped of that context would be weaker upstream knowledge than the mechanism that produced it.

In particular, the 300 mm stair preference remains a project/HFI target used by `HFI-RW-01`; it is not rewritten as a universal Ovid’s House law.

---

## 9. Pattern-language disposition

No HFI finding yet earns a new canonical pattern.

Reasons:

1. the strongest HFI results are principles, evaluation methods and target relationships;
2. many physical responses remain context-dependent alternatives rather than one stable recurring architectural relationship;
3. existing patterns already own some relevant physical interfaces, notably `Designed Threshold`;
4. Reference House geometry cannot be used as general pattern evidence;
5. HFI-07 deliberately left many interface questions `NOT-MODELLED` rather than inventing detail.

Existing patterns may later be strengthened when a concrete HFI-driven physical relationship survives design/detail/prototype work. HFI-08A does not enlarge the pattern catalogue pre-emptively.

---

## 10. Reference House firewall

The following remain downstream and are explicitly forbidden from leaking into general architecture at this gate:

- the current 280 mm Reference House stair going;
- the HFI preferred 300 mm comparison value as a universal rule;
- the three-flight / three-by-six stair arrangement;
- the courtyard/east-gallery circulation topology;
- the current upstairs sleeping/bathing + downstairs utility laundry topology;
- any particular solution to that laundry trade-off;
- current nominal ≈1000 mm stair width;
- any claim of stairlift compatibility not supported by finished geometry;
- any current Reference House room size treated as an ergonomic norm.

HFI-RW-01 remains owned by RH-03 and begins only under HFI-08B+.

---

## 11. Authority / maturity consequences

HFI-08A changes canonical **wording and interpretation** at Principles 1 and 8. It does not alter the numbering or hierarchy of the constitution.

It does not change:

- the canonical pattern count;
- the canonical strategy count;
- Reference House geometry or maturity;
- computational proof scope/maturity;
- publication maturity;
- external-validation status.

The HFI programme supplies additional internal evidence for the already-M4 public architectural position, but this packet claims no maturity promotion. External critical reading remains capable of reopening the wording.
