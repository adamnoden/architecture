**Prior gate:** HFI-05A/B/C complete  
**Question:** after causality, proportionality and cross-discipline conflict review, what is the smallest defensible set of general human-factors principles, and which surviving propositions belong instead at strategy, target, pattern, project or demand-led-equipment level?  
**Boundary:** principle formation/classification only. ---

## 1. Starting formulations

The execution plan requires HFI-05D to harden, qualify, replace or reject two working formulations:

> If an ordinary repeated action requires conscious compensation for the building, first ask whether the building is wrong.

> Human variance is a normal design input.

Both survive in substance, but neither survives unchanged.

The first is too willing to infer defect from annoyance. The second is too easy to misread as a demand that every dimension accommodate every body/state simultaneously.

---

## 2. Constitutional test

A proposition belongs at governing-principle level only if it:

1. survives multiple domains and scenarios;
2. expresses a stable reason rather than one remedy;
3. survives HFI-01D proportionality;
4. survives HFI-05C technical conflict;
5. is not merely a regulation, established dimensional rule or product preference;
6. remains meaningful if the Reference House geometry changes;
7. cannot be represented more honestly as strategy, target, pattern or project-specific implementation;
8. does not duplicate an existing constitutional provision without adding a necessary distinction.

This last test is decisive because the current constitution already contains relevant authority:

- Principle 1: foreseeable occupant change and proportional early provision;
- Principle 7: useful dimensions, circulation and permanence that must earn future constraints;
- Principle 8: repeated occupation, physical comfort, spatial comprehension and intelligible local control;
- governing constraints: life safety/legal/structural/health/environmental duties and proportional serviceability.

HFI therefore has no licence to create a twelfth principle merely because the research programme is large.

# 3. Smallest defensible HFI principle set

The synthesis yields **two principle-level propositions** and **three strategy-level rules**.

Only the first two are candidates for later upstream constitutional integration under HFI-08A.

---

## HFI-P1 — Ordinary use should not require avoidable compensation

### Hardened formulation

> **Design ordinary repeated use so that occupants do not have to compensate unnecessarily for the building. Judge the complete action, route or task in its occupied state; where compensation is material, test the building-side cause before normalising the workaround.**

### Why it survives

It explains findings across:

- stairs and level changes;
- doors/corners/circulation;
- thresholds;
- bathing/control sequence;
- hardware and controls;
- kitchen/open-state work;
- domestic logistics.

It does not prescribe width, stair going, lever handles, automation or any other remedy.

### Necessary qualification

“Compensation” is not automatically a defect.

A design response is warranted only after considering:

- frequency;
- consequence;
- evidence confidence;
- technical necessity;
- permanent burden;
- credible lower-burden alternatives.

Some compensation is trivial, occasional or the proportionate consequence of a stronger requirement elsewhere.

### Constitutional relationship

This is best understood as a **hardening of Principle 8 — Design for repose**, reinforced by Principle 7, rather than an independent twelfth constitutional principle.

It gives “unnecessary vigilance” a concrete embodied-use test: repeated bodily or cognitive compensation can be one form of vigilance.

**05D classification:** `PRINCIPLE-CANDIDATE — INTEGRATE-EXISTING`, not `NEW-NUMBERED-PRINCIPLE`.

---

## HFI-P2 — Human variance is a normal design input, not an exception

### Hardened formulation

> **Treat foreseeable variation in bodies, capability and ordinary temporary states as normal design input. Test representative people and states before deriving targets; do not design for a fictional average person, and do not universalise specialist geometry where the benefit does not justify its permanent burden.**

### Why it survives

The research repeatedly showed that:

- percentile labels are not complete people;
- reach, force and posture vary by approach/task;
- pregnancy, injury, ageing, occupied hands, wet conditions, fatigue and low light expose ordinary building mechanisms;
- wheelchair/assistance scenarios are legitimate but category/task specific;
- a single “accessible” number often hides the mechanism.

### Necessary qualification

Human variance does **not** imply:

- every room must be M4(3)-equivalent;
- every route must support two people side-by-side;
- every shelf/control must suit every body simultaneously;
- every speculative impairment earns permanent mechanism or area.

The design response remains proportional and claim-specific.

### Constitutional relationship

Principle 1 already says foreseeable occupant change should be tested by the same proportionality rule. HFI-P2 strengthens the **input model**: variation is considered before targets are selected, not appended afterward as an accessibility check.

**05D classification:** `PRINCIPLE-CANDIDATE — INTEGRATE-EXISTING`, primarily Principle 1 with links to Principles 7/8.

# 4. Strategy-level rules — important, but not constitution

## HFI-S1 — Design the sequence, not the isolated dimension

> **Judge interfaces and routes as complete sequences under the states in which they are actually used.**

This includes:

- approach;
- opening/turning;
- reach/manipulation;
- occupied/open element states;
- set-down/regrip;
- feedback;
- departure/return.

### Why not constitutional

It is an excellent general **method/strategy** but is more procedural than philosophical. It operationalises HFI-P1.

**Classification:** `STRATEGY-CANDIDATE`.

---

## HFI-S2 — Spend generosity at causal bottlenecks, not everywhere

> **Use permanent space or geometry where a demonstrated manoeuvre, sequence or future adaptation need earns it; prefer local causal correction over blanket enlargement.**

Examples include local turn/door recovery space, task staging, useful storage zones and bounded assistance geometry.

### Why not constitutional

The current constitution already requires permanence/serviceability to justify its burden. This is the human-factors application of that existing proportionality rule.

**Classification:** `STRATEGY-CANDIDATE`.

---

## HFI-S3 — Build durable capability early; keep specialist equipment demand-led

> **Where a credible future need is cheap to enable before construction and destructive to retrofit, preserve the durable architectural capacity. Keep user-specific, proprietary or maintained equipment replaceable and demand-led unless a defined present brief requires it.**

Examples that survived 05B/05C include:

- support-capable sanitary wall zones;
- credible future level-access shower capacity;
- genuine stairlift-compatible architecture;
- dual-use entrance-level fallback living;
- service/structural geometry that does not casually prevent later adaptation.

Examples deliberately **not** implied:

- installed grab rails everywhere;
- installed stairlift/lift machinery;
- universal motorised/adjustable kitchens;
- universal through-floor lift reservation (still unresolved).

### Why not constitutional

Principle 1 and the governing proportionality constraint already state the parent rule. HFI-S3 is a strong strategy-level interpretation, not a new doctrine.

**Classification:** `STRATEGY-CANDIDATE`.

---

# 5. Cross-discipline invariant

The following is not a separate HFI principle because the constitution already states it explicitly:

> **Human-factors benefit does not override life safety, legal compliance, structural integrity, health or environmental performance.**

HFI-05C adds the reciprocal procedural requirement:

> **A technical constraint should reshape the means, not erase the human need without testing alternatives.**

This belongs as an integration rule/method, not a new numbered principle.

# 6. Rejected principle candidates

## “Make circulation generous”

Rejected as principle-level wording. It is vague, area-biased and causally weak. HFI-S2 is more defensible.

## “Make the ground floor step-free”

Too project/strategy specific. The durable principle is avoidable compensation + proportional life-course capability; actual level-access targets belong to HFI-06 and project audit.

## “Design for accessibility”

Rejected as too broad. Visitability, ordinary usability, adaptability, wheelchair use and maintenance access remain distinct.

## “Design for the 95th percentile”

Rejected. Percentiles are variable-specific and do not create a universal person.

## “Make controls reachable”

Too narrow and misleading. The causal requirement includes mapping, sequence, force, feedback and persistence, not reach alone.

## “Future-proof the house”

Rejected. It invites speculative infrastructure. The project uses bounded option value and proportionality instead.

## “Provide wheelchair access everywhere”

Rejected as a universal constitutional formulation. Category-specific wheelchair requirements remain legitimate where claimed/required.

## “More space is better”

Rejected. More space can move or create friction and carries area/carbon/cost consequences.

## “Automation reduces friction”

Rejected. Automation can remove manipulation while adding state/failure complexity.

## “Install for future need now”

Rejected. HFI-S3 specifically distinguishes durable enabling architecture from demand-led equipment.

| normal human variance | Principle 1 occupant change; Principles 7/8 | HFI-P2 may sharpen existing wording; no new number |
| complete sequence / occupied state | Principle 8 + implementation layer | strategy/method, not constitution |
| local causal generosity | Principle 7 + proportionality constraint | strategy, not constitution |
| capacity now / equipment later | Principle 1 + serviceability proportionality | strategy, not constitution |
| hard technical duties | governing constraints | already explicit; preserve |
| controls mapping/feedback | Principle 8 intelligible local control | target/strategy evidence, no new principle |
| continuous ground plane | HFI-P1/P2 + technical interface rules | strategy/target/project level |
| swept-path proof | method/computational/delivery candidate | not constitution |
| laundry topology | project audit question | not principle |
| universal lift reservation | unresolved | prohibited from principle promotion |

### Result

**No new numbered constitutional principle is justified.**

That is a positive synthesis result, not a failure of the programme. The HFI evidence deepens and operationalises existing Principles 1, 7 and 8 rather than discovering a missing parent doctrine.

# 8. Proposed later HFI-08A integration route

HFI-05D does not edit the constitution now because HFI-06/07 must still derive targets and test whether the Reference House exposes material consequences.

If HFI-08A later finds upstream text change warranted, the narrowest plausible route is:

### Principle 1

Clarify that foreseeable occupant change includes **normal human variance and common temporary states**, tested before rather than after fixed targets are selected.

### Principle 8

Clarify that unnecessary vigilance includes **repeated bodily/cognitive compensation imposed by ordinary routes, controls and interfaces**, and that complete action sequences matter.

### Principle 7 / governing proportionality constraint

Likely no new wording required unless HFI-06/07 shows the present “useful dimensions / future constraints must earn themselves” language is insufficient. HFI-S2 already follows naturally from it.

No wording should be merged upstream until HFI-08A re-reads the then-current constitution and applies the architectural-specificity firewall.

# 9. Implications for HFI-06

The principle set constrains target derivation.

### HFI-06 may derive numbers only when they represent a real mechanism

For example:

- stair going can represent foot-placement/support geometry;
- corridor/door/turn targets can represent named manoeuvres;
- threshold values can represent discontinuity only while preserving drainage/weathering context;
- force/reach targets can represent defined interfaces and postures.

### HFI-06 may not create numbers merely because principles are qualitative

The following remain invalid:

- a universal “generosity factor”;
- one human percentile applied to everything;
- one accessible corridor/turning number;
- one universal control height;
- one kitchen/bathroom dimension standing in for task usability;
- a weighted low-friction score.

### Unresolved propositions stay unresolved

HFI-06 must not invent a quantitative target for:

- universal future lift reservation;
- upstairs/downstairs laundry topology;
- category-specific specialist equipment without a defined claim.

# 10. Reference House consequence

HFI-05D issues **no Reference House verdict**.

Later HFI-07 must judge the house against targets derived under the principles, not use the principles as rhetorical pass/fail labels.

Examples:

- “the corridor feels generous” is not a PASS;
- “the stair complies with AD K” is not proof of HFI-P1;
- “the house is step-free downstairs” is not proof of HFI-P2;
- “future shower possible” is not proof unless source geometry/services can represent it;
- “1000 mm stair” is not stairlift-compatibility proof.

Where source detail is missing: `NOT-MODELLED`.

---

# 11. Computational boundary

No compiler proof follows directly from a principle.

Principles are reasons. Machine checks, if later admitted, must test separately authorised source facts and methods.

A compiler must never claim `HFI-P1 PASS` from a handful of numeric checks.

Potential later checks remain narrow, e.g.:

- represented swept-path clearance;
- represented stair target correspondence;
- represented threshold discontinuity;
- represented control relation;
- represented appliance open-state conflict.

HFI-09C owns any computational admission.

# 12. Red-team

### Did 05D merely rebrand universal design?

No. Specialist/category-specific geometry remains bounded; proportionality and claim scope are explicit.

### Did 05D create doctrine from one user annoyance?

No. HFI-P1/P2 survived multiple independent domains and evidence families.

### Did “human variance” become an excuse for spatial bloat?

No. HFI-S2 and HFI-01D explicitly reject blanket enlargement.

### Did “low friction” weaken technical integrity?

No. HFI-05C hard constraints remain controlling.

### Did option value become speculative future-proofing?

No. HFI-S3 requires credible need + low enabling burden + high retrofit asymmetry; user/product-specific equipment remains demand-led.

### Are there too many principles?

No. The programme produces only two principle-level propositions, both best integrated into existing constitutional provisions rather than added as new numbered doctrine.

### Could there be only one?

Not cleanly. HFI-P1 describes the quality of ordinary interaction; HFI-P2 describes the population/state model used to judge it. Merging them would make either the interaction test or the human-variance firewall implicit.
