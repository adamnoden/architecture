## Result

HFI earns **one delivery verification family** and **no standing physical prototype family**.

Accepted delivery consequence:

> High-frequency or high-consequence domestic routes, controls and interfaces must be verified as complete actions in their relevant occupied states, using finished/net geometry and the evidence authority appropriate to the claim.

This becomes delivery requirement **N17 — Human-Factors Verification**.

The packet rejects automatic mock-up creation. Ordinary stairs, conventional doors/hardware, established threshold systems and standard spatial clearances remain design/detail/inspection questions unless a genuinely non-standard assembled condition creates uncertainty that drawings, established evidence and product information cannot resolve proportionately.

## Delivery consequences

HFI-07 repeatedly showed that topology can pass while the finished interface remains unproved; nominal widths can be eroded by strings, guards, frames, ironmongery, finishes and fixed projections; room labels do not prove complete tasks; and `NOT-MODELLED` is often the correct state until a later project stage supplies the missing facts.

Accordingly, delivery shall:

- verify human-use claims at the **finished/net installed condition** where that condition matters;
- review selected interfaces as complete action sequences rather than isolated dimensions;
- preserve external-rule, project-target, product-evidence and architectural-judgement authority separately;
- keep `NOT-MODELLED` facts open until the stage at which they become knowable;
- protect critical clearances and action sequences from substitution or site drift.

For the Reference House stair, HFI-08B's 300 mm architectural going is retained. Later technical design must prove final rise/going consistency, net clear width, headroom, landing geometry, handrail/guard continuity and graspability, relevant lighting/conspicuity, door conflicts and carrying/reduced-attention sequence. Nominal width alone still does not prove stairlift compatibility.

## Prototype disposition

**No standing HFI prototype programme is created.**

A project-specific mock-up/first article is earned only where a bespoke interface, coupled action sequence, novel assembled mechanism, tolerance/movement risk, workmanship uncertainty or stronger unproved performance claim cannot otherwise be resolved proportionately.

Conventional stairs, ordinary compliant door assemblies and established threshold systems do not become prototype candidates merely because HFI reviewed them.
