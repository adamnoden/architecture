## Result

HFI earns **one delivery verification family** and **no standing physical prototype family**.

Accepted delivery consequence:

> High-frequency or high-consequence domestic routes, controls and interfaces must be verified as complete actions in their relevant occupied states, using finished/net geometry and the evidence authority appropriate to the claim.

This becomes delivery requirement **N17 — Human-Factors Verification**.

The packet rejects automatic mock-up creation. Ordinary stairs, conventional doors/hardware, established threshold systems and standard spatial clearances remain design/detail/inspection questions unless a genuinely non-standard assembled condition creates uncertainty that drawings, established evidence and product information cannot resolve proportionately.

## Why delivery integration is earned

HFI-07 repeatedly exposed the same boundary:

- topology can pass while the finished interface remains unproved;
- nominal widths can be eroded by strings, guards, frames, ironmongery, finishes or fixed projections;
- a room label or adjacency does not prove the complete task;
- regulatory/guidance correspondence does not prove a stronger project preference;
- `NOT-MODELLED` is often the correct state until a later project stage supplies the missing facts.

Those are not reasons to invent early detail. They are reasons to define **when and how later delivery proves the condition**.

## Delivery changes

### Finished/net proof

Where human-use claims depend on usable space, Stage 4/5 evidence must account for the assembled condition. Relevant examples include stairs, door clear openings, thresholds, fixed projections, wet-room geometry, appliance open states and material object/replacement routes.

### Complete-action proof

Selected interfaces are reviewed as sequences such as:

`approach → align/turn → reach/grip → operate/open → pass/transfer → set down/regrip → feedback → close/reset/leave`

Only foreseeable states that materially affect the task are tested. Occupied hands, wet hands, low light, fatigue, temporary injury, assisting another person and carrying an ordinary bulky object are prompts, not universal simultaneous design conditions.

### Installed assembly proof

A nominal component does not prove the complete action. Where operation quality matters, final evidence must consider the installed assembly and approach geometry.

### Stage-owned unknowns

`NOT-MODELLED` survives until the correct stage makes the fact knowable. Delivery schedules name the later proof rather than convert uncertainty into invented early geometry.

## RIBA-stage disposition

- **Stage 1:** brief relevant occupants/scenarios, repeated actions and present versus future specialist-equipment needs.
- **Stage 2:** create the initial Human-Factors Verification Schedule and identify geometry expensive to change later.
- **Stage 3:** prove selected complete actions at coordinated planning resolution; keep genuinely unavailable facts `NOT-MODELLED`.
- **Stage 4:** replace nominal assumptions with finished-interface evidence and tolerances.
- **Stage 5:** protect critical geometry from substitution/site drift and verify representative installed conditions where necessary.
- **Stage 6:** confirm selected as-built conditions and record material deviations.
- **Stage 7:** look for recurring workarounds/choreography and feed material findings back to the evidence base without promoting anecdote directly into doctrine.

## Prototype disposition

### No standing HFI prototype

HFI-08C creates no new P-series prototype and no generic stair/door/threshold mock-up requirement.

Reasons:

1. the selected 300 mm Reference House stair is ordinary established construction;
2. mainstream stair/door/threshold questions already have substantial standards, product and professional-practice evidence;
3. HFI’s unresolved questions are mainly final geometry/product/site facts rather than novel physical principles;
4. unnecessary prototypes would confuse research novelty with implementation discipline.

### Prototype only if earned

A project-specific first article/mock-up is justified only where a genuinely bespoke interface, coupled action sequence, novel assembled mechanism, tolerance/movement risk, workmanship uncertainty or stronger unproved performance claim cannot otherwise be resolved proportionately.

If earned, prototype the **complete interface/action**, not a decorative fragment.

## Reference House consequence

HFI-08B selected 300 mm principal-stair going at architectural-source level. HFI-08C adds no further Reference House geometry.

Later technical development must prove the finished stair system: final rise/going consistency, net clear width, headroom, landing geometry, handrail/guard continuity and graspability, relevant lighting/conspicuity, door conflicts and carrying/reduced-attention sequence.

Nominal width still does not prove stairlift compatibility; any later stairlift proposition remains product/family-specific and demand-led.

## Authority / maturity consequences

This packet changes **delivery requirements**, not architectural doctrine or Reference House geometry.

It does not create a new principle, pattern, strategy or prototype family; promote an HFI number into universal doctrine; claim specialist accessibility compliance; broaden compiler proof scope; change Reference House/prototype maturity; or claim external validation.
