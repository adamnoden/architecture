## Result

HFI now has a bounded RH-11 external-review handoff. It does **not** create a parallel review system: the new human-factors / inclusive-design brief sits inside the existing RH-11 specialist-review architecture and uses the existing response template and rewind semantics.

The review question is deliberately adversarial:

> Does the current Reference House provide a credible, proportionate basis for ordinary domestic use across foreseeable variation in bodies, capability and temporary states, or has the architecture normalised avoidable repeated-use burdens that should be corrected before technical design?

The brief is **ready for competent review**, not reviewed.

## Review boundary

The specialist is asked to keep separate:

- law / regulatory duty;
- statutory guidance;
- standards / professional guidance;
- research evidence;
- project-specific HFI targets;
- product-specific evidence;
- architectural judgement.

The selected 300 mm principal-stair going is explicitly presented as a stronger Ovid’s House project preference, not a statutory minimum.

## Conditions sent to review

The packet exposes the current architecture to challenge across:

- principal approach, substantially level entrance and ground-floor route;
- finished/net clearances after real linings, frames, ironmongery and fixed projections;
- the 300 mm principal stair as a complete occupied sequence, including handrails/guards, headroom, landings, lighting and carrying states;
- entrance-storey fallback living, sanitary/washing capacity and life-course enabling provision;
- doors, thresholds, controls and operating-force/sequence questions;
- kitchen/garden/grocery and large-object routes where material;
- the unresolved upstairs sleeping/bathing ↔ ground-floor utility laundry burden;
- real-site approach conditions that cannot be closed against a surrogate site;
- stairlift compatibility claims, which remain unsupported by nominal width alone;
- through-floor lift reservation, which remains unresolved/no adopted target.

## Laundry treatment

The reviewer is expressly forbidden from assuming that upstairs laundry is a universal inclusive-design answer.

If the current task burden is judged material, the comparison must first test lower-disruption interventions—collection, clean-linen/storage coordination, set-down/staging and reduced-trip workflow—then weigh any relocation against moisture/leak containment, ventilation/drying, noise/vibration, services, structural build-up, replacement and garden/dirty-work relationships.

## Red-team cases

The brief requires ordinary adverse cases rather than exotic edge cases:

1. nominal clearance disappears in the finished assembly;
2. the level entrance creates a weathering/drainage/operation contradiction;
3. stair arithmetic passes but the occupied sequence fails;
4. temporary ground-floor living becomes necessary;
5. laundry burden proves material.

Each case must identify the causal bottleneck, smallest credible correction and whether an earlier RH gate actually needs reopening.

## Maturity boundary

Creating this handoff does **not** establish external professional review, accessibility compliance, inclusive-design certification or higher Reference House maturity.

Those claims remain `not-established` until a competent independent response is received, recorded, resolved and permitted to change the design.
