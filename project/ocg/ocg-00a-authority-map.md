# OCG-00A — Occupied Change Authority / Ownership Map

**Programme:** OCG — Occupied Change  
**Parent:** [#547 — occupied change and bounded intervention programme](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/547)  
**Packet:** OCG-00A  
**Baseline `main`:** `374d0a0e23f5c31c8d9998ac0d2ba89d26368cc2`  
**State:** candidate authority map; becomes the OCG-00A record when merged

## Purpose

Freeze the current project authority surrounding occupied intervention before OCG defines a baseline, research method, intervention taxonomy or design response.

OCG exists because a house may be technically maintainable while a small intervention still spreads disruption through unrelated fabric, services and occupied space. Its distinct object is therefore the **consequence envelope of carrying out foreseeable work while the dwelling remains wholly or partly occupied**.

This packet answers only:

> **Which existing project authority owns each part of that problem, and what is OCG allowed to own without duplicating it?**

It does **not** change architecture.

## OCG-00A boundary

This record does not:

- freeze the detailed occupied-change architectural baseline — OCG-00B;
- turn the originating renovation experience into evidence or doctrine — OCG-00C and later research;
- define final intervention classes or a consequence ledger — OCG-01;
- perform occupied-work, maintainability or reversible-construction research — OCG-02;
- select canonical intervention scenarios — OCG-03;
- audit existing doctrine or the Reference House — OCG-04/05;
- promote a pattern, candidate assembly or Reference House change;
- create a compiler proof family or `RH-C*` stage;
- alter drawings, publication prose or the PR-02 physical object.

The packet is an ownership map and routing rule only.

# 1. Governing authority

## 1.1 Ovid’s House constitution — owns the architectural proposition

Primary authority:

- [`docs/manuscript/governing-principles.md`](../../docs/manuscript/governing-principles.md)

The constitution already owns the propositions most likely to generate occupied-change benefits:

- build for maintenance, repair, alteration and renewal;
- preserve permanent fabric from routine shorter-life distribution and attachment;
- design interfaces;
- make credible failure detectable, containable and repairable;
- give maintenance a geography;
- use ordinary parts and reversible assembly proportionately;
- keep faster-changing layers changeable without making the architecture generic;
- preserve physical legibility and isolation;
- resolve access, replacement and technical necessities architecturally.

OCG does not create a parallel constitutional layer. If later OCG evidence earns a new or sharpened general proposition, it must re-enter through manuscript authority and survive the same proportionality and evidence discipline as existing doctrine.

## 1.2 External maintenance geography — owns exterior task access

Primary authorities:

- [`docs/manuscript/maintenance-geography-external.md`](../../docs/manuscript/maintenance-geography-external.md)
- [`docs/research/external-maintenance-access.md`](../../docs/research/external-maintenance-access.md)
- [`docs/delivery/external-maintenance-access-requirements.md`](../../docs/delivery/external-maintenance-access-requirements.md)
- Reference House external-access material, including [`docs/reference-house/external-access-maintenance-plan.md`](../../docs/reference-house/external-access-maintenance-plan.md)

This authority already treats maintenance as a complete task route: site/service entrance, approach, support/setup, working position, component handling, withdrawal, waste and replacement route.

OCG therefore **does not own a new generic concept of maintenance access**. It consumes that model and extends the review question to consequences for the occupied dwelling: contamination, service interruption, unavailable rooms/routes, reinstatement and recovery.

# 2. Pattern / strategy authority

Primary authorities:

- [`docs/patterns/README.md`](../../docs/patterns/README.md)
- [`docs/patterns/service-topology-sequence.md`](../../docs/patterns/service-topology-sequence.md)
- individual admitted patterns and strategies under `docs/patterns/`

The current pattern system already owns reusable architectural responses relevant to occupied change, including:

- controlled utility entry;
- plant room as service hub;
- accessible vertical service zone;
- coherent horizontal service route;
- high-service-room service wall;
- accessible room service route;
- designed structural penetrations and compartmented service voids;
- fail-safe water distribution, visible leakage and failure-tolerant wet-service-room responses;
- permanent opening / replaceable window and door relationships;
- designed thresholds and controlled attachment planes;
- physical service indexing;
- external maintenance routes;
- the strategy of separating structural floors from changeable layers where proportionate.

Several more intervention-friendly ideas remain explicitly **held candidates**, not selected doctrine or Reference House truth. OCG must preserve those maturity distinctions.

### Ownership rule

Patterns and strategies own reusable **architectural responses**. OCG may expose that a current response leaves an excessive intervention consequence, but it does not fork the response under an OCG name. Any accepted correction returns to the owning pattern/strategy or creates a new candidate through normal pattern admission.

# 3. Reference House authority

Primary authorities:

- [`docs/reference-house/README.md`](../../docs/reference-house/README.md)
- [`docs/reference-house/pattern-occurrence-register.md`](../../docs/reference-house/pattern-occurrence-register.md)
- current Reference House coordination records under `docs/reference-house/`
- canonical Reference House source / compiler fixture where the represented fact is computationally owned

The Reference House occurrence register is especially important because it separates:

1. an admitted project pattern;
2. this house’s selected occurrence;
3. the current implementation direction;
4. outstanding technical proof.

Current occurrences relevant to OCG include actual service hub/distribution geography, dry-side wet-room access, replaceable opening assemblies, room-edge dry-service routes, external maintenance routes and the bounded floor strategy. It also explicitly holds several apparently attractive serviceability candidates rather than assuming them into the house.

### Ownership rule

The Reference House owns **this house’s geometry, topology and selected implementation direction**. OCG-05 may later test the complete intervention sequence against it, but any architectural defect must reopen or amend the owning Reference House authority. OCG must not patch the house in an audit file, drawing or compiler projection.

# 4. Human Factors authority

Primary authority:

- [`docs/development/human-factors-low-friction-inhabitation-programme.md`](../../docs/development/human-factors-low-friction-inhabitation-programme.md)
- HFI issue [#195](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/195)

HFI is complete. It owns the person × task × state side of low-friction inhabitation: movement, controls, object routes, ordinary usability, human variance and proportionate life-course adaptability.

The overlap with OCG is narrow but important.

| Question | Owner |
|---|---|
| Can an occupant perform the ordinary task without needless compensation? | HFI / owning architecture |
| Should the house preserve capacity for a foreseeable life-course change? | HFI / owning architecture |
| What disruption occurs while that adaptation is installed or changed? | OCG consequence audit |
| Is a worker/object route also an ordinary occupant/object route? | Shared evidence; HFI owns inhabitation, OCG owns intervention consequence |

OCG must not reopen HFI as a parallel programme merely because an intervention involves a person or route. A new human-factors defect routes to its architectural/HFI-derived owner; OCG records only the consequence of performing the change.

# 5. Manufacturing / assembly authority

Primary authority:

- [`docs/development/manufacturing-strategy.md`](../../docs/development/manufacturing-strategy.md)

The manufacturing strategy already owns the project’s long-life/short-life manufacturing gradient, reversible dry assembly, tolerance translation, first-article disassembly/reinstatement proof and the qualified preference for controlled manufacture of replaceable layers.

It explicitly asks whether workshop finishing reduces wet work or contamination, while rejecting prefabrication as a universal good.

### Ownership rule

OCG may measure whether off-site preparation, dry assembly or replacement definition materially reduces the intervention consequence envelope. It does not own a new prefabrication doctrine. Manufacturing and assembly responses remain subject to cost, carbon, transport, workmanship, fire, acoustic, moisture and architectural consequences.

# 6. Delivery / construction-method authority

Primary authority:

- [`docs/delivery/README.md`](../../docs/delivery/README.md)
- downstream implementation/verification requirements under `docs/delivery/`

Delivery owns requirements, responsibilities and stage decisions for real commissions. It is downstream from architectural/evidential authority and cannot make a proposition true by writing it into a brief.

This is the principal seam for OCG’s distinction between permanent architecture and temporary work method.

Examples normally owned by delivery / contractor method unless architectural evidence proves otherwise include:

- temporary dust partitions and protection;
- sequencing and housekeeping;
- temporary extraction;
- daily cleaning;
- tool selection;
- temporary service provision;
- work packaging and trade coordination;
- site supervision and competence.

OCG may identify that permanent geometry makes competent containment or sequencing unnecessarily difficult. It must not redesign permanent architecture merely to eliminate every inconvenience that ordinary temporary works can handle proportionately.

# 7. Site / deployment authority

Primary authority:

- [`project/dpl/deployment-programme.md`](../dpl/deployment-programme.md)
- DPL site/interface/evidence records under `project/dpl/`

DPL owns real-site claims, target/jurisdiction composition, site-interface evidence and the distinction between architectural authorship and site truth.

Occupied-change questions that depend on facts such as these are site/project dependent unless separately generalised:

- external delivery or waste route;
- van/skip/staging position;
- boundary and neighbour dependence;
- real approach width/levels;
- temporary external storage;
- actual utility/sewer/site-water conditions;
- real access rights or highway occupation.

OCG may state what evidence an intervention analysis needs. It must not invent site facts for the site-agnostic Reference House.

# 8. Research authority

Primary authority:

- `docs/research/` and source-specific evidence syntheses

The originating renovation experience is a legitimate **problem signal and scenario source**, but not general evidence. OCG-02 must distinguish:

- law / standards / guidance;
- empirical or post-occupancy evidence;
- industry practice;
- precedent;
- project preference;
- anecdotal trigger.

Research owns the evidential support for later claims. OCG owns the question architecture and synthesis at its programme scope; it may not promote a convenient precedent or personal experience into doctrine without that evidence boundary.

# 9. Computational authority

Primary authority:

- [`docs/computational/README.md`](../../docs/computational/README.md)
- executable code under `compiler/`

The computational track owns only relationships that can be represented with controlled inputs and bounded proof claims. Its current service semantics can represent nodes, routes, networks, access obligations and registered crossings, but representability is not adequacy.

### OCG computational boundary

OCG has no automatic right to create:

- a new Reference House compiler stage;
- a universal intervention simulator;
- a scalar renovation/disruption score;
- inferred contractor duration;
- deterministic claims about dust/noise/cleaning burden without controlled inputs and a justified method.

Later computational admission may be appropriate for narrow facts such as declared access, isolation, withdrawal clearance, route continuity or dependency relationships. Any such work requires a separate explicit proof boundary after OCG synthesis.

# 12. OCG’s unique authority

After the overlap audit, the narrow authority that OCG can legitimately own is:

> **A cross-cutting method for describing and testing the consequence envelope of a foreseeable intervention while the dwelling remains wholly or partly occupied.**

That method may classify consequences across distinct dimensions such as:

- fabric disturbance;
- service interruption;
- occupancy loss;
- contamination/work spread;
- intervention logistics;
- reinstatement and recovery.

It may also classify causal ownership so that architecture is neither credited nor blamed for consequences principally caused by contractor method or site facts.

OCG does **not** own the permanent architectural solution. Its strongest role is diagnostic and coordinative.

# 14. Finding and re-entry protocol

A future OCG audit finding follows this route:

1. **Describe the intervention** without assuming a preferred solution.
2. **Record the consequence** in the relevant non-scalar dimensions.
3. **Classify causal ownership**: architectural, assembly/detail, contractor/method, site/project or unresolved/shared.
4. **Identify the existing owner** of the causative proposition, geometry, interface or process.
5. **Route the finding upstream** to that owner.
6. The owner may **change, reject, qualify or leave unresolved** the proposition.
7. If upstream authority changes, revalidate affected Reference House, computational, drawing, delivery and publication artefacts through their existing controls.

Do not patch a downstream representation merely to make an OCG scenario pass.

---

# 15. Duplication traps now explicitly rejected

OCG must not become:

- a second maintenance-geography doctrine;
- a second service-topology pattern language;
- a second HFI/adaptability programme;
- a catalogue of “removable everything” assemblies;
- a construction-method handbook;
- an argument for universal access floors, wall panels, risers or manifolds;
- a prefabrication programme;
- a disruption-scoring engine;
- a new compiler stage by default;
- a publication chapter merely because the internal programme is large.

A valid OCG outcome may be that existing architecture already addresses a consequence, that contractor method is the correct owner, or that accepting disruption is more proportionate than permanent provision.

# Result

**OCG-00A PASS — authority is sufficiently separable to continue.**

The programme has a distinct, non-duplicative role if it stays narrow:

> **OCG owns the intervention consequence model and cross-cutting audit. Existing architectural, human-factors, manufacturing, delivery, site, computational, drawing and publication authorities continue to own their respective propositions and implementations.**

The principal seam to protect is the difference between **serviceability/access** and **occupied-change consequence**. Existing maintenance geography already asks whether the complete maintenance task has a credible route. OCG adds value only where it examines what that task does to the rest of the inhabited house and its recovery afterwards.
