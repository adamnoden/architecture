# 2. Authority at re-entry

## 2.1 Architectural authority already says the important things

The current constitution already contains the architectural substance that OCG repeatedly found useful:

- **GP1 — Build for time:** the house is designed for repeated maintenance, repair, alteration and renewal, with foreseeable change tested proportionately;
- **GP2 — Preserve the permanent fabric:** shorter-lived systems should not consume slower fabric at their renewal rate;
- **GP3 — Design the interface:** inspection, disassembly and renewal belong among the functions an interface may need to resolve;
- **GP4 — Failure:** credible failures should be detectable, containable and locally repairable where practical;
- **GP5 — Give maintenance a geography:** access includes approach, working, handling, withdrawal and replacement paths rather than nominal reachability;
- **GP6 — Ordinary parts / reversible assembly where proportionate:** replacement strategy remains conditional rather than ideological;
- **GP7 — Let permanence be architectural:** adaptability is concentrated where change earns itself; enduring architecture is permitted and desired.

The constitution also already carries the governing proportionality constraint:

> Serviceability is pursued proportionately. Permanent provision should reflect the frequency, consequence and retrofit difficulty of the future event it is intended to support.

OCG-06 therefore correctly refused `GP12 — Bounded Change` as duplicate constitutional content.

## 2.2 Publication authority is currently frozen for PR-02

PUB-01 places the book in **PR-02 — private 12-copy object edition**. PR-02 owns physical-object production and does **not** authorise another free-form manuscript research cycle.

Any new substantive argument, figure, evidence claim or released drawing change must therefore earn an explicit freeze break.

# 3. OCG-09A — publication / doctrine decision

## 3.1 Does OCG earn a new chapter?

**No.**

A dedicated occupied-change chapter would substantially duplicate the existing Build for time / permanent-fabric / interface / failure / maintenance-geography argument while exposing the reader to internal review machinery that exists mainly to test those propositions.

It would also risk making the book read like a facilities-management or renovation-method manual, directly contrary to the publication constitution.

## 3.2 Does OCG earn a new numbered governing principle?

**No.**

`Change should be bounded` is a useful cross-cutting review compression, but its architectural content is already distributed coherently across GP1–GP5 plus proportionality. Calling it a new constitutional provision would overstate novelty and create competing authority.

## 3.3 Does OCG earn a current manuscript insertion?

**No current freeze break is earned.**

The strongest robust explanatory line remains:

> **A small foreseeable change should not become a whole-house event without good reason.**

That line is clear and memorable, but the present controlled manuscript already carries its substance in stronger architectural terms. Inserting it now would be an editorial compression, not a correction of a missing architectural proposition.

PR-02 is therefore not interrupted for it.

The phrase may remain an **editorial reserve** for a later authorised revision cycle — especially PR-04 after external criticism — if a reader demonstrably needs a human explanation linking the existing provisions. At that point it must be integrated locally and tested against duplication, rhythm and claim scope rather than imported as an OCG mini-chapter.

## 3.4 What about “The house should remain a house while it is being changed”?

**Reject as present doctrine and general performance promise.**

The motive is valuable, but the literal sentence is too broad. OCG established legitimate cases where:

- a bathroom refit removes bathing function;
- a kitchen refit removes cooking function;
- major structural work may make continued occupation unreasonable;
- some accepted disruption is the proportionate whole-life answer.

The line may survive as private motivational language, but it is not admitted to the controlled publication.

## 3.5 Figures or comparative intervention narratives

**None are earned at present.**

The canonical intervention corpus was designed as an adversarial internal test set, not as reader pedagogy. OCG exposed no architectural defect and selected no new detail whose understanding requires a publication figure.

A future external reader may identify one intervention comparison as unusually explanatory. That would be a publication decision under the then-current gate, not an automatic OCG entitlement.

## 3.6 Publication result

**OCG remains large internally and small externally — at present, externally zero additional pages.**

This is a successful result. The internal work increased confidence in the existing argument without forcing the book to display its test harness.

# 5. OCG-09C — final adversarial closeout

The following attacks are applied to the integrated result, not merely to the wording of the principle.

## 5.1 Maintenance fetishism

**Attack:** The project could optimise the house around rare future maintenance until maintenance convenience dominates ordinary life.

**Disposition:** survived. OCG-01D requires permanent responses to pay for their area, cost, carbon, technical duties, maintenance burden and architectural effect. CI-10 and CI-13 explicitly prove that accepting future disruption can be correct. OCG-07 added no infrastructure.

## 5.2 Spatial bloat / cost / carbon

**Attack:** Risers, cavities, access routes and duplicate systems can consume daily space and material to solve occasional events.

**Disposition:** survived. OCG rejected universal access floors, universal removable linings, duplicate wet-service routes, maximum isolation, duplicate kitchens/bathrooms and speculative structural flexibility. Existing concentrated infrastructure remains governed by upstream proportionality rather than OCG expansion.

## 5.3 Fire / acoustic / moisture / airtightness / thermal / structural regression

**Attack:** Reversible interfaces and voids can weaken the permanent building.

**Disposition:** survived with explicit proof boundary. OCG-02D established that every reversible interface inherits the full technical duties of the boundary it interrupts. OCG selected no new assembly and therefore creates no new unproved technical exception. Existing details remain with their competent owners.

## 5.4 Workmanship and tolerance burden

**Attack:** Demountability can create loose joints, repeated-damage interfaces, rattles, visual scars and specialist installation dependence.

**Disposition:** survived. Universal demountability was rejected. Controlled manufacture/reversible assembly remains selective and subordinate to GP6, detail performance and ordinary competent construction.

## 5.5 Proprietary dependence

**Attack:** A “maintainable” house can become dependent on proprietary panels, fixings, software or obsolete replacement ecosystems.

**Disposition:** survived. OCG adds no product ecosystem. GP6 continues to prefer ordinary components, standard interfaces, remanufacturability and independence from unique mechanisms/software.

## 5.6 Security and privacy during work

**Attack:** Easy access or permanent contractor routes can compromise domestic security/privacy.

**Disposition:** survived by non-selection. OCG did not create dedicated contractor circulation, permanent clean/dirty zoning or universal externally accessible service interfaces. Real temporary access and occupation controls remain project/delivery matters.

## 5.7 Institutional / industrial character

**Attack:** Serviceability can make a house read as a laboratory, hospital, ship or raised-floor office.

**Disposition:** survived. `Accessible ≠ exposed` remains intact; no ubiquitous access hardware or service display was added. OCG explicitly prefers concentrated infrastructure and ordinary domestic architecture over universal technicalisation.

## 5.8 False zero-disruption promise

**Attack:** “Occupied change” can imply silent, clean, instant work with all functions retained.

**Disposition:** rejected explicitly. The programme makes no zero-disruption, zero-dust, zero-noise or continued-occupation promise. Intrinsic construction consequences remain real; room surrender or temporary decant may be correct.

## 5.9 Architecture taking credit for contractor competence

**Attack:** Good containment, housekeeping and sequencing could be misrepresented as architectural achievement.

**Disposition:** survived. OCG consistently separates architecture-determined consequence from assembly/detail, contractor/method and site/project causality. Temporary containment, extraction, protection, housekeeping and sequencing remain contractor/delivery-owned by default.

## 5.10 Contractor method disguised as doctrine

**Attack:** The complete intervention sequence could become a prescriptive construction method.

**Disposition:** survived. The sequence remains an internal audit grammar only. It reveals propagation; it does not tell a contractor how to execute the works.

## 5.11 Speculative future-proofing

**Attack:** “Foreseeable change” can become a loophole for arbitrary spare capacity.

**Disposition:** survived. CI-10 refuses speculative remote wet-service provision; CI-13 refuses universal structural flexibility. Future services/products are not presumed. Permanent capacity must earn itself against frequency, consequence and retrofit difficulty.

## 5.12 Reference House overfit

**Attack:** OCG might simply celebrate the current Reference House because the scenarios were chosen to suit it.

**Disposition:** survived sufficiently for programme scope. OCG-03 froze scenarios before the RH verdicts, selected mechanisms rather than trades, included hostile negative controls, prohibited favourable mutation, and OCG-05 preserved model silence instead of converting missing detail into proof. The result remains virtual-design evidence, not universal validation.

## 5.13 Implementation detail promoted into general principle

**Attack:** Dry-side access, risers or removable pieces could be laundered into universal doctrine.

**Disposition:** survived. OCG-06 routes architectural means back to existing owners and explicitly treats them as conditional. `Change should be bounded` is a consequence test, not a mandated construction system.

## 5.14 Internal bureaucracy leaking into the book

**Attack:** Intervention classes, consequence ledgers, causal taxonomies and test matrices could overwhelm the publication.

**Disposition:** survived by final publication refusal. The machinery stays under `project/ocg/`; the current book receives no OCG taxonomy, matrix or new chapter.

## 5.15 Compiler proof-boundary inflation

**Attack:** Because route/isolation/clearance facts can be represented, the compiler could appear to prove “renovability”.

**Disposition:** survived. OCG-08 explicitly declines an OCG compiler stage, semantic domain, disruption score, duration model and OCG-specific regression family. Narrow facts may only enter existing owning domains when their controlled inputs exist.

## 5.16 Novelty where ordinary practice already suffices

**Attack:** OCG could rebrand established maintainability, access and refurbishment wisdom as invention.

**Disposition:** survived. OCG-06 explicitly states that access, isolation, replaceability and maintenance geography are not newly OCG-owned doctrine. The strongest precedent was traditional bounded replaceability rather than novelty theatre. No new constitutional claim is made.

# 6. Final surviving claims

OCG closes with the following and no more.

### 6.1 Cross-cutting review principle

> **Change should be bounded.**

Technical meaning:

> For a foreseeable intervention, the consequence envelope should remain proportionate to the thing being changed and to the permanent architectural burden required to contain it.

Status: **internal synthesis / review principle**, not a new numbered constitutional provision and not a universal machine rule.

### 6.2 Human explanation

> **A small foreseeable change should not become a whole-house event without good reason.**

Status: **editorial reserve**, not inserted into the current frozen publication.

### 6.3 Internal analytical method

The intervention consequence envelope and complete-sequence audit remain available as internal cross-cutting review machinery.

They ask what happens to:

- permanent fabric;
- service continuity;
- occupied rooms/functions/routes;
- dirty/noisy/wet/hazardous work geography;
- worker/material/replacement/waste logistics;
- reinstatement, cleaning and return to use.

These dimensions remain non-scalar.

### 6.4 Architectural ownership

The means of achieving bounded change remain with existing owners: GP1–GP7, service topology, interfaces/wet details, manufacturing strategy, RH gates, delivery and DPL/site.

OCG owns the test, not a separate house system.

# 9. Acceptance test

OCG can now answer its opening question:

> **Can foreseeable change remain bounded enough that an occupied house does not unnecessarily become a building site?**

**Yes, as a design objective and review test — with explicit proportionality and proof limits.**

The programme found that architecture materially controls whether a small technical intervention unnecessarily recruits unrelated slow fabric, broad service dependencies, domestic territory or extensive reinstatement. It also found that many dirty/noisy/logistical consequences are properly controlled by competent temporary works rather than permanent architecture, and that larger disruption is sometimes the correct whole-life result.

The current Ovid's House architecture already contains the principal propositions needed to pursue that objective. The Reference House audit found no present represented defect requiring redesign. The programme therefore improves epistemic confidence and review discipline without manufacturing a new architectural system merely to justify its existence.
