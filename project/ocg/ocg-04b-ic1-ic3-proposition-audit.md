## Purpose

Audit canonical scenarios CI-01…CI-07 against **existing** Ovid’s House propositions without repairing anything and without awarding Reference House implementation passes.

The finding unit is the OCG-04A atomic unit:

> **scenario × proposition × consequence mechanism**

Evidence and ownership remain separate from finding status. Every geometry/topology/detail statement that the proposition cannot itself prove is explicitly routed forward.

The OCG-02A causal split is particularly controlling:

- architecture may own **why** a destructive access operation is necessary;
- competent extraction, temporary barriers, housekeeping and ordinary worksite controls are contractor/method owned by default;
- service topology may determine outage **scope**, while task/product/method usually dominate outage **duration**;
- technical completion, reinstatement and return to use remain separate.

OCG-02B/D further prevent any finding from equating a removable fixing, cavity or access panel with completed replaceability.

# CI-01 — inspect and clear a foul-drainage obstruction

**Class:** IC1 — inspection / maintenance  
**Principal dimensions:** access/fabric; services; occupancy; worksite/contamination; logistics; recovery.

## OCG-04B-CI-01-R01 — controlled foul-maintenance geography

**Consequence mechanism:** destructive/occupied-space access to diagnose and clear a foul obstruction.  
**Sequence steps:** approach → expose/access → work → test → close/recover.  
**Existing proposition:** maintenance-intensive drainage should use concentrated, legible service geography with accessible inspection/rodding nodes rather than arbitrary discovery through principal fabric.  
**Sources:** Constitution 4/5/10; OCG-00B §10.1; service-topology sequence S4/S6/S7/S10; Accessible Vertical Service Zone (`supported`) and Designed Structural Penetration (`established`) where applicable.  
**Source class:** CONSTITUTION + ACTIVE_PATTERN + REFERENCE_OCCURRENCE.  
**Finding:** `PARTIAL`.  
**Causal ownership:** `ARCHITECTURE` for location/availability of intended access nodes; `SHARED` once diagnostic uncertainty moves beyond them.  
**Evidence basis:** `CONTROLLED_SOURCE` + `SUPPORTED_INFERENCE`.

**Controls:** the project explicitly selects one primary soil/vent stack in controlled service geography, accessible stack-base/rodding geography, a direct service-side building crossing and an external inspection chamber before the assumed site route.

**Does not control:** the exact location of an unknown obstruction, whether every obstruction can actually be reached from those nodes, real downstream sewer/site geometry, or contractor drain-clearance method.

**Permanent-burden note:** the current proposition is concentrated rather than proliferating inspection infrastructure; no OCG evidence supports adding access everywhere.

**Next proof route:** `MULTIPLE` — OCG-05 must test represented approach/access geometry; DPL/site evidence must establish downstream/site drainage truth.

**Outstanding facts:** exact access opening/working geometry; reach of intended rodding/inspection sequence; site sewer/chamber relationships.

## OCG-04B-CI-01-R02 — hygienic containment and cleaning

**Consequence mechanism:** contamination/odour/protection/cleaning generated by opening and clearing foul drainage.  
**Sequence steps:** protect/contain → work → clean/recover.  
**Existing proposition:** no permanent clean/dirty contractor infrastructure is selected.  
**Sources:** OCG-02A/02C causal ownership; delivery authority.  
**Source class:** RESEARCH + DELIVERY.  
**Finding:** `NOT_ARCHITECTURE_OWNED`.  
**Causal ownership:** `CONTRACTOR_METHOD` by default.  
**Evidence basis:** `EXTERNAL_EVIDENCE`.

Architecture remains answerable only if its chosen access geography makes proportionate temporary containment or cleaning needlessly impossible. That geometry question is distinct from the method itself.

**Next proof route:** `DELIVERY_METHOD_REQUIRED` for actual work controls; OCG-05 only if geometry appears to obstruct ordinary containment.

## OCG-04B-CI-01-R03 — site drainage truth

**Consequence mechanism:** whether the obstruction/inspection route lies beyond the site-agnostic building proposition.  
**Sources:** OCG-00A site/deployment boundary; OCG-00B foul-drainage baseline.  
**Source class:** SITE_DEPLOYMENT + REFERENCE_OCCURRENCE.  
**Finding:** `NOT_ARCHITECTURE_OWNED` for unknown real sewer position/access rights; building-to-site interface remains architectural only to its controlled boundary.  
**Causal ownership:** `SITE_PROJECT`.  
**Evidence basis:** current site fact is `UNKNOWN` / `NOT_REPRESENTED` for the site-agnostic RH.

**Next proof route:** `SITE_DPL_REQUIRED`.

### CI-01 synthesis

**Existing strength:** Ovid’s House already has a clear proposition against blind/room-scattered foul access and a concrete service-side access direction.

**Material remainder:** reachability of an arbitrary blockage is not established by the existence of stack-base/chamber access. Real downstream drainage is site-owned.

**Method-owned consequence:** hygienic containment/cleaning.

**OCG-05 queue:** physical access/working geometry at represented foul-maintenance nodes and the route to them.

**OCG-specific addition earned now:** **NO.** The proposition is partial in a bounded, honest way; no evidence supports multiplying permanent foul-access infrastructure before actual route proof.
