## Purpose

Reconcile the three independent OCG-04 proposition audits into one programme-level account of:

1. which existing Ovid’s House propositions genuinely carry occupied-change work across several intervention families;
2. where those propositions deliberately stop;
3. which permanent-performance tensions remain obligations rather than solved facts;
4. which consequences are not architecture-owned;
5. which negative-space / non-provision decisions are intentional;
6. exactly what OCG-05 must prove in the Reference House before any architectural response can be earned.

This is **not** a scorecard.

No number of `ADDRESSED` rows can cancel one material `PARTIAL`, `UNRESOLVED`, `UNADDRESSED` or technical-boundary obligation. Likewise, an `UNADDRESSED` row is not automatically a failure: in CI-10 and CI-13 it records deliberate non-provision whose proportionality must be preserved rather than repaired reflexively.

The governing synthesis rule is:

> **Keep the strongest existing proposition at its true maturity, keep its limit visible, and route the remaining uncertainty to the authority capable of proving it.**

# 2. Cross-scenario proposition strength register

This register is qualitative. It preserves source maturity instead of manufacturing a numerical rank.

## 2.1 Established propositions carrying repeated OCG value

### Plant Room as Service Hub — `established`

**Observed across:** CI-04, CI-11, CI-12; indirectly supports CI-09 through concentrated upstream service geography.

**What it genuinely carries:**

- major plant and principal distribution are concentrated away from principal occupied rooms;
- working space is part of plant geography, not an afterthought;
- replacement geography includes the principle that the largest foreseeable replaceable item must have a credible route out;
- principal isolation/distribution becomes more legible and accessible;
- repeated maintenance burden is concentrated where permanent dedicated area is easier to justify.

**What it does not prove:**

- actual product packing;
- actual old-out/new-in route geometry;
- independent isolation of every plant module;
- whole-system branch renewability;
- real external/site continuation.

**Synthesis:** one of the strongest existing occupied-change propositions because it serves multiple recurring tasks and therefore has a credible whole-life reason to consume permanent area. Its remaining OCG debt is mostly physical implementation proof, not missing doctrine.

### Roof Maintenance Route — `established`

**Observed across:** CI-02.

**What it genuinely carries:**

- maintenance access is selected from the actual task;
- temporary external access, internal access and permanent hardware are alternatives rather than status symbols;
- a roof hatch/anchor is not maintainability merely because it exists;
- permanent access hardware must justify weather, thermal, security and architectural burdens.

**What it does not prove:**

- the Reference House access method;
- real site support/boundary feasibility;
- temporary access-system design.

**Synthesis:** already expresses the exact restraint OCG research earned. No new occupied-change roof doctrine is needed.

### Accessible Rainwater Route — `established`

**Observed across:** CI-02.

**What it genuinely carries:**

- debris-prone collection/outlet points should be inspectable and maintainable;
- drainage failure should become visible/safe before hidden fabric is damaged where proportionate.

**What it does not prove:**

- hydraulic sizing;
- terminal/detail performance;
- real site discharge.

**Synthesis:** strong maintenance/failure proposition; OCG adds no new principle.

### Controlled Utility Entry — `established`

**Observed across:** CI-04, CI-11, CI-12.

**What it genuinely carries:**

- building/service transitions are deliberate, legible and grouped without pretending distinct utility families are one interchangeable system;
- principal isolation and transition geography can remain accessible;
- future service change begins from a known building interface rather than arbitrary buried entries.

**What it does not prove:**

- arbitrary future technology compatibility;
- real utility rules/routes;
- spare universal capacity.

**Synthesis:** useful because it defines a durable interface, not because it predicts future energy or utility technologies.

### Designed Structural Penetration — `established`

**Observed across:** CI-06, CI-07, CI-10, CI-11, CI-13; relevant wherever services cross consequential structure/boundaries.

**What it genuinely carries:**

- consequential crossings are owned and reinstated deliberately;
- services should not create uncontrolled structural/fire/acoustic/pest paths;
- known crossings are interfaces, not casual holes.

**What it does not prove:**

- spare future openings;
- permission for arbitrary new penetrations;
- generic future structural-opening capacity.

**Synthesis:** a strong cross-scenario constraint. Its most important OCG role is preventing serviceability from becoming an excuse for uncontrolled future perforation.

### Source-Capture Kitchen Extract — `established`

**Observed across:** CI-09.

**What it genuinely carries:**

- one deliberate source-to-discharge path;
- replaceable/maintainable fan/filter/control relationships;
- a bounded route rather than residual ducting through the building.

**What it does not prove:**

- actual capture/airflow/acoustic/terminal performance.

**Synthesis:** already contains the relevant maintainability proposition; downstream engineering remains owner proof.

## 2.2 Supported propositions carrying repeated but bounded OCG value

### Coherent Horizontal Service Route — `supported`

**Observed across:** CI-06, CI-07, CI-08, CI-09, CI-10, CI-11, CI-12.

**Genuine contribution:** protects slow fabric from opportunistic primary service routing and provides a legible building-scale path family.

**Repeated limit:** route coherence is not a whole-house accessible plenum and not proof that every branch/circuit can be withdrawn or replaced end to end.

**Synthesis:** high cross-scenario relevance, but OCG-05 must establish actual route continuity, capacity, access and branch segmentation before the project can make stronger renewability claims.

### Accessible Vertical Service Zone — `supported`

**Observed across:** CI-01, CI-06, CI-07, CI-08, CI-10, CI-11, CI-12.

**Genuine contribution:** gives consequential vertical services a controlled, inspectable, non-random geography.

**Repeated limit:** controlled vertical geography is not generic mixed-shaft capacity for every future service and does not prove access to every branch/end condition.

**Synthesis:** important targeted infrastructure whose value appears repeatedly, but whose physical packing and access remain OCG-05 questions.

### High-Service-Room Service Wall — `supported`

**Observed across:** CI-03, CI-06, CI-08 and, where context genuinely applies, CI-09.

**Genuine contribution:** moves high-maintenance components toward a dry/maintainable side and explicitly requires tool/withdrawal clearance rather than nominal access.

**Repeated limit:** component/short-branch access is not whole-branch replaceability; removable access still owes fire/acoustic/water/privacy performance.

**Synthesis:** particularly strong as a local intervention proposition, but must not be rhetorically expanded into universal wet-branch renewability.

### Accessible Room Service Route — `supported`

**Observed across:** CI-07, CI-11.

**Genuine contribution:** provides proportionate local dry-service change through removable architectural layers/joinery instead of routinely chasing permanent quiet fabric.

**Repeated limit:** does not promise arbitrary outlet placement, every wall, every service family or whole-circuit replaceability.

**Synthesis:** current boundedness is a virtue. OCG finds no basis for upgrading it into a continuous technical rack around every room.

### Permanent Opening / Replaceable Window — `supported`

**Observed across:** CI-05.

**Genuine contribution:** durable host opening + replaceable finite-life assembly + deliberate fixing/drainage/air/thermal/movement interface; full-frame replacement is explicitly considered.

**Repeated limit:** actual fixing/withdrawal/tolerance/air-water-thermal-acoustic-security performance is not proven by the pattern.

**Synthesis:** the clearest architectural example of bounded replaceability in the current project. OCG-02’s traditional sash-window precedent reinforces rather than invents this logic.

### Ground-Supported Façade Access — `supported`

**Observed across:** CI-02, CI-05.

**Genuine contribution:** preserves latent approach/support/working/component/waste geography for ordinary temporary external access without requiring permanent technical-display hardware.

**Repeated limit:** real ground bearing, boundaries, neighbour/highway constraints and equipment design are site/delivery facts.

**Synthesis:** useful because it keeps permanent architecture from obstructing ordinary temporary work, not because architecture owns the scaffold/tower/MEWP.

### Failure-Tolerant Wet Service Room — `supported`

**Observed across:** CI-03, CI-08.

**Genuine contribution:** reinforces separation of wet boundaries, failure paths and maintainable service relationships.

**Repeated limit:** actual waterproofing/failure/closure details remain assembly/technical proof.

### Source-Capture Bathroom Extract — `supported`

**Observed across:** CI-08.

**Genuine contribution:** short maintainable bathroom source-to-terminal path with serviceable fan/grille/control logic.

**Repeated limit:** airflow, condensate, acoustic and terminal performance remain technical-owner proof.

### Separate Structural Floor from Changeable Layers Where Proportionate — `supported`

**Observed across:** CI-06, CI-07, CI-11, CI-12.

**Genuine contribution:** slow structure should not be casually consumed by faster layers where useful separation is proportionate.

**Repeated limit:** explicitly does not create universal access flooring; ordinary layered principal-room floors may remain closed.

**Synthesis:** OCG-04 repeatedly relies on its **restraint** as much as its access benefit.

### Compartmented Service Void — `supported`

**Observed across:** CI-11 and other route contexts where boundaries matter.

**Genuine contribution:** service accessibility stops at important boundaries and crosses through controlled closures rather than creating continuous fire/acoustic/pest paths.

**Repeated limit:** actual closure construction and future workmanship remain owner proof.

## 2.3 Strategy/development propositions with conditional OCG value

### Fail-Safe Water Distribution — canonical strategy

**Observed across:** CI-03, CI-06, CI-08, CI-09, CI-12 where water coupling applies.

**Genuine contribution:** hierarchy begins by avoiding concealed vulnerable joints/routes, then selectively uses isolation, access, visible leakage and containment according to risk.

**Repeated limit:** does not select universal manifolds, containment everywhere or independent isolation for every branch/component.

**Synthesis:** a strong guard against OCG overreaction: it already encodes selective rather than maximal protection.

### Manufacturing Strategy — development strategy

**Observed across:** CI-05, CI-09 and potentially other bounded assemblies.

**Genuine contribution:** tolerance translation, controlled interfaces, first-article removal/reinstatement where reversibility is claimed, and off-site work where it materially improves control or removes useful onsite operations.

**Repeated limit:** prefabrication is not automatically superior; factory precision must not create proprietary/tolerance burdens or merely relocate rather than reduce work.

**Synthesis:** supports bounded replaceability but does not authorise a prefabricated-house doctrine.

## 2.4 Low-maturity auxiliary proposition

### Physical Service Index — `proposed`

**Observed across:** CI-07, CI-11; potentially useful to other service interventions.

**Finding across audits:** `UNRESOLVED` as evidence maturity.

**Potential contribution:** reduce discovery uncertainty by linking stable physical identifiers to richer records.

**Limit:** cannot substitute for coherent physical routes; can become stale/misleading; current evidence remains `proposed`.

**Route:** stewardship/information owner proof. OCG-05 may consume only physical/index facts actually represented; it must not rely on hoped-for indexing.

**Synthesis:** preserve as auxiliary, not foundational OCG evidence.

# 3. Repeated boundedness / partial-claim register

Across 04B–D the same limits recur. They are important precisely because they stop the project from overclaiming.

## 3.1 Access is not replacement

Repeated in CI-03, CI-04, CI-05, CI-06, CI-11, CI-12.

A proposition may provide access but still owe:

- hand/tool space;
- disconnection clearance;
- withdrawal direction;
- removal path;
- replacement introduction path;
- technical reclosure;
- return-to-use recovery.

OCG-05 must prove the complete sequence where relevant.

## 3.2 Local component access is not branch/system renewability

Repeated in CI-06, CI-11, CI-12.

Service walls, trunks and plant hubs provide real value without proving:

- every pipe is withdrawable;
- every cable/circuit is replaceable end to end;
- every emitter branch is accessible;
- every future system can reuse incumbent routes.

This is the principal reason IC5 remains more `PARTIAL` than routine local interventions.

## 3.3 Accessible isolation is not universal independent isolation

Repeated in CI-03, CI-06, CI-08, CI-09, CI-11, CI-12.

Current authority selects accessible principal/consequential isolation. It does not select a universal manifold or duplicate-system topology merely to minimise every possible outage.

Outage **scope** remains an OCG-05 topology question; outage **duration** remains substantially task/product/method dependent.

## 3.4 Controlled route capacity is not speculative reserve

Repeated in CI-07, CI-10, CI-11, CI-12, CI-13.

A coherent service route or designed penetration does not imply:

- universal spare capacity;
- spare penetrations;
- arbitrary endpoint flexibility;
- remote wet-room provision;
- future structural-opening capacity.

This limit is central to preserving architectural quality and avoiding generic technicalisation.

## 3.5 Building-side access is not site feasibility

Repeated in CI-01, CI-02, CI-04, CI-05, CI-12.

The site-agnostic house can preserve a coherent building interface and replacement/access route. It cannot prove:

- sewer alignment/access;
- ground bearing;
- neighbour/highway constraints;
- scaffold/tower/MEWP deployment;
- external equipment staging;
- future utility route.

These remain DPL/site proof.

## 3.6 Room refit remains real construction

Repeated in CI-08, CI-09.

A complete bathroom/kitchen refit may intrinsically require:

- room surrender;
- fixture/furniture removal;
- wet work or fabrication;
- curing/commissioning;
- material/waste movement;
- final cleaning/reset.

OCG judges **collateral propagation**, not whether real construction can be magically abolished.

# 4. Conflict / consequence-shift register

## 4.1 No active proposition-level `CONFLICT` was established in OCG-04B/C/D

This is a substantive result, not a declaration that the architecture is solved.

The audits found no case where two currently selected project propositions were proven to require mutually incompatible outcomes at proposition level.

However, recurring consequence-shift risks remain and must be protected in OCG-05 / owner proof.

## 4.2 Access / removability ↔ technical-boundary performance

Relevant to:

- high-service-room access;
- compartmented service voids;
- window replacement interfaces;
- structural/service penetrations.

Potential shift:

> reduce demolition now, but create weaker fire/acoustic/water/air/thermal/security boundaries permanently.

Current patterns acknowledge this risk; actual details remain proof obligations.

**Route:** owner technical proof + OCG-05 occurrence geometry.

## 4.3 Service concentration ↔ permanent area / noise / heat / character

Relevant to:

- plant room;
- vertical service zone;
- service walls;
- shared horizontal routes.

Potential shift:

> reduce future intervention territory, but consume permanent domestic area or create service-room/institutional character.

Current patterns already state area/acoustic/fire/access/domestic-integration forces. OCG-04 finds no contradiction, but OCG-05 must not credit maintenance benefit without respecting actual permanent geometry.

## 4.4 Accessible routes ↔ whole-house technicalisation

Relevant to:

- room-edge service routes;
- floor-layer separation;
- horizontal/vertical service routes.

Potential shift:

> reduce future chasing, but turn quiet walls/floors/rooms into technical racks, plenums or loose access systems.

The project currently controls this by targeted, proportionate selection and by keeping universal lining/floor solutions held.

## 4.5 Replaceable window ↔ envelope robustness / proprietary dependence

Potential shift:

> protect slow masonry from repeated replacement, but introduce extra joints, thermal bridges, seals, subframes or proprietary interfaces.

Pattern and manufacturing strategy already recognise the issue. Actual resolution remains technical proof.

## 4.6 Generic future flexibility ↔ permanent whole-life burden

Relevant to CI-10 and CI-13.

Potential shift:

> make speculative future briefs easier, but carry unused stacks, voids, spare penetrations, demountable structure, access surfaces and technical character forever.

OCG-04 resolves this at proposition level by **not selecting** generic flexibility. The physical consequence of that choice is tested in OCG-05 rather than pre-emptively reversed.

## 4.7 Prefabrication ↔ tolerance / bespoke / site-fit burden

Relevant especially to CI-05 and CI-09.

Potential shift:

> remove occupied-house fabrication, but create site-measurement, fit, tolerance or proprietary-dependency problems.

Manufacturing strategy already requires evidence that off-site preparation actually improves the sequence.

# 5. Deliberate non-provision and accepted-disruption boundaries

These are among the most important OCG-04 results because they protect the project from turning serviceability into maximum flexibility.

## 5.1 CI-10 — remote en-suite

**Current proposition-level state:** deliberately `UNADDRESSED` by permanent remote wet-service infrastructure.

The project previously removed the remote principal en-suite because its soil/waste/water/service topology imposed disproportionate burden.

OCG-04C confirms:

- existing vertical/horizontal service routes remain bounded to selected demand;
- designed penetrations are not spare future holes;
- Local Deep Service Zone, universal manifold, Selective Floor Access and Replaceable Architectural Lining remain held and cannot be imported;
- making CI-10 difficult is not, by itself, evidence that current architecture is deficient.

**Proportionality posture:** candidate for later `NO_CHANGE` / `ACCEPT_DISRUPTION` only after OCG-05 establishes the actual consequence envelope. OCG-04 does **not** assign the final OCG-01D route.

## 5.2 CI-13 — major load-bearing-wall replanning

**Current proposition-level state:** deliberately `UNADDRESSED` by low-disruption generic structural-replanning infrastructure.

Current constitutional posture permits defining structural/spatial relationships to remain slow while changeability is concentrated where it earns itself.

OCG-04D confirms:

- controlled service geography may reduce accidental embedded-service complication;
- Designed Structural Penetration does not provide generic future structural-opening capacity;
- temporary works, demolition, dust/noise/debris and potential decant are intrinsic/method consequences of deliberately altering slow primary structure;
- held/generic flexibility systems cannot be imported to make the control easy.

**Proportionality posture:** candidate for later `ACCEPT_DISRUPTION` / `NO_CHANGE`, not a mandate for demountable structure.

## 5.3 Intrinsic bathroom / kitchen refit consequence

Room surrender and genuine wet/fabrication work during complete CI-08/CI-09 refits are not themselves architecture failures.

Permanent duplicate bathrooms/kitchens, contractor routes or construction infrastructure are not earned merely to eliminate those intrinsic consequences.

## 5.4 Universal isolation/access infrastructure remains unearned

OCG-04 found repeated situations where universal manifolds, access floors, linings or deep service zones **could** make a particular intervention easier.

That is insufficient.

No audit established that their cross-scenario whole-life benefit exceeds their permanent burdens. They remain held.

# 8. Potential doctrine/publication posture

OCG-04 was deliberately designed to be **large internally and small externally**.

The proposition audit supports a strong synthesis, but it does **not** yet earn new canonical reader-facing text.

## 8.1 What appears to survive conceptually

Across the current project authority, OCG research and proposition audits, one coherent cross-cutting idea is visible:

- faster systems should not consume slower fabric casually;
- maintenance/replacement geography includes access, working, withdrawal, transport, reclosure and return to use;
- changeability should be concentrated where credible recurring change earns permanent provision;
- competent temporary works should remain temporary;
- some speculative or slow-fabric changes should remain disruptive rather than forcing permanent generic flexibility.

This is strongly aligned with existing constitutional provisions and active patterns. It does not presently justify a separate large OCG doctrine family.

## 8.2 Why publication prose is still premature

The Reference House has not yet been tortured by the 13 frozen scenarios.

If OCG-05 shows that the house cannot physically realise the existing propositions, publication language would become aspiration masquerading as doctrine.

Therefore OCG-04E records only:

> **A reader-facing occupied-change principle may be earned after OCG-05, but no new doctrine/publication prose is authorised now.**

Any eventual public expression should remain materially smaller than the internal test programme.

# 9. No-new-doctrine / no-design result

Across CI-01…CI-13, OCG-04 does **not** find a missing cross-cutting architectural doctrine family that should be added before Reference House proof.

This does not mean every intervention is solved.

It means the dominant remaining questions are now:

- does the Reference House physically realise selected propositions?;
- are route/access/isolation relationships actually adequate?;
- do technical details preserve permanent performance?;
- are site facts favourable or hostile?;
- which consequences remain appropriately method-owned?;
- are deliberate non-provisions still proportionate once their real physical consequence is visible?

Those questions are exactly what OCG-05 exists to answer.

## 9.1 Held candidates remain held
