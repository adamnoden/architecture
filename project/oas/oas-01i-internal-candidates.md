# OAS-01I — internal door candidates

**Scope:** candidate internal frame/leaf/trim family, not a doorset certification.
**House:** current layout family; 125 mm nominal dry partitions retained.

## Family comparison

| Family | Arrangement | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| I0 ordinary mechanically fixed frame | Supported adjustable frame, serviceable leaf/hardware and independently removable real architrave | Leading control |
| I1 frame on sparse replaceable grounds | Local grounds where wall type/renewal earns them | Selective comparator; no new rack or full cassette |
| I2 fully integrated door cassette | Services, structural support, trim and boundary duties replaced together | Reject as ordinary internal-door default |
| I3 buried-fixing seamless frame | Primary release covered by permanent skim/brittle finish | Reject for the intended repeated frame-renewal task |

A lightweight partition needs its own head/jamb framing and load path. Do not
copy a masonry-window anchorage or claim capacity from its 125 mm drawing width.

## Gross, frame, leaf and clear passage

Current nominal internal apertures are 800 mm at the cloak, 1000 mm at the
principal entry and generally 900 mm elsewhere. These are not selected leaf
widths. For a trial with side installation allowance g and frame occupation t:
outside frame = B − 2g; geometric space between frame sides = B − 2g − 2t.
Leaf stop, hinges, leaf thickness and opening angle may reduce practical passage
further. No escape/accessibility rating follows from this arithmetic.

At hypothetical g = 10 and t = 30, nominal B = 800/900/1000 gives frame-outside
780/880/980 and between-frame 720/820/920. These are sensitivity examples, not
order sizes, proprietary tolerances or approved clear passages.
If the actual task needs more space, change the frame/leaf or explicitly rewind
the source rather than relabel gross width as usable.

The current 2850 mm drawn head likewise does not select a 2850 mm door leaf.
Compare a proportioned door/overpanel or related tall leaf family with actual
mass, hinges and head support. HAD owns its visible rank; OAS owns workable
mechanics. Do not fabricate an immense leaf merely to repeat the aperture line.

## Trial jamb/trim interface

Compare a 125 mm nominal partition with a frame adjusted to the actual finished
wall, not a frame assumed exactly 125 after all tolerances and wet finishes.
Separately releasable architrave covers a designed gap; it does not correct an
unlimited out-of-square opening or serve as the sole acoustic/fire seal.

An illustrative 70 mm-wide by 18 mm-projecting trim is a visible-depth trial.
It is not allocated within the 125 mm wall, nor mandatory on every opening.
Return its ends independently to ordinary skirting. No long sliding piece
needs withdrawal behind a fixed wardrobe.

Support hinges and locks on the frame/grounds, not an access face.
Use ordinary replaceable hardware families where the actual door duty permits;
fixing size, hinge count, load and security are not selected generically.

## Quiet room, cloak and wet/service conditions

- Quiet bedroom/study: privacy, closure and local acoustic duty require the
  actual frame/leaf/seal relationship; no generic dB claim.
- Cloak: actual 800 mm aperture, leaf sweep and stored coats must work together.
  Do not turn a slender symbol into an 800 mm clear carrying route.
- Wet room: frame and trim stay independently renewable without casually
  breaking waterproofing. Wet-side seal/edge/material duties are explicit;
  a timber trim is not a waterproof upstand.
- Plant/service: equipment and working access outrank decorative consistency.
  A removable leaf may assist carrying only if the actual frame clear width,
  route and safe removal procedure make that useful.

No fire/smoke designation is assigned to all doors. Where the actual fire
strategy earns a rated assembly, the complete evidenced doorset, seals,
ironmongery and installation control its use. A custom removable architrave
does not automatically remain compatible. Such use may defeat this ordinary
unrated candidate and require a different complete assembly.

## Removal hierarchy

**Hardware:** operate/isolate as appropriate; support the leaf, release the
actual fitting and replace locally without unfixing the frame.

**Leaf:** support its real mass, release hinges in a safe sequence, protect the
frame/floor and carry on the checked route. Do not remove a leaf assumed to
provide a required boundary while pretending the room remains in normal use.

**Full frame:** remove independently releasable trim; support frame/leaf and
expose fixings; deliberately interrupt only the named perimeter interfaces;
remove/replace the frame; renew actual seals and supports; refit trim and
check plumb, clearances, closure/latch and every boundary duty. Cosmetic flush
redecoration is not the reinstatement test.

The permanent structural head/partition support remains independently in place.
A frame that unexpectedly supports the partition is not safely renewable by
this generic sequence.

## Result and interface handoff

I0 leads, I1 remains local and I2/I3 are rejected defaults. This does not freeze
leaf size, frame section, material, rated assembly or practical clear passage.
The source plan/swing remains held.

IAS-OAS must reconcile skirting, service rises, room linings and wet-floor edges.
OAS-HAD must reconcile visible trim, rank, leaf/overpanel expression and privacy.
OAS-03 then records the selected representative family and its exact claim
boundary; competent product/fire/acoustic/access evidence remains with its owner.
