# OAS ↔ HAD — opening expression junction

**Canonical node:** OAS-HAD-JUNCTION (OAS-02H / HAD-02O).
**Scope:** reconcile actual candidate mechanics with facade/interior expression.

## Shared direction

Use a related sash-derived window family, the retained paired front entrance
with separate fanlight, quieter service openings and a related internal door/
trim family. HAD E-A/P-A/S-A remain the leading architectural directions;
E0/I0 give them the ordinary renewable opening control.

This is contemporary construction within G-01, not reproduction by pane count.
No frame material, operable mechanism, product or performance class is selected
merely because the current drawing has six-over-six strokes.

## Front, garden and court

Retain current opening centres, nominal widths, sill/head datums and the rear
upper central solid pier. A frame/trim trial cannot quietly turn the 1000 mm
source window into a wider aperture to recover daylight after a thick section.
Compare actual sightlines and daylight consequences before product adoption.

Concentrate entrance articulation at the front doorcase. Distinguish permanent
support/head, replaceable doorset and removable decorative surround. A pale
surround is not represented as a stone lintel unless actually designed as one.

The garden/court pair remains a real doorset/threshold, not an uninterrupted
glazed hole. Meeting stiles, hardware, opening sweep and clear passage remain
visible in the eventual selected detail. Keep both court doors and the direct
route; inner-court exposure and maintenance are not inherited from outer windows.

Use the same family of opening proportions and quiet frames in the court,
with HAD's lighter mineral field. Optional ceramic material must stop at a real
repairable edge and must not obstruct sill drainage or replacement trim.
No arcade, false arch or blind rear centre opening is introduced.

## Frame material and ageing

Compare painted timber and supported alternative frame families by their actual
sightline, repair, seals, hardware, weathering and replacement duties.
The earlier slim aluminium comparator stays eligible; no unsupported thermal,
cost, maintenance or lifespan score selects a material.

For subsequent synthesis the architectural preference is a quiet painted,
sash-derived expression. That preference does not prescribe false applied bars,
historical operating technology or all-timber construction. A final material
choice must have an explicit disposition at HAD-02M/03 and OAS-03.

## Room-side trim and head strip

Retain IAS/HAD's quiet skirting and bounded true joinery, with local independent
architrave/reveal release. A nominal 70 mm-wide, 18 mm-projecting internal trim
is an appearance trial only. It is not spare volume inside a wall.

The 250 mm head-to-ceiling strip controls upper articulation. HAD's trial
100 mm cornice downstand plus 75 mm above-aperture trim leaves 75 mm visible
separation; it proves neither product fit nor fixing/tool space.
Prefer simpler termination over a crowded technical cover masquerading as
classical abundance.

A tall aperture does not require an enormous single leaf. Door/overpanel and
fanlight relationships must preserve support, operating mass and renewal.
The future selected internal family must deliberately choose the expression,
not inherit a 2850 mm leaf from a symbol.

## Privacy, shade, controls and hardware

Front WC privacy is required by the room use. Keep obscured glazing and an
operable shutter/privacy arrangement as explicit alternatives until actual
light, handle, cleaning and release consequences are compared. No product
has been selected by the earlier owner's privacy intent.

Do not place a door-side rise, switch or blind support in the frame's release/
fixing/air-water zone. Local control should be reachable after normal furnishing.
Touch hardware may use functional brass/bronze; it does not extend to a
decorative metal grid across every perimeter seam.

## Threshold expression and failure honesty

An apparently continuous floor cannot erase a real external threshold,
collector or upstand. HAD accepts that a technically earned sill/drain/overflow
may remain visible and composed. OAS must provide the actual level/water section,
not ask HAD to hide an unspecified solution.

No 50 mm drawing mark becomes a selected step. General −150 exterior ground
and 0 entrance do not select courtyard/garden paving. A low/level access target
is an intended duty, not proven weather performance or statutory acceptance.

## Result and residual owners

Candidate opening expression now agrees with actual support/release/boundary
intent and IAS depth costs. No facade/aperture/plan change or performance
claim occurs.

OAS-03 owns the selected opening topology, replacement record and bounded
details. HAD-02M/03 owns final material/room/visible synthesis. Source changes
remain explicit; unknown actual products, threshold levels and competent
performance remain visible to the selected-house and MRA/BCR gates.
