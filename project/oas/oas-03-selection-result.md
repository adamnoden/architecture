## Comparison and red team

The conventional control wins because no current task earns the extra retained
perimeter, moisture interfaces and supplier dependence of a universal cassette.
Its weakness remains real: full-frame renewal interrupts seals and may require
external handling. That consequence is included in the selected operation,
not hidden by calling the frame removable.

A false sense of completion was specifically rejected:
nominal apertures are not clear passage; sensitivity gaps are not fabrication
tolerances; a 50 mm elevation mark is not a threshold; the drawing's inner-leaf
overlap is not an anchor calculation; and a schematic water arrow is not a
failed-seal/blocked-drain performance result.
