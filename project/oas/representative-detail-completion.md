threshold at 1:5, plus ordinary internal-door jamb/trim at 1:2. The source
record distinguishes retained current datums from new study dimensions,
installation examples from manufacturer tolerances, and reserves from actual

sill tray and stone discharge, threshold level/collector and internal frame/
trim geometry. They retain the distinct courtyard/garden threshold obligation.
No whole-house model, facade, room, product or performance claim is changed.
