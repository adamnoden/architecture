# OAS-01E — external opening and threshold candidates

**Scope:** architectural assembly candidates, not installed-product approval.
**House:** RH-LAYOUT-REVISION-02 / RH-LAYOUT-FAMILY-02.
**Inputs:** OAS-00, the shared interface contract and IAS/HAD candidate handoffs.

## Comparison and disposition

| Family | What remains when the frame is exchanged | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| E0 conventional mechanically fixed frame with independent trim | Masonry, structural head, deliberately separable building-side water/air transitions where their detail permits | Leading control; replacement must include deliberate renewal of interrupted transitions |
| E1 sparse durable mounting grounds/brackets | Local supports if sound and compatible; replaceable frame and accessible fixings | Retain where the actual support/renewal task earns them; not a new universal rail |
| E2 continuous retained cassette/subframe | Continuous adapter plus extra perimeter joints | Challenger only; reject as a house-wide presumption |
| E3 frame sealed directly into monolithic room finish | Nothing reliably releasable without cutting the room finish | Reject as the intended full-frame renewal interface |

Conventional does not mean proven. E0 must still show support, packing, adjustment,
water behaviour, release and reinstatement. E2 does not win merely by giving a
part a longer name: its own eventual renewal, stagnant pockets, thermal bridges,
tolerance stack and supplier replacement must improve on E0.

## Dimension meanings and a coordination trial

Keep current host, centre, nominal width and vertical aperture datums. A nominal
1000 x 2100 window is not simultaneously a masonry tolerance opening, frame
outside, visible glazing and clear escape opening.

Use **g**, the required installation/adjustment allowance each side, as a design
input, not a fabricated manufacturer tolerance. For a symmetrical comparison:
frame outside width = B − 2g; outside height = H − 2g.
At the explicitly hypothetical sensitivity point g = 15, B = 1000 and H = 2100,
the frame is 970 x 2070. g = 10/20 instead gives 980/960 width and 2080/2060 height.
These are arithmetic trials, not selected fabrication sizes or permitted gaps.
Actual aperture tolerance, head support, packers, anchors and boundary products
must determine the adopted relationship.

A second trial places a 140 mm-deep frame between depth coordinates 220 and
360 mm, measured inward from the weather face. The current leaves are:
weather 0–102.5; cavity 102.5–262.5; inner structure 262.5–402.5; finish to 417.5.
This gives 97.5 mm nominal overlap with the inner structural zone and 57.5 mm
room-side reveal. It does **not** prove anchor edge distance, load transfer,
insulated closure or thermal performance. The alternative is an outer-set
frame with a separately designed mounting/closure relationship; neither
position may borrow support from a sill line or a strip of decorative trim.

The current 60 mm drawn frame face, 80 mm sill projection and 120 mm head profile
remain representation choices. No profile becomes fabrication authority through
these trial dimensions. Coordinate sightlines and room depth before selection.

## Head, jamb and sill duties together

| Interface | Intended primary path/duty | Failure / replacement consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Structural head | Independently designed support of permanent opening | Frame release must not remove masonry support; the drawn 200 mm zone is not a lintel design |
| Head/cavity drainage | Building-side water intercepted and discharged outward | Local perimeter seal failure must not send cavity water into the inner reveal; exact tray/ends/outlet detail remains evidence work |
| Jamb | Supported adjustable fixing and independent boundary transitions | Avoid tracking fixings and sealed pockets; replaceable cover is not the air/water layer |
| Frame/sill | Frame drainage to an outward sill with real fall/drip | Blocked drainage must have an admitted consequence; no promised secondary path without a section |
| Sill/wall base | Outward discharge clear of vulnerable interfaces | No blind pan whose only outlet is behind plaster; renew disturbed seals/closures deliberately |
| Room-side reveal | Independent finish termination and accessible first release | Remove local trim without cutting the slow wall; renew actual disturbed air/vapour/acoustic transition |

This specifies responsibilities and physical direction, not a spray-test result.
A secondary route is only credited when actually drawn/sized for the selected
assembly. Do not label an unspecified conventional seal as moisture resilient.

## Outer and courtyard windows

Use related sash-derived visible families for the public body and court, but
compare real operating mechanisms and maintainability rather than requiring
a six-over-six product by name. Operating sash, glazing, seals and hardware
maintenance are distinct from full-frame exchange.

First release should be reachable from the intended occupied/service side.
Support sash/glazing before release; protect the sill/room; remove the local
trim; expose mechanical fixings and boundary transitions; support the frame;
renew the frame and interrupted transitions; test operation, alignment, drainage
and actual seals. Retaining one sound frame for sash renewal is a separate task.

Full-frame inside removal is desirable where actual geometry permits, not a
universal carrying pass. The frame, tools, handling/guarding and onward door
route must be checked. Courtyard exposure, high-level access and the two-storey
court's maintenance geography need their own applicability check. No new roof
opening or attic route is introduced to make window work appear easy.

## External doors and thresholds

Five current external doors have different tasks: front entry, plant replacement,
garden and both courtyard doors. Treat doorset, frame, sill/threshold, interior
floor, exterior levels and water collection as one interface.

The front's 1800 mm nominal width does not establish clear passage. The 1400 mm
plant aperture does not prove an actual plant item fits. The 2400 mm garden/
court pair is not 2400 mm clear with hardware/stiles in place. State leaf-open
projection, stops, hinges, meeting stile and the actual handling configuration.

Keep the paired front-door/separate-fanlight architectural intent, but design
its frame/head support and release independently of decorative surround.
Do not transfer an internal-door trial to an external doorset.

### Three threshold directions

1. **Conventional drained doorset:** credible weather/renewal control; any real
   upstand must be explicit and assessed against passage/access duties.
2. **Low/level-access drained assembly:** retain as a candidate for the intended
   route, with complete internal/external level section, collection and overflow.
   A tiny threshold line is not proof of water performance.
3. **Flush cosmetic continuation:** reject if paving and interior are simply
   drawn as one plane with no physical water-management answer.

No courtyard/garden exterior level or drain/outfall is selected. General drawn
ground −150 and entrance 0 do not resolve these four other thresholds.
The existing sill +50 drawing mark is not an adopted 50 mm step.
Actual water levels, falls, drain/trap, weather exposure and accessible use must
be reconciled before a final threshold is claimed.

## Failure and carrying tests to retain

Test one failed perimeter seal, blocked frame weep, water tracking on a fixing,
a restricted threshold collector, and a removed frame during adverse weather.
Identify first vulnerable fabric, outward/visible path, temporary protection,
isolation where applicable and drying. MRA later assaults the selected house;
this candidate does not award itself a water pass.

A retained E1/E2 interface must also survive its own replacement. If it makes
eventual repair destructive or supplier-dependent, simplify back to E0.

## Result

E0 leads, E1 remains selective, E2 must earn itself and E3 is rejected.
Conventional drained and level-access external thresholds remain distinct
candidates; no cosmetic flush answer is accepted. Current geometry is unchanged.

IAS/OAS and OAS/HAD junctions reconcile actual room-side depth, trim, services
and appearance next. OAS-03 must select or explicitly bound the representative
family with a real replacement/reinstatement record. Product, structural,
water/air/thermal/security/fire and carrying evidence remain concrete obligations
for BCR/EPR/PHY/DEL where earned, not invented generic approvals.
