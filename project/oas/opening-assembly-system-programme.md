# OAS — Opening Assembly System

**State:** queued; starts after BSR establishes canonical chapter/authority boundaries  
**Tracking:** [#942 — OAS opening assembly system](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/942)  
**Programme map:** [`../book-resolution-programme.md`](../book-resolution-programme.md)

## Question and scope

How should the Reference House separate enduring architectural openings from renewable frames, seals, hardware and doors/windows while maintaining weather, thermal, air, acoustic, security and movement obligations?

OAS owns the Reference House opening/cassette assembly family and its architectural replacement logic. It does not own facade/room-wide composition, competent engineering/building-physics judgement, physical proof or delivery responsibility.

## Work packets

- **OAS-00 — opening taxonomy and baselines:** classify window, external-door, internal-door and threshold conditions; compare against excellent mechanically fixed conventional assemblies.
- **OAS-01 — permanent opening and mounting zone:** masonry/structure, durable subframe/cassette candidates, tolerances, adjustment and accessible restraint.
- **OAS-02 — environmental boundary details:** jamb/head/sill drainage, weathering, air/thermal/vapour continuity and replaceable seals at the appropriate design-evidence level.
- **OAS-03 — architectural trim interface:** removable reveals/architraves/beads, relationship to IAS wall/skirting systems and facade/interior expression handoff to HAD.
- **OAS-04 — door and threshold family:** external/internal doors, threshold wear/water/accessibility, hardware and replacement logic.
- **OAS-05 — replacement/reinstatement set:** sash/glazing/frame/door replacement sequences, selected inside-removal logic and boundary reinstatement.
- **OAS-06 — selection gate:** reject/hold weak or unnecessarily proprietary candidates and issue the bounded family to BCR.

## Parallel interfaces

OAS runs in parallel with IAS #941 and HAD #943 after BSR. It uses the controlled current Reference House/PLT opening geometry. Any required change to opening dimensions, wall build-up, facade order or plan/section is an explicit rewind request, never a silent fork.

IAS owns the room-side assembly meeting the opening. HAD owns facade/opening composition and visible material/trim language.

## Evidence boundary

OAS may establish architectural detail logic, dimensions, sequence and internally controlled boundary intentions. Certified product performance, structural adequacy, thermal/moisture calculations, fire/security compliance and physical water/air testing remain external/specialist evidence routes under EPR/PHY/BCR/DEL.

## Exit gate

OAS closes when representative window, external-door, internal-door and threshold families are coordinated; replacement and reinstatement sequences are drawable; interfaces with IAS/HAD/PLT are explicit; and unresolved performance claims have named downstream evidence routes.

Closure does not establish regulatory approval, certification or physical performance.
