## North-window stay travel counterexample — 3 November 2026

The [OAS-NORTH-EXPORT generation-3 result](north-export-occurrence-result.md#recorded-20-in-travel-incompatibility--generation-3-3-november-2026) separates source-bound ordinary sash motion from a **matched actual stay**. Under the previously authored pivot centres, the named AXA 20 IN range reaches **294.837 mm at 80°**, beyond its recorded **286 mm** maximum. A 70° alternative fits this ideal length test with only **4.697 mm nominal reserve**, not installation tolerance or first-release acceptance. The first 4.820° also **contracts** to 223.578 mm, only **1.578 mm** above the documented 222 mm lower travel limit. That coupled lower/upper travel vulnerability needs a verified product/pivot rather than another nominal positive envelope. Rather than import eight experimental, repetitive 70° scripts as a product claim, a compact pinned kinematic regression is now in canonical CI. The [final disposition](north-export-occurrence-result.md#final-disposition--5-november-2026) rejects this developed configuration: the finished frame's 95 mm intrusion exceeds IAS's 75 mm requirement, the recorded mechanism does not match 80° operation, and the named fitting retains synthetic constituents without an exception. The bounded rejection is complete; IAS-03 may compare maintenance routes or request an owning interface redesign. It receives no positive opening or export proof.
