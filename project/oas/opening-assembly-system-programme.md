## Question and scope

How should the Reference House separate enduring architectural openings from renewable frames, seals, hardware and doors/windows while maintaining weather, thermal, air, acoustic, security and movement obligations?

OAS owns the Reference House opening/cassette assembly family and its architectural replacement logic. It does not own facade/room-wide composition, competent engineering/building-physics judgement, physical proof or delivery responsibility.

## Work packets and shared gates

The [canonical graph](../control/nodes/architecture.ts) owns lifecycle and
dependencies; [project control](../control/README.md) owns live claims.
Issue #942 records the authorised parallel topology, superseding OAS-01…06.

- **OAS-00:** [current opening taxonomy, controls and invariants](oas-00-baseline-and-invariants.md).
- **TRIAD-IFACE-FREEZE:** shared IAS/OAS/HAD contract after the three baselines.
- **OAS-01E:** envelope windows, external doors and thresholds together, including
  mounting, drainage/weathering and air/thermal/vapour boundary candidates.
- **OAS-01I:** independent internal-door candidates with use-specific duties.
- **IAS-OAS-JUNCTION / OAS-HAD-JUNCTION:** room-side and visible architectural
  interfaces reconciled against actual core candidates.
- **OAS-03:** selected family, replacement/reinstatement and canonical drawings.
- **TRIAD-GATE:** one coordinated house before selected BCR input.

OAS-00 records 44 nominal window and 19 door occurrences across the two current
occupied levels. Five doors have external threshold duties. The conventional
mechanically fixed installation remains the comparison control; added subframes
and cassettes must earn their burden. No geometry or performance claim changes.
BSR is complete; deep candidate work follows the shared interface freeze,
which still requires HAD-00.

## Parallel interfaces

OAS runs in parallel with IAS #941 and HAD #943 after BSR. It uses the controlled current Reference House/PLT opening geometry. Any required change to opening dimensions, wall build-up, facade order or plan/section is an explicit rewind request, never a silent fork.

IAS owns the room-side assembly meeting the opening. HAD owns facade/opening composition and visible material/trim language.

## Evidence boundary

OAS may establish architectural detail logic, dimensions, sequence and internally controlled boundary intentions. Certified product performance, structural adequacy, thermal/moisture calculations, fire/security compliance and physical water/air testing remain external/specialist evidence routes under EPR/PHY/BCR/DEL.

## Exit gate

OAS closes when representative window, external-door, internal-door and threshold families are coordinated; replacement and reinstatement sequences are drawable; interfaces with IAS/HAD/PLT are explicit; unresolved performance claims have named downstream evidence routes; and the
shared TRIAD-GATE establishes one coordinated house with IAS/HAD.

Closure does not establish regulatory approval, certification or physical performance.
