# IAS ↔ OAS — opening/interior junction

**Canonical node:** IAS-OAS-JUNCTION (IAS-02O / OAS-02I).
**Scope:** coordinated candidate interface, not selected product performance.
**Inputs:** OAS-01E/01I, IAS-01V/01H/02S/02W and the shared interface contract.

## Agreed assembly order

Permanent opening/partition support → designed mounting and boundary transitions
→ replaceable frame/doorset → independently removable room-side trim/lining.
The faster room face neither supports the frame nor becomes the primary
environmental or safety boundary by covering it.

The wall candidate remains V0/V1 for ordinary surfaces, V3 at actual service
access and V2 only when its extra depth is explicitly adopted. The floor remains
H0 as control. OAS E0/I0 can meet those families without a universal cassette.

## Worked depth checks

For the OAS frame-location trial 220–360, the current finished inside face 417.5
leaves 57.5 mm room-side reveal in section. A decorative lining cannot silently
occupy that space while retaining all the same opening/finished-face claims.
V2's 30 mm extra depth would change that relationship; a candidate V2 reveal
needs its own explicit section.

An 18 mm trim is a projection beyond the actual finished wall, not part of the
125 mm partition by default. Service skirting's 45 mm trial and ordinary 18 mm
control remain real projections. The west gallery does not recover its nominal
1015 mm width by calling projecting trim part of the wall datum.

At the 2850 opening head beneath 3100 ceiling, only 250 mm nominal remains.
A head cover, cornice, fixing access and movement allowance must coexist there.
If actual products cannot fit, simplify trim or explicitly rewind; never
reduce a required boundary or movement allowance invisibly.

## First release and replacement

For frame renewal: clear/support the working field; release the short local
skirting end where it genuinely overlaps; remove independent architrave/reveal
cover; expose fixings and the named perimeter boundary; support sash/leaf/frame;
renew the assembly and interrupted transitions; test boundaries and operation;
refit room-side finishes. No whole floor, wet face or adjacent room is assumed
to come apart for an ordinary frame change.

For routine service access, V3's face releases independently of opening trim.
At I00-C1 do not enlarge an architrave to conceal the old overlapping face.
The shifted trial and unresolved section to the upper lining stay IAS adoption
obligations, not a solved opening junction.

Avoid trapping the first release behind a fixed kitchen, wardrobe or plant.
If a removal needs temporary boundary loss or guarded working at height,
name that maintenance state rather than presenting ordinary occupation as
continuing unchanged.

## Boundary reinstatement

Record which perimeter transition is sacrificial and renewed, which retained
part is inspected and when replacement is mandatory. Trim overlap is never
the test for air/water/fire/acoustic reinstatement. DEL's eventual records
must follow actual selected materials and required acceptance tests.
No particular tape, foam, sealant or frame gasket is specified by this junction.

## Result

IAS and OAS agree the candidate edge, support, release and boundary ownership.
No silent wall/floor/aperture changes are needed to express the intended family.
This does not prove an actual V2 reveal, rated doorset, wet threshold or
component carrying operation. IAS-03/OAS-03 must retain those actual product/
source dispositions; MRA/BCR assess the integrated selected house.
