## Basis and use

The owner-confirmed 12 × 12 m Reference House and [shared interface contract](../triad-interface-contract.md) define the working study. Follow the current owning source for dimensions; this matrix does not duplicate their budgets. IAS has a bounded dry selection with wet/service holds; OAS has an architectural family and nominal interface studies; HAD has partial furnished studies. These are not an integrated selected house. HRS-BASELINE must determine historical-evidence applicability before the early physical studies consume it. This scaffold does not anticipate that result.

Every row below is **routed-pending**, with **no closure evidence yet recorded by BCR**. The evidence named in “Starting evidence” establishes the question, not its resolution. Routes nominate repository/design functions, not appointments or authority to contact specialists. A proposed evidence route is not a newly imposed universal test, a new workstream or an earned design pass.

Classification: **A** architectural/detail; **RW** roof/rainwater system; **M** moisture assurance; **T** technical/specialist; **P** physical evidence; **D** delivery/procurement; **S** real-site/applicability. Multiple classes identify interfaces; each row names a primary design/decision owner. P is conditional where an actual selected proposition warrants a test. Routine conventional work does not acquire a bespoke prototype merely by appearing here.

The “Next packet” is the preparation/integration route, **not permission to execute outside the typed DAG or a substitute for a lease**. A study may finish with an adverse result. Its physical/design obligation remains open until it meets the programme's exit rule. Missing site facts can be explicitly bounded for a pre-site study; unresolved internal fit, route, topology or safety dependencies cannot.

## Moisture traceability without a second threat register

[MRA-00](../mra/mra-00-moisture-constitution-threat-model.md) owns its threat vocabulary, source/path/failure schema and scenario identifiers. The rows above reference those identities to avoid reducing moisture to one generic line. This initial routing covers EXT-01–10, INT-01–04, WST-01–04, VAP-01–04, CON-01/02 and CMP-01–05; ground/capillary sources enter BCR-016 and site-specific exposure BCR-002. These are inventory links, not findings that those scenarios pass or fail. MRA-00 remains subject to its own completion sweep.

When MRA-04 produces findings, BCR-CONVERGENCE must append the exact finding/source revision and receiving row (or a new warranted row), record its design correction and disposition, and link closure evidence. A finding spanning rows retains one MRA identity. No material finding disappears merely because it was assigned to a designer or future test.

## Controlled update and closure

BCR-PREFLIGHT alone integrates STRUCTURE/WET/SERVICES/WATER/OCCUPATION results into this shared matrix. Each parallel study writes its own result and proposed rows, citing these BCR IDs, exact source revision, tested relationship, evidence and limitation. It must supply the physical study required by the programme, not simply repeat this inventory. Final BCR-CONVERGENCE consumes proportionality, selected-source deltas, MRA-04, HRS-TECHNICAL, EPR-REFRESH and PHY-APPLICABILITY; recheck only evidence affected by changes, but do not carry a stale pass forward.

For every subsequent disposition record: row ID; affected object/revision; evidence and scope; current owner; design consequence; disposition; remaining trigger/owner; dependent outputs reconciled. Use only the programme's meanings: **resolved at stated scope**, **eliminated**, **bounded residual**, or **routed-pending / blocked**. If eliminated, link the adopted removal/simplification. A bounded residual must explicitly explain why it conceals no unresolved internal fit, route, topology or safety dependency and state the later evidence trigger. Missing engineering approval is never rewritten as technical adequacy.

Keep original source/closed evidence identity when a row is revised or split. New issues or prototype families require a concrete gap not already covered by the existing EPR/PHY/DEL/DPL routes; this scaffold creates none. External contact, tests, purchases and appointments are outside this inventory's authority.
