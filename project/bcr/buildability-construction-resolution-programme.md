# BCR — Buildability & Construction Resolution

**State:** early current-house feasibility and proportionality studies; final convergence follows selected-house integration, current technical applicability and MRA-04

**Tracking:** [#944 — BCR buildability and construction resolution](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/944)  
**Programme map:** [`../book-resolution-programme.md`](../book-resolution-programme.md)

## Question and scope

What remains between the coordinated architectural design and a house that a competent appointed team could carry through detailed design and construction, who owns each answer, and have material findings been reconciled back into the architecture?

BCR owns construction-resolution coordination and evidence routing. It does not replace ROF/RWT architectural system selection, MRA whole-house moisture assurance, EPR professional judgement, PHY physical proof, DEL responsibility/stage control, statutory approval or real-site deployment.

## Early and final scope

The accepted 12 × 12 m house is the working architectural basis. Early BCR work tests its physical relationships against current candidate systems; it does not reopen the brief, select architecture or wait for finished IAS/HAD/ROF/RWT outputs. Findings return to the existing design owners before integration. Final convergence rechecks the adopted result rather than treating a successful early study as permanent assurance.

## Ground and boundary-service construction obligations — 31 October 2026

Carry the owner's [P0 structural ground brief](../str/ground-movement-and-substructure-brief.md) and [Boundary Service Lodge / accessible raceway candidate](../had/boundary-service-lodge-and-raceway-brief.md) into the **existing** BCR obligation matrix, not a parallel task register. Require buildable tree/root-sensitive foundation and utility operations, verified concrete/pile/void workmanship and inspection holds, safe service-lodge/cover access and lifting, labelled independent house and boundary isolation, movement-tolerant supports/penetrations, supplier-approved meter compartments, frost/ventilation/electrical segregation and demonstrable buried-to-accessible transitions. Carry site-dependent choices explicitly to DPL/DEL rather than guessing connection locations and sewer levels. These are new design obligations, not currently resolved construction details.

MRA supplies the deep moisture-domain source/path/failure register rather than moisture being reduced to one generic row.
- **BCR-01 — ownership classification:** classify each gap as architectural/detail, ROF/RWT system resolution, moisture-assurance, technical-specialist, physical-test, delivery/procurement, or real-site/jurisdiction dependent.
- **BCR-02 — specialist/physical routing:** issue or update the relevant EPR/PHY/DEL routes only where the selected design, roof/water system or MRA failure review creates a concrete question.
- **BCR-03 — reconcile findings:** return material consequences to IAS/OAS/HAD/ROF/RWT/RH/PLT rather than stacking downstream workarounds; MRA-originated obligations remain traceable through the correction.
- **BCR-04 — construction-readiness boundary:** establish what the virtual Reference House can legitimately claim and what remains impossible without a real site/commission.
- **BCR-05 — manuscript handoff:** provide the evidence/disposition basis for Part V, **Technical Closure and Construction Readiness**.

## Early feasibility packets

`BCR-SCAFFOLD` supplies the [initial construction-resolution matrix](construction-resolution-matrix.md) and [scaffold result](bcr-scaffold-result.md), including ownership classification and explicit discharge conditions. The inventory is not technical resolution. Each study below depends on that scaffold and `HRS-BASELINE`; the six studies can then run independently. Each study owns a separate concise result with proposed finding rows. BCR-PREFLIGHT alone integrates the five physical studies into the shared matrix; final BCR integrates proportionality. Parallel study agents must not mutate the shared matrix.

| Node | Bounded question and required output | Design/evidence route |
|---|---|---|
| `BCR-STRUCTURE` | Show current support/load paths, representative floor section and stair/entrance-void framing reservations. Check geometry, headroom and the consequences of plausible member/support envelopes; distinguish nominal zones from verified capacity. | Reference House/HAD; IAS for layers; targeted EPR/PHY where competent or physical evidence is necessary |
| `BCR-WET` | Show representative fixture/door/approach fit, complete wet-floor section, drain/trap/branch-to-stack route and isolate/service/dry sequence. Test actual layer budgets and the compact en-suite/access-front conflicts. | IAS/HAD; OAS at thresholds; MRA for failure assurance; BCR/EPR for technical evidence |
| `BCR-SERVICES` | Test plant and riser packing, representative horizontal/vertical distribution and crossings, segregation/support allowances, maintenance and replacement routes against declared duty/component envelopes. No historical service-zone result substitutes for current fit. | Reference House/IAS/HAD; targeted MEP/acoustic evidence through EPR; DEL for installation/reinstatement |
| `BCR-WATER` | Trace normal water, blockage/exceedance and maintenance routes from current roof/court catchments to the building-side handoff. Compare candidate roof/water consequences and threshold interfaces without choosing the ROF/RWT answer. | ROF/RWT/HAD; OAS at thresholds; MRA challenge; targeted EPR/PHY/DEL |
| `BCR-OCCUPATION` | Test complete representative actions: entry, WC/shower use, stair/bedroom access, door/window operation and large-component renewal. Record finished clearances, void/guard/headroom and escape assumptions; a walking grid alone is insufficient. | HAD/OAS/IAS; existing human-factors evidence; EPR for competent/regulatory judgement |
| `BCR-PROPORTIONALITY` | Compare materially unusual provisions with credible conventional controls using spatial/depth, material, installation, maintenance/replacement, damage/reinstatement, supply/tool and owner-attention burdens. Use evidence-backed quantities/ranges; record uncertainty instead of inventing lifecycle savings. | Existing design owner decides retain/simplify/reject; BCR records comparison and final disposition; DEL carries delivery consequences |

Each study completes when it names its current source, provides the stated physical study, identifies tested relationships and explicit limits, and records every material conflict or evidence need in its own result for serial integration into the shared matrix with its owner and discharge condition. An adverse result is a valid completed investigation. It is not a passed design. A blank study or generic referral does not complete the packet. Component envelopes or calculation assumptions must have a declared basis; unknown performance remains unknown.

The [occupation study](bcr-occupation-result.md) records the accepted-source bedroom-to-en-suite route obstruction, guest-WC door approach, stair opening/guard obligations and complete entry, opening and renewal sequences. Its adverse findings remain pending design resolution; completion of the study does not establish usable sanitary fit, safe escape or construction readiness.

The [structure study](bcr-structure-result.md) compares source-bound floor/support geometry, entrance-void frames and stair-opening alternatives. It identifies doorway-bearing and bridge/service crossings without selecting members, capacity or a source aperture; its findings remain pending design resolution.

The [wet feasibility study](bcr-wet-result.md) tests source sanitary/service reservations, tiling-layer versus qualified water-boundary scope, tray/drain datums and connected cabinet routes. Its physical and product-qualification holds remain pending design resolution; it selects no sanitary assembly or source rewind.

`BCR-PREFLIGHT` consumes the five physical studies and reconciles their shared spatial budgets and interfaces. It publishes the constraints and unresolved findings that `TRIAD-GATE` must consume. It can finish with explicit adverse findings, but cannot call an incompatible candidate feasible. TRIAD cannot adopt the affected occurrence until its design owner resolves/eliminates the material conflict or establishes a legitimate bounded residual under the exit rule below. This separates investigation completion from architectural acceptance without creating a cycle through finished design streams.

`BCR-PROPORTIONALITY` feeds final convergence. Its early comparison must be updated for material selection changes. Unknown cost alone does not make every design impossible, but a claimed whole-life advantage cannot survive without support. Carbon comparisons are included where the available quantities and evidence support them.

The [physical preflight result](bcr-preflight-result.md) integrates all 35 findings from the five physical studies into the shared matrix, reconciles their common floor/bridge/wet/service/court budgets, and supplies explicit architectural admission constraints and affected-change rechecks. Every material physical finding remains routed-pending / blocked at its design claim. Completion earns preparation of current review/prototype applicability and the bounded construction-bay brief; it does not admit an incompatible candidate or close BCR.

## Dependency gate

`BCR-SCAFFOLD` may run while IAS/OAS/HAD, ROF/RWT and MRA-00 are in progress. The six early studies require `BCR-SCAFFOLD` and `HRS-BASELINE`, not finished selected-system gates. `BCR-PREFLIGHT` is an input to `TRIAD-GATE`.

Substantive `BCR-CONVERGENCE` begins only after:

1. the selected IAS/OAS/HAD outputs have passed `TRIAD-GATE`, including `ROF-RWT-GATE` and explicit treatment of BCR-PREFLIGHT findings;
2. `BCR-PROPORTIONALITY` supplies the conventional comparisons;
3. `HRS-TECHNICAL` supplies current-house computational support and explicit unknown/applicability boundaries; and
4. MRA-01/02/03 have been composed into `MRA-04`, so the integrated house has been adversarially attacked for external water, internal water, vapour/condensation and drying failure.

The roof/water gate ensures that final convergence receives a selected architectural system rather than a provisional PLT roof and symbolic drainage diagram. Recheck all five physical studies against adopted changes; repeat only affected evidence where applicability is established.

## Agent and external-evidence boundary

Scaffolding, source-applicability checks, bounded feasibility studies, conventional comparisons, brief/test preparation and processing supplied evidence are self-service work. Competent independent review, physical builds/tests, real-site evidence acquisition and new external commitments remain with their existing manual owners. Admit a hard EPR/PHY/site dependency only for a concrete material obligation that cannot honestly be resolved or eliminated within available evidence. An unresolved referral is not evidence; neither is the absence of a full professional design package a blanket blocker to bounded virtual-design work.

## Roof/water technical obligations expected downstream

ROF/RWT selection may expose, without resolving prematurely:

- roof structural support/load/wind questions;
- covering/substrate/fixing manufacturer or specialist constraints;
- condensation/thermal bridge/air-vapour continuity questions;
- movement and metal/material compatibility questions;
- outlet/downpipe capacity assumptions needing competent confirmation;
- overflow/exceedance details;
- roof access/fall-protection/maintenance requirements;
- courtyard/threshold/wall-base technical coordination;
- site-water handoff requirements that remain DPL/site dependent;
- workmanship, inspection and commissioning requirements for DEL.

BCR owns disposition/routing of these once the architecture has selected them; it does not choose the preferred roof or rainwater system by technical attrition.

## Rewind rule

Classify before adding complexity:

1. detail problem → local owning design stream;
2. roof/rainwater system problem → ROF/RWT;
3. broader architectural problem → Reference House/HAD/PLT rewind;
4. moisture-assurance problem → MRA source/path/failure model, then the owning design/evidence stream;
5. evidence problem → EPR/PHY/DEL/DPL or explicit site/commission deferral.

A specialist finding may constrain architecture but may not silently redesign it by attrition.

## Exit gate

Every material construction-resolution obligation must have a current-house applicability decision, owning authority, evidence and one of these dispositions:

- **Resolved at stated scope:** the discharge condition is met by evidence applicable to the adopted design.
- **Eliminated:** an adopted simplification or source rewind removes the obligation; affected outputs and evidence are reconciled.
- **Bounded residual:** the unearned claim is expressly withheld and the remaining uncertainty does not conceal an unresolved architectural fit, route, topology or safety dependency. State the later trigger and evidence owner.
- **Routed-pending / blocked:** a question has an owner or future test but its discharge condition is unmet. This is not resolution. A material dependency remains blocking until evidence is processed or a substantiated fallback is adopted.

BCR closes only when material ROF/RWT and MRA-04 findings satisfy the first three dispositions, the conventional comparisons have current decisions, applicable EPR/PHY/DEL evidence has been reconciled, and the five physical studies agree with the adopted house. It must be stable enough for MRA-05 and HRS without calling unresolved physical relationships completed architecture. Moving an obligation to another programme does not discharge it.

Closure does not claim planning/statutory approval, real-site suitability, complete engineering certification or M5 external validation.

Current BCR convergence also requires `EPR-REFRESH` and `PHY-APPLICABILITY`: current-revision briefs and protocol applicability must be reconciled before technical scope is represented as coherent. These are preparation gates, not substitutes for actual external evidence. The typed graph owns the full dependency list.


## Owner-house construction-bay demonstration

The [DEL route](../del/owner-house-delivery-plan.md) adds a concrete demonstration alongside existing whole-house feasibility and convergence. It does not replace them or gate book release.

**BCR-BAY-BRIEF** follows DEL-BRIEF, HRS-BASELINE and BCR-PREFLIGHT. Select one consequential, bounded bay (default candidate: an exterior window with adjoining wall, floor edge and service zone) and explain the choice. Reuse OAS interface studies and existing IAS/PHY evidence rather than inventing a new family. Record `project/bcr/bay-brief-result.md`: exact current source/extent, conventional comparator, occupation/installation/replacement questions, dimensional budgets, required drawings, technical evidence and named design owners. Exit: ADE and a competent reviewer can identify what this package must answer, including what is site-dependent. Do not require resolution of an entire house to start the study.

The [completed bay brief](bay-brief-result.md) selects the actual ground-floor front study window and adjoining wall/floor boundaries. It compares court-threshold/carrier and entrance-bridge alternatives, defines the technical issue and retains their separate unresolved critical obligations. No bay design or construction authority is claimed.

**BCR-BAY-DESIGN** follows that brief and TRIAD-GATE. Coordinate actual selected layers, joints, supports/reservations, tolerances, water/air/thermal/fire/acoustic boundaries, access and reinstatement for this bay. Keep numeric source with its owning design layer and route material IAS/OAS/HAD changes back to that owner under the normal lease protocol. Produce `project/bcr/bay-design-result.md` and source-owned design records suitable for ADE to render and schedule. Distinguish adopted selections from candidate dimensions and essential external calculations. Exit: the represented design is internally coordinated at the declared review scope, with every unresolved technical choice explicit. It is not construction-authorised; no nominal support envelope can be presented as an engineered member.

Bay packets own separate results and design-study files. They do not concurrently mutate the whole-house BCR matrix or others' selected geometry. Integrate necessary source changes serially with the relevant owner. The current BCR-CONVERGENCE retains its existing graph prerequisites; no new bay/manual dependency is silently added.
