# BCR — Buildability & Construction Resolution

**State:** inventory/scaffolding may start early; substantive convergence follows IAS/OAS/HAD integration and MRA-04  
**Tracking:** [#944 — BCR buildability and construction resolution](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/944)  
**Programme map:** [`../book-resolution-programme.md`](../book-resolution-programme.md)

## Question and scope

What remains between the coordinated architectural design and a house that a competent appointed team could carry through detailed design and construction, who owns each answer, and have material findings been reconciled back into the architecture?

BCR owns construction-resolution coordination and evidence routing. It does not replace MRA whole-house moisture assurance, EPR professional judgement, PHY physical proof, DEL responsibility/stage control, statutory approval or real-site deployment.

## Inputs

- IAS #941 selected interior system;
- OAS #942 selected opening system;

- shared IAS/OAS/HAD triad integration gate;
- MRA #959 `MRA-04` whole-house moisture failure matrix and admitted obligations;
- current Reference House and PLT #422 geometry/drawings;
- DEL #854 responsibility/evidence baseline;
- available PHY #912 / EPR #913 evidence.

## Work packets

- **BCR-00 — obligation inventory:** establish one construction-resolution matrix across structure/foundations, fire, acoustics, moisture/air/thermal, weathering, drainage, ventilation, electrical/services, security, accessibility, tolerances, fabrication, products, commissioning and reinstatement. MRA supplies the deep moisture-domain source/path/failure register rather than moisture being reduced to one generic row.
- **BCR-01 — ownership classification:** classify each gap as architectural/detail, moisture-assurance, technical-specialist, physical-test, delivery/procurement, or real-site/jurisdiction dependent.
- **BCR-02 — specialist/physical routing:** issue or update the relevant EPR/PHY/DEL routes only where the selected design or MRA failure review creates a concrete question.
- **BCR-03 — reconcile findings:** return material consequences to IAS/OAS/HAD/RH/PLT rather than stacking downstream workarounds; MRA-originated obligations remain traceable through the correction.
- **BCR-04 — construction-readiness boundary:** establish what the virtual Reference House can legitimately claim and what remains impossible without a real site/commission.
- **BCR-05 — manuscript handoff:** provide the evidence/disposition basis for Part V, **Technical Closure and Construction Readiness**.

## Dependency gate

`BCR-SCAFFOLD` may run while IAS/OAS/HAD and MRA-00 are in progress.

Substantive `BCR-CONVERGENCE` begins only after:

1. the selected IAS/OAS/HAD outputs have passed `TRIAD-GATE`; and
2. MRA-01/02/03 have been composed into `MRA-04`, so the integrated house has been adversarially attacked for external water, internal water, vapour/condensation and drying failure.

This ordering prevents BCR from declaring a technically converged house and only later discovering that moisture was treated as a collection of local details rather than a whole-house failure domain.

## Rewind rule

Classify before adding complexity:

1. detail problem → local owning design stream;
2. system problem → relevant technical family;
3. architectural problem → Reference House/HAD/PLT rewind;
4. moisture-assurance problem → MRA source/path/failure model, then the owning design/evidence stream;
5. evidence problem → EPR/PHY/DEL/DPL or explicit site/commission deferral.

A specialist finding may constrain architecture but may not silently redesign it by attrition.

## Exit gate

BCR closes when every material construction-resolution obligation has a disposition, owning authority and evidence state; all MRA-04 obligations have been resolved, routed or explicitly bounded; available EPR/PHY/DEL findings are reconciled; unresolved external/site matters are explicit; and the house is stable enough for MRA-05 final moisture closeout and then HRS without hidden technical unknowns being presented as completed architecture.

Closure does not claim planning/statutory approval, real-site suitability or M5 external validation.
