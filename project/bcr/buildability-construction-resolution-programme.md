# BCR — Buildability & Construction Resolution

**State:** queued convergence workstream; inventory may start early, substantive closure follows IAS/OAS/HAD selection  
**Tracking:** [#944 — BCR buildability and construction resolution](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/944)  
**Programme map:** [`../book-resolution-programme.md`](../book-resolution-programme.md)

## Question and scope

What remains between the coordinated architectural design and a house that a competent appointed team could carry through detailed design and construction, who owns each answer, and have material findings been reconciled back into the architecture?

BCR owns construction-resolution coordination and evidence routing. It does not replace EPR professional judgement, PHY physical proof, DEL responsibility/stage control, statutory approval or real-site deployment.

## Inputs

- IAS #941 selected interior system;
- OAS #942 selected opening system;
- HAD #943 selected architectural design;
- current Reference House and PLT #422 geometry/drawings;
- DEL #854 responsibility/evidence baseline;
- available PHY #912 / EPR #913 evidence.

## Work packets

- **BCR-00 — obligation inventory:** establish one construction-resolution matrix across structure/foundations, fire, acoustics, moisture/air/thermal, weathering, drainage, ventilation, electrical/services, security, accessibility, tolerances, fabrication, products, commissioning and reinstatement.
- **BCR-01 — ownership classification:** classify each gap as architectural/detail, technical-specialist, physical-test, delivery/procurement, or real-site/jurisdiction dependent.
- **BCR-02 — specialist/physical routing:** issue or update the relevant EPR/PHY/DEL routes only where the selected design creates a concrete question.
- **BCR-03 — reconcile findings:** return material consequences to IAS/OAS/HAD/RH/PLT rather than stacking downstream workarounds.
- **BCR-04 — construction-readiness boundary:** establish what the virtual Reference House can legitimately claim and what remains impossible without a real site/commission.
- **BCR-05 — manuscript handoff:** provide the evidence/disposition basis for Part V, **Technical Closure and Construction Readiness**.

## Rewind rule

Classify before adding complexity:

1. detail problem → local owning design stream;
2. system problem → relevant technical family;
3. architectural problem → Reference House/HAD/PLT rewind;
4. evidence problem → EPR/PHY/DEL or explicit site/commission deferral.

A specialist finding may constrain architecture but may not silently redesign it by attrition.

## Exit gate

BCR closes when every material construction-resolution obligation has a disposition, owning authority and evidence state; available EPR/PHY/DEL findings are reconciled; unresolved external/site matters are explicitly bounded; and the house can pass to HRS without hidden technical unknowns being presented as completed architecture.

Closure does not claim planning/statutory approval, real-site suitability or M5 external validation.
