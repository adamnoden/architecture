## Question and bounded answer

Can the accepted house's known construction questions be made usable by parallel feasibility studies without waiting for selected-system convergence or inventing engineering evidence?

The [construction-resolution matrix](construction-resolution-matrix.md) supplies 27 stable BCR row identities with starting source, classification, primary owner, existing next packet/evidence route and a concrete discharge condition. All begin routed-pending. The result completes the initial inventory/ownership work formerly described as BCR-00/01 within the programme; it does not create new executable nodes for those historical headings.

This helps the owner's house by giving the six early studies a shared set of real questions: load paths/voids, complete wet assemblies and sanitary access, plant/distribution, normal/abnormal water paths, occupied actions and proportionate conventional comparisons. It cannot supply those physical studies itself. Their dependencies on HRS-BASELINE remain intact.

## Decisions and adversarial checks

- One shared matrix, with separate study-result files and serial PREFLIGHT integration; no concurrent mutation of selected geometry or competing evidence register.
- All programme domains are represented: foundations/structure, fire/acoustics, moisture/air/thermal, roof/weathering/water, ventilation/electrical/services, occupation/security/access, tolerances/products/fabrication and commissioning/reinstatement.
- Existing IAS IS/IW/I00-C1 and OAS OS obligations remain their owners' records; BCR rows reference rather than silently close them.
- Moisture is distributed across actual source/path duties. The MRA seed families map to receiving rows and MRA-04 must provide finding-level traceability later.
- A technical referral does not discharge a conflict. Ordinary construction does not automatically pass water, fire or maintenance duties; it also does not acquire a new bespoke test without a concrete reason.
- Missing site evidence is distinguishable from an unresolved internal physical relationship. Only a genuinely bounded pre-site residual may be deferred; an impossible en-suite or disconnected overflow may not be labelled resolved.
- Historic EPR/PHY packages are not declared applicable to the current house. Existing manual gates remain manual; no contact, procurement, build or appointment was made.
- No geometry, architectural selection, compiler behaviour, generated house output, public manuscript, maturity or authority ceiling changes.
