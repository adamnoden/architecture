# HRS — House Resolution & Synthesis

**State:** queued final synthesis; blocked on BCR stability  
**Tracking:** [#945 — HRS house resolution and synthesis](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/945)  
**Programme map:** [`../book-resolution-programme.md`](../book-resolution-programme.md)

## Question and scope

Given everything the Reference House has learned through IAS, OAS, HAD and BCR, what is the final architectural answer that should be handed to PLT and shown in Part VI?

HRS owns post-BCR architectural synthesis. It is not a second open-ended design exploration, another technical-validation programme or the final plate/editorial/publication owner.

## Inputs

- IAS #941 selected/bounded interior system;
- OAS #942 selected/bounded opening system;
- HAD #943 architectural design;
- BCR #944 reconciled construction-resolution matrix and evidence consequences;
- current Reference House/PLT source and all approved rewinds.

## Work packets

- **HRS-00 — consequence inventory:** identify every BCR/EPR/PHY/DEL result that materially changes architecture or visible interfaces.
- **HRS-01 — architectural reconciliation:** absorb necessary changes to proportions, assembly thicknesses, openings, grilles, joinery, thresholds, materials and room expression without cosmetic concealment.
- **HRS-02 — final system selection:** confirm the actual IAS/OAS variants used by the Reference House and remove superseded candidates from current authority.
- **HRS-03 — final house configuration:** freeze one coherent exterior/interior/material design at the Reference House’s bounded scope.
- **HRS-04 — Part VI handoff:** define the concise monograph/plates content for **The House** with minimal re-argument and explicit residual proof boundaries.
- **HRS-05 — PLT handoff:** provide final architectural decisions/dimensions/interfaces so PLT can draw rather than invent.

## Governing rules

- HRS synthesises; it does not protect previous design work from a justified rewind.
- If a technical finding reveals a deeper architectural error, reopen the correct upstream owner rather than hide it.
- The final house should feel settled and permanent in occupation; maintainability should be present in the logic, not dominate the visual field.
- Real-site, planning, ground, utility and statutory matters that cannot be established by the virtual Reference House remain explicit residuals.

## Exit gate

HRS closes when one authoritative resolved-house configuration exists, every material BCR consequence has been absorbed or explicitly deferred, Part VI and final PLT have complete architectural handoffs, and no downstream drawing/editorial stream is being asked to invent unresolved architecture.

After HRS: final PLT integration/freeze → renewed EDA assurance → PUB-02 final manufacture gate.
