## Question and scope

Given everything the Reference House has learned through IAS, OAS, HAD, MRA and BCR, what is the final architectural answer that should be handed to PLT and shown in Part VI?

HRS owns post-BCR/post-MRA architectural synthesis. It is not a second open-ended design exploration, another technical-validation programme or the final plate/editorial/publication owner.

## Inputs

- IAS #941 selected/bounded interior system;
- OAS #942 selected/bounded opening system;
- HAD #943 architectural design;
- BCR #944 reconciled construction-resolution matrix and evidence consequences;
- MRA #959 `MRA-05` post-BCR moisture-assurance closeout;
- current Reference House/PLT source and all approved rewinds.

## Work packets

- **HRS-00 — consequence inventory:** identify every BCR/MRA/EPR/PHY/DEL result that materially changes architecture or visible interfaces.
- **HRS-01 — architectural reconciliation:** absorb necessary changes to proportions, assembly thicknesses, openings, grilles, joinery, thresholds, materials and room expression without cosmetic concealment.
- **HRS-02 — final system selection:** confirm the actual IAS/OAS variants used by the Reference House and remove superseded candidates from current authority.
- **HRS-03 — final house configuration:** freeze one coherent exterior/interior/material design at the Reference House’s bounded scope, including all resolved moisture-related rewinds.
- **HRS-04 — Part VI handoff:** define the concise monograph/plates content for **The House** with minimal re-argument and explicit residual proof boundaries.
- **HRS-05 — PLT handoff:** provide final architectural decisions/dimensions/interfaces so PLT can draw rather than invent.

## Governing rules

- HRS synthesises; it does not protect previous design work from a justified rewind.
- If a technical or moisture-assurance finding reveals a deeper architectural error, reopen the correct upstream owner rather than hide it.
- HRS must not weaken an MRA disposition merely to recover a preferred visual detail; any conflict returns to the owning design/assurance boundary.
- The final house should feel settled and permanent in occupation; maintainability and moisture resilience should be present in the logic, not dominate the visual field.
- Real-site, planning, ground, utility and statutory matters that cannot be established by the virtual Reference House remain explicit residuals.

## Dependencies

HRS begins only after `BCR-CONVERGENCE` and `MRA-05` are complete. MRA-05 exists after BCR specifically to verify that technical closure did not create a new moisture trap, inaccessible defence or hidden water-risk debt.

HRS then hands off to final PLT #422 drawing/plate freeze, followed by renewed EDA editorial assurance and PUB-02 final content/manufacture gate.

## Exit gate

HRS closes when one authoritative resolved-house configuration exists, every material BCR/MRA consequence has been absorbed or explicitly deferred, Part VI and final PLT have complete architectural handoffs, and no downstream drawing/editorial stream is being asked to invent unresolved architecture or conceal an undisclosed moisture vulnerability.

After HRS: final PLT integration/freeze → renewed EDA assurance → PUB-02 final manufacture gate.
