# HRS — House Resolution & Synthesis

**State:** early current-house authority repair; selected technical applicability after house integration; final synthesis after BCR and MRA-05

**Tracking:** [#945 — HRS house resolution and synthesis](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/945)  
**Programme map:** [`../book-resolution-programme.md`](../book-resolution-programme.md)

## Question and scope

Given everything the Reference House has learned through IAS, OAS, HAD, MRA and BCR, what is the final architectural answer that should be handed to PLT and shown in Part VI?

HRS owns early current-house authority/applicability reconciliation and final post-BCR/post-MRA architectural synthesis. Its intermediate technical gate accepts the selected house's supported computational claims and explicit unknowns. Reusable source/compile infrastructure is implemented early by the [current-house integration programme](../ccr/current-house-integration-programme.md), not deferred until HRS-TECHNICAL. HRS does not own open-ended redesign, competent technical validation or final plate/editorial/publication production.

## Early and intermediate packets

### HRS-BASELINE — current-house identity and applicability

Establish the accepted 12 × 12 m Reference House as the current architectural basis, with its exact source identity and owning plan/section/opening/roof/service records. Repair reader entry points and technical summaries that still call a different house current. Preserve historical fixtures, calculations, gates and drawings as labelled evidence; do not silently relabel or delete them.

Check existing technical/review briefs against the current source. For each material carried-forward result, record whether it applies unchanged, applies only at a narrowed scope, needs rechecking or is historical. A brief advertised as issue-ready must name the house revision it actually reviews. Update current questions and facts without claiming a competent review has occurred. Reuse existing ownership records and BCR's matrix rather than creating a second evidence system.

This self-service packet changes authority/applicability presentation, not accepted architectural geometry, system choices, maturity or historical proof identities. It completes when the current entry path identifies one house; contradictory current labels are corrected; the material technical/review applicability gaps and owners are explicit; and the early BCR studies can start from an unambiguous source.

### HRS-TECHNICAL — selected current-house technical applicability

After `TRIAD-GATE` and `CCR-IDENTITY`, reconcile the selected current-house source with supported computations, technical summaries, identities, reports and drawing references. Consume the early model/adapters where implemented and return changed accepted inputs to their owning modules. This gate is not permission to postpone source integration until after physical design. Distinguish supported/re-proved results from genuine unsupported evidence; visual representation is not technical adequacy.

Preserve historical fixtures under their own identities. Reuse an existing method only when its inputs, assumptions and domain apply; otherwise withhold the result and route the concrete gap. Do not silently run the old full-system fixture for a current claim. Existing calculable results must not be marked unsupported merely because their adapter is unfinished. Relevant migration gaps remain tracked in the existing CCR programme and prevent its final release.

The packet completes when every current claim in its declared technical scope resolves to the selected source and applicable evidence or a genuine explicitly withheld question; regeneration, mutation and regression checks pass; and BCR can distinguish current results from historical ones. Missing external performance evidence can remain visible. Missing physical fit or a material architectural dependency cannot be hidden by calling it unsupported. Final synthesis rechecks applicability after any later source correction. This bounded technical acceptance does not close CCR-CURRENT-RELEASE's broader active-consumer audit.

## Final synthesis packets

- **HRS-00 — consequence inventory:** identify every BCR/MRA/EPR/PHY/DEL result that materially changes architecture or visible interfaces.
- **HRS-01 — architectural reconciliation:** absorb necessary changes to proportions, assembly thicknesses, openings, grilles, joinery, thresholds, materials and room expression without cosmetic concealment.
- **HRS-02 — final system selection:** confirm the actual IAS/OAS variants used by the Reference House and remove superseded candidates from current authority.
- **HRS-03 — final house configuration:** freeze one coherent exterior/interior/material design at the Reference House’s bounded scope, including all resolved moisture-related rewinds; reconcile physical studies and current technical applicability after final changes.
- **HRS-04 — Part VI handoff:** define the concise monograph/plates content for **The House** with minimal re-argument and explicit residual proof boundaries.
- **HRS-05 — PLT handoff:** provide final architectural decisions/dimensions/interfaces so PLT can draw rather than invent, through the same current-source contract used by computation.

## Governing rules

- HRS synthesises; it does not protect previous design work from a justified rewind.
- If a technical or moisture-assurance finding reveals a deeper architectural error, reopen the correct upstream owner rather than hide it.
- HRS must not weaken an MRA disposition merely to recover a preferred visual detail; any conflict returns to the owning design/assurance boundary.
- The final house should feel settled and permanent in occupation; maintainability and moisture resilience should be present in the logic, not dominate the visual field.
- Real-site, planning, ground, utility and statutory matters unavailable to the virtual Reference House remain explicit residuals.
- Final source choices must be supplied as versioned model inputs, not disconnected prose, renderer constants or copies in historical fixtures. Relevant parameter changes must re-evaluate or invalidate dependent results under the [executable design contract](../../docs/computational/executable-design-contract.md).

## Dependencies

`HRS-BASELINE` starts early and unlocks BCR feasibility and the current-source foundation. It does not wait for selected design or final synthesis.

`HRS-TECHNICAL` requires `TRIAD-GATE` and `CCR-IDENTITY`; it feeds `BCR-CONVERGENCE`. This is a selected-design acceptance order, not a prohibition on earlier reusable model/adapter work. ADE-MODEL now starts from CCR-CURRENT-SOURCE; its final technical outputs still require HRS-TECHNICAL.

`HRS-SYNTHESIS` begins only after `BCR-CONVERGENCE`, `MRA-05`, `PCO-APPLICABILITY`, `DEL-CURRENT` and `EDA-NARRATIVE` are complete. MRA-05 verifies that closure did not create a moisture trap, inaccessible defence or hidden water-risk debt. Reconcile any materially affected early results before acceptance.

After synthesis, `CCR-CURRENT-RELEASE` audits the final selected house and actual ADE technical outputs against the implemented current compile path and change suite. `PLT-FINAL-PREP` requires both that release and HRS-SYNTHESIS. It is followed by the owner's final design and physical print decisions, PLT freeze, EDA and PUB. This does not introduce a cycle through the first owner design review or a default external-review requirement.

## Exit gate

HRS closes when one authoritative resolved-house configuration governs the design summary, technical coordination, selected assemblies, review briefs, supported computational claims and handoffs. The five physical studies agree after final changes, and each material BCR/MRA consequence is resolved at its stated scope, eliminated by an adopted change, or retained as a legitimate bounded residual under BCR's exit rule.

Routed-pending evidence is not a resolved finding. An unresolved material fit, route, topology or safety dependency cannot be deferred merely to declare the house settled. Genuine later site/product/performance questions may remain bounded where their withheld claims and architectural consequences are clear.

No current reader path may describe a different house as current. Historical fixtures remain recoverable and labelled; no drawing may borrow assurance from an unmigrated predecessor. HRS completion alone does not claim the current-engine migration finished: the separate final release must prove every active consumer's source and change behaviour before publication preparation.
