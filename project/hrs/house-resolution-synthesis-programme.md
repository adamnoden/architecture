# HRS — House Resolution & Synthesis

**State:** early current-house authority repair; bounded technical migration after selected-house integration; final synthesis after BCR and MRA-05

**Tracking:** [#945 — HRS house resolution and synthesis](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/945)  
**Programme map:** [`../book-resolution-programme.md`](../book-resolution-programme.md)

## Question and scope

Given everything the Reference House has learned through IAS, OAS, HAD, MRA and BCR, what is the final architectural answer that should be handed to PLT and shown in Part VI?

HRS owns early current-house authority/applicability reconciliation and final post-BCR/post-MRA architectural synthesis. Its intermediate technical migration maps the selected house to supported computational claims and explicit unknowns. It does not own open-ended redesign, competent technical validation, compiler-wide refactoring or final plate/editorial/publication production.

## Inputs

- IAS #941 selected/bounded interior system;
- OAS #942 selected/bounded opening system;
- HAD #943 architectural design;
- BCR #944 reconciled construction-resolution matrix and evidence consequences;
- MRA #959 `MRA-05` post-BCR moisture-assurance closeout;
- current Reference House/PLT source and all approved rewinds.

## Early and intermediate packets

### HRS-BASELINE — current-house identity and applicability

Establish the accepted 12 × 12 m Reference House as the current architectural basis, with its exact source identity and owning plan/section/opening/roof/service records. Repair reader entry points and technical summaries that still call a different house current. Preserve historical fixtures, calculations, gates and drawings as labelled evidence; do not silently relabel or delete them.

Check existing technical/review briefs against the current source. For each material carried-forward result, record whether it applies unchanged, applies only at a narrowed scope, needs rechecking or is historical. A brief advertised as issue-ready must name the house revision it actually reviews. Update current questions and facts without claiming a competent review has occurred. Reuse existing ownership records and BCR's matrix rather than creating a second evidence system.

This self-service packet changes authority/applicability presentation, not accepted architectural geometry, system choices, maturity or historical proof identities. It completes when the current entry path identifies one house; contradictory current labels are corrected; the material technical/review applicability gaps and owners are explicit; and the early BCR studies can start from an unambiguous source.

### HRS-TECHNICAL — bounded current-house technical migration

After `TRIAD-GATE` and `CCR-IDENTITY`, map the selected current-house source to the computational relationships the project actually supports. Reconcile the current technical summary, source adapters/inputs where legitimate, model identities, reports, drawing references and proof claims. Distinguish supported and re-proved results from unsupported/unknown questions; a visual or geometric representation is not technical adequacy.

This is not a requirement to reproduce every historical compiler capability or turn missing engineering into software. Preserve historical fixtures under their own identities. Reuse an existing method only when its inputs, assumptions and supported domain genuinely apply; otherwise withhold the result and route the concrete gap. Compiler correctness repairs remain with CCR/computational ownership.

The packet completes when every current claim in its declared migration scope resolves to the current source and applicable evidence or is explicitly unsupported/unknown; relevant regeneration and regression checks pass; and BCR can distinguish a current result from historical evidence. Missing external performance evidence can remain visible. Missing physical fit or a material architectural dependency cannot be hidden by calling it unsupported. Final synthesis rechecks applicability if later corrections change the source.

## Final synthesis packets

- **HRS-00 — consequence inventory:** identify every BCR/MRA/EPR/PHY/DEL result that materially changes architecture or visible interfaces.
- **HRS-01 — architectural reconciliation:** absorb necessary changes to proportions, assembly thicknesses, openings, grilles, joinery, thresholds, materials and room expression without cosmetic concealment.
- **HRS-02 — final system selection:** confirm the actual IAS/OAS variants used by the Reference House and remove superseded candidates from current authority.
- **HRS-03 — final house configuration:** freeze one coherent exterior/interior/material design at the Reference House’s bounded scope, including all resolved moisture-related rewinds; reconcile the five BCR physical studies and current technical/computational applicability after final changes.
- **HRS-04 — Part VI handoff:** define the concise monograph/plates content for **The House** with minimal re-argument and explicit residual proof boundaries.
- **HRS-05 — PLT handoff:** provide final architectural decisions/dimensions/interfaces so PLT can draw rather than invent.

## Governing rules

- HRS synthesises; it does not protect previous design work from a justified rewind.
- If a technical or moisture-assurance finding reveals a deeper architectural error, reopen the correct upstream owner rather than hide it.
- HRS must not weaken an MRA disposition merely to recover a preferred visual detail; any conflict returns to the owning design/assurance boundary.
- The final house should feel settled and permanent in occupation; maintainability and moisture resilience should be present in the logic, not dominate the visual field.
- Real-site, planning, ground, utility and statutory matters that cannot be established by the virtual Reference House remain explicit residuals.

## Dependencies

`HRS-BASELINE` starts early and unlocks the BCR feasibility studies alongside `BCR-SCAFFOLD`. It does not wait for selected design or final synthesis.

`HRS-TECHNICAL` requires `TRIAD-GATE` and `CCR-IDENTITY`; it feeds `BCR-CONVERGENCE`. This order allows feasibility to constrain design before the selected house is mapped to supported computational claims.

`HRS-SYNTHESIS` begins only after `BCR-CONVERGENCE`, `MRA-05` and the current `HRS-TECHNICAL` result are complete. MRA-05 exists after BCR specifically to verify that technical closure did not create a new moisture trap, inaccessible defence or hidden water-risk debt. If later changes invalidate a material early result, reconcile that result before final acceptance rather than relying on its completed node.

HRS then hands off to final PLT #422 drawing/plate freeze, followed by renewed EDA editorial assurance and PUB-02 final content/manufacture gate.

## Exit gate

HRS closes when one authoritative resolved-house configuration governs the design summary, technical coordination, selected assemblies, review briefs, supported computational claims and PLT/Part VI handoffs. The five physical studies agree after final changes, and each material BCR/MRA consequence is resolved at its stated scope, eliminated by an adopted change, or retained as a legitimate bounded residual under BCR's exit rule.

Routed-pending evidence is not a resolved finding. An unresolved material fit, route, topology or safety dependency cannot be explicitly deferred merely to declare the house settled. Genuine later site/product/performance questions may remain bounded where their withheld claims and architectural consequences are clear.

No current reader path may describe a different house as current. Every inherited result must have an applicability decision; historical fixtures remain recoverable and labelled. No drawing may borrow assurance from an unmigrated predecessor. Final PLT and editorial work receive complete architectural decisions and explicit proof limits rather than an instruction to invent missing architecture or conceal moisture debt.

After HRS: final PLT integration/freeze → renewed EDA assurance → PUB-02 final manufacture gate.
