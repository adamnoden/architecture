Merely flipping the roof to 5–12° at the old +7100 eave then collides with the retained +6620 protected-ceiling interface if its current roof stack is inherited.

## Candidate: outward roof, lifted eave, one wettable outdoor through-route

A **single** architectural rewrite, conditional on ROF/HAD/STR/MAT and later RWT/DPL selection:

1. **Roof:** four continuous pitched faces falling **outward** to all four exterior edges, with hips from the four outside corners to the four high court corners. No flat area, inward valley or routine inner court gutter. Retain full outer rainwater duty rather than disguising moved flow as saved flow.
2. **Physical roof section:** test **8°** outward pitch with the **outer eave raised 175 mm** from +7100 to **+7275**, holding the current +6620 protected-ceiling top as a geometric comparison. Both the angle and the eave lift are *authored trial choices*, not selected geometry, minimum permitted pitch, or an adopted material/structural product.
3. **Court and garden circulation:** the existing 2400 gross north outdoor loggia and the court form **one visibly falling and intentionally wettable outdoor walking surface in ordinary conditions**, not two narrow rainwater channels plus an invented independent dry lane. Under high rain, blockage or dangerous wetting, ordinary garden passage may need to be **closed**. The protected house-to-front route remains distinct, but that does **not** prove a stranded garden occupant has safe alternative passage.
4. **Site:** carry the surface toward a **real** lower, lawful garden receiver when its survey, groundwater/flood envelope and onward discharge are known. Do not inherit the historical unverified −800 water level or certify a slope that merely ends at the boundary.

This is substantially simpler *if earned*: it could delete the high inner collector and its weather-critical high-court renewal, the covered 400 gross normal carrier and wet whole-body removal, the parallel 1800 relief reservation, the 600 steep chute and two unlanded receiver boxes. It **does not** delete roof structural load, outer rainwater goods, court floor weathering, a first-harm condition, site tailwater or full-mouth failure.

## Joined roof section: test the real ceiling and facade height, not just the pitch

STR's matched existing roof has eave **+7100**, ridge **+8090.904**, MAT normal stack **500.7 mm**, support face inset **332.5 mm** and inherited purlin secondary drop **44.135 mm**. The actual protected ceiling top is **+6620**. At the old eave, the *unchanged* stack has negative geometric clearance at 5–12°, while a roof high rim constrained below the old ridge permits at most 13.124°.

The trial raises the **outer** eave by 175 mm and recomputes *both* roof-to-ceiling and court-wall elevation. For each hypothetical angle:

| Outward roof angle | New high court rim | Below old ridge (+8090.904) | Modelled purlin-to-protected-ceiling gap |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5° | +7646.83 mm | +444.08 mm | +137.34 mm |
| **8°** | **+7872.30 mm** | **+218.61 mm** | **+151.97 mm** |
| 10° | +8024.39 mm | +66.51 mm | +161.07 mm |

The proposed **8° / +175** section therefore defeats the *particular old* zero/negative-clearance counterexample **without** exceeding the selected old ridge height. It does **not** prove the unchanged 25° roof covering/assembly is admissible at 8°. The positive **151.97 mm is nominal inherited-model geometry, not installed clearance or deflection/thermal/fire reserve**. A matched covering, secondary roof support, wind/snow, hold-down, warm-cold boundary, high-rim flashing, hip and real eave section must replace the old stack before any construction or maturity claim.

The high court rim would rise **772.30 mm above the existing +7100 courtyard eave**. That is a major exterior/court daylight, window, silhouette, masonry and access cost; it cannot be concealed by noting that the absolute roof stays below the former ridge. Raising the external eaves changes the five-bay facade and window/head/interface geometry. A sunlight/daylight and full elevation review could reject it outright. No source drawing or facade identity was silently updated.

**Structural gravity is still present.** STR's conditional two-line reaction model transfers **339.15 kN trial ULS down** and **−121.125 kN trial uplift** into the high court rim over the whole 131.75 m² wall-line roof field. Those are **not** member loads or capacities; the real hips, longitudinal/eaves bearings, continuous upper walls, arches, movement, ties and entire support to ground must be redesigned. The old 144.15 m² structural roof ledger includes a different exterior extent, so the 12.4 m² difference is not a quantified load saving. Deleting the gutter does not delete the masonry or steel/tension resistance.

These grades describe a **single open surface**, not a pipe below permanent masonry. The 2400 mm is **gross** finished width, not a guaranteed level, continuously dry, slip-resistant, accessible or protected pedestrian width. A realistic section must show underfoot grating/drainage, durable non-plastic and repairable surface/weather boundary, independent structure, wall-base/threshold first wetting, change of slope, guards and maintenance access. A wettable outdoor route can be pleasant in ordinary rain but **must not be advertised as a second dry escape under flood**.

An outward-only roof reallocates **47.8125 m²** of projected ordinary inner-roof catchment to the outside. At the inherited **216 mm/h illustrative stress**, court flow falls from **3.60375 L/s** (roof-face inward-failure plus court) to **0.735 L/s** of direct court rain, while exterior roof discharge rises to **7.905 L/s** on 131.75 m² of faces. The source-consistent, unblocked 2400-wide 1% floor has **3.40 mm ideal uniform depth** under direct-court-only flow. This is *not* an actual walking water depth, flood height, inlet pass or weathered-floor qualification. Wind-driven rain, roof defects, splash and boundary transfers are not excluded by that number.

The current court has an **80 mm elevation difference** between −110 mouth and the *unqualified* −30 pan-end/masonry/DPC wetting comparison. If the north mouth is completely cut, the previous ideal geometric storage probe finds only **280 litres** below that comparison in the court. The +15 geometric door crest is not an alternate first-harm proof. Complete blockage, debris-mat common cause, external water arriving back up the open surface, leaky floor boundaries and occupation during flood remain **unresolved** even with the roof moved. Do not credit storage as an evacuation time; do not call a visible surface N+1 drainage.

## Rejection controls and selection order
**Adverse full-mouth check added 4 November:** The [complete blockage/direct-court-rain calculation](rwt-06-court-mouth-closure.md) confirms that even the outward-roof family reaches an ideal geometric pool **above the −30 unqualified wall-base comparison** in the 216 mm/h ten-minute case, whether using 12.25 m² wall-line or 9.61 m² hypothetical exposed court. Neither this 8° roof nor a single open pedestrian/water passage is a fault-safe rainwater solution without a physically independent passive relief or a separately proven wetting-tolerant house. This is a decisive admission failure for any claim made from the roof-transfer figures alone, not a forecast of failure time.


**No automatic adoption.** A joined HAD/ROF/STR/MAT replan should falsify or earn this candidate in the following order, rather than independently polishing gutter catalogues:

- **House and roof form:** actual matched 8° roof covering and eave/inner-rim/hip support section, elevations/daylight, upper rooms, fire/thermal continuity and safe roof repair. Abandon this candidate if it fundamentally damages the occupied Georgian house or creates a hidden wet trap/structure conflict.
- **People and courtyard:** define everyday entry/garden use and a credible weather closure/recovery regime; demonstrate safe thresholds, slip resistance and an alternative human route when the north surface cannot be crossed. Do **not** mistake the protected front indoor escape for an independent garden evacuation route.
- **Actual first harm and water:** detail each susceptible sill, DPC, pan, wall base, support and concealed cavity level; model full-flow, blocked-mouth, debris, tailwater and splash with an authentic downstream gravity boundary. The present −30 is not a permitted operating water level.
- **Land and services:** establish actual garden levels, receiving-water envelope, below-ground foundation/soil interface, clear externally maintainable water path and lawful discharge. If the plot cannot fall away safely without a wet foundation, grade change, pump or unsafe pedestrian gradient, reject this geometry for that plot.
- **Roof exterior:** distribute the **7.905 L/s** sensitivity across the four exterior faces with independent maintainable copper/other approved collection and harmless overflow; no assumption that an exterior gutter or hedge is a flood-safe sink.

If these checks fail, **recompose the house, widen or reroute the garden connection, reduce the inward catchment by an alternate roof support/coverage scheme, or choose a better site**. Do not restore hidden full-depth copper carriers as the default simply to save this plan.
