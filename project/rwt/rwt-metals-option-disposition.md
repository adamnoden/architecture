# RWT-METALS — metal rainwater assemblies: conditional option disposition

**1 November 2026 · RWT-METALS packet closeout at candidate-development scope.** This is **not** a selected network, approved product assembly, hydraulic pass, site discharge design or building detail. The owner brief, [material policy](../../docs/reference-house/material-policy.md), [ground-water policy](../../docs/reference-house/ground-water-policy.md) and [rainwater elegance brief](rainwater-elegance-brief.md) take precedence. RWT-ROBUSTNESS, RWT-06, RWT-07 and ROF-RWT-GATE retain the unearned decisions below.

## Decision in one paragraph

**Use accessible, conventional copper collection and exposed gravity conveyance as the pre-site control; permit outboard ground capture only where a dry wall boundary and harmless failed-state escape can actually be proved; reject hidden joint-dependent routes and unqualified brass hydraulic bodies as default architecture.** An independently removable brass/bronze grate may be an architectural finish over a disclosed copper wet body, but it must never be described as a brass channel. The engineering benefit is not the word *metal*: it is a genuinely reachable joint, deliberate movement, renewable units, and failure consequences that do not consume permanent fabric.

This closes the **comparison/development question** in RWT-METALS, not the separate **network selection, hydraulic redundancy, structural support, buildability or material-constituent acceptance** questions. Earlier packet studies saying “remains planned” describe their historical state; this dated disposition supersedes the programme-status statement, not their adverse findings.

**Current pre-site recommendation:** pursue **M1** as the base everywhere collection requires positive control, and challenge selected *outer garden* edges with **M2**. Start with controlled collection in the enclosed courtyard and at entrances; there is no court-wide free-shedding adoption. **M3** is held as a fabricated specialist challenger, **M4** as a last-resort service-zone exception. That is an actual option reduction, not four equal unresolved recommendations.

## Why the source studies support this reduction

1. [Segmented copper access and crossing](segmented-copper-access.md), [copper component study](copper-access-first-result.md), and [retained court-tail leak route](retained-tail-leak-route.md) establish that nominal modular pipe, Endex fitting dimensions, a brazing allowance or a small slip coupling cannot alone prove installed joint release, bore closure during work, clearance, seal or watertightness at the court threshold. A high return fails the published ponding screen; an outward alternative has a longer unproved continuation. Do not import an earlier successful detail by label.
2. [Outer-west receiver construction and backing](outer-west-receiver-renewal.md) distinguish independent removable top units from the **retained and soldered wet body**. The local seat and seam movement calculations are useful gauges, not a selected copper thickness, accepted support span or entire-body withdrawal solution. The terminal is a separate datum and must be releasable.
3. [Passive leak-witness challenge](outer-west-leak-witness.md) rejects a single telltale, groove or concealed support as evidence of prompt observation. The sound direction is to reduce consequences by placing the entire receiver and its wet backing outside the protected building territory. An underside leak may remain initially invisible; inspection is not a substitute for harmless position.
4. [Shared roof/rainwater material compatibility](../../docs/research/roof-rainwater-material-compatibility.md) identifies copper, brass/bronze, forming, solders, flux, galvanic contact, material and coating limitations. Material family evidence cannot be promoted to a complete made-up hydraulic product. Owner synthetic-polymer minimisation applies to concealed fixtures and sealants as strictly as visible ones.

## What a selectable option must declare

One **system schedule per edge**, with no alternative silently winning through missing inputs:

| Required record | Failure or acceptance question |
|---|---|
| ROF contributing planes and eave/court geometry; effective roof catchment and rainfall method | Identify `A`, intensity `i`, concentrations and the nominated events. Screening flow is `Q = iA/3600` L/s for `A` m² and `i` mm/h at runoff coefficient one; no invented design storm |
| Collector, outlet and branch IDs, nominal and damaged-state flows, accessible cleaning routes | Can every participating element be reached, isolated and renewed without breaking adjacent permanent fabric? Does debris or one blocked throat disable nominally separate outlets? |
| Actual fall, joint elevation, rim/spill elevation, *first harmful* threshold and outfall high-water elevation | Does a blocked primary discharge emerge to a **separate** gravity path **before** water reaches the vulnerable boundary, under flowing head, splash and a justified margin? An arrow on a section is not a pass |
| Cross-section through wall base → support → receiver → hedge/root zone → site handoff | All backing, splash, capillary bridging, future raised paving, saturated ground, local flooding, independent load paths and cleaning/renewal footprint visible |
| Complete constituent schedule for installed wet body **and** all hidden accessories | Copper/brass alloy and temper, adjoining metal, solder/braze, flux residues, mechanical locks, fasteners, supports, separators, bedding, finish, coating, gaskets, sealants and potential contaminants; mark unknowns **unaccepted** rather than assigning zero |
| Movement/release and repair ledger | One fixed datum and the restrained/sliding interfaces; actual temperature movement, bearing, uplift, first release, whole-unit removal, terminal reconnection and temporary rain diversion; no repeated nails/sacrificial permanent fabric |
| Rejected-state evidence | Surviving flow with either outlet obstructed, outlet/backwater high, local joint leak, debris/wind splash, absent hedge, and simultaneous primary + observation-channel blockage; give a real failed state when one is found |

A collector described as `full duty` must survive the nominated single primary failure **with independent upstream entry, receiving elevation and downstream disposal**, not merely possess a second hole. A common blocked gully, flooding destination or shared narrow gutter is a common-mode failure; RWT-ROBUSTNESS owns the explicit adversarial comparison. Existing route descriptions are **not** that test.

## Design-rule interfaces, not invented acceptance

- **Wet/dry separation:** keep every receiver's leaked, splashed and overflowing water away from the cavity base, DPC, suspended-floor ventilation, adjacent door and foundation. The baseline cannot rely on a hedge, porous gravel, infiltration beside foundations, active pump, synthetic hidden pan or occupant alarm. A single failed-state wetting path into permanent fabric disqualifies M2 at that edge.
- **Supports and movement:** permanent structural support should not be pierced/replaced by ordinary gutter repair. Independently support gratings and pedestrian/impact loads; retain support beneath the wet copper body and its seams; use an explicitly defined fixed point and allow thermal movement elsewhere. Where dissimilar-metal wet contact or separation is unresolved, no compatibility pass is awarded.
- **Materials:** copper hydraulic sheet, its fabrication and an exposed brass component are **candidate families**, not a batch-specific no-plastic certificate. Do not invent an organic-free seal, lead-free solder, corrosion life or chemical-stress approval. An unqualified polymer residue is a MAT exception request, not automatic permission.
- **Courtyard:** the architectural trapped-catchment condition is materially higher consequence than an outer garden edge. Choose controlled visible catchment in the next network comparison; no free-shed court transition or buried threshold connection is adopted here.
- **Boundary Services Lodge / accessible raceway:** coordinate a service crossing against [the owner brief](../had/boundary-service-lodge-and-raceway-brief.md); the lodge is a utility-interruption and observation architecture, **not** proof that a rainwater outfall can be routed there or may wet the utility enclosure.
- **SITE-WATER-HANDOFF:** always explicit, but **no particular sewer, soakaway, infiltration rate, attenuation or street datum** selected pre-site. A safe house-side spill is a separate matter from availability of an approved site outlet.

## Earned closeout and forwarded obligations

**RWT-METALS earns a bounded comparison closeout:** four families are reduced to one controlled base, one site-constrained garden challenger, one held specialist wet-body candidate and one last-resort concealed crossing; the available copper/brass and joining evidence is reconciled without material-performance invention; known failed/leak paths and complete constituent, access and renewal obligations are enumerated.

**It does not earn:** an actual all-metal installed product, a guaranteed plastic-free seal, a complete court-tail connection, a gutter/receiver section sizing pass, a leak witness, a site-water outfall, or the roof/court network design. All such claims are excluded explicitly so closure of this *packet* cannot be mistaken for closing RWT or promoting house maturity.

**Later selection:** RWT-07 makes a small edge-by-edge network candidate set; ROF-RWT-GATE coordinates roof, structural, architectural and moisture consequences.
