It follows the [accepted partial](rwt-06-access-court-transition.md) and the distinct [HAD court proposal](../had/had-03-court-levels.md). ## Decision earned by the comparison

Do not carry forward the earlier bookkeeping lid as an available insulated, load-bearing access cover. A real shallow-cover comparison consumes that entire depth before separate insulation, and the proposed relief loses its small above-grade advantage. Also reject a 300 clear aperture as a vertical withdrawal route for a 400 gross carrier. Those are concrete adverse findings, not a generic request for more detail.

The alternative investigated here **removes the internal access strip**. Two independently removable, straight carriers pass below a continuous floor through separate permanent sleeves. The carrier ends remain exposed beneath removable court paving and at the exterior transition; each full-length carrier is withdrawn outside. This reduces occupied-room dismantling, but deepens the crossing and creates demanding exterior access and receiving-level requirements. It is not yet admitted: the supporting floor/threshold assembly, sleeve/connection products, water-boundary continuity and high-level access still require resolution. Neither those internal design duties nor the hydraulic failure cases are delegated to a future site designer.

## Why the shallow combination fails this comparison

The official [ACO AL shallow table](https://www.buildingdrainage.aco/products/collect/floor-drainage/cleaning-seals/cover/aco-access-covers/aco-access-cover-uniface/aco-access-cover-uniface-al-shallow) gives a 50 mm frame and 46.5 mm cover, with the stated load category depending on infill/finish. Its 400 clear opening requires a 500 outside frame. The official [2025 global catalogue](https://www.aco-accesscovers.com/fileadmin/standard/ACO_Access_Covers_catalogue_2025.pdf) specifically withholds a watertightness declaration for this shallow variant. These official texts were available through indexed primary excerpts; direct full-document retrieval timed out. No unseen installed section, bottom-up pressure rating or thermal performance is claimed.

UK FACTA variants and global EN 1253 variants are not one matched product. UK triple-seal marketing or a bedding detail cannot silently qualify the global shallow part. Concrete infill cannot be replaced by insulation while retaining its load claim. The listed weight cannot be assumed to be the installed, infilled handling mass.

For the earlier higher carrier, crown−60 below a 50 lid leaves10. Adding insulation thickness *i* and retaining clearance *c* requires downward shift `max(0,i+c−10)`; its old20 outlet margin falls by the same amount. At i30/c10, outlet−160 is10 below schematic grade−150. Spending the entire clearance gives outlet−150, with no positive margin. The30 is a sensitivity input, not a compliant insulation selection. The result rejects this particular combination; it does not prove every thin-floor system impossible.

## End access, removal and leakage

A real [Wavin OsmaDrain manual](https://mediahub.wavin.com/m/2b338ab4d06816a0/original/Wavin-OsmaDrain-product-and-installation-manual.pdf) lists110 plain-end pipe in3 and6 m lengths and the4D105 slip coupler; the [2026 primary product list](https://mediahub.wavin.com/m/3134865c4ab04fef/original/Wavin-Below-Ground-Trade-Price-List-1st-Jan-2026.pdf) corroborates the range. The indexed manual gives115 overall length for that coupler. Full direct retrieval timed out; socket travel, external diameter and insertion/withdrawal forces have not been verified. This establishes a plausible joint-free length family, not a matched approved carrier/sleeve installation or pressure rating.

The body comparison is4450:4250 crossing plus100 exposed at each end. The exterior straight withdrawal territory is5250 long by1600 wide, x5200–6800/y12000–17250, including an authored800 handling allowance. It must remain legally and physically available for actual tools, supports, temporary barriers and set-down. It is **not an assumed plot or universal adequate workspace**. A 4450 body cannot be extracted into the3500 court. The court-end600-deep access territory x5400–6600/y7150–7750 requires removable pavers/supports and reachable joints; an inaccessible coupling would reject the design.

Remove the coupling at the exposed end before withdrawal; do not assume a socket or115-long coupler passes through the160 bore. Provide a removable, continuous carrier support/skid within the20 lower envelope so the pipe does not rest on isolated unknown points. Its geometry, friction, sag, installation and settlement accommodation are outstanding actual design inputs. The sleeve must remain straight and drain outward; a trapped belly invalidates both renewal and leak observation. Testing one empty bore does not establish a loaded carrier's pull force.

Each sleeve has a separately visible exterior tell-tale drain/inspection end. The court end needs a removable, water-resistant annular closure that prevents court floodwater bypassing the intended inlet via the sleeve. It must not form a hidden reservoir: carrier leakage must emerge at the accessible exterior tell-tale without wetting floor insulation or entering the house. That complete seal/support/tell-tale assembly has **not** been selected. A sealed sleeve with no observable drainage is rejected. Failure of the permanent sleeve or its structural protection still entails a larger excavation/floor repair; carrier renewal is not whole-system replaceability.

## Threshold and operating water remain a separate controlling boundary

The [Stormguard SG2000/13 outward product page](https://www.stormguard.co.uk/stormguard-products/sg200013-outward-opening/) confirms an actual outward-opening sill family with drainage/fixing holes and double-door accessories. The manufacturer's2024 retail catalogue, [hosted by JWL](https://jwl-online.co.uk/images/documents/143/stormguard-catalogue-2024.pdf), page45, shows its cross-section. This is a useful geometry comparator, **not** the selected whole2400 doorset. Stock length and a weather test claim do not prove meeting-stile, frame-end, pan, anchor or immersion performance. Current accessory links misroute to unrelated products, so compatible end and thermal components remain unverified. No dimensions from an inward-fitting instruction are transferred to this outward assembly.

The HAD trial's underside−45 versus operating-water target−50 leaves5 nominally. It does not identify the actual first vulnerable joint. [LRWA Guidance Note12](https://www.lrwa.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Guidance-Note-No-12-Termination-of-Waterproofing-at-Cills-and-Thresholds.pdf) is2017 roof/terrace guidance with older standard references: its drainage-layer/upstand distinction and compatibility warning inform the question but do not certify a ground court or flood resistance. The dry135 difference between pan−180 and trial underside−45 cannot substitute for a wet operating-head check.

The connected candidate therefore needs a selected sill/frame, continuous pan turned behind it, actual end dams and wall/cavity returns, compatible seals and every anchor penetration located. The lowest joint/weep/closure exposed to water must be compared against the computed operating level, splash and tolerances. Raising a label to+15 does not raise a concealed joint. This is a **feasible internal design task still open**, not an external permission or an exhausted option search.

The lower route expressly abandons the earlier free-outfall advantage. As a **comparison requirement**, set the maximum exterior receiving water at−585,50 below the carrier's outside bottom−535. This is435 below the schematic grade−150. The50 is a sensitivity margin, not a required legal air gap or demonstrated flood protection. The sleeve inner bottom at the far end is−555, leaving only30 to that receiving level; its separate leak tell-tale must be checked at its own lower elevation, not credited with the carrier’s50. A survey and actual receiving arrangement must show whether such a condition is possible; otherwise reject this building/site pairing or re-author its level/route. No lower garden, soakaway, pump, sewer or lawful outlet is invented.

Primary and relief need separate inlet, carrier, transition and receiving identities. If both exterior receiving surfaces flood together, they are not independent. The required maximum must hold for each under the named primary obstruction and the specified rainfall/backwater event; a valve that closes does not remove new rain. The complete jointed carriers may be pressurised below a−50 court water target: do not infer open-channel capacity from their slope or diameter. Actual inlet losses, transitions, flow/operating levels and leakage behavior must be assessed together with all assigned inner-roof plus direct-court rain.

A safe temporary discharge/collection arrangement is needed before disconnecting either carrier. It cannot rely on the removed primary or its blocked receiver. A favourable forecast alone is not an isolation method. Court pavers, people, tools and temporary hoses must coexist without an unguarded fall/trip or a concealed flood route.

## Can a coiled carrier remove the long exterior renewal corridor?

**Adverse product-bound comparison, 30 October 2026; no new selection.** The current straight north-axis trial reserves a **5250 × 1600 mm** exterior handling strip: a 4450 mm uninterrupted removable body plus 800 mm trial handling allowance. That is **8.4 m² of nominal plan territory**, not confirmed site land, a minimum working regulation, or a guarantee that people and tools can complete removal there. An apparently shorter scheme cannot simply replace it with the assertion that a pipe is flexible.

**A coiled PE100 alternative is real, but not an installed design.** [Radius Systems' May 2025 PE100 black product table](https://www.radius-systems.com/uploads/documents/downloads/Pipe-Brochure-Black-Pipe-Only-20-May-2025.pdf), p. 3, lists **110 mm SDR11 and SDR17** straight 6/12 m lengths *and* 50/100 m coils; the accompanying declared uses concern non-potable water and pressure drains/sewers. This is a different material, joining and product standard from the current [Wavin OsmaDrain PVC-U 110 mm plain-ended comparator](https://mediahub.wavin.com/m/2b338ab4d06816a0/original/Wavin-OsmaDrain-product-and-installation-manual.pdf). Coiled delivery does not establish a suitable gravity-service lining, an inlet joint, a watertight removable sleeve system or an acceptable free-bending installation.

The [PE100+ technical guidance on trench alignment](https://www.pe100plus.com/PE-Pipes/Technical-guidance/model/Construction/Trenching/Is-trench-alignment-critical-when-laying-PE-pipe-i1054.html) gives **conditional laid radii at 20°C for non-oval pipe** of DN×15 for SDR11 and DN×25 for SDR17/17.6: at a nominal 110 mm diameter these would be **1650 mm** and **2750 mm**. It warns against joints in minimum-radius bends and calls for larger radii in cold weather. Other product-specific technical guidance (for example [Performance Pipe's DR guidance](https://www.cpchem.com/what-we-do/solutions/performance-pipe/faq/pe-rt-faq)) uses different figures, including **25× outside diameter for DR11**. These are **not interchangeable installation approvals** and must not be converted into a selected minimum radius for an unidentified pipe.

A horizontal 90° turn contained wholly within the study's **1600 mm-wide** straight extraction strip is therefore **not demonstrated**, even under the smaller **1650 mm centreline-radius** generic example, before allowing for pipe outside, handling, control of the coil, bends or a fused fitting. An externally supported vertical or wider sweep is a distinct access study, not a free reduction of the site corridor. Any option of pulling out and cutting an old carrier incrementally must also demonstrate how a new **joint-free** carrier can be fed in, kept on grade and removed later. Inserting inaccessible socket/fusion joints into the permanent sleeve to make the handling geometry easier merely moves the repair problem under the house.

**Disposition:** Retain the located **5250 × 1600** corridor as this north-candidate's conditional plot/renewal screen. Do not claim that it is universally necessary: a genuinely matched flexible system, guided feed/withdrawal rig and verified bend/force/connector/gradient/tell-tale arrangement could replace it. To change the trial, first select the exact pipe/SDR/product and demonstrate its approved service duty; locate the full installation/removal sweep including rigging and safe isolation; preserve independent accessible ends, straight sleeve grade, continuous support and leak observation; then revise the requirement and the generated study together. Until those tasks succeed, neither coil supply nor cut-out demolition earns a shorter site handoff. The water-level, threshold and receiving-backwater failures elsewhere in this investigation remain untouched.
