# RWT-06 — integrated access trial: first-motion control

**2 November 2026. Development only; no service method is accepted.** This joins one compact conventional stair reservation to the existing ground-screen trial so that gate operation can be tested before claiming that access fits. It is one step towards the coordinated scaffold, lifting and ground-water method, not another selected method. The [source-bound calculation](studies/integrated-access-first-motion.mjs), [ledger](studies/integrated-access-first-motion.json) and [plan](studies/integrated-access-first-motion.svg) preserve an actual adverse configuration and a potential route for redesign.

The compact tower centreline rectangle is x6050–7450 / y5280–6850. The manufacturer gives its outer size as 1530 × 1700 without console brackets, so its outer rectangle is x5985–7515 / y5215–6915. These are the selected 3500 court and trial three-sided screen from the [prior studies](rwt-06-manufacturer-access-comparison.md); this placement moves the tower from the earlier reservation. The outer edge is 115 beyond the end of A's y5100 gross withdrawal, 161 before the east screen's x7676 inner face and 85 before the y7000 inlet. A 150-square foot centred on y6850 ends at y6925; a 205-wide spindle nut aligned north–south reaches y6952.5. These numerical gaps are neither working clearances nor proof that brackets, sole boards, tie loads, bracing, plumbing, weather protection, workers and moving parts coexist.

Give the tower a **trial continuous south-face ledger** from x6050 to7450 at y5280 and z1980. Its selection here is an explicit conservative construction to attack, not a claim that the manufacturer requires that particular member or has approved the assembly. The solid east screen leaf is 1195.5 long, hinged at (7197,4300), and reaches +2250. As it opens into the court its tip reaches the ledger plane at **55.06°**, x6512.30, inside that ledger's length and vertical extent. This declared arrangement cannot open that leaf fully: relocate or redesign the gate/member/transfer as a coordinated guarded assembly. A drawing of its fully opened position cannot establish the first motion. A ledger omission cannot be inferred safe; all required guarding and support must be replaced by a competent actual design.

The west leaf, hinged at (4803,4300), has a minimum plan distance of 1494.77 to the tower outer rectangle, leaving 299.27 beyond its 1195.5 sweep. It is therefore worth testing **court-side west-leaf operation** while the screen and ordinary court-south building door remain in their declared controlled state. This does not establish a usable route: the gate's actuator, latch, seals, forces and first motion, access from the stair, worker width, door use, rescue, fire strategy and water-live states remain unproved. The earlier south-door trap still applies to a person inside behind both closed leaves; isolating that door depends on an independently usable actual front exit and an authorised occupation/escape policy. It is not established by a drawn front door.

The compact tower is not yet a documented outdoor assembly. Its [manufacturer brochure](https://www.layher.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Layher-Temporary-Access.pdf) describes its ordinary use in internal stairwells and asks purchasers to obtain specific assembly and use instructions. An indexed copy of a UK technical guide also reports an internal confined-space condition, but its current revision and authenticity have not yet been independently confirmed. Do not use that reported condition as a universally proved product ban; equally, do not infer that the single-handrail internal configuration is suitable in this open court. Obtain the exact manufacturer's instructions and qualified complete outdoor configuration before relying on it.

The separately documented **four-standard 1400 × 2570 platform stair tower** is an outdoor-capable family worth joining, not a certified placement. An axis trial x5650/7050, y5200/7770 puts the northern 150-square foot plates beyond the y7000–7350 inlet but inside the x4800–7200 loggia opening. Its west and east feet occupy x5575–5725 and x6975–7125, respectively; both intersect the existing x5300–7100 **1800-clear surface relief**. The x4825–5225 normal path is a different, covered channel: do not infer that a cover or load-bearing route exists over the surface relief. Within the opening, the x gaps outside the normal and relief reservations are only **25, 75 and 100**; none holds a 150-square plate. These are plan-reservation conflicts, not proof a particular foot completely blocks hydraulic flow. An actual bridge, elevated support, different geometry or route must retain the relief, the normal route, independent terminal identities and the maintained boundary.

This test deliberately leaves visible the remaining integrated work: draw the actual permitted stair arrangement and level transfers, complete feet/jacks/sole boards, braces and compatible screen ties; place every platform, guard, toe board, gantry, lifting line, tools, workers and rescue envelope against all phases of A handling. Develop screen top, corners, weather seal, apron and gate, an independently draining receiver and deck drains through blocked and common-failure states; demonstrate safe erection, stop, wet pause and reversal. Retain direct court rainfall and the conserved whole event without double counting. Identify the external site interface without assuming a surveyed outlet, tailwater or safe destination. A nominal shape or one clear leaf does not establish any of those duties.

## Joined four-standard and vertical-screen trial

A new [plan and numerical trial](studies/integrated-shutter-trial.svg) turns the published four-standard 1400 × 2570 platform-stair family east–west: axes x4720–7290 and y5425–6825. Four authored 150-square feet are at the axis intersections. Their northern edges stop at y6900, **100 before the inlet**, and the south standards' 48-diameter reservations begin at y5401, **301 beyond A's final y5100**. The 65 per-side outer reservation is an explicit trial allowance, not a claimed manufacturer outer dimension. Plates alone do not establish adequate sole boards, a clear relief, jacking, bracing, ties or an assembly; the feet may bear over an unbounded W2 withdrawal pocket. The tower overlaps the prior +5540 platform in plan and needs an actual guarded landing and transfer; it must not be superimposed on the old unqualified tiny-foot tower.

Replace the swinging screen leaves **in this trial** with a vertically rolling curtain spanning a 2400 clear opening x4800–7200. A temporary independent frame reserves 48-wide guides outside that opening, and a housing x4752–7248 / y4300–4600 / +2250…+2550. This 300-square housing is a space reservation, not a product specification. The manufacturer's [rolling-shutter manual](https://rollac.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/2025V2_Shutter_Manual_and_Warranty.pdf) illustrates manual crank operation and explicitly warns against forcing a frozen or obstructed curtain. Obtain the actual curtain, opening width, box, controls, forces and wind/seal requirements; its manufacture, attachment to the temporary scaffold and weather resistance are not established. The trial housing underside is **131 above** the selected +2119 building leaf top and its top is **1650 below** the first-floor rough sill. Its outer y4600 edge is 760 from the tower's authored outer reservation. These comparisons do not establish safe loads or door operation.

The curtain travels vertically in its guides rather than sweeping through the tower ledger. First, from the **court side**, fully raise its curtain and bottom slat clear above the building-leaf sweep; only then open a building leaf. Closing reverses that order: close the building leaf before lowering the curtain. The closed curtain remains inaccessible from behind the closed building door unless a separately reachable control is supplied. The controlled state therefore requires the actual front final exit and occupied-room/fire strategy independently maintained; this is not yet demonstrated. A jam, freeze or lost control prevents the credited operation; stop work and use a separately proved worker/rescue route. No such independent destination is yet admitted. A hand crank is not an emergency-release proof, and a closed leaf or shutter is not a water seal.

The shutter resolves one geometrical sweep conflict, but the integrated lift is still open. A PGRS20 maximum 1500 clear span does not cover the 3300 receiver, and moving it must preserve its real support and unloaded first motions. Its legs, hoist, sling, tool and the supported receiver's whole withdrawal must be placed against the stair, platform and guards: a beam rectangle is not a lift. Similarly, local screen clearances do not prove complete top, base, corner and threshold weathering, direct and roof-water capture, intact roof layers, isolated branch operation, blocked outlets, high tailwater or site acceptance. The same event still totals 60.0625 m² / 3.60375 L/s; redirecting roof water into the court does not create another roof catchment.

## Scaffold-mounted hoist reach target

A potentially simpler lifting component belongs to the same scaffold family: the [Layher hoist catalogue](https://www.layher.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Layher-System-Free-Accessories-Catalogue.pdf) lists the 150 kg swing arm **4416.015**, swing-arm holder **4416.003**, and Mini/Maxi bottom-mounted rope winches. It expressly requires additional scaffold load transfer and possibly additional anchors. The catalogue does **not** supply an arm reach in this comparison, and its permissible arm load is not the assembled receiver's mass or a completed scaffold design.

The [conditional reach ledger](studies/integrated-swing-hoist.json) tests those arm locations against the seven existing A modules. In the four-standard trial add a **third proposed standard** at (6000,5425), with its own 150-square foot, coupled into the structure by a specifically engineered connection. It is not an automatic standard component of the four-standard stair: actual couplers, member forces, bracing, anchorage, ground load spread and stair entry must be designed. Together with the two south tower standards at x4720/7290, the three nominal arm pivots give a maximum plan distance **1015.995** to the centre of any of the seven modules in the installed line at y4525. The actual arm's permissible centre-to-hook reach must be at least this distance plus the actual wheel, rigging and motion tolerances; no source currently establishes that it does.

The added middle foot occupies x5925–6075/y5350–5500. Between the open building leaves' 48-diameter axis reservations and this foot, west and east approach strips are each **751 gross**. This is only a ground-plan opportunity: actual stair entry, frames, guards, equipment, 600-body passage, shutter and rescue remain to be placed. After the trial 170 lowering and p-shift, the two interior extraction centres x5587.5 and6412.5 are respectively 1001.3 and 1010.0 from the neighbouring pivots when the 400 outward shift reaches y4925. A centre moving on that theoretical circular radius can reach y5900 within the tower width. Its full 471.43-long body, carrier, sling, pulley, roof/corner weathering and end fittings have not been swept: the centreline is not proof of an unobstructed or safe lift. The nominal lower and upper guard-band separations remain 176 and 46, before real post, gate and support projections.

The intended physical sequence, if a specified and engineered system passes those checks, is: isolate and support one module; engage its actual rigging and take the load; lower it 170; move it to a clear interior bay; withdraw it while it remains supported; control the hoist and swing the module into a separately guarded landing; secure and release it. Reverse in controlled steps after repair. The supporting platform and remaining receiver, direct rain, protected ground catch and accessible failure route must remain live throughout or the work stops. No person occupies the suspended-load sweep; the actual controlled operator position, safe line of sight and assistance must be authored. The present study does not establish that the pulley, rigging and hook fit above the installed +6740 lip, that any particular sling clears roof layers, or that the module and carrier satisfy the hoist rating. It does not authorise use of the apparatus or remove the need for the independent failed-shutter and wet-state recovery paths.
