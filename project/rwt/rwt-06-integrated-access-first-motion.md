# RWT-06 — integrated access trial: first-motion control

**2 November 2026. Development only; no service method is accepted.** This joins one compact conventional stair reservation to the existing ground-screen trial so that gate operation can be tested before claiming that access fits. It is one step towards the coordinated scaffold, lifting and ground-water method, not another selected method. The [source-bound calculation](studies/integrated-access-first-motion.mjs), [ledger](studies/integrated-access-first-motion.json) and [plan](studies/integrated-access-first-motion.svg) preserve an actual adverse configuration and a potential route for redesign.

These are the selected 3500 court and trial three-sided screen from the [prior studies](rwt-06-manufacturer-access-comparison.md); this placement moves the tower from the earlier reservation. Give the tower a **trial continuous south-face ledger** from x6050 to7450 at y5280 and z1980. The west leaf, hinged at (4803,4300), has a minimum plan distance of 1494.77 to the tower outer rectangle, leaving 299.27 beyond its 1195.5 sweep. This does not establish a usable route: the gate's actuator, latch, seals, forces and first motion, access from the stair, worker width, door use, rescue, fire strategy and water-live states remain unproved. The earlier south-door trap still applies to a person inside behind both closed leaves; isolating that door depends on an independently usable actual front exit and an authorised occupation/escape policy. It is not established by a drawn front door.

The compact tower is not yet a documented outdoor assembly. Its [manufacturer brochure](https://www.layher.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Layher-Temporary-Access.pdf) describes its ordinary use in internal stairwells and asks purchasers to obtain specific assembly and use instructions. An indexed copy of a UK technical guide also reports an internal confined-space condition, but its current revision and authenticity have not yet been independently confirmed. Do not use that reported condition as a universally proved product ban; equally, do not infer that the single-handrail internal configuration is suitable in this open court. Obtain the exact manufacturer's instructions and qualified complete outdoor configuration before relying on it.

The separately documented **four-standard 1400 × 2570 platform stair tower** is an outdoor-capable family worth joining, not a certified placement. An axis trial x5650/7050, y5200/7770 puts the northern 150-square foot plates beyond the y7000–7350 inlet but inside the x4800–7200 loggia opening. Its west and east feet occupy x5575–5725 and x6975–7125, respectively; both intersect the existing x5300–7100 **1800-clear surface relief**. The x4825–5225 normal path is a different, covered channel: do not infer that a cover or load-bearing route exists over the surface relief. These are plan-reservation conflicts, not proof a particular foot completely blocks hydraulic flow. An actual bridge, elevated support, different geometry or route must retain the relief, the normal route, independent terminal identities and the maintained boundary.

This test deliberately leaves visible the remaining integrated work: draw the actual permitted stair arrangement and level transfers, complete feet/jacks/sole boards, braces and compatible screen ties; place every platform, guard, toe board, gantry, lifting line, tools, workers and rescue envelope against all phases of A handling. Develop screen top, corners, weather seal, apron and gate, an independently draining receiver and deck drains through blocked and common-failure states; demonstrate safe erection, stop, wet pause and reversal. Retain direct court rainfall and the conserved whole event without double counting. Identify the external site interface without assuming a surveyed outlet, tailwater or safe destination. A nominal shape or one clear leaf does not establish any of those duties.

## Scaffold-mounted hoist reach target

The catalogue does **not** supply an arm reach in this comparison, and its permissible arm load is not the assembled receiver's mass or a completed scaffold design.

The added middle foot occupies x5925–6075/y5350–5500. Between the open building leaves' 48-diameter axis reservations and this foot, west and east approach strips are each **751 gross**. The present study does not establish that the pulley, rigging and hook fit above the installed +6740 lip, that any particular sling clears roof layers, or that the module and carrier satisfy the hoist rating. ## Direct-roof bypass around blocked chute ends

The first shaft proposal still left an intrinsic omission: the two exposed side chutes intercept the adjacent side-edge drips, and a blockage at their ends can make those open chutes overflow upstream onto the deck **before** the collector backs up into the shaft. Each lower conduit has a +6310→+5860 floor, 50 sides and a sealed 2 mm cap. It receives the common collector and must retain its water and pressure through three sealed openings in the y5350 shaft wall. Direct rain falling on the cap is collected in an **upper trough** with floor +6362→+5912 and continuous side-wall tops +6412. At y5350, the upper floor is +6337; provide a full 300-wide return opening with crest +6350 into the shaft, above the lower sealed conduit. If the upper outlet becomes blocked, its water can return to the shaft rather than spilling sideways onto the deck, provided the return, shaft, canopy and their joints remain open and intact. The middle upper trough is retained as the same complete geometry, not credited with extra rainfall. This is a physical route proposal; the actual connections, support, ventilation, access for clearing and watertight pressure cap have not been fabricated.

For the conservative **entire 3.60375 L/s** on one 300-wide return, a sharp-crested idealisation gives **34.93 mm** head, placing ideal level 27.07 below the +6412 upper side top. It is a necessary screen only: a 13 mm step, entrance contraction, uneven flow, debris, wind, splash and real receiving head can defeat it. No capacity or freeboard is earned. At the y6150 outer guard plane the upper floor is +5937 and wall top +6412: the existing +6190 midrail passes **through** that wall envelope. The complete rail must pass through two sealed and structurally supported sleeves per trough, without cutting its continuity; it becomes an internal obstruction whose effect on flow, debris removal and loading is unproved. The field, three troughs, return collars, shaft, canopy and south weather protection form one wet assembly; no independent route is earned from labels alone.

Similarly, an obstructed canopy end, outside backwater above the vulnerable ground boundary, failure of a sealed port, or a frozen shutter cannot be cleared by the ideal head calculation.
