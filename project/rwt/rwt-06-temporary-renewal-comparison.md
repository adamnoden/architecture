## Decision and actual cost

**Develop a removable surface bypass in a declared, closed-to-occupation major-renewal state.** Put a top-access, separately supported, short-module copper trough in **x5300–5600**, inside the broad route, and take it to a *separate temporary chute* over the already authored N/W receiver. Its own cleaning and replacement take place from above in short pieces while the permanent normal route and full broad route are available. The remaining **x5600–7100 is 1500 mm**, not the selected 1800 mm. This is a conscious temporary loss of 300 mm and can be allowed only if the remaining full-length relief and E chute are proved at the complete event with the temporary route blocked. The normal 1800 width returns after removal. A 4250 liner cannot be laid in the existing body stage x5700–6050/y7100–11350: that stage blocks the remaining relief. Export it north at height to a separate exterior bridge/stage instead. The service corridor and both receiver territories become a plot-selection and temporary-works cost; neither is currently surveyed.

This direction costs a surface collector/diverter at the **actual full-width court inlet**, a **removable 350-wide collector and 300-wide finish strip** with independent RC-supported shoes and normal-state flush load-rated infill, at least twelve ≤500 mm longitudinal modules over y7350–13200 (5850 mm), a separately modular full-width collector and a terminal chute, a supported exterior 4250 mm stage and roughly 5900 mm northward trolley travel. The strip deliberately removes the local walking finish to a maximum 31 mm depth; it does not cut RC200 or treat the remaining stone sliver as structural. The V-collector itself needs separately releasable top-access transverse lengths: x4250–5300 needs at least three ≤500 pieces and x5300–7750 at least five, plus the outlet/transition. Their joints and supports cannot be hidden under a one-piece 3500 cover. Remove a collector module upward into a protected court staging row while the ordinary liner and full 1800 relief are intact; repair/replace its accessible joint, test and reinstate before diverting rain. During liner exchange, collector failure relies on the separately proved 1500/E route, not an instant wet repair or an assumed live normal liner. Exact stock lengths depend on the joint detail; no continuous supplied product or seamless joint is asserted. No permanent court widening is selected. If the head, independent receivers, 1500 residual relief, stage or worker protection cannot be qualified, **reject this service state and replan the court/portals**; a favourable forecast does not make the old liner removable.

## Installation, whole-body exchange, and removal

1. **Prepare while both source routes are intact.** Qualify the 1500/E fallback and inspect the N/W and E receivers and guards; keep the existing 1800 relief unobstructed until the temporary modules have a controlled construction zone. Release the designed flush infill pieces and set independent shoes in the x5300–5600 recessed finish strip without drilling the permanent liner, loading the copper or using a wet joint as an anchor. Fit at least twelve ≤500 longitudinal modules from north to south with each seam top-accessible and independently releasable. Attach the visible N/W chute, provide an open water-witness/controlled leak path, and prove it discharges at a receiving level below its controlling water level. The 300 occupied width is declared from the first module onward; normal intake and ordinary carrier remain live while the 1500 residual route is built and checked.
2. **Intercept before cutting.** Release the full-width existing intake cover while its lower normal path remains live. Fit the separately releasable transverse V-collector modules above that depressed throat at the stated x/y inverts; its upper east/west entries must actually receive roof arrivals and direct-court rain, not strand a lower original opening under an uphill temporary outlet. Provide the open y7350/−94 overrun and y7750/−110 apron into x5600–7100 without a continuous court dam, and protect the south walkover. Prove the coupled whole 216 event and controlled single faults through collector, modules, continuing terrace fall, both chutes and both receiving levels with the normal route blanked **before** disconnecting the liner. Restore the normal intake and full 1800 surface after the work, including the separate first water boundary at the old U.
3. **Exchange with both temporary N/W and residual E paths present.** Remove the normal end joints, nine covers and understrips in the MAT-authored release order. The former 4250 body stage intersects x5700–6050 of the residual relief and is forbidden for this state. Pick the 25.704 kg liner at its two authored stations with balanced cradles and a separately designed remote gantry; first release and hand positions need actual access. Raise **1350 mm**, then translate north without setting load, foot, outrigger, scaffold or tool into x5300–5600 or x5600–7100. With an authored 1100 guard above terrace−152.5, its top is +947.5; the liner's north bottom at −256.25 +1350 is +1093.75, **146.25 mm nominal** over that guard. Its south rigging top is +1362.75, **812.25 mm nominal** below the +2175 tie underside. Those are screens only: cradle depth, deflection, horizontal swing, portal radii, guard fixings and raised-terrain profiles can erase them. Translate north about **5900 mm** to an exterior body stage y13650–17900, supported above/bridging the N/W receiver without blocking either discharge or the separate E receiver. The actual beam, rail, outriggers, receiver bridge, ground/terrace load path and plot territory need a single temporary-works design; the old 600 lift cannot clear the guard.
4. **Control people and rain.** Close the working loggia to ordinary occupation. During a suspended load, evacuate the 1500 residual flow/handling strip and keep controls at protected south-court or north-exterior stations, outside the load and receiver splash. The south exit crosses the full-width intake and the north exit reaches the garden ramp; both need actual supported, non-slippery walkover and guarded continuity in this temporary state. A wet surface flow path is not an occupied escape aisle during the lift. Stop lifting and return to the safe service state if rain, joint leakage, a blocked chute, rising tailwater or loss of either operator route occurs; the rain path must remain functional during a stopped lift. Do not credit a forecast, a pump or an operator moving a body at the moment rain starts.
5. **Reinstate, test, and remove.** Install the new whole liner by the reversed supported north travel and lower it onto its own seats without loading the temporary modules. Rebuild/inspect the U joints, seals, clamps, understrips and covers; test actual capture and N/W discharge with the normal route restored. Only then move the selector back and remove the 300 modules from south to north, preserving normal flow and progressively restoring the full 1800 relief. Inspect the removable shoes, floor/threshold water boundaries, temporary inlet and terminal sites, dry disturbed construction, restore the guard and record both receiving paths. The temporary modules can be taken away separately; no concealed third permanent trough is inherited.

## Rejected control and architectural fallback

A **300 clear ×38 wall** U below the permanent 303-outside liner screens at **28.77 mm** uniform depth for the 216 probe. Fifty millimetres lower, it would invert −263.75 at y7750, −306.25 at12000 and −318.25 at13200. The present 100 mm recess gives only **2.75 mm** bottom space at the flat terrace end; an explicit 15 mm deeper outdoor recess increases that to **17.75 mm** while reducing the deck-soffit-to-low-tie-top comparison from 27.5 to about 12.5 mm at the north end, keeping RC200. It cannot connect uphill to the permanent liner's higher −256/−268 tail. A separate lower chute into the existing −600 N/W downbody would be required.

This apparently clean full-1800 solution is **rejected as the current service choice**. A retained lower body beneath an intact 4250 liner has no top access to its seams/supports; removing the liner into the current stage blocks the 1800 relief. Axial extraction of a seamless 4250–5450 body would demand a real straight exterior launch/runway across the y13200 guard, the normal downbody and N/W receiver, with temporary support and reachable end releases; neither that geometry nor a sourced sectional joint that can be assembled and renewed under the liner is in the selected source. Building it first would conceal a new maintenance trap. The geometric comparison is retained to prevent a shallow recess or a shared receiver string being mistaken for an installed bypass.

Permanent retention of 1800 relief **during** renewal would require added passage width. The present 2400 consists of 400 normal +75 separation +1800 relief +125 residual. A separate 300 gross trough with useful separation needs at least **2700 finished width**, and a practical wall/arch/support margin is more than a mere 300 added to the loggia. A 2700 finished portal with the same 280 radial wraps gives at least3260 rough span; within the existing3500 court only120 remains on either side, less than the present212.5 RC pier before any bearing/tolerance. Widening the court, changing the portal/roof catchment and room/support grids, relocating thresholds and recasting the 60.0625 m² event would be a new architectural source decision. It may be justified if the conditional 1500 maintenance state or exterior stage fails; it is not an unchanged-width fix.

**Disposition:** the surface major-renewal state is the lower architectural-cost development candidate. It has explicit inlet, receiver, first-leak, failure, temporary-works and human-operation gates, so no wet-state maintenance or node completion is claimed. RWT-07 receives no accepted network. The high eave at +7100 is a separate receiver-clearing/replacement task: a ground liner gantry or surface bypass does not put workers at the zinc hem. Develop its guarded working position, independently supported receiver handling and temporary roof-water interception under ROF/RWT before high-level renewal, with the same first-harm and both-receiver failure test. If that cannot coexist with a live temporary route, revise the collector/roof access architecture, not the status label.
