# RWT-06 — reject the single-north topology for independent court-water escape

**5 November 2026. Starting main: `3c0a42576bfedb5fab6d7de07dfeaac87836cbda`.** This is a design-admission rejection and correction of the architectural prerequisite. It does not complete RWT-06, adopt a new roof or passage, undo HAD-03's bounded architectural development result, or establish a real-site failure prediction.

## Decision

**The selected compact B/open north loggia cannot be admitted as the court-water topology for the required blocked-normal-path and independent passive emergency duty.** Stop autonomous RWT-06 carrier, receiver and equipment refinement against this unchanged topology. Complete the source-owning **HAD-WATER-REPLAN** before resuming it.

The [HAD selection](../had/had-03-open-north-loggia.md#water-disposition-and-source-order) provides two nominally separate water strips but one north mouth and receiving terrace. It explicitly retains a blanket spanning both routes and flooded/high-tailwater terrace as a common failure. A second liner, pipe or inlet within that same disabled territory does not supply the required independent escape. The [court-egress decision](court-egress-architectural-rewind.md#reframe-the-design-as-two-separate-egress-obligations) already requires that escape; this result applies that requirement to the *current selected source* rather than continuing the historical carrier search.

The failure is topological before it is a pipe-capacity question. This does **not** assert that every storm floods the house, that all north-loggia architecture is impossible, or that an east passage is automatically the right solution.

## Executable architectural handoff

The [HAD replan contract](../had/house-architectural-design-programme.md#court-and-roof-water-replan) owns the finite next design task. It requires **one joined, source-bound plan/section selection**, a genuinely separate passive full-duty escape territory, located first-wetting boundaries, credible support and full weather-wall reservations, retained occupation and renewal operations, and explicit downstream source reconciliation. It permits rejection or substantial recomposition of the courtyard arrangement; it does not require preserving the losing drain layout or selecting the outward roof.

The replan develops the architectural source and support reservations with STR; it does not require completed STR-HOUSE or the downstream RWT-06 hydraulic acceptance to invent those inputs. RWT then computes and tests the chosen actual normal/failed water routes. ROF-RWT-GATE and TRIAD remain the later shared admission gates. A real-site handoff records required receiving-water limits and independence as **conditions**, not guessed plot facts; actual plot assessment and independent professional/physical evidence retain their existing owners.

**Control change:** HAD-03 stays complete at its earned scope. The new self-service HAD-WATER-REPLAN node precedes RWT-06; RWT-06 stays planned and becomes blocked. RWT-07 remains blocked. This adds one real architectural obligation; it does not manufacture completed packets or promote maturity.

**Registration boundary:** this PR bootstraps only the new node identity and its programme contract under the existing RWT-06 lease, following coordinated root-cause review. The node did not exist on main and could not previously be claimed. No worker is assigned its execution here, no existing HAD source geometry is edited, and no lease is fabricated for the new node. Release the RWT-06 lease promptly after this dependency change enters main so it does not remain an invalid blocked-node occupancy.
