## Question and earned result

What can the pre-site house legitimately use to compare rainwater systems before a plot, selected roof topology and drainage calculation exist?

The result establishes effective-area/flow reasoning, a demand-and-event record, whole-route capacity questions, separate obstruction/exceedance scenarios and named evidence owners. It does not pick a default London storm, a generic downpipe size, an unearned fall or a safe ponding depth. RWT-02/03/04 can use this common basis without waiting for site information that is unnecessary to compare families.

The essential finding is that collection topology and failure consequence matter before nominal bore selection. The nominal 12.25 m² court is small but can still accumulate consequential water; the worked sensitivity shows the arithmetic without presenting it as a design flood. The 131.75 m² roof annulus and eight drawn arrows do not establish effective catchments or eight equal hydraulic loads.

## Evidence and red-team dispositions

- The England Approved Document H route and BSI standard identity/scope were checked against their primary publishers. The licensed standard was not accessed, so there is no invented clause-level compliance result.
- Historic England’s effective-area/rainfall/failure guidance challenges the convenient fixed-intensity shortcut. EA management-catchment climate guidance is retained as actual-project input, not converted into one London multiplier.
- A manufacturer’s simple-face equation supports an illustrative method; it is not applied blindly to the ring roof or used to approve its products.
- Overflow activation alone was rejected as proof: the operating water level at required discharge and the downstream condition also matter. Two outlets sharing a blocked route are not evidence of independent backup.
- Capacity, site acceptance, safe access and construction tolerances remain distinct evidence obligations. No hydraulic solver or permanent test harness is justified by this research packet.

The existing roof/water seam, selected geometry, public house claims and maturity ceiling remain unchanged. The owning programme and RWT-01 node are reconciled; generated control projections are left to main automation.
