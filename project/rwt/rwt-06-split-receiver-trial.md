## Staged movement

All paths are contingent on an independently tested live A and temporary routes, with both roof layers and direct court rain accounted for. A single failed branch requires the other nominated system to carry the conservative entire 3.60375 L/s event; no unearned allocation is assigned.

1. With A and adjacent receivers working, install and support corner pieces, independent outlets and shields first; then install the middle and both its independent outlets. Resolve the intermediate wet seams during installation, not merely the final closed state. Test the middle and each corner separately through its selected fixed branch while A and the alternative remain available. The drain to the real ground routes and common failure conditions must be modelled before operation.
2. Open and remove one A module only while B has proved full live capture and disposal. Restore all A pieces, permanent outlets and seams, then prove A's full live path. Establish and separately test **each** temporary return into A before detaching that piece's fixed pipe. The box's two plates are intended to make this transition reversible: prove the return with pipe gate closed, reopen the pipe if needed, and only then uncouple it; the opposite live path must be capable throughout. Four physical branches, not a nominal pair, need this procedure.
3. Corners and their shields remain live. Move the middle outward: its p4501.5–7498.5 envelope misses both perpendicular primary lines x4450/7550 and the transferred x4470/7530 lines. At 240 out, the south drips are outside it and its return still overlaps A by 30. Drain and remove it completely from the corner movement and access envelope before moving either corner. The 30 overlap is a nominal geometric reserve, not a wet-flow proof.
4. With the middle gone, move each independently supported corner **toward court centre and outward**. At 155 in p and 130 outward, the side and south secondary drip lines have just 10 nominal clearance and the return overlaps A by 140. A larger 170/145 stop increases both nominal clearances to 25 and retains 125 overlap; use this as the trial stop. The attached shield and return move with the corner. Stop, drain, verify the surviving live receiver, and remove the corner apparatus. Reverse the complete sequence for installation. Actual box, fixed pipe, guide, gate and support disconnection sweeps must also clear this diagonal path; the plan alone does not prove them.

The middle is assumed removed from the whole work area before corner movement, not merely parked at 400.
