# RWT-06 — conventional access versus the selected court

**2 November 2026. Development comparison, not an equipment selection, revised house or safe method.** The tiny-foot ladder/rescue reservation and hand-operated gate in the [ground challenger](rwt-06-ground-catcher-challenge.md) are useful collision studies but are **not a demonstrated scaffold or rescue apparatus**. Prefer a complete conventional guarded stair, compatible working scaffold, lifting and weather protection designed as one temporary system. Do not delete stabilisers, braces, guardrails, gate controls or required components to make a product fit. The [calculation](studies/manufacturer-access-comparison.mjs), [ledger](studies/manufacturer-access-comparison.json) and [comparison sheet](studies/manufacturer-access-comparison.svg) and [bounding plan](studies/manufacturer-access-plan.svg) bind the selected house and edge and retain both actual and hypothetical catchment arithmetic.

## One published mobile stair tower fails an axis-aligned fit

The manufacturer describes the [BoSS Staircase AGR](https://www.bossaccesstowers.com/products/staircase-tower) as 1450 wide with 1800 or 2500 platform lengths. Its manufacturer-linked [instruction manual PN126639-01](https://wernerco.widen.net/view/pdf/cowf3dw4mg/BoSS_Staircase_AGR_IM_PN126639-01.pdf?t.download=true&u=sdv0ai), [Rev B July 2025 copy](https://pasma.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/PN126639-01_BoSS_Staircase-AGR_IM_RevB-0725_PRINT.pdf), quantity schedule and stabiliser table pp.8–9, gives four fully extended **SP10** for the external 6400-platform configuration and X/Y **4832 × 4832** for the 1800 platform length. On an axis-aligned plan this exceeds the selected 3500 court by **1332 each way**, before screen frame, access, wall, door, inlet and working tolerance. The actual stabiliser-pad positions, slope, wheel delivery and contact loads still need specific assessment; the rectangle does not establish that every rotated configuration or every other scaffold is impossible.

The external 4400-platform option has an advertised 6400 working height. At a hypothetical base at world 0 that is **606.74 below** the selected +7006.74 physical eave; at −110 it is **716.74 below**. The advertised working-height convention is not a worker/tool-reach proof. The next listed 6400 platform has its own actual guard/roof and transfer questions even if its stabilisers fit. The manual also states on p.4 that the tower is a work platform, not a means of access to another structure, and prohibits attaching sheets, tarpaulins or signs outdoors. Thus even a larger court would not authorise transferring workers from this stock tower onto a separate scaffold. Therefore the ground challenger's rainscreen cannot be declared part of a standard outdoor BoSS tower; a separate properly supported enclosure is another spatial and wind-load problem. This is an exact product/configuration rejection for the shown orientation, not rejection of conventional access as a class.

## A smaller fixed modular staircase deserves an actual joined test

[Layher's temporary-access catalogue](https://www.layher.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Layher-Temporary-Access.pdf) describes an Allround modular stair-tower ground plan **1400 × 2570** with a **640-wide** comfort staircase. Its [site-stair description](https://www.layher.co.uk/site-stairs/) identifies 640-wide platform-stair modules, 2570/3070 lengths and 1/1.5/2 m lifts, and requires an actual tied/design arrangement. Do not infer that those separately described dimensions automatically certify the exact assembled tower, staircase, weather sheeting, loading or lift heights here. The source is a real product-family lead for one integrated designed scaffold; verify the exact component and assembly manual with the supplier.

Place a **bounding plan only** at p6200–7600/y5125–7695. The nearest boundary is 25 beyond the trial A's final y5100 withdrawal; this has no allowance for fasteners, loaded cradle, workers or movement. Its plan spans the **entire 350 inlet length** y7000–7350 and overlaps **900 of the 1800 relief width** in x. A scaffold is not a solid rectangle: the box does not prove the inlet is blocked. Equally, it cannot prove it is open. The exact base plates, diagonals, ladder/stairs, bracing, water-live bridges, guards and swing/handling envelopes must be placed against the selected full-width intake, both routes, A's continuous travel, the south screen and the closed/open screen-gate operation. The east screen frame face at p7676 is 76 from the bounding box. The fully open east screen gate axis p7197/y4300–5495.5 lies inside that box for 370.5 along y; the west gate axis p4803 is outside it. This does not establish a leaf/member collision because the box is not solid, but it prevents claiming both gate routes clear without actual member and intermediate-swing geometry. The tower and previously drawn scaffold pads/ladder cannot simply be superimposed: design **one** coordinated system with compatible access, receiving deck, screen support, independent receiver handling and rescue, and route the court rain around its actual feet and members.

The same manufacturer's catalogue p.8 describes a **different compact stairtower**, 1570 × 1400 in ground plan **without console brackets**, using a 1250 × 600 U-stair with listed exit spacings 2500/2750/3000. It is worth testing before enlarging the house. A nominal axis plan p6200–7600/y5340–6910 has an [outer envelope 1530 × 1700](https://www.layher.com/-/media/project/layher/layherkg/downloads/en_system_solutions_civil_engineering-_2021.pdf), p6135–7665/y5275–6975. This corrects the earlier treatment of axis dimensions as the outside box. Its outer edge is 175 beyond A's y5100 final withdrawal and only 11 from the east screen frame, before brackets, fixings and tolerance. With a hypothetical [Layher 150-square foot plate](https://www.layher.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Layher-System-Free-Accessories-Catalogue.pdf) centred on its north end, the plate reaches y6985, only 15 before the inlet begins y7000. The same catalogue p.6 gives a 205-wide spindle nut wing: if centred at y6910 and oriented north–south, its plan reaches y7012.5, 12.5 into the inlet. The nut elevation and rotation matter, so this is a projected clearance challenge, not proof of hydraulic blockage. The complete jack, spindle, nut, foot, sole board and required console assembly must be drawn at its actual elevation and orientation; the 15 foot-plate gap is not full support clearance. The opened east screen gate axis p7197 lies in this outer bounding plan for 220.5 along y, while the west gate lies outside it. The tower box is not a solid object, so an actual gate/member clash or opening is not established. The prior ground-challenger deck pipes p5000/7000 at y5900 also put the east pipe inside the box. Two revised vertical-drop candidates at (4800,6300) and (5000,6700), each 150 outside, are outside the compact box and the p5125–5950 escape ramp, with 50 nominal between them in p; their respective actual ground continuations, common blockage, water-live supports and connection to normal/E receiving routes remain undesigned. Do not claim four independent discharge routes merely by drawing two nearby pipes.

For a height comparison only, the selected landing is −21.8 at y5340. If the exact permitted compact assembly had two 2750 exit spacings from that level, it would reach +5478.2, **61.8 below** the trial +5540 working deck. This is arithmetic, not a manufacturer's permitted two-flight assembly, adjustable-jack setting or safe 61.8 step. Obtain the matched assembly and actual level adjustment, support, ties, guard, transfer and rescue arrangement; a compact ground rectangle without its required brackets is not a complete tower. The 600-wide stair is not automatically a suitable casualty-handling route, and no distinct qualified rescue hoist has been matched.

The manufacturer also identifies its [Protect panel family](https://www.layher.co.uk/protect-system/) as compatible with Allround and SpeedyScaf. That offers a conventional integrated screen direction with complete manufacturer components, in contrast to attaching sheeting to the prohibited BoSS mobile tower. The product page describes steel-sheet or plastic-web infill in an aluminium frame; the temporary assembly's actual constituent choice, frame-to-roof/base/door weathering, wind, structural ties, panel sizes and erection/reversal are not established by that family description. Do not infer a water-tight weather seal or alter a rated temporary kit by selecting a material in prose.

A separate [REID PGRS20](https://uk.reidlifting.com/s/REID-UK-MasterBrochure-V22-lo-res.pdf) is a real portable lifting-family comparator. The cited V22 table gives 2000 beam length, 1100–1500 clear operating span, **1445 minimum to the lifting eye**, **1776 minimum to top of beam** and **1158 width**. The separate safety brochure rounds the minimum eye to 1450; do not mix a selected product revision's dimensions. On the +5540 trial deck these give minimum eye +6985 and top +7316. A p5200–7200/y4886–6044 bounding placement leaves 106 at each y edge of the 1370 deck before guards and feet, and lies within the source's physical roof opening p/y4450–7550. Thus top above the +7006.74 eave does **not** alone prove a roof collision. At the lower-drip section the eye is 71.11 above +6913.887, so it cannot be treated as an unobstructed below-drip hook placed directly at that line.

The 3300 A line is **1800 longer than the gantry's maximum 1500 clear span**; one fixed setup cannot cover all of it. The proposed gantry bounding plan overlaps the compact tower box by **0.818985 m²**, and A's outward movement overlaps the gantry's y box by 214; neither intersection proves a clash of actual members. For this PGRS20 comparison, multiple separately supported, manufacturer-permitted unloaded relocations, actual A-frame feet, top/beam, hoist, hook, slings, loaded sweep, rescue rating and deck guard/worker positions must be designed in the same coordinated scaffold. The published goods WLL is not evidence that a particular personnel rescue or a tower-mounted gantry arrangement is approved.

## If the actual standard assembly cannot fit, replan the architecture deliberately

A conservative **axis-aligned dimensional stress**, not an adopted court, takes the 4832 BoSS stabiliser rectangle, a screen centre 50 inside each opposing wall, 24 half-frame and an extra authored 50 clear to each screen. A square court of **5080** gives those 50 gaps; the current 3500 does not. The 50 is an explicit design reserve, not a rule that it suffices for assembly, access, ground contact, wind or rescuing a person. It also assumes four-sided screen reservations for comparison even though the current screen trial has three; it does not establish that a 5080 square is a universal minimum.

If centred inside the unchanged hypothetical 12000 shell, this moves each court edge **790** outward: x/y4250–7750 becomes 3460–8540; each nominal side band drops from 4250 to **3460**, and the existing straight north-loggia length 7750–12000 falls from 4250 to **3460**. It is a substantial house redesign, not a field adjustment. HAD must decide the room, protected/accessible circulation, doors, court use and roof form; STR the span, foundations, piers and temporary support; MAT the actual perimeter, DPC, cavity drainage, air/gas/thermal joins and repair; RWT the collector, intake, normal/relief routing and first harm. MAT explicitly notes that the present 519.5 loggia/portal and separate 429.5 south-west occurrence cannot be extended by symmetry. No one of those sources is changed by this stress.

Holding **only for the calculation** the 12000 shell, 2125 ridge inset, 200 overhang, same roof topology and 216 mm/h event, the total remains 60.0625 m² but its ownership changes. The wall-line ledger becomes roof **34.2561 m² / 2.055366 L/s** plus court **25.8064 m² / 1.548384 L/s**. The consistent physical-overhang ledger becomes roof **38.1601 m² / 2.289606 L/s** plus exposed court **21.9024 m² / 1.314144 L/s**. Both total **3.60375 L/s**; one must not add the enlarged roof offset to the full wall-line court. Conservation here is an algebraic sensitivity, not proof the revised roof still has this topology, the same concentration, safe drainage or site acceptance. Actual HAD roof/corner redraw and coupled event must replace the assumption.

**Decision:** keep the present house source and all unresolved safety gates. Test the actual smaller fixed modular staircase and a separately compatible hoist/enclosure as a *single* source-bound temporary-works assembly before selecting it; account for its inlet, relief, gate, access and rescue, and test its installation and reversal while rain remains managed. If the whole assembly cannot fit, the 5080 stress describes a cost for coordinated architectural reconsideration rather than silently shrinking a real manufacturer's footprint. RWT-06 remains planned and no RWT-07 system is accepted.
