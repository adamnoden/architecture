# RWT-06 — live receiver renewal: a reversible edge trial

**2 November 2026. Constructive development, not an admitted method; RWT-06 remains planned.** The [preceding same-section comparison](rwt-06-high-eave-service-comparison.md) rejected its outboard cup: it missed the lower drip and occupied the receiver withdrawal. This trial changes the *ordinary receiver itself* and adds a temporary full-length tray; it does not adopt the rejected gutter, the roofward cut, a site, or a product. The [generator](studies/live-receiver-renewal.mjs), [ledger](studies/live-receiver-renewal.json) and [section sheet](studies/live-receiver-renewal.svg) pin the selected house and MAT adverse edge source. All dimensions are millimetres and world heights unless stated. **A nominal section clearance is not a wet-operation, hydraulic, load or human-use pass.**

## A reversible live sequence to qualify

The sequence is a design obligation for **both ends**, not a claim that it has been performed. Roof capture and direct court rainfall remain live in every stop state. Each branch and receiving route must first be demonstrated against the whole assigned 3.60375 L/s stress, with its opposite branch out of action; only one branch is isolated at a time and the other must remain proved for that full duty. This stress is deliberately conservative and is not an invented allocation of flow to the two ends.

1. With A, its two outlet routes and the court's normal and relief routes live, establish the protected, independently supported scaffold, both B downpipes, receiver destinations and the B handling rig. Fit returns to B before movement, including end closures, seals and restraints. Test A with both returns and full branch demand before relying on it. The moving 300-wide return has to clear actual fixed pipe and hopper geometry; no flexible joint or invisible sliding socket is assumed.
2. Enter B from 400 outward in stages, while A remains live. Return overlap starts just before 270 outward, before the secondary drip first enters B at 120 outward and the primary at 100. At 240 outward the return end is q−420, overlapping A's outer edge by 30, and the B inner lip q−340 remains 120 outward of the secondary drip. Thus a return can be receiving before B reaches either drip. Continue only with a supported, controlled transfer and measured water containment; test both drip paths, ends, B outlets and each downpipe while A remains available. Connect the fixed B pipes through accessible releasable connections. This geometry alone does not show an installable connection.
3. Demonstrate each B branch alone at the assigned stress and at actual downstream head; check both primary and secondary capture and end/corner continuity, then disconnect and remove one A module. Isolate and seal its real joints and outlet as necessary, lower 170, shift into its named p bay, then withdraw 400. Each 450 × 350 × 30 carrier reserves recessed, flush attachment eyes; none may project outside that envelope. A separate rated hoist or cradle must engage and control the actual eyes and load within the swept envelope, including slings, travel and release; no hand grip or clip may be imagined outside it. The reservation does not yet prove a device can engage or lift there. Maintain B's drainage during a stopped lift, rainfall and replacement. Repeat individually, preserving retained modules and their supports.
4. Restore every A module, seal and outlet, and prove A's complete live path at full stress, including both layers, all seams, its end terminations and its receiving routes. With B still catching both drips and its fixed downpipes live, connect **both** independent B return chutes to discharge into A. Prove each return alone at full assigned stress into live A; check that switching and any isolation cannot block the only surviving path. Only after this test detach the fixed B downpipes **one at a time**, with the opposite branch still proved, and secure each released end. This requires an actual controllable transfer socket, support and safe access; a drawing of adjacent channels is not that device.
5. Withdraw B and its attached returns together. Between installed and 240 outward, water from B goes by the returns into A. At 240 outward the returns still overlap A by 30, while both drips are already outside B and enter A. Pause, drain and verify no retained water can spill on the receiver, people or fabric; only then move the remaining 160 outward. Do not keep a fixed downpipe attached through B's travel. Restore the ordinary receiver, remove the temporary apparatus and independently verify roof, court and both receiving routes. Reversal in step 2 depends on the same overlap and stop conditions, not a forecast of dry weather.

The current proposal is **not ready to execute**. In particular, a real two-position transfer at the constrained +6776.8 tail/+6760 return lip has not been drawn or proved; its seals, insertion path, free surface, access and individual branch isolation may consume the entire 16.8 gap. If it cannot be made, revise the section or tray topology rather than claiming the sequence from clearance arithmetic. A branch passing the necessary ideal energy screen does not prove the combined apparatus at full event, with tailwater, blockage, wave, air entrainment and overflow. A blocked shared B, corner, A, court inlet or common receiving water can defeat both branches. There is no demonstrated harmless overflow.

## Working position and support trial

The earlier 700-deep deck placed the downpipes through the working strip and put ground-level inner posts in the outward door sweep. Replace that layout in this trial with deck p4350–7650, y4780–6150 at +5540. Reserve 200-square holes around each downpipe at x4400–4600 and 7400–7600, y5200–5400; the strip beyond the holes to an outer guard at y6150 is nominally 726 after a 24 tube radius. Provide inner and outer top/mid rails, end guards, toe boards, protected pipe-hole edges and a closable guarded access gate; these are required *complete* components, not a claim that they have been sized. A 150-high toe board ends at +5690, 700 below the lowered carrier; a 20-deep inner-post base plate at deck level is lower still. The deck beams must remain below deck +5540 and their projecting edges and fixings must stay clear of moving channels and the door. Inner top and midrail centres are +6640 and +6190. Suspended inner guard posts start at the elevated deck, **not on ground through the door sweep**. Ground pads 400 square centred at (4500,5900), (6000,5900), (7500,5900) start at y5700, 600 beyond the gross open-leaf north tip y5100, and finish at y6100 before the y7000 inlet. The door's actual leaf, closer, locking, landing, escape route and ground-level braces still require joined examination.

This is a **support topology reservation**, not an engineered scaffold. Deck beams must carry the inner edge and guard load to the outboard posts without a post or brace entering the door's operational volume; outer overhang, beam depth, guard attachment, base plates, bracing, tie/ballast, uplift, bearing, loading and rescue need actual design and verification. Pipes and holes must be protected without reducing the travel/handling route or blocking the live court. The first-floor window rough heads are below the lowered A carrier, but real sash sweeps, frames, deck framing and safe worker reach are not represented. The selected source supplies first openings directly; coordinated HAD `courtSouth` projection is in unmerged PR #1267 at head `6a889aa7b26b0a10719a915972f0204047becf88`, blob `eb70d77ee57571088c4a73caa076681dc00590cd`, and must be reconciled after integration. It is not silently treated as merged authority here.

## Conserved event and remaining design work

At 216 mm/h the wall-line ledger is roof 47.8125 m² / 2.86875 L/s plus court 12.25 m² / 0.735 L/s. The physical-overhang ledger transfers area from exposed court to roof: 50.4525 m² / 3.02715 L/s plus 9.61 m² / 0.5766 L/s. Both conserve 60.0625 m² / **3.60375 L/s**. Adding the overhang to the roof while retaining the full wall-line court double-counts it. No equal valley or branch allocation is assumed.

Before execution, RWT/ROF/MAT must author the physical weather ends and corner/valley capture, seals and supported finite movement including handles, working envelope, actual transfer sockets, end discharge, A installation, module joints, apparatus and full live failure states. Qualify flow with entrance, turn, roughness, local water levels, freeboard, splash, debris, overflow and receiving head; independently check retained support, sash and door operation. DPL supplies the actual site and receiving levels; physical and product trials and competent execution evidence remain later duties, not substitutes for missing internal geometry. No result here completes RWT-06 or unlocks RWT-07.
