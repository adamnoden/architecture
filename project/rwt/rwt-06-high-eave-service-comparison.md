## Decision and design price

**Keep a limited in-basket clearing comparator. Reject the current outboard-cup/two-branch receiver-renewal proposal.** A Genie AWP-25S standard-base CE machine with outdoor outriggers can in principle reach the eave height. Its published 3000 × 2800 deployment, however, cannot occupy the 3500 court wholly south of the full-width y7000–7350 inlet: that dry-to-inlet depth is 2750. Even with its short side along the court axis and zero wall clearance, at least **50 mm** of the setup envelope reaches the inlet; the historical centred x4500–7500/y4600–7400 trial reaches it for **350 mm**. Rotation makes the minimum overlap 250. A full machine setup cannot be declared to stand on intact continuous floor, and the source does not identify the four jack-pad coordinates within that rectangle. Its delivered wheels must cross the inlet. Design an independent load-rated water-live wheel bridge, then prove whether all four pad/contact/sweep envelopes can straddle the inlet or need separate designed support over it; reject this machine if neither fits. This is a specific floor/support redesign, not an averaged 8.4 m² load.

Receiver renewal still requires a scaffold with located bases, ties, working and loading bay, guarded access/rescue and a separate hoist/cradle. The **roof-water architecture must change first**: a cup below the primary hem but above the lower secondary drip, inside the gutter's withdrawal sweep, cannot supply the claimed live temporary branches. A roofward two-layer interception direction needs a new roof cut, supported primary/secondary lap and independent west-normal/N/W and east-relief/E exits. This is a substantial ROF/MAT/RWT source change, not a field-fitting fix. A single lift basket is neither receiver hoist nor path onto the roof. Do not disconnect the receiver until that upstream route can be installed and tested while the original gutter stays live and can stay live through every removal/repair stop state.

### Deliberate roofward development alternative and its installation test

Develop **one independently supported dual-layer roofward interception cassette at q250**, with separate primary zinc runoff and lower breather-water laps that merge only into a visible accessible collector **after** both have been safely intercepted. Provide two genuinely full-duty, demountable west/east exits to the existing normal/N/W and broad/E ground territories, without using the receiver being repaired. The upper zinc and lower breather must each have a downhill route from their q250 elevations to those exits; a horizontal line drawn through the 50 vent, rails, 320 glass or air-control sheet is a collision, not a pipe. Their required lateral slot, turning radii, roof-support/ventilation price and temporary air/thermal/weather restoration must be authored by ROF/MAT/STR. A protected roof work platform, scaffold and receiver hoist need an actual coexisting plan, loading and tie section around the south door and both temporary branches.

The **finite live-state sequence** to prove before accepting this alternative is:

1. Keep the existing roof gutter/receiver, normal court carrier and full 1800 relief functioning. Set up independently supported guarded roof work access and a temporary weather sheet *above the intended q250 cut*, whose entire runoff still reaches that live gutter. It must itself be installable and removable without opening the roof wet boundary into a storm.
2. Cut/rebuild the zinc and lower breather only within that protected work state; install the independent q250 cassette, its two separately renewable exits and ground connections. Test each layer's downslope capture, joint witness, full roof source and one-branch failure with the original receiver temporarily isolated but still physically available as the rollback route. Direct court rain continues to normal/relief.
3. Only after a coupled event/head/tailwater/first-harm proof, release the gutter and use its specified 150 lowering/400 outward translation with a separate cradle/hoist and protected worker/ground escape. No temporary water member, tie, tool or loaded receiver may occupy the sweep. The actual body length/mass/wet load, first bolt and replacement's reverse travel must be located. During a stopped lift or rain, both temporary exits must keep draining without moving the suspended receiver.
4. Reinstall and test the original roof collector/secondary exit and both ground routes. Reverse the temporary zinc/breather cassette operation behind an independently draining weather sheet, then restore all roof, vent, air, thermal and structural boundaries before removing protected access. A temporary lower seam cannot become a concealed permanent maintenance trap.

This is a **development criterion, not an installed method**. It presently fails at step 2: the selected source has no supported dual-layer roofward cut, accessible lateral exits through the actual roof build-up, or joined receiver/scaffold geometry. Therefore receiver disconnection in step 3 is prohibited. The design price is explicit roof topology/weathering and work-access intervention, not another nominal gutter size. If the dual-layer cassette and complete reversal cannot be made physically continuous, change the permanent collector/component division or roof/eave access architecture; do not move a safety-dependent cut to later site certification.

### Conserved event and adverse states

At the existing 216 mm/h probe, full inner-roof wall-line projection **47.8125 m² = 2.86875 L/s** and direct court **12.25 m² = 0.735 L/s**; each surviving receiving system would need the conserved **60.0625 m² = 3.60375 L/s** combined demand, without double-counting court water. The assigned local roof receiver catchment, actual concentration/jets, q250 interception, inlets, losses and receiving heads are not authored. No uniform-flow probe makes the new cassette effective.

- West branch or N/W blocked: roof and court flow need the east branch plus the full 1800/E route, with actual first weak court/door boundary tested. East or E blocked: they need the west branch and normal/N/W. A shared q250 capture obstruction, two failed joints, a blocked full-width court inlet, or both site receivers high remains a common failure with **no demonstrated harmless passive route**.
- The −30 pan/DPC join is an unqualified water-sensitive comparison. An 80 mm continuous dam from mouth−110 reaches its elevation before positive overflow head; +15 door crest is too late. Real leakage may start lower. The roof cassette cannot claim emergency court storage or pressure-tight normal covers.
- The exterior N/W and E territories have authored −600 outlets and nominal −800 water planes, not a measured plot, correlated tailwater or independent site acceptance. DPL must bind their event levels; MAT/OAS the first wetting/leak path; RWT the complete flow/fault state. A forecast, pump, operator intervention or a halted receiver lift is no rain isolation.

**Disposition:** the in-basket small-clearing comparator remains conditional on actual contacts/bridge, work position and rescue. The drawn outboard-cup/two-branch receiver-renewal method is **rejected by the same-section primary/secondary/gutter-sweep test**. The roofward q250 dual-layer alternative gives a finite development target and a continuous-live-route sequence to qualify; it is not yet spatially connected. RWT-06 stays planned, RWT-07 remains blocked. ROF owns roof collector/component and weather/lap source changes; MAT/STR/IAS/HAD own connected support, opening/vent/thermal/worker geography; RWT owns actual interception, branch, event and site handoff design. BCR/EPR/PHY/DEL and DPL retain subsequent physical, execution and site admission, without inheriting an internally missing route.
