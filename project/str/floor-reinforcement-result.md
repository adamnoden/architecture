# RC200 reinforcement: retain the floor, replace the anchorage question

**The failed vertical U-return does not reject RC200. Develop ordinary continuous bars with an explicit detail at each short edge and opening. Do not thicken the whole floor to preserve that return.** The new callable comparison distinguishes section steel, bond development, end geometry and the physical field available for anchorage. It selects no cage.

The actual solved plate supplies its cells, rotations, openings, mass and material law. `floor_reinforcement.py` calls the same constitutive bending recovery used by the joined successor. Its settings are authored controls; sources, results and approvals cannot be supplied as settings. The method has no alternative floor definition and does not modify head reactions.

## Retained mechanical control

The [reproducible control](studies/floor-reinforcement-proof.json) postprocesses the explicitly retained native35 W31/G0.9 result. It is **not a newly executed final design-action envelope**. The recovered absolute bending-plus-twisting inspection envelope is 29.578329 kNm/m in the 15 mm source stair cell. No smoothing hides that narrow cell; local-stress convergence remains unproved.

An openly authored H12/100 comparison has four bar layers: two directions at each face. Main-bar nominal cover is 50 mm, allowing both the existing 5 mm cage and 5 mm independent-bar setting comparisons while retaining 40 mm minimum cover. Copying the earlier 45 mm *link* cover directly to an unconfined floor main bar would leave only 35 mm under those combined settings. Orthogonal crossings are intentionally tied; independent parallel bars retain 78 mm adverse clear space. The inner layer and adverse position give an effective depth of 122 mm. With the existing source stress comparator of 400 MPa and an explicitly authored 0.9 lever factor, the necessary section moment screen is 49.67235 kNm/m. This is not a complete flexural resistance, cracking, shear, punching or anchorage check.

Keeping the source poor-bond comparison at 2.1 MPa and crediting no shape or confinement reduction requires **571.43 mm** at the full source design stress; the retained conservative lap multiplier gives **857.14 mm**. These are full-stress comparisons, not lengths reduced to rescue a convenient bend.

The exact source-cell boundary inventory has 434 segments. At 104 segment midpoints the normal continuous physical floor field is too short even to contain that straight development; 106 lack the lap run. These are mesh-boundary segments, **not a count of reinforcing bars or fabricated details**. Of 130 edge-adjacent source-core comparisons, 110 cannot develop the full stress between the core's inner face and the outer bar end. A long inward field alone does not prove anchorage beyond the last support. Actual force introduction, lateral cover, every bar station, corner congestion and lap confinement still need their drawn detail.

The 160 mm vertical-return template needs **308 mm** depth for the inner direction of the four-layer arrangement. Its full-source-stress mandrel screen independently requires **168.73 mm**, so merely increasing the body does not validate that unchanged bend. A uniform 320 mm quantity comparison adds **11.851126 m³ / 296.278144 kN** over the known plate domain. It does not receive a changed stiffness/52-head result or become an adopted local edge band. This is the real cost of the tempting global fix, not a recommendation to apply it.

## Method and next design obligation

`assess_floor_reinforcement(verticalResult, settings)` operates on the actual joined result and can be consumed by the saved masonry-frame stage. The route inventory intersects each inward line with all actual plate rectangles and merges continuous intervals; point sampling cannot skip a narrow opening. Changes to pitch, cover, bond and authored bend alter their respective screens; source openings alter actual field runs. No old head response is transferred to the unexecuted thicker alternative.

Before reinforcement selection, derive the complete governing action envelope including the coupled successor, verify local plate convergence, and draw a conventional anchorage schedule at the actual exterior, courtyard, entrance, stair and service-opening edges. Choose force-level straight development or a physically detailed standard end arrangement where supported. A necessary change in the concrete body must carry its actual geometry, mass, stiffness, clearances and fresh joined replay; it cannot inherit the control's result. Keep the already rejected curved portal cage separate from this floor decision.

Six Python tests cover analytical moments, two-direction cover/depth, exact short-route interception, reversible settings with unchanged input solve, the charged thickening quantity, adverse material/cover and rejected injected settings. The canonical STR Node wrapper runs these and checks deterministic source-bound JSON/SVG reproduction. The actual SVG was rendered and inspected. No physical, professional, owner or structural selection is claimed.

## Recoverable technical basis

The existing cage owns the 3 MPa good-bond, 0.7 poor-bond, 17 MPa concrete-compression and steel/cover comparison assumptions; this method does not establish their installed applicability. The Concrete Centre's [*Concise Eurocode 2*, sections 11.3–11.4](https://www.concretecentre.com/TCC/media/TCCMediaLibrary/Events/Online%20course/CCIP_Concise_EC2.pdf) supplies the first-generation method basis: distinguish steel-damage mandrels from concrete bend resistance, derive bond length from bar stress and bond resistance, and retain minimum anchorage. Its [detailing guidance](https://www.concretecentre.com/Structural-design/Eurocode-2-concrete/Detailing.aspx) also separates nominal surface cover, spacing and anchorage assumptions. Accessed 20 November 2026. These explain the retained comparison; they do not silently select the project's final standard edition/National Annex, exposure, concrete or bond conditions.
