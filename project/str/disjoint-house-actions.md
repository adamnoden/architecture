# Disjoint first-storey trial actions and opening routes

**STR-HOUSE continuation, 2 November 2026. Conditional reference design, not released for construction.** The [preceding eight-opening study](spine-opening-joined-path.md) superposed a gross wall challenge on a floor budget that might already include some wall weight. This study makes a **different, explicit trial allocation**: upper structural core is counted once in a three-dimensional union; the floor budget excludes all named wall cores. It is disjoint *within this declared model*, not a measured takeoff, a demonstrated complete assembly or a selected structural design. The [reproducer](studies/disjoint-house-actions.py) authenticates the actual HAD generator, selected HAD JSON and prior STR outputs by SHA-256.

## Separate occurrences before summing

The continuous inner-axis RC200 trial remains **100.6602 m² × 5 kN/m² = 503.301 kN characteristic concrete**, with 1.901 kN additional bridge downstand; neither is added again to the floor-edge reactions derived from the same slab. The ordinary first-floor screen uses 5.0 kPa slab G, a **2.5 kPa non-wall maximum budget** for layers, services and ceiling, and Q2.0 kPa. This is a proposed reallocation of the previous budget, not evidence that the actual layers fit it: those constituents must be itemised, fitted and checked, and no wall-core weight may be charged to it. Where the prior gross floor reaction is used below, its slab and non-wall budget are already included. The exposed-loggia extra 1.0 kPa is not involved in these eight listed support-edge segments; other areas retain their own provision. Doors, arch units, reinforcement, finishes, local openings and head replacements still need their own non-overlapping assignments.

HAD's `groundWalls` and `upperWalls` are **not the whole enclosure**. The generator independently constructs eight outer/court lines, authenticated here alongside the selected JSON. The structural core polygon is *not* exported by that generator: this study constructs a 140 mm trial behind the 27 mm room finish, extends or trims the eight lines to meet at corners, and joins those polygons with the 17 actual upper-wall cores. For each finite 3D cell, enclosure is the first owner and then the upper walls follow HAD source order. Changing that ownership convention can change local assignments and support reactions; it must not change union volume. The disjoint union avoids counting a junction twice.

For one specified preliminary geometry, all cores are 3.1 m high at 20 kN/m³. The first-storey window rectangles use each actual source width, sill and head; internal door holes use an explicitly **trial 2250 mm rectangular height**. This removes more masonry than a true curved opening above its spring, and does not establish any real head/spandrel geometry. It also does not model roof gables, weather leaves, dry/wet wall finishes, ground-storey walls or portal wraps. The resulting union is **35.176804 m³**: **19.824000 m³ enclosure** and **15.352804 m³ internal**, or 703.536 kN characteristic under this density assumption. The same cut-up counted per intersecting owner before deduplication would be **36.139199 m³**: 0.962395 m³ is shared junction volume, not extra masonry. These figures are for this simple rectangular-cut trial only, not an actual quantity, a conservative total-building bound, or a validated core arrangement. Actual material density, curved rings, head substitutions, crown masonry, core continuities and the 140 mm assumption need reconciliation before adoption.

## Connected gravity and signed lateral route

The preferred enquiry is **roof and upper core → floor/head and real piers → warm receiving grid → mineral/AAC/DPC interface → connected cold grid → site-dependent support groups**, with the independent weather leaf on its own cold support. The table's end actions are intermediate, not additional ground loads. At each receiving line assemble all originating actions only once, including lower core, the rest of the floor, arch thrust and roof collector, and explicitly map transfers through actual piers and grid spans to support groups. The values here do not establish sharing between supports or a chosen foundation.

The previous unit horizontal roof-level challenge and signed −324.3375 kN no-favourable-G uplift remain **separate unallocated challenges**. Neither can be inserted into gravity nodes without an actual diaphragm/collector, pier and shear/tension crossing model, wind combinations and contact behaviour. Friction or compression through an AAC/mineral bed is not a qualified reversal or uplift route. The old 400 mm node and its low-tie/cage defects remain rejected; the 850 kN/100 kNm cap is not inherited. Actual site soil, roots, water, wind, support groups and independent engineering acceptance are later duties, while the joined head, core/arch geometry, diaphragm and warm/cold crossing remain internal constructive work.
