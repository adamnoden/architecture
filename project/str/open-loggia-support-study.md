## Lower grid and level target

The field floor is380 deep, but its underside alone cannot choose the house plinth. A concrete lower-grid hypothesis is calculated:250wide×450deep, top−180/bottom−630,40 cover comparator,2H22 bottom and H8/100 links, maximum3000 support pitch. Its explicitly conservative line envelope includes two140×3100 masonry storeys, two3.585/2 floor tributaries at7.5kPa, a10kN/m permanent/3variable roof allowance and beam self-weight. It checks moment, shear, strut capacity, link spacing and minimum steel. Actual roof and wall roles must lie within the eventual agreed schedule.

A separate650×650×500 pile-cap **envelope**, down to−680, is reserved rather than silently omitting the node. The four-arm demand, not an assumed pile capacity, is recorded. The cap's strut/tie design, real pile head, actual support stiffness and site soil remain unearned. A target D1200 follows only if that deepest envelope works:680+300 authored heave+50 construction loss+150 ventilation comparator=1180, leaving20 after rounding. It is neither inherited D1300 nor a field-only D900 claim.

The masonry DPC is provisionally a150 band **above** the wet-tolerant concrete grid, −180..−30. This is a deliberate alternative to putting150 beneath every RC beam/cap. It does not qualify the suspended concrete's moisture resistance, lateral cavity protection, floor air/gas boundary or mineral wall/floor splice. MAT must earn the complete route; no membrane is waived and no glass insulation is used as an invented masonry bearing.
