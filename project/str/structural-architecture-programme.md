## Decision to earn

What permanent structural arrangement makes the owner's actual house feel substantial and intelligibly supported, while remaining spatially convincing, maintainable and credible to engineer and build?

STR owns the architectural choice of structural system and its coordinated load-path proposition. It develops the owner's preferences for solid internal masonry, direct bearing, genuine arches, aligned walls and non-timber primary structure into options that can be accepted, changed or rejected. It does not turn those preferences into universal doctrine, calculated capacities or a construction specification.

This is a bounded design workstream within the existing house programme. BCR has exposed physical conflicts and routes obligations; it does not choose the answer. HAD owns the inhabited composition and adopted house geometry. STR supplies the missing structural design proposition, using those findings and sources rather than starting a second house model.

## Five bounded packets

Lifecycle and readiness live in the graph. A packet's result must identify its source revision, alternatives, adverse findings, decision, limits and downstream rechecks. Reuse existing sources and registers; do not create a second obligation matrix or a material encyclopaedia.

### STR-00 — establish direction and integration

**Question:** is the brief sufficiently precise and correctly located to commission useful design studies?

**Inputs:** owner direction, charter, accepted house, BCR findings, current programmes and live graph. **Outputs:** the structural brief, this programme, [direction result](structural-direction-result.md), source/interface links and canonical packets.

**Exit:** distinguish solidity from loadbearing, preference from performance, primary structure from renewable assemblies, and authoring completion from engineering acceptance. Record known conflicts and the dependency route into existing gates. This packet completes the authorised workstream-authoring task; it does not complete STR.

### STR-MASONRY — walls, openings and compression

**Depends on:** STR-00. **Owner:** STR, coordinating with OAS/IAS/HAD.

Compare a small credible set of solid wall constructions and structural opening families against a conventional control. Provide representative wall/arch sections and a wall-role schedule: gravity-bearing, stability/restraint, enclosure only, or explicitly combined. Show unit/bond/leaf build-up, actual thickness, self-weight basis, bearing and restraint assumptions, and what remains unknown. Do not assign nominal future capacity without a defined load case.

Explore genuine external and internal arches, including flat arches where suitable. Each example names the supported leaf/load, geometry, abutments, horizontal thrust path, adjacent openings, movement and centering/temporary works. Distinguish a weathering-leaf arch from support of the inner structural leaf. Check clear passage, door mounting, seals/fire boundaries, headroom and service crossings. Include a conventional lintel/beam comparison so any exceptional transfer has an intelligible cost and reason.

**Exit:** a reasoned shortlist with limits, rejected cases and room/structural interface envelopes. Compression alone must not conceal lateral instability, slender piers, heavy partitions on an unproved floor, or inaccessible services. No member sizes or assembly performances are certified by this option study.

### STR-SPANS — primary floor and roof systems

**Depends on:** STR-00. **Owner:** STR, coordinating with ROF/IAS/MAT.

Compare a small number of plausible non-timber primary floor/roof families, with a conventional baseline and an explicit account of where tension remains. Screen real construction arrangements rather than material names: spans and bearing widths, total depth and mass, openings/trimmed edges, diaphragm/tie strategy, lateral restraint, creep/deflection/movement, fire/corrosion/moisture exposure, erection and temporary support, access and replaceability. Reinforced or prestressed systems must disclose reinforcement, ties and wet joints; they do not become compression-only or demountable by description.

Coordinate complete floor layers with IAS and roof reactions/support spacing with ROF. Show consequences for wall/foundation demand, lifting/installation and future service renewal. Check materials through MAT rather than silently accepting coatings, adhesives or polymer parts. Timber primary structure is a disclosed fallback requiring an owner decision, not an unannounced continuation of an earlier concept.

**Exit:** a manageable shortlist and declared support/depth/load envelopes with evidence and uncertainty. Identify which calculations require competent review and which geometric comparisons can proceed now. Do not manufacture site loads, soil capacity, lifecycle savings or product availability.

### STR-HOUSE — test the structure and the inhabited plan together

**Depends on:** STR-MASONRY, STR-SPANS and BCR-PREFLIGHT. **Owners:** STR / HAD.

Compare the least-disruptive credible arrangement with a coherent replan where alignment or thicker walls requires one. Use paired ground/upper plans and sections on the actual house, not disconnected typical details. Trace roof and floor reactions through walls/piers to a foundation concept; show a credible lateral/restraint route, including heavy nonbearing walls. Distinguish in-plane bridging from sideways transfers.

Resolve at candidate level the courtyard doorway/entrance-void conflict, stair aperture/headroom, bridge, piers/corners and representative service crossings. Show full floor/roof sections, door operation and representative arch heads. Quantify changed usable room/circulation areas, clear widths, wall/material quantities and load consequences using declared assumptions. Test the four-bedroom programme, sanitary access, daylight/opening relationships and component renewal. Record construction/temporary works, cost and site implications proportionately; unknown values remain unknown.

**Exit:** one preferred, source-bound structural proposition with rejected alternatives and explicit adoption conditions, or a documented failure requiring redesign. It must pass both structural-topology and inhabited-space tests. A generic referral cannot close an identified reaction or fit conflict. Proposed geometry changes use the existing shared rewind procedure, with old/new values and affected views; no source adoption occurs merely because a study drawing exists.

### STR-RECONCILE — candidate handoff and affected-evidence review

**Depends on:** STR-HOUSE. **Owner:** STR, with HAD/IAS/OAS/ROF/BCR reconciliation.

Bind the preferred proposition to exact source and candidate identities. Reconcile wall/finish budgets, floor/ceiling layers, opening heads, roof support/reaction envelopes and access/service boundaries in the existing shared contract. Record each affected BCR obligation and the evidence that resolves, eliminates or legitimately bounds it. Recheck affected parts of OAS-DETAIL and BAY-FRONT-STUDY-01; identify updates required to ER-STR-01, EPR-REFRESH, PHY-APPLICABILITY and later HRS technical migration. Retain unrelated historical results at their original scope.

Provide a compact handoff: adopted versus proposed geometry; gravity/lateral scheme; opening/arch and exceptional-transfer schedule; named assumptions, review inputs and remaining site-dependent duties; exact compatibility conditions for the selected roof and integrated house. Separate a coherent provisional design from later calculated adequacy. Materially unexplained load paths, insufficient space and incompatible interfaces must be resolved or redesigned before claiming this handoff complete. An adverse study is useful evidence but does not unlock final selection as a passed scheme.

For the book, map the earned findings to existing discussion of repose, permanence, lifespan separation and the Reference House. Start with [Repose — structural reading](../../docs/manuscript/principle-08-repose.md), the [permanent opening / replaceable door pattern](../../docs/patterns/permanent-opening-replaceable-door.md) and [G-01 plan/section/elevation coupling](../../docs/grammar/g01-plan-section-elevation-coupling.md), preserving their distinct principle, pattern and grammar scopes. Identify existing passages needing qualification, a small set of illustrative details, and what remains personal testimony. Author only changes supported by the design evidence; do not add a universal anti-tension doctrine or a new writing workstream.

**Exit:** an inspectable, coherent provisional structural proposition and precise downstream obligations. Competent calculation/review, testing where necessary, real ground conditions and construction approval retain their existing owners. No final engineering acceptance is claimed by the five packets.

## Dependency and change discipline

STR-MASONRY and STR-SPANS may proceed independently after the brief. Their results join in STR-HOUSE and then STR-RECONCILE. Both **ROF-RWT-GATE** and **TRIAD-GATE** require the reconciliation result and must check compatibility with their actual selected assemblies/geometry. A later change outside a declared envelope requires a targeted structural recheck before those gates can pass.

STR does not wait on those final gates, MAT-COORDINATION, HRS-TECHNICAL or BCR-CONVERGENCE. Existing IAS/HAD/ROF/RWT research can continue; its selection assumptions remain provisional where affected. Completed BCR, OAS, EPR and PHY work is not globally reopened. The handoff names affected evidence and required fresh checks instead of relabelling historical completion as current validation.

Use conventional technical evidence where it answers the actual question. Escalate a concrete unresolved duty to competent engineering or physical testing; do not make general external review a substitute for useful design, or internal drawings a substitute for that review. If the brief cannot be met within the accepted house, return a reasoned redesign or explicit owner decision rather than burying the exception.

These primary sources seed the studies, not a selected design. Links checked 30 October 2026; applicability, edition and jurisdiction must be established for each later claim.

- [Brick Industry Association, Technical Note 31: Brick Masonry Arches (1995)](https://www.gobrick.com/media/file/31-brick-masonry-arches.pdf): arch action, thrust, abutments and construction; US guidance, not UK approval.
- [Concrete Centre / BDA, Eurocode 6 masonry vertical resistance guide (2013)](https://www.brick.org.uk/uploads/downloads/d-eurocode-6-masonry-vertical-resistance.pdf): strength, eccentricity, slenderness, restraint and concentrated bearing; verify the applicable standard and national provisions before design use.
- [Approved Document A](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/structure-approved-document-a): England structural guidance and restraint questions; distinguish statutory requirements from a particular guidance route and its scope.
- [Approved Document E](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/resistance-to-sound-approved-document-e) and [Approved Document B](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/fire-safety-approved-document-b): applicable acoustic and fire assembly duties; do not infer them from material mass or noncombustibility alone. Check effective dates and transitional provisions.
- [MPA Precast / Precast Flooring Federation, Hollowcore Floor Systems (2019)](https://mpaprecast.org/MPAPrecast/media/root/Publications/Flooring/p2.pdf): a real non-timber structural family and its prestress, joints, bearings and diaphragm interfaces; no generic span/depth is adopted here.
