# Actual perforated floor joined to the northern RC challenger

The source perforated floor, four finite outer L paths, four authored continuous RC northern portal paths and all 52 ground heads now share one compatible calculation. This is an integrated numerical advance: the former free-floor portal boundary has been removed. It is not an installed structural selection or a complete-house acceptance.

The simpler RC upper topology removes the masonry/spandrel receiving join and the structural M30 tie/bore. Its raw 2960 mm span, 2250 mm spring, 600 mm rise, 215 mm radial ring and 775 mm finished portal envelope remain. The 215 mm normal core and 140 mm floor upstands are an explicit control, with unresolved cage/connection duties. The owner has not accepted removal of the visible transom.

## One body and actual floor work

`studies/joined_rc_floor.py` reuses the native CST mesh, Schur condensation, positive-energy modes and spatial FrameModel. The floor retains four L interfaces and 22 internal upstand stations: these are numerical coordinates, not additional canonical packets. The four actual north receiving duties are connected through their full three-dimensional upstands and RC portal frames.

The two central north strips are exclusively owned by the full 750/650 mm upstands. Their old 200 mm slab-level band/chord stiffness is omitted, and the CST field already excludes their material. Actual work at Z3270 is transferred to the 3545/3495 mm beam masters, preserving eccentric force and moment. Floor/aperture and joint/band intersections reject. No old fixed-base roof/foundation increment or retained floor/core gravity is added.

The passive floor carries the existing source membrane and edge laws; it receives the explicitly defined local portal challenges. The next worked replacement must add the source-owned first-floor wind recipes with the lower-wall contribution reconciled before adding direct portal pressure. This control does not silently claim that all native floor/roof wind has been applied.

## Contact physics, not a fabricated anchor

Negative affine DPC corner pressure only rejects full contact. The corrected free-floor calculation separately proves its signed pressure cases cannot equilibrate inside the actual 215 mm normal support footprint. That free-floor bound is disabled once the floor supplies real additional interface wrenches.

The joined comparison can instead solve **compression-only normal contact**, with unchanged authored normal stiffness and no normal tension anchor. An explicit exact active-patch alternative clips the affine gap against the physical rectangle, triangulates the compressed polygon and reuses the same FrameModel with exact degree-two tangent quadrature until the contact patch, normal forces and moments agree. Intermediate bilateral tangent forces are not accepted as final compression contact.

## Actual change propagation

An authored outer-upstand depth change from 750 to 850 mm moves its centroid from Z3545 to Z3595, rebuilds finite-member stiffness, end offsets and selfweight, and changes the full 52-head result by up to **0.543578 kN/kNm**. Physical replacement G changes from 45.442945 to 46.627695 kN. The in-plane Timoshenko shear parameter remains unchanged because both its inertia and area scale with depth; the physical work offsets and three-dimensional member matrices change.

Moving the actual plant-riser aperture changes the mesh from 2534 to 2538 triangles and changes head components by up to **0.003109 kN/kNm**, while preserving the actual ground-grid members and physical portal mass. This invalidates the edited service/host applicability; no old clearance receipt survives automatically.

Refining the floor pitch from 1400 to 700 mm gives 3050 triangles, changes head components by up to **0.101071 kN/kNm** and changes actual floor-port movement components by 0.005413 mm/mrad. The floor remains a bounded membrane model, including the existing circular-hole bounding-square approximation and withheld principal wet territory; it supplies no missing vertical plate or adopted roof proof.

## Reinforcement and remaining decisions

The new 4H16/H8 trial belongs only to the authored RC challenger. It is not a transverse reinforcement attribution to the old jamb. Separate continuous post bars and developed arch legs need a real joint cage: four bars cannot branch into eight. Coplanar laps, rounded-link corners, placement tolerances, actual archbar jogs and the 140 mm upstand cannot be approved by a nominal four-bar fit screen.

Compression-only DPC behavior also does not imply continuous anchored starters across the break. Their bar termination, return, shoe or other actual load transfer must be resolved without silently introducing tensile normal restraint. The native rectangular-section calculations remain necessary strength comparisons, with anchorage, shear/torsion, joint panels, second-order/crack behavior and actual floor-beam reinforcement open.

The current exact 215/140 control exposes why a more conventional worked section is justified: its contact resultant lies within about 4 mm of the nominal normal edge and its local pressure/gap is concentrated. Root has selected one subsequent **290 mm normal core / 200 mm upstand** challenger for detailed comparison, with raw arch geometry retained. At the portal, 290 mm core plus two 202.5 mm cellular-glass layers and two 40 mm linings still totals 775 mm. That prices a real 15.625% loss of the original 240 mm glass thickness; it is not MAT or thermal approval. The wider upstand must also reconcile its actual upper-wall envelope and floor material union.

The next case must include actual native first-floor wind choices. The source P1 recipe already includes half the lower-wall pressure contribution. A direct actual open-portal body load therefore requires explicit subtraction/replacement over the same signed loaded interval; naïve addition would duplicate load. Courtyard pressure is a separate named challenge, not an invented part of the current 96-programme floor recipe.

All **28 canonical complete receiving duties remain open**. Four northern finite paths are assembled conditionally, with other receiving paths, source GMAX overlap, roof actions, vertical slab behavior, physical connections and material resistance still explicit. No roof adoption, installed acceptance, transom aesthetic approval or complete-house approval is claimed.

## Reproduce

Run `python3 project/str/studies/joined_rc_floor_proof.py --write` for the actual 11 signed/contact/geometry controls. The proof and drawing bind the full 28-entry source/method tuple. `node --test project/str/studies/joined-rc-floor.test.mjs` runs the analytical contact/work controls and an actual joined 52-head challenge.

`joined_rc_floor.py --read` returns missing laws/integration choices as null. Standard input accepts exact `{case,boundary}` with boundary schema `str-joined-rc-floor-boundary/1`, a name and explicit supported family multipliers. `run_joined_rc_floor_case` returns the source-bound native result, schedule, floor, diagnostics and actual computed `drawings['floor.svg']`.

The compiler owns one editable floor stage. The joined stage must consume that same floor geometry/material/aperture input and save only its own portal, contact and upstand authoring; it must not introduce a second editable floor authority.
