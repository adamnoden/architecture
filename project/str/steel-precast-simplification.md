# Ordinary steel and precast: development choice

## Decision — 28 November 2026

**Develop ordinary rolled steel with precast floor units as the preferred replacement for the unresolved monolithic floor/portal joint. Allow a 750 mm intermediate floor zone.** Put the loggia's wall-carrying beams beside the parallel precast units, within their depth. Retain genuine, separately supported masonry arches below. Stop treating the inherited 350 mm floor zone as fixed.

This is a reasoned development choice under the owner's steel permission, prefab preference and latest simplicity priority. It is not adoption of the higher house, a complete framing layout or closure of STR-FLOOR-PORTAL. The actual coupled structure, occupied house and owner-facing architectural review still have to agree. The earlier RC and separated-bearing investigations remain evidence; their forces and passes do not transfer to this scheme.

This is a useful size/depth comparison, not member resistance: section class, lateral torsional buckling, erection torsion, restraint, shear/local bearing, connections, vibration and fire remain to be established. The floor widths and stair point load are explicit challenges rather than a complete tributary-area take-off. Gross upper masonry is added separately, without credit for door or window openings. The rear wet-wall alternative reaches the existing outer-north wall directly. It does not retain the old 155 mm loaded overhang or assume the special seated RC post/cage is needed. Its south reaction and the stair trimmer's north reaction meet the same rear wall and **must be combined**. A geometrically fitting pad does not establish the capacity of the 140 mm receiving wall or settle the stair-landing corner.

The 254.6 mm floor-beam flange allows a geometric comparison of two nominal 75 mm seats, a 20 mm joint and 25 mm setting allowance at each end: 220 mm total, leaving 34.6 mm. The 166.9 mm edge beams are not credited with two opposed seats. Their one-sided bearing, erection twist and actual restraints need explicit details.

## Why the portal arrangement matters

The loggia floor spans east–west between its side supports. The two portal wall beams also run east–west, so they can lie beside those units. They carry the wall above without requiring the units to sit on top of them. At the selected floor level this leaves space for the retained 280 mm insulation/lining envelope above the masonry arch.

The same calculation rejects placing the portal beam below the units: its insulated underside falls 39.6 mm below the arch's outer crown. The 350 mm inherited floor zone also fails the ordinary beam-and-unit stack by 365.4 mm. These failures are retained in the generated receipt. No shallow fabricated beam, hidden shelf, reduced hollowcore end or special sliding joint is introduced to rescue those arrangements.

The arches still need real abutments, ties, their own gravity/thrust checks and erection support. The source portal includes RC pier/skewback details; this comparison has not proved that replacing those piers with masonry works. Steel above the arch does not erase that negative evidence. Prefer ordinary factory-made bearing/post details where the source wall cannot receive the new loads; do not restart the rejected curved cage merely because it exists in the old model.

## Precast and site work

The retained sixteen RC field rectangles cover 90.57855 m². The source additionally has a 2.447425 m² entrance bridge and 7.634225 m² of residual strips, closures and wall-head territory, totalling 100.6602 m². **Sixteen rectangles are not a precast panel schedule.** Actual unit widths, end bearings, trimmed openings, wall-head closures and service holes must be located before quantities or installation completeness are claimed.

Use grouted longitudinal keys, necessary tied end/core closures and local bearing beds as explicit site operations. A full structural topping is not selected by this comparison. Its necessity must be decided from the actual diaphragm, ties, load distribution and floor finish. Factory precast is preferred, but an unqualified untopped diaphragm is not a simplification. Installed materials, fire protection, seals and insulation remain subject to the whole-assembly brief.

## Next work and affected evidence

Complete one ordinary framing and unit layout, then design the concentrated receiving locations and diaphragm/ties. Use their actual loads and stiffness to recompute the complete vertical and lateral path, including roof, movement and foundation support. The common rear bearing and the loggia wall/pier junctions are specific remaining receiving problems. Do not launch another generic joint-refinement programme before locating those ordinary details.

Reconcile the 400 mm datum change and 46.9 mm void change with the occupied stair, window/elevation drawings, court proportions, service risers and fire heights before architectural adoption. Ground, roof, service and interior findings remain valid only within their original source geometry. The shared saved document visibly withholds their applicability to the new structural stage.

### Reproduction and change propagation

Authored inputs: [settings](studies/steel-precast-inputs.json) and [primary sources / assumptions](studies/steel-precast-sources.json). The pure `steel-precast-kernel.ts` computes simple-beam equilibrium and elastic deflection, floor/portal envelopes, stair dimensions, entrance fit and combined rear-bearing demand. The current-source adapter authenticates the occupied and structural sources. The public CLI and saved `steelPrecast` engineering stage call the same methods; old stages are not edited into apparent compatibility.

```sh
node --import tsx project/str/studies/steel-precast-comparison.ts --check
node --import tsx --test compiler/reference-house/current-design-steel-precast.test.ts
```

CLI entry points are `--read-steel-precast`, `--steel-precast`, and `--read-steel-precast-document`; the saved document uses the existing `--bundle` route. Changing the floor datum changes section, stair and roof-height consequences while the explicitly unchanged load challenge remains an independent control. Changing floor actions changes affected beam forces and deflections while the parallel portal wall-beam actions stay unchanged. Too-shallow floors, a stacked portal, excessive floor actions, an undersized common pad, missing/unknown settings and stale sources are tested as failures or explicit withheld evidence.

Residual computational work is the complete panel/support network, signed structural response and joined occupied/roof/service geometry. STR-FLOOR-PORTAL owns that integration before the coherent provisional scheme can close. No current native RC analysis is relabelled unavailable or deleted.
