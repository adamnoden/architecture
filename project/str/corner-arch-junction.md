# Eight corner windows and their structural arches

Keep the genuine outer brick arch. On the inner leaf, develop a curved RC arch into the adjacent corner pier and a separate jamb on the first-floor edge beam. The window and guard can still be removed through the full opening. The present 27 mm finished reveal leaves too little allowance for frame movement.
