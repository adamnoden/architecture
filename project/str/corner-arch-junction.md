The OAS guard fits to two actual brackets at each side, with M12 studs at the weather face and 100 mm available withdrawal. In the [receiver comparison](studies/current-house-window-receivers.json), two sleeved M16 ties through the 140 mm inner core secure each jamb. Two 40 × 40 × 3 square tubes carry the outer bracket across the weather leaf and cavity. A 1.5 kN project point load plus wind on the actual steel gives 8.40 kN maximum bolt tension and 72.1 MPa in the tubes when both sides are engaged. The single-jamb challenge reaches 245 MPa against 182 MPa. The guard therefore needs independent support before releasing either side. Across eight windows, the tubes alone add 29.84 kg and an ideal direct metal conductance of 0.866 W/K. The bolts add 0.256 W/K before plates, edges and thermal spreading. Plate prying, bearing, welds, head/anchor clashes, moisture collars, weak-axis frame response and exterior hoisting are still open.
