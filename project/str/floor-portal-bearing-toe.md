For the old **95.16 kN** lateral action the 105 mm tooth projection gives **13,537.5 mm²** effective face with two 5 mm settings and a declared 75% area factor. Mean pressure **7.03 MPa** is below the **8.976 MPa** cracked-strut comparator. An 80 mm projection gives **9.54 MPa** and fails. Full edge application gives **9.99 kNm** at the wall section. A pressure field capped at 8.976 MPa would give at most **5.98 kNm**, excluding uncredited edge pressure. Prove that pressure limit, bedding, reversing contact, force spread into the continuing toe and absence of harmful concurrent friction before using the lower moment.

The larger moment needs **67.51 kN** in one pair of positive horizontal ties. Each unheaded H16 would need **319.79 mm** projected poor-bond development, against **145 mm** at the retained outside face. A generous cracked axial cone calculation for a pair of short heads with **124 mm effective embedment and 60 mm separation** gives **65.14 kN** before splitting, coupler or actual edge reductions. It also falls short. Extending the V lever to at least **163.4 mm nominal** only reaches that theoretical upper limit. The 158 mm trial cannot be adopted. The published KSN slab-box method addresses vertical shear and horizontal tie force. Its limits explicitly exclude lateral loading and bending. Do not claim a proprietary capacity for this key. [Manufacturer design method and limits](https://www.ancon.co.uk/downloads/6256/Design%20of%20Shear%20Connections%20Using%20KSN%20Anchor%20Box.pdf)

**Redesign the contact face before adding more anchor steel.** Keep the U215 tip but recess both V faces 15 mm from U200 outwards. Limit active bearing to U110–200. The effective area is **11,400 mm²** and mean pressure **8.35 MPa**. The adverse end station U205 bounds moment at **9.04 kNm** and reduces pair demand to **61.08 kN**. The dead tip retains only **5 mm** after paired placement settings. It must remain clear during load reversal and actual relative movement. The difference to the ideal cone is **4.06 kN** before real edges, splitting or unequal sharing. Provide positive anchorage at both ends and continuity through the root cage. Longer head spacing alone moves the trial into existing rail steel. The recessed contact is the preferred lateral form to test and remains withheld from house selection.
