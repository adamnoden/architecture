# Structural direction — authoring result

**Date:** 30 October 2026.  
**Scope:** STR-00; [parent issue #1103](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/1103).  
**Starting main:** `886c9a3fe85e0edaf33701872c076b757cd72c4c`.

## Authority and decision

The owner asked for a structural workstream to be thought through and authored in the context of Ovid's House. The [structural brief](../../docs/reference-house/structural-brief.md) records strong preferences for solid masonry indoors, direct support, genuine arches, storey-to-storey alignment and investigation of non-timber primary structure. It preserves the distinction between solid and loadbearing walls and adopts no universal wall thickness.

The [programme](structural-architecture-programme.md) assigns STR the structural design proposition. It uses BCR's existing physical findings and obligation register, HAD's house composition and source ownership, IAS/OAS assembly interfaces, ROF's roof demands and the established EPR/PHY/DPL/DEL evidence routes. It creates no second house model, obligation matrix, engineering authority or publication workstream.

## What changed

The canonical graph now contains a completed authoring packet, two parallel option studies, a joint structural/architectural house comparison and one reconciliation packet. The existing roof/water and shared house-selection gates consume the reconciliation result. STR does not depend on those downstream gates, so the programme does not create a selection cycle or freeze useful parallel design studies.

The owner's charter, current house basis, brief ledger and owning programmes identify the new direction and its boundaries. The shared interface contract separates primary structure from renewable layers and requires actual selected-assembly compatibility. The accepted layout, dimensions, compiler geometry and previous dated technical results are unchanged by this authoring decision.

## Adverse evidence carried forward

The [current structure study](../bcr/bcr-structure-result.md) supplies specific problems: entrance-void support over the courtyard doorway, an unselected offset-frame alternative, unresolved stair aperture/headroom and bridge/service crossings. The 350 mm floor has no spare depth; the 125 mm partition reservation is not a masonry selection. The [physical preflight](../bcr/bcr-preflight-result.md) and [bay brief](../bcr/bay-brief-result.md) require affected-evidence checks as structure and opening heads change.

STR must test both structural topology and the inhabited house. More solid material does not excuse a lost circulation route, unworkable sanitary access, unsupported partition or unexplained roof reaction. Arches must identify their loads and thrust resistance; essential tension cannot be deleted to satisfy an aesthetic preference. Personal repose is recorded without claiming a neurological treatment or universal material superiority.

## Validation and claim ceiling

Authoring validation comprises source/ownership review, graph admission regressions, canonical project-control validation and repository health on the published head. The PR records the executed commands and outcomes. These checks establish documentation and scheduling integrity, not physical feasibility, calculated capacity, fire/acoustic performance or construction approval.

STR-00 is complete only at this bounded authoring scope. The four design/reconciliation packets remain future work. No structural system, member size, foundation or wall thickness is adopted, and no competent review or physical test is claimed.
