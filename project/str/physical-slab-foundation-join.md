## Heavy-wall openings and the cold grid

The prior result inventories 17 upper gross wall rows, including both 215 mm rear-east cores, and 20 ground rows. This calculation now identifies **eight** places where an upper wall's *gross* self-weight challenge meets a lower opening. For each, it conserves both load and first moment across the actual lower opening and reports the two pier-end reactions; examples include 5.273 kN at each side of the 900 mm rear-west opening, 8.098 kN at each side of the rear-east opening with a 215 mm core, and 8.203 kN at the 1400 mm plant opening. These are **redistributions of the same gross wall rows**, never extra building mass. Where the upper wall also opens, full-height gross wall weight over the hole is deliberately adverse and must be replaced by the actual spandrel/head mass. The piers then need the *combined* floor, roof and wall reactions, real arch horizontal thrust, opening support, movement and assembled masonry demand; proximity to a cold route is no capacity.

The preferred pre-site load path is a continuous RC200 upper diaphragm with local collector/return/header, structurally participating masonry and RC boundary piers, a reinforced warm ground receiving grid, **compression-qualified** mineral/AAC/DPC band, and a separately connected cold RC grid to site-dependent deep support groups. The weather leaf has its own cold route. Horizontal reversal and roof uplift cannot pass through an AAC block or mineral mortar bed by wishful friction or tension; a *localized designed shear/tension crossing at selected robust nodes* is therefore an explicit requirement. Its geometry, strength, movement, thermal penalty, moisture/gas/fire continuity and construction sequence require a deliberate MAT/STR source decision. It is not an unrecorded steel starter through the thermal layer. If no acceptable crossing can be demonstrated, this preferred route fails and the wall/foundation arrangement must change. A raft/piled raft remains a legitimate site-specific challenger.

Four **candidate crossing stations** are the existing cold grid's outer corner junctions (300/300, 11700/300, 300/11700, 11700/11700), each only 32.5 mm in each axis from the paired inner-leaf axes. This makes the orthogonal wall-line lever arms legible without inheriting the old cap. For the previous 1 kN roof-level horizontal unit challenge, the global opposing-side couple is 0.5952 kN. Two equal corner stations on that side would each take 0.2976 kN; loss of one puts the full 0.5952 kN at the other *before* any torsion or site pressure change. These are equilibrium sensitivities, not an adopted stiffness split or a support rating. The source's −324.3375 kN no-favourable-G roof uplift also needs real signed distribution through the same roof/diaphragm/wall and local crossing geography. Four equal quarters would be a mathematical average only, not a valid anchor schedule. The load case that governs each node remains to be assembled with actual gravity, wind, wall restraint and ground movement.

The old 400 mm pile joint and low-tie/cage failures remain rejected. The 850 kN/100 kNm old node ceiling and D1800 *member-only* clearance cannot certify the new combined N/M/H/uplift nodes. Localized shear/tension crossings, wall-line stiffness and actual diaphragm chords are internal constructive work, not deferred shop fabrication alone. Actual soil, roots, water, pile group, wind and installed evidence remain later site/engineering acceptance. The south loggia end, southwest collector and lower opening pierheads must also fit the occupied and service sections without a hidden threshold, blocked vent or failed passive water route.

**Disposition:** inner-axis RC200 mass is conserved for the declared rectangular trial, while 15.517775 m² of exterior shell bands remain outside slab mass and need their own actual leaf/cavity/edge support account. The south loggia edge, southwest pocket and eight gross opening challenges have traceable *candidate* destinations. Their member connections, cumulative node force paths and material interfaces remain unqualified. STR-HOUSE stays open; this is a concrete next design candidate for HAD/MAT/STR review, not a foundation selection or STR-RECONCILE pass.
