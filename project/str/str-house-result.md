## Open design boundary

The working B plan must still resolve closed room boundaries and actual doors, ground living/dining and plant renewal, finite opening supports, diaphragm/collector routes, arch-thrust destinations and an accessible service crossing within real vertical space. Wall collinearity and a 600 mm geometric route witness do not establish those things.

A coherent candidate may carry explicit calculation, product, physical-review and site conditions. An unexplained reaction, room bypass or spatial clash is not such a condition: it must be resolved or the candidate retained as adverse/partial evidence. No STR-RECONCILE or final roof/house gate is unlocked by this checkpoint.
