# STR-HOUSE — first joined structure and occupation comparison

**Result: substantial partial progress; STR-HOUSE remains open.** The option studies now meet on paired plans and located sections. The comparison rejects simply replacing the existing partitions with thicker masonry and develops a materially better aligned alternative. It does **not** yet earn a preferred whole-house selection: the remaining floor-bearing, frame, riser, roof-heel and occupied-service junctions are real design work, not matters closed by a generic engineering referral.

No accepted house geometry, structural system, product or foundation is adopted. STR-RECONCILE and the final roof/house gates remain unpassed. The [programme](structural-architecture-programme.md), [structural brief](../../docs/reference-house/structural-brief.md), [owner-house charter](../owner-house-charter.md) and existing [shared rewind](../triad-interface-contract.md) control any later selection.

### A — least-change control

Keep the existing topology, distinguish supporting from enclosing solid walls, and accept that any upper wall without a lower counterpart requires a designed floor/transfer route for its own weight. The measured upper wall-centreline reservation is **37.9925 m**: **21.390 m has no collinear lower wall**, while **3.500 m of the aligned 16.6025 m crosses lower door openings**. Door thresholds are not deducted from those line reservations.

This is a substantial problem for the proposed masonry, not a criticism of every framed partition. An arch can bridge an opening in an aligned wall; it cannot move an offset upper wall sideways onto another one. Uniform thickening also makes the existing front bedrooms, stair junction and small en suite tighter. The source's 2170 mm front-bedroom depth does not acquire a convincing double-bed arrangement merely by changing its wall material.

A remains useful where a particular solid enclosing wall on a designed floor earns its place. It does not satisfy the stronger alignment preference as a whole-house proposition.

## 2. Arches become actual construction choices

The serious bridge comparison keeps 200 mm trial members wholly outside the new void, at **x4685–4885 / 7115–7315**. Each overlaps the old 2400 opening by 85 mm. With the 1800 alternative, each clears the opening by **215 mm**. The earlier 115 mm result belongs to a different, centred-on-edge footprint that intrudes into the void; it is not the preferred member placement.

The 1800 opening still has a genuine arch to design. At 2250 springing, 600 rise and a hypothetical 215 radial ring, the intrados/extrados crowns are 2850/3065. Its radius is 975, not the old wider arch's 1500. The radial ring approaches the compared frame patches closely; no adequate coursing, bearing spread or abutment follows from the plan gap alone.

Radial ring depth is also **not wall thickness**. A credible first inner-leaf enquiry is a genuine 140 mm through-wall solid-concrete masonry arch with custom/cut voussoirs. Real units and arch-construction guidance establish that family; they do not approve the proposed cut orientation, bond, geometry or capacity. If an actual 215 mm clay arch is required at the west stair window, the dimensioned fallback grows a supported bay 75 mm outward over y2650–6550. It preserves the room face, 160 mm cavity and stair, at the cost of 0.2925 m² additional footprint and real facade, foundation, roof and site changes. It is not placed on an unexplained shelf.

The junction study also locates internal 900/1400/1500 openings and external 1000/1800/2400 families. Each requires a real frame/fanlight below its curved intrados. A rectangular 2850 head cannot silently coexist with a lower arch springing. The retained wide garden opening has much smaller side territories than the improved court-south case and remains a separate thrust duty.

A transverse return does not automatically resist an arch's in-plane thrust. The comparison therefore names two distinct routes: actual masonry abutment/foundation resistance, or an enduring springing-level tie anchored to the structural fabric. The latter is a permanent structural transom with visible opening consequences, independent of the replaceable door. Necessary tension is disclosed; a higher floor collector needs its own intervening moment path.

## 3. Floors, stair and roof now have located consequences

The complete conventional floor enquiry is **20 finish + 35 upper functions + 75 topping + 150 hollowcore + 55 services + 15 ceiling = 350 mm**. It puts the flat support-level hollowcore soffit at 3170; a declared 10 mm contact reservation puts receiving masonry at 3160. Neither the former timber-seat level nor a hypothetical bedding reserve is a selected detail. A separate camber/tolerance allocation shows what the same 350 budget would have to surrender.

Two opposed 100 mm slab ends cannot fit a 140 mm wall. Rotating the southwest room field can remove that local conflict at the L wall, but creates a **4327.5 mm structural-face field**. Rotating the east gallery produces a **5462.5 mm field** and can move the opposed-end problem to its front supporting wall. The source's ordinary 3445 mm structural-face gap is not a universal house span or an accepted effective span. Upper masonry needs an actual path through solid closures/support bands or a direct wall gap; it cannot be assumed safe on hollow plank ends.

The shifted stair is a particularly useful test. A 200 mm header footprint kept south of the aperture, y4232.5–4432.5, reaches the structural west-window arch and the court-west support. The compared arch leaves **113.2–186.8 mm** to the 3160 masonry datum before header downstand. A simple 150-deep beam with its top at 3170 conflicts with that envelope. Calling the adjacent slab HC150 does not supply an integrated beam seat.

One explicit redesign lowers the stair-window springing by 250 to 2300 while allowing a 250 header downstand. It preserves the geometric reserve but lowers the window intrados crown from 2850 to **2600** and requires both leaves, frame and elevation to be redrawn. The protected underside is then 2905 under the declared 15 lining; the member must remain outside the stair aperture. This is a meaningful architectural alternative, not an accepted 250 member.

The roof enquiry names ordinary cross-ring tied trusses and diagonal corner members with supported jack/purlin fields. Finite corner bearings, collectors and the actual heel remain unresolved. The represented roof and ceiling zones overlap at the support; they cannot each be spent independently on steel, insulation, lining and storage.

The exposed masonry-vault floor remains in the comparison on the same support plan. At the retained hypothetical crown, a 200 rise gives 3100 springing; a 500 rise lowers it to 2800. Its real horizontal reactions, irregular edges and service route must be designed. The prestressed plank enquiry depends on steel tension; the vault does not escape ties, edge supports or foundation/thrust duties by being called compression-led.
