## Lateral and uplift geography

The current study carries both horizontal directions and reversals as explicit unknown actions. For a **unit 1 kN** horizontal roof action at the source's 6.746721 m primary centre, base shear is 1 kN and overturning is 6.746721 kNm. Across the 11.335 m opposing inner-leaf axis separation, a global boundary force couple would be 0.5952 kN per 1 kN applied. This is equilibrium bookkeeping, **not** a distribution to individual piers or a site wind load.

For east–west action the candidate x-running lines are the outer north/south inner cores, court north/south and segmented rear returns; for north–south action they are the outer east/west inner cores, service spine, court sides and loggia returns. Actual windows, the entrance void, the court and open north loggia interrupt these lines. Their stiffness, continuity and support shares cannot be assumed from plan overlap. Roof rail/post/purlin signed actions must enter a qualified warm ring and diaphragm; chords and collectors must carry them around openings into selected masonry/RC stability lines; floor diaphragm and upper-wall anchors must receive the transfer; the warm spreader must transmit shear, moment and uplift across the *actual* mineral bearing/DPC and AAC to the cold grid and site supports. The trial avoids a steel starter automatically bridging the thermal layer. Wind pressure, accidental/robustness action, torsion, corner ties, pierced concrete, masonry anchorage, ground lateral/uplift capacity and the wind/roof gravity combination remain unearned.

## Preferred conditional section and failed interfaces

The joined pre-site direction uses the MAT warm receiving recipe: room floor layers 15 + 65 + 200 + 320 mm, warm grid top −80/soffit −380, AAC −605..−390, mineral DPC −765..−615 and cold grid top −765. Its **−1215 cold-member soffit** is only a member hypothesis. The warm reinforced receiving grid must spread real concentrated stair/wet/bridge/roof actions across adequate bearing, with engineered contact, compression, horizontal shear and tension/restraint through the insulation/mineral band. Separate cold weather-leaf support and a connected cold grid follow; actual supports and node depths follow combined actions and ground/site evidence. MAT's 75 kN and 2.30 kNm stair point gives a bounded contact screen of 1.117 MPa at its declared fan and eccentricity, not proof of the actual coupled interface or neighbour nodes.

Deep supports with a connected grillage and suspended mineral floor remain the conditional G1 leader because the owner requires tree/heave resilience. Raft/piled-raft and site-justified alternatives remain comparative. The old 400 mm pile joint, low tie and cage clashes are rejected evidence; the old 850 kN/100 kNm node ceiling cannot be inherited from the roof/floor tables; D1800 is a member-only depth cost, not a selected full node/grade solution. The 300 mm heave + 50 mm construction loss + 150 mm ventilation stress needs the *actual lowest cold node*, not merely the member soffit. The court grade/DPC, passive water escape, actual root/rig/site and moving gravity service crossings remain open. Final pile-bar fabrication is an adoption and construction design duty, not a newly invented prerequisite for recording this bounded HOUSE result.

**Disposition:** this study earns named reaction territories, a finite bridge action, source-bound gross heavy-wall and slab-gap challenges, and an explicit lateral/thermal-bearing transfer sequence. It exposes where a complete whole-house reaction model is still absent. It cannot close STR-HOUSE or unlock STR-RECONCILE until the remaining physical mass, corner members, arch/jamb and diaphragm/connection paths, warm/cold force transfer and inhabited section are resolved or the arrangement is redesigned. Competent/site acceptance remains separately required at the existing EPR/DPL/DEL gates.

The subsequent [physical slab and support transfer](physical-slab-foundation-join.md) partitions this study's 23.152 m² remainder under the declared continuous RC200 rectangle, corrects the side-beam mass overlap and traces loggia ends, southwest return and gross opening challenges. It remains a conditional design study with unqualified member and warm/cold connections.
