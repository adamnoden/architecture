# J-RC3 — compact B joined concrete floor, riser and court passage

**1 November 2026. Internal reference design; unadopted.** Starting main `1e7b2cbce408834ca3cbb91c286af37eda066829`. This answers a construction question left open by the [hanger study](house-riser-trim-study.md): can a conventional solid slab remove the complicated riser trim while keeping actual floor, support and service boundaries? It also supplies the finite structural components for HAD/MAT's single raised court-passage candidate. Compact B remains the trial coordinate frame, including x9300/140-core spine, two front singles and the disclosed storage cost. No accepted house source changes.

**Recommendation:** develop the solid RC200 riser floor rather than pursue a special HC150 hanger as the default. Its continuous concrete and deliberately formed penetrations remove the unsupported cabinet base, the 0.110 m² floor gap and the missing opposing east slab end. The joined court candidate is much more burdensome: its complete section requires D1300 under the declared movement stress. That is an actual architectural price, not a recommendation to raise a real site automatically. The nominal 100-total-cover/400-box/+300-rise package is rejected. STR-HOUSE remains open; this component reference does not resolve stair/bridge, roof heel, every arch, actual ground or the whole service programme.

## Preliminary calculation basis and finite reinforcement

This is a first-generation Eurocode-style internal calculation, not a claim of current-code compliance. C30/37, B500, fctm2.9MPa, fcd=0.85×30/1.5=17MPa, fyd=500/1.15=434.78MPa, concrete25kN/m³, E=22000(38/10)^0.3 are **trial material specifications**, not measured strengths or procured products. Cover25 is an explicit conditional input, not an accepted exposure/fire requirement. Bar diameters and counts below are calculated proposals, not assumed existing reinforcement.

For a simply supported uniform member, M=wL²/8 and V=wL/2. The rectangular compression block gives x=As·fyd/(0.8bfcd), z=d−0.4x, MR=As·fyd·z. The calculation checks x/d≤0.45, minimum steel and the concrete shear expression with k≤2 and rho≤0.02. No redistribution or favourable arching is credited. All values are reproducible from the JSON; resistances are conditional on the stated material/detail model.

## Serviceability, bearings and actual destinations

The corrected bridge is cracked under service load: SLS moment exceeds its4.872kNm gross cracking estimate. The calculation therefore uses a fully cracked transformed section, no tension-stiffening credit, E_s200GPa and the derived concrete E. Permanent load uses E/(1+phi), variable load immediate E. For phi0/1/2/3, immediate-plus-creep sensitivity deflections are approximately2.76/3.21/3.59/3.94mm. A **separate hypothetical**300microstrain top-to-bottom differential shrinkage adds0.531mm. The phi3 combined4.471mm is below the deliberately declared L/250 comparator5.212mm. Neither phi3 nor300microstrain is a prediction; restraint, cracks, actual exposure, construction age and the masonry tolerance still need explicit criteria. The thin RC100 predecessor is not retained as an equally endorsed option.

Closed U-side H6 links/150 provide a calculated steel shear route; the plain concrete-only shear result is close to demand and is reported rather than advertised as a generous margin. Actual torsion, lateral soil/water pressure, uplift, shrinkage/thermal restraint, long-term side-beam cracking and differential support movements are outside this vertical reference. No water-tight concrete or hydrostatic uplift performance follows.

The U and the masonry bridges have **different supports**. At N, four U side-beam seats are100×300 rectangles at x6098.5 or7001.5 and y7932.5 or11517.5. At E, seats are300×100 at x7932.5 or11517.5 and y6623.5 or7526.5. These centre on court/outer inner-leaf **grid axes8082.5 and11667.5**, on designed concrete pedestals connected to the continuous foundation grillage; end overhangs remain within the conservative4250 calculation envelope. They are not inferred masonry face widths or automatically existing piers.

Crosswall bridge seats instead lie wholly **outside** the1003 U: N x5798.5–6098.5 and7101.5–7401.5; E y6323.5–6623.5 and7626.5–7926.5. Each seat is300 across corridor and700 along travel, on independent permanent piers into the same structural grid. Bridge→pier→grid carries masonry; U sidewall→end seat→grid carries the trough. Neither relies on the removable cap, gas lid, pipes or soil. Bearing stresses in JSON are demands on the receiving detail, not capacities of unspecified masonry/DPC. The grid's reinforcement, support stiffness, piles and geotechnical load path remain the actual-site foundation design; D1300 proves only a geometric clearance stress.

The upper continuous slab bears over the full140 spine, with the upper wall vertically aligned above the lower wall. It does not require two independent100 HC end seats on140 or use a cabinet component as a tie. Its outer-east reaction still meets the ground plant-door territory: this reference does **not** clear that arch/spandrel, actual roof stability, or every load in STR-HOUSE. Named lower reactions remain in [house support](house-support-study.md), not erased by the floor replacement.

## Construction, renewal and rejection limits

An arch is the structural-direction preference where it has real abutments and room for its thrust/depth. Here a crosswall arch spanning1003 clear with, for example,250 rise and215 radial ring occupies at least465 vertically and develops horizontal thrust requiring a tie/abutment. It cannot inherit the shallow below-floor bridge envelope or leave the same service withdrawal/headspace. The reinforced flat bridge is the explicit conventional exception for this crossing, with disclosed tension and a thermal bridge; no universal anti-arch conclusion follows.

Cast the U/base and permanent seat/bridge system with recoverable formwork before the surrounding floor closes. Verify bar spacing/returns, plinths, lid/flange access and full soil gap before concealment. Install formed penetrations with reinforcement routed around them and added H10 edge bars top/bottom, anchoring beyond the hole zone; do not cut prestressing strands or rely on later drilling. The upper cabin frame remains fixed through the IAS strainer task. Complete service loading, fire/acoustic seals and future stack extraction are separate from the local cap/screen witness.

A1003×400×100 cap alone is approximately**100.3kg** at2500kg/m³. Adding80mm mineral layers at2200kg/m³ gives170.9kg before insulation, hardware or retained water. It is a **plant-assisted lift**, not an ordinary hand-removable tile. First lift the separately detailed finish/insulation layers where designed, then release the RC cap with rated anchors and a planned gantry/trolley route; the bridge and cabinet are never lifted. No lifting anchor, staging-floor capacity, actual equipment or full withdrawal event has been qualified. A requirement for ordinary unassisted full-cover release rejects this particular module. MAT's independent lid/flange must then release and reinstate without supporting floor load; HAD/IAS must demonstrate carrier translation below each700-long permanent bridge.

Before selection, resolve: actual exposure/cover and concrete cage; complete riser inventory and seals; wall/plinth thermal bridge and stepped mineral DPC/air-gas boundary; actual longitudinal/asymmetric/U-base restraint and lifting details; plant-door arch and the remaining house supports; actual roof and foundation/site movements. These are specific decisions on a concrete reference, not a supplier-catalogue waiting loop. Their named existing owners are STR-RECONCILE/BCR-003/ER-STR-01, MAT-ENVELOPE/BCR-016, IAS/HAD and DPL/DEL/PHY.
