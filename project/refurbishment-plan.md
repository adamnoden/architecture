## Meaningful closure

Every material obligation must end in one of:

1. **Resolved at the stated scope**, with inspectable evidence and current-house applicability.
2. **Eliminated**, through an adopted simplification or architectural rewind and reconciled downstream consequences.
3. **Bounded residual**, with the unearned claim withheld and no concealed unresolved fit, route, topology or safety dependency necessary to the intended result.

`Routed-pending`, a named consultant, a proposed test, an assumed product or a future verification date are not resolution. An indispensable evidence dependency keeps the relevant adoption/convergence gate blocked until evidence arrives or a substantiated fallback is adopted. Specific justified dependencies may be added to PCO; not every possible EPR/PHY question becomes a blanket hard gate.

An agent can prepare a review brief, research primary sources, build a bounded model, inspect supplied evidence and record an architectural decision. It cannot fabricate a physical test, external professional response or actual-size print verdict. Those remain explicitly manual. No external messaging, commissioning, manufacture or spend is authorised merely by making a node swarmable.
