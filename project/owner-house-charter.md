# Owner-house charter

**Authority:** the owner's explicit direction on 29 October 2026: the primary purpose is to design and ultimately build the owner's own house on a later-selected London plot. The book is a thoughtful mechanism for designing that house; computation makes its information dependable. This charter owns that intent, not architectural dimensions, execution status or evidence maturity.

## What success means

The principal outcome is a home the owner wants to inhabit, with coherent architecture, credible cost and construction, and maintainability that works in ordinary occupation. The project should ultimately produce and coordinate the drawings, specifications, schedules, calculations and evidence needed by the appointed team to deliver it, then retain useful handover and maintenance information.

The book is a serious architectural enquiry conducted through that design: it tests intentions, compares alternatives and communicates what the work actually teaches. The compiler is supporting infrastructure for consistent information, selected checks and change traceability. Neither a finished book nor a general-purpose software platform is a prerequisite for progressing a genuine house commission.

Priority when objectives conflict:

1. The owner's life, needs, values, resources and the actual site's constraints.
2. A coherent, desirable, deliverable house.
3. Research and writing that improve decisions and earn transferable conclusions.
4. Computation that supports those decisions and dependable information production.

Writing and design remain reciprocal: writing exposes muddled design; design exposes empty prose. A feature does not earn a place in the house because a chapter or software abstraction needs it. Conventional construction and deletion remain legitimate results.

## Three identities

- **Architectural proposition:** transferable questions and principles, with evidence limits.
- **Pre-site Reference House:** the accepted 12 × 12 m design study. Current dimensions and choices remain owned by the Reference House sources and shared interface contract; this charter does not duplicate them.
- **Commissioned house:** the eventual site-specific design, with surveyed context, adopted brief, responsibilities, applicable targets and evidence. It may legitimately differ from the study through explicit owner/design decisions.

The accepted envelope is a strong project choice and a plot-selection criterion. It is not evidence that the house fits an unknown site. Retain dated studies as evidence; do not silently promote their assumptions or old technical proofs to the commissioned design.

## Material and rainwater direction — 30 October 2026

The owner requires the strongest practicable avoidance of plastic across the **whole installed house**, including concealed parts and especially visible/touchable surfaces. This is a binding design constraint, not an optional finish preference. Concealment, industry custom and a metal exterior do not exempt hidden constituents. The [whole-house material policy](../docs/reference-house/material-policy.md) owns the exact scope, evidence and explicit owner-approved exception procedure. No exception is approved by this charter, and no safety/performance duty may be omitted to produce a nominally plastic-free result.

Prefer simple, forgiving roof shedding into designed ground receivers where the full geometry and failure case support it; where controlled collection is needed, develop copper rainwater goods. Explore English hedges and genuine brass channels as integrated architecture, without depending on living foliage to prevent water damage. Provide demonstrably independent backup paths and safe overflow, not merely an extra pipe sharing the same failure. The [rainwater rebrief](rwt/rainwater-elegance-brief.md) owns the design questions and acceptance boundaries.

The [MAT programme](mat/material-integrity-programme.md) and existing RWT workstream carry this direction into the canonical execution graph. Earlier plastic carrier and polymer-dependent assembly studies remain historical evidence; their material preferences do not override the owner's new requirement. The actual assembly redesign and hydraulic proof still have to be earned.

## Structural direction — 30 October 2026

The owner prefers solid internal masonry, genuine arches at internal and external openings, direct bearing and walls/supports aligned between storeys. Explore non-timber primary floor and roof structure first. The [structural brief](../docs/reference-house/structural-brief.md) owns these preferences, their exception boundaries and the distinction between solidity and loadbearing; it selects no blanket wall thickness and does not prohibit necessary tension resistance.

The [STR programme](str/structural-architecture-programme.md) develops a coherent structural proposition with HAD and the existing assembly/evidence owners. It must test both load paths and the quality of the inhabited house. The owner's sense of repose is a valid design motive, not a general physiological or safety claim. This direction does not silently adopt new geometry or turn earlier structural concepts into verified systems.

## Ground water and rising-damp direction — 30 October 2026

The owner requires **dryness by position and geometry**: accommodation and essential services must not depend on active dewatering or inaccessible waterproofing holding back groundwater. Ground-contact foundations may tolerate their assessed wet exposure; the house must not depend on pumping to suppress groundwater around it. This constrains plot selection, building levels and any proposed basement, undercroft, deeper service trench, sunken perimeter margin or courtyard. Raising the house must preserve a coherent accessible principal approach.

Plastic damp-proof courses are rejected as the design direction. Develop suitable slate and engineering-brick alternatives with **substantial construction and generous evidence-based margin**. The owner clarified that "triple redundancy" meant overbuilding, such as greater slate depth, with thickness and arrangement left to design judgement; three separate systems or a literal multiplier are not required. The owner's **1,000-year ambition** governs long-term durability and stewardship; it is not an established service life or a zero-risk promise. No material build-up is selected by this instruction.

The [ground-water and rising-damp policy](../docs/reference-house/ground-water-policy.md) owns the exact acceptance boundaries and source evidence. Existing MAT, DPL, RWT, BCR, MRA and delivery work carry the requirement into design and assurance. Policy adoption does not mark those technical questions resolved.

## P0 ground movement and boundary infrastructure — 31 October 2026

The owner directs the house to **resist damaging differential settlement and clay heave on a potentially tree-heavy London plot**, instead of restricting plot choice to inexpensive or convenient geology. The guiding relationship is **house → deliberate ground/site interfaces → real site**. This is a design-order and quality ambition, not a claim of geological independence: the actual soil, mature trees, groundwater and street networks must still be surveyed and the foundation appropriately engineered. Prefer deep load transfer, stable continuous wall support and an intentionally raised/suspended mineral floor as the first research hypothesis, with quantified masonry movement performance, verified concrete/pile workmanship and honest comparison against shallow foundations, rafts and historic stone/brick alternatives. No foundation type, movement tolerance or site-wide suitability is adopted by this policy. The [P0 ground movement brief](str/ground-movement-and-substructure-brief.md) owns the detailed structural investigation.

**Boundary Service Lodge** is the adopted name for a proposed small, beautiful, secure brick utility structure just inside the street-side site boundary, containing appropriate upstream owner isolation, useful low-maintenance indications, durable physical service identities, safe working access and built-in task lighting. It complements **independent internal house isolation**. Examine a deliberately accessible, covered exterior service raceway between the lodge and the house; **below grade need not mean buried in backfill**. Compare grates, solid liftable covers and separate utility compartments without assuming gravel makes a trench dry. Public utility control/metering rules, frost, air/water/gas seals, tree-root preservation, soil movement, gravity foul drainage and flood/backwater behaviour remain binding design constraints. Network locations cannot be assumed centralised, and approval of a dedicated electrical brick kiosk does not authorise mixed utility occupancy. The [Boundary Service Lodge and raceway brief](had/boundary-service-lodge-and-raceway-brief.md) owns this owner-house architectural enquiry; [PAT-001](../docs/patterns/controlled-utility-entry.md) remains the general entry pattern.

STR and HAD own constructive structural/architectural candidates respectively; DPL owns site/network evidence, MAT complete material assemblies, MRA adversarial failure scenarios, and BCR/DEL eventual installation and access obligations. Record new executable packets through the existing PCO graph rather than starting a second site or services programme.

## Development and assurance — 31 October 2026

The owner directs the project to exhaust useful internal research and design work before treating external review as a reason to stop. Agents may complete comparative studies, preliminary calculations, reference designs and technical reviews through primary-source research, explicit assumptions, reproducible methods and adversarial internal challenge. They must reach a reasoned recommendation, including rejection or a conventional alternative where the evidence warrants it. A second brief asking someone else to think is not completion of that work.

External review is not a default prerequisite for **development completion**. Keep it separate from **acceptance for the affected use**. Each retained hold must name the missing evidence, the decision or claim it prevents, its responsible route and the latest point at which it must be resolved. Missing measurements, product evidence, physical tests, justified specialist checks and statutory decisions remain real obligations. A safety-critical uncertainty may prevent fixing a dependent design decision while options and unrelated work continue; do not defer every review indiscriminately until construction.

Bind findings to the actual source revision; distinguish adopted design from trial alternatives and recheck affected findings when inputs change. Internal AI challenge is not independent professional review. Completing an investigation may expose a failed or unresolved design; it does not turn that design into a pass or increase its maturity. Existing BCR, EPR, PHY and DEL records own the residual obligations; do not create a parallel assurance register.

Use this short status note on development packages where needed: **Reference design — not released for construction. Assumptions and outstanding verification accompany the proposal; internal checks do not constitute external assurance or certification.** The note communicates status and does not discharge evidence obligations. Competence and design-risk duties can arise during design, not only on site; this policy creates no exemption from them. See the [England building-regulations duties guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/design-and-building-work-meeting-building-requirements) and [HSE designer duties](https://www.hse.gov.uk/construction/areyou/designer.htm).

## Working limits

- Establish the brief and cost questions early. No budget, household detail, completion date or procurement decision may be invented from this objective.
- Work before a plot is selected should improve choices, geometry, interfaces or a plot brief. Pause site-dependent detail where further precision would be fictitious.
- Keep personal taste distinct from general architectural claims. A specific home can support a valuable book without being a universal house system.
- Professional judgement, statutory decisions, physical testing and construction evidence remain real activities with competent owners. Software coordinates their inputs and results; it does not manufacture them.
- External contact, appointments, purchasing, construction and other commitments require their own actual authorisation. This charter authorises project planning and existing scoped repository work, not those commitments.
- Private site, budget, household and contractual evidence should have an appropriate private home and a controlled reference where necessary; do not publish it merely because the book or repository exists.
