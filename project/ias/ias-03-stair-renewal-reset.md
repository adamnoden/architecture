Opposite-face handlers can turn a narrow load locally, but a fore/aft pair cannot simply enter the landing as an unchanged rigid procession: the forward handler reaches the back wall before the rear support reaches level floor. A positive first rest spanning unequal stair/landing levels or an alternative transition must be supplied. No invented pair of hands, unrestrained leaning panel or unsupported strap change earns that event.
