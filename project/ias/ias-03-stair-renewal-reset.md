# IAS-03 — rear-tray first-release and renewal reset

**5 November 2026 · bounded source correction; IAS-03 remains planned.** The OAS north-window configuration has been rejected; no window, stair route, drain, tray or handling assembly is adopted here. The current whole-tray exterior rig is a conditional development direction, not an owner requirement or a demonstrated consequence of stair geometry.

Protect the longer-lived house from maintenance damage. A retired replaceable product need not automatically leave intact with all its original supports and pipework. Its replacement must nevertheless enter, assemble and connect. Neither destructive retirement nor a bare moving rectangle proves that inbound operation.

## Next architecture decision

Stop extending hypothetical cargo trajectories until a **complete actual first-moving package** exists. Within the existing IAS/MAT/HAD scope, resolve one matched tray, frame, flange, outlet/elbow/socket and port setout. The immediate measurable decision is whether a real permitted outlet orientation and fitting envelope can reach the current port, or whether the port/full kit must change. Record the actual engagement lengths, bend/body solids, support and tool access; do not treat110 as available empty space.

Then demonstrate independent lower-body/tail retention, flange release, seal renewal and frame-clamp access before lifting. Name exactly what remains attached to the tray, with its complete envelope, mass, grips and first supported rest. Prove the incoming replacement and its connection sequence as well as outgoing retirement. Only that bound package earns renewed human/equipment route comparison. Existing [wet-export requirements](ias-03-wet-export.md) and [selection closeout](ias-03-selection-closeout.md) remain conditional; this correction supplies neither IAS completion nor a TRIAD selected-occurrence pass.
