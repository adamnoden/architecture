# IAS-03 — rear-tray first-release and renewal reset

**5 November 2026 · bounded source correction; IAS-03 remains planned.** The OAS north-window configuration has been rejected; no window, stair route, drain, tray or handling assembly is adopted here. The current whole-tray exterior rig is a conditional development direction, not an owner requirement or a demonstrated consequence of stair geometry.

Protect the longer-lived house from maintenance damage. A retired replaceable product need not automatically leave intact with all its original supports and pipework. Its replacement must nevertheless enter, assemble and connect. Neither destructive retirement nor a bare moving rectangle proves that inbound operation.

## Actual connection comes before a carrying envelope

The service baseline at blob `2d8f7243fb87d350763f2bca3d103b89b9d14b7d` records rear1000 drain centre **2012.5/9452.5** and port/tail centre **2122.5/9452.5**: **110 mm east**. Its maximum75 stripped stem and500 below-FFL tail are an **unmatched compatibility requirement**, not a qualified product connection. See [the owned inventory and first-release contract](../mat/whole-house-service-baseline.md#whole-inventory-and-first-release). No MAT source is changed by this IAS finding.

The actual manufacturer source is [BetteCompact+ B568 installation instructions, Z0020405 Rev.2, September2026](https://shop.my-bette.com/out/media/Z0020405-REV2.pdf), retrieved5 November2026, SHA256 `72b5e6f3a822058dd28da087b48ed7ee605e6f2b62c4e7b76a76c6395ab2f7b1`. Therefore the **direct east-facing outlet followed by a downward turn centred on the existing110-offset port fails before any downstream elbow engagement or bend envelope is added**. The illustrated Ø40 conversion does not cure it. This is a counterexample to that specific setout, not proof that every rotated/dogleg fitting or revised port position is impossible. No arbitrary additional spigot shortening, smaller unproved fitting or empty elbow volume is allowed to rescue it. The MAT-SERVICES owner and coordinating reviewer independently inspected the same native p5 and confirmed the dimensional reading.

Printed p5 separately identifies the removable cup **Z0020431**, retaining ring **Z0020433**, seal **Z0020434** and lower body. Printed p6 figures4–5 show the three screws fastening the upper ring through the tray into the lower body, using a hand screwdriver at **maximum1.5 Nm**. Figure6 inserts the cup separately. Routine cup removal is therefore not whole-body separation.

That bolted topology makes controlled reverse flange release a mechanically plausible **candidate**. The instructions do not establish an OEM reverse-removal procedure, above-only separation, safe independent retention of the lower body/tail, reused-seal suitability, or access to every frame clamp. [Foot-System Z0009038 Rev.15, December2025](https://shop.my-bette.com/out/media/Z0009038-REV15.pdf), pp7 and10–13, shows the under-rim clamped installation; it does not earn in-situ frame separation. The [existing MAT alternative](../mat/interior-shower-tray-alternatives.md#matched-frame-separate-service-face-and-first-moving-unit) requires reachable clamps, temporary support and controlled release before a reduced carrying envelope can be used. B568's polymer constituents also remain subject to MAT admission; dimensional usefulness is no material exception.

## Next architecture decision

Stop extending hypothetical cargo trajectories until a **complete actual first-moving package** exists. Within the existing IAS/MAT/HAD scope, resolve one matched tray, frame, flange, outlet/elbow/socket and port setout. The immediate measurable decision is whether a real permitted outlet orientation and fitting envelope can reach the current port, or whether the port/full kit must change. Record the actual engagement lengths, bend/body solids, support and tool access; do not treat110 as available empty space.

Then demonstrate independent lower-body/tail retention, flange release, seal renewal and frame-clamp access before lifting. Name exactly what remains attached to the tray, with its complete envelope, mass, grips and first supported rest. Prove the incoming replacement and its connection sequence as well as outgoing retirement. Only that bound package earns renewed human/equipment route comparison.
