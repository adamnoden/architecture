## Ground support, materials and installation

Retain the continuous permanent support and independent services. The 15 mm wearing zone uses 13 mm complete oak or a 10 mm stone face in a 3 mm tray, plus 2 mm loose bedding. Under the retained MAT/HAD datums, the 0.3 mm separator leaves **64.7 mm before levelling** against the existing 65 mm conventional screed comparator. This is still short; a floor whose finish lifts neatly is not a proved substrate. Do not borrow depth from the permanent slab, insulation or thresholds. Resolve the full mineral supporting course or a justified alternative and preserve the actual level/tolerance challenge.

Timber grain and remanufacturable parts, tile release joints, wear edges, key engagement, backing and tray stiffness, electrical protection, skirting services, air continuity, fire/acoustic boundaries and wet transitions retain their existing material and assurance owners. Painted brass appearance is not an alloy declaration. No polymer, sealant, bedding or concealed fixing receives an exception by being hidden. This dry Study tile comparison is not a waterproof room.

Time a two-person prepared-room assembly only after surveying the shell, gauging supports and separately installing/testing service and protective carriers. Stage labelled parts and spares before the start. Count the floor, wall faces, mouldings, positive stops, skirts, grilles and non-live covers; stop only after seating, restraint, fit and cleaning checks. Record workshop work, shell preparation, transport/unpacking, finish assembly, service commissioning and snagging separately. The number of small parts makes the one-hour ambition a demanding proposition to test, not an achieved result or a reason to hide work outside the stopwatch.
