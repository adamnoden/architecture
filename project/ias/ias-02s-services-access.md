# IAS-02S — services and access family

**Scope:** candidate service/access architecture against IAS-01V/01H.
**House:** RH-LAYOUT-REVISION-02 / RH-LAYOUT-FAMILY-02.
**Inputs:** [vertical candidates](ias-01v-vertical-candidates.md),
[horizontal candidates](ias-01h-horizontal-candidates.md), shared contract.

## Route decision

Use the plant/service geography and declared riser for primary distribution,
with local accessible dry routes where demand earns them. Do not populate the
historic RH-06 service network in this changed house. Current routes are
reservations, not sized MEP. Joints needing ordinary inspection/renewal should
prefer plant, cabinetry or dry-side service fronts to an inaccessible floor.

| Service/task | Candidate location | Exclusions / next input |
|---|---|---|
| Electrical/data/control branches | Selective room-side skirting or joinery route, with declared rises | No water/soil/extract; actual segregation, protection, bend and thermal capacity require design |
| Water isolation and unions | Plant/utility or an accessible wet-service front | No default shallow skirting joints or inaccessible floor connections |
| Waste/trap service | Actual fixture cabinet or dry service face | Gradients, diameters, trap withdrawal and acoustic duties cannot be inferred from a line |
| Extract/fan/terminal maintenance | Service zone with terminal-side/local release | No assumption a duct fits 55 mm or can cross framing freely |
| Plant replacement | External plant aperture and actual working strip | No storage or cabinetry in the replacement field; clear opening/equipment remain inputs |
| Ordinary finish renewal | Local trim or wearing layer | Do not turn structure, critical seals or waterproofing into the first removable layer |

These are task classes, not a selected equipment schedule. No capacity, circuit,
pipe diameter, fall, duct size or product mass is invented.

## S-D — worked dry skirting trial

Compare ordinary 150 mm-high, 18 mm-projecting skirting with a **150 mm-high,
45 mm-projecting** service trial. An 18 mm removable face leaves **27 mm gross**
behind it, before supports, fixings, segregation and bends. That is not 27 mm
continuous free cable capacity. Use a bounded run between real interruptions;
a trial removable length up to 1200 mm is a handling comparison, not a
manufacturer/module standard.

Use separately supported service containment and replaceable face fixings.
Decorative timber is not automatically an electrical enclosure. Where removing
trim exposes an electrical route, connections retain their required enclosure,
protection and controlled isolation. No live termination depends on the face
being present. Do not fasten a cable through a removable decorative piece.

Prefer one declared service class/run while its actual requirements are unknown.
Do not claim a thin internal divider automatically makes power/data segregation
adequate. Count any tray, divider, insulation or protection within the budget.
Accessories use actual backboxes/supports and independently removable covers;
do not require detaching live accessories just to release a long skirting.

### Floor-area and circulation consequence

The west gallery has 1015 mm nominal wall-face clearance. Two ordinary 18 mm
projections leave **979 mm at skirting level**; two 45 mm service trials leave
**925 mm**. A mixed 18/45 arrangement leaves **952 mm**. These are local
projection calculations, not a person/escape/accessibility certificate.

Do not introduce the deep service trial as the west-gallery default. Prefer
the existing eastern service geography and selective room/joinery routes.
Elsewhere, show actual projection against furniture/doors before adoption.
No source wall face or room clearance changes in this packet.

### Corners, end pieces and door-side rises

Use short independently released corner/end pieces, with a supported service
bend behind them. A neatly mitred cover does not establish the bend radius.
A door-side rise is a separately supported route, not an instruction to hollow
every architrave. Stop it before the OAS-owned mounting/environmental zone.

Release the short end/cover; expose ordinary accessible captive screws; support
and withdraw the face normal to the wall; inspect without pulling services;
renew local damaged capture/seats; refit and check. No lateral sliding behind
fixed cabinetry and no paint/filler bridge over the release seam.

At significant room/boundary changes, stop and design the crossing. Removability
does not justify a continuous smoke, noise, pest or moisture path. No cornice
is a catch-all duct, and no door threshold is a casual cable crossing.

## Access hierarchy

| Tier | Actual task | Candidate release / reinstatement |
|---|---|---|
| Routine | Control, isolation, filter or ordinary terminal access | Direct reachable operating cover; no room dismantling |
| Trade service | Union/trap/small component exchange | Independently released local V3 face, actual tool/support/withdrawal field |
| Equipment renewal | Cistern/fan/appliance/plant exchange | Sized leaf/door/module route; never inherit a small inspection aperture's result |
| Fabric/assembly renewal | Frame, carrier or datum replacement | Explicit support/isolation and boundary reinstatement; specialist evidence where needed |

A panel is successful only at its declared tier and actual component. A view
through an opening is not a withdrawal route.

## Upper service fronts and I00-C1 disposition

Carry V3's 700 x 600 gross / 650 x 550 clear / 740 x 640 face as a candidate
family. At family-bath centre y 4750, the face interval 4380–5120 leaves
467.5 / 1317.5 mm to the nominal return faces. The principal-en-suite 900 mm
source reservation needs its own actual aperture/frame/face; do not silently
substitute this smaller family.

For the small en suite retain the **y 3300 alternative** for comparison:
217.5 / 117.5 mm face margins. The old y 3450 reservation is **not fit for
adoption as this face**. Status I00-C1: **source/task reconciliation required
before selected-family adoption**, not closed by a green interval calculation.

The current access front at wet-spine x 9500 and lining reservation at x 9862.5
still lack a demonstrated contiguous service section. A 250 mm rectangle is
not proven clear depth. Define the actual component and its support, connection
and withdrawal first; resolve the interposed construction/space at the owning
house source. Do not tunnel a sleeve across an occupied bathroom by implication.

A source change must preserve the room/door/window/boundary and branch geometry
or record the exact rewind. Inspection-only access cannot stand in for cistern
or soil-stack replacement. IAS-03 owns adoption and reconciliation after the
remaining opening junction; this packet changes no reservation.

## Grilles and failure declaration

Use a separately removable grille/terminal on a supported frame, with cleanable
edges and access to the actual terminal. Face pattern comes after required
free area, pressure/noise, weather/fire and cleaning duties are known. No
decorative perforation is counted as ventilation.

Keep wet failures out of a long dry skirting trough. A leak should declare at
the source/service side rather than migrate behind several rooms. Candidate
wet components need first-wetted surface, visible declaration, isolation and
drying recorded in IAS-02W; sensors do not replace that path.

## Result and downstream obligations

The family is selective dry distribution plus task-sized local service access,
with ordinary independent releases. No full-house floor access or removable
wall is earned. No installed MEP, performance class or carrying pass is claimed.

Concrete issues for IAS-03/BCR are: actual service containment/capacity and
crossings; upper-front contiguous section and I00-C1 disposition; real
component/tool/withdrawal dimensions; boundary treatment at each release;
grille/terminal duty; and local failure declaration/isolation/drying.
These are actual design obligations, not generic requests for more review.
