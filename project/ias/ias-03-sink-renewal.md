## Result that changes the next cabinet detail

Franke's actual installing document supplies information absent from the first study: a **234 depth below the worktop underside to the illustrated waste's lower datum**, a **497 × 407 cutout**, and a waste axis **209 from the counter back**. At the shared example of top 900 and worktop 30, the illustrated waste ends at approximately **636 above floor**. This is 59 below the previous simplified bare-bowl trial 695 and leaves **456 gross above the trial cabinet base 180**, before any trap, branch, supports, hands or release.

The outer 530 × 440 rim exceeds even the maximum 499 × 409 cutout. Straight upward translation while level and in the same orientation is therefore unavailable. Develop **supported lowering into the cabinet followed by front withdrawal**, paying for clip access, full perimeter seal release and disconnection before movement. This is a proposed renewal direction, not a manufacturer-certified removal sequence or a demonstrated swept volume.

The [A3 analytical sheet](studies/sink-renewal.svg) and [quantity record](studies/sink-renewal.json) locate these findings in the existing conditional 800 sink module. They neither select this product assembly nor replace the old simplified comparison. ## Renewal: the physical actions the cabinet must permit

At its largest published cutout, the unrotated level bowl is 31 wider and 31 deeper than the opening. This rejects that specific straight upward translation. It does **not** establish that every possible tilted/rotated route is impossible, nor propose removing the worktop as an ordinary maintenance step.

The manual's installation direction supports investigating the following conditional sequence. The responsible design must make the relevant services safe, control residual water and provide safe handling; no lifting load or person count has been determined.

| Stage | Required physical provision | Unresolved here |
|---|---|---|
| Expose and support | Open/remove the intended front faces; place an independent support beneath the bowl before releasing its load path | Actual clear opening, hinge/stop intrusion, support contact points and access for a person/helper |
| Disconnect | Release the real waste, overflow/branch and any other connections that restrain this particular bowl; support retained pipework | Actual kit interfaces, required axial travel, whether some parts must be detached first and resulting wet surfaces |
| Release clips and perimeter joint | Reach every underside fixing and release the complete seal without damaging retained worktop/supports | Clip coordinates, wrench/driver approaches, access along all sides and compatible seal-release method |
| Lower below obstructions | Lower the supported bowl far enough to clear the retained edge/rail/clip geometry | Required travel, any front-rail obstruction, remaining base clearance and handling envelope |
| Withdraw to the front | Move the bowl and retained fittings through the true opening into the cleared maintenance field | Complete swept volume, grasp positions, door states and carrying route |
| Renew and reassemble | Inspect and dry first-wetted materials; renew the specified joint/fixings and reconnect the verified assembly | Material compatibility, drying access, retest and return-to-use criteria |

Subtracting the 530 outer width from the parent's 752 gross opening gives **222 total**, or 111 per side if centred. This is before hardware, hands, tools or a retained fitting. It is not a human-access pass. A top rail is not automatically prohibited: the actual section must establish whether the released bowl can lower past it. Retain the proposed absence of a fixed centre mullion and a fixed shelf through the task, but verify that the cabinet/worktop support does not depend on them.

The dashed direction arrow in the analytical sheet is deliberately not a movement simulation. Required lowering distance, actual front aperture, clip/tool envelope, seal-release access and safe handling remain unknown in the machine-readable record.

## Material and wet-service decisions remain coupled

The existing resin bowl and polypropylene siphon findings remain unchanged. Franke's undermount method still includes a sealing function; the recovered manual does not furnish a fully declared plastic-free joint or justify deleting it. The metal bowl, overflow/waste parts, damping or underside materials, anchors, seal and worktop must be assessed as an assembly. No owner material exception is assumed.

The S8905AA metal-trap enquiry has not recovered a complete installed/release envelope or matched dishwasher branch. That is a different seal regime from the parent's 75-seal comparison. The sheet does not resolve release travel, seal constituents or an appliance branch. Do not insert it as a drop-in solution or infer universal regulatory suitability from its dimensions.

If the exact undermount assembly cannot achieve admissible materials and practical renewal, compare a real alternative mounting family with its own waste and support details. A generic inset outline would simply reopen the same gaps. The witness surface, first-wetting paths, accessible isolation, appliance disconnection before withdrawal and all-affected-surface drying duties from the parent result remain active.
