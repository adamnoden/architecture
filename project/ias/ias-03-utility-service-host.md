## Declared project load domain

This is a comparison target, not measured nut force, a certified cabinet rating or permission to stand on the top: 2.0 kN aggregate vertical load including the host/top/sink/contents/services, plus a 1.0 kN local point applied to the frame. The service reaction allocation is 100 N horizontal and 200 N vertical at the valve axis, with the vertical allocation included in the aggregate. Actual forces must fit the domain or the host must be redesigned.

The model uses E = 193 GPa, a 220 MPa assumed proof-strength floor, required section-property lower bounds, a 160 MPa project stress ceiling and a 3 mm movement ceiling. The corrected comparison gives 103.08 MPa/1.309 mm for the top rail and 64.36 MPa/**3.871 mm** for the post. The post therefore **fails the 3 mm movement target** in this original section/domain; no member-pass claim is made. Post displacement includes 2.277 mm from horizontal force plus 1.594 mm from the same 35 kNmm overhang couple used in its stress model. The top-rail result adds a conservative couple-displacement bound to its force deflection. For the square service arm, horizontal force normal to the arm plus vertical force gives 65.625 MPa biaxial corner stress; horizontal force parallel to the arm gives axial plus vertical bending instead. The 0.259 mm arm displacement uses the transverse force vector magnitude. Actual terminal normal offset/torsion and connections remain unqualified. These calculations do not qualify joints, welds, bolts, local bearing, frame stability, worktop or a native fitting. Each proposed floor receiver has a 1 kN tension/1 kN shear target with linear combined interaction; the conservative unloaded-host screen is 325 N uplift plus 100 N shear on one receiver. The floor link remains unearned.

Nominal members, feet and reaction pad total approximately 25.20 kg at the declared 8000 kg/m³ mass-screen input. Covers, joints, fixtures, worktop, bowl and water are excluded. This is not a weighed moving package or a handling pass. Stainless primary members remain a project material proposal; actual grade/finish, joints, corrosion/contact, all functional residues and owner exceptions remain explicit and unapproved. No manufacturer internals are modified.
