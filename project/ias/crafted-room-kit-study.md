# The crafted Study

Develop individually seated timber finish pieces and independently gravity-hung wall faces. Compare stone-faced trays on the same floor set-out. Retain the existing finish plane in the east handling neck while the backing and window returns are designed. This is a current-house design candidate. The floor, wall, window and handling results have the limits stated below. [Owner direction](crafted-room-kit-brief.md)

## Ground-floor support selection

Prefer the **15 mm wearing zone for the next support detail**. It leaves the same Z0 floor, top of concrete at Z−180 and 100 mm cellular glass. Use 13 mm oak or a 10 mm stone face in a 3 mm steel tray. Both preserve 2 mm for removable bedding and leave 65 mm to the top of the glass. A 0.3 mm separating interface leaves **64.7 mm before levelling and tolerance**, below the ordinary lightly loaded floating-screed comparator. The 20 mm oak case is 5.3 mm short and the stone-on-raised-deck version 2.3 mm short on the same basis. Keep the shared continuous substrate with qualified floor edge and wet protection. Do not reduce the cellular glass or alter thresholds by borrowing depth from the permanent structure. [Screed guidance](https://www.concretecentre.com/Building-Elements/Floors/Floor-Screeds.aspx) · [Glass and floor precedent](https://www.foamglas.com/en-gb/applications-and-solutions/interior/floor/solid/b-i-floor-solid)

The compiler checks the [MAT stack](../mat/studies/loggia-envelope-section.json), its [HAD projection](../had/studies/water-replan.json) and the [MAT floor-pour receipt](../mat/studies/floor-pour-boundary.results.json) against the local Study datums. The allocated distribution layer and the 65 mm comparator are separate inputs. The comparator covers lightly loaded conventional floating sand/cement screed over insulation firm enough for compaction. The 0.3 mm separator is a dimensional trial. The room-kit option `groundDeckLevelLossMm` charges a declared downward deck variation to the course, while retaining the same floor and glass datums. A 1 mm trial leaves 63.7 mm. With the current stack, finish and bedding must fit within 14.7 mm before any level loss, or 13.7 mm in that trial, to meet the dimensional comparison. Those are geometry limits for a follow-up package. Ferrule engagement, true bedding, uniform bearing, screed, threshold and separator qualification remain open. [Concrete Society guidance](https://www.concrete.org.uk/fingertips/screeds-general/)

Use a through-threaded, flush ferrule in the 13 mm moving depth. A local Ø14 × 1 mm recess in the stationary bedding can clear the temporarily inserted stem without drilling the permanent deck. Test 12 mm effective engagement with the selected tolerances and seat. The oak has only about 9 mm solid between the trial recesses. The thinner stone finish integrates its carrier and edge retention at the factory. Trial complete moving masses remain below the 6 kg handling bound even allowing the entire 2 mm loose bedding to stick to the stone tray during first release. The source-bound room schedule keeps the 15 mm candidate and its unresolved substrate beside the 20 mm comparison.
