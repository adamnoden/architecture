## Service and removal sequence

Use the **300 mm** nominal floor set-out. In this Study it yields **146 pieces**, including **106** border or cut pieces. The smallest finished face dimension is **67.5 mm** and clear joints are **3 mm**. The trial timber field piece is **1.11 kg**. The stone and steel field piece is **4.94 kg** before bedding or lifting fittings. For the latter, the top finish has **3 mm** of support depth still to allocate. Keep the two wearing finishes over independent continuous support and keep all exposed edges positively located. A shifted or loose edge, a stepped seam or an unproven thin tile backing defeats the proposal.

For a floor removal, expose two compact positive keys at X3950/Y600 and X3950/Y700. Keep hands outside the adjacent seam. Lift the full panel before translating 300 mm east, support it on a flat padded first rest, then refit and check edge seating. The assumed crouched shoulder at Z360 and handles at Z180 give a **543.38 mm** straight reach to the farther key. The occupied plan leaves **13.75 mm** body reserve per side after setting. Retaining an extra 27 mm on one wall cuts the best evenly shared reserve to **0.25 mm**. Reject that proud build-up at the east handling neck. These numbers describe posture and plan geometry. Verify a restrained full-depth pickup, breakaway, neighbouring floor stability, working grip exchange and first-rest landing under the complete moving mass.

For wall removal, isolate only services affected by the cover. Leave services on separate carriers. Take out the locally screwed fillet and anti-lift stop, lift the panel on its gravity rail and draw towards the room. A **30 mm nominal** pocket leaves **5.95 mm** beyond the 19.05 mm benchmark after 5 mm setting. Give the last panel and each reveal its own stop and roomward handling field. The current 27 mm finish territory can take the proposed **18 mm face, 7 mm capture and 2 mm fit**. Keeping the original 27 mm mineral layer behind it doubles the depth. Design the protective layer and its supports explicitly before selecting the flush assembly. Retain the G1 grille and its independent duct and seals during decorative renewal.

The steel receiver and wet-side lining remain independent across the tile threshold. Keep wet drainage, fire and air seals accessible outside ordinary decorative release. The removable tray ends on the dry side. Hold the wet-zone overlay, mineral and metal finishes, coatings, pads and temporary release stops until their actual materials and geometry are coordinated.
