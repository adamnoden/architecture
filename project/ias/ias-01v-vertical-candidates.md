# IAS-01V — vertical assembly candidates

**Scope:** candidate architecture, not selected products or installed performance.
**House:** RH-LAYOUT-REVISION-02 / RH-LAYOUT-FAMILY-02.
**Contract:** [shared interfaces](../triad-interface-contract.md).

## Question and comparison

Develop one quiet wall/attachment condition and one service-access front,
including corners, release and reinstatement. Do not turn every wall into a
removable lining. The [baseline](ias-00-baseline-and-challengers.md), existing
tectonic language and HAD's P-A/P-B/S-A/S-B candidates control the comparison.

| Candidate | Physical arrangement | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| V0 quiet wall | Existing masonry and independently designed boundary treatment; 15 mm nominal direct finish | Leading control for ordinary walls; no routine service joints behind it |
| V1 local attachment datum | Local ground replaces the corresponding finish zone; ordinary removable joinery fixed to it | Develop at real skirting/joinery/hanging tasks; no continuous rack |
| V2 removable mineral face | 15 mm retained background + 18 mm support/adjustment zone + 12 mm panel = 45 mm | Selective challenger; 30 mm more than the current 15 mm finish allowance, not a drop-in substitute |
| V3 service front | Separately supported service components and retained boundary duties; local dry-side framed aperture and removable face | Develop at upper wet spine and utility/plant tasks; not a full wall cassette |
| V4 integrated cassette | Face, services and critical boundary duties exchanged together | Reject as the ordinary wall family: too many coupled duties and no current task that requires it |

Dimensions above are explicit design trials. In V2 the 18 mm zone includes
support and adjustment, not 18 mm of empty service space. A support that needs
more depth defeats that trial; do not subtract a background or boundary layer
to recover fit. No anchor spacing, capacity, fire class or acoustic rating is
selected by this table.

## V0 / V1 — quiet wall with a useful local datum

The slow background continues behind the room. Where that background needs a
continuous air, fire, acoustic or moisture treatment, a ground or anchor must
be incorporated without simply replacing that duty with timber or trim.
The 15 mm finish allowance alone does not prove such continuity.

At a real skirting or joinery datum, compare a local timber ground and ordinary
accessible screws with conventional direct fixing. The ground may replace
local finish thickness; it does not author a chased cable route in masonry.
Heavy furniture, rails and safety-critical fittings have independently designed
attachments. A small replaceable face is not their structural support.

Keep an intelligible no-fix/service record. Ordinary hanging uses known grounds
or an appropriate safe wall field. No claim that every occupant fixing can be
carried by a 12 mm panel is made.

### Release and return

1. Identify the local item and any isolation prerequisite.
2. Reach the screws through a real removable cover or from the exposed service
   side; do not bury the first release under skim, filler or a fixed worktop.
3. Support and withdraw the local trim normal to the wall. Leave the floor and
   adjacent architrave in place.
4. Inspect the ground, edge and continuing boundary; replace a damaged local
   piece rather than drill fresh arbitrary anchors.
5. Refit, mechanically capture and check alignment, rattle and operation.

At an internal corner use independently removable short return pieces. Do not
make a long interlocked mitre require sliding a whole room's skirting sideways.
At a door keep a separately releasable stop/end piece; the definitive overlap
with the frame belongs to IAS-OAS-JUNCTION. Decoration must not bridge a release.

## V2 — mineral lining challenge, with its cost made visible

Use a bounded panel field on an actual solid wall, not a universal module.
The candidate panel is mechanically captured on independently replaceable
supports; release fixings remain reachable after decoration. Real edge joints
remain joints. Do not fill them to imitate monolithic plaster.

A 600 x 900 x 12 mm trial face is 0.00648 m³ before finish/hardware. Its mass is
**0.00648 × actual material density**, plus finish/hardware; density and safe
handling are not supplied. A full-height equivalent is not assumed manageable.
The trial is a local inspection/renewal piece, not a selected production module.

Against the current wall datum V2 consumes 30 mm extra room depth per affected
face. Two opposed applications consume 60 mm. It cannot be installed while
claiming unchanged room clearances. Keep it out of the 1015 mm west gallery as
an early default. No source face is moved in this packet. A specific location
requires an explicit depth reallocation/rewind before adoption.

The background retains its independent duties when the face is off. Stop the
support cavity at significant boundaries; no full-house open void is admitted.
Compare tactile solidity, edge wear, rattle, appearance, impact, ordinary fixing
and repeated reinstatement against V0, not merely the ease of first removal.
PHY-01 remains held research; drawing this candidate does not reactivate it.

## V3 — worked dry-side access front

Use a **700 x 600 mm gross wall aperture**, 25 mm frame border on each side and
a **740 x 640 mm removable face** overlapping 20 mm beyond the aperture. The
nominal clear opening is therefore **650 x 550 mm**. Face thickness, capture,
support, height and material are candidate detail choices, not a certified
access-panel product. Put the gross aperture at z 500–1100 mm for this trial.

Separate three objects: wall aperture, clear passage and face footprint. A
700 mm line on the plan was not all three. Use captive ordinary screws into
replaceable frame hardware, face removal normal to the gallery, and a supported
handling operation. No strong magnet is the sole positive restraint.

### I00-C1 — quantified candidate, not a source correction

For the small-en-suite front the nominal clear wall interval is y 2712.5–3787.5.
At the old centre 3450, the 740 mm face reaches 3820: **32.5 mm overlap** with
the return. Moving the candidate centre to **3300** gives face interval
2930–3670 and end margins **217.5 / 117.5 mm**. The 700 mm gross aperture then
occupies 2950–3650. This resolves the *trial face/return geometry*, not actual
component access or room fit. No plan reservation is silently moved.

The intended trial task is isolation/union inspection and local small-component
exchange. A declared 300 x 250 mm component cross-section inside a 650 x 550 mm
opening leaves 175 mm nominal space each side and 150 mm above/below if centred.
Those are comparison envelopes, **not an actual valve, tool or cistern product**.
Axial withdrawal, coupling release, reach, component support and handling still
need actual component geometry. Cistern/soil/fan replacement cannot inherit
this small-component task's result.

The authored upper lining begins at x 9862.5; the wet-spine centre is x 9500.
Its 362.5 mm offset does not establish a contiguous service void behind the
gallery panel. Do not call the 250 mm lining reservation clear panel depth.
IAS-02S/02W must resolve that section and the actual component route before an
installed access front or changed house source is selected.

### Service-front removal and reinstatement

Isolate the relevant service from a reachable location; protect the gallery;
support and unscrew the face; withdraw it without removing skirting, wet finish
or opening trim; inspect the frame and independent boundary; support the actual
component before disconnecting; renew and test the disturbed service; refit and
check capture, seals where required and closure. No useful release requires
entry through the shower's waterproof finish.

If the panel itself carries a required acoustic/fire boundary, that contribution
must be explicitly designed and evidenced. Boundary independence is a design
goal, not a claim that an access hole through a partition has no consequence.

## Result and handoff

V0/V1 lead ordinary-room development; V3 leads the high-service comparison;
V2 is depth-costed and selective; V4 is rejected. No wall, opening or service
reservation changes. [Section studies](studies/ias-core-sections.svg) depict
candidate layers and aperture meanings, not fabrication or performance proof.

IAS-02S/02W own actual tasks/routes, I00-C1's adopted disposition and wet variants.
IAS-HAD reconciles visible trim and panel fields. IAS-OAS owns opening-adjacent
interfaces. IAS-03 makes the eventual selection; BCR routes concrete anchor,
boundary, moisture and test obligations. Candidate completion does not close
any of those gates.
