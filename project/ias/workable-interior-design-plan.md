**Authority:** owner direction, 14 November 2026; registration action [#1516](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/1516).  
**Scope:** the accepted 12 × 12 m pre-site Reference House, its book and representative assembly drawings; not a construction release.  
**Status:** **new design direction / unselected alternatives**. The registered [architecture](../control/nodes/architecture.ts) and [publication](../control/nodes/publication.ts) nodes govern readiness, dispatch and closure.

## Architectural decision

Develop a house whose **interior finishes can be deliberately opened, worked on and reinstated**, without making ordinary life feel like a plant room. The owner specifically wants to explore:

- **modular, easy-to-place/lift/replace floor tiles or panels**, potentially across ordinary occupied rooms rather than only a tiny local hatch;
- honest, beautifully detailed **metal grates and removable cover plates** where their function and safety are credible;
- deliberate **removable skirting that functions as accessible service infrastructure**, with switch/outlet/door/arch junctions designed, not improvised;
- **extensive or nearly universal panelled wall finishes**, including a genuinely panelling-dominant house, not merely token wainscoting.

This is a binding instruction to **design and put these alternatives before the owner**, not a declaration that a particular clip, floor grid, covering material, hidden void, all-over panel treatment or proprietary product is already selected. The owner's explicit approval is still required. Existing IAS-03/HAD-02M choices of continuous floors, direct mineral/plaster walls, restrained local panelling and a rejected universal cassette are **earlier scope-bounded selections**, not final owner-approved exclusions; preserve their evidence as controls and label their new decision status visibly.

The principle remains **permanent structure and enclosure first; maintainable, replaceable interior fabric second**. A removable finish does not become a load path, fire barrier, water defence, acoustic partition or vapour control just because it is convenient. Avoid hidden services in permanent masonry, but do not invent deep universal cavities to satisfy the new brief. "Accessible" means a complete safe task: isolate, expose, support, remove, work, test, refit and restore the original boundary.

## Candidate families — test, draw and compare

| Family | Owner-visible proposition | Necessary adverse/control comparison |
|---|---|---|
| **F0 — excellent conventional floor** | Continuous robust finish and discrete targeted access, the previously selected default | Benchmark its superior simplicity, acoustic continuity, cleanability and ordinary room appearance |
| **F1 — modular floor field** | Repeated mechanically retained, liftable finish modules over an **independent permanent structural deck**; seamless enough for a composed room | Quantify tile size, unsupported edge, dead load, point load, rocking, walking noise, edge wear, thresholds and lift force; no invented spare depth |
| **F2 — deeper accessible floor** | A genuinely serviceable raised/interstitial assembly where the services and walking task justify it | Price height, stairs, rooms, fire/acoustics, water ingress, thermal movement, supports, cost and whole-house consequences; reject if the retained 350 mm floor/finish reservation cannot support it |
| **W0 — direct quiet walls** | Mineral/plaster faces with selective local replaceable treatment | Benchmark hygrothermal and acoustic behaviour, cheap repair, valuable solid-wall feel and room dimensions |
| **W1 — extensive panelling** | Coherent continuous wall panels and reveals with accessible discrete attachment; explicit corner, arch, window, door, radiator and skirting composition | Demonstrate fire, fixing/support, moisture, acoustic, dust and pest pathways; do not imply an all-room services cavity |
| **W2 — panelled room with local service spine** | Broad panelled appearance plus strictly bounded independently isolated accessible service route(s) | Show what actually opens, where it opens, and whether a discreet service strip does the job without removable full walls |
| **S1 — service skirting** | Independently supported, segmented skirting cassettes, removable cover, reachable isolation/junctions and honest room character | Check circuit segregation, cable/bend/heat limits, impact, seals, future additions, furniture, radiators, stairs and door surrounds |
| **G1 — grates and service covers** | Metal grates in justified walkable, ventilating, drained or access locations; deliberately legible metalwork | Check open-area/pressure/drainage needs, slip/heels/fingers, load/support, sharp edges, noise, cleaning, corrosion and wet/fire/acoustic boundaries |

The design may combine families by room and exposure. Nearly universal **visible** panelling does not imply a single universal backing, timber type, panel thickness or removable structural substrate. Treat wet rooms, cooking, fire separations and load-bearing masonry as real constraints with compatible materials and waterproof/airtight backing preserved.

### Fixed geometry, material and engineering checks

- Start from **the source-owning current house**, not a convenient historic fixture. The existing floor reservation is **350 mm gross and already allocated**; IAS/HAD examples use **20 mm nominal dry finish** and constrained wall finishes. Subtract actual structure, levelling, retention, services, acoustic and fire layers before claiming the modular system fits.
- Keep support and service carriers distinct. A module lifted for pipe access must not leave an unguarded structural opening, destroy a fire compartment, unload a partition or require lifting fixed cabinetry.
- Test 1-person and 2-person handling with *actual estimated mass*, grip/rotation clearances, lifts and parking. Ordinary furniture, doors, stairs and radiators stay in the scene. Include obstructed, wet and failed-fastener cases, not just the ideal exploded view.
- Detail reversible joints: tolerance chain, retaining methods, thermal/movement allowance, replacing one damaged tile, wet-floor and threshold transitions, cleaning, dust ingress and long-life availability without dependence on adhesives or frequent proprietary consumables.
- Give metal grates genuine purposes. Floor grates must not create a trip/heel, noise, cleaning or child-access liability; an attractive slotted panel is not automatically a compliant air terminal or an effective drain.
- Preserve the owner's material policy and the permanent-fabric/service separation. List **whole assemblies and constituents**, including membranes, gaskets, wiring containment and coatings. Do not quietly smuggle a prohibited synthetic layer into a metal-faced system; a justified exception needs an explicit separate owner decision.
- Test all dependencies with STR, IAS/OAS, HAD, MAT, MRA, BCR, real finished room use and adjoining roof/court/wet works. If the detail requires a house geometry change, request an actual upstream rewind. Do not satisfy the brief by drawing an unrelated showcase floor.

## Minimum visible design package

**Early alternative package** (`IAS-WORKABLE-CONCEPT`, self-service):

1. A current-house floor-field diagram over actual ground and first-floor rooms showing where each module family could go, panel grid origin, room/door/threshold transitions, radiator and service locations.
2. Representative **floor-to-wall/skirting**, **wall-to-ceiling**, **door-reveal/arch**, **window-frame/trim**, **wet-room**, **stair/landing** and **metal-grate** sections. Architectural 1:5 details and at least one enlarged 1:2 retention/release detail per consequential family; explicit provisional dimensions and current-source ties.
3. A room-elevation sequence showing **panelling-dominant** and **restrained** alternatives in the actual study, family/dining, bedrooms and wet/service rooms. Include inhabited perspective/axonometric views with a person, furniture and credible light, not empty schematic diagrams.
4. An exploded kit-of-parts, material/element list, visible joint/finish palette, minimum service and opening operations, end-of-life replacement and conventional comparator. Show cumulative floor/room depth, indicative cost/complexity and compromises.
5. A **one-page owner decision sheet** with clear variants, recommended choices, what would be forfeited and what is still unproved. Do not convert the author's choice into an owner decision.

**Owner's first approval** (target `HAD-OWNER-DESIGN-REVIEW`, manual after `TRIAD-GATE`): issue **one joined architectural design pack**, including the actual court/roof and windows/doors, furniture, interior alternatives and the proposed mechanical working approach. Record owner-specific *accept / revise / reject* responses by design family, source version and drawing. Owner may want broadly panelled walls but reject a visible floor-grid pattern or choose different zones. Silence and an earlier invitation to explore are not sign-off.

**Coordinated detail package** (target `IAS-WORKABLE-DETAILS`, self-service after that approval): turn the approved family into source-bound buildability drawings and representative 1:5/1:2/1:1 joints, floor/wall/grate/skirting schedules, fastening/tolerance and removal sequences, including the actual front window/wall/floor bay consumed by BCR/ADE/DEL. Express site/product/physical proof limits; do **not** call it a universal fabrication/shop drawing package.

**Owner's final approval** (target `PLT-OWNER-FINAL-DESIGN-ACCEPTANCE`, manual after `PLT-FINAL-PREP`): show the assembled Working Edition, final architectural drawings/plates, representative detail pack and a **specific change record** versus the first approval. Obtain explicit owner design disposition before `PLT-FINAL-FREEZE`. The existing separate physical BOOK/WALL print-quality verdict and owner manufacturing decision remain.

## Acceptance and stopping rules

- The pre-site book must contain a **credible, illustrated interior-system proposition** and its true limits, not paragraphs promising hypothetical panel designs. It need not prove a product is ready for a real contractor to fabricate without selected materials, actual site geometry, testing and detailed delivery specifications.
- A **failed study can close** by rejecting a variant with legible evidence and offering a simpler alternative, but cannot call the owner's desire implemented if no convincing workable family survives. Escalate an architectural rebrief; do not force a system through fire, moisture, acoustic or access constraints to preserve the initial hope.
- Owner approval accepts **aesthetic, spatial and preference decisions**, not physical or regulatory adequacy. Specialist/physical evidence remains an explicit later route. Neither EPR professional sign-off nor a real site becomes a default prerequisite for publishing an honestly bounded design-research book.
- The completed IAS-03 and HAD-02M records remain dated study results; new selected authority belongs to the registered successor(s), `TRIAD-GATE` and ultimately HRS, never to an ad hoc hand-edited history.
- [#1516](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/1516) records the owner's direction and registration scope. The canonical graph makes the concept a prerequisite of TRIAD, the approved details a prerequisite of BCR convergence and bay design, and the final owner decision a prerequisite of plate freeze.
