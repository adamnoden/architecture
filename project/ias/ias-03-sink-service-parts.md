## Located access before lowering

The following is an architectural consequence of the **existing conditional cabinet**, not a new manufacturer layout. [The predecessor's quantity record](studies/sink-renewal.json) locates the authored outer rim at x8835–9365/y11046–11486, within the 800 × 600 module at x8700–9500/y10982.5–11582.5. The [cabinet comparison](studies/sink-cabinet.json) supplies the two 24-thick side trials. Coordinates and distances are millimetres.

| Territory | Available gross interval in the retained comparison | Task that must be located before a release claim |
|---|---|---|
| West side | Inside side face x8724 to outer rim x8835: **111** | Fixing approach and released clip/strip path; expose any side-access or appliance-removal dependency, including prior isolation/disconnection. |
| East side | Outer rim x9365 to inside side face x9476: **111** | Approach without interference from the side, retained support or adjacent storage. |
| Front | Counter edge y10982.5 to outer rim y11046: **63.5** | Front-face/rail state, hand/tool approach and the rim-seal release path. This strip is not a clear aperture. |
| Rear | Outer rim y11486 to counter back y11582.5: **96.5** | Approach around the actual tap, overflow, tails, support and back closure, while the bowl is still retained. |

The sink body blocks direct access through its footprint while installed. An open front and the previous 752 gross width do not demonstrate reach to the rear fixings or a complete perimeter joint. Likewise, having room for a screw head does not establish the driver's alignment, hand clearance, released clip movement or access to a failed anchor.

Develop two different service states. **Ordinary waste/plug/trap maintenance** must work with the bowl supported in place. **Bowl renewal** additionally needs an independent temporary support, isolation and disconnection, full fixing and seal release, then sufficient lowering and a proven front swept path. Neither state may assume that extracting the adjacent dishwasher is the first action needed to reach its own isolations or connections. The parent maintenance field remains temporary working territory, not a demonstrated handling arrangement.

A useful falsifying detail is now precise: if a rear fixing can be released only after the bowl moves, but that fixing retains the bowl, the proposed sequence fails. A removable front does not resolve that dependency. Another is a replaceable clip whose failed captive anchor cannot be renewed without consuming a supposedly retained worktop. Show a different maintainable mounting assembly or explicitly record that larger replacement boundary; do not call either a local dry release without evidence.
