# IAS — reject the furnishing workaround; compare the whole renewable wet boundary

**6 November 2026 · bounded development decision; no selected installation.** This supersedes the preferred next-step direction in the [modular furnishing handoff](ias-03-modular-furnishing-handoff.md) and the provisional-baseline wording in the [access/cold-boundary comparison](ias-03-access-and-cold-joint-comparison-wip.md). ## Decision and reason

**Reject the particular 48-package bespoke furnishing contract as the preferred way to enable rare wet repair. Develop the existing BetteUltra 5940 / B50-3064 / BetteDown B594 conventional control before extending that workaround or the all-welded pan.** Preserve the 1000 mm shower function and the two ordinary 1900 mm seat footprints as comparison controls. Neither a bought sofa's inseparability nor its exact construction is an owner requirement. This decision does not reject other modular furnishings, the Pecolift family or the correctly configured BoSS tower.

The rejected furnishing contract creates 48 outbound and 48 returning loaded carries. At the retained 18.33 m one-way path, loaded travel is about 1.76 km; empty returns between those 96 trips add about 1.74 km, hence about 3.5 km before the helper's door travel, tools, disassembly, restraint, storage and restoration. Exact endpoint accounting changes the result by one route length, not the decision. These are event distances, not elapsed hours, a human lifting rating or an owner labour budget. No renewal frequency or contractor price is known.

The eight thin sideframes, twelve rails, eight seat halves, sixteen pads and four table packages also require a new structural furniture design, full upholstery/material/fire evidence and a 1430 mm-high preliminary storage stack whose placement, stability and retrieval are unproved. Their supported first release is unearned. The conditional carrying path resolves none of those duties and still does not admit the 196 kg lift, its actual rolling axle/gate-end method through the 755 mm protected doorway, or the repair event. More first-release choreography would improve a prototype whose whole-house benefit remains undemonstrated. That is disproportionate development at this stage; no absolute labour-cost threshold is invented.

Retain [the complete-current carrying control](studies/furnishing-carry-control.py) as a conditional geometric result. It includes all 38 furniture records, fixed fixtures, stair, ground walls and the entire court/weather enclosure. It is not a disposal instruction, selected furnishing or physical handling method. The withdrawn PR #1335 podium result remains withdrawn.

### First-release aperture check: reject simple upward whole-waste exchange

The B594 drawing's **120 mm lower flange does not pass axially upward through the retained receptor's 90 mm drain opening**. Its nominal radial excess is `(120−90)/2 = 15 mm`. Even the narrower 89 mm body has only `(90−89)/2 = 0.5 mm` nominal radial clearance before shape, seals, lugs and tolerances. Removing the top fixing flange does not remove the lower body's larger flange. Do not credit deformation, splitting the body, an enlarged enamel hole or an altered supplied product. This rejects the simple coaxial whole-body extraction, not routine bell cleaning or every possible replacement method.

The manufacturer's [regional 6965 product page](https://www.viega.sg/en/products/Catalogue/Drainage-technology/Drains-for-bathtubs-and-shower-trays/Tempoplex-series-from-2026/Tempoplex-Drain-6965.html) links its [full instructions](https://pspstmsimanuals.blob.core.windows.net/pub-production/Manuals/32925071328827d0ac114094703acec1_en_SG.pdf), INT/2021-08/VPN210065, recovered 6 November 2026. Printed pages 8, 10–12 were visually inspected. The 6965 drawing has the same named 81/137/89/120 mm dimensions as the B594 comparison. It requires an opening greater than 120 mm, assembly of the seal/body below the tray, evenly tightened top screws, a tension-free downstream connection and a visible leak check. Its maintenance instructions remove the bell above; they provide no whole-body reverse-release or support method. This corroborates topology, **not exact B594/OEM supplied identity, constituent equivalence or UK acceptance**. Do not import the older 6962 model or unrelated safety-locking screws as if fitted here.

**Consequence for this actual comparison:** whole-body exchange needs either a deliberately supported lower/side extraction with the tray retained, or release/lift of the actual tray/frame followed by access from above. A greater-than-120 mm floor core alone does not create that route through the 90 mm receptor. The existing 800 mm ceiling hatch supplies a potential lower access boundary; its real worker/platform/support/door/furniture event is still unearned. Alternatively, lifting the tray/frame is a larger, less frequent renewal boundary whose water junction, independent body/branch capture, first rest and return must be earned. Neither is called ordinary cleaning or a free above-only method. Keep the fixed branch/floor closure independent where an actual joined detail supports it; no conventional cold joint automatically achieves that separation.

### Cold lower access has a different equipment question

Do not transfer the all-welded retirement's Pecolift, guarded hot-work envelope or modular tower to a light cold body/socket task without assessing that actual task. A native smaller control exists: ZARGES Seventec S **40336**, in its [manufacturer-hosted 2025 catalogue](https://epdf.zarges.com/de/zarges-hauptkatalog-2025/epaper/Zarges-Hauptkatalog.pdf), printed page 77. The row lists 1350 mm platform height, 1970 mm vertical ladder height, 520 mm lower outer width and 9 kg; the family has a 380 × 260 mm platform, rigid spreader connection and 150 kg stated capacity. Those are catalogue facts, not supplied-unit dimensions, an operator rating or a complete room method. No stabiliser or projecting part may be omitted from its eventual gross bill.

The current living furniture leaves 687.5 mm between the west sofa's east edge at x1650 and the other sofa's west edge at x2337.5. A 520 mm width plus two authored 25 mm reserves totals 570 mm, leaving 117.5 mm across that interval. This is a **one-axis necessary screen only**: it supplies no deployed length, platform origin, longitudinal placement, climb approach or simultaneous helper. The manufacturer catalogue does not resolve those fields, and the historical distributor-hosted sheet's 510 mm base versus 520 mm outer width is not silently reconciled into an actual footprint. Both sofas, their tables, the rear stack, radiator, fixed fixtures and room enclosure remain present unless an explicit complete movement is earned.

At that nominal deck height the retained 1750 mm standing-head reference reaches 3100 mm, 70 mm below the current RC underside; it necessarily enters the ceiling opening above 2575.375 mm. The 1450 mm shoulder reference reaches 2800 mm. These heights make a small-platform cold task worth testing, but an actual socket/grip/tool ordinate has not yet been joined to that stance. The native 137 mm waste extent is measured from its flange datum, not RC underside; the whole 200 mm slab, actual larger core, retained closure and socket/support may put the grip inside a restricted bore rather than freely below the slab. Neither a nominal reach radius nor the 800 mm ceiling opening provides hand access through that bore. Do not turn a marketing working height into reach, move the worker through retained ceiling, or price the lower event as zero work. The old 300 mm-square foot-contact control does **not** fit the 260 mm deck depth. A different actual foot arrangement and supported working posture must be established openly for this equipment; neither a narrower clothed person nor an arbitrary overlap is credited. This limitation is not proof that ordinary stepladders are unusable.

[HSE's stepladder guidance](https://www.hse.gov.uk/work-at-height/ladders/types-of-ladder.htm), checked 6 November 2026, permits brief light two-hand work with both feet and the body supported, while retaining a handhold before/after. It requires level feet, engaged locks, no overreach and control of side loading; side-on work needs justification. Cold-joint adhesion, release force, body capture, time and available body support are therefore actual admission questions. The worker must not become the support for the waste, pipe or tray. A helper and independent positive support remain charged where the sequence requires them.

## What the comparative result earns

The conventional control deletes **the specified field-welded sectional-pan construction**, not all construction work: it has no authored 2.33 m pan field/upstand/curb seams and no required cut/reweld diaphragm as an intrinsic drain connector. It does not automatically delete lower access, temporary equipment, the floor seal or eventual pipe work. The all-welded reference's approximately 3.40 m site seams remain a declared fabrication/retirement cost; an isolated PC068 saves only about 0.38 m and leaves approximately 3.02 m, while its independent renewal is still unearned. RS100's rejected raised arrangement adds about 167 mm daily edge rise/184 mm curb and an unjoined insulation/access/material burden. Neither is preferred here.

The finite conventional control has actual manufactured mating topology and no invented secondary walking deck. It is the **preferred next development comparison**, because its ordinary top service and distinct drain/tray renewal can be tested without first designing 48 bespoke furniture packages. Its full installed depth, water junction/support, native body retention/socket release, material disposition, incoming tray/frame route and rare lower repair method still prevent selection. The exact Pecolift static reference and one rejected direct rolling turn remain limited evidence; the mandatory 4 SP7 BoSS control remains unearned deployment, not a universal failure. Hot work may remain proportionate for a genuinely rare fixed-branch/floor retirement; it is a burden to price and qualify, not an absolute ban.

The decision improves the owner's ordinary house by declining an access-driven furniture redesign before it has earned a coherent wet family. It is a completed **negative/comparative investigation**, not completion of IAS-03. IS-01–06, the actual current occurrences, the whole selected-family drawings/disassembly and TRIAD join remain open. A named evidence owner cannot turn an unfitted whole boundary into an adoption pass.
