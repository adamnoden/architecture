## Three distinct maintenance events

**Routine top service.** Isolate affected use; lift the retained-in-service cover using its actual slots, then lift the bell65, clean/inspect the accessible body and straight outlet, renew the small bell/cover if needed, restore guides/stop and water seal, then test/restore use. No lower ceiling, diaphragm, pipe weld, support collar or floor boundary opens. Grip force, hot-water safety, drop prevention, cleanliness and repeatable seating remain a physical test. Bare-metal noise, dirty slots, sharp edges and incorrect bell replacement can defeat the benefit.

**Rare pan/body/stem retirement.** Isolate/drain and protect both occupied levels; move the actual furniture and erect a qualified platform. Open the existing800 hatch.
