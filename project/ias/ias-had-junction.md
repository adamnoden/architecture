# IAS ↔ HAD — interior expression junction

**Canonical node:** IAS-HAD-JUNCTION (IAS-02H / HAD-02I aliases).
**Scope:** reconcile candidate interior mechanics with HAD's actual architectural
directions, not final material selection or the OAS junction.

## Shared decision

Retain HAD P-A/S-A as the ordinary-room control: direct quiet wall fields,
continuous conventional floors, true useful joinery and selective access.
V0/V1/H0 supply the compatible interior candidates. V3 and HAD S-B may share
a coherent dry service front. V2/P-B remain local challengers with their depth
cost visible; they are not selected around every room.

No dimension is frozen on the assumption that OAS will later shrink its frame,
and no decorative field takes over an independent water/air/fire/acoustic duty.

## Head-to-ceiling fit

The nominal aperture head is 2850 mm below a 3100 mm ceiling: 250 mm available
in elevation. HAD's trial 100 mm cornice downstand and 75 mm trim above the
aperture leave **75 mm visual separation**. That does not allocate depth,
movement, frame fixing or product tolerance. Keep the independent room-side
terminations provisional at OAS until those actual dimensions are known.

If they cannot fit, the agreed response is simpler upper termination/trim,
then an explicit source rewind if necessary. Do not squash a required movement
zone or quietly lower an aperture. Existing PHY-04 remains the wall/ceiling
comparison if its applicability is confirmed; no physical result is claimed.

## Release versus appearance

The local face must remain releasable after normal furnishing and decoration.
Use accessible ordinary mechanical capture with local short return/end pieces.
No filler/paint bond crosses the release; no hidden interlocked corner makes
one room depend on dismantling another. A real joinery stile may cover a joint
only when that stile is itself reachable and independently releasable.

Visible brass/bronze is optional at touch, wear, indexing or a functional cover.
It does not mark every removable seam. No false panel outline implies an
access function that the back construction does not provide.

Do not support a safety-critical object or fixture on a removable face merely
to preserve an uninterrupted wall image. Structural support remains its own
system, with technical evidence routed through BCR.

## Furniture, working width and local controls

HAD's source desk/bed/route relationships remain held. Panel/tool release fields
must be checked after actual cabinetry and furnishings, not in an empty room.
The west-gallery floor-level projections are 979 mm with two ordinary 18 mm
skirtings and 925 mm with two 45 mm service trials; the nominal 1015 mm wall-face
clearance must not be reported as the latter finished-floor width.

Door-side controls and task lighting are coordinated with the actual room
and service rise, not universally embedded in every architrave. Backboxes,
enclosures and fittings remain independently supported and serviceable.
No fitting positions, illumination levels or circuit design are selected here.

## Residual boundaries

- OAS still owns frame/reveal/architrave overlap, release order and thresholds.
- IAS-03 still owns chosen assembly depths, actual tasks, adopted reservation
  changes and the canonical detailed/disassembly set.
- HAD-02M/03 still own final material and visual selection after OAS-HAD.
- Small-en-suite fit, I00-C1, lining access section and wet depth deficits remain
  explicit adoption obligations; this junction does not assert they are solved.
- MRA/BCR test the integrated selected-house consequences and route actual evidence.

## Result

The IAS/HAD candidates now agree on ordinary wall/floor controls, local joinery,
projection costs, trim/release priorities and the response when depth fails.
No selected-family, material, opening or performance claim changes. This is a
completed candidate junction reconciliation with named residual owners, not
TRIAD-GATE or construction approval.
