# IAS-03 — kitchen sink cabinet: material reduction and maintenance boundary

**30 October 2026 · conditional design comparison · IAS-03 remains planned.** Starting main: `04de5a6b46523a43d10d581dd736d7c0322c7de6`. This narrows IS-04 / IW-04; it does not discharge the kitchen, utility and all-wet-component obligation. The [programme](interior-architecture-system-programme.md), [selected-family adoption limits](../../docs/reference-house/interior-family-selection.md), [material policy](../../docs/reference-house/material-policy.md) and later [structural brief](../../docs/reference-house/structural-brief.md) govern.

## Result that changes the design

The previously drawn Fragranite sink is **not an admissible default under the new material policy**. A plain stainless bowl fits within its plan envelope, but changing the bowl does not establish a compliant or maintainable installation. The manufacturer's conventional single-bowl siphon is positively identified as polypropylene; its undermount guidance uses silicone; a plausible metal trap still lacks recovered connection and removal dimensions. A dimensioned brass isolation comparator also discloses EPDM seals. These are actual product findings, not an assumption that every metal-labelled part contains no plastic.

Retain **hinged cabinet access with independently supported services, no fixed centre mullion and no fixed shelf through the service task** as the arrangement to develop. Reject isolation or detachable connections whose only access is behind the dishwasher: they make withdrawal depend on a disconnection that cannot yet be reached. Do not place a convincing generic trap inside the cabinet to conceal the missing connected assembly.

The [A3 plan/section](studies/sink-cabinet.svg) and [quantity record](studies/sink-cabinet.json) expose the remaining territory. They are a cabinet and mounting comparison, **not a product installation drawing**. No sink, valve, trap, appliance, worktop, material exception or house geometry is selected here.

## Cabinet proposition and two numerical traps

**Propose a locally openable joinery cabinet**, supported independently of the wall lining, plumbing and any removable face. The study authors24 side members, a30 worktop comparison and base-top180. These are trial allocations, not a selected timber species/joint, verified worktop span or a known floor assembly. Avoid adhesive-bound sheet material as an assumed default. A mechanically joined solid-timber candidate still needs its actual moisture movement, fixings, finish and load design.

First, **800 nominal is not800 clear**. Subtracting only the two24 side trials leaves **752 gross**. Hinges, stops, any face frame and the actual opening angle reduce or alter the release envelope further. The record therefore keeps actual clear opening unknown. Two hinged faces should have no fixed central member across the service task; the sink/worktop cannot depend on that omitted member without another verified support arrangement.

Second, a shallower bowl does not automatically free the same space below it. For an intentionally simplified comparison, align the historical200-deep inset bowl's nominal rim with top900: its bottom is700. The175-deep undermount bowl, with its trial rim below a30 worktop at870, ends at **695**, five lower. Thus the nominal25 depth reduction does not earn25 more plumbing space. Real rim, seal, clip, slope and waste-body datums are absent; **neither700 nor695 is an accepted installed underside**. The515 gross interval from the trial695 bottom to base180 is unallocated territory, not trap capacity or hand clearance.

Do not introduce a full-depth drawer or fixed shelf through that unresolved territory. A removable shelf may return only after the connected assembly shows its first release and staged withdrawal. The current diagram deliberately leaves the service region hatched and unspecified.

## Maintenance sequence that the actual assembly must support

The [Bosch SMV2ITX18G instructions, 9001937254_A, §19.1, p47](https://media3.bosch-home.com/Documents/9001937254_A.pdf) put power/water isolation and drainage/supply disconnection before appliance withdrawal. The same manual requires accessible electrical disconnection, protects the supplied hose and identifies the AquaStop housing as plastic. These duties and that material residue survive a timber door or metal sink; do not alter the appliance or delete its protection to claim compliance with the owner's material requirement.

Use that sequence as a **layout obligation**. Put the relevant electrical safe-isolation point in a suitable accessible dry location and the water isolation and detachable appliance connections in a reachable adjacent service position. Their actual position, segregation, support and applicable electrical/plumbing duties require design; this study does not locate a socket under a leaking trap.

| State | Required physical action | What the present study establishes |
|---|---|---|

| Trap cleaning | Control water use, collect residual water, reach the actual cup/cleaning point and support connected pipework | The metal candidate has replaceable parts, but its lowering/rotation clearance is unknown |
| Complete trap exchange | Release the actual inlet/outlet, account for insertion/axial travel, and remove through the true front opening | No positive removal result; a bottle fitting inside a box would not prove it |
| Dishwasher replacement | Make safe and disconnect first; release appliance fixings/plinth, then move onto the cleared temporary field without snagging services | The rear-only connection arrangement is rejected. Actual terminal sizes, hose paths and protected cabinet pass-through remain unproved |
| Cabinet/pipe drying | Expose the first-wetted surfaces and every affected joint without leaving a concealed wet pocket | Requires the real cabinet constitution, service positions and water paths |

The dishwasher's internally pumped wastewater cannot be connected to an arbitrary sink-trap point merely by adding a drawn tee. Retain the actual manufacturer's branch/clamp/height arrangement and verify backflow/combined discharge duties. No matched metal replacement for the conventional single-bowl/appliance kit was established in this investigation.

## First water, visible declaration and drying

The source geometry does not yet establish that any credible leak first lands on a safely replaceable surface. Treat the following as distinct cases: sink flange/overflow leaks; tap-tail or supply-union leaks; trap/waste leaks; dishwasher hose/connection leaks; and supply failure upstream of local isolation. The actual wetting path can run along a pipe, underside or cabinet member before dropping. A pan drawn below the trap does not intercept them all.

Compare a **small independently removable metal witness surface** only after those actual paths are known. It would need a visible, cleanable declaration point, continuous inspected corners, no trapped service penetrations and a removal path that does not require dismantling the sink. No hidden storage volume, emergency drain or whole-kitchen flood-protection credit is assigned. If it hides leakage, wets supporting timber or obstructs service, omit/redesign it. A visible drip on the room side can itself cause damage or slipping; declaration is not safe disposal and ordinary vigilance is not a safety barrier.

The ground-floor300 reserve is still unresolved. No selected mineral floor, waterproofing boundary, fall or harmless overflow route follows from this cabinet study. Permanent masonry must not be chased and the floor must not be drilled to rescue a poorly coordinated fitting. The utility and other wet components still need their own located cases; MRA's later integrated assault remains separate.

## Precise next input and closeout limit

The next useful design input is a **complete manufacturer-bound sink mounting/waste/overflow, kitchen trap, isolation and appliance-branch set**, with constituent declarations, installed coordinates, tool access and release travel. MAT-INTERIORS and MAT-SERVICES own the material alternatives/residues with IAS; HAD owns whether OptionB's sill and kitchen trade-off is adopted; STR/BCR own support interfaces and later evidence duties. Actual performance evidence remains downstream where appropriate, but missing geometry cannot be passed as a technical afterthought.

This packet rejects two demonstrable polymer defaults, retains a plain-metal bowl direction, exposes the mounting-datum and access-width counterexamples, and specifies the required order of maintenance. It does **not** select a complete assembly, close IS-04, alter the house source or complete IAS-03. No owner exception has been proposed as already approved. The remaining unknowns are sufficient to prevent further honest connected-fit detail with the recovered evidence.
