# A gauged thin lining in the Study dry field

Develop a small trial on the south pier. Keep the existing mineral plaster at the protected Study door, L-return and both window reveals. The current-design room-kit compiler produces the [section](studies/crafted-study-wall-transition.svg) and [receipt](studies/crafted-study-wall-transition.json) from the same fitted Study geometry. The result binds the G1 enclosure, OAS window section and IAS door comparison. The existing materials and installation holds still apply.

## Keep the air plane independent of the rail

Use a dry specimen at **X2675–3125 / Z1280–1730**, between the two raw jambs at X2600/3400. Test two wall-side hard lands at X2700/3100 and Z1700. Support the cleat on fixed metal lands that carry its forces through to the actual structural wall. Do not rely on board bearing, a flexible sheet or mortar packing of unknown strength. A 10 kg complete trial gives **98.1 N** vertical, **49.05 N** at either land only if reactions are equal and **2.649 N·m** about the core face when the entire weight is charged at 27 mm. If one land loses contact, the full vertical action reaches the other. The numbers size no anchor or anti-lift stop. Identify solid substrate, local reinforcement, edge distances, anchor shear/tension and failed-retainer behaviour before accepting the rail.

The continuous trial air plane is 0.5 mm formed metal with a fixed, sealed collar around each structural penetration. Shop or site sequence must leave access to verify both sides of the collar before it is concealed. Extend the fixed plane **30 mm** behind the retained 27 mm lime return within the 50 mm jamb strip. This is a geometrical overlap. It needs an actual sealed, movement-compatible bond at the plaster and the permanent OAS air line. An unsealed lap or a screw through the plane with only a face washer fails the air-path concept. Compare the fixed no-panel case with the installed and removed cases, then repeat after representative movement. Continuous closure at the head, floor and opening returns requires its own evidence. Do not move the permanent window seal, support or drainage to the removable trim. Keep the old 250 mm half-head withdrawal and its 2 mm pocket reserve apparent in the adjoining detail.

## Gauge the face without altering the backing

A maximum stack is **26.35 mm**. It comprises 0.5 mm fixed air plane and 0.5 mm correction, 6.5 mm fixed board at its upper thickness, 6.35 mm gravity cleat, 2 mm fitting space and **10.5 mm maximum complete moving cassette**. Gauge the assembled depth locally. Add captive non-compressible packs at the hard lands on the dry side of the sealed plane, in 0.10 mm increments. The pack does not add another liner, trim the air plane or use another penetration. A 26.35 mm reading takes a **0.50 mm** pack. With a combined gauge and set uncertainty of **±0.15 mm**, the possible face is **26.70–27.00 mm** from the core. This allows shallow recess but no modeled proud face. The same procedure can accommodate a 25.35 mm measured stack using the 1.50 mm packing limit. The uncertainty and lowest/highest pack are trial process requirements. Measure at the two upper hard lands and the lower locators. Verify all four corners and the span between them against a datum spanning the adjoining plaster. A bowed or twisting face can fail between flush supports. The lower locators resist tilt without carrying gravity by assumption. Reject an actual surface needing more than 0.5 mm plane correction, a stack outside the usable packing interval, or a finished face that projects. Do not plane the structural wall or pierce the air plane to obtain a flush appearance.

Keep the existing 30 mm head pocket, 25 mm after setting, under the independently supported Z3100 ceiling. Take a short cornice fillet off its wall-side carrier before lifting. Release each corner horizontally after its own vertical travel with no shared mitre across adjacent modules. The illustrated 10 mm vertical trial takes the chosen module to Z1740, **158 mm below** the fixed G1 keep-out. Raising it by 200 mm makes it intersect G1 and is rejected. The 10 mm envelope checks only clip disengagement and grille geometry. Finger, stop and first-rest geometry still require a complete motion study.

This is an ordinary dry-wall trial. Retain fixed wet-side lining, waterproofing, upstands and threshold support. Stop this cassette before the wet return and keep all wet closure outside the decorative release operation. Keep independently supported skirting and its services separate. In the protected reveal retain both present 120 mm removable ends and their 22 mm south draw with the door latched. This trial adds no skirting depth and grants no clearance to the larger service skirting in the pinch. Continue to use the existing plaster control wherever the field, movement or materials fail.
