## One cold pipe joint: actual Fernco PC068, not a polymer-free union

[Fernco's own product page](https://www.fernco.de/en/product/drain-coupling) identifies EPDM sleeve and1.4301/304 clamping bands, for drainage without shear loading. Its [DC/PC data sheet](https://www.fernco.de/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Fernco_DC-PC_Data-Sheet-1.pdf), SHA256`347c5d1efe9f867ce1db94bea2065d0b87a33d2cae529cc84fb6f86750b58660`, lists **PC68/PC068:60–68 pipe OD,80 axial width,95 external dimension,6Nm,0.6bar,0.2kg net**. The authored60.33 pipe is inside that diameter range; material-specific use and delivered dimensions still need binding. The0.2kg is the **whole coupling**, not EPDM mass, a chemical bill or a harmless-residue finding. The wetted EPDM body is functional installed polymer, not trace contamination. Exact compound/additives, rubber quantity, ageing/temperature/cleaning exposure, change criteria and applicable supplied certification remain unknown. No concealed-parts waiver or approved owner exception exists; [MAT policy](../mat/material-water-direction-result.md) still binds.

Do not substitute this non-shear coupling for structural support. In any candidate, the retained collar supports the upper module, with separately qualified lower pipe support and temporary positive capture on both sides before release. The coupling cannot carry pan weight, pipe misalignment, accidental axial withdrawal or a handler's reaction. Actual support/anchor capacities and access are not furnished by the range table.

### Unchanged repair datum does not earn a renewable connection

The standing-bell reference has diaphragm bottom2947/top2950, original pipe repair plane2910 and proposed elbow straight-end2858.075. Centre an80-long coupling at2910 and it reaches2950: its larger outer envelope interferes with the diaphragm's60.33 stem opening. Moving the prospective joint centre to2903 makes top2943/bottom2863, leaving only **4mm below the diaphragm and4.925mm above the elbow** before tolerances, band/socket and hand access. This is authored nominal arithmetic, not a matched installation or an instruction to retain these failed service datums.

Even an installed envelope is not a replacement sequence. The80 sleeve cannot simply be credited as sliding fully onto the short exposed lower straight, and the diaphragm blocks its larger envelope from travelling above it. No sourced slit-and-reclose, side-wrap, arbitrary stretching or repeat-use method is supplied. One possible *pan-retirement* construction state has the diaphragm open and upper module removed, so a new sleeve can be introduced before closing that boundary; that does not prove independent coupling-only renewal with the pan/floor boundary retained. A coherent cold design must deliberately provide accessible engagement, supported release and a renewable seal path; it may replan the branch datum rather than force this accessory into the all-welded layout. No such replan is adopted here.

### Whole-boundary cost decides, not the accessory count

Routine bell/cover cleaning already takes place from above without either coupling or lower hatch. A cold pipe joint would replace the two pipe welds (about0.38m) during rare retirement. It leaves about2.33m pan field/upstand/curb seams and0.69m welded floor diaphragm: **about3.02m site seams remain**, including below-floor work. It therefore does **not** remove the access platform, occupied-house hot-work protection or reinstated fire/air/acoustic closure. The reference's3.40m total remains a real cost, not an absolute prohibition; skilled time, protection, inspection, downtime and expected renewal events have not been priced.

**Decision:** do not select PC068 as an isolated improvement to this unchanged wet module. It adds a permanent functional polymer and a new seal-renewal duty while neither its independent replacement nor the claimed access/hot-work deletion is earned. This is a rejection of that isolated placement, not of conventional cold joints or all permitted residual material. Keep the all-welded reference unselected too. The next useful comparison is a **whole renewable wet boundary**: distinguish ordinary top service, replaceable cold seals and eventual pan/body retirement, give both directions actual incoming/replacement/support/protection and lifecycle costs, and obtain explicit MAT/owner disposition for any actual residual. A credible modular furnishing or temporary furniture export may simplify rare access, but must carry its real parts, mass, route, storage and restoration against the complete scene. No additional catalogue family, false geometric pass or IAS completion follows from this checkpoint.
