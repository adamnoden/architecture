Dimensions below are **gross concept-zone dimensions** read from the rough rectangles. They are not clear finished room sizes and do not deduct wall build-up.

## A — Narrow / Familiar

Representative ground zones:

```text
drawing        ~3.2 × 4.4 m
study          ~2.4 × 3.8 m
family         ~3.8 × 7.4 m gross zone
rear dining    ~3.4 × 4.0 m
kitchen        ~3.8 × 4.0 m
court           3.4 × 3.4 m
```

Opening concept:
- principal entrance into front hall/stair;
- major garden opening from rear dining zone;
- secondary garden openings to family/kitchen where façade composition permits;
- major court connection on front/south and rear/north court faces;
- courtyard side windows from occupied flanks.

Replacement-path concept:

`technical side passage → rear staging → wide garden/dining opening → rear court opening → courtyard`

This deliberately keeps the route concept similar to B4 so the scheme tests footprint change rather than a wholesale logistics rewrite.

## B — Narrow + Deep / Rebalanced

Representative ground zones:

```text
drawing        ~3.5 × 3.8 m
study          ~3.5 × 3.8 m
family         ~3.65 × 4.5 m
rear dining    ~3.2 × 4.5 m
kitchen        ~3.65 × 4.5 m
court           3.2 × 3.6 m
```

Opening concept:
- centred principal entrance into an axial entry hall;
- visual/physical continuation from entry toward the court;
- major rear opening from dining to garden;
- secondary family/kitchen garden openings subject to façade resolution;
- court openings on entry/dining axis;
- technical-side gallery and wet/service openings remain subordinate.

Replacement-path concept:

`2.5 m technical side → rear staging → dining/garden opening → court north opening`

The 2.5 m flank is intentionally kept substantially clearer than B4; the concept should not spend the recovered width immediately on external equipment.

## C — Court-led

Representative ground zones:

```text
drawing        ~3.7 × 3.6 m
study          ~3.7 × 3.6 m
family         ~3.8 × 4.1 m
rear dining    ~3.6 × 4.1 m
kitchen        ~3.8 × 4.1 m

```

Opening concept:
- centred entrance on a deliberate front → hall → court → dining → garden axis;
- large controlled openings on the north/south court faces make the court part of the sequence rather than a residual void;
- family/kitchen retain independent garden light/openings;
- court side windows serve the flanking rooms/gallery.

Replacement-path concept:

`technical side → rear staging → dining opening → full-width court opening`

This concept gives the clearest replacement path through the domestic axis, but detailed resolution must ensure it does not turn the dining room into a permanent service corridor.

## D — Service-spine

Representative ground zones:

```text
drawing        ~3.4 × 4.1 m
study          ~4.0 × 3.4 m
family         ~3.4 × 7.9 m gross longitudinal zone
rear dining    ~3.4 × 4.4 m
kitchen        ~4.0 × 4.4 m
service spine  ~2.6 × 4.2 m gross zone
court           3.4 × 3.5 m
```

The long family zone is intentionally **not** accepted as a resolved room. If D is selected, RSI-05B must prove whether it becomes one good long room, two connected territories, or a revised partition arrangement.

Opening concept:
- principal entrance remains front-centre;
- garden dining/kitchen openings remain substantial;
- court front/rear connections remain major;
- service-spine external access is biased toward rear service staging rather than opening equipment directly into the side passage.

Replacement-path concept:

`clear technical side → rear technical staging → service/gallery or garden route → courtyard`

The defining experiment is that the outside flank is treated principally as **access territory**, while plant/meters/wet infrastructure are packed into the internal/rear technical geography.

## Control / B4

The current B4 opening arrangement remains the source-traced control, including:
- front entrance;
- widened 1.8 m garden door;
- current courtyard openings;
- study/service side windows;
- external plant/service-side access inherited from the current coordination.

Its RSI-03 weakness is not absence of openings. It is that a 1.75 m technical flank must plausibly serve route, technical and maintenance duties simultaneously.

## Gate boundary

They do not author:
- jamb/reveal/threshold construction;
- exact door swings;
- final sash/bay rhythm;
- furniture layouts;
- equipment dimensions;
- clear finished room sizes;
- structural openings;
- fire/escape compliance.

Those become due only after OWNER GATE A selects or combines a concept.
