## Question and owner direction

On 10 November 2026 the owner approved separating two useful investigations from
whole-house integration, then executing them. [Issue #1474](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/1474)
records the direction and registration boundary. At main
`a06013820dfdc2b5ee1918abf42abce8423eb0ad`, 125 of 158 non-cancelled packets were

they do not permit concurrent changes to another worker's selected design.

checks. Unresolved roof reactions, site access, product or physical performance
remain named limits. A defensible adverse result can complete the bounded
investigation without passing the affected assembly.
