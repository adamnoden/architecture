**Owner direction:** 15 November 2026, [#1539](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/1539) and [#1542](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/1542).

This result registers the engineering contract and migration plan. The eight CCR packets and the ADE browser packet each require their own implementation evidence.

## Changes

- Require authoritative parameters, reusable computation, explicit dependencies and mutation/invalidation tests during engineering work, not only deterministic reproduction afterward.
- Define the serialisable semantic model and edit/result boundary needed by a future browser house editor; Node, CLI, browser and specialist-runner adapters must consume the same model.
- Register early current-source/current-compile work and parallel structural, service and assembly integration, followed by headless edits, cross-output change proof and final current-source release.
- Move reusable ADE-MODEL implementation ahead of selected-house technical acceptance; preserve that acceptance on final detail/schedule/evidence outputs.
- Register the browser client after change proof and outside the publication prerequisites.
- Require final current-model release before PLT-FINAL-PREP. Keep historical fixtures as explicit history and forbid silent historical fallback for current consumers.
- Preserve live engineering work, selected geometry and all actual owner approval gates. No external review or real plot is introduced as a default publishing dependency.

## Verification and authority
