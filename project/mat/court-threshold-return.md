# South court threshold: finite field return trial

The [calculation](studies/court-threshold-return.py), [coordinates](studies/court-threshold-return.json) and [schematic section](studies/court-threshold-return.svg) bound one unadopted way to bring the MAT ground-field candidate toward the selected HAD south court door. The selected rough opening is **x5100..6900**, the weather face is **y4250**, and the proposed bounding field ends at **y3987.5**. The trial must span **262.5 mm** from that field edge to the weather face. This study does not establish an actual threshold or an acceptable field.

The trial extends a 1 mm metal return across the 1,800 mm opening and **40 mm beyond each jamb**, x5060..6940, ending at y4250. A 40 mm wing is a set-out assumption, not a qualified weld or bearing width. The 1,880 × 262.5 mm horizontal blank has a gross plan area of **0.4935 m²**, before folds, welded seams, support, waste or any conflict. Wings lie within the adjacent jamb territories; placing metal there changes the actual bearing, mortar, capillary and load interface. The sheet may not simply sever or replace the structural jamb or its supporting DPC. Actual jamb, receiving structure and reinforcement must be located and designed with STR.

There is a **decisive source-datum conflict** in the unchanged combination. The merged STR lower-grid `court-S` comparator is x3950..8050, y3650..4250 and z−630..−180, so it encloses the whole trial horizontal return at −381..−380. The sheet would cut through that concrete rather than form its underside. This historical lower-grid comparator has not been reconciled with the newer HAD-aligned slab and separate cold-grid development: do not call it a selected beam or assume its bearing. An owning structural/architectural resolution must locate a continuous field below or around the actual receiver without cutting its load path; until then the L return is rejected as a combined construction.

An apparently simple local escape is to step the field under the historical receiver, placing its 1 mm sheet at **−631..−630**, with its top 250 mm below the −380 field plane. Under the separate MAT warm-grid comparator this sheet sits inside the mineral DPC band **−765..−615**, leaving nominal thicknesses 134 mm below and 15 mm above; the cut alters the bonded bed, thermal and bearing arrangement and is not an accepted DPC. More fundamentally, a wrap restricted to x5060..6940 must close along two sides that both fall **inside** the historical receiver length x3950..8050. Reconnecting those sides to the unchanged −380 field would cut the receiver. The historical `court-W` and `court-E` routes meet the ends of `court-S`, so even a full-length wrap must resolve those nodes and any other intersecting members; a short disconnected underpass is not a continuous field. These are comparisons between unreconciled source arrangements, not instructions to cut or rebuild an actual beam.

A short upstand ending at the HAD slab top **−180** would stop **180 mm below** FFL 0. It has no demonstrated connection to a door frame and therefore is an explicit negative control for a complete air/gas boundary. Extending the vertical return to **0** is a geometric alternative, not a selected sill: the 1,880 × 380 mm vertical surface is **0.7144 m²**, giving **1.2079 m²** together with the horizontal blank before folds and joints. Neither the frame position and bottom profile, court paving height, drainage falls, water path, accessible crossing nor thermal separation are defined by that endpoint. The frame could be mounted elsewhere, and an unsupported exposed sheet edge is not a sealed or replaceable frame joint. The apparent two-dimensional L must be closed at both jamb wings and connected to the face boundary and the door frame around the complete opening. Opening the sheet for fixings or a drainage route cannot be treated as sealed without a designed interface.

A possible installation sequence is to set out the actual jamb/receiver and surveyed sill first, build and support the lower return while its underside and seams remain accessible, close and inspect its jamb corners, protect it through the concrete and floor installation, then join a specifically located replaceable frame and its air/weather boundary. This is a sequence to develop, not evidence that it fits or is feasible. Concrete restraint and differential movement, drainage independent of the dry-side air/gas line, load and bearing continuity, corrosion, compatible welds, inspection, repair and frame replacement all require actual details. [OAS](../oas/oas-00-baseline-and-invariants.md) explicitly retains the frame, sill, outside level, drainage and access as unselected. No product qualification, waterproofing, airtightness, gas resistance, fire or accessibility pass follows from these coordinates.
