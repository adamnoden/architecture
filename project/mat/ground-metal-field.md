# Continuous metal ground-field direction

The [plan and datum comparison](studies/ground-metal-field.svg), [data](studies/ground-metal-field.json) and [reproducer](studies/ground-metal-field.py) develop one candidate after the SW corner's below-slab pan failed to establish a complete boundary. This is an **unadopted field topology with decisive fit failures**, not a specification, an installed DPC, a gas/water-tight assembly or an approved structural change. It pins the selected HAD scene, MAT receiving-grid comparison and merged STR corner station.

The candidate is a continuous, fully welded **1 mm bare-metal sheet at −383..−382**, with a 300 × 300 × 3 mm plate at the actual SW pier **−383..−380**. The upper rod ends start at −380 and the lower rod ends terminate at −383, on opposite plate faces. A sealed butt join around the plate and field joins are design intentions: the welds, actual metal grade, compatibility, tensile force route, plate bending and compression/shear contact are unqualified. The plate lies immediately below the warm-girder soffit, so it avoids a sheet cutting the warm RC, but occupies **3 mm of the 10 mm upper mortar bed** and leaves 7 mm below. The neighbouring 1 mm field divides the bed into **7 mm below and 2 mm above**. The contact planes are real potential slip and leakage interfaces; do not credit the original continuous mortar bond or monolithic structural transfer. The full 215 mm AAC and 150 mm mineral DPC may be retained geometrically, not automatically accepted with this altered bearing.

## One whole-field control, not a completed field

A first bounding field spans x/y262.5..11737.5, the outer ordinary core's exterior faces. It excludes the courtyard core-face rectangle x/y3987.5..8012.5 and the outdoor-loggia core-face strip x4447.5..7552.5 from y8012.5 to11737.5. The latter x values come from the selected 519.5 loggia sidewall cores, not an imagined mirror of the ordinary SW wall. The plan is a single connected **bounding surface**, with **103.909 m²** gross after those rectangular exclusions, not the actual surface area, a net cut list, a warm-floor takeoff or proof that every strip is constructible. At 1 mm and an assumed 7,850 kg/m³ the sheet-only gross sensitivity is **815.7 kg**, before seams, support, wastage or openings. The real perimeter, internal grid and all intersecting walls must be set out against the actual house before using a quantity or asserting coverage.

Four interfaces falsify a claim of completion:

- **SW:** the plate/rod junction meets the −50..777 cold receiver, the exterior weather leaf, the 50 mm drained cavity and the 110 mm wall glass. The metal must not make a wet-to-warm bypass, block cavity discharge or rely on the old 600-square node. Rod, reinforcement, weld and AAC/mortar contact are a jointly designed STR/MAT task.
- **South court doorway:** the selected rough door spans x5100..6900 at weather face y4250. The bounding field edge at y3987.5 is **262.5 mm away**. That interval contains a real support/threshold/weather transition; an unconnected line on the plan is not a sealed pan, door joint or first-harm route.
- **Loggia and north portal:** the clear outdoor route and its 519.5 sidewalls have separate water, thermal and movement duties. The rectangle is a conservative geometric cut at structural-core faces, not a drawing of the door arch, cavity head, sill or their continuous sealed interfaces.
- **Services and internal support lines:** wall, pipe, cable, drain, structural and threshold crossings need actual centre, extent, sleeve, welded return, movement, access and repair. None may be silently omitted to retain an unbroken-looking sheet.

The separate [joined court/service reference](joined-ground-floor-and-court-boundary.md) creates another exact failure. Its ordinary mineral DPC field starts at −380 and a plane at −383 lies **3 mm inside that bonded DPC band**, not necessarily in the upper AAC mortar described by the other comparison. The band diagram does not locate the exact upper brick/bed interface, so the affected constituent cannot be asserted without a bond schedule. Its retained W1 carrier has crown −378 and invert −538: the proposed sheet passes through its section **5 mm below its crown**. The independent W2 tray and A/G lid at −310..−316 with seal to −318 have their own functions. A flat sheet or a generic sleeve would obstruct W1 renewal and potentially bypass W2 or A/G. A deliberately stepped field, reinforced bridge and sealed, inspectable W1/W2/A/G transition must be set out against the actual court and spine paths. These are incompatible conditional floor references, not simultaneous selected constructions; neither can be erased by assuming the other.

## One service-box crossing candidate

The [transverse wrap study](studies/ground-metal-service-wrap.svg) tests one way to keep the W1 lid accessible without cutting the ground field for ordinary maintenance. At the **1003 mm outer box faces** the field at −383 would turn down outside the RC sides, continue beneath the whole RC underside at **−839.5**, and rise outside the opposite face to rejoin the field. The **456.5 mm** vertical drop is actual geometry from the joined reference; this is a continuous fixed surface, not the removable W1 carrier or its lid. W1's −378 crown/−538 invert and A/G lid remain above it; W2 remains a separate retained tray and drainage duty.

For one **840 mm longitudinal witness segment**, the 1003 mm underside plus both 456.5 mm sides gives **1.609 m²** of metal or **12.63 kg** at the same 1 mm/7,850 kg/m³ assumptions, before end closures, seams, supports and protection. A flat sheet over the same segment would be 0.843 m² / 6.61 kg; the sides add 6.02 kg. This is not a whole-box takeoff or a discrete removable pan. The source requires real turns at the north/east routes and a complete return around every end; an open-ended trough is not a continuous gas boundary.

This geometry does **not** yet solve the crossing. The outside fold passes through the zone of the permanent RC side-piers and stepped mineral DPC, so their load-bearing bond, separate weather/capillary path and metal return must be drawn together. Concrete would be cast or placed against the sheet; support below it, protective layer and friction-separation duties must be established without inserting an undisclosed polymer. The 456.5 mm added depth changes thermal bridges, soil movement and groundwater pressure, and the underside is not automatically accessible for welding, inspection or repair. W2 telltale/discharge and its fixed collars must remain independently functional, and opening W1 must not disturb the retained field. Welded corners, longitudinal/turn connections, seal tests, material compatibility and movement still require explicit design. If those cannot be made to fit the actual piers and site, this candidate fails rather than licensing a hole or deleting a separation duty.

## Slab and hanger failure

The warm-grid comparison places its 200 mm floor RC at **−80..−280**, followed by 320 mm glass down to −600. There the field top at −382 is **102 mm below** the proposed slab soffit. A local 1 m panel with four 40 × 40 × 4 mm upper perimeter angles and four Ø8 × 102 mm hangers is a nominal reservation, not a product or anchor design. Its gross metal is **17.56 kg / 0.172 kN** self-weight, before fixings, seams, glass retention, pressure, water, work loads or factors. An equal quarter split is an arithmetic sensitivity, not evidence for four suitable anchors. The rails occupy 40 mm of the 320 mm glass locally and the hangers pass through it. Glass support, heat bridge, condensation and service clearance have not been checked.

The selected HAD scene instead records ground RC **−180..−380**. At this datum the same field has only **2 mm** clearance under the slab: a 40 mm top-side rail overlaps it by **38 mm**. The candidate therefore **fails on the unchanged selected HAD section**. It cannot claim the MAT 102 mm reservation until HAD/STR deliberately reconcile the slab and ground-floor construction. Even under the MAT comparison, the warm girder itself ends at −380, so every rail that meets it hits the same 2 mm constraint. Stop or turn rails in actual clear bays and design independent end support, welded field transitions and access at each girder; simply continuing the grid through RC or trimming the field is invalid.

## Sequence and evidence

A trial sequence is to establish the cold support and surveyed lower anchors; lay the mineral DPC/AAC and altered upper bed; fabricate and inspect the plate and its lower welds; cast the warm receiver and floor with the upper rods; install bay supports and cut/retain glass; fit and join sheets; form every perimeter, threshold and service return; then inspect and test the complete barrier before it is concealed. This sequence is not a demonstrated method: welding under a concrete slab, locating and testing seams against the girders, protecting glass, thermal movement, access to both sides, temporary support and later repair need a physical plan. The full field must tolerate its actual support and differential settlement without relying on brittle bonded mortar or an unproved sliding seal.

The UK water-industry [stainless sheet guidance](https://standards-board.water.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/IGN-4-25-02-Issue-2-August-2023-published-version-1.pdf) identifies groundwater-separation applications; the Nickel Institute's [sheet-lining guide](https://nickelinstitute.org/media/1816/stainlesssteelsheetliningofsteeltanksandpressurevesselsl_10039_.pdf) describes vacuum-box leak examination of accessible welds. These are useful evidence for an engineered metal concept, not a gas-barrier certification or an acceptance criterion for this building. Exact alloy, filler, shielding gas, cleaning, anchors, coatings, separators, sealants and temporary/retained constituents remain to be identified under the material policy. The inspection medium and its removal must be assessed too. Groundwater chemistry, pressure, ground gas, corrosion and movement remain site-dependent.

**Result:** retain this direction for further joined design, but do not call it a continuous installed barrier. The unchanged HAD slab rejects its proposed rail, the mortar/metal load route is unresolved, and threshold, weather, service, movement and repair closures are not yet drawn or proven. A complete finite assembly and internal structural/thermal challenge precede any product, site, physical or professional acceptance. No owner exception or 1,000-year performance is asserted.
