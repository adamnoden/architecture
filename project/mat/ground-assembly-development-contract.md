# Ground assembly: one development geometry

The [source-bound interface](studies/ground-assembly-contract.json), [plan](studies/ground-assembly-contract.svg), and [reproducer](studies/ground-assembly-contract.py) establish a common **development** basis for MAT and STR. This defines coordinates to challenge, not an accepted slab, foundation, material assembly or site condition. STR owns the further structural support, cage and force-route design; MAT owns the slab/envelope geometry and environmental interfaces. The selected HAD scene establishes the enclosure and RC200 levels, but **does not already author this exact ground slab polygon**. The plan here is an explicit proposed core-face footprint derived from that scene and the ordinary SW layer arrangement.

## Preferred slab control

Use ground FFL 0 and HAD RC200 **−380..−180**. Start at the ordinary outer core face q262.5, giving x/y **262.5..11737.5**. Exclude the courtyard core-face rectangle x/y **3987.5..8012.5**. Exclude the open north-loggia strip x **4447.5..7552.5**, y **8012.5..11737.5**, using the actual two 519.5 loggia sidewall core faces. Those coordinates come from the selected HAD courtyard and sidewalls plus the separately source-bound ordinary layer stack; they are not a duplicated or mirrored house definition. The proposed rectangle difference is one connected gross plan, **103.908875 m²** or **20.781775 m³** of 200 mm concrete before edges, openings, service boxes, pours or structural revisions. It is not an actual takeoff or claim that HAD selected every slab edge.

The south court door rough opening is x5100..6900. This control ends at y3987.5, whereas its weather face is y4250: **262.5 mm remains unassigned** at the door. Loggia portals, jambs, all other door approaches, external levels and service cuts likewise need actual support and threshold details. The core-face proposal must be reconciled with real walls, slab bearing, reinforcement, penetration, construction access and dimensional tolerance before it can become a buildable footprint. Its enclosure bounding line is not a gas, air, water or capillary boundary.

## Explicit SW development node

The merged STR SW trial warm pad is x/y **262.5..562.5**, z **−380..−80**. This proposed slab plan contains its full 300 × 300 mm plan. If they are deliberately cast as one monolithic geometry, the pad shares **0.018 m³** with the RC200 slab and contributes **0.009 m³** above its −180 top. The resulting 100 mm upstand ends 80 mm below FFL; that volume division excludes reinforcement, intersecting arms, service openings and replacement of other material. It does not prove a monolithic cage, shear connection, floor build-up, thermal break, pad capacity or construction sequence. The previous 230 × 230 mm first-floor projection was a different conditional footprint proxy and is not the overlap of this explicitly proposed core-face plan.

The merged STR cold node remains a **development challenger**, x/y **−50..777**, z **−1257..−765**. The two local 600 mm-wide arms run on axes (300,300) to (3150,300) and (300,3150); the later STR21 development keeps the local −1257..−765 section through axis coordinate **3450** and tests a later soffit transition at **3450..4450** as a separate candidate. The continuation beyond the local node group remains open. The earlier **2050..3050** transition is a rejected comparator because it passes through the adjacent node; it is not the preferred route. Its 600 mm width underlies the pad's 262.5..562.5 cross-section with a nominal 37.5 mm inward plan margin, but plan overlap does not establish support, restraint, insulation, drainage, thermal separation, steel anchorage or site conditions. The node's 50 mm outward projection beyond the nominal weather face is real trial geometry and requires an exterior moisture, excavation and bearing interface. Four longitudinal ties and their mineral-band penetrations remain separate open structural and envelope duties.

## What is and is not assigned

No continuous whole-house warm-receiver geometry, current court-S beam, entrance nodes or whole-house cold network is authored by this interface. STR will own and export the next finite receiver proposal against this same contract; neither party should silently extend the single SW pad or the older network into the missing regions. The older HAD/STR lower-grid **−630** and deeper **−750** node study remains a named historical comparator, not the preferred present network. The previous MAT general slab **−280..−80** is likewise a separate comparator and is not mixed into the RC200 −380..−180 proposal. The differing floor finish/glass and mineral DPC/AAC recipes still need a joined material and load-bearing design.

Threshold, roof collar, movement, box/service crossings, air/gas/water continuity, ground chemistry, maintenance and physical testing remain open. The selected scene, a gross slab polygon or a geometric pad overlap do not approve structural capacities, a product or a site. No owner material exception is implied.
