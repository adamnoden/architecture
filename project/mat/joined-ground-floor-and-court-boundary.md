# One raised mineral floor and independently renewable court boundary

**1 November 2026 · MAT-ENVELOPE joined reference design, starting main `1e7b2cbce408834ca3cbb91c286af37eda066829`.** Reference design — not released for construction. Assumptions and outstanding verification accompany the proposal; internal checks do not constitute external assurance or certification.

## Decision

**Reject the inherited 100 mm complete cover / 400 mm service box / 450 mm relative elevation package. Develop one fully allocated D1300 raised-house candidate instead.** The 1300 mm is a derived design stress case, not a site level, adopted building raise or prediction of ground movement. It requires a whole-house rise of 1150 mm relative to the accepted synthetic 150 mm difference. HAD owns the approach, whole-house height and room consequences; STR owns the actual structural candidate. None of those choices is adopted by this note.

The candidate has an RC200 ground-floor field, independently supported RC100 mineral cover and permanent wall-crossing spreaders, a retained insulated stainless secondary containment box, and a separate bolted air lid. It makes each layer's size and renewal duty visible. **This is a constructive reference assembly for further qualification; reject its technical admission today.** In particular, no source qualifies its custom rectangular gas seal or the actual wall/door transitions. No polymer exception is requested or assumed. A sealed metal body does not make an undisclosed installed package compliant.

## Why D1300, and what it buys

Use the **lowest outside surface**, −839.5, not the carrier invert or bare tray bottom. The declared stress assumptions are upward soil movement **H=300 mm**, a **separate50 mm construction/setting loss**, and **50 mm final geometric reserve** after both. These are internal design stresses, not a site prediction, a statutory heave requirement, an accepted compressible filler or adequate ventilation clearance. The construction loss is not the final reserve, and is not spent twice.

`D_required = 839.5 + 300 + 50 construction loss + 50 final reserve = 1239.5 mm`, rounded upward to the single authored **D1300** candidate. It gives460.5 mm raw underside-to-soil clearance,110.5 mm after H300 plus50 loss, and60.5 mm beyond the final50 reserve. The intermediate D1200 stress control leaves10.5 after movement/loss and therefore fails the final50 reserve by39.5 mm. Field soffit clearance is920 mm at D1300, but **the box underside controls**. Pile caps, ground beams, ducts, pipe saddles, temporary works or a thicker base can create a still lower controlling surface.

## Mineral wall base: substantial, continuous and protected

Carry the **two-course bonded DPC1 baseline**, rather than fixing four courses without evidence of benefit. Ketley WCBL65 solid Class A is the exact unit candidate. Two 65 mm units plus nominal bedding territory reserve150 mm. The field support band is **−380..−530**; local bridge seats may step to **−300..−450** only where STR's actual support requires it. Draw and bond the stepped return continuously through external inner/outer leaves, internal supports, piers and threshold jambs; do not stop it at the service box or allow a wet RC support to bypass it.

This is already substantial mineral construction. Its lowest field edge is770 mm above the authored −1300 grade, giving620 mm *geometric separation beyond an ordinary150 mm comparison*, before site water/splash and actual DPC-datum assessment. This margin comes from protected position and continuity. It does not quantify capillary reliability or a thousand-year life. Adding courses would lower the bottom edge, add mortar interfaces and alter bearings; without a demonstrated defect mechanism benefit, retain that material for better beds, fully bonded returns and inspection rather than assert a multiplier.

M12 is a performance class, not a chemical recipe. Specify an evidenced cement/lime/sand mortar with every retained additive declared and matched low-suction-brick bond/shear evidence; the existing Cemex comparator's undisclosed workability aids remain unknown. No exact mortar is approved here. WCBL65's current DoP says215×102×65 while the separate family sheet says102.5 wide; do not derive bonding or masonry dimensions from an unresolved discrepancy. Slate remains an alternative needing exact DPC-use evidence, not an extra automatically credited sheet in this stack.

Keep cavity rain drainage distinct from upward capillarity. The retained secondary tray's turned-up edge/collar must meet a qualified cavity/threshold pan outside the room boundary, while the DPC remains unpunctured. Mortar droppings, plaster, screed, roots, raised paving and fixing bridges can bypass a sound mineral DPC. A welded tray does not prove its bond to masonry, and the brick's absorption test proves neither the tray connection nor ground-gas exclusion.

## Three water/air boundaries and a real renewal sequence

**W1** is the removable carrier and its own wet joints. **W2** is the retained court pan, fixed collars and independent secondary tray, with its own separately observable gravity declaration at the exterior. **A/G** is the occupied-room air boundary and any actual site-required ground-gas boundary. They are separate duties. An open telltale drain through an otherwise sealed enclosure can create an air/gas bypass; it must terminate outside the admitted A/G boundary through a qualified fixed arrangement. A water trap, damper or nominal metal grille is not silently selected.

The constructive topology is one retained U tray with welded fixed wall collars, separate carrier saddles and a removable inner air lid above it. Its lateral fixing/support points stay on the house-supported structure. The tray's exterior declaration and courtyard pan must not share W1's detachable joints or be blocked by its removed segment. Full N/E tray independence and blockage-safe declaration require RWT's actual topology. No second tray is credited as an independent backup if it drains through the failed carrier.

The mineral cap, air lid and wet segment are **three operations**. Lift the dry cap using a rated mechanical method and protected room staging; unbolt/renew the separate lid/seal only after an actual temporary wet and gas isolation state is established; then release the bounded carrier section, with permanent masonry spreaders and W2 left in place. A joint under a fixed spreader still needs HAD's side-then-lift operation and real flange-tool envelope. Reinstate W1, test it, close/test A/G with a new seal, then replace the supported dry cap. Ground-gas requirements may defeat this maintenance state until a qualified temporary closure is provided; opening the lid is not assumed safe merely because the underfloor void is ventilated.

At a 1003×500 module and authored densities stone2600, screed2000, glass110 and RC2400 kg/m³, the dry mineral cap is about **210.6 kg** before lifting fittings. A660×500×6 mm stainless lid at8000 kg/m³ adds about15.84 kg before flange/bolts; its nominal30 mm perimeter copper ring adds about1.2 kg at8960 kg/m³, handled separately. These are mass screens, not certified masses or handling plans. The old72 kg bare600×500×100 RC estimate misses the wider supports and upper layers. Ordinary one-person lifting is rejected; the room approach, lifting machine, set-down floor capacity and replaceable rigging must fit HAD/IAS's actual maintenance geography.

## First harm and the admission decision

The first *demonstrated* error is the inherited physical cover overlap; it is not a proven water-entry elevation. The actual lowest door/frame drain, jamb, meeting stile, fixing and rear-pan junction remain unlocated. Retain the existing rejection of a5 mm comparison between −50 water and −45 sill underside. Neither the new deeper carrier nor D1300 raises a low door drain relative to court water. **No accepted lowest first-harm datum exists yet.** HAD/OAS must nominate the actual complete threshold and use the connected centre/jamb/carrier sections before RWT compares blocked-primary operating water with vulnerable fabric.

This investigation earns one spatially explicit reference candidate, a derived position with a falsifiable heave stress, complete constituent questions, a mineral-DPC recommendation and a physically separated renewal sequence. It rejects technical admission at the exact controlling interfaces: custom lid/flange seal and its installed constituents; tray-to-pan/cavity/masonry/A/G transitions; thermal bridges; STR's real cage/support/exposure; and actual door first harm. Those are parts of this design, not grounds for replacing this work with a general referral. The existing BCR/MRA/DPL/EPR/DEL routes retain final product/site/physical/competent evidence before fixing dependent technical design or construction. No whole-floor gas omission, material pass, house adoption, MAT-ENVELOPE completion or maturity increase is earned.
