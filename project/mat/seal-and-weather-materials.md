# Coupled seal, protective return and temporary weather materials

**6 November 2026 — development comparison, not a material selection or owner exception.** The selected [renewal reference](upper-access-renewal.md), [protective return](../had/studies/north-protective-return.mjs) and [temporary weather states](../had/studies/maintenance-rain.mjs) remain the governing geometry. This successor changes none of their numeric controls.

## First constructive seal comparator

The broad flat copper gasket has no demonstrated seating at the authored 8 kN TOTAL preload. Repeating its 0.5 mm thickness does not solve that contact problem. One exact alternative is the previously identified non-adhesive Rogers BISCO HT-800 0.79 mm silicone stock, cut as a replaceable U. The [existing source screen](upper-access-renewal.md#one-cold-replaceable-functional-residue-comparator) records manufacturer revision, asymmetric thickness tolerance, density, compression data and the absence of a thin-stock water-seal qualification. The polymer formulation remains incompletely disclosed and unapproved.

A **project-authored comparator** adds four captive metallic compression stops around the existing four side bolts: each 0.5 mm thick, with a 24 mm outside diameter and a 13 mm bore. They sit between the 12 mm faceplates, entirely inside the existing 920 mm coupling territory and outside the wet U. This is a proposed load-bypass detail, not supplied hardware or a change to the selected assembly. Matched alloy, thickness/flatness tolerance, contact/buckling, capture and corrosion evidence are still needed.

For each stop, area is π(24²−13²)/4 = 319.657 mm²; four give 1278.628 mm². Even assigning the entire 8 kN to them gives 6.257 MPa nominal direct compression. At an illustrative 8000 kg/m³ density, their combined mass is 5.115 g. This arithmetic does not prove plate bending, corner stiffness, retained preload or uniform gasket contact. The old U-frame screen does not automatically become proof of this changed load path.

At a 0.5 mm closed gap, nominal 0.79 mm stock is 36.7% compressed and recovers 0.29 mm when released. Its published 0.688–1.171 mm thickness range instead gives 27.3–57.3% compression before face tolerances or deformation. The converter's 62 kPa at 25% gives only 1.364 kN over the illustrative 0.022 m² U land; it cannot be extrapolated to the working compression or 8 kN bolt preload. Stops are therefore a necessary constructive comparator, not a seal pass. The U remains a cold, captured replacement part; release/recovery and retention must fit the actual joint motion, not silently inherit the copper body's 0.5 mm permanent extent.

**Bounded next decision:** obtain the actual thin-stock force/compression envelope and permissible working compression, then compare it with measured thickness plus the face/stop deformation budget. Either produce a compatible controlled-compression reference or reject this stock. Do not raise preload, narrow the land, alter the closed gap or substitute a thicker gasket without an explicit successor change. Exact wet testing, constituents and owner disposition remain required.

## Public compression evidence changes the decision

The [manufacturer CFD tool](https://tools.rogerscorp.com/ems/cfdcurve/index.aspx) tests 38 mm-diameter specimens layered to 6.35 mm, at 5.08 mm/min. Its public curves therefore do not establish the single-layer 0.79 mm U's response. [Rogers' design guidance](https://www.rogerscorp.com/blog/2024/the-ultimate-guide-to-compression-force-deflection-free-tool) describes 25–35% as a general minimum range and warns that 50–70% can crush cellular structure; the application still controls. The [0918 family curve](https://www.rogerscorp.com/-/media/project/rogerscorp/documents/elastomeric-material-solutions/bisco/english/technical-information/cellular-silicones-compression-force-deflection-cfd-curves-in-psi.pdf), publication 180-058, expressly makes its typical curves unsuitable as specification limits.

Take **25–50%** as a conservative project comparison window, not a manufacturer-approved working range. With compression C=1−gap/thickness:

| Full published stock tolerance | Necessary fixed-gap bound |
|---|---:|
| At least 25% compression at the 0.688 mm thin end | gap ≤ 0.516 mm |
| At most 50% compression at the 1.171 mm thick end | gap ≥ 0.5855 mm |

These bounds are disjoint by 0.0695 mm. **No fixed gap, including the current 0.5 mm stop, can put the complete published stock tolerance inside this window.** This rejects an unrestricted catalogue-stock substitution rather than rejecting silicone in principle. The 50% ceiling is an authored conservative comparison, NOT an owner invariant or manufacturer prohibition. If a separately justified 25–70% window is allowed, the full-stock fixed-gap interval becomes 0.3513–0.516 mm and nominal 0.5 mm is feasible; however only 0.016 mm positive-gap error remains before the thin end falls below 25%, while the thick end enters the manufacturer-described cell-crush range. That case needs its own aged/recompression/water and actual face-gap evidence. The arithmetic does not prove the stock cannot seal or force metal to win. A hypothetical measured 0.79 ± 0.05 mm lot with an all-inclusive 0.5 ± 0.05 mm closed-gap budget would instead span 25.68–46.43%; neither that supplied tolerance nor the joint's deformation budget has been earned. The earlier 0.097/0.063 mm frame screens are not proof of a 0.05 mm assembled gap tolerance.

The useful successor is therefore a lot-controlled, stopped, retained thin-stock specimen with measured single-layer force and water response. No catalogue range, stacked CFD curve or FDA statement can replace that evidence. The selected gap, preload and gasket remain unchanged.

## Keep the protective return a separate functional boundary

The N wet shoe/cavity tray/collars are a new continuous protection reference; their 15 mm crest does not certify first harm. A seam at the −180 mm sole would see 145 mm head at the authored quiet −35 mm stage: 1.422 kPa hydrostatic pressure before the separate splash/tolerance allowances. A 100 mm overlap alone is not a watertight seam, and fasteners above 150 mm do not by themselves establish compression at the lower wet edge.

The exact folded metal/temper, seam process, retained flux/filler/finish, movement and mineral bearing must therefore be considered together. Do not spread the E-joint silicone comparator across every 1500 mm shoe segment by default. Prefer an evidenced folded/welded/otherwise suitable non-plastic shoe connection if its movement and replacement work; use a bounded functional residue only where that challenge fails and the actual location can be inspected and renewed. This section does not claim a compatible alloy, qualified weld or completed shoe detail.

### Exact non-plastic shoe process comparator

One recoverable stock identity is [AMCO1110400001](https://shop.amco-metals.com/en/copper/sheets/cw024a/r240/1.5x1000x2000mm/1110400001/): 1.5 × 1000 × 2000 mm Cu-DHP/CW024A, R240, EN1652, listed 27.12 kg. This identifies bulk stock, not a supplied finish/residue certificate. [Alumeco's 11 January 2021 rolled-product sheet](https://www.alumeco.com/media/ye3mpgue/cw024a-rolled.pdf) discloses Cu 99.90% and P 0.015–0.040%, and 17 µm/m/K thermal expansion. A stated 40 K sensitivity gives 1.02 mm over 1500 mm and 2.89 mm over 4250 mm; it is not site weather, an allowed movement or a lifetime prediction.

For a shop-welded comparator, [TWI Job Knowledge 111](https://www.twi-global.com/technical-knowledge/job-knowledge/welding-of-copper-and-its-alloys-part-1-111) supports deoxidised filler rather than assuming porous autogenous copper is watertight; it identifies ERCuSi-A and argon TIG in this thin-sheet range. It also warns that welding softens cold-worked copper, so R240 strength cannot be carried unchanged through the heat-affected region. An exact bare-wire candidate is [ESAB OK Tigrod 19.30](https://esab.com/lt/eur_en/products-solutions/product/filler-metals/copper/mig-wires-tig-rods-gmaw-gtaw/ok-tigrod-19-30/), ERCuSi-A/CuSi3Mn1: typical Cu 96%, Si 3%, Mn 0.9%, Sn 0.01%, Zn 0.05% and Fe 0.05%. Typical composition is not exhaustive batch trace chemistry or a qualified joint.

**Decision:** develop this metal-sheet/deoxidised-filler shop process as the shoe's non-plastic comparator; do not copy the E foam to every lap. The candidate has located bulk/filler chemistry and a plausible welding process, but no qualified seam, movement, surface-cleaning residue, mineral-bearing, heat-protection or later repair method. A 1500 mm segment's nominal developed straight width 100 + 195 + 152.5 + 45 = 492.5 mm fits the 1000 mm stock width before bend/end allowances; that is a fabrication starting envelope, not a finished blank or installed mass. Existing 100 mm overlaps and above-water fixings alone do not earn its movement or water resistance. No product or current geometry is adopted.

## Lighter temporary weather-tool comparator

The retained bounding-area comparison is 26.17 m² and approximately 308 kg of steel weather plane before frames, posts, gutters and anchors; the complete apparatus is recorded at an approximate 1 tonne. Current clipped regions remain HAD-owned, so this is not an actual supplied-kit bill. The next comparison is one disclosed reusable weather-sheet stock and its complete tensioning/retention method, not an unexplained mass substitution. Lower sheet dead load does not reduce the assigned wind, anchor or cantilever duty automatically, and it does not turn erection into one-person work.

The [policy](../../docs/reference-house/material-policy.md) governs the whole installed house. A removed temporary tool must be identified as such, with its composition, contact/retained residues, reuse and removal recorded; that distinction cannot authorise permanent polymer skirts, gaskets or coatings. No temporary-tool material is selected here. Exact sheet/finish chemistry, mass, water/fire behaviour and connection evidence will determine whether the lighter comparator is worth developing within the selected spatial envelope.

### One exact removed-tool comparison

[Layher Protective Systems Catalogue 2023/2024, edition 04.2023](https://www.layher.it/cataloghi/EN_Protective_System_Catalogue.pdf), pp. 11, 14–15, identifies 630 g/m² PVC-coated polyester and stock reference 5972.306: 11 × 2.57 m, 23.5 kg. It lists 780 mm deep Keder XL beams and 18°/20° ridge versions. SHA256: `6d24f561b5035c8e26df122d88dd23a5a160a331e3cc210020b4fa9f482e4c10`. Additives and transferred residues remain undisclosed; its flame label does not certify this installation.

The bounding 26.17 m² fabric-only screen is 16.49 kg, 291.51 kg below 308 kg steel; it excludes reinforced edges, rails, gutters, skirts, supports and anchors. This is not a whole-tool mass or one-person erection result.

**Reject the whole-kit drop-in:** 780 mm depth exceeds 151.5 mm by 628.5 mm; 2570 mm design width exceeds the 2400 mm opening by 170 mm. No source admits the selected sub-degree slope, clipping or pipe/crew phases. Cutting/re-edging cloth or using existing frames creates a new tensioned assembly requiring explicit sag/ponding, uplift, connection and runoff proof. Lower weight does not waive wind/anchors. Retain the sheet as a lighter removed-tool comparator; adopt neither a permanent polymer exception nor new geometry.
