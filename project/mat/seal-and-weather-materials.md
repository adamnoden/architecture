The broad flat copper gasket has no demonstrated seating at the authored8kN TOTAL preload. Repeating its0.5 thickness does not solve that contact problem. One exact alternative is the previously identified non-adhesive Rogers BISCO HT-8000.79mm silicone stock, cut as a replaceable U. A **project-authored comparator** adds four captive metallic compression stops around the existing four side bolts: each0.5 thick,24 outside diameter,13 bore. They sit between the12 faceplates, entirely inside the existing920 coupling territory and outside the wet U. This is a proposed load-bypass detail, not supplied hardware or a change to the selected assembly. Matched alloy, thickness/flatness tolerance, contact/buckling, capture and corrosion evidence are still needed.

For each stop, area is π(24²−13²)/4 =319.657mm²; four give1278.628mm². Even assigning the entire8kN to them gives6.257MPa nominal direct compression. At an illustrative8000kg/m³ density, their combined mass is5.115g. This arithmetic does not prove plate bending, corner stiffness, retained preload or uniform gasket contact. The old U-frame screen does not automatically become proof of this changed load path.

At a0.5 closed gap, nominal0.79 stock is36.7% compressed and recovers0.29 when released. Its published0.688..1.171 thickness range instead gives27.3..57.3% compression before face tolerances or deformation. The converter's62kPa at25% gives only1.364kN over the illustrative0.022m² U land; it cannot be extrapolated to the working compression or8kN bolt preload. Stops are therefore a necessary constructive comparator, not a seal pass. The U remains a cold, captured replacement part; release/recovery and retention must fit the actual joint motion, not silently inherit the copper body's0.5 permanent extent.

**Bounded next decision:** obtain the actual thin-stock force/compression envelope and permissible working compression, then compare it with measured thickness plus the face/stop deformation budget. Either produce a compatible controlled-compression reference or reject this stock. Do not raise preload, narrow the land, alter the closed gap or substitute a thicker gasket without an explicit successor change. Exact wet testing, constituents and owner disposition remain required.

## Public compression evidence changes the decision

The [manufacturer CFD tool](https://tools.rogerscorp.com/ems/cfdcurve/index.aspx) tests38mm-diameter specimens layered to6.35mm, at5.08mm/min. Its public curves therefore do not establish the single-layer0.79mm U's response. [Rogers' design guidance](https://www.rogerscorp.com/blog/2024/the-ultimate-guide-to-compression-force-deflection-free-tool) describes25–35% as a general minimum range and warns that50–70% can crush cellular structure; the application still controls. The [0918 family curve](https://www.rogerscorp.com/-/media/project/rogerscorp/documents/elastomeric-material-solutions/bisco/english/technical-information/cellular-silicones-compression-force-deflection-cfd-curves-in-psi.pdf), publication180-058, expressly makes its typical curves unsuitable as specification limits.

Take **25–50%** as a conservative project comparison window, not a manufacturer-approved working range. With compression C=1−gap/thickness:

| Full published stock tolerance | Necessary fixed-gap bound |
|---|---:|
| At least25% compression at0.688 thin end | gap ≤0.516 |
| At most50% compression at1.171 thick end | gap ≥0.5855 |

These bounds are disjoint by0.0695. **No fixed gap, including the current0.5 stop, can put the complete published stock tolerance inside this window.** This rejects an unrestricted catalogue-stock substitution rather than rejecting silicone in principle. A hypothetical measured0.79±0.05 lot with an all-inclusive0.5±0.05 closed-gap budget would instead span25.68–46.43%; neither that supplied tolerance nor the joint's deformation budget has been earned. The earlier0.097/0.063 frame screens are not proof of a0.05 assembled gap tolerance.

The useful successor is therefore a lot-controlled, stopped, retained thin-stock specimen with measured single-layer force and water response. No catalogue range, stacked CFD curve or FDA statement can replace that evidence. The selected gap, preload and gasket remain unchanged.

## Keep the protective return a separate functional boundary

The N wet shoe/cavity tray/collars are a new continuous protection reference; their15 crest does not certify first harm. A seam at the−180 sole would see145mm head at the authored quiet−35 stage:1.422kPa hydrostatic pressure before the separate splash/tolerance allowances. A100 overlap alone is not a watertight seam, and fasteners above150 do not by themselves establish compression at the lower wet edge.

The exact folded metal/temper, seam process, retained flux/filler/finish, movement and mineral bearing must therefore be considered together. Do not spread the E-joint silicone comparator across every1500 shoe segment by default. Prefer an evidenced folded/welded/otherwise suitable non-plastic shoe connection if its movement and replacement work; use a bounded functional residue only where that challenge fails and the actual location can be inspected and renewed. This section does not claim a compatible alloy, qualified weld or completed shoe detail.

### Exact non-plastic shoe process comparator

One recoverable stock identity is [AMCO1110400001](https://shop.amco-metals.com/en/copper/sheets/cw024a/r240/1.5x1000x2000mm/1110400001/):1.5×1000×2000 Cu-DHP/CW024A, R240, EN1652, listed27.12kg. This identifies bulk stock, not a supplied finish/residue certificate. [Alumeco's11January2021 rolled-product sheet](https://www.alumeco.com/media/ye3mpgue/cw024a-rolled.pdf) discloses Cu99.90% and P0.015–0.040%, and17µm/m/K thermal expansion. A stated40K sensitivity gives1.02mm over1500 and2.89mm over4250; it is not site weather, an allowed movement or a lifetime prediction.

For a shop-welded comparator, [TWI Job Knowledge111](https://www.twi-global.com/technical-knowledge/job-knowledge/welding-of-copper-and-its-alloys-part-1-111) supports deoxidised filler rather than assuming porous autogenous copper is watertight; it identifies ERCuSi-A and argon TIG in this thin-sheet range. It also warns that welding softens cold-worked copper, so R240 strength cannot be carried unchanged through the heat-affected region. An exact bare-wire candidate is [ESAB OK Tigrod19.30](https://esab.com/lt/eur_en/products-solutions/product/filler-metals/copper/mig-wires-tig-rods-gmaw-gtaw/ok-tigrod-19-30/), ERCuSi-A/CuSi3Mn1: typical Cu96/Si3/Mn0.9/Sn0.01/Zn0.05/Fe0.05%. Typical composition is not exhaustive batch trace chemistry or a qualified joint.

**Decision:** develop this metal-sheet/deoxidised-filler shop process as the shoe's non-plastic comparator; do not copy the E foam to every lap. The candidate has located bulk/filler chemistry and a plausible welding process, but no qualified seam, movement, surface-cleaning residue, mineral-bearing, heat-protection or later repair method. A1500 segment's nominal developed straight width100+195+152.5+45=492.5 fits the1000 stock width before bend/end allowances; that is a fabrication starting envelope, not a finished blank or installed mass. Existing100 overlaps and above-water fixings alone do not earn its movement or water resistance. No product or current geometry is adopted.
