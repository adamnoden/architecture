## What this audit establishes

The house has architectural material intentions, geometric reservations and some named comparison products. It does **not** yet have a complete installed-product schedule. The audit therefore records both located synthetic constituents and explicit unresolved assemblies. An unresolved product choice is a useful design input; it cannot be counted as zero plastic.

The strongest new findings are in secondary components: wet-room bonding products, a resin shower tray and plastic waste; roof underlays and vapour layers; pipe spacers; ventilation ducts and accessories; and insulation inside a steel hot-water cylinder. None is represented as already installed or accepted for the house. Conversely, the inherited timber floor, old service networks and prototype parts must not be mistaken for current selections after the structural and material rebriefs.

### Reading the register

- **P — synthetic constituent evidenced:** the named source identifies a plastic, synthetic resin or synthetic elastomer. This establishes presence, not its full formulation or installed quantity.
- **N — non-plastic constituent evidenced:** only the stated component is evidenced, such as a specified copper tube body or steel vessel. It does not clear adjacent layers, coatings, joints or equipment interiors.
- **U — unresolved:** product not selected, composition undisclosed, accessory not reconciled or evidence insufficient. A mineral/metal/timber appearance or generic material name alone leaves the complete assembly U. Natural wood is not classified as prohibited plastic merely because it is chemically polymeric.

States can coexist. **No row clears a complete installed assembly.** The policy's exception process remains the only exception authority; this audit approves none.

Applicability is kept separate from constituent state: **intent** is architectural direction; **candidate** is a current unadopted comparison; **historical** is retained evidence whose old preference cannot be adopted by default; **unselected** is a required or conditional function without a product; **example** is adverse or alternative evidence, not a house proposal. Visible/touchable faces, concealed construction and accessible equipment interiors all remain in scope.

**Quantity rule for every row:** installed counts, lengths, areas and constituent masses are **unknown** unless an owning selected-house schedule subsequently supplies them. The audit has found no such complete schedule. Study lengths, spatial allocations, catalogue roll sizes, specimen weights and supplier material fractions are not installed-house quantities. Future records must name the unit and basis, source revision, uncertainty and whether the quantity is for a selected assembly or a comparator. No polymer mass percentage or whole-house material-compliance percentage is calculated.

## Audit outcome and downstream use

This register completes **the current-concept constituent inventory** required before alternatives work. It exposes the relevant known conflicts and gives every major represented assembly or unselected service function an explicit material question and owner. It does not complete product disclosure for an unspecified future house, grant exceptions or accept any construction assembly. Later designs must update their affected rows with actual products, quantities, accessories and evidence; audit completion does not freeze unknowns forever.

The three existing alternatives packets can now consume the same baseline:

1. **MAT-ENVELOPE:** develop the ground/wall/floor moisture section first, including suitable mineral DPC construction and justified extra margin. Reconcile structural options, load transfer, floor/gas/air/thermal continuity, thresholds and penetrations. Also resolve roof underlays, complete wall/roof insulation and opening installation boundaries. A DPC material name alone will not discharge G02/G03/G06.
2. **MAT-SERVICES:** carry the actual supply/waste/heating/ventilation/electrical/equipment constituents through ordinary installable alternatives. Reuse RWT-METALS for copper/brass design and compatibility. A metal collector, vessel or conduit cannot close the associated U rows; required safety and performance functions remain.
3. **MAT-INTERIORS:** revisit the whole wet assembly, including the visible resin tray and concealed adhesive/waste/cistern chain. Resolve dry-floor/paint/joinery constituents as actual products. Preserve actual room fit and water-boundary duties rather than replacing one layer's name in the old 25 mm comparison.
