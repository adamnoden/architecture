# Study wall plaster: a named material and a real depth conflict

## Result and location

**MAT-INTERIORS partial comparison — 31 October 2026.** Conserv's hydraulic lime base and finish plasters provide a specific substrate candidate for the [mineral coating study](interior-mineral-coating.md). Its recommended three-coat arrangement totals **24 mm**, so it cannot occupy the house's existing **15 mm** envelope-wall finish allowance unchanged. A **14 mm two-coat arrangement is an authored challenger**, not an established manufacturer system. Neither arrangement is selected.

Locate the comparison at **GROUND / Study / outer-south**, through the solid front pier at **x2800, z1500**. This is outside the source window apertures x1300–2300 and x3400–4400. In [RH-LAYOUT-REVISION-02](../../compiler/reference-house/georgian/layout-revision.ts), south is the front and positive y points into the room. The inherited [section allocation](../../compiler/reference-house/georgian/section-exemplar.ts) is 102.5 weather leaf + 160 cavity + 140 inner zone + 15 finish. Its nominal supporting face is therefore **y402.5** and finished face **y417.5**.

The 140 inner zone has no selected unit/mortar/surface constitution. This study does not turn it into brick, concrete block or a prepared plaster substrate. The [IAS dry selection](../../docs/reference-house/interior-family-selection.md) permits direct finish and local repair within the 15 allocation; it supplies no extra depth. No allowance is transferred to the 125 partitions, ceilings, reveals or wet-room water boundaries.

## Compare the complete depth, not only the final skim

All figures below are nominal millimetres. Coating, additional preparation and deviations are excluded from the plaster totals and must be added before a fit result.

| Comparison | Plaster coats | Total | Face measured from y0 | Balance against 15 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Current source reservation | Unspecified | 15 | 417.5 | 0; this is an allocation, not a product |
| P3 recommended arrangement | 10 scratch + 10 float + 4 finish | 24 | 426.5 | **9 over**, before additional work |
| Authored two-coat challenger | 10 base + 4 finish | 14 | 416.5 | **1 gross remaining**, before additional work |

The 24 arrangement is an evidenced dimensional rejection for unchanged adoption. It is not a finding that every lime plaster needs 24. Retaining it would require an explicit finish-depth and downstream source rewind. Moving the supporting face back by 9 would consume the unselected structural inner zone; this study does not authorise that exchange.

For the 14 challenger, let `k` be added key/preparation thickness, `p` the complete coating thickness and `d` the positive allowance needed for actual substrate/application deviation. It fits only if **14 + k + p + d ≤ 15**. None of those three values is established. The one-millimetre arithmetic balance is neither a workmanship tolerance nor permission to omit a required coat. Wet paint consumption or wet density cannot supply an invented dry-film thickness.

If the 24 face were continued across the Study front, the fixed desk reservation beginning at y600 would be **173.5** from that face, compared with the present **182.5**. This is a 9 mm loss of plan reserve, not a human-reach or furniture-operability test. At x2800 the pier itself is clear of that desk. Window returns, independent 18-projecting trim/skirting and adjacent finish planes would also need their own matching sections. Neither desk nor opening is moved to conceal the change.

## Preparation and the coating interface

P3 requires a firm, clean background, repair of defects and control of suction. It identifies a separately supplied key coat for difficult smooth/dense backgrounds and metal mesh extending at least 100 each side of unlike materials. These are real additions to investigate, not contents of the two plaster bags. Drying between plaster coats also precedes decoration; the guide's interval is not a coating-readiness test.

For this plain pier, first identify the actual unit face, joints and surface preparation. Do not assume the hypothetical block surface is sufficiently rough, substitute an unreviewed bonding agent, or omit required reinforcement to retain the depth or material claim. A field section cannot resolve the window-head, jamb, skirting fixing or movement junction.

The [BEECK Powdered Pigment technical sheet](https://www.beeck.com/bfw-files/produkt-downloads/bmf--id143-715.pdf), **7 March 2018, §§2.1–2.3**, requires a firm, porous, water-wettable mineral substrate. It permits grinding off sinter skin on solid plaster as an alternative to its named etching treatment; absorbent plaster receives diluted standard Fixative. Defects can require further products. These are conditional preparation routes. The plaster's reported approximate strength is not proof of BEECK substrate acceptance or a transferred plaster classification. Compatibility, strength, finish and repair must be demonstrated on the proposed background before selection.

The [mineral coating study](interior-mineral-coating.md) retains the two-coat finish, actual shade/revision and auxiliary-product questions. Neither the new plaster candidate nor its nominal depth resolves those outstanding inputs. The intended maintenance route remains local plaster repair and recoating, with independent trim release. No invisible patch, fixed renewal interval or universal wall/ceiling compatibility is asserted.

## A thinner named family was also checked

[Lime Green Solo's technical sheet](https://www.lime-green.co.uk/Documents/Datasheets/solo-one-coat-lime-plaster-tds.pdf), **4 July 2024**, includes masonry in its scope, but its detailed application list does not establish the masonry-specific coat thickness needed here. Coverage at 10 mm is not an installation specification. Some other backgrounds require acrylic primer; that instruction is not transferred to this masonry field.

The manufacturer-hosted [Kiwa BDA Agrément BAW-22-242-S-A-UK](https://www.lime-green.co.uk/Documents/3269%20BAW-22-242-S-A-UK%202023.pdf), **Table 1, printed page 4**, describes Solo as selenitic lime, sand, chalk and other additives. Their unresolved identity prevents a material pass; it does not prove that every additive is a synthetic polymer. The document assesses the complete Warmshell internal-insulation system, not this direct-plaster field. No system certification or coating compatibility is transferred. Solo remains a thinner-family enquiry, not a demonstrated better assembly.

## Next design decision and verification

Retain the named Conserv family and the **24 mm adverse baseline**. Carry 14 only as a question for a substrate-specific two-coat specification. The choice now requires the actual inner-leaf surface, complete key/repair/edge constituents, supported coat thicknesses and the finish/deviation allowance. If those cannot fit 15, compare another fully disclosed assembly or reconcile a real finish-depth change through IAS/HAD and the house source. Physical finish/bond/repair evidence is needed for adoption, not invented by this arithmetic.

The source dimensions, window interval, sums and desk difference were independently reviewed. This document changes no house source, lifecycle or maturity. MAT-INTERIORS and I02 remain unresolved as complete installed assemblies.
