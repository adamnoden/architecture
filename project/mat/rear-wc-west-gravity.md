# Rear WC and shower west-wall gravity route

**Develop the Y9400 exterior outlet as a coupled service and structure alternative.** Keep the rear WC, its pan and flush axes, the shower tray and trap, and the independent shower branch. Route the WC outlet above the first finished floor and take the shower branch below the slab through a separate west-wall sleeve. Both can then join one exposed, vented foul stack. Removing the retained Ø160 opening at **X567.5/Y11177.5** from the new floor design would free the NW receiver for uninterrupted reinforcement. This comparison does not qualify the receiver, existing holes, new wall penetrations or installed drainage. ## Use the solid west wall at Y9400

The WC branch turns south at **X510**, reaches **Y9400** and turns west through the wall. From the existing +185 mm soil outlet and its retained connector land, the square-plan reservation is **2,677 mm**. The existing 1:40 fall gives **66.925 mm** at the west wall. A trial Ø160 sleeve leaves **38.075 mm** between its underside and FFL Z3450, before setting. The straight branch extends the west low box **537.5 mm** south of its current end. Keep both turns and joints accessible. The nominal **150 mm** box leaves **40 mm gross** around a straight **110 mm OD** pipe before wall finish, supports and fitting allowance. A single cut length from the actual 3 m stock can run between the accessible end boxes. The compared cast iron coupling is **170 × 137 mm** before tools and belongs in an end box. Confirm its seal and coating constituents, allowable use, end-first removal and both straight-support and fitting clearances. It fails the 1.3 m WC and 2.0 m branch stub-stack comparisons even using the conservative host axis before its invert.
