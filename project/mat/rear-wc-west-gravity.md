# Rear WC and shower west-wall gravity route

**Develop the Y9400 exterior outlet as a coupled service and structure alternative.** Keep the rear WC, its pan and flush axes, the shower tray and trap, and the independent shower branch. Route the WC outlet above the first finished floor and take the shower branch below the slab through a separate west-wall sleeve. Both can then join one exposed, vented foul stack. Removing the retained Ø160 opening at **X567.5/Y11177.5** from the new floor design would free the NW receiver for uninterrupted reinforcement. This comparison does not qualify the receiver, existing holes, new wall penetrations or installed drainage. ## Use the solid west wall at Y9400

The WC branch turns south at **X510**, reaches **Y9400** and turns west through the wall. From the existing +185 mm soil outlet and its retained connector land, the square-plan reservation is **2,677 mm**. The existing 1:40 fall gives **66.925 mm** at the west wall. A trial Ø160 sleeve leaves **38.075 mm** between its underside and FFL Z3450, before setting. The straight branch extends the west low box **537.5 mm** south of its current end. Keep both turns and joints accessible. The nominal **150 mm** box leaves **40 mm gross** around a straight **110 mm OD** pipe before wall finish, supports and fitting allowance. A single cut length from the actual 3 m stock can run between the accessible end boxes. The compared cast iron coupling is **170 × 137 mm** before tools and belongs in an end box. Confirm its seal and coating constituents, allowable use, end-first removal and both straight-support and fitting clearances. [DN100 straight pipe](https://www.pambuilding.co.uk/sites/default/files/document/2025-06/AP000%20-%20Pipe%203m.pdf) · [DN100 coupling](https://www.pambuilding.co.uk/sites/default/files/document/2025-06/EC002%20-%20Two-Piece%20Ductile%20Iron%20Coupling%20-%20v25.pdf)

The separate lower shower branch keeps the actual trap and first leg to **X1750/Y9452.5/Z2768.06**. Turn south to **Y9400**, then west at 1:40. It reaches the west wall at **Z2723.00**, **27.625 mm** higher than the current junction. Confirm actual long-sweep fittings at this change of direction or form a graded diagonal, retain the first release, and keep the two branches independent until the external collector. Confirm the lower finish, wall support, fire stopping, cleanouts and a visible leak route. [Rear shower and trap boundary](../ias/ias-03-three-bathing-sections.md)

## Keep the foul stack independent

Trial the exterior stack axis **X−300/Y9400**. The plain 110 mm body leaves **35 mm** to the nominal 200 mm west overhang after 10 mm paired setting. That is a body screen. Price and draw external bends, supports, rainwater hardware, appearance and access independently. Actual nearby opening heads give **Z7200** as the minimum open vent level in this screen. Approved Document H calls for 900 mm above any opening within 3 m. Confirm that datum against actual operable windows, air inlets, roof overhang and access. Keep the old independent foul destination concept, with the new site connection and invert proved. Keep the wet-room liner and inner wall air seal independent of the removable covers and keep the drained cavity empty. A proposal to fill an already formed slab opening has its own strength and boundary obligations. [Approved Document H](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/drainage-and-waste-disposal-approved-document-h) · [Wall and soffit boundary](loggia-soffit.md)
