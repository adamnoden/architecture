## A real damper family exposes a startup interlock conflict

The official [Lindab DTBCU sheet](https://www.lindab.dk/globalassets/commerce/lindabwebproductsdoc/assets/production/zjixota4ymutnja3zi00mgy2ltg4oditzdvknzkwngm4nwzl/5250153177914824269/dtbcu.pdf?v=1770509596) lists a Ø400, 100 mm axial, 6.42 kg configuration with SF24A-SR 24 V modulating actuator and closed-blade class 4. Lindab describes the powered 2–10 V option as spring-return closed on power loss; [Belimo's actuator data](https://www.belimo.com/mam/general-documents/datasheets/en-gb/belimo_SF24A-SR_datasheet_en-gb.pdf) gives 150 seconds for 90° powered opening, <20 seconds spring return at −20..50 °C, 2–10 V position feedback with ±5% accuracy and IP54. A separately listed [S2A-F auxiliary switch](https://www.belimo.com/mam/general-documents/datasheets/en-gb/belimo_S2A-F_datasheet_en-gb.pdf) offers adjustable contacts for an independently commissioned end position. The damper design 3 drawing calls out 230, 140 and 105 mm around its mounted actuator; those callouts are **not** a complete insulated module or usable tool envelope.

That actual 150-second stroke **fails** the inherited five-second full-open proof on manual hood start. A possible control sequence therefore asks the user to **pre-arm makeup separately while the hood remains OFF**: command full opening, wait for actual end-position contact plus separate analogue position and pressure plausibility, and show READY. The user then manually turns the hood ON; the controller never commands hood ON. In an idealised clean 400 intake law, full-open pre-arm with hood still OFF would bring about 20.521 L/s outdoor makeup into the kitchen and 57.479 L/s through habitable vents in the local-normal case; all-boost pre-arm gives about 25.26 L/s direct and 70.74 L/s habitable. Each still conserves its 78/96 L/s background extract. Pre-arm is an actual cold-air and room-distribution transient, not a hidden zero-flow waiting period. Its full-opening time, energy, noise and readiness behaviour need human-factor and installed pressure tests.

During RUN, a separate kitchen pressure loop **would need to** modulate the damper toward −4.5 Pa, with all three factory hood speeds retained and independently commissioned capture. The end-position contact is a **start proof**, not a continuously made contact under modulation. **The manufacturer’s underlying DTU sheet describes blade regulation only on rare occasions.** The 2–10 V SF-SR option proves commandability, not service life or suitability for frequent cooker pressure correction. Require explicit OEM approval of the configured DTBCU repeated-modulation duty cycle, or reject this family for RUN. Manual hood OFF closes the makeup after hood airflow ceases. A position disagreement, measured low makeup, weather/fouling limit or controller/sensor loss must latch alarm and inhibit/trip the hood through an **OEM-qualified external power/control interface**, then require manual reset. If a user can manually energise the hood before READY, or that protective interface is not qualified, this sequence fails. Common power failure de-energises the hood as the spring closes; a separately powered hood would require its own immediate trip. No automatic hood ON is introduced. The product's pressure loss and response under the required roughly 75.615–81.550 L/s at only 4.5 Pa are **not published in this source**, so the previous K=2/75%-free flow estimate cannot be credited to this damper.

The underlying [DTU sheet specifies rare regulation and an EPDM rubber blade seal](https://www.lindab.com/globalassets/commerce/lindabwebproductsdoc/assets/production/ztqymjq1mditzgm1zs00nju5lwjlyjktyzdhnzc4yte0odcz/5250235942702241834/dtu.pdf?v=1772929437), and actuator wiring/housing constituents need a full declaration. There is no owner material exception; **DTBCU is a physical and control-sequence comparator, not an admitted RUN controller or selected unit**. The fast [Lindab DTFU](https://itsolution.lindab.com/lindabwebproductsdoc/pdf/documentation/ads/lindab/technical/dtfu.pdf) with 2.5-second modulating LMQ24A-SR is listed only through Ø250 and is non fail-safe; it does not repair the 400 assembly. The [TROX TVR/400](https://cdn.trox.de/aa453eca605bdc74/63e8031d227b/TVR_PD_2025_05_13_DE_en.pdf) is 500 mm axial with an Easy/Compact attachment, but its published low sizing point is around 165 L/s, more than twice this make-up duty. Neither can be relabelled as a qualified 400 low-pressure cooker intake.
