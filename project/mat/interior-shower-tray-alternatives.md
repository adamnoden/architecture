**The recovered package audit adds the Upstand Rev.8 wall-datum check and Tecom PDOTT90 brass-body comparison.** The supplied polypropylene adapter prevents an unmodified material pass, and tray fit and water seal remain unproved. A separate brass-trap route remains a component enquiry, but the examined standalone outlet does not match the 90 mm tray opening. No fitted waste or west-access section is earned by these findings.

**The subsequent [52 mm outlet alternative](#52-mm-outlet-alternative) changes the tray comparison.** BetteQuinta 5600 retains the nominal metre-square plan and drain offsets while providing a smaller opening and a deeper bowl.
