## Question and result

Can a manufactured enamelled-steel tray remove the visible resin tray in the [Bedroom 4 wet-assembly comparison](../ias/ias-03-bedroom4-assembly-successor.md), while retaining water protection, support and renewal?

**The steel/enamel body is a useful alternative to develop. The examined installation packages do not establish a plastic-free assembly.** Bette's integral upstand offers a wall-junction approach without an exposed silicone joint, but its published detail retains concealed sealing materials. Both the EPS Minimum support and the aluminium/plastic Basic support contain an evidenced synthetic constituent. Neither is admissible as a no-plastic default. No exception is requested or approved here.

This changes the comparison from choosing a tray surface to resolving the tray, support, wall junction and waste together. It does not authorise omitting waterproofing, isolation or safe support, or modifying a proprietary system outside its evidenced installation conditions.

## Geometry and renewal consequences

The 1000-square catalogue body permits a like-size starting comparison with the IAS tray reservation. It does not establish fit. The catalogue **25 mm depth is not the installed height**, the total tray-body envelope, or the old 25 mm wet-floor package. Support, drain, connection, finish and upstand datums must be bound separately. The JT corner-waste position, 45 mm profile, Kit 6 support and earlier outlet-clearance arithmetic cannot transfer to this product by changing its name.

B3's wall accommodation requires an actual section through the proposed substrate and finish. It cannot be assumed spare within IAS's existing 25 mm wet-wall allocation or obtained by cutting a new solid structural wall without review. Its tiled overlap is not a flood freeboard. Ends, corners, screen fixings, penetrations, floor transition and the remaining wet-room boundary still need continuity.

**Design inference:** integrating the upstand behind a bonded lining makes complete tray renewal a local lining-and-junction operation. The detail does not establish damage-free withdrawal. The next drawing must show a deliberately renewable finish zone, full reinstatement of the water boundary, access to disconnect and replace the whole waste body, support release, and the tray's lifting/carrying route. Top cleaning is a separate task. A silicone-free visible joint does not prove that concealed seals or the entire tray need no future renewal.

## Next bounded decision and authority retained

IAS/HAD retain room, enclosure, threshold and carrying geometry; BCR/STR retain support and substrate adequacy. MAT supplies constituent evidence, and services retains the connected waste. Before advancing this alternative, bind one current tray/upstand variant and:

1. Disclose the complete support, isolation, seal, bond, finish and waste package. Reject substitutions that retain an undisclosed polymer as an assumed zero; record any unavoidable exception through the existing policy.
2. Draw the matched plan/section and replacement sequence against the actual room and structural proposal, including underside/waste clearance and the wall-junction ends. A catalogue depth or nominal support range cannot discharge this gate.
3. Establish the complete water-safe installation and its applicable performance evidence, retaining slip, load, movement and acoustic duties. Optional anti-slip or easy-clean finishes need their own chemistry; required performance cannot be removed to simplify the material claim.
