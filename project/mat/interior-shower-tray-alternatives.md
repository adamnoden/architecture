## Question and result

Can an enamelled-steel tray replace the visible resin body in the [Bedroom 4 wet-assembly comparison](../ias/ias-03-bedroom4-assembly-successor.md), with a credible support, water boundary and replacement route?

**Carry the steel/enamel body forward, with a west-facing drain as the next access comparison.** The manufacturer's base-tray drawing locates the drain differently from the former corner outlet. The existing north-box access and waste-route arithmetic cannot transfer. A new access face at the tray's west side is a useful design direction; its opening, working space, support and matched waste still need to be resolved.

The research also identifies a **manufacturer-described metal Foot-System** worth developing. That is a better principal-support candidate than the explicit EPS or aluminium/plastic packages; its complete installation remains undisclosed. The examined current BetteCompact+ waste is plastic, despite an optional enamel cover. The integral Upstand removes an exposed silicone wall joint but retains concealed sealing. No complete no-plastic assembly, exception, purchase specification or service life is accepted.

**The package audit narrows three decisions.** Use the newer Upstand Rev.8 rough-opening detail, retain the four-foot frame recipe as a component comparison, and investigate the actual brass body of Tecom PDOTT90. Its supplied polypropylene adapter prevents an unmodified material pass, and its tray fit and water seal remain unproved. A separate brass-trap route remains possible in principle, but the examined standalone outlets do not match the 90 mm tray opening. No fitted waste or west-access section is earned by these findings.

## Wall, height and renewal boundary

The catalogue **25 mm depth is not installed height**, total body envelope or the old 25 mm wet-floor package. DW_0082 has separate 25/30/40 section labels; none alone binds the lowest underside, noise pad, waste seat and configured Upstand. The old JT 45 mm profile, 140 mm support allowance and 185 mm rim cannot be reused. Foot-System's 80–200 mm family range is a product parameter, not a selected plinth or accessible step height.

The existing IAS 25 mm wet lining is a polymer-containing comparison. B3's wall accommodation is neither automatically an additional inward loss nor proven spare inside that lining. ### Waste screening before a fitted section

B9's drawing labels a **Ø118 cover**, **167 mm from waste centreline to brass outlet end**, **Ø32 neck / Ø40 terminal**, and a separate **106 mm adapter**. Its **43 mm** body dimension is from the depicted upper flange/body datum; it is neither the installed height below the wet tray face nor a water-seal depth. No 90 mm aperture or clamp-thickness range is labelled in that sheet.

**Conditional geometric screen:** if B9 were matched to 5940 and its outlet pointed directly west, its brass end would be at **x1545−167 = 1378**, just **43 mm inboard** of the proposed x1335 access face. That is a point location, not space for the joint, hand or tool. Adapter engagement, the connected fitting and disconnection travel are unknown; do not add 167+106 as an installed length or claim the joint fits inside the tray. This comparison remains prose arithmetic and does not promote an unmatched waste into the plan drawing.

For the England guidance comparison, [Approved Document H, 2015 edition](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a80cf9ded915d74e33fc8ae/BR_PDF_AD_H_2015.pdf), printed p7, §§1.3–1.6/Table 1, gives shower trap size **40 mm** and seal **50 mm**. The **25 mm** retained seal under working/test conditions is a different criterion; the **38 mm** exception concerns direct discharge to a gully. Separate traps belong directly after the appliance with removal or cleaning-eye access. This is guidance, not whole-building compliance or permission for an arbitrarily distant trap. B9 supplies no evidence for these duties; its shallow external dimension cannot establish them.

B11 offers a documented 50 mm seal, but still needs a matched outlet immediately upstream. B10's smaller flange is a decisive rejection of that named direct-fit route. Neither inserting an invented reducing plate nor substituting a basin/bath connection resolves a 90 mm tray seat. Keep the separate-trap option open only as an unassembled alternative.

## Next bounded decision and authority retained

The next decision is whether one **current, exact 5940/two-wall-Upstand/metal-frame/waste package** can satisfy the named interfaces. Obtain the configured frame/upstand identity and extra-foot resolution; disclose pad/strip/seal constituents; establish a compatible outlet seat, trap/flow performance and permitted metal connection. Then draw the north/east wall datum and west service section with whole-body withdrawal, local finish renewal and carrying. The public evidence inspected here does not yet supply those missing interfaces. If a complete permitted package cannot be established, redesign the sanitary assembly or raise a specific exception through the existing policy; do not waive the water or support duty.

IAS/HAD retain room/enclosure/threshold/carrying geometry; STR/BCR retain support and substrate adequacy; services retains the connected waste. MAT supplies component evidence and the material comparison. The present result updates **I05/I07/I08**, advances the location and renewal question, and leaves wider floors, paint, joinery, sanitaryware and concealed linings open. It creates no new workstream or parallel approval system. Repository, reproducibility, visual and exact-head CI checks protect integration; they cannot establish physical fitness or a 1,000-year life.
