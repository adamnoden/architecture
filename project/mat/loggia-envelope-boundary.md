## Ground and near-level threshold: the controlling join

**Do not fix a new grade until STR's lowest complete support is known.** A field-only380 mm slab is not its ground beam, cap, bedding or mineral DPC. The separate300 heave stress,50 construction-loss challenge and actual residual ventilation/ground-clearance criterion must be applied to the lowest retained outside surface. None is a forecast of site movement. A50 mm final stress reserve does not replace the suspended-floor ventilation criterion.

Near-level loggia paving cannot be treated as dry ground beside a DPC copied at the old below-bearing datum. Test different mineral bands for the separated leaves: the inner wall's DPC must protect its real load/floor path; the outer wall's complete band can be placed above the actual loggia surface, with a deliberately drained cavity separating them. Any inner/outer level difference needs actual bonded returns, tray outlets and door-jamb connections. Paving, cavity fill, RC support, plaster or retained debris must not reconnect the wet and dry paths.

STR’s agreed research hypothesis is a wet-tolerant40-cover ground RC grid, with a150 mm bonded masonry DPC band above its top−180, ending−30 beneath the inner wall. The support/cap underside−680 and derivedD1200 remain calculation hypotheses, not fixed geometry. This does not qualify floor-field/end moisture or air/gas. With that deliberate strategy, do not add150 mm beneath every beam by habit. Conversely, if the floor relies on a bonded150 mm mineral band beneath its bearing, include that real depth before the cap and grade are fixed. The floor field's moisture, air and any site-required gas function remain separate. ADC2013's suspended-concrete example includes40 mm in-situ reinforcement-cover guidance; the old preliminary25 mm cap/U calculations do not supply that acceptance.

For accessible exterior door landings, ADC§5.33's1:40–1:60 outward fall uses10–15 mm over600 mm. Allocate that fall before the actual channel and walking edge. It does not make a doorset flood-resistant. Locate the new door/frame drains, fixing holes, jamb/meeting-stile junctions and pan return on the actual profile; a nominal−45 sill underside cannot become the lowest first-harm datum by repetition.

The normal copper route should remain accessible along one outdoor edge. The separately visible relief must have its own physically available surface/intake and admitted receiver, rather than borrow the same blocked pipe/grille. The open-loggia roof/upper floor does not establish zero wind-driven rain. RWT retains actual assigned inflows, water levels, common-debris/high-tailwater challenge and lawful outlets; the next joined section must give it finite geometry to calculate.
