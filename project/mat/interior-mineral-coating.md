# Dry mineral coating and its substrate boundary

## Result and authority

**MAT-INTERIORS partial study — 31 October 2026.** Retain BEECK Pure Crystalline
Finish / Reinkristallin, made from its Powdered Pigment and standard Fixative, as
an evidenced non-plastic coating candidate for a compatible bare mineral
plaster. The separate constituent declarations improve I02's generic mineral
finish question. They do not select a finished wall, ceiling or supplied colour.

The [HAD finish family](../had/had-02m-material-finish-synthesis.md) calls for a
warm off-white mineral field. The [IAS dry selection](../../docs/reference-house/interior-family-selection.md)
retains direct finish within the ordinary envelope wall's 15 mm allowance and
accepts local plaster repair. This study develops that direction under the
[material policy](../../docs/reference-house/material-policy.md); it adds no
lining, changes no geometry and does not transfer the wall allowance to every
partition or ceiling. MAT-INTERIORS remains incomplete.

## Candidate assembly and where it fails

The useful candidate is an **opaque coating on sound, porous, water-wettable
mineral plaster**, with the exact standard Fixative where the substrate needs
priming. F4 identifies lime, lime-cement and cement plaster and requires a dry,
firm substrate without separating contamination. Air-lime plaster must mature
sufficiently before coating; the cited English instructions require carbonation
and an on-site trial. Absorbent plaster receives standard Fixative diluted with
two parts water before the two-coat finish. These are manufacturer conditions,
not a finding that the house already has such a substrate.

This route does not clear gypsum, wood-based boards, plastic or film-forming
coatings: F4 identifies them as unsuitable substrates for this system. A nominal
“mineral” board or ceiling cannot be substituted without its actual face and
compatible preparation. Hairline cracks, surface defects or poor wettability
invoke additional preparation or named auxiliaries. Those products need their
own constituent review; do not omit a required treatment or substitute
MBA-Fixative while retaining the standard Fixative's zero-organic finding.

For the dry plaster candidate, retain the distinction between required substrate
preparation and optional exterior hydrophobing. F4 allows mechanical removal of
sinter skin on suitable solid plaster as an alternative to its named etching
fluid; the actual substrate must decide the method. This does not approve an
untested omission of preparation. No exterior water-repellent, wet-room water
boundary or protective clearcoat is added by this comparison.

## Design consequence and remaining decision

I02 can now distinguish the evidenced powder/binder components from the
unresolved whole finish. The next design step is to bind the actual plaster,
patching materials and preparation to a room surface, including complete
constituents and thickness within its owning allocation. Confirm the supplied
powder shade and Fixative revision, applicable instructions and any retained
auxiliaries. Assess colour, texture, adhesion, chalking/cleaning and repair on a
representative substrate before adoption; no sample or physical test is claimed.

The manufacturer's chemical bond to the mineral substrate directs the renewal
study toward recoating and local substrate repair. That is a design consequence,
not proof of invisible patching, an established maintenance interval or a
service-life guarantee.
IAS's independent trim releases and movement/boundary duties remain intact.
No fire/acoustic assembly, wet-room coating, timber-joinery finish or complete
room is accepted by this component result.
