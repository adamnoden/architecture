## Result and next decision

This packet establishes a **reproducible failure of the proposed300 makeup route in the connected room network**, while distinguishing normal whole-house conservation from preferred equal room share. The350 challenger has little clean boost margin; the400 challenger has better capacity but fails the inherited indoor box and has only a conditional staged first-release route. The12kW reference fails30K all-boost heat, while14kW restores whole-house arithmetic without rescuing the kitchen emitter at65/55 under all local boost. The next design comparison must jointly qualify a complete400 bore/550 formed/600 face territory, tested room/fan/capture/control distribution and classified transfer or balanced-room-supply route, actual mounted emitter and delivered plant at the required weather/water condition. These are not authorised openings, mounted radiators, conditioned air plant or house/material exceptions. Site, terminal/fan/door curves, pressure/fouling tolerance, cooker capture, support/weather/fire, hydraulics and whole-life first release determine selection. A failure to join them rejects the conditional package rather than lowering its duties.
