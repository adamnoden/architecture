## Result and next decision

This packet establishes a **reproducible failure of the proposed300 makeup route in the connected room network**, while distinguishing normal whole-house conservation from preferred equal room share. The next design comparison should jointly test a complete400 clear bore/550 formed/600 face territory and an actual classified transfer or balanced-room-supply route, then size local heat at the resulting maximum direct intake. Its wall face and nominal H700 fit are geometric stress screens, not an authorised opening, mounted radiator, new conditioned air plant or house/material exception. The source's actual site, installed terminal/fan/door curves, room pollutant flows, cooker capture/controls, support/weather/fire, radiator/hydraulic output and whole-life first release determine selection. A failure to join them rejects the conditional package rather than lowering its duties.
