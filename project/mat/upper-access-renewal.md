# Upper access and the maintainable E joint

**6 November 2026 — project-authored reference, not released for construction.** The preferred method is a same-level two-piece E channel with an accessible, side-bolted U-flange. The [numeric control](studies/upper-access-renewal.json), [preferred producer](studies/upper-access-renewal.py) and [results](studies/upper-access-renewal.results.json) and [inspection figure](studies/upper-access-renewal.svg) now describe and test this joint. The retired two-level receiver data remain a labelled comparison only; their earlier pass is not evidence for this method.

## Decision and maintenance duty

Keep a fixed outdoor walking deck at elevation 0 mm with 180 mm structural territory; outdoor covers do not inherit occupied stone/screed/glass/RC layers. No person or 900 mm high hinged panel travels through the rejected −900 mm deck/−630 mm beam space. E work starts only with N intact and independently verified for its assigned full duty, E isolated/drained and guards installed before opening covers. A concurrent N failure is not invented to require a second E receiver.

The extra lower receiver is withdrawn as disproportionate. A single 4700 mm drawer would remove the joint but entails a 4.7 m handling field ending around 18200 mm; an illustrative 150 mm box-frame/pan screen is about 160 kg before hardware. Mechanical handling could carry it, but that does not earn its footprint or make a two-person hand lift acceptable. The accessible joint uses the actual 1467.5 mm bay between the spine and outer support instead.

## Stiffness, seal and leakage

The U-frame screen assumes E = 190 GPa, 8 kN total preload and 4 kN distributed bottom reaction. It gives 0.09694 mm bottom/0.06314 mm side deflection and 12.24/28.79 MPa nominal stress. Independent review confirmed the arithmetic and orientations. These simplified supported-beam/cantilever cases do not establish real preload distribution, welded corner stiffness, local faceplate/crush behaviour, allowable stress or uniform gasket contact.

A 0.5 mm annealed-copper U gasket is an explicit project material hypothesis, not a supplied or admitted seal. Actual material state, faces, compression, movement, corrosion, tightening sequence and water testing remain open. No synthetic substitution or seal-free watertightness is inferred. Retain/capture the gasket during initial OUTER separation and remove it after the faces are accessible. Reinstatement fits/captures the replacement on the exposed INNER face before OUTER approaches; bolting follows closure.

The local exterior catch tray sits 25 mm below the 150 mm carriage; it is not a second full E receiver. Its floor follows bed − 275 mm. The tray now extends to its integral terminal outlet at x = 10250 mm, with no unexplained 50 mm gap. Its floor drops openly into the lower spigot invert. A separate 80 mm OD/3 mm wall pipe starts at x = 10250 mm/invert −1125 mm and falls to x = 13500 mm/invert −1175 mm, with a receiving-water condition below −1300 mm. It runs between the two longitudinal rails, clear of the finite supports, rather than borrowing the foul route or an unexplained centreline. Pipe/support low point gives a route-local D lower bound of 1688 mm; proposed local D = 1800 mm is not an actual site/global-foundation selection. Capture capacity, outlet/site applicability and actual first harm remain unearned; qualified first-harm margin stays null.

## Bounded metal-gasket feasibility screen

The frozen stiffness case is **8 kN TOTAL across four bolts**, not 8 kN per bolt. Even ignoring both side legs, the 600 mm wide bottom with 20 mm land has 12000 mm² contact area, so its optimistic average pressure is at most 8000 N / 12000 mm² = 0.667 MPa; real U-land average is lower. This does not establish continuous contact.

[KLINGER metal-profile guidance](https://www.klinger-awschultze.de/en/metal-profile-gaskets/) discloses solid-metal 0.5–1 mm thicknesses where flange finish/evenness permits, special shapes and copper materials. Its A1 table gives copper at 20°C deformation/upper pressure limits of 135/300 MPa, and warns that even slight flange twist can upset flat-metal sealing. Those are manufacturer profile values, not a water-channel acceptance criterion. They nevertheless provide no basis for claiming this broad flat copper land seals at the authored preload. Thickness fit is plausible; seating feasibility remains **unearned**. Lower hydraulic pressure does not create missing assembly contact.

The same source explains that convex/narrow profiles reduce required force, but no disclosed formed profile has been shown to fit this exact 0.5 mm slot, open U shape and reversible motion. No coating, polymer filler, paste, preload increase or narrower land is silently admitted. This is a separate source screen, not a change to frozen HAD geometry or a physical seal pass.

## One cold, replaceable functional-residue comparator

**Comparison only; no owner exception or frozen-geometry change.** [Rogers BISCO HT-800](https://www.rogerscorp.com/-/media/project/rogerscorp/documents/elastomeric-material-solutions/bisco/english/data-sheets/180-070-ht-800---medium-cellular-silicone.pdf) is cellular silicone, with 0.79 mm stock, 352 kg/m³ typical density (300–473 kg/m³ specification) and 41–97 kPa CFD. The actual downloaded manufacturer sheet is 0826-PDF, publication 180-070, SHA256 `94bf07b55f09b265b579c6d8814c48070ca3f072d424cc675da173adc8c4033f`; the web text cache still shows 1123. Its 0.79 mm thickness tolerance is +0.381/−0.102 mm. [UK converter Parafix HT-800-0.8](https://parafix.com/product/rogers-ht-800-0-8/) identifies adhesive NONE and 25% compression deflection 62 kPa. The silicone formulation/additives remain incompletely disclosed; flame/FDA statements do not clear MAT policy.

At the existing 0.5 mm closed gap, nominal 0.79 mm stock compresses 36.7%; published thickness extremes imply 27.3–57.3%. A stated illustrative 20 mm × 1100 mm U strip has 0.022 m² area: 6.12 g at nominal thickness/density, 12.19 g at maximum thickness/density. These are comparator quantities, not an installed-house schedule. At the converter's 25% test point, area × 62 kPa gives 1.364 kN; that does not predict force at 36.7% or permit applying the entire 8 kN directly to foam without compression control.

[Rogers sealing guide](https://rogerscorp.com/-/media/project/rogerscorp/documents/elastomeric-material-solutions/bisco/english/technical-information/sealing-design-guide.pdf), printed p. 19, reports water tests using 3.175 mm silicone and 6.35 mm width, including 10.3 kPa. It does not qualify this thin 0.79 mm open-U joint. This is a credible, small, cold-replaceable residue challenger to flat copper, but requires an explicit successor compression-stop/load-bypass and retention detail, measured thickness/face tolerance and exact thin-stock seal test. No increased gap/preload, adhesive or automatic polymer exception is adopted. The reversible first-release path survives only if that new retention detail stays within its checked envelopes. The subsequent [coupled seal/return/weather comparison](seal-and-weather-materials.md) rejects unrestricted catalogue-stock substitution: no fixed gap keeps its full published thickness tolerance inside the authored 25–50% comparison window. It also distinguishes a much lighter weather sheet from a compatible complete temporary roof.

## Both modules, storage and reversal

OUTER must remain removed while INNER is drawn. Reinstating it first would block the inner path. Full profiles and the 30 mm draw connector are checked against the outboard operator field, with the complete 920 mm coupling, not the 763 mm carrier proxy.

A temporary static two-tier rack stores outer below and inner above, with no load on either wet pan. Lower cradle is 100 mm high; complete upper 100 mm deep bearers seat at elevations 450–550 mm. Fork cheeks end at elevation 600 mm and stand outside the payload; there is no assumed slot through a solid post. Rack assembly follows cover opening; its footprint is empty during cover-hook motion. Upper bearers are ABSENT during first placement, inserted unloaded only after outer is landed and restrained. Both loads lift 1700 mm, clearing the actual 600 mm high post/fork assembly before lateral transfer. Base fixity, chocks and caps act on the frame; the two upper bearers carry the upper frame independently. The component 5 kN reference gives 0.2101 mm/19.79 MPa for the stated 1100 mm simply-supported bearers; actual load sharing, restraints, bases, deck and wind remain qualification duties.

A separate 2700 × 1200 mm handover pad is required. Outgoing INNER must leave that pad before incoming INNER arrives; INNER reinstates first, upper bearers are removed unloaded, then OUTER is exchanged/reinstated. The actual raised approach and transport/handover route remain site acceptance conditions, not silently assumed land. Operators vacate the entire load sweep and never stand below a suspended module. Rack dismantling precedes cover closing.

This is the selected development method for the bounded first-release question, with explicit project geometry and limited internal checks. It is not MAT-ENVELOPE completion, a whole-house pass, a supplied lifting kit, physical seal acceptance or permission to build. HAD must consume this exact assembly and rebind the proof to its current owning geometry before adoption.
