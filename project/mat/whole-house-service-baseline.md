## Working selection and falsifiers

Develop continuous local mechanical extract at the six actual wet rooms, measured background supply terminals at the four bedrooms/study/living room, a separate cooker hood, low-temperature surface radiators, one outdoor air-to-water monobloc and one 300-litre stored hot-water station. This selects a service architecture for design testing, not manufacturer apparatus or exceptions. No combustion plant, gas supply, active groundwater pumping, concealed floor heating, free attic plant, ornamental-water circuit, battery, EV charger or dedicated cooling is assumed. The omitted optional functions can be added only with their own inventory and route cost. Electrical cooking, laundry and refrigeration remain explicit duties.

Natural/background ventilation with intermittent extract has fewer continuously powered parts but is a poor baseline for the intended airtight fabric; the current F guidance restricts its simple route to less airtight dwellings. Central MEV needs a network across the protected ring or an unproved roof service zone. Balanced MVHR recovers ventilation heat and controls intake, but adds supply ducts, filters, exchanger, condensate and appliance residues. The attic is an unproved roof void, with no admitted warm floor, headroom or replacement route. Local continuous extract trades six maintainable powered stations and unrecovered heat for short direct outer-wall paths and no ventilation duct across the protected ring. The quantified thermal penalty must fit the eventual energy model; failure triggers MVHR with a real new architectural distribution proposal, not an invented attic.

The protected-stair/ring interface is separately pinned to HAD `a324a50d047843603d6fc275c4062bc9d0c1cb90`, JSON blob `1412e628aa6b539664c8f5efd507595623cf2a21`; the room/furniture source remains c83 with the explicit rewinds below. Its current intent is E20+ doors at the protected boundary; Sa/smoke requirements are not fixed. Normal ventilation transfer requires specific classified doorsets supporting the necessary finished threshold free area. E20 alone proves no permitted undercut. Cutting a selected fire door, omitting seals or claiming stair air as an unexamined plenum is prohibited.

Six fan assemblies, one monobloc/indoor hydraulic station, one300L store and its safety kit, fourteen cold/nine hot endpoints and all matched waste/valve kits, complete electrical/fire/controls, and eightPV modules plus inverter are explicit residual groups.
