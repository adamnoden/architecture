**Development checkpoint, 1 November 2026. No whole-system, product, material, statutory or site acceptance.**

The bounded question is whether one complete, inspectable environmental and domestic-service arrangement can replace the inherited unselected heat-pump/MVHR examples. ## Working selection and falsifiers

Develop continuous local mechanical extract at the six actual wet rooms, measured background supply terminals at the four bedrooms/study/living room, a separate cooker hood, low-temperature surface radiators, one outdoor air-to-water monobloc and one 300-litre stored hot-water station. This selects a service architecture for design testing, not manufacturer apparatus or exceptions. No combustion plant, gas supply, active groundwater pumping, concealed floor heating, free attic plant, ornamental-water circuit, solar array, battery, EV charger or dedicated cooling is assumed. The omitted optional functions can be added only with their own inventory and route cost. Electrical cooking, laundry and refrigeration remain explicit duties.

Natural/background ventilation with intermittent extract has fewer continuously powered parts but is a poor baseline for the intended airtight fabric; the current F guidance restricts its simple route to less airtight dwellings. Central MEV needs a network across the protected ring or an unproved roof service zone. Balanced MVHR recovers ventilation heat and controls intake, but adds supply ducts, filters, exchanger, condensate and appliance residues. The attic is an unproved roof void, with no admitted warm floor, headroom or replacement route. Local continuous extract trades six maintainable powered stations and unrecovered heat for short direct outer-wall paths and no ventilation duct across the protected ring. The quantified thermal penalty must fit the eventual energy model; failure triggers MVHR with a real new architectural distribution proposal, not an invented attic.

HAD's forthcoming protected-stair/ring revision is an interface, not yet this packet's frozen source. Its current intent is E20+ doors at the protected boundary; Sa/smoke requirements are not fixed. Normal ventilation transfer requires specific classified doorsets supporting the necessary finished threshold free area. E20 alone proves no permitted undercut. Cutting a selected fire door, omitting seals or claiming stair air as an unexamined plenum is prohibited.

## Complete duty boundary

The schedule must include continuous ventilation and purge, cooker capture and makeup, room heat and controls, stored hot water and hygiene/scald protection, cold supply and backflow, four WCs/four basins/two showers/one bath/two sinks/one washer, foul vents and gravity outfall, rainwater-owner interfaces, heat-pump condensate, cylinder safety discharge, power/protection/earthing, lighting, detection, communications and every maintenance isolation. Kitchen/utility sink and washer positions are proposals where not drawn, not recovered fixtures. Dishwasher, outdoor tap, irrigation and other discretionary loads are excluded explicitly rather than silently invented.

Detailed finite routes, terminal positions, heat sensitivities, operation/renewal envelopes and constituent residue schedule are being generated in the companion study. Site supply/soil/outfall, pressure/noise/climate and exact apparatus remain named decision inputs. This checkpoint deliberately makes no complete route pass.
