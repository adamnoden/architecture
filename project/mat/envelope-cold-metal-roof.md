# A cold metal roof: remove the backing without deleting the roof

**1 November 2026 · MAT-ENVELOPE bounded alternatives result.** Reference design — not released for construction. This compares complete functions and constituent choices for the [current roof candidates](../../docs/research/roof-system-candidates.md), under the [material policy](../../docs/reference-house/material-policy.md). It does not select a roof, change the house or close the envelope packet.

## Decision

Develop **Natural VMZINC on directly supported, untreated open-gap softwood boards, with a continuously ventilated cold space and a separately resolved ceiling thermal/air boundary**, as the first metal-roof material challenger. Its important earned advantage is removing the coated backing and plywood/slipsheet dependency immediately beneath the metal. It is **not an established complete non-synthetic roof**: weather protection at insulation level, warm-side air/vapour continuity and the wall-head geometry still decide admission.

Reject the existing warm VMZINC representative as an unmodified no-synthetic solution because its Aludex Max polymer layer is positively identified. Reject “change the skin to copper and omit everything beneath it” as an evidenced CDA construction. Keep the discrete tile/slate family as the ordinary repair control, with its secondary water layer intact until a complete alternative earns that duty. Neither a rejected polymer package nor a thinner roof makes an owner exception automatic.

Do not create a second compatibility schedule. [RWT's shared material record](../../docs/research/roof-rainwater-material-compatibility.md) remains authoritative. This result tests the actual interfaces needed by this challenger and sends any eventual selected revision back to ROF/RWT.

## The wall head spends the depth

The [section source](../../compiler/reference-house/georgian/section-source.ts) puts the ceiling-member top at +6690. The [current revision](../../compiler/reference-house/georgian/layout-revision.ts), through its held section geometry, retains +6800 exterior eaves and 25° pitch. At the inner cavity edge, **x = 102.5 + 160 = 262.5** inward from the outer weather face, the nominal roof top is `6800 + 262.5 tan(25°) = 6922.406`. The vertical space above ceiling-member top is therefore **232.406**.

An intentionally optimistic screen counts only horizontal insulation plus `(50 vent + 24 boards + 0.7 metal) / cos(25°)`. It excludes secondary drainage/support, clips, seams, tolerance, steel framing and special closure depth. The source 300 vertical roof band is not extra space to add again.

| Insulation probe | Bare core R = t/0.041, m²K/W | Already-spent vertical depth, mm | Nominal residual at x262.5, mm |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 150 | 3.659 | 232.422 | About zero |
| 200 | 4.878 | 282.422 | −50 |
| 250, e.g. 100 + 150 | 6.098 | 332.422 | −100 |

These unrounded intermediate values make the arithmetic recoverable; the roughly 0.017 mm difference in the first row is **not a physical tolerance failure**. It shows no useful margin even before omitted functions. R is a bare-material calculation, not the sheet's rounded declared RD or a whole-roof U-value. The point is a junction probe, not a claim that the actual thermal line or member support has been selected there.

**Reject an unchanged-depth, full-thickness 200 mm ceiling insulation/drop-in at this trial wall-head connection.** About 50 mm additional local height is already needed before the omitted functions. Do not meet the drawing by tapering away required insulation, blocking the vent or borrowing a cavity. Compare an explicit roof/eaves rise, lowered ceiling, different thermal boundary or different complete roof family; each changes architectural/energy/support consequences and needs its own owning source decision. The field above the room may fit while the wall head fails. Storage decks cannot be credited with free clearance or used as roof support.

## Copper boundary, failure and repair

Keep the shared compatibility record's distinctions: copper runoff onto zinc and wet direct contact are excluded; zinc draining toward a copper collector is a different directional case and still needs an actual separated joint. Develop a **free zinc drip above an independent copper collection line**, without a copper apron turned back beneath the zinc. Locate full-water/blocked receiver levels, splash and capillary return before claiming separation. A dry drawing gap alone supplies no backwater or contamination pass. Route the insulation-side drainage independently to an admitted outward edge without sealing off ventilation; identify whether the same blocked receiver defeats both paths.

Opening a standing seam for board or buried-layer renewal disturbs a wider strip than exchanging one tile. Provide safe external working geography, temporary water control, board removal, clip reinstatement and inspectable drying before enclosure; no inside-only renewal is promised. Keep receiver replacement clear of the moving hem. A fully soldered pan or a second complete metal catch roof may provide useful specific duties, but it adds mass, movement joints and fabrication/renewal work. **Do not select a second roof merely to replace an undisclosed sheet** without the comparison earning that burden.

## Result and exact remaining acceptance decisions

This comparison earns the unbacked/open-board challenger, rejects three material shortcuts and identifies a located wall-head failure at the 200 mm probe. It supports **conditional cold-roof development**, rather than accepting the warm polymer package or declaring all non-synthetic roofs impossible.

ROF/BCR must resolve the exact permitted insulation-protection variant and wall-head/hip/valley section before fixing the roof family; MAT must resolve all retained layers, especially R7's interlayer bond and any joint/AVCL products before material selection. MRA must attack failed skin, blocked collection, air leakage and wet construction against those actual sections before technical convergence. STR owns support/uplift/secondary timber and any eave-level revision before structural issue. RWT owns receiver water levels and replacement separation before collection selection; DPL supplies actual climate/exposure and receiving conditions before site-dependent acceptance. DEL/owner cost work needs like-for-like fabrication, access and renewal pricing before procurement. None is deferred generically to an unspecified professional, and none prevents the bounded internal comparison completed here.

**No whole roof, product schedule, owner exception, house geometry, packet completion or maturity is selected.** R01–R05 remain explicit mixed constituent questions. No new compatibility table, moisture matrix, compiler or alternative roof authority is created.
