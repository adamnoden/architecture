It is unadopted and not silently substituted for accepted RH-LAYOUT-REVISION-02. The protected-route revision is not yet frozen: its exact room boundaries, doors, glazing, penetrations and roof closure must be joined before rating occurrences.

Develop ordinary mineral wall/floor fields and mechanically released solid joinery. Keep structural fabric, service supports and water/fire/acoustic boundaries independent of decorative faces. Use a complete rated assembly where a real duty requires it, retaining its mandatory constituents even when the material preference cannot yet be satisfied. No “hidden”, “small” or “certified” polymer exception is inferred.
