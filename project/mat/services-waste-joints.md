**31 October 2026. Bounded S02 comparison from main `2512f116e277d3ab724fd0bcf0f13ba1d04533c3`; MAT-SERVICES remains planned.** This advances the [single constituent register](constituent-audit.md) under the [material policy](../../docs/reference-house/material-policy.md). No pipe size, installed system, material exception or service life is selected.

## Decision

**Carry a glass-fibre/lead-wool caulked socket forward as a material challenger, with its exact current system and complete constituents still to establish.** Manufacturer evidence concerns actual soil and waste pipework; it is not an invented transfer from rainwater. Current evidence also prevents treating every traditional cast-iron system as this construction: Hargreaves Premier's published joint recommends a polymer sealant. Its bitumen coating and other accessories need their own disclosure.

**Develop a separately supported, replaceable straight pipe section at an accessible service front.** A useful comparison must clear both retained ends before the section can move out. The named stainless slip coupling supplies a real mechanism to investigate; it remains a synthetic-sealed comparator. An ordinary socket, an opened clamp or a maintenance-panel outline alone does not establish that first movement.

The examined Premier sealant-jointed, Halifax epoxy/EPDM and EuroPipe elastomer-sealed packages do not pass the default material policy as supplied. Required seals and protective coatings cannot simply be removed. No complete no-synthetic alternative has yet been established.

The November 2025 manual requires joint lubrication. This establishes a movement and consumable duty; it does not prove removal between two immovable opposed ends. The exact present lubricant formulation remains undisclosed here.

## First movement and boundary retention

Reuse the [water-joint study's distinction between ordinary service and fixed-end repair](services-water-joints.md#separate-the-plant-service-break-from-pipe-repair). For S02, the next located section must show the **pipe's real end condition and the complete sealing sleeve**, not only a removable metal band.

| Joint case | Specific evidence and design consequence |
|---|---|
| Ordinary EuroPipe socket | Its engaged spigot needs actual separation movement. Two opposed fixed ends do not supply that movement merely because both sockets are visible. A controlled release, dedicated repair fitting or explicit cut-and-renew operation must be drawn |
| EuroPipe 842 slip coupling | Develop two accessible couplings around a plain-ended, branch-free removable section. Show the actual parked position, seal engagement, supports and first axial movement that clears each fixed end before lateral extraction. The named product makes this a real enquiry; no required parking distance or approved sequence is inferred from 130 or 97 |
| Halifax split-coupling comparator | The current guide p68 requires support above/below the insertion, allows a cut-out **15 mm taller than the inserted fitting** for that joint system, and retains cut-end coating repair and subsequent testing. This is evidence for that bounded modification, not generic extraction clearance or a transfer to the materially preferred challenger |
| Caulked LCC challenger | Provide access to release and renew the actual joint media and withdraw the pipe, or explicitly replace a bounded section. No reviewed source establishes a reversible release equivalent to the 842. Cost, workmanship, affected coatings and repair occupation remain unquantified |

For a vertical section, the retained upper stack and the moving section need independent support before a joint is released. The access face is not the stack support. Locate the repair on the accessible side of a separately designed floor/shaft boundary where the actual branch and support geometry permits; do not make disturbance of that boundary an unexplained first step. Required fire/acoustic performance and its reinstatement remain **S10** duties. A branch, base bend, penetration or whole stack adds obligations beyond this first straight-section comparison.

The located detail must account for controlled use of upstream appliances, residual contents, protection of retained ends and adjacent services, reinstated joints and any disturbed boundary, and access to inspect/dry affected fabric. No universal test pressure, tool envelope or installation procedure is prescribed by this study.

## Handoff and verification

The useful next decision is a **current, fully disclosed caulked-joint package and its repair section**, compared against the actual space/workmanship burden of the mechanically released comparator. Bind the socket, pipe, packing, lead, coatings, inspection-door seals, supports and all transition parts to one applicable system. Then draw first release, removal and reinstatement through the owning IAS/BCR service geography. If a permitted whole assembly cannot be established, change the service arrangement or raise a concrete exception through the existing material policy; no exception is approved here.
