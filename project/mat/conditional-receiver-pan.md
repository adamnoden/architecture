# Conditional whole-network receiver-following pan

The [plan](studies/conditional-receiver-pan.svg), [illustrative source-coordinate section](studies/conditional-receiver-pan-section.svg), [finite surface mesh](studies/conditional-receiver-pan.json) and [reproducer](studies/conditional-receiver-pan.py) test one connected, stepped pan following the HAD RC200 soffit and the **historical** STR lower-grid network. STR confirms there is no authored current replacement for its court-S receiver or entrance nodes; this is a conditional exercise, not selection of that network or reconciliation to the current structural design. It must not be combined as if its −630/−750 receiver soffits were the newer SW cold-grid soffits or its court nodes were the unadopted B pad.

Start with the MAT bounding house domain, exclude its court and loggia, and add the **x5060..6940, y3987.5..4250** threshold trial and one E-box wall-crossing witness **x7750..8167.5, y6623.5..7626.5**. The E-box width and −839.5 underside come from the separate MAT service trial; the finite 417.5 mm crossing length is a geometric witness, not a surveyed or selected service route. The mesh lays a nominal sheet top at **−380** below the HAD slab wherever there is no represented receiver. It follows the underside of each historical 600 mm-wide route at **−630**, extends across every represented 600-square cap node and follows the four deeper represented nodes to **−750**. Where the E-box witness meets the domain it drops to its **−839.5** underside and adds that short witness area to the domain. At every internal step it includes the vertical return between the adjoining levels. Where routes cross or meet nodes, their rectangle union has a single lower surface and perimeter, rather than independent, open-ended U pans. The source lists 33 axis-aligned routes and 58 cap nodes. STR independently matched all 33 routes, 58 nodes and four deeper node locations, and checked the stated cell-and-riser connectivity. The axis-end rectangles and square node bounds are the stated idealized geometry; actual beam sections, radii, tolerances, cap transitions, cast stages and reinforcement are not inferred. Its plan cells are connected and the explicit vertical faces connect adjacent levels; that mathematical topology is not a welded assembly or a leak test.

The interior vertical returns are **129.844 m** of 250 mm steps, **2.340 m** of 120 mm steps, and **1.313 m** of 209.5 mm steps around the E-box witness, or **33.017 m²** gross vertical area. These numbers exclude every boundary upstand, penetration, roof connection, folds, seams, overlap, collar and waste. Horizontal plus vertical ideal area is **137.683 m²**; at 1 mm and 7,900 kg/m³, the sheet-only sensitivity is **1,087.7 kg**, not a cut list or actual supported weight. The mesh leaves **64.290 m** of plan perimeter open, including courtyard, loggia, exterior and threshold boundaries. It cannot claim a continuous envelope until finite turns, frame connections, drainage, services and roof connection are designed. The E-box witness is only one segment; its ends, drainage, W1/W2 renewability, the north box, movement features, structural penetrations and the SW collar are not closed by this mesh and require new surfaces and route changes.

This joined topology exposes a major material and structural incompatibility. The separate MAT comparator has mineral DPC **−765..−615**, AAC **−605..−390** and warm receiver soffit **−380**. A 1 mm sheet immediately below a −630 historical receiver would be **−631..−630**, dividing the nominal mineral band into **134 mm below and 15 mm above**. Under a −750 node it would be **−751..−750**, leaving only **14 mm below and 135 mm above**. The historical concrete itself occupies the upper 15 or 135 mm of that nominal mineral band, respectively, so this is not a mere sheet-thickness substitution. The concrete and mineral recipes are unreconciled. No bonding, bearing, capillary break, compressive strength or thermal performance survives by assumption; the actual structural and materials owners must redesign the support and cold/warm interfaces together.

The E-box witness adds an important conflict: its full **0.4187525 m²** plan lies within the historical court-E 600 mm route footprint, while the box underside is **209.5 mm lower** than its historical soffit. Of that plan, **0.155465 m²** replaces −630 surface inside the earlier clipped floor domain; the remaining **0.2632875 m²** was outside that domain. The box envelope includes void and insulation as well as RC sides, so its plan overlap is not a solid-concrete intersection; any actual 3D clash also depends on the box top and both unadopted constructions. Dropping the mesh to −839.5 produces a mathematical return, not a resolution of the overlapping receiver and box envelopes or a load path. The 1 mm sheet below the box reaches −840.5, **75.5 mm below** the old MAT DPC bottom −765; the actual void, ground, support and moisture interfaces there are unestablished. Its 417.5 mm crossing is a finite witness whose exposed end is not connected to an outside service route. W1/W2, their removable access and drainage cannot be inferred from the metal below.

The surface also changes a vast amount of construction. At each −380/−630 step, temporary support must follow the 250 mm downstand and meet slab backing without gaps; at −750 nodes a further 120 mm return is needed. Field joints and each three-dimensional branch need weld access and inspection before being covered, while allowing placement and compaction around real reinforcement and avoiding damage to mineral layers. A realistic temporary support, retained fixing, release/repair route and staged integrity test have not been drawn. The previously tested 1 m prop cell cannot simply be copied beneath the downstands or their intersections. Cast concrete bears on the sheet in this hypothesis; slip, shear transfer, shrinkage, differential movement, corrosion, fire, product compatibility and uplift/compression routes require design. No composite action is credited.

The court-S return in this connected conditional model avoids the local horizontal field cutting through that historical downstand by following its underside and rising at network boundaries; it does **not** solve the door. At the trial threshold's boundary the surface is still at the conditional receiver depth, with no located frame, sill, load-bearing junction or drainage path. It also does not establish a physical metal connection at the unselected corner roof hip. The benefit is a finite network and explicit returns to coordinate with STR and the opening owner; a continuous actual air, gas, moisture or structural boundary remains unproven.
