## Decision

Keep the20 L/min Utility and45 L/min Plant reference duties and genuinely independent Lodge/indoor isolation. The existing Plant receiver and tall cabinet remain rejected. Do not adopt a raised floor, whole-room tank or extra annex merely to obtain a zero-water claim that the governing moisture brief does not require.

[MRA-00](../mra/mra-00-moisture-constitution-threat-model.md#4-credible-failure-boundary) tests a credible single defect with the remainder ordinarily serviceable and a bounded concurrent maintenance/environmental case. Its target is prolonged concealed attack on permanent fabric. Local, visible and dryable wetting is allowed. Simultaneous independent ruptures with both independent exits blocked remain useful declared stress limits; they are not a new universal pre-site acceptance criterion. Conversely, an unoccupied house is a credible concurrent state: do not assume a person, alarm, powered pump or automatic valve saves an otherwise concealed wet path.

Owner direction[#1477](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/1477) specifically retains the former cabinet comparison with both lower drains shut and half the daylight throat open. Those may be intentional maintenance-cock states, so this requirement is not dismissed as independent N+2 failure. A successor must map each former drain/overflow/isolation function to its actual new state before superseding that apparatus-specific case.

The [inlet completion boundary](inlet-resolution.md#evidence-boundary-and-graph-disposition) distinguishes a coherent pre-site route, isolation, supported renewal and bounded failure interface from commissioned/physical/product acceptance. Neither a site survey nor a prototype will cure a known geometry conflict. Missing construction acceptance does not by itself prohibit an honestly qualified Working Edition proposition.
