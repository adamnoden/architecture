# HAD-03 — one allocated court passage design, with the plant room retained

**1 November 2026. Reference design — not released for construction. Assumptions and outstanding verification accompany the proposal; internal checks do not constitute external assurance or certification.**

## Architectural decision

Develop **north N plus pantry E-W1**, rather than continuing to alternate between a full crawlable undercroft and an unspecified thin channel. Reserve an accessible **600 mm floor strip** along x 5700–6300 from the north court to the garden, and y 6825–7425 from the east court across gallery, service spine and pantry. Retain the accepted 12 × 12 m shell, courtyard, four-bedroom programme, all room partitions and opening apertures. Make a **300 mm whole-house relative-level rise** an explicit unadopted source rewind, with front and garden approach consequences; do not present it as already available site head.

This is a spatial allocation and a reasoned next reference design, **not selection of a working rainwater system**. The [plan](studies/court-local-portals-plan.svg), [paired longitudinal/transverse sections](studies/court-local-portals-sections.svg) and [exact ledger](studies/court-local-portals.json) make its carrier, cover, room exclusions and repair direction inspectable. They replace neither the current house nor the complete installed floor/threshold design. The same [existing source-bound generator](studies/court-egress-study.mjs) guards current intersections and checks all committed drawings; the earlier B comparison remains a dated comparator.

The recovered [east-pier study](had-03-east-egress-pier-options.md) is useful evidence: a 300 mm piece at y 6000 lies within both solid-pier projections. It does **not** reserve space for the cover, tools or separate supports. At the present **600 mm access width**, its court-pier shoulders reduce to **50 mm nominally each side**, while its whole 600 mm band crosses the fixed `plantDry` reservation. Its overlap with `plantWorking` is different: a proved load-capable closed cover may be working floor, although opening it requires a controlled maintenance state. Fixed equipment obstructing the first release cannot be treated that way. Therefore **reject E-P as a drop-in retaining today's plant arrangement**. A genuinely repacked plant room remains a separate possible rewind, not a mandatory detour before testing the pantry allocation.

The pantry allocation costs storage: its source y 6650–8105 room band leaves **175 mm south and 680 mm north** of the access strip, before actual fit/finishes. Do not call the 175 mm strip a useful cabinet, place fixed bases over covers, or count the whole pantry floor twice. The source contains no fitted pantry storage model to preserve. The 600 mm E-W1 band extends **75 mm beyond each end of the 450 mm common window projection**; this intentionally abandons any claim of a structurally clear under-window slot. Both window bases and the 125 mm spine require independently supported bridge caps/load paths. The normal N strip similarly passes under both 2400 mm door thresholds. The water carrier supports none of them.

## Repair is an operation, not a future access promise

Room-floor covers are divided at walls/spine and into pieces **no longer than 500 mm**; permanent wall crossings have independently supported bridge caps. The service carrier is also segmented at no more than 500 mm, with an authored **300 mm gross detachable-joint envelope** inside the 600 mm corridor and 150 mm side tool reservations. Room pieces have **450 mm total vertical lifting travel** through removed covers, enough to bring the downstream bottom above FFL; 200 mm would not suffice. **Permanent bridge caps stay in place.** A piece beneath one must first translate 500 mm, following the 1% slope (5 mm change in z), into an already emptied adjacent piece position, then lift 450 mm in the opened floor. This needs sequential removal of the neighbor and an open room strip; it is not universal vertical-only renewal. No 4.25 m pull through a buried sleeve is assumed. Exact flange bolts, splice separation and tool rotation may still invalidate the reservations.

## Approach, rejection criteria and completion boundary

A 300 mm additional rise takes **6000 mm horizontal run at an illustrative1:20**, before turns/landings. That arithmetic does not establish access compliance, available plot length, a garden terrace or a surveyed high-water level. The whole house, roofs, windows and upper floors would move together in an eventual adopted rewind; do not raise just the ground floor and silently consume existing storey height or alter stair geometry. Current accepted source and applicability remain unchanged.

Reject or revise this **single candidate**, rather than multiplying thinner variants, if the complete floor/portal exceeds the reserved depth, first release cannot remain accessible, actual pan/jamb/weep joints are reached before full-duty E operates, a leak can enter inaccessible room fabric, carrier release consumes permanent support, the pantry loses required function, or actual lawful independent receivers/approaches cannot support the relative levels. RWT retains full assigned inflow and common-debris/high-tailwater testing; the provisional 87.5 mm outlet separation is not a substitute. STR, IAS/OAS, MAT and DPL retain the identified decisions, each at the actual point its evidence controls admission. Internal development of this design can continue now; external review is not used as a reason to stop that work.

**Result:** a concrete N/E room/cover/repair topology and bounded level rewind have replaced an undefined local-passage preference. E-P is rejected for the unchanged plant layout; E-W1's pantry cost and structural bridge obligation are explicit. The proposal has **not** earned a complete no-plastic dry floor, hydraulic acceptance or a selected house. **HAD-03 remains planned; RWT-06 remains blocked**, also preserving the outstanding whole-house room convergence obligations. This is neither a packet closeout nor new maturity credit.
