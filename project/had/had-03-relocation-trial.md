# HAD-03 — test the small en suite outside its original footprint

**Disposition:** a useful unadopted design direction, not a selected-room pass.
The [retained-footprint review](had-03-ensuite-fit-review.md) was followed by a
local relocation trial. It preserves the 12 × 12 m shell, courtyard, four bedrooms,
two en suites and every existing external aperture. It exposes further connected
route, door, storage and service questions rather than declaring them resolved.
No accepted source or interface contract is changed by this record.

This direction introduces a second local stack. It may be more proportionate
than forcing a110 soil route through an unproved shallow floor to preserve an

structural acceptance. The existing ground wet-floor reserve and upper wet-floor
build-up deficit remain unresolved; adjacency to a downstairs WC does not solve
either.

## Current decision and bounded continuation

Keep this relocation as the leading **trial to develop**, with the original
source still current. It has a plausible service adjacency and avoids moving the
external windows, but has not earned adoption or HAD-03 completion.

The next coordinated design pass must choose the actual bedroom base/headboard,
storage and window operations; establish a connected furnished route; resolve
the bedroom/sliding and wet-room doors with OAS; resolve the added cabinet and
wet section with IAS/BCR; then author one source rewind and regenerate all
affected plan, section, elevation, room, service and applicability projections.
A viable narrower source change may supersede this trial. No general
impossibility or mandatory programme reduction is claimed.

If the owner's required bed size or ordinary room use defeats the compact
solution, put the actual alternatives before the owner: retain the large bed
with a different room allocation, reduce the second en-suite programme, or
revisit another accepted spatial feature. The draft owner brief does not supply
those priorities. Do not treat an unspecified household preference as consent
to a smaller bed or a lost room feature.

Independent review checked the source extents, the70 mm connected-route failure,
manufacturer base/headboard evidence, door sweeps and the lower-WC/void service
boundary. This record is retained negative/conditional design evidence. It is
not a fitted layout, regulatory assessment or construction issue.
