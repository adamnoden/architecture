Those derived comparisons do not bound source-fed rupture flow. A smaller valve does not reduce the preserved9bar source case, stored release, backfeed or relief obligations.

The supplier's [generic bronze pressure-temperature chart](https://www.milwaukeevalve.com/wp-content/uploads/technical-service-information/BronzePressureTempChart.pdf) has no demonstrated exact UP105/C89833 correspondence. Do not borrow another valve's150SWP/300WOG hot curve. Exact pressure-temperature coverage for the whole adverse duty remains missing; 300WOG is not a dynamic shock qualification.

The below-axis body depth, orthogonal body envelope, actual pressure area, packing-nut across-flats, operating forces, body support lands and external resistance remain unknown. NPT-to28mm tails, separating unions, supplied sealing materials, first release travel, cold capture and replacement are unproved. Keep the complete BSA6T6kg mass as an explicitly labelled conservative **mass comparison control** if useful; neither it nor a fictitious copper valve member bounds compact-valve stiffness or joint forces.

Development conclusion: continue the simpler split-room network and retain this exact product as a bounded option. Do not invent its body shape to produce a fitting screen, reduce the water/contact-temperature duties or select it on mass alone.
