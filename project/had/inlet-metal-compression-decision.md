# Incoming water: metal compression and a removable valve module

**20 November 2026 — bounded decision: develop the metal-joint mechanism, but reject an immediate matched-system substitution.** A genuine28 mm compression alternative exists; a complete no-synthetic, potable, independently supported, ordinary-union inlet has **not** been established by the inspected products. No owner exception, product selection, active geometry or pressure method changes here.

The useful simplification is to exchange a supported valve module at permanent separating joints, avoiding routine press-tool docking and progressively longer cold cuts. Merely replacing press fittings with stopped compression couplers does not achieve that operation. Nor does a union remove the upper pipe's gravity/restraint duty.

This decision extends the dated [MAT water-joint comparison](../mat/services-water-joints.md) at the actual28 mm inlet size. The [current fixed proposal](fixed-inlet-entry-candidate.md), independent wet body and [compact support](fixed-inlet-receiver-development.md) remain their existing candidates. Wet-neck/back-support development can continue on the actual current pipe axes without waiting for another valve catalogue.

## A separating joint has to exist in the actual kit

The ordinary Conex olive seals around the tube and its body contains a stop. Loosening the nut does not remove the26 mm engagement or release an olive as an unobstructed28 mm bare spigot. The present L has orthogonal exterior and upper ends: it cannot be translated sideways through an engaged upper socket simply because the exterior nut is loose. A body-only transport drawing would conceal this first movement.

A real copper union witness is [Yorkshire YP11/08253,28 mm](https://aalberts-ips.co.uk/products/item/08253/), with its own metal cone interface. It is not the Conex301 joint. The newer [Endex2023 instructions, printed p.56](https://aalberts-ips.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Endex-09969088_56035915_Brochure_2023.pdf) describe UK BS1010 metal cone unions and qualify jointing compound/PTFE as **“if required”**; they also forbid treating different European unions as interchangeable. That is narrower than a universal instruction to add compound. It is still not permission to assume every supplied cone can be assembled dry, or to ignore solder/flux on its tube ends. Bind the exact union's instructions and mating section before claiming an all-metal separating assembly. No actual28 mm compression-ended central union with evidenced first-release travel was established here.

**The operation that would earn the simplification** has two supported, accessible separating faces surrounding the complete valve/tails; the permanent upper half remains independently supported while the module's nuts release. Exact cone/pilot engagement must permit the first movement, then the whole module must leave within the open hood. Nuts and counterholds must remain accessible without loosening a permanent seal, flexing the fixed pipe or unscrewing a valve from opposed stationary pipes. This is the missing operation in the inspected assembly, not a product already fitted to it.

Even that arrangement cannot cancel the upper load path. If the850 clamp and lower L leave, a29 mm sliding guide does not acquire axial grip. A retained upper union half must have a real independent support and the actual pipe-to-fitting load path; a pressure rating is not an external support rating. A positive valve/union-body bracket can be investigated from actual load-bearing geometry, but no nominal “union” supplies it. Whole upper-tube replacement remains a distinct supported task. Keep the current cold-keeper limitation until the actual handover is proved.

## Copper radius: a useful geometry result, not a silent material switch

[Yorkshire Copper Tube's primary guide, §3.4](https://yorkshirecopper.com/wp-content/uploads/YCT_BRO_techguide.pdf) supports machine bending half-hard R250 and appropriate adjustable machines through28 mm; above22 mm it does not guarantee spring bending. [ROTHENBERGER25628](https://us.rothenberger.com/us-en/products/bending-accessories/bendset-f-robend-3000-4000-28mm-25628/) provides a named28 mm/R112 former. Exact tube temper, wall and tooling compatibility still need their common supplied instruction. Do not treat R290 as the same cold-bending proposition or confuse inside radius with centreline radius.

The actual existing source-bound inlet method was run atR98 andR112 on coherent source072b90d3, with all other settings unchanged. This is explicitly a **geometry-only hypothesis**: its material/thermal calculations still use the existing stainless facts and are not copper evidence.

## Result for convergence

**Do not adopt either a hybrid Conex/VIR assembly or an imported graphite valve with improvised metric threads.** The inspected UK combination has a real PTFE residue; the positive graphite alternatives lack a complete matched28 mm union/thread/constituent operation. The ordinary301 removes the need for a press machine at its joint, but does not establish repeated cut-free module exchange or eliminate upper support.

The constructive result is narrower and useful: compatible28 mm stainless compression is a real option, copperR112 is not geometrically excluded, and pure metal/graphite valves exist as genuine product leads. Neither the8-seal Sanpress residue nor its press/repair architecture is an unavoidable law of this house. However, the actual no-synthetic matched replacement has not yet earned selection or deletion of its dependent duties. Keep the current runtime and wet-body work intact; retain this precise rejection instead of spending those unresolved benefits as an accomplished simplification.
