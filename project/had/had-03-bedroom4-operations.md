## Pan removal is a sequence

The [Duravit UK product source](https://pro.duravit.co.uk/pro/html/default/product-details.uk-en.html?nser=7120&product=94738)
identifies21150900002 as a back-to-wall pan with independent water supply,
355 ×560 ×385 and22kg. It is not a complete close-coupled suite. The cabinet
therefore needs an actual separate cistern, isolation, flush connection, soil
connection and service faces. A body-only cistern rectangle cannot stand in for
its brackets. Those details are being reconciled with IAS before the candidate
can be frozen.

The [located A3 maintenance study](studies/bedroom4-maintenance-study.svg) and
[coordinate/result record](studies/bedroom4-maintenance-study.json) reserve:

1. Isolation, drainage and floor protection, with the room door held open90.
2. Actual fixing/connector release followed by400 forward pan withdrawal. This
   is available task space, not a proven required connector disengagement stroke.
3. A separate northern helper field and front helper path; a rear helper cannot
   stand inside the cabinet. After the protected pan reaches2100/8845, the second
   person can move around the emptied fixture area to the rear regrip field.
4. A660 ×455 protected component envelope (560 ×355 ceramic plus50 each side)
   translated through the door. Fore/aft600 body fields are650 from the component
   centre; their paths are distinct from the component's swept rectangle.
5. A protected stage centred4500/8547.5 against the bedroom's south side. The
   front person returns north of the staged pan before turning south of the open
   door and reaching the now-clear cabinet east face at1200/9027.5.
6. Reverse transfer, fixing and connection reinstatement, restoration of the
   water boundary, then actual leak/function inspection before reuse.

The temporary return corridor is625 between protected pan and bed: **only25
total spare width beyond the600 circle**. It is not a simultaneous carrying or
occupied-bedroom claim. A north-west stage was rejected because the connected
route fails despite the apparent empty destination. The retained witnesses
check all complete axis segments; diagrams do not replace omitted bends with
diagonal shortcuts. The whole movement of the two people while lifting/lowering,
actual hand positions, safe handling of22kg ceramic and crossing the20 saddle
still require a real handling method. Neither a mass figure nor a clear plan
certifies lifting. No route down the stairs or to an external setdown is claimed.
