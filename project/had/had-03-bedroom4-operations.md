## Finished faces and the room door

IAS is developing a constitution for the proposed new partitions: 90 stud,
12.5 dry board and a 25 wet-side assembly. Holding the dry face of the earlier
125 reservation moves its wet face 2.5 inward. The west envelope's existing
15 finish becomes 25, costing 10 inward. This comparison therefore uses clear
x 427.5–2335 and y 8295–10822.5, with the north-east 1000 tray at 1335/9822.5.
The current IAS successor owns the assembly and support questions; these are
proposed finished coordinates, not a technical house migration. The waste-box
comparison now includes a 250-deep north run and 150-wide west run; the larger
north box pays for actual trap-body release rather than merely pipe containment.

Compare an ordinary **outward-opening 826 × 2040 × 44 leaf**, north hinge,
against the previous inward swing. Widen the proposed wet-room rough opening
from 900 to 950, keeping its centre 8850: y 8375–9325. An authored 32 jamb each side,
two 3 fitting gaps and the 826 leaf make 896 outer frame, leaving 27 packing each
end. Jamb clear is 832 (8434–9266); two conservatively full-depth 13 stops leave 806.
These 32/13 sections are **authored bounding allowances**, not dimensions claimed
from the manufacturer's universal-lining sheet. The actual lining, hinge axis,
fixings, lock, stops, seal and wall-depth transition still have to be bound.
The wider aperture buys packing tolerance; it does not enlarge an 826 leaf.

[JB Kind's actual oak leaf](https://www.jbkind.com/range/oak) lists KOAK826 at
2040 × 826 × 44. Its [universal lining sheet](https://www.jbkind.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/JB_Kind_Universal_Door_Linings_Sets.pdf)
accepts heights through 2040 and widths through 980, with 108/133 depth variants,
but omits jamb/stop section dimensions. The leaf's fire designation is not a
rating for this proposed assembled door. The source nominal 2850 head is not
silently reduced: a local 2040 leaf needs a deliberately composed fixed upper
field and separate actual head/support detail. Oak and the hardware below are
geometric comparisons, not a re-selection of HAD's painted-door/subdued-metal
intent.

The [Carlisle Brass catalogue](https://www.carlislebrass.com/media/ebrochure/file/c/b/cb_e-catalogue.pdf),
printed 36, gives FG3 lever length 102 and projection 62, with 64 rose. The model
bounds both handles by a 135-radius circle at an authored 766 station from hinge;
this exceeds the diagonal of 102 tangential and 84 normal (22 half-leaf + 62).
It is a deliberately conservative geometric envelope, not arm articulation,
strength, grip, latch or assistance evidence. The whole swept bound ends 52
south of the wardrobe. The stopped open 90-degree state uses the same handle's
separate 102/84 rectangular bounds, rather than treating a spherical bound as
the actual orientation of its lever.

The [coupled witness](studies/bedroom4-door-operation.py) checks complete body
translations and complete five-degree door sectors with a 650 authored
centre-to-handle reach. A 600 and 650 diameter circular body can enter from the
bedroom and close the door inside. A 700 body has no witness on the stated 20 grid;
that is neither a universal impossibility nor permission to shrink real people.
The witness is reversible geometrically. It does not establish human usability,
accessible design, installed frame closing contact or simultaneous room use.
IAS's proposed mineral saddle has crest 20 above the dry floor (15 above its
proposed wet floor); a flush uncontained edge is not substituted. Actual bevels,
undercut, step-over and lifting consequences remain in the assembly comparison.

## Pan removal is a sequence

The [Duravit UK product source](https://pro.duravit.co.uk/pro/html/default/product-details.uk-en.html?nser=7120&product=94738)
identifies 21150900002 as a back-to-wall pan with independent water supply,
355 × 560 × 385 and 22 kg. It is not a complete close-coupled suite. The cabinet
therefore needs an actual separate cistern, isolation, flush connection, soil
connection and service faces. A body-only cistern rectangle cannot stand in for
its brackets. Those details are being reconciled with IAS before the candidate
can be frozen. The successor reserves a larger 300 × 900 gross cabinet at
x 427.5–727.5 / y 8650–9550 and retains pan y 8800–9155. Moving the pan
50 north would reduce the WC-to-shower interval from 667.5 to 617.5, leaving
only 17.5 beyond the 600 body; that unnecessary squeeze is rejected. A
manufacturer-described 500-wide cistern application centred on the retained
pan needs more than the original cabinet once ends and actual fittings count.
The south extension consumes the unused wet corner and remains west of the
moved ground sofa; its structure and complete service packing are not inferred.

The manufacturer's actual rear and side drawings now locate the connections:
[rear 157](https://wgassets.duravit.cloud/dimg/991267157_web2_tech_draw_2.gif)
gives flush centre 340 and soil centre 185 above the pan floor;
[side 156](https://wgassets.duravit.cloud/dimg/991267156_web2_tech_draw_2.gif)
gives respective diameters 55 and 102. Its 135 dimension runs from the finished
rear wall to the soil spigot end; 40 is the difference from that end to the
flush opening, **not a measured engagement stroke**. At pan back x727.5,
the soil end is x862.5 and flush opening x822.5. With wet floor +5, the centres
are +190/+345 relative to dry floor. These datums constrain IAS's actual
cistern/connector section; they do not prove that a whole connector fits.

The [located A3 maintenance study](studies/bedroom4-maintenance-study.svg) and
[coordinate/result record](studies/bedroom4-maintenance-study.json) reserve:

1. Isolation, drainage and floor protection, with the room door held open 90.
2. Actual fixing/connector release followed by 400 forward pan withdrawal. This
   is available task space, not a proven required connector disengagement stroke.
3. A separate northern helper field and front helper path; a rear helper cannot
   stand inside the cabinet. After the protected pan reaches 2100/8845, the second
   person can move around the emptied fixture area to the rear regrip field.
4. A 660 × 455 protected component envelope (560 × 355 ceramic plus 50 each side)
   translated through the door. Fore/aft 600 body fields are 650 from the component
   centre; their paths are distinct from the component's swept rectangle.
5. A protected stage centred 4500/8547.5 against the bedroom's south side. The
   front person returns north of the staged pan before turning south of the open
   door and reaching the now-clear cabinet east face at 1200/8977.5.
6. Reverse transfer, fixing and connection reinstatement, restoration of the
   water boundary, then actual leak/function inspection before reuse.

The temporary return corridor is 625 between protected pan and bed: **only 25
total spare width beyond the 600 circle**. It is not a simultaneous carrying or
occupied-bedroom claim. A north-west stage was rejected because the connected
route fails despite the apparent empty destination. The retained witnesses include the northern helper’s connected approach and
the separate tray-service stance at (950,10200), below the released north cover.
The far corner of the first cover at (1335,10822.5) is about 732 from that
stance: even reusing the door study's authored 650 reach would not cover it.
Arrival therefore does not prove full cover/union manipulation. A segmented
cover, different located stance or actual tool method remains an internal
design action, followed by checking real grip and release movement.
They check all complete axis segments; diagrams do not replace omitted bends with
diagonal shortcuts. The whole movement of the two people while lifting/lowering,
actual hand positions, safe handling of 22 kg ceramic and crossing the 20 saddle
still require a real handling method. Neither a mass figure nor a clear plan
certifies lifting. No route down the stairs or to an external setdown is claimed.

## Shower enclosure and remaining action boundaries

The [Kudos Divera manufacturer instructions, document 19741-7](https://www.claygate.co.uk/product/attachment/K22K1005007-0001/Divera%20Bi-fold%20Fitting%20Instructions.pdf)
are manufacturer-authored instructions hosted by a distributor. Their diagrams
show an inward folding assembly, profile positioning 20 from the tray edge,
retained sliding/pivot parts, external sealing and 24-hour curing; the cover
calls for two people. These are installation duties, not a complete moving
panel geometry. The published 750 access alone does not locate the opening,
folded stack or handle relative to this 1000 tray. No schematic half-leaf sweep
is being substituted for missing dimensions. Actual tray support/waste,
finished-wall position, entry/closure and component renewal remain coupled.
A conventional curtain offers a specific alternative to the unresolved glass
mechanism. [Croydex AD118941](https://www.croydex.co.uk/product-31.html) has a
manufacturer-listed 900 × 900 L configuration and 22 tube. For this 1000 tray,
an authored rail line 100 inside its two open edges would run x1435 and y9922.5
between the two finished walls. This is a 900-square curtain line, not a claim
about the tray's actual flat standing area. Its
[manufacturer instructions](https://www.plumbworld.co.uk/documents/ad118941-croydex-4-ways-to-fit-modular-shower-curtain-rod-kit-chrome-instructions.pdf)
require sound screw fixing and prohibit using the rod or curtain as body support.
The wet-wall grounds and sealed penetrations belong in the IAS assembly.

[GP85108](https://www.croydex.co.uk/product-284.html) is a real 2100 × 2100
polyester curtain with weighted hem and twelve eyelets. One curtain has only
300 excess width over the nominal 1800 rail run before fullness, end closure
and overlap; no watertight or generous closure is inferred. A nominal hem 50
below the proposed tray rim would place its top 2050 above that rim, subject
to actual eyelet/hook offsets and clearance from the tray floor. The hem must
discharge inside the tray and must not trail underfoot. Actual parked fabric,
hooks, entry movement, shower position and splash containment require a located
assembly check. This remains a named conventional comparison, not an adopted
enclosure or a fabricated rigid folding-panel sweep.
