# HAD-01P — principal-interior candidates

**Result:** architectural candidates, not selected assemblies or final rooms.
**House:** `RH-LAYOUT-REVISION-02` / `RH-LAYOUT-FAMILY-02`.
**Inputs:** [brief](../../docs/reference-house/architectural-design-brief.md),
[shared contract](../triad-interface-contract.md), IAS-00 and OAS-00.

## Question

How should arrival, stair, court and the open family/dining room feel complete
while the interior/opening assemblies are still being developed?

The current house has no separate formal drawing room. Do not invent one,
divide the open rear room to imitate an old programme, or describe a whole-room
panelling system as already selected.

## Visual comparison

[Shared rear north wall](studies/principal-wall-candidates.svg) compares the
mineral control with one lower joinery field. It includes the kitchen side of
the shared space. Trial heights are unadopted; assembly depth and release remain
IAS work. No cornice, frame or glazing product is implied.

## Compared room languages

| Candidate | Main move | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| P-A — mineral field / true joinery at useful edges | Quiet walls, substantial skirting, selected upper termination, framed openings and bounded cabinetry | Leading control; least dependence on extra lining depth and arbitrary seams |
| P-B — selective painted panelling | A real lower/full-height joinery field in a room or service-facing bay where composition and an actual task earn it | Retain challenger with IAS depth/removal/fixing burden; never automatic around the whole house |
| P-C — universal coffer/panel/grid | Full-room removable panels, repeated ceiling grids, metal joints and access everywhere | Reject: unresolved depth, over-articulation and hypothetical access dominate domestic use |

P-A is a design direction, not proof that conventional finish automatically
satisfies every movement, acoustic or service duty. P-B may beat it locally.
No proprietary panel, floor or cornice system is selected here.

## Arrival, void and bridge

The entrance axis continues through the courtyard toward the garden, but the
court is not the sole indoor circulation route. Preserve the accepted routes
around it in normal weather and when external doors are closed.

Treat the void as one tall room with an upper edge and bridge, not two stacked
hallways with competing decoration. Align room divisions with the source's
flush bedroom/WC/cloak faces. Keep a quiet mineral central field and concentrate
joinery at real door openings. Do not add a dado through the void merely to
repeat a ground-floor wall datum.

The guard should read as securely borne: a small repeated vertical family and
a graspable handrail are the leading visual direction. It remains a candidate,
with no selected height, spacing, load, fixing or climbability claim.
Transparent unsupported-looking sheets and conspicuous cable mechanisms are
poor starting points for the repose brief. No structural decision follows
from that visual preference.

No chandelier/pendant is presumed in the tall volume. Compare a reachable
wall-light family and local arrival light before introducing a fitting that
requires special high-level maintenance. The ceiling, sightline from upstairs,
glare and maintenance reach must be reviewed together.

## Stair and west gallery

Keep the current 1100 mm flights, 1635 mm intermediate landing and 1015 mm
west-gallery planning clearance. These are held source dimensions, not spare
joinery width or evidence of adequate headroom, guarding or furniture carrying.

A timber handrail, quiet repeated painted or metal guard elements and a plain
soffit are the leading family. Actual structural support, guarding, acoustic
behaviour, step finish and slip resistance remain technical questions.
No decorative newel or casing may narrow the usable turn before that turn is
checked with the actual assembly.

The gallery does not need a second architectural rail simply to carry services.
IAS should compare a restrained skirting/door-side rise and a bounded service
front, with the room side composed around real access pieces. Do not install
bookcases, display shelves or radiator covers in the nominal clear route.

## Family / dining / kitchen relationship

Compose the rear as a continuous shared room. The kitchen occupies its accepted
east corner and belongs to the whole, while work and dining remain legible uses.

The principal north wall is a good control elevation: retain all four windows
and the central 2400 mm garden opening. Let those openings establish the main
rhythm. Avoid full-height cabinets or a chimney breast inserted to create a
false centre. No fireplace, flue or stove is selected.

Use a continuous dry-room floor direction across family/dining. Kitchen
junctions need real wear, spill and wet-service consideration; a different
finish is possible but cannot conceal a level change or arbitrary threshold.
IAS-01H and IAS-02W own its viable build-up.

Furniture supports composition without becoming geometry authority. Before
fixing lights, sockets or wall panels, the next worked room must show:
a useful seating group, a real table/chair field, the garden-door operation,
the route from court/gallery to kitchen and pantry, and service-release space.
Do not count a line drawn through furniture as circulation.

### Wall and ceiling comparison

Start with one substantial skirting and a quiet upper finish. In the leading
P-A room, cornice appears only where it resolves the room perimeter and a
compatible wall/ceiling junction. Avoid a separate ceiling border, cove,
picture rail and dado all competing in the same field.

The existing window/door head is at 2850 mm beneath a 3100 mm ceiling: only
250 mm nominally separates them. A proposed 100 mm wall downstand at the
ceiling and 75 mm trim above the aperture would leave 75 mm between them.
That is a **composition trial**, not allocated construction clearance. The
actual movement, grounds, frame and tolerance stack may require a simpler
square termination. No field is adopted by arithmetic alone.

P-B tests painted panelling on a bounded solid wall field, with removable
pieces at actual tasks. Lower-panel top should be composed with furniture and
window sill, not selected from a universal Georgian ratio. A full-height
pilaster grid across each opening is rejected as an early default.

## Lighting and local control

The leading approach has three distinct duties: ambient orientation, task light
and local evening light. A room need not have three separate circuits simply
because three duties are named.

| Place | Candidate approach | Dependency before selection |
|---|---|---|
| Entrance | Reachable wall/arrival light | Door swing, void sightline, glare and reachable maintenance |
| Stair/gallery | Legible landings and continuous route light without exposed point glare | Actual guard, stair/headroom and electrical route |
| Family/seating | Local lamps plus restrained ambient light | Furniture, reachable outlets, shade and service rise |
| Dining | One focused light opportunity over a real table; ordinary alternative retained | Table position, head/door views, ceiling support and replacement access |
| Court threshold | Quiet inside/outside transition | Weathering, glare, outdoor electrical applicability and OAS threshold |
| Kitchen | Real work-surface light coordinated with cabinetry | HAD-01S layout and IAS service/assembly family |

No illumination value, emergency-lighting provision, circuit schedule or
fitting certification is invented. Door-side controls remain a direction to
coordinate, not an instruction to fill every architrave with devices.

## IAS / OAS junction handoff

- IAS must compare actual ground/lining thickness and local removability for
  P-B against P-A. A good-looking removable field does not earn a universal
  backplane.
- A cornice covers a designed movement interface; paint/plaster must not lock
  the release. Existing PHY-04 remains the evidence route if applicable.
- Skirting stops and return pieces must leave a reachable first release near
  doors/corners without taking apart a whole room.
- OAS frame/reveal/architrave proportions must fit the 250 mm upper strip and
  preserve real opening operation, shade and privacy.
- Visible metal grilles are confined to actual air/service tasks. Their free
  area, acoustic/fire/weather duties and cleaning route precede appearance.
- Thresholds appear at real interfaces; a continuous floor is not interrupted
  by a decorative strip to advertise the system.

## Adversarial result

P-A leads; P-B survives selectively; P-C is rejected. The most important design
constraint is that the calm image must survive ordinary furnishing and access.

Reject any candidate that steals gallery width, hides a first release behind
fixed cabinetry, requires an extra floor layer without reallocation, makes
lighting maintenance extraordinary, blocks a window, or turns the court route
into the only usable circulation. Resolve guard/void/stair technical questions
through the owning sources rather than representing a visual preference as
safe construction.

HAD-01P supplies candidate design and junction questions. IAS-HAD,
OAS-HAD, material synthesis and selection remain separate gates. HRS and final
PLT later consume the coordinated result.
