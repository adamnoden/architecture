### Actual cold-joint alternatives, and why none is silently adopted

The manufacturer identifies [Viega Sanpress Inox 2360](https://www.viega.co.uk/en/products/Catalogue/Piping-technology/Sanpress-Inox/Screw-fittings/Sanpress-Inox-Screw-fitting-2360.html), article 437411, as a flat-sealed 28 mm union with L101/Z53 and two 46 mm spanner faces. Its public dimension table does not establish the seat overlap or captive nut's fully disengaged position. Flat sealing alone does not earn zero retreat. The [2359 flange](https://www.viega.co.uk/en/products/Catalogue/Piping-technology/Sanpress-Inox/Flanges/Sanpress-Inox-Flange-transition-2359.html), article 593322, gives an explicit DN25/28 mm bolted alternative: Ø115, L68, four Ø14 holes on an Ø85 circle. The manufacturer's [instructions](https://pspstmsimanuals.blob.core.windows.net/pub-production/Manuals/e951f22969c331bbac11409464b375ca_en_GB.pdf), sections 3.4.7–3.4.8, require its matched M12×60 A2-70 mounting set 611262 and 48–57 N·m, separate support and restored leak testing. The actual pressure sleeve is **Ø150 clear within Ø160 formed**, from `south-inlet-relief.json`'s preferred wide bore. A Ø115/L68 adapter retained at the existing released tip has at most **1.89 mm** radial room at a complete wall-cut crossing under the unchanged horizontal draw; the existing 5 mm fabrication allowance makes the reserve at most **−3.11 mm**. This is a setting-screen failure, not a nominal hard clash or a universal flange rejection. Both source branches have an 18 mm vertical axis offset, slope 0.02 and released tip at Y−140. With `c = 1/√(1+0.02²)`, the most favourable flange plane within 68 axial millimetres lies at `−140−68c = −207.9864 mm`. Its full Ø115 disk clears the outer wall cut at Y−90 after `117.9864+57.5×0.02c = 119.1362 mm` of horizontal draw. The remaining normal eccentricity is `(18−0.02×119.1362)c = 15.6142 mm`; `75−57.5−15.6142−5 = −3.1142 mm`. A relocated/coaxial flange and revised cut or motion remain unproved alternatives. The permanent front-support conflict independently rejects the complete arrangement.

Cold-cut renewal removes the requirement to reuse these couplings; it does not remove their actual space or material requirements. The [28 mm stopless coupling 2315.5](https://www.viega.co.uk/en/products/Catalogue/Piping-technology/Sanpress-Inox/Couplings/Sanpress-Inox-Slip-coupling-2315-5.html), article 436407, is **96 mm long**, with **24 mm minimum insertion**. A standard union's 101 mm body also exceeds the present **75.5 mm** Utility straight tangent between the inner face and R75 bend. Neither fits as an unexplained short inside joint. A revised fixed entry must grant a deliberate exposed cutting and coupling zone, plus complete tool motion, rather than moving the missing stroke into a hidden splice.

The same [system instructions](https://pspstmsimanuals.blob.core.windows.net/pub-production/Manuals/e951f22969c331bbac11409464b375ca_en_GB.pdf), sections 2.2–2.4 and 3.3–3.4, identify PN16/potable use, EPDM seals, chloride limits, fixing duties and **R≥3.5d** for bent pipe. For d28 that is **R98**, so the current generic R75 routing cannot be relabelled as this matched system. Factory elbow geometry or a rerouted R98 system must be checked through the existing occupied consumers. EPDM and any other constituents retain their MAT/material-policy disposition; these catalogue facts do not fabricate an owner exception, a fitted tool envelope or installed evidence.

This is the bounded unresolved decision and recommended replacement. Its actual next proof is one fixed bay showing both matched pressure-end renewals, real independent anchors/receiving body, the admitted pressure/flow and passive wet paths, closed controls and ordinary/occupied access on the saved house. If the retained envelope cannot supply that space, bring the priced external bay and its architectural consequences to the owner.
