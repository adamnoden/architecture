## Question and scope

What should the Reference House look and feel like once doctrine, G-01 and the
controlled current plan/section become complete architectural design?

HAD owns exterior/interior composition, room hierarchy, panelling expression,
kitchens, bathrooms, joinery, lighting, hardware and the visible resolution of
technical elements. IAS owns interior mechanics; OAS owns opening mechanics
and environmental/replacement transitions. ROF owns roof-system architecture;
RWT owns building-side rainwater collection/conveyance. HAD owns how those
systems resolve visibly into the house. HAD does not establish competent
performance validation, final construction readiness or publication freeze.

## Structural architecture coordination

The [STR programme](../str/structural-architecture-programme.md) develops solid walls, arches, aligned support and primary floor/roof choices under the owner's [structural brief](../../docs/reference-house/structural-brief.md). HAD retains room quality, circulation, composition and adopted house geometry. STR-HOUSE is a shared comparison of structural and inhabited-space consequences, using the owning plan/section and the existing source-rewind procedure; no second authoritative house model is introduced.

Current HAD studies may continue with named provisional structural assumptions. STR-RECONCILE feeds both ROF-RWT-GATE and TRIAD-GATE, which must verify compatibility with their actual selections. Thicker walls or changed opening heads must earn their place in the rooms, stair, sanitary access and renewal routes; a visually convincing structure cannot conceal a known fit failure.

## Boundary Service Lodge and outward site-service architecture — 31 October 2026

The owner has adopted **Boundary Service Lodge** as the name for an elegant, small, secure brick utility structure near street-side connections, with practical safe access, integral lighting, necessary owner-side isolators, durable system labels and restrained low-maintenance indications. It serves as the **upstream private service interface**, complemented by independent isolation and a service hub inside the house. A removable-cover external service raceway joins them where lawful and physically effective; services may remain below grade without being buried in backfill. Consider a pitched roof and an architecturally quiet, truthful masonry/metal expression.

The [owner-house lodge/raceway brief](boundary-service-lodge-and-raceway-brief.md) defines comparative cabinet/lodge/segregated-compartment options, utility-provider constraints, wet/dry routes, weather, root-sensitive construction and movement interface obligations. HAD owns form, access and architectural site composition; PAT-001, STR, MAT, DPL, MRA and BCR retain their separate technical authorities. This is an **outward site infrastructure candidate**, not an alteration to the accepted house footprint or a pass through HAD-03. Do not treat a pretty image as supplier-approved equipment or construct a duplicate site programme; register any independent execution packet under PCO.

The [pre-site lodge/raceway comparison](boundary-service-lodge-options-result.md) compares three built forms and a connected route with frost and flooded-outlet counterexamples. It recommends separate small front-access cells with a separate protected water route and conventional burial as the benchmark. The bounded comparison is complete; actual site, operator-approved equipment/roof/entry, material admission, movement, lifting and maintenance acceptance remain explicitly unearned. No house or site installation is adopted.

## Governing tests

The house should read as settled domestic architecture. Technical intelligence
must resolve into room composition, material, hierarchy and craft. G-01 is not
permission for false historical construction. Concealment requires credible
release and reinstatement; no image may invent spare depth or suppress an
unresolved water, access or safety boundary.

Use the controlled current Reference House/PLT source. Material changes to
plan, section, openings, wall depths, thresholds or service space require an
explicit source rewind and downstream reconciliation. The present no-flat-roof
constraint remains operative unless ROF explicitly earns a rewind; historic
roof descriptions do not replace the current source.

MRA's [moisture design questions](../mra/mra-00-moisture-constitution-threat-model.md)
apply during exploration. ROF/RWT construct the preferred roof/water system;
MRA later attacks that selected result. BCR routes actual evidence obligations;
possible future PHY/EPR/DEL work is not invented as an early blocker.

## Exit gate

Close HAD-03 when exterior and representative principal/private/service
interiors form one coherent architectural selection and its remaining taste/fit
questions are bounded. Do not imply that HAD-03 alone finalises the roof/water
system.

the selected roof/rainwater architecture and TRIAD-GATE establishes one house.

HRS owns later post-BCR/post-MRA architectural synthesis. PLT owns publication
drawings. Neither is asked to invent unresolved HAD/ROF/RWT decisions.
