# HAD — House Architectural Design

**State:** queued; starts after BSR establishes canonical chapter/authority boundaries  
**Tracking:** [#943 — HAD house architectural design](https://github.com/adamnoden/architecture/issues/943)  
**Programme map:** [`../book-resolution-programme.md`](../book-resolution-programme.md)

## Question and scope

What should the Reference House actually look and feel like once the platform doctrine, G-01 grammar and current plan/section are forced into a complete architectural design?

HAD owns visible architectural authorship: exterior composition, room hierarchy, material language, panelling composition, kitchens, bathrooms, joinery, lighting, hardware and the visible resolution of technical elements. It does not own the engineering mechanics of IAS/OAS or competent technical validation.

## Work packets

- **HAD-00 — architectural brief and hierarchy:** freeze the intended character, room ranking, exterior/courtyard relationship and boundaries of the Georgian-derived language.
- **HAD-01 — exterior design:** facade composition, masonry/material hierarchy, openings as composition, roof/cornice/parapet expression and courtyard dialect.
- **HAD-02 — principal interiors:** drawing/family/dining/stair/court spaces, panelling fields, floors, ceilings, joinery and repose.
- **HAD-03 — service/private interiors:** kitchen, bathrooms, utility, study, bedrooms, storage and service-facing spaces with no drop in architectural resolution.
- **HAD-04 — fittings and interfaces:** lighting, switches/sockets/controls, grilles, thresholds, access pieces, ironmongery and hardware in coordination with IAS/OAS.
- **HAD-05 — material/finish family:** timber, stone, plaster/mineral finishes, metals, colour, patina and room-to-room hierarchy.
- **HAD-06 — visual synthesis:** representative elevations/rooms/details sufficient to establish the Part VI visual language and expose unresolved conflicts before BCR.

## Parallel interfaces

HAD runs in parallel with IAS #941 and OAS #942 after BSR. It uses the controlled current Reference House/PLT plan/section as its field. Any material change to geometry is an explicit rewind request.

IAS supplies assembly constraints while HAD decides their visible expression. OAS supplies opening/cassette constraints while HAD decides facade and trim composition. PLT remains drawing/plate owner and must not be asked to invent missing architectural decisions.

## Governing tests

The finished house should not read as a maintenance machine. Technical intelligence should become quiet architecture. G-01 is a bounded grammar, not permission for false historical construction or decorative noise. Visible elements should tell a substantially true story about their role.

## Exit gate

HAD closes when the exterior and representative principal/private/service interiors form one coherent design; visible IAS/OAS interfaces have intended expression; materials, joinery, lighting, kitchen/bathroom and hardware decisions are resolved to the level BCR needs; and unresolved aesthetic decisions are bounded rather than delegated downstream.

Closure is architectural design resolution, not construction readiness or publication freeze.
