# HAD-03 — a continuous kitchen run, with its window cost

**Unadopted architectural comparison.** The [A3 plan and rear-elevation comparison](studies/kitchen-options.svg)
revisits the [discontinuous pier stations](had-03-furnished-design-study.md).
The earlier 650 × 650 cold-storage reserve does not accommodate the real fridge
installation below. Keeping that reserve and shifting a loose table by a few
millimetres would not resolve the kitchen.

Compare two explicit choices:

| Choice | Useful result | Material cost / unresolved action |
|---|---|---|
| A — retain all 750 sills | Existing rear facade and low outlook; sink and hob stay on solid piers | Separate preparation table and interrupted storage; real cold-storage bay still needs 750 width; dishwasher position and whole appliance actions were not established by the earlier furniture boxes |
| B — raise only ground north windows at x 8100 and 10200 to 1050 | Continuous 900-high north counter, dishwasher beside sink, 1300 preparation/storage module, no loose preparation table | Two apertures become 1800 rather than 2100 high; changed rear composition, seated outlook, daylight, opening/cleaning and OAS source applicability require an explicit decision |

Option B retains the 1000 widths and **2850 heads**, every other opening and the
12000 shell. Nominal aperture area falls **0.30 m² per affected window, 0.60 m²
total**, or 14.3% for those two rectangles. That is not a calculated daylight or
net-glass reduction. The higher sill removes low seated views; standing views,
actual frame bars, shading and controls must be shown with the selected opening.
The 150 between counter and nominal sill is not an installed frame/waterproofing
clearance. A 600-deep counter also makes window reach and cleaning harder; an
approach to its front is not direct access to the sash. Outward operation or a
reachable control may be possible, but neither has been selected here.

## Actions, rather than a row of product rectangles

The generator tests a 600-diameter body over complete axis segments to the open
fridge front, dishwasher loading position, sink, preparation drawer position and
oven working position. It includes the fridge's reflected open bounding envelope,
the dishwasher's actual 90° depth, an authored 600 preparation-drawer extension,
and the oven's 650 projection reserve. These are deliberately separate working
states; simultaneous two-person cooking is not claimed. All five paths pass.
The pantry's conservative 900 inward leaf field remains clear of the fridge bay.
Removing the loose table is what makes a generous common working floor possible.

Replacement starts with emptying and isolation, removing the dishwasher front or
oven fixings, disconnecting at the accessible adjacent module, then pulling onto
protected floor. The drawing marks a **1200-deep temporary staging strip** in
front of the run; it overlaps working positions and must be cleared for that
maintenance state. It is not a tested trolley/carrying route through the house,
nor proof that a 33.8 kg oven can be lifted by one person. Pantry/garden doors,
packaging, helpers and the final external route still need a complete action.

The ordinary layout gains useful working space, but actual cabinetry and appliance
installation sections remain necessary. No appliance is selected for purchase,
no performance/compliance claim is made and HAD-03 remains incomplete. Adoption
would change only the two identified ground north aperture sills first, then
regenerate the facade/room drawings and reconcile OAS, current-house source
identities and BCR occupation/services applicability. Historical 750-sill evidence
must retain its original source, rather than being rewritten as if this proposal
had always been the house.
