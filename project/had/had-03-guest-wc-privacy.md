# HAD-03 — guest-WC privacy in the actual window bay

**Result:** retain full-pane acid-etched glazing as the leading architectural direction, with a separately controlled upper opening as the occupied ventilation candidate. Reject a clear upper vision pane and the unallocated two-leaf internal shutter as defaults. This resolves the direction to develop, **not an accepted private, ventilated or fitted window**. Night-time optical privacy, the actual window/control assembly and site viewpoints remain admission conditions. HAD-03 stays incomplete.

The [A3 plan and section](studies/guest-wc-privacy.svg) and [coordinate/result record](studies/guest-wc-privacy.json) make three different questions explicit: what a translucent pane obscures, what an open gap exposes, and whether an opaque shutter can actually operate in this room. No drawing of frosted glass answers all three.

These are planning reservations, not selected sanitary products or heights.

The [OAS nominal interface study](../../docs/reference-house/opening-interface-studies.md) reserves a 970 × 2070 frame occupying depth coordinates220–360 (140 deep), with a 60 section occupation. Its resulting 850 × 1950 inner rectangle is only an envelope before actual sash members and hardware. This study introduces two **hypothetical clear gaps**, both x7675–8525: upper z2500–2775 and lower z825–1125. Their heights are not measured sash travel, airflow area, manufacturer tolerances or a prescribed restrictor setting. An actual product must establish its own clear gaps and operating states before using these results.

No house aperture, sanitary position, wall, sill or source selection changes. STR-HOUSE may alter the room and supports; any resulting geometry must be checked again. A 492.5-deep-wall sensitivity retains the same frame and rays only to test whether additional reveal depth rescues the adverse examples. It is not a thicker-wall adoption or permission to retain the old fixtures after replanning.

## Why an ordinary shutter is not a free fallback

The challenger is a new authored envelope: two 520-wide, 22-thick opaque leaves, hinges x7580/x8620 at y457.5, covering z725–2875 with an ideal closed perimeter and overlapping meeting joint. The overlap is a required joinery duty, not a manufactured detail. A fully closed ideal opaque screen blocks both example rays, but also blocks direct daylight and the direct top-gap path. Airflow around a shutter is not credited.

At 90 degrees the left leaf occupies x7569–7591/y457.5–977.5, overlapping **8305 mm² of the basin's plan reservation**. Actual basin and tap heights are unknown, so this is not a proved three-dimensional collision; it is a demonstrated reason that the drawn room cannot claim shutter fit. At 180 degrees the left leaf extends 202.5 beyond the west room face. It cannot simply lie flat against an imaginary wall field. Folding leaves require their own complete sweep and stack; a narrow final parked stack alone does not prove the intervening motion.

A lower opaque screen ending at 2250 leaves the example elevated ray above it. Thus a café shutter is not automatically an answer either. If opaque screening is necessary, develop a located folding/removable arrangement or an explicit basin/room change, then recheck occupied operation, light, ventilation, cleaning and renewal. No pocket is cut into permanent fabric or borrowed from the 57.5 nominal internal reveal. The present disposition retains glazing first and rejects this unproved shutter pair as the default; it does not rule out all shutters.

## Controls, cleaning and renewal

Normal use and maintenance require different states. The [Allan Brothers June 2024 operating manual, printed pp. 12–13](https://www.allanbrothers.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Operating-manual-web.pdf) documents vertical sash operation and a travel restrictor. Its separate Simplex hardware permits the lower sash to swing 90 degrees for cleaning before lowering the upper sash. These are real operation precedents, **not hardware or clearances inherited by this house**. In particular, no casement 100 mm rule is borrowed as a limit for the top sash.

| State | Required disposition for the actual assembly |
|---|---|
| Occupied, glazing shut | Reach catches and any ventilation controls without standing on the WC/basin. Actual handle positions, forces and reach remain unproved; a clear floor rectangle is insufficient. |
| Occupied, upper opening | Actual hardware must maintain the verified clear-gap limit and keep the lower sash in its privacy state. Site views and required ventilation must still work together. |
| Cleaning | Take the room out of use; provide safe access to both actual faces. A lower-sash inward swing must be checked against basin/taps and the operator before selecting Simplex or similar hardware. |
| Sash/frame renewal | Release any separate privacy device first, then the independently removable trim and actual sash mechanism, with support, handling and a protected outward route. Sanitary fittings are not work platforms or assumed removable obstructions. |

[Ventrolla's sash-removal system](https://www.ventrolla.co.uk/windows-doors/the-sash-removal-system-srs/) is a precedent for internal removal rather than an external-access assumption. It is not selected here: its actual release sequence, work space and complete material composition must be bound. No cosmetic shutter, coating or glaze label may conceal those duties.

## Integration and remaining gate

This comparison narrows the existing HAD privacy choice without adopting room geometry, completing OAS product duties, passing MAT, or claiming HAD-03 closure. OAS must bind a real glazed frame, sash/restrictor/control and cleaning/removal method; HAD must inspect the resulting expression and occupied actions; DPL supplies the actual outside viewpoints; MAT admits the complete constituents. The physical optical sample remains an explicit evidence task. These conditions are not deferred claims of an already fitted private WC.

The reproducible geometry includes independent adverse controls for an elevated viewpoint, a lower opening, a reduced upper gap, a narrow slit and an insufficient-height opaque screen. The A3 sheet is a bounded design drawing, not a whole-house performance result.
