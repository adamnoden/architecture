# HAD-03 — larger second en suite in Bedroom 4

**Current result: a coordinated candidate, not adopted source geometry.**
The [compact front-bedroom relocation](had-03-relocation-trial.md) is parked:
its tiny stopped-door margin and missing ordinary wardrobe make it a poor basis
for claiming a fitted room. This next comparison changes which secondary
bedroom has an en suite, while retaining the shell, court, all apertures, four
bedrooms and two en suites. The larger rear room can retain a larger actual bed
and sliding wardrobe without relying on a highly constrained door sequence.

## A visible separate rear-west stack

This is a genuinely **new rear-west service geography**, not adjacency to the
existing utility, which is in the south-east. Reserve a gross **300 × 750**
cabinet at x 417.5–717.5, y 8800–9550. Its increased length brings the WC outlet
inside the reservation while preserving an upper route past the WC toward the
shower and cabinet. The envelope is not evidence that a 110 pipe, insulated
branch fitting, brackets, cleanout or removable lining fits.

Use a differentiated access scheme:

- Routine isolation/cleanout must actually be placed at the **north end**. The
  north task field is offset, not a centred full-front 600 opening. Its hand/tool
  reach and removable face still require actual fittings.
- The far southern WC connection is not reached through a 750-long tunnel.
  It requires isolation, draining and **pan removal**, followed by a separate
  east-face release. A body field is possible after pan removal; the pan's
  disconnected envelope, staging, lifting, branch access and reinstatement must
  be drawn and checked before claiming maintainability.
- Below, the cabinet is visible in the rear sitting room. Move the south sofa
  only 50 east, to x 750–2650, so it no longer intersects the gross cabinet.
  The north task field is x 442.5–1042.5/y 9580–10180. The existing low table blocks
  its complete approach; maintenance therefore translates the empty low table
  300 east and 300 north to the dashed stage, then restores it. The lower route
  passes in that explicit maintenance state. Table mass, swept handling and
  component-length renewal are separate checks, not invented by the body path.

The cabinet's adjacency to the west inner wall meets a possible floor end-bearing
zone. BCR must identify actual framing direction, ends/hangers, trimmed opening,
load transfer, lining/fire/acoustic/movement allowances and support. The entire
cabinet footprint is not automatically a floor opening. A new below-ground
branch/outfall and vent termination remain separate real design work.
