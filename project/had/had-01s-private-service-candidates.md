# HAD-01S — private and service-interior candidates

**Result:** bounded candidate exploration; no fitted-room or assembly approval.
**House:** `RH-LAYOUT-REVISION-02` / `RH-LAYOUT-FAMILY-02`.
**Inputs:** [architectural brief](../../docs/reference-house/architectural-design-brief.md)
and [shared interface contract](../triad-interface-contract.md).

## Question

How do the actual kitchen, study, bedrooms, wet rooms and service rooms acquire
domestic dignity without stealing the space or access their programmes require?

A service room is not excused from architecture. A small room is not made
successful by a richer wall finish. Furniture/envelope symbols and nominal
room areas do not establish a fitted installation.

## Shared candidate language

| Candidate | Move | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| S-A — quiet fixed fit-out / independent access | Mineral field, painted true cabinetry where useful, robust work/wet surfaces and separately reachable service releases | Leading control |
| S-B — selective joinery service front | A larger coherent cabinet/panel field on the maintainable side, locally removable without disturbing wet finishes | Retain challenger only where actual task, depth and dry-side operation beat S-A |
| S-C — concealed fitted spectacle | Wall-to-wall bespoke cabinetry, tiled invisible hatches, monolithic wet surfaces and hidden plant | Reject as default: appearance depends on unproven depth, blocked release or brittle reinstatement |

The family's visual consistency comes from related edge/door/hardware
proportions, not identical materials and fittings in every room. A conventional
serviceable cabinet can beat a bespoke cassette.

## Kitchen and pantry

The kitchen remains in the rear east corner and visually part of family/dining.
Begin with a restrained perimeter working run on available solid walls and
local returns. Preserve real windows, pantry entry, the garden/court route and
the eastern service geography. An island is **not** assumed to fit or required
for rank. Test the perimeter arrangement first; no drain/service island is
introduced as an aesthetic default.

Use a quieter painted cabinetry field, a durable replaceable worktop and a
bounded splash zone. Timber finish is suitable for dry fronts if its edge
details suit the actual duty; do not expose vulnerable timber below a credible
wet source just to make the palette continuous. Exact worktop material, sink,
appliances, extraction and cabinetry dimensions remain candidates requiring
actual product/task and services coordination.

The later worked kitchen must show:
appliance doors and removal, useful work areas, tap/sink/waste access, local
water isolation, cooking/extract route, ordinary cleaning, chair/route conflicts
and how a leaking appliance declares itself before reaching hidden fabric.
No joinery plinth may become an invisible water trough.

Keep pantry storage simple and reachable. The accepted door from the kitchen
remains; no plant replacement crosses pantry storage. Shelving and food storage
do not imply a new wet branch, heat source or shared plant ventilation strategy.
An attractive cabinet elevation is not enough to establish food/heat conditions.

## Study and cloak

The study retains its L-shaped source footprint, two front windows and the
current desk/chair reservations. Start with quiet mineral walls and bounded
storage on solid wall fields. Do not add full-depth bookcases across the front
windows, recess joinery into permanent masonry, or widen into the hall/gallery.

A useful picture/hanging datum may be considered for the real hanging task;
a dado plus picture rail plus full-height panelling is rejected as a default
for this small room. A bounded S-B joinery piece can be tested only with
actual floor depth, desk/chair operation and a removal route.

The cloak is a real hanging/store room. Retain the accepted back-wall hanging,
door access and source footprint. Durable edges and simple shelving matter
more than principal-room mouldings. Stored coats must not conceal a required
service release or operating control.

## Bedrooms and wardrobe

Four bedrooms remain in the accepted locations. The principal suite's rank is
quiet privacy, outlook and useful storage rather than compulsory wall panelling
or a second ceiling system. Its L-shaped plan and internal wardrobe must be
read as actual geometry, not drawn as an imagined rectangular suite.

Use continuous quiet dry-room floors and a related restrained door/skirting
family. Bedside task light and reachable control/outlet opportunities follow
actual bed positions; do not fix a symmetrical lighting pair before confirming
bed approach and storage. Tall windows need real privacy/shade operation,
including access to handles and OAS releases.

Bedroom 4's west-gallery entry and shallow rear band remain held. Bedroom 3's
en-suite connection must remain private to its room without swallowing the
gallery or inventing a second service route. Smaller rooms receive less fixed
joinery rather than ornamental compensation. No bed template proves an
accessible bedroom, full furniture carrying route or generous double-room use.

## Wet rooms and guest WC

Use calm mineral wet surfaces with clearly bounded dry-side joinery/service
faces. Avoid a fully timber-lined shower enclosure and decorative metal seams
through waterproofing. The selected wet assembly must own its physical
water/air/vapour and maintenance transitions; HAD cannot choose a seamless
appearance and leave IAS to make it magically removable.

### Small en suite — an explicit unresolved fit

U-ENS3 is approximately 2.17 m² clear in the current record. Its north/south
partition centrelines at y 2650/3850 leave only **1075 mm nominal clear depth**
after the two 62.5 mm partition halves, before local services/finishes.
This is a real constraint, not a certified fitted bathroom.

Compare three bounded directions:

1. **Retained-footprint compact shower room:** actual selected sanitary items,
   door operation and useful approach must be overlaid before adoption.
   No fabricated generic shower/pan/basin arrangement is presented as a fit.
2. **Local room/door/service-front adjustment:** only if a demonstrably workable
   arrangement exists; record the exact source rewind and loss to bedroom or
   adjacent bathroom/gallery before drawing it as adopted.
3. **Programme reduction/reallocation:** retain as an honest fallback if the
   compact arrangement cannot earn ordinary usability. Removing an en suite
   changes the accepted programme and requires an explicit owner design choice.

No option is silently adopted. Candidate completion here means the architectural
directions and failure condition are explicit; it does not resolve sanitary
fit. IAS-02W and HAD/IAS junction work must carry the actual fixture/task study.
The existing access reservation I00-C1 is independently unresolved; moving a
panel 150 mm does not make a bathroom fit.

### Family bathroom and principal en suite

Develop the family room as the more general bathing room and the principal
en suite as private, without prematurely claiming a bath or two basins fit.
Fixture count and selected products follow measured clear space, service zones,
door operation and water-control surfaces.

Prefer maintaining valves, wastes and concealed components from the declared
dry/service side where practical. An ordinary visible access piece may beat
a disguised tiled hatch. A panel should be large enough for the task and
locally removable; real free opening, tools and withdrawal space remain IAS work.

### Guest WC privacy

Retain the front opening. Compare obscured glazing with an operable
privacy/shutter arrangement: the first risks reducing outlook; the second
needs real reveal/handle/cleaning space and useful privacy in every normal
position. No device is selected before OAS-HAD review. Privacy must coexist
with daylight and ventilation; pretending the window is a solid wall is rejected.

## Utility and plant

Treat these as working rooms with robust, repairable surfaces and clear labels.
Keep plant replacement through the external 1400 mm nominal opening as an
intended route, not a demonstrated carrying pass. Equipment size, frame clear
opening, hand/tool space, thresholds and any external approach remain actual
inputs. Do not convert the drawn working strip into storage.

Utility fit-out must show appliance isolation, hose/waste release, ordinary
cleaning and failure water declaration. No full-height fitted wall may conceal
the riser, panels or dry controls behind equipment that must first be dismantled.

Plant access does not need a fake cabinet front. A well-proportioned real door,
legible durable index and useful work light are the leading expression.
No ornamental floor drain or tanking scheme is selected by HAD: IAS and MRA
compare consequences and BCR routes actual evidence.

| Front WC opening | Privacy/shade/ventilation/cleaning and frame operation | OAS-HAD |
| Plant threshold/door | Equipment handling, weather/ground relationship and reinstatement | OAS with IAS/HAD, technical routing through BCR |

Reject S-A where an actual service task cannot be reached independently;
retain S-B only where it improves the task without a hidden moisture or depth
penalty. Reject every candidate that hides a product or clearance assumption.
A possible source rewind remains an unresolved obligation until authored at
the owning source and reconciled downstream.

S-A leads the private/service family. S-B stays selective; S-C is rejected.
No product, fixture fit, complete kitchen, watertight room, ventilation design
or performance class is claimed. This candidate handoff is sufficient for the
next IAS/OAS junction work; it is not final HAD selection or BCR acceptance.
