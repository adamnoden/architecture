## Load path and independent boundaries

Each support takes **1.50 kN vertical and 2.27 kN normal** for these separate actions. The window, actual sill, fastening and handling have to fit those budgets or the support must be resized.

A sharp-corner **16 × 100 × 3 mm** east-stile comparison uses the 100 mm dimension through the wall. At 620 mm high it fits the 20 mm strip with two 2 mm settings and zero additional width. Put the bearing and anchor spread into the head/base wall-normal territory, not across the wet-cell opening. The box is a section comparator.
