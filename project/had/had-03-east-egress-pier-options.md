These are **plan-interval facts**, not engineering capacities. The court window-base support, actual masonry bonding and structural load redistribution are unselected even when a below-sill passage aligns under the solid pier. A narrow arch, vault, lintel or beam supporting the relevant permanent wall will have actual depth, thrust/bearing, reactions and temporary-work consequences. The width of a reversible metal piece is not the width of its structural corridor after covers, insulation, tool space, movement and separate water/gas/air closures.

## Architectural choice to develop first

**Prefer E-P at y6,000 as the next architectural challenger**, not an adopted path. Its two pier projections can admit a genuinely coherent designed support route in a way the old y7,000 line obscured; it also moves drain access from **food storage** into a room already designated for equipment and service replacement. These are real potential benefits for the owner's no-service-in-permanent-fabric principle.

The price is equally real. The existing `plantDry` rectangle x9,862.5–10,362.5 and the larger `plantWorking` x10,662.5–11,582.5 both cross the new y-band. A wet court service passage **must not** run silently beneath inaccessible plant bases or expose electrical plant, air intakes, equipment movement or dry-room materials to a failed outlet. The plant room is not a sacrificial drainage corridor. Its fixed support routes, equipment service clearances, inward/outward door operation, covers and carrying tasks must be redrawn and checked **before** a more technically attractive masonry pier becomes an actual selected solution.

In the next **STR-HOUSE/HAD-03/IAS/OAS** combined candidate, use one connected plan and paired section to show the E-P passage against actual masonry/ground-beam bearing, the plant door and wet/dry partitions. Draw a *removable* load-capable floor/cover and an independent observed leakage route; identify first release, retained water control, replacement length, equipment protection and full reinstatement. Also preserve N at the distinct north court/garden axis, with its own source intake and genuinely independent downstream failure consequence. If E-P forces unacceptable plant packing or unrepairable floor/threshold joints, return to E-W1 with a genuinely designed window-base bridge or deliberately replan the courtyard ring; do not claim either from a 300 mm stripe.

## Why this is only a partial gate

The [RWT fault-tree](../rwt/rwt-robustness-result.md) still demands an **E** route which passes the entire assigned degraded rainfall under a nominated N blockage, stays below the *actual lowest* susceptible sill/pan/wall/plant interface at operating water head, and escapes to a harmless independent exterior destination. A paired set of structurally convenient passages may share the same outside backwater, ground movement or storm exposure. **Opposite room destinations and wider piers do not prove hydraulic independence.** Site receiving-water events, lawful disposal and foundation/root conditions remain DPL obligations; unknown stays unknown.

**Disposition:** E-P is the stronger **nominal masonry alignment** and deserves the next exact 3D section/plant-fit falsification. The older centreline y7,000 remains a dated counterexample, not an admitted 300 mm service opening; E-W1 is the bounded fallback only if real window support, repair and room access are demonstrated. **HAD-03 remains planned; RWT-06 remains blocked.** No house-source wall/window/plant alteration is adopted, no rainfall system sized, and no packet/maturity credit is asserted for a geometric comparison.
