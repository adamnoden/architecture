# HAD-03 — courtyard threshold: first susceptible joint and renewal gate

**30 October 2026 · bounded architectural challenge · not an adopted sill, pan or safe-drainage detail.**
**Owner:** HAD-03, in coordination with OAS, IAS, BCR and RWT. The earlier [court walking-level proposal](had-03-court-levels.md) and [north-axis RWT comparison](../rwt/north-court-carrier.md) remain separate source identities.

## The decisive question

Can both **2400 mm north/south court doors** retain an ordinary near-level walking route while the lower court pan collects rain, **without the first water-susceptible interface being reached before a genuinely functional escape path**? The current source places the raised court walking surface at z0 and the waterproofing top at z−180 around the perimeter and z−215 at the illustrated low point. A proposed sill-support envelope is +15 to −45, rear pan upstand +15 and end returns +75. Those levels are **not an installed section**, not a four-way-pan survey and not a door product specification.

RWT's −75 relief-activation and −50 maximum operating-water levels are **uncomputed trial targets**. The underside at −45 is only **5 mm nominally above** the latter; its elevation is **not proof that it is the first vulnerable joint**, nor that a 5 mm gap accommodates splash, movement or construction tolerance. Raising the label on the back of a pan does not raise a lower frame drain, fixing, end-joint or concealed wall closure. [RWT-06 owns the water-level comparison and hydraulic acceptance](../rwt/rwt-06-access-court-transition.md#current-raised-court-pan-and-relief-level-reconciliation-30-october-2026).

## Matched-product reality check

The official [Stormguard SG2000/13 **outward-opening** sill page](https://www.stormguard.co.uk/stormguard-products/sg200013-outward-opening/) offers 1/2/3 m lengths, concealed fixing, **pre-drilled drainage and fixing holes**, supplied top-section end caps and accessories for double doors. It states a BS 6375 Part 1 weather claim. This makes a **2400 mm trial-length sill component plausible**; it does **not** make a complete 2400 mm doorset, its meeting stile, frame-end junction or an under-sill waterproofing pan a matched system. Neither the profile's drain holes nor its fixing pattern has yet been placed against the court's proposed operating-water elevation. The weather claim does not authorise immersion or court-flood resistance.

The [Schlüter TROBA-LEVEL manufacturer installation sheet](https://eu.schluter.com/statics/www_schlueter_pl_pl_2x/downloads/cumulus/schlueter_db_7_6_troba_level_uk.pdf), already considered by HAD, supports *investigating* raised removable paving over a fall-bearing substrate; it does not prove a threshold pan, scaffold loading or frame-end seal. These two products are comparators from **different assembly systems**, not an approved combined specification.

The register identifies **possible** first losses, not a ranked leak sequence. Every actual joint requires an assembly-specific elevation, material, continuity drawing, water direction and inspection/removal path. Unknown stays unknown; neither a render nor a supplier's generic weather test fills it.

## Architectural option reduction

**A — Independent sill pan behind a step-free walking edge (preferred for further design, not selected).** Keep the court walk at z0 over demountable pavers and an independently supported doorset. Investigate a mechanically seated, accessible pan below/behind the outward sill, with a genuinely raised rear leg and end returns **behind** the frame, positively joined to the wall/cavity and court waterproofing. Arrange drainage to a separately removable exposed threshold strip. All membrane laps, gaskets, fastener locations and frame-end details require matching, not an aesthetic thin line of sealant. Replacing the door must not require breaking the permanent wall; replacing the pan itself must have a credible, more extensive but bounded task. A high rear leg concealed behind the frame may preserve walkability, **but only** if lower joints are also controlled.

**B — Exposed raised kerb under the doorset.** It may move a susceptible boundary away from ponded water, but introduces a real step and competing doorway geometry. The current owner-house direction explicitly seeks an ordinary near-level through route. Do not silently select a kerb to make a section pass; return that trade-off to the owner/HAD if A cannot be made robust.

For **either** option, a rear return higher than the water target is insufficient if its front drain, side splice, fastening or meeting stile is lower and unprotected. A single centreline section is not enough: draw the door centre, **both jamb/end returns**, double-door meeting stile, adjacent masonry cavity and **the carrier/sleeve-support crossing** as connected details. Test door **closed and open**, under blocked-primary and blocked-relief cases; no resident intervention is an assumed safety barrier.

## Required bounded demonstration and decision

1. **OAS/HAD:** nominate the exact outward-opening frame/sill family and cut profile; produce manufacturer-bound elevations for drain/weep, fixing, first seal, meeting stile, end junctions and threshold support, not merely the nominal door opening.
2. **HAD/IAS/BCR:** draw the fully allocated floor/threshold assembly, structural bridge, cavity/DPC join, pan layers, thermal bearing and all service sleeves. Identify accessible renewal/removal directions and a safe temporary wet-weather condition.
3. **RWT:** only on that connected section, calculate the primary-blocked relief head, local transient operating level, independent outfall and common-debris/backwater cases using assigned roof plus direct-court rain. Return a water-elevation envelope and first-fabric comparison, not the previous −50 as an accepted ceiling.
4. **Integrated gate:** accept, revise or reject the section against the **lowest demonstrably susceptible joint**, with tolerances, loads, thermal/water continuity, operation, repair and real-site handoff all stated. Actual product testing and competent structural/waterproofing review remain real evidence tasks.

**Result today:** the previously tempting **5 mm underside comparison is explicitly rejected as a threshold acceptance argument**. A is the cleaner spatial direction to develop; it is **not** a selected door/pan assembly, certified flood barrier, complete section or instruction to construct. HAD-03 remains planned, and neither RWT-06 nor TRIAD-GATE is unlocked. Any discovered lower joint is a design rewind, not a permission to raise the nominal upstand until a diagram looks safe.
