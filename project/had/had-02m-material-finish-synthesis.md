The court's optional green ceramic accent is **not selected**. The kitchen's
bounded green fronts provide enough secondary colour without adding exposed
wet facade junctions. All-over panelling, decorative service grids, false
rubbed arches, the ornamental villa option and a universal metal-seam language
are rejected. A future local panelling field must earn its actual task and depth.

Painted timber is a design preference, not a fabricated OAS product selection.
Do not claim thermal, weathering, carbon, cost, lifespan or maintenance superiority
without actual evidence. This record does not close OS-01/02/05 or the product
part of OS-06 in the opening selection register.

## Lighting and fitting family

Select reachable replaceable lamps/wall fittings for arrival and ordinary room
ambient use, local task fittings at actual desks/worktops/beds and one restrained
dining fitting only after the table field is established. No pendant is selected
in the tall entrance void. Stair/landing light must reveal the real walking/guard
edge without exposed point glare; exact fitting and photometric design remain
technical work. Switches and outlets are ordinary independently supported
accessories, composed beside actual door/furniture positions, not hollowed into
every architrave. No lighting circuit count, lux, emergency strategy or IP class
is invented. Wet/outdoor applicability and safe maintenance are actual inputs.

Use a single related ordinary handle family; front entry may be more substantial
without changing the operating logic. Hinges/closers/locks and any rated doorset
remain selected as an actual working assembly. Grille appearance follows required
duty/free area; no decorative mesh is adopted as ventilation.

## Why this survives the junctions

The material hierarchy does not demand IAS V2's extra 30 wall depth, H3's extra
40 floor depth, a deep gallery skirting, tiled routine-access hatch or universal
cassette. Plain upper termination removes the crowded 100 cornice trial.
OAS retains independent frame support, room trim and deliberately renewed
environmental interfaces. Pale court masonry is not an extra surface layer.
Water-shedding surfaces and repair/release joints remain visible responsibilities.

The preference fails locally if an actual frame/product, moisture exposure,
required boundary, safe maintenance or ordinary fitted use cannot support it.
Respond with a simpler material/detail, or an explicit source rewind; do not
hide the failure with colour. Samples, product dimensions and wear/cleaning tests
can change a finish within this hierarchy without pretending evidence already exists.
