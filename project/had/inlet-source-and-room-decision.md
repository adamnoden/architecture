No aperture carrier, rating, approval or concealed material has been invented. A plate with a hole is not automatically a maintainable potable assembly.

## Fault and renewal decision

The executable matrix retains thirteen distinct states. Its failures are deliberate results, not suppressed diagnostics:

- **Ordinary and closed control:** prove real indoor reach, two-branch source availability and actual delivery; closed downstream valves retain full static/transient pressure.
- **Downstream rupture:** the largest aperture supplies its bounded fault flow only while the aperture and total-head ceiling remain valid. Existing receivers fail the default flow; upper joint and house-cut rupture remains uncontained by a low box.
- **Paired faults:** charge 352.543 L/min to the actual independent outfalls and common site-water case. No shared receiving depression, pump or buried drain is credited.
- **Blocked, high or submerged receivers:** both blocked remains an unattended spill failure. High/submerged tailwater invalidates free-drop capacity. No manual-response time or finite storage becomes a pass.
- **Missing, bypassed or eroded restriction:** withdraw limited-flow credit and restore the source-only full-bore sensitivity, above 1300 L/min per branch. Upstream rupture remains outside the aperture bound and belongs to the Lodge's separate wet territory/provider isolation route.
- **One branch isolated, sibling live:** isolate at both appropriate ends, drain the retained house inventory, prove zero pressure, and leave the sibling enclosure/supports intact. Restriction or house-cut replacement is two-sided cold work; the flow bound is absent until verified reinstatement.
- **Wall-spool renewal:** retained inward/outward tool, body, washer-first-rest and permanent-pan conflicts remain obligations of the real new room. A diagram of the pressure path is not an extraction proof.
- **Frost/loss of warmth:** qualify the Lodge, aperture, external route, wall spool, room and drains as one route. Open permanent spill slots lose the warm-envelope assumption. No trace heater, antifreeze, moving flap or unlimited room heat is silently added.

The comparison retains the six known PTFE/EPDM valve constituents and **zero approved owner exceptions**. A replan is not an exception. If the owner rejects the itemised valve exception, the complete potable alternative and adapters remain necessary before selecting the pressure route.

## Primary evidence for pressure recovery

[NASA SSC-00161, Routines for Computing Pressure Drops in Venturis (2004)](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20110016729) explicitly treats pressure drop/recovery and cavitating/non-cavitating throat states as separate calculations. [Valmet Flow Control Manual, Liquid Flow §§3.1–3.3](https://www.valmet.com/flowcontrol/valves/flow-control-manual/liquid-flow/) distinguishes the minimum vena-contracta pressure from the downstream pressure after recovery and explains cavitation/choking and erosion. Those sources support correcting the modeling boundary; they do not qualify this proposed water assembly. The absolute-pressure upper comparison and recovery sensitivities above are this project's conservative/optimistic deductions, with their assumptions explicit.
