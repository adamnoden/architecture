## Question and authority

What common character, room hierarchy and visible-truth boundary can guide
independent HAD candidates without silently redesigning the accepted house?

The authored answer is [the architectural design brief](../../docs/reference-house/architectural-design-brief.md).
It is local Reference House design authority. G-01 v0.1, the current tectonic
language and the accepted layout/section remain their own owners.

## Decisions

- Settled English masonry exterior, lighter intimate court and contemporary
  domestic interiors; ornament concentrated at ranked architectural loci.
- Hierarchy comes from role, arrival, openings, outlook and articulation while
  retaining equal 3100 mm clear ceilings.
- Wall composition precedes module geometry. Quiet direct finish, sparse
  attachment datums and continuous principal-room floors remain controls.
- No universal panelling, cassette, raised floor, brass seam or material palette.
- Existing entrance void, two-ended court route, compact rooms and east service
  geography are design inputs, not invitations to enlarge them silently.
- The current source is a twin-pitched inward/outward roof ring. Earlier
  outward-only prose must not mask inner-eaves collection and overflow duties.
- Material families are candidate directions; actual finish, fitting and trim
  selection waits for the relevant room/assembly interfaces.

## Adversarial review

A ceremonial arrival risks overpowering the small study and galleries: detail
is concentrated without adding area or a new pendant requirement.
A warm court risks becoming a decorative lightwell: no fountain, arcade or
permanent centre object is required.
An elaborate panel system risks becoming an aesthetic mandate: room-side
composition and a declared replacement task must both earn it.
Equal storey heights risk flattened hierarchy: route and shared-room rank
provide hierarchy without an invented golden ratio.
Concealed technical features risk dishonest completeness: releases, depth,
working space and physical failure routes remain explicit.
A coherent image risks laundering old technical evidence: no prior-house
engineering pass is transferred to this changed house.
