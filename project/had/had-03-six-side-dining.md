# Six side chairs: the table underside controls the arrangement

**31 October 2026 · bounded HAD-03 continuation · unadopted comparison.** Starting main `6c3c7c6d4c5fabd1112b13b86d309edfa1db4df3`. The [previous dining study](had-03-dining-garden.md) does not establish six places by showing a four-place alternative. This continuation tests a larger real table and its matching chair while retaining both sofas, the low table, the house geometry and the central route. Required regular and guest seating remain unknown.

## Decision

**Reject the claim that three chairs at 600 mm centres fit within this table's published longitudinal chair-placement space.** The table top is 2200 long when extended, but that placement dimension is only 1540. Three 450-wide chair envelopes at 600 centres span 1650: 110 too much for this conservative screen.

A 500-centre sensitivity reduces the span to 1450. It leaves 45 at either end if centred, and only 50 between adjacent chair envelopes. It is an unresolved comparison, not an adopted comfortable dining arrangement. The manufacturer describes three matching chairs per side when extended; that does not prescribe the earlier study's 600 pitch or establish the actual household's occupied use. Neither a chair-placement dimension nor a whole-chair rectangle is a complete three-dimensional collision model.

The [A3 comparison](studies/dining-six-side.svg), [calculation values](studies/dining-six-side.json) and existing [dining generator](studies/dining-garden.ts) retain the distinction between outer room fit, the table's construction and human use. HAD-03 remains planned and incomplete.

## Authored position and actions

Orient the extended table north–south at **x3600–4550 / y8950–11150**. This is loose-furniture trial geometry. Its centre is x4075/y10050; an eventual selection must relocate the dining light opportunity and coordinate nearby outlets. The two sofas and low table retain the original furnished-study positions.

The three stations on each side are centred at **y9550, y10050 and y10550**. There are **no end chairs**. The inferred centred 1540 placement interval is y9280–10820; centring is an authored assumption because the drawing does not separately dimension both top-edge-to-placement offsets. The combined chair span is y9325–10775, leaving the stated 45 each end. At 600 pitch, the centres would instead be y9450/10050/10650 and the span would exceed that interval by 55 at each end.

Unlike the earlier Quaker comparison, this screen assumes **zero insertion under the table**. From an unoccupied edge-adjacent position, each whole chair translates straight outward by an authored **400 mm**. This is not a manufacturer operating requirement or evidence of a seated posture. It removes reliance on an unproved 100 mm tuck for the room-envelope result. Both the initial and final rectangles and every intermediate translation are included.

The separate [Plank/Quaker insertion review](had-03-table-chair-fit.md) keeps the earlier 100 mm tuck unverified. This zero-insertion screen neither validates that pairing nor establishes occupied Fairmile posture.

This positive route assumes the **source scene's outward garden symbol** and court leaves opening into the court. It stops at the garden opening's room face; actual frame, hardware, threshold and exterior continuation are absent. The higher route margin is one improvement, but the smaller west/east furniture margins and narrower chair pitch are costs. Do not describe the six-chair sensitivity as an overall room improvement or accessible route simply because this one disc passes.

## Extension and maintenance are different states

The [manufacturer assembly instructions](https://www.ercol.com/download_file/30063/9566), pp4–6, require access to the catches, lifting a pair of legs while separating the table, moving and lifting the centre leaf from its internal rails, then closing and locking the assembly. They do not dimension its maximum temporary separation, operator positions or leaf-handling space. The finished 2200 rectangle therefore does not prove that the table can be extended or closed in this location. Moving a 41 kg table is not treated as an effortless daily reset.

The closed table's 1140 placement length is 310 shorter than the 1450 span of the 500-pitch row. The six-side arrangement cannot simply be retained while removing the extension leaf. Changing to end seats, storing chairs or moving furniture would need its own complete use state and the actual regular/guest brief.

Likewise, dining use does not establish [RWT floor-panel opening and component renewal](../rwt/copper-access-first-result.md). Furniture staging, protected floor, lifting, helpers and a clear withdrawal route must be checked with any adopted RWT assembly. Window/shade operation and the separate proposed rear-west stack also remain unresolved. Neither a maintenance corridor nor a household capacity is reduced silently to preserve the drawing.

## Next decision and validation

The useful next input is the required regular and guest seat count, followed by an actual paired table/chair and occupied-use decision. If six side places at the authored 600 pitch are retained as a design objective, this conservative screen needs at least **1650 mm** of relevant placement space before any extra margin; that is a derived project requirement, not an ergonomic standard. A different base/leg arrangement may achieve it. Do not narrow the required pitch, reduce capacity or change house geometry merely to declare this product accepted.
