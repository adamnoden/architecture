## Recommendation

Keep the pressure-pipe compact frame independent. Develop removable split upper backs and a real clear-bore collar locally, using cold manual first cuts to avoid requiring the press jaw behind the closed back. Do **not** accept the existing low chassis as the wet enclosure's support on this evidence: its conservative combined steel and isolated anchor screens fail. This is a finite rejection of that support proposal, not proof that every possible connection to those rails fails.

The proposed two outer stays and top beam are ordinary fixed wet-body support stock. They add no actuator, moving pressure gland, pipe restraint or pipe-frame replacement. The representation gives a complete prospective path from enclosure to jamb and exposes where it is inadequate. Further development should shorten that wet load path or use a conventional independently supported isolation cabinet, rather than continuing to thicken this disproportionate low frame without a whole-arrangement comparison.
