# HAD-01E — exterior and courtyard candidates

**Result:** candidate exploration, not final architectural selection.
**House:** `RH-LAYOUT-REVISION-02` / `RH-LAYOUT-FAMILY-02`.
**Design brief:** [architectural brief](../../docs/reference-house/architectural-design-brief.md).
**Interface:** [shared contract](../triad-interface-contract.md).

## Design question

How can the accepted classical body, five-bay front, garden opening and small
court acquire a materially convincing exterior without hiding the roof-water
burden or asking OAS to fit an imaginary window?

The source plan, aperture positions/widths, level datums and roof remain held.
This packet authors compositional/material alternatives and intended visible
interfaces. It selects no product, changes no source geometry and does not
re-evaluate the old house's G-01 certificate as a pass for the new one.

real comparator if brick/reveal/water/interface consequences make E-A worse.
Do not decide by generic cost, carbon or durability scores unsupported by
current products, quantities and exposure.

## E-A composition

### Public front

Retain the five front axes 1800/3900/6000/8100/10200 mm and the centred
1800 mm entrance. Four ground windows and five upper windows form the body.
The upper central opening belongs to the entrance volume; it is not an invented
bedroom window. Related tall window proportions provide the repetition.

Give the entry the only fully expressed exterior doorcase. Retain the source's
paired-leaf/separate-fanlight intent without prescribing its fabrication.
The leading surround is pale, shallow and planar rather than a new portico.
Its material is legitimate cladding/trim if non-loadbearing: it does not imply
a stone structural lintel. No porch roof or terrace is introduced.

Retain the present plinth and upper cornice ordering while testing actual
projection and weathering with OAS/BCR. Avoid a new belt course that cuts the
two-storey body into equal stripes. Brick bond, mortar, head construction and
lintel expression remain linked decisions: no decorative rubbed arch is drawn
over an unselected structural head.

### Garden and service faces

The north ground 2400 mm opening remains the shared-room threshold, flanked by
four windows. Four upper windows and their central solid pier remain exactly
as accepted. Do not insert a blind central window to recover five-bay symmetry:
the solid field is an honest difference between entrance and private frontage.

Use the same sill/head/window family with less entrance articulation.
The external plant door is a working aperture: related proportion and quiet
finish, with accessible hardware and a legible carrying route. It is not
disguised as fixed masonry or a false sash. Vents and terminals belong to the
service-side composition only after their required duty and positions exist.

### Upper termination and roof

The current 180 mm drawn cornice band is a compositional starting point, not a
designed gutter. A quiet eaves/cornice line terminates the brick body; the dark
pitched roof remains visible. No extra parapet conceals it.

A replaceable, reachable rainwater component may be visually subordinate; it
may not be sealed behind permanent decorative work. The ring has inner as well
as outer eaves. Downpipes, outlets and maintenance positions have not been
selected. Keep explicit collection and access reservations rather than drawing
a false finished water system.

## Court dialect

The court is a small, tall external room. Its value is sky, reflected daylight,
wall depth and a direct relationship with the house. It should not borrow a
palace's density of detail.

- Keep the paired ground doors on the north/south axis and the actual window
  positions. Frame/reveal relationships remain recognisably related to the
  exterior opening family; court exposure is checked separately by OAS.
- Test a light warm mineral field with real reveal depth. Do not flatten the
  sill/drip into render or use render to conceal a missing water path.
- Prefer one calm paving field with perimeter/interface decisions driven by
  drainage and threshold duties. Paving height, falls, drain and overflow remain
  unselected; no flush interior/exterior plane is claimed.
- Keep the route between doors unoccupied. Any pot, seat or planting is movable
  and cannot be counted as available access space.
- A restrained green ceramic opportunity may occupy one bounded, repairable
  field after splash/water and backing are resolved. No all-over tile skin,
  decorative arch, fountain, tree pit or permanent bench is the baseline.

The warmer dialect comes from light and material, not imitation Islamic motifs
or a pretend historical construction system.

## OAS and IAS handoff

OAS-01E must compare frame material and real section/sightlines before HAD
chooses painted timber, aluminium or another supported family. The nominal
60 mm drawn face is not a product limit. Six-over-six representation is not a
mandatory operating mechanism, glazing bar construction or escape dimension.

At each front/court/garden opening the next drawing must distinguish the
masonry field, actual frame, visible reveal/trim and water-shedding geometry.
HAD supplies hierarchy; OAS supplies viable relationships. IAS receives the
room-side reveal/architrave position without gaining invisible depth.

The 2850 mm head below a 3100 mm ceiling leaves a nominal 250 mm room-side
strip. Interior cornice/architrave work must fit that strip after real frame,
movement and fixing allowances. A grand exterior image does not buy space
inside the room.

## Failure, access and explicit unresolved items

| Item | Candidate response | Still required / owner |
|---|---|---|
| Roof water | Avoid new parapets/hidden basins; preserve eaves access as a real duty | Collection, overflow, inner-court access and discharge: house/MRA/BCR |
| Window seal failure | Retain sill/drip/reveal depth in composition | Actual secondary water path and reinstatement: OAS, then MRA |
| Court drain restriction | Keep route and failure visibility in brief | Drain/overflow destination and level section: house/OAS/MRA; outfall/site evidence later |
| Brick/mineral junction | Minimise material transitions at wet edges | Physical termination, movement and drying: OAS/BCR |
| Roof and facade access | No permanent decorative concealment over maintenance items | Reach, support, safe work and carrying: house/BCR/EPR |
| Shade/privacy | Front WC requires privacy; tall windows need controllable shade | OAS/HAD operational comparison; no frosted product or shutter fit selected |

A possible inward-court overflow obstruction is a design question, not an MRA
pass. If the selected water architecture cannot fit without an inaccessible
concentration, raise a form/level/source rewind rather than an ornate gutter.

## Result and falsifiers

The preferred exploration direction is E-A with a quiet court, while E-B stays
available. E-C is rejected. These are candidate dispositions only: OAS-HAD
junction, HAD-02M and HAD-03 own later coordination/selection.

Reconsider E-A if actual opening depth, moisture behaviour, roof access or a
credible maintenance task defeats its expression. Reconsider the court surface
if reflected glare, drying/cleaning or real drainage makes it worse.
No competent performance, external aesthetic approval, physical print proof or
final PLT authority is asserted.

Candidate evidence is the source-specific composition, comparisons and
obligation handoff above. Repository/PCO validation protects integration;
it does not prove this facade beautiful or technically adequate.
