## Decision

Do not treat the existing 800 × 1600 table and its 600 mm perimeter field as a proven six-place dining arrangement. With the product and authored movement below, the southwest and south end chair envelopes encounter the sofa and court corner; a further garden-leaf conflict is conditional on an **unselected inward** opening direction.

Advance a **four-side-place alternative** for a furnishing and occupied-use comparison: move the loose table 200 east and 150 south, use the stated closer side stations, and keep end chairs out of that result. It has a continuous route through the modelled room, but only **32.5 mm** of spare clearance around the authored 600 mm disc at its tightest point. That is a reason to test and improve the arrangement, not a comfort or accessibility result. Required regular and occasional seat counts are unknown; four places are not adopted as the owner's requirement.

The [A3 sheet](studies/dining-garden.svg), [source calculation](studies/dining-garden.ts) and [values](studies/dining-garden.json) separate actual product dimensions, authored positions and unresolved duties. No furniture or door product is selected.
