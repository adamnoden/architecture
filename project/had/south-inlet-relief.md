## Pass an upper sleeve rupture to a lower, separate outlet

Reject pitch alone for the existing Ø100 clear pressure sleeve. Its Ø28 raised carrier leaves 49 mm beneath the wetted-air limit. With half the bottom width, uniform capacity would reach 90 L/min around 1:27. Critical depth is already 55.74 mm before charging the carrier. A 10 mm bed deposit raises that ideal lower bound to 59.69 mm. With the outside upper mouth blocked, the actual 1:50 reverse rise and critical energy need 88.87 mm under half-width clearance before friction, against 85 mm available with the 15 mm airway. Adding a 32 mm continuous bed deposit raises the lower bound to 119.42 mm. A full Ø74 jacket never inherits the raised bare carrier's recovery. [Critical flow condition](https://www.hec.usace.army.mil/confluence/rasdocs/ras1dtechref/6.0/modeling-culverts/culvert-hydraulics/critical-depth-of-flow-in-the-culvert)

Try **Ø150 clear and Ø160 formed** upper sleeves, with separately restrained bare Ø22/28 metallic carriers retained 18 mm above the sleeve axis. Reserve independently inspectable continuously welded secondary shells and full end-box bulkhead junctions. Exclude permanent fabric, insulation joints and covers from the fault path. A burst inside the span hits the inner skin of that metallic shell. With the exterior upper mouth obstructed, allow the shell to wet around the bare line and discharge from its upper inside mouth into the captured wet box. From there it leaves by the independent lower run. Keep the vent above the hydraulic water line along this steel path. Restore hygienic separation and inspect and drain after a contained rupture. The upper shell normal outdoor drop gets no credit in this state.

The half-width, 32 mm raised-bed reverse comparison for the Ø150 sleeve charges the real 10.39 mm uphill rise, critical energy, 519.5 mm Manning friction at critical depth and K=1 local loss. It uses **121.72 mm of 135 mm**, retaining approximately **13.28 mm** against the minimum 15 mm crown airway. The Ø140 alternate leaves only **0.08 mm** of that reserve. Keep these as gravity sensitivities and prove the actual transient pressure, inlet loss, seam continuity, moving seals, ability to vent with partial obstruction, and safe relief for every rupture position along the sleeve. Both mouths fully blocked defeat the air and outfall case.

A continuous thin carrier jacket may recover some thermal protection without using the relief bore. At the retained +18 mm axis, complete Ø40/50/60 envelopes leave **13.28/11.98/6.68 mm** above the 15 mm airway requirement in the same reverse case. Ø74 overruns it by **3.00 mm**. For a Ø28 tube these first three trials allow only **6/11/16 mm radial territory** for insulation, jacketing and any distributed protrusions. Specify a real non-displacing lining and prove no-demand frost, support and drainability before crediting a jacket. A wet jacket cannot supply its dry-state thermal result.

## Carry 90 L/min with the normal route unavailable

Use independently captured wet boxes at X10385–10555 and X10590–10760, Y429.5–665. The lower vented relief mouths have inside inverts **Z183.29 and 283.29**. Take no credit for the ordinary upper route. At half-width and a 32 mm continuous bed obstruction, 1:12 gives normal depth 60.13 mm and critical depth 76.39 mm. Charge K=1.5 at the captured inlet. The head is **139.25 mm**. Set the lowest dry return/seam/vent relation at invert +180 mm. After 20 mm splash and 10 mm setting, **10.75 mm remains**. K=1.93 exhausts that reserve. Put the solid jet target above the draining throat and spread the flow across the open lower half. Provide an internal downturned wet return and no solid step in the available low channel. The short packed entrance or sudden surge may require more than the declared K. [Culvert entrance loss guidance](https://www.fhwa.dot.gov/engineering/hydraulics/pubs/12026/hif12026.pdf)

Turn neither relief into the other cell. The terminal at **Y−650** has invert **Z93.33 utility / Z193.33 plant**. Dedicated receiving lanes X10370–10550 and X10585–10765 leave 25 mm after setting. Both leave the southern roof station lane at X10950 outside the work. Neither requires crossing hot D2 or the east plant dock. Give both below-lip receivers a separate 180 mm active overflow. At half width, with a 32 mm raised edge and Cd 0.50, 90 L/min demands 50.32 mm over that edge. With crest **Z−35 / Z65**, water reaches **Z47.32 / Z147.32**, leaving another 11.01 mm after 10 mm setting beyond the required 25 mm free drop. Vertically and laterally separate ordinary upper outlets and their exterior overflow routes from these low receivers. The roof, separate wet cells and inspection access need a measured lawful downhill destination without a shared flood, snow or ice obstruction. A backwater at or above **Z68.33 / Z168.33** consumes the nominal free-drop provision. [Rectangular weir sensitivity](https://www.usbr.gov/tsc/techreferences/mands/wmm/chap02_09.html)

Compare a captured **8 mm metal impact panel** on at most **60 mm clear supported spans**. A separate ideal full Ø28-area 9 bar jet gives 1.108 kN stopped normal. A full reflection and 1.5 force factor give **3.325 kN**, a simple strip bending stress of **167 MPa** and nominal 4.73 MPa local punching shear. Transfer at least 1.663 kN per rib for symmetric comparison before side eccentricity, welds, anchors, fasteners and plate discontinuities. The much lower 63.64 N stopped-jet momentum applies only if the 90 L/min source is independently capped and actual free speed bounded by 9 bar. This chamber remains a vented impact enclosure. Never rate its complete cover as a 9 bar pressure vessel from a local strip. Test welds, removable lid stiffness and captive isolation, through-sleeve impingement, exterior union strikes, inboard strikes and a submerged or blocked relief as different events.

The boxes fit behind the rear service strip. They cannot be fully serviced with the washer installed. With it restrained on its existing north rest at Y1500, the proposed X10575/Y1050 standing position has 140 mm after setting from the washer. Close the electrical working field during the temporary maintenance state. Daily branch cuts must stay outside this enclosure in a convenient dry position. Model the actual cap and socket sweep, captive baffle and closure first rest, positive axial sleeve extraction, fixed reaction supports and return to dry use before claiming overhaul access.
