# Two separately isolated supplies through the south utility wall

**Prefer the south utility wall for the next inlet design. Retain the indoor inlet reference until the new pressure and building interfaces close.** The Boundary Service Lodge splits domestic cold into a utility branch and a plant branch. Keep both joint-free through the permanent wall, individually isolated and renewable. This moves the common source-fed tee and its working connections outside the house and could remove the three-drain bearing-wall opening from the design. ## Require a pressure-contained sleeve and separate passive receivers

Place branch split fittings and the openable outside unions behind independent wet shields in their own thermal compartments, with visible separate free spills below. Carry each straight, joint-free pressure segment through a metal secondary sleeve to its own supported indoor end box. Capture an inward end-face strike, an outside union strike and a rupture under the sleeve separately. Show that a closed box intercepts the jet without pressurising the masonry sleeve, wetting a dry approach or sharing the other branch receiving path. A wholly blocked or submerged outlet defeats this comparison and must remain an adverse case. The site must bound the actual steady incoming fault independently of pressurised cylinder and hot D2 inventories.

An Ø100 clear annulus around the full Ø74 jacket gives **4.95 L/min** below the jacket at 1:50. A clear Ø150 with the jacket gives 98.08 L/min clean at 30 mm head but falls below 39 L/min for a half-width obstruction at the same head, and the larger opening loses the structural edge allowance at these centres. Use a separate metal sleeve, provisionally **100 mm clear plus 2 mm metal and 3 mm fit per side**, and keep the lower channel free of the jacket and supports. Draw the Ø22 pressure tube **18 mm above the sleeve axis**, co-pitched at 1:50, with the thermal layer outside the channel and protected independent end boxes. At 52 mm depth, the half-width calculation leaves 5 mm below the bare tube and passes **72.78 L/min**, versus **35.36 L/min** for the centred tube. The clean raised case gives 202.89 L/min under the same idealisation. The Ø28 dimensional comparison passes **66.19 L/min** at 49 mm and leaves 18 mm at the crown before installation offsets. Both half-width shapes fail 90 L/min. This is uniform gravity arithmetic with n=0.015, no favourable transient pressure or tailwater, and a specified half-width section. It cannot qualify a wet pressure end box, an actual sleeve inlet, a full-width raised lip or a receiving obstruction.

**Reject a passive restriction as the present basis for the 60 L/min limit.** A fixed quadratic resistance must consume at least `Pmax × (Q/60)²` bar at demand `Q` to cap a downstream open failure at 60 L/min. The Megaflo Eco Plus comparator requires 50 L/min and 1.5 bar while flowing. Comparing 6 and 9 bar static fault heads gives at least **5.67 and 7.75 bar dynamic** at the Lodge before pipe, altitude or connection losses. At 5 bar dynamic neither case works. The comparator does not supply the matched vessel or simultaneous house duty. A long small-bore run also loses its assumed full-length resistance in a near-Lodge rupture. Credit a fixed restriction only after establishing the maximum head at the lowest failure, minimum residual pressure at combined demand and the selected plant/fixture flow. Otherwise size physically independent outlets for the demonstrated fault rate or separate service and fault flow by redesign. [Megaflo installation instructions](https://www.heatraesadia.com/-/media/themes/heatrae/literature/installation-manuals/megaflo-eco-plus-400_500_570_7032406_issue_02.pdf)

The continuous bare pressure segment is about **580 mm** including 30 mm under thermal protection at each end. The installed terminations, slope-compatible supports, condensation drainage and no-demand freeze limit are unverified. Preserve separate private protected burial. A continuously air-filled cover channel does not inherit the thermal behaviour of soil. The new architecture is ready for a joined wall-end/support and wet-boundary design, while the controlling inlet architecture and packet remain open.
