# HAD-03 — Bedroom 3 after the proposed suite relocation

**Unadopted furnished-room comparison.** Moving the second en suite to Bedroom 4
must also leave a usable third bedroom. This [A3 plan](studies/bedroom3-reuse-study.svg)
and [exact geometry](studies/bedroom3-reuse-study.json) test a real double base,
hanging/shelf storage and connected approach points without calling an empty
former bathroom a finished room.

The proposal moves the former en-suite west partition from x 9500 to x 9300
only between y 2650 and 3850. The local east gallery becomes **1070 clear**,
against its current 1270 and the source west gallery's 1015. Remove the old
south partition east of x 9300; retain the family bathroom boundary at y 3850.
The former service lining, branch and panel are not simply erased: IAS must
record their actual removal, retention or reuse and the revised bathroom access.
This is a local width tradeoff, not permission to narrow every circulation route.

Use the previously sourced [Hypnos actual base and headboard family](https://www.hypnoscontractbeds.com/download_file/c3247136-15b6-4036-a286-48200a53d354/6776),
printed pages 21/27: **1500 × 2000** base at x 9387.5/y 1697.5, with a **90**
headboard against the solid bathroom boundary. The continuous east bedside
field is **695**, the southern foot field **1280**, and the west gap **25**.
The last is deliberately **not a usable bedside route**. This is an ordinary
one-sided double-bed comparison and a visible compromise for a secondary room,
not a claim about the owner's household or two-person access. It avoids a
headboard in front of the existing external windows.

The [IKEA PAX/GRIMO 195.030.20](https://www.ikea.com/gb/en/p/pax-grimo-wardrobe-white-white-s19503020/)
manufacturer configuration, retrieved 29 October 2026, is **1000 × 600 × 2012**,
with two nominal 500 doors, a hanging rail and shelves. It lies along the west
partition at x 7262.5/y 1300. Handles are separate, and no extending drawers are
assumed. Wall anchorage, two-person assembly and replacement remain real tasks.
A separate drawing state shows both wardrobe doors at 90 degrees with an
**authored 25 mm leaf/handle envelope**. A 600-diameter body can occupy the
working space between them while the bedroom entrance is closed. That is an
open-state clearance check, not a complete proof of opening the wardrobe,
reaching every shelf or handling its packed components.

The six connected paths check a 600-diameter circle over every whole axis
segment to the bed foot, east bedside, both front-window approaches, east-window
approach and wardrobe. The gallery is real approach floor, clearly labelled as
outside the bedroom. The 900 source entrance aperture remains; actual installed
lining, stops, leaf, hardware and closing action are not included in those
positive route claims. Likewise a window approach point is not proof of sash
movement, handle reach, cleaning, privacy, curtain operation or safe glazing.
Mattress making, emergency assistance and carrying still need their own actions.
