# Preparing the first owner design review

This assembly map identifies the drawings and decision pages to bring together for `HAD-OWNER-DESIGN-REVIEW`. Issue the coordinated pack after `TRIAD-GATE` on a named house revision. The comparisons below are available for preparation now.

## Suggested drawing order

| Page | Start from | Resolve before issuing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. House and court | [Furnished house plans](../ias/studies/workable-house-plans.svg), [principal section](../../docs/reference-house/publication-principal-section.svg), [front elevation](../../docs/reference-house/publication-principal-front-elevation.svg) | Check the published plan, section and elevation against the selected TRIAD model. Show the main rooms, approaches, daylight and the relationship between the Georgian front and court. |
| 2. Roof, court and arrival | [Joined court plans](studies/court-b-joined-plans.svg), [court sections](studies/court-b-joined-sections.svg), [roof-water reconciliation](../rof/roof-water-reconciliation.md) | Replace any provisional roof sections with the integrated bearing, collector, drain and renewal arrangement. Show the visible effect of edges, outlets and access. Carry the actual inlet and plant access arrangement into the service-side plan. |
| 3. Window and reveal | [Conventional opening detail study](../oas/studies/nominal-interface-studies.svg), [opening renewal sequence](../oas/studies/selected-opening-renewal.svg), [interior reveal comparison](../ias/studies/workable-measured-sections.svg), [local Study carrier comparison](../oas/study-subframe-comparison.md) | Reconcile the existing side-by-side Study comparison with the selected TRIAD support and boundaries. | 4. Show where broad panelling changes room character, reveal depth and maintenance, and where direct mineral finishes remain practical. |
| 5. | 6. List what each changes in room area, finish and frame depth, appearance, ease of maintenance and evidence needed for detail design. |

## Decision sheet for the issued pack

| Decision | Side-by-side alternatives | Record against the issued drawings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Floor fields | Liftable finish in the nominated dry areas, or a continuous finish with local access | Rooms and exact field limits. Include the seam, movement and lifting consequences. |
| Wall composition | Broad panelling, selective panelling with local service routes, or direct mineral walls | Rooms, panel rhythm, corners and reveals. Attach material and finish samples or visual specifications. |
| Skirting and metalwork | Ordinary skirting, local removable service skirting, and a separately supported grille or access cover where a named task needs it | Locations, visible profiles and finish, and how each opens and reinstates. |
| Openings | Conventional renewable frame, or a retained local subframe at the tested occurrence | Openings, visible frame and reveal, removal direction and reclosure sequence. |
| Court and exterior | Integrated roof and court proposal with its material and service choices | Visible edges, outlets, guards and access points. Identify any design revision requested across adjacent drawings. |

Give each line a drawing locator and a recorded **accept, revise or reject** decision on the exact presented revision. Carry open technical checks with the line they affect. At final design acceptance show the rendered book, current drawings and the changes since this first decision. The [PLT handoff](../plt/refurbishment-handoff.md) records the later review and print duties.
