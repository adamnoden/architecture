## Question and starting object

Can the reader encounter a concrete house and consequential interventions before granting significance to the framework, without inventing a resolved architectural payoff? This pass begins from accepted main `db3661b1` after EDA-ARGUMENT and EDA-ADAPTER. The preceding exact production proof is PR #1077, head `b6f96d5d353c02cd3509343541efd83d8f363ae3`, run `40981763689`: A4 281 pages and B5 334. Its house begins on A4 page 171 / B5 page 207; the complete reference catalogue precedes it.

The six-part sequence is retained only for its distinct functions below. Part IV and VI remain distinct because one explains the proposition and the other must eventually show the reconciled object. The latter cannot yet supply a completed architectural payoff; that limitation belongs to HRS, not to an editorial invention.

This is an alternative reading path, not an assertion that every architect will find the sequence compelling. Actual reader testing has not occurred.

## Subtraction and ownership

The preface falls from 2,634 to 2,108 whitespace-delimited words; the guide from 1,330 to 714. Paxton was the fourth cross-domain case and repeated the outsider justification already made directly. Its removal is an editorial return judgement, not a rejection of the historical precedent. Thorpe supplies a spatial reorganisation, the kitchen supplies embodied rehearsal, and the container supplies a common interface: those examples perform different explanatory jobs. The Ovid ending and material/ordinary-room aspirations retain the author's voice. The unused Paxton note is removed and the remaining local Ovid note renumbered; stable chapter, pattern and source identities are preserved.

The guide no longer duplicates ten glossary definitions or an abstract stack of categories. Its present-house and three intervention paragraphs replace them. The existing Glossary remains the definition authority. Part III's opening now directs consultation to the later catalogue; Part V gains 104 words identifying actual conventional choices and unresolved adverse cases before discussing future duties. The short reference opening earns its space by identifying the transition into consultation and preserving active/strategy/held distinctions. Across these five reader sources the net change is -930 whitespace-delimited words; no claim is made that a word count certifies editorial quality.

The manifest owns order. Publication Architecture owns the changed reading roles. The new reference opening is authored in `publication/pattern-reference.md`, using the existing back-matter role to reset running context to Appendices. No prose is hidden in an adapter, and no new pagination engine, typography role or special context mechanism is introduced. The catalogue entries keep their exact IDs, sources, titles, kinds and relative order. Canonical pattern content, doctrine, figure/plate bindings, HRS Part IV/VI and Primer framing remain unchanged.
