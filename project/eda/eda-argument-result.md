**Result:** the argument review is complete at the present design-research boundary. The defensible contribution is a documented method of comparing domestic interventions and accepting limits on flexibility. The evidence does not establish a new construction system, superior whole-life performance or a resolved, compelling house.

## Central proposition and reading basis

Design the owner's home by tracing consequential interventions from approach and isolation through removal, damage and reinstatement; provide extra space or interfaces only where their demonstrated benefit earns the installation, occupation and eventual renewal burden. Ordinary well-detailed construction remains the control and often the preferred answer.

That proposition is useful as an accountable design discipline. Its strongest present evidence is the ability to reject attractive but disproportionate provisions and expose unresolved physical tasks. It is not evidence that the method is unique, that every proposed interface works, or that the resulting house is architecturally excellent. Room quality, light, privacy, arrival, section and garden/court relationships still have to earn the house on architectural terms.

The review read the actual preface, Parts I, II, IV and V, the Part VI placeholder, the authored Part III introduction, evidence notes, publication architecture and current programme. It inspected the prior compact-terrace/hostile-intervention screen, current IAS/OAS selections, BCR proportionality and service/water findings, HAD room/court/kitchen studies and the corrected computational results below. This is a contribution review, not a new full-corpus stylistic audit or final physical print acceptance. The existing preface already acknowledges ordinary expertise and inherited ideas; a long preface is not by itself evidence of a false novelty claim.

## Four inspectable interventions

These comparisons extend the existing screening evidence with the current selected families and adverse findings. None is narrated as an operation already performed in a built house. The excellent conventional control includes competent support, boundaries, working access and making-good; it is not careless concealment.

| Intervention | Before / during / after comparison | Present consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Renew a dry finish or reach a local service | Before: direct finish and local grounds versus a universal removable field. During: ordinary work may sacrifice local finish; a panel needs a real first release, handling field and supported edges. After: patch/decoration versus reseating, joint quality, solidity and replacement-face duties. BCR's lining comparison adds 30 mm per treated face; its floor challenger adds 40 mm to the 350 mm reservation | **Conventional winner at current selection scope.** IAS retains ordinary dry finishes and H0. Those depth differences are package space, not solid material or carbon. No intervention frequency or physical panel performance repays a universal grid |
| Replace a window frame | Before: conventional mechanically fixed E0 with separate trim versus a continuous retained cassette. During: reveal fixings, support and remove the actual frame, interrupt and renew seals, use actual inside/outside working access. After: reinstate air/weather boundaries and finish; inspect any retained component rather than assuming it immortal. A hypothetical extra cassette perimeter is 6.2 m for a 1000 × 2100 opening | **Conventional family selected, detail conditional.** [OAS selection](../../docs/reference-house/opening-assembly-selection.md) avoids an unearned continuous cassette. A designed mounting interface remains useful, but no universal inside-only frame exchange or passed weathering/replacement test follows |
| Service a wet component or replace plant | Before: local front-service cupboard/product versus remote rear access, with connections and isolations located. During: tool reach, disconnection, temporary support and the whole component route. After: reconnect, test, dry and reinstate independent boundaries. A nominal access face is not a withdrawal path for a ventilation unit or cylinder | **Task-led conditional choice.** BCR-SERVICES exposes the missing packing/handling proof. HAD's larger wet-suite comparison deliberately accepts a separate stack and living-room maintenance cost rather than hiding services in a nominal void. No product/system sizing or fitted network is accepted |
| Clear or renew roof/court drainage after blockage | Before: inward/outward ring and an ordinary gravity route requiring its own complete specification versus proposed accessible strips or changed roof topology. During: reach, clear retained water, remove actual pieces and maintain safe occupied boundaries. After: restore supports, waterproofing and usable walking surface. Outward-only roof geometry reduces inner collection but raises the court termination; it still leaves direct court rainfall | **Adverse result, no adopted solution.** Current RWT/HAD studies do not establish the first vulnerable interface, complete supported crossing or safe receiving route. Covers and raised paving add maintenance/load duties. Visibility and an authored overflow line cannot substitute for hydraulic or physical independence |

The evidence supports simplification where an ordinary solution meets the duty. It supplies no matched quotations, observed renewal frequencies, applicable whole-life material inventories or demonstrated saving. Permanent maintenance infrastructure can itself consume scarce room area, daylight, thresholds and owner attention. The owner must eventually manage actual faults and access, not preserve an elaborate system merely because the book describes it.

## Transfer and audience

The earlier T-01 screen used a hypothetical 105–120 m² two-storey terrace with party walls, front/rear access only and no assumed rear alley. It already rejected literal transfer of the dedicated service flank, large plant room, side replacement aperture and whole-house access infrastructure. Its predecessor-house dimensions and historical dispositions remain historical; this review does not repin them to the current house.

Current evidence strengthens three of its decision-level conclusions: ordinary dry interiors survive without a universal access layer; conventional renewable frames can preserve the opening without a cassette; and actual equipment/route duty must decide whether a service cupboard or special space is justified. Current water and wet-suite difficulties also limit the earlier claim that concentration itself solves failure geography. A stacked wet cluster may shorten a route while still failing at a trap, joist, socket, inaccessible joint or receiving drain.

The admitted audience is therefore the owner and designers reviewing a particular pre-site home, with bounded intervention comparisons that may inform other projects. The work does not establish a transferable compact-house plan, an affordable housing system or universal service geography. A constrained real project must re-test its own route, area, tenure, access and performance consequences. No second complete house is invented to manufacture generality.

## Primary lineage checks

Checked 29–30 October 2026. These sources establish prior ideas and practices, not the performance of this house. The checks are deliberately limited to accessible primary text; no claim is made to have freshly read every cited book or a paid standard in full.

- Habraken's own [introduction](https://www.habraken.com/html/introduction.htm) and [Supports account](https://www.habraken.com/html/supports.htm) describe intervention levels, participants and replaceable systems. Support/infill is importantly a distinction of control and design responsibility, not merely a technical assembly diagram.
- Brand's [author essay](https://stewartbrand.org/pace-layering-how-complex-systems-learn-and-keep-learning/) explicitly credits Duffy's four-layer model and describes his expansion to six. This verifies the acknowledged lineage through Brand's primary account; it is not a new direct reading of Duffy's original text. The manuscript already disclaims conceptual advance beyond that lineage.
- The [ISO 20887 public abstract](https://www.iso.org/standard/69370.html) establishes an existing discipline of design for disassembly/adaptability and design-process strategies. Its abstract does not certify any project performance, and the full standard was not read here.
- [Historic England's service guidance](https://historicengland.org.uk/advice/technical-advice/building-services-engineering/installing-new-services/) already joins reversibility, limited fabric damage, reused routes and accessible maintenance/replacement. Its historic-building context differs from a new house, but the underlying repair duties cannot be claimed as newly discovered.
- The official [CIBSE M1 catalogue](https://www.cibse.org/knowledge-research/knowledge-portal/guide-m1-guidance-for-designers-2023/) identifies maintainability guidance for services designers. This is a catalogue/scope check, not inspection of the full paid guide.
- Alexander's [author/CES site](https://www.patternlanguage.com/) identifies the established pattern-language work and its sequence across town, building and construction scales. The project's service sequence is a local synthesis; a catalogue of named patterns alone earns no equivalent generative or general validity.
- buildingSMART's [IFC terminology](https://user.buildingsmart.org/knowledge-base/terminology/) and [IDS description](https://www.buildingsmart.org/Standards/Bsi-Standards/Information-Delivery-Specification-Ids/) already establish semantic objects/relations and computer-interpretable information requirements. IDS's declared alphanumeric boundary is not a geometric solver. The project cannot claim novelty merely for representing or checking information.

## Strongest objections and accountable dispositions

1. **This is competent practice with new names.** Substantially fair. The useful contribution is an inspectable domestic application, including rejected choices and reinstatement costs. No first-invention, immense-value or superiority claim survives merely from internal coherence.
2. **The house has not earned the method's architectural promise.** Fair. HAD/HRS must resolve room use and demonstrate architectural quality with alternatives and drawings. EDA cannot supply that merit through prose; unresolved wet and water geometry remains capable of rewinding the design.
3. **Prepared success scenarios hide damage and owner burden.** The four comparisons retain ordinary making-good, seal renewal, inaccessible connections, accepted later renovation and the maintenance of added access systems. Part V's unearned water, roof, dry-side-access and stair-fallback outcomes are corrected at their owning source.
4. **A generous detached fixture proves little about constrained housing.** The existing terrace screen is retained and its present applicability narrowed. No area-efficient, cost-efficient or morphology-neutral house system is claimed.
5. **Computational detail can overwhelm architectural judgement.** The three requests above separate candidate studies, repaired correctness and propagation from physical merit. EDA-NARRATIVE should retain the small architectural payoff and move technical apparatus to the companion.
6. **The book asks for significance before showing a house.** The preface and repeated constitution/platform/pattern sequence deserve structural testing, but that belongs to EDA-NARRATIVE. Use an early provisional house encounter and these comparisons to test the reading order; do not fabricate a resolved Part VI or remove qualifications just to improve pace.
