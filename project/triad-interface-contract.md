# IAS / OAS / HAD — shared interface contract

**Scope:** candidate-design interface freeze, not selected-system or construction freeze.
**House:** `RH-LAYOUT-REVISION-02` / `RH-LAYOUT-FAMILY-02`.
**Owners:** IAS, OAS and HAD; canonical gate `TRIAD-IFACE-FREEZE`.

## Inputs and claim boundary

This contract composes [IAS-00](ias/ias-00-baseline-and-challengers.md),
[OAS-00](oas/oas-00-baseline-and-invariants.md) and
[HAD-00](had/had-00-brief-result.md). Their controls and challengers are retained:
no new assembly, opening product or architectural selection is invented by
calling their common boundary frozen.

[architectural brief](../docs/reference-house/architectural-design-brief.md)
and `project/design-constraints.ts` govern applicability.
Historical fixtures remain reproducible at their own scope.

## Candidate obligations

Each candidate must state:

- its actual room/opening/task and source applicability;
- conventional control and the reason any added system earns its burden;
- finished faces, layer budgets, projection and any changed dimension;
- the first release, ordinary tool approach and withdrawal/handling route;
- which permanent boundary stays intact, which interface is interrupted and
  how reinstatement is checked;
- visible expression before and after normal wear, access and reinstatement;
- credible water contact, declaration, isolation and drying questions;
- conflicts, evidence limits and the owner of the next decision.

Use a small representative family. Do not enumerate a bespoke system at every
occurrence before one useful condition works. Candidate completion is not
selection, installation approval or evidence of performance.

## Carried coordination obligations

### I00-C1 — upper small-en-suite service front

IAS-00 found the 700 mm reservation at y 3450 overlaps its nominal return by
12.5 mm. The comparison centred at y 3300 is an unadopted alternative, not an
approved panel. IAS-01V / IAS-02S / IAS-02W must resolve the actual task, frame,
returns and handling. HAD must not decorate the current rectangle as a viable
installed access opening. The source remains unchanged by this contract.

### Roof and courtyard water

Current inner slopes make courtyard collection, overflow and maintenance
material questions. Earlier outward-only statements, historical low-slope roof
evidence and the no-flat-roof constraint do not resolve them. HAD candidates
must show the rainwater geography honestly; MRA-01 later assaults the selected
house. A hidden internal basin or inaccessible gutter is not cured by a cornice.

### Opening and room depth

OAS retains separate masonry/frame/visible/clear/withdrawal dimensions. The
nominal 60 mm frame face, 80 mm sill projection, head profile and section head
zone are drawn choices, not fabrication sections. IAS/HAD do not invent extra
reveal space or universal full-frame inside removal.

### Small rooms, service rooms and entrance void

The compact en suite still needs actual sanitary/door fit. Plant replacement
still needs selected equipment and carrying geometry. Entrance void/bridge,
guarding, stair headroom and fire/structure remain unresolved. A candidate may
bound or reject an arrangement; it cannot publish these as solved through
furniture symbols, attractive panelling or a nominal walking-grid result.

## Change protocol

1. Identify the conflicting task or relationship, not merely a preferred image.
2. Check whether a simpler conventional arrangement or local reallocation fits
   without moving a controlled face or transferring a boundary duty.
3. If not, record a bounded rewind with old/new value, affected source, reason,
   space/appearance consequences and all dependent views/interfaces.
4. Change the owning source only under that explicit decision; reconcile
   affected plan/section/elevation, assemblies and PLT projections together.
5. Reassess evidence applicability. Historical proof is retained, not silently
   relabelled as validation of the successor.

A proposed rewind is not permission to draw the changed geometry as adopted.
Unknown product mass, capacity, tolerances, flows and performance stay unknown.

## Gate and falsifiers

The freeze is satisfied when the three constitutions are complete, they name
the same house, budgets have compatible meanings, every shared interface has
a primary owner and the provisional questions above remain explicit.

It fails if a stream assumes spare depth, a different opening schedule/roof,
an invisible environmental boundary, unowned water duty or a silent geometry
fork. The selected systems can still require a later rewind; that does not
retroactively turn an interface freeze into construction proof.

The gate unlocks IAS-01V/01H, OAS-01E/01I and HAD-01E/01P/01S. Junctions, material
synthesis, selection, TRIAD-GATE, MRA and BCR retain their own dependencies.
No physical test or competent review has been completed by this contract.
