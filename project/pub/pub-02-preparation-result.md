## Paper and cloth comparators

**S1 — Arctic Paper, [Munken Pure product specification](https://www.arcticpaper.com/our-products/our-brands/munken/munken-design-range/munken-pure/), inspected 30 October 2026.** The manufacturer describes cream, smooth uncoated paper and publishes the following target values. It expressly allows those targets to change. General printing suitability does not establish compatibility with a particular press, grain, supplied sheet or the binder's sewing/imposition process.

| Munken Pure candidate | Target opacity | Target sheet caliper | Calculated raw paper stack per 100 printed pages |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 90 g/m² | 90% | 117 µm | 5.85 mm |
| 100 g/m² | 92% | 113 µm | 5.65 mm |
| 120 g/m² | 94% | 136 µm | 6.80 mm |

Calculation: `raw stack mm = (printed page count / 2) × sheet caliper µm / 1000`, for an even count of two-sided pages on that one stock. This is a comparison, **not finished spine width**. Final blanks and separate stocks must be counted; endpapers, sewing swell, compression, spine treatment, adhesives and case construction remain outside it. The 90/100 comparison demonstrates why weight alone cannot determine thickness. Opacity targets do not prove acceptable show-through behind small labels, grey hatching or dense text. Obtain actual duplex samples before choosing.

**S2 — Winter & Company, [Wicotex Brillianta technical datasheet, February 2025](https://storage.googleapis.com/wintercompany-ecom/product_files/Datasheet_Wicotex_Brillianta.pdf?language=en&region=us), inspected 30 October 2026.** This is a paper-lined woven viscose cloth candidate. The manufacturer lists foil stamping subject to testing. Its approximate 0.31 mm cloth thickness is not board or case thickness. A physical oxblood shade/lot and dull-gold foil trial remain necessary; the colour displayed by a default web page is not selected. Confirm small-quantity supply through the eventual binder rather than assuming a whole roll or custom production is appropriate for twelve books. No claim of colour, foil adhesion or wear performance is earned here.

These named materials make a bounded enquiry possible. They are neither a product selection nor a claim that a binder stocks them. A proposed substitute must return its own shade, surface, opacity/caliper, press behaviour and construction implications for comparison; a similar grammage or colour name is insufficient.

## Physical review and final gate

A later authorised blank dummy can test heft, opening and construction before content is frozen. A duplex printed section on the actual proposed stock/press can test show-through, ink behaviour and the accepted type/line hierarchy. A cloth/foil coupon can test the restrained title treatment. These are ways to obtain the existing physical evidence, not newly claimed verdicts or additional automatic DAG gates. Samples made on different materials or processes must disclose that limitation.

Record reviewer, date, object/source identity, stock and construction, observed problems and keep/revise/reject decisions. In particular, test access to inner text/critical drawing information without forcing the spine, page turning, dense-versus-light reverse-page show-through, fine monochrome distinctions, cloth/foil appearance and case/joint behaviour. An early dummy cannot certify final pagination or the enlarged house's drawings. A digital proof cannot certify paper, sewing or opening. Material or construction substitution invalidates the affected sample inference and requires its owning review again.
