<p class="home-kicker">Ovid’s House</p>
<h1>Permanence<br>without stasis.</h1>

Ovid’s House explores how a home can endure while the things inside it change. The aim is to make maintenance, repair and renewal part of the architecture, so that replacing a pipe or a window need not mean damaging the building around it.

## A house worth keeping

Durability is only part of the question. The rooms must also be comfortable, well proportioned and useful to successive generations. Adaptability belongs where it helps; ordinary construction should win where it works better.
