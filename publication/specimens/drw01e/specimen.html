Drawing doctrine specimen

This bounded proof tests the publication drawing doctrine before any Reference House renderer is implemented. The geometry in these specimen plates is deliberately synthetic: it exists to test line hierarchy, notation, page classes and physical legibility, not to make architectural claims about the house.

The flow condition remains prose-led. Small abstract figures can sit inside reading text; controlled architectural drawings escalate to dedicated plates rather than being shrunk until they fit.

Dedicated plates on the following pages keep drawing annotation in physical paper units: 0.50 / 0.35 / 0.25 / 0.18 mm line roles, 7.5 pt principal labels and 6.5 pt notes/dimensions.

Proof target: A4 at current publication margins and ISO B5 (176 × 250 mm) as the conservative smaller-than-A4 robustness surface. B5 is not selected as the final PR-02 trim.
