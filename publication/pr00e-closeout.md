# PR-00E — Whole-Book Editorial Hardening — Closeout

**Status:** PASS  
**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Programme:** PR-00 — Candidate-Book Freeze Execution Programme  
**Entry baseline:** `12c3d7ce0ca1de88c4bdb99081418018716aeeb9`  
**Umbrella issue:** #71 — PUB-01 publication credibility and release programme

PR-00E asked whether the completed candidate still read like a cleaned export of project documentation rather than one architectural monograph.

The answer is now **no**, with an important qualification: the book is intentionally heterogeneous. Parts I–II are argument, Part III is reference, Part IV is a worked house, Part V is making/testing, and the appendices are documentary. Editorial hardening should clarify those mode changes, not flatten them into one voice.

## E1 — front matter and preface — PASS

The Reader’s Guide contained the clearest surviving publication-process residue.

Corrected:

- removed `candidate-book reconciliation point` language;
- removed the instruction that the web edition is the better place for provenance/live project state;
- restated maturity in direct reader-facing present tense;
- removed `current publication` phrasing where the book should simply describe itself.

The Preface remains substantially unchanged. Its corridor / kitchen / container / Paxton sequence earns the outsider-method argument rather than narrating project administration. Its late Ovidian section remains the right place to explain the title; the rest of the book should not repeat that explanation as branding.

One literal repository-path reference remains in Preface note [5]. That is not an argument-continuity defect; it is a cross-reference/provenance defect and is explicitly transferred to PR-00F.

## E2 — Parts I and II — PASS, no material rewrite

The audit found Parts I and II to be the strongest continuous prose in the manuscript.

No broad compression was made. Apparent repetitions around proportionality, access and removability usually return the same proposition at a different architectural scale and therefore carry structural rather than accidental repetition.

In particular:

- the progression from changing timescales → selective permanence → architectural platform remains clear;
- the Ovidian frame is concentrated in the selective-permanence argument rather than scattered through Part II;
- repose remains evidence-bounded rather than made more rhetorically certain;
- the designed-interface, failure and maintenance arguments now gain visual compression from D-F02→04 without requiring their explanatory prose to be removed.

Rewriting these chapters merely to demonstrate editorial activity would reduce specificity and increase manifesto language.

## E3 — Parts III and IV — PASS, mode difference retained

Part III deliberately changes register from argument to reference. Its opening hinge — `A house cannot be drawn from principles alone.` — makes that change legible.

D-F05 now carries the visual distinction between the five reading fields and one generative service-topology sequence. The surrounding prose remains because it explains forces and limits that the diagram cannot carry cleanly. No dense all-pattern dependency graph or repository authoring narrative has been reintroduced.

Part IV succeeds as a worked interpretation rather than a gate log. Its authority qualifications were reviewed for repetition. Most are retained because they govern materially different claims:

- no commissioned site;
- G-01 does not own courtyard morphology or project dialect;
- structural coordination is not structural adequacy;
- service geography is not exact MEP design;
- roof/failure geometry is not hydraulic capacity.

These are not one repeated disclaimer. They are different boundaries at the points where a reader could otherwise infer too much from increasingly concrete drawings.

The new D-F06→11 figures carry orientation and spatial relationships that previously had to be held in prose, but the existing prose is not merely a verbal duplicate of those figures.

## E4 — Part V and ending — PASS, no structural rewrite

Part V is procedural because the proposition must survive making, procurement, commissioning and later intervention. That procedural character is earned rather than accidental.

The editorial test was whether the book disappeared into administration by the end. It does not.

Two late propositions recover the architecture strongly:

> **rewind before workaround**

and the final criterion:

> **The platform succeeds when maintenance becomes less destructive without making ordinary life feel like maintenance.**

No separate conclusion chapter is added. The existing ending returns to settled domestic life and is stronger for being brief.

## E5 — closeout reread

The primary Parts I–V path now contains no known candidate-book/gate/PR narration required to understand the project. Publication-specific control remains outside the manuscript under `publication/`.

The remaining integrity tasks are appropriately PR-00F work rather than editorial rewriting:

- stable PAT-* identity and strategy/candidate distinctions;
- chapter / figure / back-matter cross-references;

No upstream architectural contradiction was found. No authority or maturity is promoted by PR-00E.
