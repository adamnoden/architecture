R7 did not find that the pattern corpus was empty or incoherent. It found that the **language-level claim was ahead of the demonstrated generative structure**.

R8-D therefore tests the classification rather than defending the established label.

# 1. P005 admission rerun

`HSA-P-005 — Designed Structural Penetration` received the strongest individual taxonomy challenge.

The old wording was vulnerable because its imperative—coordinate penetrations before construction—could be read as ordinary professional procedure rather than a pattern.

## Admission result

| Admission criterion | Result | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| recurrence | **PASS** | routes crossing structure/protected construction recur across domestic systems and construction families |
| context | **PASS** | applies only where a route actually crosses slower/protected fabric |
| forces | **PASS** | route economy, host integrity, boundary continuity, future capacity, tolerance and access genuinely conflict |
| relationship | **PASS — narrow** | the architectural invariant is `route → controlled crossing interface → host/protected boundary`; “coordinate the hole” alone would fail |
| variation | **PASS** | ordinary engineered opening, sleeve, framed opening, grouped penetration, collar/closure and other families can satisfy the invariant |
| diagrammability | **PASS** | host, route, crossing geometry and boundary transition are spatially legible |
| consequences | **PASS** | every crossing spends permanent fabric and creates structural/boundary/reinstatement obligations |
| composition | **PASS** | completes horizontal-route and high-service-room relationships when those routes cross protected construction |
| evidential honesty | **PASS** | standard professional practice supports the duty; the page now explicitly disclaims novelty and special-system necessity |

## Decision

**RETAIN P005 as an active pattern, but narrow it to the physical crossing-interface relationship.**

R8-D rewrites the pattern statement so the process instruction is no longer masquerading as the invariant. It also adds an explicit conventional implementation: a standard engineer-designed opening can satisfy the pattern completely.

The review does not demote P005 merely to demonstrate severity. The admission test is allowed to return PASS.

This result is consistent with R8-C: a compact terrace can satisfy P005 through ordinary engineered coordination with no special Ovid-specific penetration family.

# 2. Corpus-level relationship audit

R2 already read every active pattern, every strategy and every held candidate. R8-D does not repeat that reading to manufacture more edges.

The durable conclusion is uneven by domain.

## Service topology

The strongest language-like structure is real:

- controlled utility entry;
- service hub;
- vertical distribution where justified;
- high-service-room service walls;
- coherent horizontal distribution;
- compartmented voids / controlled crossings;
- water-failure responses;
- room-scale routes;
- physical stewardship identity.

The existing `service-topology` sequence orders those moves, creates information for later stages and contains explicit rewind conditions. It is genuinely generative rather than a list.

## Other families

The opening / movement / attachment, water/failure, external-maintenance and extraction patterns contain recurring architectural relationships, but they are **not yet demonstrated as one equally mature generative network**.

Examples such as:

- Permanent Opening / Replaceable Window;
- Permanent Opening / Replaceable Door;
- Designed Threshold;
- Movement / Slip Junction;
- Controlled Attachment Plane;
- Visible Leakage Path;
- Failure-Tolerant Wet Service Room;
- Accessible Rainwater Route;
- Roof Maintenance Route;
- Ground-Supported Façade Access

can be valid patterns without pretending that a canonical sequence among all of them has been established.

R8-D therefore adds **no synthetic relationship edges** and no new sequence merely to satisfy the word “language”.

---

# 3. Status decision

The smallest truthful reader-facing status is now:

> **a pattern set with demonstrated generative sequences, not a uniformly mature pattern language.**

The public pattern index has therefore been retitled:

**Patterns and generative sequences**

and now states explicitly that:

- 21 patterns remain active;
- service topology is the strongest mature sequence;
- other families have uneven connectivity/generative maturity;
- that asymmetry is evidence about the current work, not a graph-completion task.

This does **not** forbid the term “pattern language” as historical influence, modelling concept or future maturity target. It removes the blanket claim that the whole present corpus has already earned Alexander-like language status.

R8-F owns the final book-wide nomenclature reconciliation so old part titles, Reader’s Guide references and structural records do not retain a stronger claim than the corrected index.

---

# 4. Stable IDs

R7 also found that stable `HSA-P-*` identities carried too much reader-facing weight.

The IDs remain valuable for:

- provenance;
- computational crosswalks;
- occurrence registers;
- long-running references;
- retirement discipline.

They are not architectural titles.

The pattern index now presents **name first, ID second**. R8-F should preserve this hierarchy wherever publication formatting makes the ID compete with the name.

No IDs are renumbered. No historical computational result is rewritten to pretend the IDs never mattered.

---

# 5. Scope of the corpus

The current set is strongest where Ovid’s House is strongest and most distinctive:

- service geography;
- maintenance;
- lifecycle separation;
- failure consequences;
- openings / replaceable assemblies;
- movement / attachment;
- external maintenance;
- stewardship.

It is **not** a complete language for domestic architecture. It does not attempt to generate room composition, social programme, style, landscape, urban form or architectural grammar as a whole.

G-01 remains a separate grammar layer. Repose remains primarily doctrinal/architectural judgement rather than being forced into a new pattern family for balance.

The absence of patterns in those fields is not a defect to cure with invented entries.
