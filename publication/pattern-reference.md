# Pattern Reference

These entries support consultation after the worked house and its intervention tests. Part III explains their fields, selection logic and the demonstrated service-topology sequence. The catalogue contains twenty-one active patterns, three broader strategies and four held candidates. Their evidence boundaries remain distinct; inclusion is not a recommendation to install all of them.

Use a pattern for the context and consequences it addresses, then compare its proposed relationship with competent conventional construction. Read the held candidates as unresolved questions. The order here is editorial, not an obligatory design sequence. The house's actual adoption and physical performance must be established separately.
