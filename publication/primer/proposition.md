# The proposition

Ovid’s House is a project to design and build the owner’s home on a future London plot. The book develops the architectural argument through that design; drawing, calculation and testing establish whether it can be built, occupied and maintained as intended.

A house continues to change after completion. Pipes leak, controls become obsolete and windows need replacing while the masonry around them may remain sound. A cable chased into that masonry, a valve sealed behind a finish, or plant without a withdrawal route makes routine renewal consume otherwise serviceable work. The project seeks permanence through transformation: give foreseeable change deliberate places to occur, so the house’s long-lived fabric and defining architecture can endure.

## What should endure

**Selective permanence** allows rooms, stairs, principal axes and the structural order to remain deliberately stable while concentrating adaptability where change is likely or consequential. A well-proportioned room can serve several generations without becoming physically indeterminate.

**Proportional serviceability** limits what is provided in advance. Access zones, spare capacity and reversible assemblies consume space and materials, with immediate cost and carbon burdens. They earn that provision where future change is frequent, damaging, difficult to accommodate later or cheap to allow for now.

## The architectural platform

The platform has four parts:

- **Permanent fabric:** foundations, principal structure and major masonry intended to remain substantially undisturbed.
- **Replaceable systems:** services, plant, controls and shorter-lived assemblies expected to wear or change.
- **Designed interfaces:** junctions that manage support, restraint, movement, sealing, drainage and disassembly where required.
- **Maintenance geography:** approach routes, working positions, clearances, supporting ground and withdrawal paths that make repair and replacement physically possible.

**Workmanship robustness** means setting clear datums, tolerances and adjustment so ordinary construction variation can be accommodated and assembly checked. **Repose** asks that the result remain comfortable and calm to occupy, with intelligible spaces, privacy, retreat and effective local control.
