# From constitution to patterns

The governing constitution is intentionally too general to specify a building. Ovid’s House therefore keeps four levels of authority distinct:

**Constitution** — core doctrine plus standing disciplines.  
**Strategy** — a general way to satisfy it.  
**Pattern** — a reusable architectural response with stated trade-offs and evidence.  
**Reference implementation** — one project-specific choice.

A pattern is not a product detail and does not become universally correct because it has a stable name. It is specific enough to shape design, but general enough to admit materially different implementations. Evidence maturity is recorded separately from pattern identity.
