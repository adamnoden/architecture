# 1. C06 — apparent equilibrium

## Finding accepted

The project has credible reasons to care about thermal comfort, acoustics, privacy, local control and spatial comprehensibility. The evidence for long-duration domestic comfort from a particular **visual account of structural equilibrium** is materially weaker.

The old wording risked turning a personal/architectural preference into a quasi-neuroscientific proposition.

## Correction

`docs/manuscript/governing-principles.md` and `docs/manuscript/principle-08-repose.md` now state that:

- physical stability is perceptually legible;
- apparent support/equilibrium may be reviewed where structure is conspicuous;
- the long-duration comfort inference is a project hypothesis;
- apparent precariousness is not prohibited;
- cantilevers are not treated as harmful and arches are not treated as therapeutic;
- structural reading is recorded as architectural judgement, not a metric.

The stronger measurable and evidence-backed parts of Repose remain.

**C06 / F005 — CLOSED.**

# 2. C07 — constitutional audit

The number eleven is not protected.

Each provision was tested against four questions:

1. what disappears if it is removed?
2. is it doctrine, a standing architectural discipline, strategy or implementation advice?
3. does it shape architecture materially?
4. can a project violate it in a way that is meaningfully reviewable?

## Result

The stable numbered provisions remain because each still carries useful governing work, but they are **no longer presented as eleven co-equal novelty claims**.

### Core doctrine

| No. | Provision | Why constitutional |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Build for time | establishes repeated change as a design horizon |
| 2 | Preserve permanent fabric | defines the principal lifespan-separation constraint |
| 3 | Design the interface | makes lifespan/system junctions architectural objects |
| 4 | Failure detectable / containable / repairable | converts foreseeable failure consequence into architecture |
| 5 | Give maintenance a geography | makes maintenance spatial rather than nominal access |
| 7 | Let permanence be architectural | prevents the project collapsing into generic flexibility and subjects permanence to proportionate justification |
| 10 | Make the building legible across generations | carries intergenerational recoverability beyond the original project team |

Removing any of these materially changes the long-life proposition.

### Standing architectural disciplines

| No. | Provision | Correct category |
|---:|---|---|
| 6 | Prefer ordinary parts in extraordinary arrangements | delivery / production discipline |
| 8 | Design for repose | occupation criterion |
| 9 | Let passive architecture do the first work | environmental discipline |
| 11 | Resolve technology as architecture | architectural-resolution discipline |

These remain governing because they prevent the core doctrine producing a high-maintenance technical system, but they are not represented as Ovid’s House inventions.

Stable numbering is retained because pattern metadata, research and project records already refer to it. Category is now explicit; numbering is provenance, not evidence of equal conceptual type.

R8-E also narrows P6 so extra prototype/installer/manufacturing process is activated only by consequential non-standard work rather than becoming a universal ritual.

**C07 / F006 — CLOSED.**

# 3. C08 — future climate

## Finding accepted

A long-life project that designs explicitly for future service replacement but tests permanent geometry only against present environmental conditions contains a real asymmetry.

The correction must not invent one universal climate future or make a site-free Reference House claim site-specific performance.

## Correction

Standing environmental provision P9 now requires a **real project** to test expensive-to-change geometry against a reasonable range of future climate / hazard conditions appropriate to its site.

The test includes, where relevant:

- summer temperature / overheating severity;
- solar exposure and shading opportunity;
- purge-ventilation viability;
- future active-cooling implications;
- intense rainfall / drainage consequence;
- drought / landscape consequences where material;
- wind or other locally material hazards;
- interaction with envelope, openings, roof geometry and plant strategy.

The project must state:

- scenario / source / time horizon;
- uncertainty;
- which permanent decisions are sensitive;
- whether low-burden adaptation remains available;
- what condition would force architectural rewind.

No single future-weather file becomes doctrine. A real site, applicable guidance and competent environmental analysis are required.

The Reference House remains unable to close this site-dependent test; its implementation/research records must carry it as a future site/professional gate rather than implying current proof.

**C08 / F022 — CLOSED at doctrine/candidate scope; real-project evidence remains deliberately downstream.**

# 4. Constitutional effect of R8-A→F

After adversarial correction, the project’s governing logic is smaller and harder:

- foreseeable change does not automatically justify infrastructure;
- future occupants count as foreseeable change, but do not automatically justify a lift;
- the Reference House does not establish transfer;
- the pattern corpus is not claimed as one uniformly mature language;
- delivery process is not attributed to architecture;
- stewardship may fail;
- Repose keeps its environmental/spatial core while losing pseudo-neuroscientific structural certainty;
- passive-first now includes explicit future-climate robustness rather than present-day optimisation alone.

No new governing provision is added.
