Controls for how the project is written rather than what it claims architecturally.

The [EDA — Editorial Doctrine Audit](eda-programme.md) is closed through EDA-08. It records source, paragraph, whole-book, adversarial and typeset acceptance under frozen v0.4, including the final proof identities and boundaries. Its editorial block on PR-02 is cleared; the independent PLT drawing freeze remains required.

[EDA-00](eda-00-doctrine-audit.md) records the hardened standard and self-audit. [EDA-01](eda-01-corpus-register.md) owns corpus membership and prose modes. The [baseline candidate register](eda-02-candidates.json) retains 2,088 non-authoritative findings across 128 canonical sources, and the [review register](eda-review-register.json) records their contextual source dispositions and reviewed hashes. Reproduce the baseline scan in its recorded checkout with `node --import tsx scripts/eda-candidates.ts <baseline-sha>`.

The [Editorial Overhaul Plan](editorial-overhaul-plan.md) is retained as the historical execution record for the completed repo-wide prose migration. Its phase order and branch instructions are provenance, not the current editorial work queue.
