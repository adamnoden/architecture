# Roof Access and Renewal

An opening that admits scaffold components does not establish a safe place to work. The current house's enclosed 3500 mm court makes that distinction decisive: a real ordinary mobile-tower comparison fits at a low level, but its required deployed footprint at useful roof-working heights does not fit. This rejects that access option, not every possible means of maintaining the roof.

This is an architectural comparison of foreseeable tasks, geometry and recovery burdens. It is not an equipment selection, scaffold design, contractor method statement or permission to work at height. Do not omit, retract or push stabilisers through a doorway, or borrow an unverified wall tie. Those would be different configurations requiring explicit manufacturer/design authority.

All start with the actual component/defect, permissible weather, occupancy restrictions and a verified access/support plan. All end with boundary reinstatement, drainage/condition inspection and a record of disturbed work.

## Temporary weathering and renewal are part of the system

Removing a receiver below an inner valley interrupts a concentrated water path. A tarp attached to the rejected mobile tower is not a solution. The actual temporary cover/catchment needs independent supported edges, wind restraint, a named discharge and a maintained escape that does not overload the court, flood the door or obstruct the workers. Primary and secondary roof drainage must not quietly share the disabled receiver. If no safe temporary state exists for a foreseeable repair, the component division or roof/water topology must change.

Separate three scales of removal: a local covering element; an installed seam/receiver assembly; and a major roof field with its layers. Each has different adjacent-work disturbance and handling space. At the four-way ridge and concentrated court corners, independent replacement must be demonstrated by actual fixing/lap access and temporary water control, not granted by the word “modular”. A large sheet or whole receiver may need a different route from the small pieces that initially assembled its support.

Roof-system comparison receives a concrete condition: retain only candidates with a credible high-level court method, roof-slope work route and temporary recovery state at the claimed architectural scope.
