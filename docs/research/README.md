# Research

Research supports, qualifies or rejects propositions used elsewhere in Ovid’s House. It does not create doctrine merely by being detailed.

The useful distinction throughout is between:

- established professional or empirical knowledge;
- evidence that supports a bounded architectural inference;
- project judgement;
- unresolved hypothesis.

## Transformation, persistence and precedent

[Transformation, Persistence and Precedent](preface-precedents.md) develops the intellectual background to the project’s temporal argument: architectural pace layers and Open Building, repair and maintenance, Ovidian metamorphosis, Heraclitean continuity through change, persistence conditions, Ise Jingu, Nakagin Capsule Tower and several precedents for redrawing inherited relationships and interfaces.

These sources clarify the argument; they do not create architectural requirements by themselves.

## Human factors and repose

[Repose and Low Vigilance](repose-and-low-vigilance.md) develops the architectural proposition that a good home should minimise unnecessary vigilance. [Repose — Evidence and Limits](repose-evidence-audit.md) separates established environmental evidence, supported context-sensitive claims, architectural inference and propositions that should not be promoted into doctrine.

The objective is broader than any one psychological measure: domestic space should not require continuous monitoring, compensation or interpretation merely to remain comfortable and usable.

## Workmanship and robustness

[Workmanship Robustness](workmanship-robustness.md) examines tolerance, mistake-proofing, inspection, installation sequence and representative-installer testing. Its central proposition is that design should accommodate ordinary competent construction variability deliberately rather than asking later trades to improvise around unresolved geometry.

## Floor and wall systems

Three options studies test whether more reversible assemblies add enough value to displace excellent conventional construction:

- [Floor-to-Masonry Structural Interface](seated-floor-structure-options.md)
- [Accessible Floor Systems](finish-agnostic-floor-platform-options.md)
- [Replaceable Wall Lining](replaceable-wall-system-options.md)

They remain evidence and comparison material, not automatic Reference House selections. The current house retains conventional continuous floors and direct quiet wall finishes unless a more complex assembly earns departure through a named task and credible evidence.

## Maintenance and external access

[External Maintenance Access](external-maintenance-access.md) examines the spatial consequences of maintaining façades, roofs, rainwater goods and external plant with credible ordinary access methods. It treats approach, working position, temporary support, handling and replacement routes as architecture rather than assuming that a hatch or nominal reachability is proof of maintainability.

Across all of these subjects, the controlling question is the same: **what does the evidence justify, and what does it not justify?**
