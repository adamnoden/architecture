# Research

The research area holds evidence synthesis, precedent and options appraisal for propositions used elsewhere in Ovid’s House.

Its role is evidential rather than doctrinal. Research may support a proposition, narrow its scope or give reason to abandon it. A finding does not become doctrine merely because the supporting work is detailed or well evidenced.

The work spans building systems, workmanship, maintainability, human factors, construction families and precedent. Where possible, the records distinguish established professional knowledge from project inference and unresolved hypothesis.

## Long life, change and precedent

[Preface Precedents](preface-precedents.md) develops the bounded intellectual background behind the opening argument and the **Ovid’s House** title: architectural pace layers and adaptability, repair and maintenance, Ovidian metamorphosis, Heraclitean continuity through change, persistence conditions, Ise Jingu and Nakagin as a useful counterexample.

The purpose is interpretive support, not a claim that those precedents themselves establish architectural requirements.

## Human factors and repose

[Repose and Low Vigilance](repose-and-low-vigilance.md) and the [Repose Evidence Audit](repose-evidence-audit.md) examine the stronger and weaker evidence behind privacy, control, comprehensibility, environmental comfort and perceived structural stability.

The architectural objective remains broader than any one psychological measure: domestic space should not require unnecessary vigilance merely to feel settled or usable.

## Workmanship and robustness

[Workmanship Robustness](workmanship-robustness.md) examines tolerance, mistake-proofing, inspection and representative-installer testing. [Candidate Pattern Hardening](candidate-pattern-hardening-summary.md) records where initially attractive serviceability propositions survive, narrow or fail when confronted with ordinary construction and proportionality.

## Floor and wall systems

The floor and wall studies compare conventional construction with more reversible challengers:

- [Primary Floor Structure Baseline](primary-floor-structure-baseline.md)
- [Seated Floor Structure Options](seated-floor-structure-options.md)
- [Finish-Agnostic Floor Platform Options](finish-agnostic-floor-platform-options.md)
- [Replaceable Wall System Options](replaceable-wall-system-options.md)

These are options research rather than automatic Reference House selections. Later coordination deliberately retains conventional continuous floors and direct quiet wall finishes unless a more complex system earns departure.

## Maintenance and external access

[External Maintenance Access](external-maintenance-access.md) examines the spatial consequences of maintaining façades, roofs, rainwater goods and external plant with ordinary temporary access equipment rather than treating a roof hatch or nominal reachability as proof of maintainability.

Across all of these subjects, the useful question is the same: **what does the evidence justify, and what does it not justify?**
