# Ovid’s House — Publication Architecture

**Status:** current editorial authority for the reader-facing publication structure.  
**Governing rule:** **The publication contains the resolved work, not the history of its resolution.**

This document defines what belongs in the public intellectual product and how the principal reading forms relate. Development chronology, migration records, release programmes, gate histories and superseded taxonomies remain repository history; they do not acquire publication status merely because they once helped produce the work.

## 1. Working Edition

The Working Edition is an illustrated architectural design-research monograph. It carries the complete resolved architectural argument, the current pattern language, one worked Reference House, the ordeal through which that proposition is tested, and the evidence/professional boundaries required to read those claims responsibly.

### Front matter

1. **Preface — The Obvious, Eventually** — establishes the temperament of the enquiry and the Ovidian frame of permanence through transformation.
2. **Reader’s Guide** — explains the authority layers, evidence boundary and intended reading paths without narrating project development.

### Part I — The Proposition

The opening part establishes the problem of buildings changing at different speeds, selective permanence, the architectural platform and the governing constitution.

The eleven numbered governing provisions remain stable cross-reference identities. They are not eleven co-equal novelty claims: core doctrine and standing architectural disciplines are distinguished explicitly, and all remain subordinate to life safety, law, structural integrity, health and environmental performance.

### Part II — Architecture of the Platform

Part II develops the architectural consequences of the proposition: designed interfaces, failure architecture, maintenance geography, environmental resilience, boundaries, replaceable layers and stewardship.

The permanent/changeable distinction is treated as an architectural relationship rather than a demand for maximum demountability. Conventional construction remains the baseline wherever additional serviceability has not earned its burden.

### Part III — Patterns and Generative Sequences

Part III is the current reusable architectural language:

- **21 active patterns**;
- **3 broader strategies**;
- **4 held candidates** whose admission questions remain unresolved.

Pattern identity is separate from evidence maturity. The service-topology field has the strongest demonstrated generative sequence; the remaining fields are not presented as one uniformly mature Alexander-style network.

Stable `PAT-*` identifiers remain because they support current cross-reference across architectural evidence and computational representations. Retired identities, pilot catalogues, migration crosswalks and pattern-hardening history remain internal.

### Part IV — The Reference House: Design Proposition

Part IV owns one coordinated interpretation of the proposition, not a style doctrine and not proof of the general system.

It forces doctrine, selected patterns, G-01, one courtyard morphology, one domestic programme and bounded technical choices to coexist in one building. Project-specific decisions stay project-specific even when repeated throughout the house.

The part owns the designed object: site/type, architectural order, material/structural proposition, maintenance geography, environmental systems and the selected interior/opening architectural interfaces. The dedicated interior-architecture and opening-assembly chapter interfaces may remain visibly incomplete until their substantive owners resolve them; structural placement is not a claim of maturity.

Future-intervention scenarios do **not** live here. They belong to Part V so the book first presents the house and then attacks it.

### Part V — Making and Proving the Platform

Part V addresses proportionality, interface ownership, reliable assembly, physical testing, procurement, commissioning, failure/change/occupation scenarios, the Reference House intervention ordeal and technical/construction closure.

Its function is to show how unusual propositions earn their place, how the worked house is challenged, and where architecture must rewind when technical resolution exposes a bad assumption. It is not a project-management log of the tests used to develop the manuscript.

### Part VI — The House

Part VI is the concise architectural payoff after technical reconciliation and final synthesis.

It is intentionally visual and spare: the resolved house, selected plans/sections/elevations/views and only enough text to understand the final architectural object and its remaining project-specific limits. It must not repeat Part IV as a second explanatory case study, and it must not absorb unresolved proof work merely to produce a satisfying ending.

Until the house has passed the required synthesis and publication-drawing gates, Part VI may remain a deliberate structural placeholder rather than pretending that the conclusion already exists.

## 2. Back matter

The Working Edition contains five pieces of back matter. Each has a continuing reader function:

A. **Evidence and Precedent Notes** — distinguishes kinds of support and the limits of precedent, internal design research, computation and professional/physical evidence.  
B. **Research Agenda** — records genuinely open architectural questions capable of falsifying, narrowing or materially improving the work.  
C. **Reference House Implementation Schedule** — marks where virtual coordination ends and real-site, professional, regulatory and physical proof must begin.  
D. **Glossary** — defines project-specific terms used throughout the work.  
E. **Sources and Further Reading** — provides the external intellectual and technical routes into the subject.

No appendix exists to preserve draft migration history. The old doctrine-register crosswalk remains recoverable in the repository but has no reader-facing function.

## 3. Primer

The Primer is a compact route into the same resolved work, not an independently authored doctrine.

Its sequence is:

1. proposition;
2. governing constitution;
3. current pattern language, with representative patterns expanded;
4. Reference House;
5. making and proving the platform.

The Primer does not need to reproduce Part VI while that final resolved-house section remains intentionally incomplete.

Where possible, Primer content is deterministically derived from canonical sources through publication adapters. Compression may reduce detail; it must not create a second authority layer or reintroduce obsolete history.

## 4. Website

The website is broader than either PDF because it can expose resolved research, G-01 evidence, Reference House technical surfaces, prototype questions and the bounded computational track.

Broader does not mean archival. A repository file is public only when it has a current external-reader function.

`project/public-surface.ts` defines the public/internal boundary used by the VitePress build and navigation. Development programmes, editorial controls, migration records, old gates, stale fixtures and pre-result test administration remain internal even when retained in Git.

## 5. Computational track

House Kernel and Metamorph Compiler remain a parallel research/computational track within Ovid’s House.

Reader-facing computational material states current concepts, demonstrated executable capability and proof limits directly. The work may describe semantic models, obligations, evidence, targets, supported domains, deterministic inspection and bounded calculations where those are genuinely current.

Paper-compilation sequences, H1 fixture history, compiler-hardening programmes, capability gates and one-off research families are internal once their durable semantics have current owners.

The architectural manuscript does not become a software pitch. Computation earns public space only where it clarifies architectural responsibility, evidence, contradiction or reproducibility.

## 6. Authority and source ownership

`publication/publication.ts` owns the mechanical Working Edition and Primer manifests. This document owns their editorial structure. Canonical architectural claims remain owned by their substantive source documents: manuscript parts, governing principles, pattern records, G-01, Reference House, research/evidence syntheses and current computational semantics.

Publication adapters may transform canonical sources for print, but they must not become hidden stores of architectural meaning. If a source can be corrected cleanly, correct the source rather than accumulating a publication-only shim.

Internal audit, release and development records may explain how a conclusion was reached. They do not outrank the resolved source that now owns the conclusion.

## 7. Editorial invariant

A reader should be able to encounter Ovid’s House as a coherent body of architectural thought without knowing which doctrine version, research stage, issue number, gate, migration or publication programme preceded it.

Development history remains available to the project. The publication presents the result.
