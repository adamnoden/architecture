## 1. Purpose

This ledger reconciles the book the publication architecture promises with the book the current Working Edition actually compiles.

It is not a quality score. It is the finite worklist for PR-00.

The status vocabulary is:

- `READY` — a genuine publication-facing source exists at useful depth; later whole-book reconciliation/editorial proof still applies;
- `NEEDS AUTHORSHIP` — promised reader-facing material does not yet exist in the required publication form;
- `NEEDS RECONCILIATION` — material exists but must be aligned to current authority, publication sequence or reader-facing register;
- `NEEDS FIGURE` — the argument materially requires or strongly benefits from a publication-grade figure not yet secured;
- `NEEDS EVIDENCE HARDENING` — a reader-facing claim requires explicit evidence/qualification work before freeze;
- `REMOVE OR REPLACE` — a current Working Edition source is useful repository authority but should not survive as a primary candidate-book unit in its current form.

Multiple states may apply to one unit.

# 2. Baseline findings

## 2.1 The publication pipeline is not the present problem

The previous Working Edition proof established a functioning deterministic print path. PR-00 therefore treats the pipeline, adapters and Vivliostyle setup as controlled infrastructure rather than a new design problem.

The present problem is editorial completeness.

## 2.2 The actual Working Edition is a 46-entry mechanical manifest

It currently comprises:

- Preface;
- Reader's Guide;
- Parts I and II as composites;
- Part III index, two method/sequence records, 21 active patterns, three strategies and four held candidates;
- Part IV overview plus six Reference House control/development records;
- Part V;
- Sources and further reading.

The manifest is intentionally a Working Edition. It is not the candidate-book architecture.

## 2.3 Part IV is the principal structural deficit

The publication architecture promises six chapters:

13. Site and type  
14. Architectural order, repose and tectonic language  
15. Material, structural and assembly system  
16. Maintenance geography  
17. Water and environmental systems  
18. Future maintenance scenarios

None currently has a publication-native chapter source.

The Working Edition instead exposes:

- Reference House overview;
- Tectonic Architectural Language;
- Vertical Bay Options;
- Vertical Bay Coordination;
- Whole-House Coordination Fixture 01;
- Pattern Occurrence Register;
- External Access & Maintenance Plan.

These are valuable source authorities. They are not an acceptable substitute for a six-chapter reader-facing architectural narrative.

## 2.4 Back matter is mostly promised rather than authored

Of the seven back-matter items in the publication architecture, only **Sources and further reading** currently exists as a publication-facing manuscript source.

The absence is not merely mechanical: several appendices require editorial synthesis and one — the promised 63-item doctrine register — has no identifiable canonical source in the current repository tree.

## 2.5 Part V is materially developed

Part V already contains substantive chapters 19–25. It is not a placeholder section. It enters PR-00B as an existing publication source requiring reconciliation rather than wholesale authorship.

## 2.6 G-01 and the Reference House have moved beyond the earlier print proof

The candidate must now reflect:

- G-01 v0.1 frozen at M4;
- the Reference House passing frozen G-01 v0.1 without rewind;
- the Reference House remaining M3 at virtual-design scope;
- physical validation remaining M2;
- external professional review remaining M1;
- the compiler remaining M4 only at its bounded proof scope.

The candidate book must not retain older “G-01 still under research” language where it is no longer true, nor convert these new internal results into external proof.
