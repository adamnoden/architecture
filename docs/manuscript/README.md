# Manuscript

Publication-facing architectural argument for **House Systems Architecture**. This is where the project becomes a coherent work for a reader rather than a record of the research process.

Start with the [Preface](preface.md), then the [Governing Principles](governing-principles.md). The intended structure of the finished publication is recorded in [Publication Architecture](publication-architecture.md).

## Architectural specificity rule

The manuscript may use concrete architectural and construction examples—masonry, plaster, cornices, architraves, thresholds, timber, metalwork and the Reference House—without making those examples HSA requirements.

Read authority from the claim, not the noun. A general proposition must survive a materially different architectural language and construction family. Where a specific form or material is itself required, the text should identify the narrower owner: pattern context, supported technical domain, selected grammar, Reference House, implementation family or precedent.

The Preface may state the author's architectural preferences and the Reference House chapters may be strongly specific. Neither promotes those preferences into doctrine by proximity. The governing placement rule is the [Architectural Specificity Boundary](../development/architectural-specificity-boundary.md).
