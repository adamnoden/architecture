**Status:** complete gate record

**Umbrella issue:** #71 — `PUB-01 — publication credibility and release programme`  
**Purpose:** reconcile the candidate manuscript to current canonical authority before figure completion and whole-book prose hardening.

PR-00C is not a new architectural-research gate and not the later PR-01 hostile intellectual review. It asks a narrower question:

> **Does the book describe the project that actually exists, at the strength actually earned, without silently promoting a project choice, internal result or unresolved hypothesis into a stronger claim?**

## 2. Ownership test

Material reader-facing claims are assigned to one of the following owners before publication polish:

- **OH** — Ovid’s House architectural position / governing principle;
- **PAT** — canonical pattern or strategy;
- **HELD** — held candidate;
- **G01** — frozen G-01 grammar;
- **RH** — Reference House project decision or occurrence;
- **TECH** — implementation or technical family;
- **COMP** — bounded computational result;
- **EXT** — established external knowledge / regulation / precedent;
- **OPEN** — hypothesis, unresolved research debt or downstream evidence obligation.

A claim fails PR-00C if its wording promotes a narrower owner upward. Typical failures include:

- `RH → OH`: a Reference House choice presented as doctrine;
- `TECH → PAT`: one implementation presented as the pattern itself;
- `G01 → OH`: Georgian-derived language presented as a general long-life-house requirement;
- `COMP → TECH/RH`: a machine-checked relation presented as physical adequacy;
- `OPEN → ESTABLISHED`: an untested hypothesis presented as settled fact.

## 4. Claim-strength vocabulary for the remaining PR-00 passes

Reader-facing material should resolve sensitive claims into one of five forms.

### Supported proposition

External evidence, precedent or project evidence supports the stated relationship at the declared scope.

### Bounded inference

Evidence supports a narrower fact from which the project makes an explicitly limited architectural inference.

### Project decision

A choice made for the Reference House or another project occurrence. It may be well reasoned without becoming general evidence.

### Hypothesis / research debt

A plausible relationship or implementation whose important validation remains open.

### Illustrative example

A concrete example used to explain a proposition without carrying evidential authority for the proposition itself.

Editorial compression must not erase these distinctions.

## 6. Computational boundary

The publication retains the established decision: **the computational track is recorded, not promoted into a main manuscript part.**

Where the book mentions Metamorph Compiler, the maximum justified claim is that bounded semantic, topological, identity and selected calculation relationships can be represented and checked deterministically within declared supported domains.

It must not be described as:

- a finished CAD compiler;
- a Building Regulations compliance engine;
- proof of structural or environmental adequacy;
- proof that the Reference House is buildable;
- a complete implementation of the architectural grammar;
- a substitute for competent engineering or external review.
