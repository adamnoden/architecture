**Execution rule:** numbered stages establish dependency order; **lettered packets are the units of work**.

This plan is retained as historical/restart provenance for the completed HFI programme. It is **not** a live work queue and authorises no further HFI packet. Future human-factors evidence should reopen its destination authority rather than revive HFI automatically.

This plan exists because HFI cuts across doctrine, evidence, Reference House geometry, publication, delivery and computation. It must therefore be easier to pause safely than to continue vaguely.

## Methodological spine

HFI evaluates:

`person × task × state × route/interface × consequence`

The programme is not allowed to select dimensions first and invent a user afterward. Representative scenarios are frozen before target derivation.

HFI distinguishes:

- visitability;
- ordinary usability;
- adaptability;
- wheelchair usability;
- maintenance access.

These may overlap but are not synonyms.

### HFI-01 — research method, evidence architecture and scenarios

#### HFI-01A — terminology and scope

Freeze working definitions for `friction`, `compensation`, `sequence of use`, `movement generosity`, `continuous ground plane`, `principal route`, `object route`, `visitability`, `usability` and `adaptability`.

#### HFI-01B — normative/evidence register

Record source authority, jurisdiction/date, evidence type, applicability and known limits. Mandatory starting families include current Approved Documents M/K and relevant G/H/Q interfaces; the 2025 government-commissioned *Access to and use of buildings: research on demographic and ergonomic requirements*; BS/inclusive-housing guidance; Habinteg/Lifetime Homes lineage; anthropometry; falls/biomechanics; OT/human factors; HAPPI/ageing evidence; and housing post-occupancy evidence.

Separate:

- law / statutory guidance;
- non-statutory standards/guidance;
- empirical evidence;
- precedent;
- explicit Ovid’s House preference.

#### HFI-01C — representative scenario matrix

Freeze the people/tasks/states to be tested before target derivation. Include, at minimum:

- larger/taller and smaller/shorter bodies;
- older occupant and temporary lower-limb/upper-limb impairment;
- wheelchair/mobility-aid use where relevant;
- occupied hands and one-handed operation;
- carrying groceries, laundry, a child or awkward object;
- assistance/care by a second person;
- wet hands/floor, fatigue and low light;
- furniture/appliance/pram movement.

The scenario set must also expose assumptions about who performs housework and care.

#### HFI-01D — proportionality/evaluation method

Freeze how interventions will be compared. Evaluate benefit, frequency, consequence and irreversibility against:

- area;
- initial and whole-life cost;
- embodied/operational carbon and energy;
- moisture/weathering risk;
- fire/security/structure/services conflict;
- workmanship burden;
- architectural effect;
- later retrofit difficulty.

**Gate:** HFI-02 cannot start until 01C/01D prevent target cherry-picking.

### HFI-02 — anthropometry, movement and vertical circulation

#### HFI-02A — anthropometry, reach and operating force

Body/foot distributions, reach envelopes, grip/dexterity and operating effort. Record population/percentile limitations; do not equate the 95th percentile with an automatic project minimum.

#### HFI-02B — stairs and fall risk

Study rise, going, pitch, descent behaviour, landings, handrails, headroom, lighting/contrast and carrying conditions. Explicitly separate:

1. legal/guidance correspondence;
2. evidence-supported comfort/safety ranges;
3. any later Ovid’s House design target.

#### HFI-02C — vertical life-course

Study stair-lift compatibility, assistance, entrance-level living/bathing and the proportional case for or against future lift provision or other irreversible reservations. Do not assume that step-free ground-floor entry alone solves life-course use of a two-storey house.

#### HFI-02D — carrying / reduced-attention movement

Test stairs and ordinary movement under occupied hands, fatigue, distraction and carrying loads/children.

**Outputs:** candidate ranges, evidence limits and rejected hypotheses; no final project dimensions yet.

### HFI-03 — routes, thresholds and logistics

#### HFI-03A — circulation geometry

Corridors by role/traffic, clear door openings, passing/overtaking, pinch points, corner/turning geometry, assistance space and conflicts with doors/cabinetry/radiators.

#### HFI-03B — continuous ground plane

Approach, principal entrance, internal floor changes, external/garden access, wet-room/shower transitions, gradients and honest water management. Separate site-independent principles from real-site inputs such as pavement levels, parking/set-down and approach slope.

#### HFI-03C — object movement

Define representative furniture/appliance/pram/mobility-device envelopes and a swept-path method. Test complete route feasibility rather than isolated doorway width.

#### HFI-03D — domestic task routes

Study repeated routes for groceries, laundry, waste, cleaning, childcare/caregiving and similar tasks. Capture conflicts between doors, storage, carrying posture and route geometry.

### HFI-04 — use, controls and fixed interfaces

#### HFI-04A — bathing, toileting and dressing

Use/transfer zones, shower/bath sequences, leaning/reach hazards, wet conditions, assistance and proportionate future support/grab-rail capacity.

#### HFI-04B — doors, windows and hardware

Reach, force, grip, one-handed operation, latch/lock/closure behaviour, swing conflicts and feedback.

#### HFI-04C — environmental/electrical controls

Lighting, shade, ventilation, heating and relevant fault/isolation controls. Review through action sequences, not only mounting heights.

Canonical question:

> Where is the occupant, what are they trying to do next, what hazards exist, and what action sequence does the building force?

#### HFI-04D — kitchen, storage and fixed work zones

Only the architectural/fixed geometry that materially constrains use: clearances, appliance access, reach/bending and hot/wet interaction. Do not turn HFI into an interior-design specification system.

### HFI-05 — synthesis, proportionality and conflict resolution

#### HFI-05A — causality and taxonomy

Group frictions only where a real common cause survives the evidence. Reject false unification.

#### HFI-05B — proportionality test

Apply HFI-01D. Generosity does not automatically win; reject interventions whose permanent area/cost/carbon/technical burden buys trivial benefit.

#### HFI-05C — cross-discipline conflict matrix

Reconcile candidate HFI propositions against moisture/drainage, fire/escape, structure, services, acoustics/privacy, security, workmanship, maintenance geography and G-01. Historic appearance is not by itself a reason to preserve an avoidable barrier.

#### HFI-05D — principle formation

Harden, qualify, replace or reject the working formulations:

> If an ordinary repeated action requires conscious compensation for the building, first ask whether the building is wrong.

> Human variance is a normal design input.

Produce the smallest defensible principle set. A negative result or adoption of established best practice is a valid outcome.

- evidence-supported range;
- Ovid’s House minimum/target where earned;

#### HFI-06B — movement targets

#### HFI-06C — use/control targets

#### HFI-06D — target red-team

#### HFI-07E — finding / rewind register

Map each material defect to the **owning RH gate** and every downstream artefact made stale by it. Likely ownership includes:

- ground-floor order → RH-02;

Existing geometry has no incumbency advantage.

### HFI-08 — controlled architectural integration

#### HFI-08A — upstream architecture

Route general conclusions to the narrowest correct owner: governing principles, strategy, pattern or research. Apply the architectural-specificity firewall; Reference House conveniences do not automatically become doctrine.

#### HFI-08B+ — Reference House rewinds

**One owning RH gate / coherent defect family per packet and normally per PR.** Update stale downstream authority and Pattern Occurrence Register entries. Never patch only the latest drawing/compiler artefact.

#### HFI-08C — delivery / prototype consequences

Add only requirements/tests that are earned. Do not create a prototype when established evidence already answers the question.

#### HFI-08D — external-review handoff

Decide whether a dedicated access/inclusive-design/human-factors review brief is warranted or whether RH-11 should gain a bounded addendum. HFI cannot claim external validation merely because internal research is deep.

### HFI-09 — downstream formalisation and publication re-entry

#### HFI-09A — publication impact / freeze-break audit

Identify every accepted HFI change affecting the controlled publication corpus, site, Working Edition, Primer and DRW output. Record stale surfaces explicitly.

#### HFI-09B — DRW reconciliation

DRW may continue on current controlled geometry, but **DRW-12 / PR-02 physical-object freeze must not close with known unresolved HFI publication or geometry changes**. Correct architecture upstream and regenerate/re-proof affected plates; never hand-patch a drawing to hide a rewind.

#### HFI-09C — computational admission

Determine which accepted HFI propositions are machine-checkable with controlled source inputs.

HFI does **not** silently create RH-C6 or broaden the existing M4 computational proof scope. A new proof family must earn a separately bounded demand-led computational gate/programme.

Specific invariant: RH-C5’s `ADK-2013-PRIVATE-STAIR-GEOMETRY` method remains an external-guidance correspondence check. If HFI later adopts a stronger Ovid’s House stair target, that is a separate project rule; do not rewrite the Approved-Document method to force the current house to pass/fail.

#### HFI-09D — regression locks

Add regression controls only for accepted stronger targets that could otherwise silently retreat to minimum-compliance geometry or stale publication output.

### HFI-10 — adversarial closeout

Attack the integrated result for:

- spatial bloat;
- median-body/ableist assumptions;
- false universality;
- standards worship;
- ergonomic pseudo-precision;
- clinical/institutional character;
- hidden housework/care assumptions;
- moisture/fire/structure/services/security/privacy conflicts;
- cost/carbon disproportion;
- Reference House overfit;
- implementation detail promoted into doctrine;
- stale drawings/publication residue;
- compiler proof-boundary inflation;
- novelty seeking where established practice was already sufficient.

Close only when every surviving proposition has explicit evidence, owner, maturity/proof boundary and downstream disposition.

## Concurrency contract

1. HFI-00 through HFI-06 are research/target formation and do not silently alter current geometry.
2. DRW may continue against the current controlled Reference House, but its drawings are projections of that baseline, not protection from architectural rewind.
3. A material HFI defect reopens its owning RH authority before any drawing/compiler correction.
4. PR-02 cannot freeze a known stale physical object; HFI publication impact must be disposed of first.
5. HFI may generate demand for new computation but does not own automatic compiler expansion.
6. Real-site access facts remain site-specific rather than invented for the site-agnostic Reference House.

## Packet closeout format

Every packet closeout records:

`packet → baseline main SHA → exact question → evidence/sources → findings + rejected hypotheses → artefacts/changes → checks → authority/maturity consequences → stale downstream items → exact next packet`

Split rather than grind through when a packet:

- contains multiple independent architectural questions;
- mixes research and implementation;
- reopens more than one RH gate;
- creates a new computational proof family;
- triggers a publication freeze break that cannot be reconciled locally.
