# Tectonic Integration Register

**Status:** current integration ledger; original tectonic migration complete  
**Purpose:** keep tectonic/reversible-assembly propositions in their correct authority layer as doctrine, patterns, strategies, Reference House choices and physical evidence evolve.  
**Historical source baseline:** `House Design Doctrine v7` remains preserved and is not rewritten in place.

## Integration rule

Every material idea must end in one of four states:

- **Integrated HSA knowledge** — carried through the appropriate doctrine → strategy/pattern → delivery/test chain;
- **Held / experimental** — useful proposition retained behind an explicit evidence gate;
- **Reference House only** — intentionally specific to G-01, the courtyard house or its tectonic dialect and not claimed as universal HSA;
- **Rejected / superseded** — deliberately excluded or replaced, with the reason recoverable from provenance.

Nothing is considered integrated merely because it appears once in prose or in the Reference House.

## Anti-drift controls

The following interpretations are explicitly **not** the intended HSA position:

- **“no screws”** — wrong; screws and bolts are valuable reversible fasteners. The target is few uncontrolled fixings into permanent fabric;
- **“all movement is good”** — wrong; load paths and required restraint remain deterministic;
- **“all plaster is forbidden”** — wrong; wet finishes are appropriate where permanence, movement and future access consequences are compatible;
- **“all visible joints must be metal”** — wrong; brass/bronze belong to one Reference House dialect, not universal doctrine;
- **“honesty means expose every technical mechanism”** — wrong; concealment is legitimate when the technical condition is resolved and the concealing architecture is real;
- **“prefabrication is inherently superior”** — wrong; controlled manufacture is preferred only where it improves repeatability, quality, reversibility or replacement enough to justify its burden;
- **“panelisation means a panelised aesthetic”** — wrong; panel boundaries may be absorbed into architectural composition;
- **“replaceability outranks fire, acoustics, moisture, structure or comfort”** — wrong; boundary and performance obligations remain non-negotiable;
- **“design for ordinary workmanship means accepting poor workmanship”** — wrong; the target is explicit achievable tolerances, clear datums, bounded adjustment and visible rejection of out-of-range work;
- **“mistake-proofing means eliminating craft”** — wrong; skilled judgement should be preserved where it creates architectural or technical value rather than spent rescuing unresolved interfaces;
- **“a Reference House prototype proves HSA”** — wrong; project fit and general evidence are separate verdicts.

## Completion audit

For a typical physical proposition the expected chain is:

**why** — governing principle / manuscript argument  
**how** — strategy  
**reusable response** — pattern where a stable architectural invariant exists  
**project choice** — Reference House occurrence / implementation  
**responsibility and stage** — implementation brief  
**proof** — prototype / calculation / commissioning / replacement test

Not every proposition needs a pattern identity. Strategy or implementation-family status may be the correct endpoint.
