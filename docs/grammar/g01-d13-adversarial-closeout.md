# G-01 D13 — Adversarial Closeout

**Status:** **PASS — G-01 v0.1 retained as the controlled baseline at M4 internal scope**  
**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Frozen grammar:** [G-01 v0.1](g01-v0.1.md)  
**Reference House trial:** [D12 G-01 v0.1 Conformance](../reference-house/g01-v0.1-conformance-d12.md)  
**Programme:** [G-01 Completion Programme](g01-completion-programme.md) / issue #78

## 1. Purpose

D13 does not ask whether the preceding programme was thorough. It asks whether its result should survive.

The closeout therefore attacks:

1. Reference House contamination;
2. famous-villa / Palladian overfit;
3. historical social arrangements laundered into contemporary rules;
4. false dimensional precision;
5. framework contamination;
6. contemporary sterility / reconstruction cosplay;
7. false positives against materially different architectural languages;
8. false negatives at the edge of the intended Georgian-derived scope;
9. façade bias / underdeveloped interior specificity;
10. the suspiciously clean all-PASS Reference House result.

A pass means only that **G-01 v0.1 is stable enough to act as the controlled internal baseline at its stated scope**. It does not make the grammar a historical classifier, complete generative design system or externally validated architectural authority.

---

# 2. Attack A — Reference House contamination

## Strongest criticism

G-01 exists because the Reference House already had a Georgian-derived temperament. The same project authored the house, selected the precedent lineage and performed the grammar research. A later claim that the house passes G-01 could therefore be circular even if the files are formally separated.

## Evidence against circularity

The programme inserted several real barriers between project taste and released grammar:

### A1 — D8 removed project-convenient rules rather than preserving them

D8 rejected or narrowed:

- universal room ratios;
- universal centred entrance;
- one principal-room sequence;
- mandatory double-height hierarchy;
- fixed plan/elevation authority;
- automatic historical service-route separation.

The direction of travel was subtraction, not formalisation of the house's existing geometry.

### A2 — D9 used predeclared hold-outs outside the Reference House

Kings Nympton, 2 New Street, 29 Storey's Way and Tiverton House forced additional narrowing before normative selection.

The most consequential example was 29 Storey's Way, which defeated any attempt to make historically separate principal/service circulation a hard G-01 rule.

### A3 — D10 refused a release that would have flattered the project

After D8/D9, the surviving set was judged **too style-neutral to deserve the name G-01**. D11 was explicitly blocked rather than releasing a weak grammar merely to advance the programme.

### A4 — D10-S froze a fresh validation set before positive extraction

Ridgehanger, 20 Castle Street and Basildon Park were withheld before positive-specificity candidates were written.

Ridgehanger then falsified the proposed `roof subordinate to façade order` rule. The rule was rewritten and the rewrite had to survive the two remaining validators rather than inheriting Ridgehanger as evidence.

This matters directly to the Reference House because its low-slope/recessive roof would have fit the rejected wording easily. The external validator forced the grammar to become **less Reference-House-shaped** before D12.

### A5 — D11 froze the grammar before D12

`G-01 v0.1` was merged to `main` before the Reference House conformance trial began. D12 was not permitted to edit it.

### A6 — Reference House-specific content is explicitly absent

The frozen grammar does not require:

- courtyard morphology;
- Andalusian courtyard influence;
- red brick;
- low-slope outward-draining roof;
- service flank;
- brass/bronze tectonic dialect;
- cavity/loadbearing masonry;
- House Kernel maintainability patterns.

## Residual risk

The research is still authored by the same project. Internal methodological independence is not independent professional judgement.

That residual risk blocks **M5**, not M4.

## Verdict

**PASS at internal-control scope.**

The Reference House influenced the question and selected lineage, but the final v0.1 rule set cannot reasonably be described as a transcription of the house.

# 3. Attack B — famous-villa / Palladian overfit

## Strongest criticism

Marble Hill and Danson are unusually canonical, elegant and well documented. A grammar derived around them could mistake elite Palladian-villa traits for Georgian-derived domestic architecture generally.

## Counter-evidence

The programme deliberately introduced morphology and scale pressure:

- 76 Dean Street — urban four-bay townhouse / off-centre entrance;
- Bedford Square — row/block scale;
- 2 New Street — ordinary mid-C18 urban control;
- 20 Castle Street — regional three-bay townhouse;
- Kings Nympton — close Marble Hill relative that did **not** execute the referenced cube room;
- 29 Storey's Way — twentieth-century Neo-Georgian adaptation;
- Ridgehanger — suburban Neo-Georgian adaptation;
- Tiverton House — contemporary Palladian-derived family house.

The rules that survived are precisely those able to tolerate those differences.

## What remains intentionally Palladian/classical

G-01 is not a neutral classifier of every building erected in the Georgian period. Its charter explicitly centres **English Georgian domestic classicism with a strong Palladian inheritance**.

Classical order in the released rules is therefore lineage, not accidental bias.

## Verdict

**PASS within declared lineage.**

Do not describe G-01 v0.1 as “the grammar of Georgian architecture”. It remains **a Georgian-derived domestic grammar**.

# 4. Attack C — historical social arrangements laundered into present rules

## Strongest criticism

Historic Georgian plans encode servants, class, entertaining convention, gendered/private domains and other social structures. A computational or normative grammar can accidentally make those conditions look timeless simply by calling them spatial hierarchy.

## Programme response

The research explicitly separated historical description from contemporary normative choice.

The clearest case is circulation:

- historic primary/service stair distinctions were recorded as evidence;
- D9 supplied 29 Storey's Way as counterpressure;
- D10 transformed the claim from `separate movement networks are required` into `movement networks may carry different architectural rank where current architecture creates that distinction`;
- no servant stair or segregated route appears in G-01 v0.1.

Likewise:

- piano nobile is not required;
- inaccessible entrance steps are not identity-bearing;
- historical sanitation/service arrangements are absent;
- room rank is not attached to historical household roles.

R03 explicitly permits an accessible contemporary threshold while retaining entrance hierarchy.

## Residual risk

`Hierarchy` itself is not socially innocent. A future project may deliberately choose a less hierarchical domestic language.

G-01's answer is not to pretend hierarchy is universal: such a project may simply fail G-01 while remaining good architecture.

## Verdict

**PASS.**

The released grammar preserves architectural hierarchy without naturalising the Georgian household's social hierarchy.

---

# 5. Attack D — precision theatre

## Strongest criticism

Classical/Georgian research is unusually vulnerable to attractive numerical folklore: golden ratios, perfect room proportions, fixed bay widths and exact window formulae can acquire false authority simply because they are easy to encode.

## Evidence

The programme repeatedly moved in the opposite direction:

- D5 rejected a universal room ratio using direct Marble Hill/Danson evidence;
- D8 retained C07 only as a negative guardrail;
- D9/D10 did not resurrect positive room bands;
- D10-S rejected 70 Dean Street's square-based façade example as a universal cross-G-01 rule;
- v0.1 contains no numerical dimensions, tolerances, pane counts, bay counts, storey ratios or roof pitch requirements.

Reference House numbers such as 2.70 m bay spacing or +6250 mm cornice datum remain **project coordinates**, not grammar authority.

## Risk in the opposite direction

Avoiding false precision can make a grammar so qualitative that almost anything passes.

D10 exposed exactly that failure and triggered D10-S to recover positive specificity through architectural relationships rather than fake numbers.

## Verdict

**PASS.**

Positive dimensional research remains a legitimate future question, but it is not standing work and must be reopened only by a concrete need and better evidence.

---

# 6. Attack E — framework contamination

## Strongest criticism

Several surviving ideas—hierarchy, scoped order, section, route/sequence distinction—are not Georgian-specific. If placed in G-01 as membership claims, the project would silently contaminate its supposedly style-neutral grammar framework.

## Finding

This attack found a real distinction during D10:

### Framework / representation capabilities

These remain outside G-01 ownership:

- connectivity / route / sequence represented separately;
- attribute-specific plan/section/elevation coupling;
- scoped symmetry/alignment semantics as a representation capability;
- blind/simulated/solid opening states;
- historical phase and uncertainty;
- explicit deviations.

### G-01 architectural use

The released grammar uses those capabilities to state a particular positive language:

- ordered **classical domestic** wall field;
- related/ranked **opening family**;
- ranked principal threshold;
- deliberate roof/termination relation;
- relational spatial/sectional hierarchy;
- concentrated classical articulation.

R01 and R05 would be weak discriminators in isolation. They become G-01 membership material only inside the released bundle.

## Framework maturity consequence

This work does **not** promote the architectural grammar framework above M3.

The strongest style-neutrality test remains a materially different second grammar using the same framework without special pleading. D13's negative controls test discrimination, not framework reuse.

## Verdict

**PASS. No framework rewrite required.**

# 7. Attack F — contemporary sterility / reconstruction cosplay

## Strongest criticism

A historically derived grammar may survive research only by becoming a museum piece: a contemporary house must then choose between genuine present-day use and stylistic conformity.

## Counter-evidence

The programme deliberately included later and contemporary adaptation:

- 29 Storey's Way — Baillie Scott's independent planning intelligence inside a Neo-Georgian language;
- Ridgehanger — early-C20 Neo-Georgian with an emphatic roof that corrected D10-S;
- Tiverton House — contemporary family programme, equal-sized bedrooms, modern occupation, high rooms and interconnected spaces.

The release itself permits:

- centred or off-centred entrance;
- concealed, low or emphatic roof families;
- contemporary accessible thresholds;
- modern high-performance window assemblies, including a potentially aluminium sash-derived family;
- contemporary room programmes;
- project-specific morphology;
- modern structure/services outside grammar authority.

It does not require servants' routes, historic construction or exact period window technology.

## Residual risk

The current positive specificity is strongest at **body/elevation/threshold** scale. Contemporary internal planning therefore has considerable freedom.

That is preferable to fake historical planning, but it also exposes the façade/interior limitation addressed below.

## Verdict

**PASS at v0.1 scope.**

# 8. Attack G — negative controls / false-positive test

D12's six-for-six Reference House pass is only meaningful if materially different coherent houses can fail G-01 without being called bad architecture.

D13 therefore applies the frozen rules qualitatively to two intentionally different English domestic languages.

## NC-01 — Red House, Bexleyheath

**Source:** Historic England list entry 1064203  
https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1064203

Historic England describes Philip Webb's 1859 Red House as:

- L-shaped;
- **picturesque**;
- simplified **Gothic**;
- gabled porch;
- oriel;
- staircase tower with pointed roof/finials;
- a mixed opening language including sash windows, paired/single openings and leaded casements.

### G-01 result

`G01-R01 — FAIL`

The house's architectural field is intentionally picturesque/Gothic rather than a classical domestic wall field. That alone is sufficient to reject G-01 membership.

R03/R04/R05 may still find meaningful entrance, roof and hierarchy relationships. That is expected: different languages can share architectural virtues.

**Negative-control verdict:** `G-01 FAIL / coherent non-G-01 architecture`.

## NC-02 — The Homewood, Esher

**Source:** Historic England list entry 1365884  
https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1365884

Historic England describes Patrick Gwynne's 1938–39 house explicitly as **Modern Style**:

- concrete and glass;
- flat concrete roofs;
- principal volume raised/projecting on concrete piers;
- continuous first-floor glass panels;
- continuous window band;
- stainless-steel / glass-block entrance composition;
- glazed/panelled upper floor over brick piers.

### G-01 result

`G01-R01 — FAIL`  
The principal composition is intentionally modern/free rather than a bounded classical domestic field.

`G01-R02 — FAIL`  
Continuous glazing/window bands do not form the vertically ranked sash-derived/opening-field relation required by the invariant.

Other high-order architectural virtues—hierarchy, integrated roof, ranked arrival—do not rescue membership because G-01 invariants are conjunctive.

**Negative-control verdict:** `G-01 FAIL / coherent non-G-01 architecture`.

## Conclusion

The negative controls materially reduce the concern that G-01 v0.1 is merely a checklist for competent houses.

They also demonstrate an important property of the framework:

> **G-01 can say “not this language” without saying “bad architecture”.**

## Verdict

**PASS.**

# 9. Attack H — adjacent-classical false positives

## Strongest criticism

G-01 v0.1 may admit buildings from adjacent classical traditions—later Palladian, restrained neo-classical, some Neo-Georgian or other English classical work—that a historian would not classify narrowly as Georgian.

## Finding

This is substantially true and **intentional at v0.1 scope**.

The grammar is named **Georgian-derived**, not “historical Georgian period classifier”. Its purpose is contemporary architectural authorship, not date/style attribution.

A building can therefore belong to G-01 even if a historian would describe its specific idiom more precisely as:

- Palladian revival;
- Neo-Georgian;
- later classical;
- contemporary English classical.

If the project later needs historical-style classification, that should be a separate taxonomy problem rather than forcing G-01 to distinguish every neighbouring classical dialect.

## Boundary

This tolerance must not expand until “classical” alone is sufficient. The coordinated R01–R06 bundle and declared English Georgian/Palladian lineage remain the boundary.

## Verdict

**ACCEPTED LIMITATION, not a release blocker.**

# 10. Attack I — façade bias / weak positive interior specificity

## Strongest criticism

D10-S recovered positive specificity mainly through:

- wall field;
- openings;
- entrance;
- roof/termination;
- classical articulation.

R05 supplies interior/sectional hierarchy, but that relation is deliberately broad. G-01 v0.1 therefore has much stronger positive membership authority over the **architectural body/elevation/threshold** than over Georgian-specific plan or room composition.

A contemporary free plan placed behind a conforming classical exterior might satisfy more of v0.1 than a mature full grammar should permit, provided some high-order hierarchy remains.

## Finding

**Criticism sustained.**

This is the largest remaining intellectual limitation of v0.1.

## Why it does not invalidate v0.1

The release never claims to be a complete generative grammar. Section 4 of the frozen release explicitly states that formal morphology sub-profiles are not yet released.

D4/D8/D9 also showed why premature plan specificity is dangerous:

- Marble Hill distributor logic is not Danson circuit logic;
- urban double-pile planning differs again;
- later Neo-Georgian adaptation differs further.

Promoting a weak historical plan rule merely to balance the grammar would be worse than recording the boundary honestly.

## Consequence

Record as **research debt, not standing next work**:

> A future v0.2 may derive positive interior/morphology profiles if a real project, publication critique or second worked grammar makes that extra specificity valuable.

Until then, describe v0.1 accurately as a **bounded core grammar**, strongest at architectural body/elevation/threshold plus high-order spatial/sectional hierarchy.

## Verdict

**QUALIFY, retain v0.1.**

# 11. Attack J — suspiciously clean Reference House pass

## Strongest criticism

All six G-01 rules pass the house that originally motivated G-01. That result may indicate the rules are tailored or too forgiving.

## Test

Ask whether the Reference House could have failed each rule without ceasing to be a technically viable House Kernel project.

### R01 could fail

If room partitions punched unrelated openings through each outer wall, the technical house could still function while failing the ordered classical field.

RH-04 explicitly considered and rejected this option (`F2`).

### R02 could fail

The house could use unrelated window types, horizontal strips or technical openings as equal façade events while remaining weather-tight and serviceable.

RH-04/RH-10 instead maintain ranked opening families.

### R03 could fail

The entrance could be visually anonymous or treated as equivalent to a plant/service door. House Kernel does not require classical entrance rank.

The project deliberately ranks it.

### R04 could fail

The roof could become an unresolved parapet-hidden technical cap whose waterproofing/plant geometry has no architectural upper termination.

RH-10 explicitly forbids that even while selecting a recessive low-slope roof.

### R05 could fail

The programme could flatten room/storey hierarchy into equalised generic spaces while remaining technically coherent.

RH-04 tested `H0` and rejected it architecturally.

### R06 could depart

The house could use uniform applied classical ornament or none at all without violating House Kernel doctrine. Its restrained, concentrated articulation is a project choice.

## Important procedural point

The one D10-S candidate especially convenient for the Reference House—`roof subordinate`—was **falsified before D12 by Ridgehanger** and replaced with a broader externally challenged rule.

That is strong evidence that the clean D12 result is not merely achieved by preserving house-shaped candidates.

## Verdict

**PASS.**

The all-PASS result is plausible and non-tautological.

# 12. Internal inconsistency / redundancy check

## R01 versus R02

R01 owns the whole classical architectural field. R02 owns membership and rank of openings inside that field.

Retain both: a coherent wall field could still contain an unrelated opening catalogue, and a repeated window family could exist on a façade with no classical field order.

## R01 versus R06

R01 is invariant composition. R06 is optional/preferred concentration of explicit classical articulation.

Retain both: restrained astylar G-01 work can satisfy R01 while using little R06 ornament.

## R04 versus technical roof authority

R04 owns architectural integration only. Water/drainage/structure remain technical/project authorities.

No conflict.

## R05 versus framework hierarchy

The framework supports hierarchy generically. R05 states that G-01 actually uses a relational hierarchy at whole-house scale.

This is legitimate use of a generic capability, not framework ownership.

## Verdict

**PASS. No v0.1 rule deletion required.**

# 14. What is frozen versus open

## Frozen / controlled

- `G-01 v0.1` rule text;
- D8–D13 evidence chain;
- no universal room ratio;
- no centred-entry rule;
- no fixed roof type;
- no servant-route invariant;
- no Reference House courtyard/material/tectonic content in the grammar.

## Open research debt

- positive interior / morphology-specific grammar;
- positive dimensional families if better evidence and a real use case justify them;
- more precise sub-dialects only if they earn practical value;
- framework neutrality under a materially different second grammar;
- independent professional/architectural criticism.

These are **not standing implementation tasks**.

---

# 15. Reopen triggers

Reopen G-01 only if one of the following occurs:

1. competent external review produces a material criticism;
2. a real project cannot resolve a legitimate contemporary condition inside v0.1 without excessive deviations;
3. a second grammar exposes G-01 assumptions leaking into the style-neutral framework;
4. publication adversarial review finds a serious historical/architectural error;
5. new measured evidence materially contradicts a released relationship;
6. positive interior/morphology specificity becomes necessary for a concrete design task.

Do **not** reopen merely because more Georgian buildings are interesting to study.

# 17. Programme close

D8 → D13 is complete.

The active G-01 research programme closes with:

- one immutable v0.1 release;
- one documented Reference House conformance result;
- an explicit limitation boundary;
- no standing v0.2 programme;
- no compiler work authorised;
- no claim of external validation.

The next meaningful G-01 work is evidence-selected, not automatic.

> **The programme began by asking whether Georgian architecture could be made explicit. It ends with a smaller answer: enough of one Georgian-derived domestic language can be made explicit to constrain a project, survive counterexamples and reject materially different languages—without pretending that architectural quality or Georgian history has been compiled.**
