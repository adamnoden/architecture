# G-01 — Georgian-Derived Domestic Grammar Charter

**Status:** canonical scope and authority statement for G-01  
**Current specification:** [G-01 v0.1](g01-v0.1.md)  
**Evidence boundary:** [Validation and limits](g01-validation.md)

## Identity

**G-01 is a Georgian-derived domestic architectural grammar.**

It is one selected architectural language within the House Kernel framework because the Reference House has a Georgian-derived architectural temperament.

G-01 is not Ovid’s House doctrine, a construction system or a definition of good architecture in general.

Its job is narrower:

> **Describe a coherent contemporary domestic language derived from selected English Georgian/classical precedent strongly enough that membership, preference and explicit deviation can be discussed and represented.**

## Authority boundary

Keep these axes independent:

```text
OVID'S HOUSE DOCTRINE
  long-life architectural / technical propositions

G-01 GRAMMAR
  Georgian-derived spatial / compositional language

SUPPORTED TECHNICAL DOMAIN
  systems and parameter ranges the compiler can reason about

REFERENCE HOUSE PROJECT
  site, programme, courtyard morphology, selected dialects and implementations
```

A house may therefore satisfy G-01 while failing an Ovid’s House project requirement, or satisfy the architectural doctrine while departing from G-01. Technical support remains another independent question.

No result acquires the authority of another axis.

## What G-01 may own

Subject to evidence and derivation, G-01 may be opinionated about:

- spatial hierarchy;
- topology and sequence;
- axes, bays and ordering relationships;
- dimensional and proportional families;
- storey and opening hierarchy;
- plan / section / elevation coupling;
- element families and compositional relationships;
- historically grounded preferences and explicit deviations.

These rules define membership in G-01. They are not Ovid’s House requirements.

## What G-01 does not own

G-01 does not, merely by being the first grammar, own:

- maintenance geography;
- lifespan separation;
- failure tolerance;
- passive-first environmental strategy;
- technical service topology;
- cavity masonry or any other supported-domain family;
- the Reference House courtyard;
- the courtyard's slight Andalusian influence;
- the Reference House brass/bronze tectonic dialect;
- every historical Georgian construction or social arrangement.

Those belong to doctrine, technical scope, project morphology, local dialect, implementation or historical evidence as appropriate.

## Contemporary adaptation

Historical description and present normative grammar are different operations.

G-01 may identify recurrent Georgian relationships and then preserve, modify or reject them for contemporary domestic use. Accessibility, environmental performance, contemporary occupation, structure and servicing can constrain a project using G-01 without becoming part of the definition of Georgian architecture itself.

Where Ovid’s House creates an additional project requirement, record the interaction as a compatibility condition between grammar and doctrine rather than silently rewriting G-01 around it.

## Evidence discipline

The continuing derivation method is stated in [G-01 — Scope, Evidence and Derivation Method](g01-research-brief.md). The corpus, precedent cases, topology comparison, dimensional analysis and plan/section/elevation evidence remain inspectable separately from the normative specification.

The important separation is:

- Georgian core grammar;
- courtyard project morphology;
- local Andalusian courtyard dialect;
- Ovid’s House doctrine;
- Reference House tectonic dialect.

## Governing test

A materially different future grammar should be able to use the same House Kernel and computational framework without inheriting G-01 rules.

Conversely, G-01 should remain recognisably and unapologetically Georgian-derived even if the Ovid’s House doctrine were removed from the comparison.
