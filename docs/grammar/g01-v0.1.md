# G-01 v0.1 — Georgian-Derived Domestic Grammar

**Status:** controlled specification  
**Version:** `G-01 v0.1`  
**Scope:** contemporary low-rise English domestic architecture derived from selected English Georgian/classical precedent, with a strong Palladian inheritance but not limited to Palladian villas  
**Authority:** architectural-language membership only; not Ovid’s House doctrine, technical proof, regulation, or a definition of good architecture in general  
**Evidence basis:** [Grammar Charter](g01-grammar-charter.md), comparative precedent research and [G-01 Validation and Limits](g01-validation.md)

> **G-01 v0.1 defines a small architectural language. It does not attempt to reconstruct the eighteenth century.**

## 1. Core claim

G-01 v0.1 describes enough positive architectural structure to distinguish a Georgian-derived domestic language from generic architectural order without claiming that Georgian architecture can be reduced to a fixed plan, universal proportion, centred entrance, sash pane count or roof type.

The specification is intentionally small.

Its membership-bearing core is a coordinated bundle:

```text
ORDERED CLASSICAL BODY / WALL FIELD
        +
RELATED OPENING FAMILY
        +
RANKED ENTRANCE
        +
INTEGRATED UPPER TERMINATION / ROOF ORDER
        +
RELATIONAL SPATIAL + SECTIONAL HIERARCHY
        +
RESTRAINED / CONCENTRATED CLASSICAL ARTICULATION
```

No single item proves G-01 membership by itself.

## 2. Conformance states

A project evaluated against G-01 v0.1 may return:

- `PASS` — all invariants satisfied; no material preference departure;
- `PASS WITH PREFERENCE DEPARTURE` — invariants satisfied; one or more language preferences deliberately not followed;
- `FAIL` — one or more invariants are not satisfied;
- `FAIL — EXPLICIT DEVIATION` — an invariant is knowingly violated and the project records the deviation rather than pretending to conform;
- `HUMAN REVIEW` — evidence/relationship is insufficiently determinate for an automatic or categorical architectural judgement;
- `NOT APPLICABLE` — rule genuinely does not apply to the morphology/condition in question.

A G-01 failure is not a judgement that the architecture is bad.

# G01-R01 — Ordered classical architectural field

**Authority:** `INVARIANT`  
**Scope:** each declared principal/public elevation; secondary/service elevations may use a quieter or materially different field  
**Confidence:** high

## Statement

A declared principal G-01 elevation must read as a **coherent classical domestic architectural field**, not an accidental aggregation of openings and applied details.

The field must establish, where applicable:

- a legible bay/order structure;
- storey/vertical ordering;
- a bounded upper condition;
- explicit principal/subordinate conditions;
- named axes, centres, edges or grouped bays where the design relies on them.

Symmetry and alignment are **scoped relationships**. The rule does not require:

- whole-building bilateral symmetry;
- identical elevations;
- equal bay widths everywhere;
- an odd number of bays;
- a centred entrance;
- an applied columnar order across the façade.

## Evidence

Supported by comparative precedent and validation including Marble Hill, Danson, 76/70 Dean Street, Combe Bank, Clandon, Ditchley, 20 Castle Street, Ridgehanger and Basildon Park. Global-symmetry and centred-entry simplifications do not survive that evidence.

## Counterpressure retained

- 76 Dean Street: ordered four-bay front with off-centre entrance;
- Clandon: four different elevations within one strongly ordered house;
- ordinary/urban cases: unequal or morphology-driven local bay conditions can remain coherent.

## Contemporary rationale

The rule preserves the classical domestic idea that a façade is one ordered architectural object while allowing contemporary programme, construction and site conditions to vary its realisation.

## Permitted deviation

A project may explicitly deviate where a different architectural language is intentionally introduced. A façade whose principal field is intentionally picturesque, free-composed or non-classical should not be silently labelled G-01.

## Does not prove

Beauty, structural logic, environmental performance, historic authenticity, or any exact proportion.

# G01-R02 — Related and ranked opening family

**Authority:** `INVARIANT` for family/order; sash-derived character is a `LANGUAGE PREFERENCE`  
**Scope:** principal/public elevation fields  
**Confidence:** high

## Statement

Openings within a principal G-01 field must belong to **recognisably related architectural families** and participate in the bay/storey order.

Coherence may be expressed through:

- recurring vertical axes;
- related width/height families;
- repeated head/sill/storey relationships;
- storey-specific differentiation;
- controlled principal-opening or entrance exceptions;
- blind/simulated/solid states where intentionally selected.

Variation should read as **ranked differentiation**, not arbitrary mixing of unrelated opening types.

## Sash-derived preference

For the primary domestic field, G-01 prefers a **vertically proportioned sash-derived architectural language**.

This preference concerns the architectural type and rhythm. It does not require:

- historical timber technology;
- exposed sash boxes;
- six-over-six glazing;
- one pane size;
- one window ratio.

A contemporary high-performance or metal window may satisfy the architectural family if its visible composition belongs coherently to the G-01 field.

## Evidence

Built precedent and validation repeatedly preserve related sash/opening fields while allowing wide variation in subdivision, size, storey hierarchy and local exceptions. The Georgian Group window evidence explicitly rejects the idea of one standard Georgian pane/window size.

## Counterpressure retained

- blind openings demonstrate that exterior composition and spatial aperture are not identical;
- later/Neo-Georgian cases vary glazing and framing technology;
- opening hierarchy is real without one numeric size formula.

## Permitted deviation

A principal field dominated by unrelated strip windows, curtain walling or randomly mixed opening geometries would normally fail R02 rather than create a minor preference departure.

## Does not prove

Daylight, ventilation, thermal performance, view quality, window durability or exact historical replication.

# G01-R03 — Architecturally ranked principal entrance

**Authority:** `INVARIANT`  
**Scope:** principal arrival condition  
**Confidence:** high

## Statement

The principal entrance must be **architecturally ranked above an ordinary opening** and intelligible within the order of its approach/frontage.

Rank may be expressed through one or more of:

- doorcase;
- porch/portico;
- hood/pediment/entablature;
- steps or transformed accessible threshold condition;
- differentiated bay;
- vertical relation to an opening above;
- forecourt/axis/approach relation;
- local material or compositional emphasis.

The entrance does **not** have to coincide with:

- geometric façade centre;
- whole-building centre;
- garden-front centre;
- an odd-numbered central bay.

## Evidence

Warbrook, 76 Dean Street, Ridgehanger, 20 Castle Street and Basildon collectively span axial/central, off-centre urban and more monumental entrance conditions while preserving entrance rank.

## Contemporary rationale

G-01 preserves legible threshold hierarchy without making historic inaccessibility—such as unavoidable entrance steps—the carrier of identity.

## Permitted deviation

A deliberately concealed or equal-status entrance belongs outside conforming G-01 unless another equally legible ranking device is demonstrated and accepted under `HUMAN REVIEW`.

## Does not prove

Security, accessibility, weathering or circulation performance.

# G01-R04 — Integrated upper termination and roof order

**Authority:** `INVARIANT`  
**Scope:** principal body/elevation  
**Confidence:** high

## Statement

The principal architectural field must terminate deliberately, and the roof condition must participate coherently in the same architectural order.

Valid families include, among others:

- parapet with concealed/reduced roof presence;
- strong cornice/eaves termination with visible pitched/hipped roof;
- low hipped/pyramidal roof tied to the principal body;
- mansard/attic condition subordinate to principal-storey order;
- positively expressed Neo-Georgian roof with dormers/stacks where those elements remain compositionally governed.

The invariant is **integration**, not suppression.

## Evidence

Ridgehanger demonstrates that an authoritative Neo-Georgian house can use a wide, active roof with upswept eaves, dormers, gables and prominent stacks; 20 Castle Street and Basildon Park provide materially different confirming conditions. G-01 therefore requires a governed relationship between body and roof, not a universally subordinate roof.

## Contemporary rationale

Contemporary roof form may be driven partly by maintenance, drainage, insulation, PV, ventilation and planning. G-01 requires those systems to resolve into an intentional architectural termination rather than an unrelated residual cap.

## Permitted deviation

A roof deliberately belonging to a different architectural dialect requires an explicit G-01 deviation or project-specific dialect record.

## Does not prove

Roof drainage, waterproofing, structure, energy performance, PV output or maintenance safety.

# G01-R05 — Relational spatial and sectional hierarchy

**Authority:** `INVARIANT` at high order; mechanisms are project/morphology dependent  
**Scope:** whole house / principal spatial system  
**Confidence:** high

## Statement

A G-01 house must establish a legible **architectural hierarchy of spaces and movement** rather than treating all rooms, routes and vertical conditions as architecturally unranked.

Hierarchy may be expressed through combinations of:

- room role;
- position;
- route/arrival privilege;
- connection/sequence;
- ceiling/room height;
- double-height or stair volume;
- principal-storey condition;
- opening/elevation treatment;
- relationship to garden/exterior.

No one geometric proxy owns rank.

In particular:

- largest room does not automatically mean principal room;
- centre does not automatically mean principal;
- first floor does not automatically mean principal storey;
- a separate service route is not required;
- equal bedrooms do not erase hierarchy elsewhere.

## Section requirement

For multi-storey houses, section/vertical order must be treated as part of the architecture rather than as an unexamined extrusion of plan.

The rule does not require:

- double-height rooms;
- piano nobile;
- fixed floor-to-floor ratios;
- one stair type.

## Evidence

Marble Hill, Danson, 76 Dean Street, Kings Nympton, 29 Storey's Way, Tiverton House and Ridgehanger demonstrate materially different hierarchy mechanisms. Comparative falsification and held-out validation reject the temptation to universalise any one of them.

## Historical/social boundary

Historical servant/class separation is not a contemporary invariant. G-01 may distinguish route roles when current architecture/programme creates them; it does not manufacture segregated circulation for historical resemblance.

## Permitted deviation

A deliberately non-hierarchical domestic language should normally select another grammar rather than accumulate exceptions.

## Does not prove

Social value, occupant preference, accessibility, circulation efficiency or technical-service adequacy.

# G01-R06 — Concentrated classical articulation

**Authority:** `LANGUAGE PREFERENCE`  
**Scope:** principal fields, entrances, key bay/storey conditions  
**Confidence:** medium/high

## Statement

Where explicit classical articulation is used, G-01 prefers it to be **hierarchically concentrated at architectural loci that carry rank** rather than applied at equal intensity everywhere.

Typical loci include:

- principal entrance;
- central or terminal bay/group;
- base/plinth condition;
- principal storey/opening;
- cornice/upper termination;
- selected quoins/pilasters/pediments/architraves.

A restrained or astylar wall field may still conform if R01–R05 establish the language coherently.

## Evidence

Clandon, Ditchley, Combe Bank, 70 Dean Street, Ridgehanger, 20 Castle Street and Basildon show repeated concentration of classical devices at ranked points while leaving subordinate surfaces quieter.

## Counterpressure retained

Classical abundance is not itself G-01 quality. The rule deliberately avoids requiring an archaeological order, moulding catalogue or decorative density.

## Preference departure

A project can `PASS WITH PREFERENCE DEPARTURE` where articulation is unusually sparse or unusually rich but the core language remains legible and the departure is deliberate.

## Does not prove

Historical correctness, tectonic truth, construction quality or aesthetic success.

# 3. Explicit non-rules in v0.1

The following are **not** G-01 v0.1 rules:

- universal room ratio;
- golden-ratio requirement;
- centred principal entrance;
- odd bay count;
- whole-building bilateral symmetry;
- one principal-room circulation sequence;
- separate servant/service stair;
- mandatory piano nobile;
- mandatory double-height room;
- one roof type;
- roof concealment;
- six-over-six sash requirement;
- timber-window requirement;
- blind-window requirement;
- courtyard requirement;
- Andalusian courtyard language;
- red brick requirement;
- Georgian historical construction requirement;
- Ovid’s House serviceability or maintenance doctrine.

If later evidence promotes any positive dimensional family, it requires a new G-01 version.

# 4. Morphology and profile boundary

G-01 v0.1 is a **core grammar**, not a complete morphology generator.

It has been challenged across compact villas, urban townhouses, later Neo-Georgian houses and larger Palladian controls, but it does not define formal sub-profiles for every morphology.

Project morphology may therefore sit beside G-01 where it does not contradict the core.

The Reference House courtyard remains project morphology. Its slight Andalusian influence remains a local dialect unless separately developed and justified.

# 5. Framework concepts used but not owned by G-01

G-01 relies on style-neutral framework capabilities without claiming them as Georgian rules:

- connectivity / route / sequence represented separately;
- attribute-specific plan/section/elevation coupling;
- scoped symmetry/alignment semantics;
- intentional absence / blind / simulated opening states;
- historical phase and evidence uncertainty;
- explicit deviation records.

A future non-Georgian grammar should be able to use the same capabilities with different architectural rules.

# 6. Technical and computational boundary

G-01 v0.1 says nothing by itself about:

- structure;
- fire;
- acoustics;
- moisture;
- energy;
- overheating;
- accessibility compliance;
- Building Regulations;
- service topology;
- maintainability;
- buildability;
- cost/carbon;
- compiler support.

A shared coordinate or geometry may create technical obligations, but grammar conformance does not satisfy them.

# 7. Versioning rule

`G-01 v0.1` is stable in meaning. Any change to an invariant statement, rule authority, scope, membership consequence or positive dimensional family requires a new version.

Editorial corrections that do not change meaning may be made without changing the specification identity.
