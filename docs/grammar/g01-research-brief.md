# G-01 — Scope, Evidence and Derivation Method

G-01 is a bounded Georgian-derived domestic architectural grammar. It describes a selected architectural language; it is not Ovid’s House doctrine, a technical construction system, a regulatory route or a universal definition of good architecture.

The current normative specification is [G-01 v0.1](g01-v0.1.md). [Validation and limits](g01-validation.md) records the main conclusions and counterexamples that bound it. This page states the continuing scope and evidence method behind those documents.

## Scope

G-01 concerns relationships that can plausibly distinguish a Georgian-derived domestic language at building, room, opening and threshold scales, including:

- architectural fields, bays, axes and ordering relationships;
- hierarchy between spaces, storeys, fronts, entrances and openings;
- topology and sequence where evidence supports a specifically Georgian-derived relation;
- dimensional and proportional tendencies without assuming a universal sacred ratio;
- plan / section / elevation coupling;
- upper termination and roof order;
- element families and concentrated classical articulation.

It does not own Ovid’s House maintenance doctrine, service topology, failure tolerance, passive-first environmental strategy, the Reference House courtyard, its service geography, its technical construction families or its local tectonic choices.

## Descriptive evidence is not normative authority

Historical frequency answers one question: what relationships recur in the selected precedents?

A contemporary grammar answers another: which of those relationships remain architecturally meaningful and transferable now?

The distinction is deliberate. Historical servant circulation, inaccessible arrangements, obsolete servicing assumptions or poor environmental conditions do not become present rules merely because they were common. Conversely, a contemporary preference does not become Georgian merely because the project likes it.

## Evidence corpus

The evidence base deliberately spans unlike Georgian conditions rather than one admired villa type. It includes freestanding villas, urban houses and terrace/block conditions, with source quality and historical phase tracked explicitly.

A famous building is not automatically strong evidence. A source must be adequate for the particular claim being made: geometry, phase, room use, elevation, section, chronology or architectural interpretation.

## Evidence states

Observations are kept distinct as:

- **fact** — directly supported by a cited source;
- **derived fact** — calculated or logically derived from supported facts;
- **interpretation** — an architectural reading whose factual basis remains visible;
- **hypothesis** — a possible cross-case relationship awaiting attack;
- **unknown** — a question for which the evidence is not adequate.

Unknown is a legitimate state. Missing evidence is not completed by intuition simply to make a table look whole.

## Phase discipline

Geometric authority and historical-phase authority are separate.

A precise measured drawing may describe a later alteration rather than the state relevant to a grammar claim. Room uses, openings, connections and dimensions therefore carry phase and uncertainty where necessary. Later fabric may be valuable evidence about the building, but it is not silently projected backwards.

## Relational before numerical

G-01 begins with relationships before exact ratios.

The research therefore asks first about hierarchy, topology, axes, bay structure, room/opening rank, entrance rank, storey relationships and coupling between plan, section and elevation. Dimensions and ratios are then tested as possible families or tendencies within that relational structure.

This is why the current grammar rejects a universal Georgian room ratio even though proportion remains architecturally important.

## Scoped symmetry

Symmetry is never reduced to one building-level Boolean.

A principal façade can be strongly symmetric while the plan, service arrangement, later accretions or secondary ranges are not. Evidence records the domain and scale at which symmetry or balance operates.

## Coupling rather than façade decoration

Plan, section and elevation are treated as projections of one architectural order, but the direction of influence can differ.

An opening may exist because of façade composition while its centreline responds to an interior condition. A room may acquire sectional privilege without controlling the entire external order. Evidence therefore distinguishes the attributes and scope actually coupled rather than asserting a single universal plan-façade rule.

## Counterexamples and controlled mutation

Candidate relationships are attacked rather than accumulated.

Useful tests include changing an entrance position, opening alignment, circulation edge, room rank, sectional privilege, roof relation or symmetry condition while holding other facts stable enough to see what architectural load the relationship was carrying.

Counterexamples are equally important. Urban irregularity, blind or simulated openings, non-centred entrances, different roof forms and varied room sequences have all prevented attractive simplifications from becoming hard rules.

## Authority classes

A candidate G-01 statement may end as:

- a **language invariant** — required for conformance;
- a **language preference** — admissible departure with architectural consequence;
- **human judgement** — relevant to the language but not honestly reducible to a deterministic rule;
- **rejected** — insufficiently specific, insufficiently supported or falsified by the corpus.

Technical adequacy remains separate. A G-01 pass is not structural, environmental or regulatory proof.

## Current result

G-01 v0.1 retains six bounded relationships concerning coherent classical fields, ranked opening families, entrance rank, integrated upper termination, relational spatial/sectional hierarchy and concentrated classical articulation.

It deliberately does not require universal room ratios, centred entrances, whole-building bilateral symmetry, one principal-room sequence, one sectional mechanism, one roof type, fixed sash-pane counts or historic service circulation.

The result is intentionally smaller than the field of Georgian architecture. The grammar records what the present evidence can defend, not everything that might be associated with the style.

## Relationship to the Reference House

The Reference House selects G-01 and then adds its own programme, courtyard morphology, technical systems and tectonic dialect.

Those project decisions may negotiate with G-01, but repetition in one house does not promote them into the grammar. Likewise G-01 does not acquire Ovid’s House authority merely because the Reference House uses both.

## Open boundary

The strongest future test of the grammar framework is a materially different second architectural language. Until that exists, style-neutral concepts such as hierarchy, scoped order and plan/section/elevation coupling remain plausible framework abstractions rather than proven universal machinery.

G-01 itself remains unapologetically Georgian-derived and explicitly bounded.
