# G-01 Completion Programme — D8 to D13

**Status:** active programme control  
**Programme issue:** #78 — `G-01C — complete G-01 through falsification, hold-out validation and Reference House trial`  
**Owner:** Architectural grammar  
**Authority:** subordinate to [G-01 Grammar Charter](g01-grammar-charter.md) for identity/scope and [G-01 D5 / D6 Evidence Gate v0.3](g01-d5-d6-web-pass-v03.md) for current D5/D6 findings.  
**Objective:** produce the smallest defensible G-01 v0.1, prove that it generalises beyond the precedents that generated it, then make the Reference House answer to it rather than quietly defining it.

## 1. Why this programme exists

G-01 has completed enough exploratory work that further broad precedent accumulation now carries more risk than value.

The current research has already established several durable negative and modelling conclusions:

- there is no earned universal Georgian room ratio;
- proportion must remain attached to role, absolute size, morphology, section and uncertainty;
- connectivity, route role and architectural sequence are distinct;
- symmetry and alignment have explicit scope rather than one building-wide boolean state;
- coupling authority belongs to attributes and architectural scope;
- section is first-class;
- plan, section and elevation can share architectural variables without technical proof authorities collapsing together;
- controlled conflict between interior and exterior ordering can be legitimate architecture.

The architectural-rule layer remains deliberately unpromoted. The next task is therefore falsification and controlled promotion, not more unbounded discovery.

## 2. Governing boundaries

Throughout D8–D13 keep the following authorities independent:

```text
HOUSE KERNEL DOCTRINE
  long-life architectural / technical propositions

G-01 GRAMMAR
  selected Georgian-derived architectural language

SUPPORTED TECHNICAL DOMAIN / METAMORPH COMPILER
  bounded executable relationships and proof scopes

REFERENCE HOUSE
  site + programme + project morphology + selected grammar + local dialects
```

This programme does **not** authorise new Metamorph Compiler capability. The compiler remains at its frozen controlled baseline unless a later concrete proof question independently justifies reopening it.

The following remain outside G-01 unless independently earned:

- Reference House courtyard morphology;
- the courtyard's Andalusian influence;
- Reference House brass/bronze tectonic dialect;
- House Kernel serviceability, maintenance, failure-tolerance or lifespan-separation requirements;
- historical servant hierarchies as present-day social requirements;
- any particular construction family merely because historical Georgian work used it.

# D8 — Controlled falsification / mutation programme

## D8 purpose

Discover which candidate relationships actually carry architectural load by changing one controlled relationship at a time.

The mutation programme exists to falsify candidates, narrow their scope and distinguish four different outcomes:

1. **representational failure** — the model cannot express the changed condition;
2. **architectural grammar failure** — the mutation leaves the intended language;
3. **preference departure** — the mutation remains inside the language but becomes less preferred;
4. **no material grammar consequence** — the candidate relationship was overstated or irrelevant.

Never use “looks worse” as the complete verdict.

## D8 record contract

Every mutation record must state:

```text
source state
→ one controlled mutation
→ candidate relationship(s) under test
→ evidence quality / uncertainty
→ architectural consequence
→ technical consequence, if any
→ representational consequence
→ historical plausibility
→ contemporary relevance
→ verdict
→ consequence for candidate rule
```

Where human architectural judgement is unavoidable, say so explicitly rather than laundering it into a hard rule.

## D8 mutation set

The existing Danson principal-room shortcut mutation is prior completed evidence and is not repeated.

### D8-M01 — Danson principal-room shortcut — COMPLETE PRIOR EVIDENCE

Source: original principal-floor circuit around the stair.  
Mutation: later direct Hall → Saloon shortcut.  
Tests: `G01-CAND-02` sequence / connectivity distinction.  
Current result: connectivity improves while the declared architectural sequence changes; supports the modelling distinction but does not justify one universal Georgian sequence rule.

### D8-M02 — Danson blind-window exterior regularisation

Source: blind/simulated windows whose existence is elevation-led while centreline can respond to interior geometry.  
Mutation: force the blind-window centreline to the perfect exterior field/grid.  
Tests: `G01-CAND-04`, `G01-CAND-06`, `G01-CAND-09`.  
Question: does exterior regularisation destroy a legitimate interior relationship, and what authority owns the compromise?

### D8-M03 — Danson universal-ratio normalisation

Source: Entrance Hall, Library, Dining Room and Saloon using different plan families under a common principal-floor sectional datum.  
Mutation: force principal rooms toward one selected width:length family while preserving the principal section/elevation as far as possible.  
Tests: `G01-CAND-07`, secondarily C01/C08.  
Question: does ratio regularity improve or impoverish the architectural order?

### D8-M04 — Danson route-role erasure

Source: principal and service circulation retained as distinct architectural roles.  
Mutation: model the routes as one undifferentiated circulation class.  
Tests: `G01-CAND-03`.  
Question: is architectural information lost even where physical connectivity is unchanged?

### D8-M05 — Marble Hill sectional flattening

Source: 24 ft cubic/double-height Great Room occupying the dominant central principal position.  
Mutation: retain plan position/footprint but reduce the Great Room to an ordinary single-storey condition.  
Tests: `G01-CAND-01`, `G01-CAND-08`.  
Question: how much of the hierarchy is carried by section rather than plan?

### D8-M06 — Marble Hill dominant-centre drift

Source: shared architectural centre expressed through principal room, section and central façade composition.  
Mutation: shift one principal centre relation while holding the others.  
Tests: `G01-CAND-04`, `G01-CAND-06`.  
Question: is exact coincidence required, is a tolerance/bounded relation sufficient, or is this human judgement?

### D8-M07 — Marble Hill hierarchy flattening

Source: explicit differentiation between dominant principal room, principal spaces and subordinate movement/service conditions.  
Mutation: flatten declared spatial and route rank without necessarily changing geometry.  
Tests: `G01-CAND-01`.  
Question: does the model lose an explanatory relation that the architecture materially depends on?

### D8-M08 — 76 Dean Street centred entrance

Source: ordered four-bay façade with off-centre entrance responding to urban/plan condition.  
Mutation: move entrance to an abstract global façade centre.  
Tests: `G01-CAND-04`, and the broader “classical = centred whole-building symmetry” simplification.  
Question: does naïve symmetry improve the grammar or destroy a legitimate scoped order?

### D8-M09 — 76 Dean Street piano-nobile flattening

Source: first-floor opening/storey hierarchy stronger than adjacent storeys.  
Mutation: make opening hierarchy uniform across storeys while retaining basic façade bay structure.  
Tests: `G01-CAND-05`, `G01-CAND-08`.  
Question: how much architectural hierarchy disappears without changing plan adjacency?

### D8-M10 — 76 Dean Street route-role erasure

Source: principal and secondary/service stair conditions coexist in a constrained urban house.  
Mutation: treat them as one undifferentiated movement network.  
Tests: `G01-CAND-03`.  
Question: does the higher-order principle of circulation rank survive outside the villa cases without preserving servant-era social segregation?

## D8 completion gate

D8 passes when:

- the ten-target set has either been evaluated or explicitly reduced because a proposed mutation is redundant/unanswerable;
- every D7 candidate is touched by at least one meaningful falsification path;
- candidate outcomes are classified `SURVIVE`, `NARROW`, `MORPHOLOGY-SPECIFIC`, `PREFERENCE ONLY`, `HUMAN REVIEW`, or `REJECT`;
- representational limitations are not presented as Georgian architectural truths;
- missing measured-source work is acquired only where a named mutation actually requires it.

Eight strong mutations may close D8 if the remaining two would be artificial duplicates. The target exists for coverage, not numerology.

# D9 — Held-out validation

## D9 purpose

Test surviving D8 claims against buildings deliberately withheld from derivation.

Do not tune the grammar against the hold-outs. A surprising failure is evidence.

## Hold-out order and role

### H1 — Kings Nympton Park

Role: **close-family anti-overfit test**.

Why first: the corpus records it as closely following Marble Hill while remaining a different built work. A rule shaped partly by Marble Hill that cannot describe Kings Nympton without special pleading is likely overfit.

### H2 — 2 New Street, Leicester

Role: **ordinary-scale / urban control**.

Question: which high-order relationships survive when elite villa scale and monumental composition disappear?

### H3 — 29 Storey’s Way, Cambridge

Role: **twentieth-century adaptation control**.

Question: can G-01 recognise a Georgian-derived language under materially different occupation and planning assumptions without requiring eighteenth-century social topology?

### H4 — Tiverton House

Role: **contemporary adaptation control**.

Question: which rules survive contemporary family occupation, modern servicing, accessibility and construction while remaining recognisably English-Palladian/Georgian-derived?

## Hold-out record

For every surviving candidate record:

```text
candidate
hold-out applicability
observed state
PASS / COUNTEREXAMPLE / NOT APPLICABLE / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
whether failure attacks scope, authority or existence of rule
```

Do not reduce D9 to a numeric accuracy score. The hold-outs answer different architectural questions.

## D9 gate

Pass when every surviving D8 candidate has met at least one appropriate held-out challenge and all counterexamples have been dispositioned without tuning after the fact.

# D10 — Descriptive → normative reduction

## D10 purpose

Historical recurrence is not contemporary authority.

For each surviving descriptive relationship ask:

```text
historical evidence
→ architectural function
→ historical/social dependency
→ contemporary usefulness
→ present-day conflict
→ PRESERVE / TRANSFORM / REJECT
```

### PRESERVE

The underlying relationship remains useful and defensible substantially as observed.

### TRANSFORM

The historical relation carries architectural value but its social, technical or environmental implementation must change.

Example type: differentiated circulation rank may survive; servant segregation does not.

### REJECT

The historical condition is incompatible with the contemporary grammar's stated purpose or has insufficient architectural value independent of obsolete circumstance.

## D10 gate

No G-01 v0.1 rule may exist solely because “Georgian houses often did this.” Each requires a contemporary architectural rationale.

# D11 — G-01 v0.1 freeze

## D11 purpose

Turn the research programme into a small, explicit, versioned grammar.

The release should be smaller than the candidate register. Promotion is selective.

## Rule contract

Every rule must state:

- stable ID;
- statement;
- morphology scope;
- authority: `INVARIANT`, `PREFERENCE`, or `HUMAN_JUDGEMENT`;
- supporting evidence;
- counterexamples / counterpressure;
- descriptive history;
- contemporary normative rationale;
- permitted deviation behaviour;
- confidence;
- does-not-prove boundary.

## Expected shape

A plausible v0.1 contains approximately 5–10 high-order relationships around:

- declared architectural hierarchy;
- scoped order / symmetry / alignment;
- circulation role and sequence where morphology invokes it;
- sectional hierarchy;
- storey/opening hierarchy;
- attribute-specific plan/section/elevation coupling;
- intentional solid/absence;
- morphology-specific rather than universal topology.

This list is not a quota.

It is entirely legitimate for v0.1 to contain **no positive universal room-ratio bands**. D5 has not earned them.

## D11 release gate

Freeze only if the resulting grammar can say both:

- “this relationship is outside G-01”; and
- “this project deliberately deviates from G-01 here”

without confusing either statement with bad architecture in general.

If the evidence does not support a coherent release, record **WITHHOLD** and narrow/reframe the grammar rather than manufacturing completeness.

# D12 — Reference House conformance trial

## D12 purpose

Test whether a grammar derived independently can constrain the project that originally motivated it.

Freeze G-01 v0.1 before evaluating the Reference House.

For each grammar rule resolve:

- `PASS`;
- `PASS WITH PREFERENCE DEPARTURE`;
- `EXPLICIT DEVIATION`;
- `NOT G-01 / PROJECT MORPHOLOGY OR DIALECT`;
- `HUMAN REVIEW`.

## Project-specific exclusions

Do not bend G-01 to absorb:

- the courtyard merely because the Reference House has one;
- Andalusian courtyard character;
- brass/bronze interface expression;
- House Kernel technical/service requirements;
- one selected structural or envelope system.

If a defensible G-01 rule conflicts with the Reference House, the default response is to change the house unless a deliberate deviation is architecturally justified and recorded.

## D12 gate

Pass when every v0.1 rule has an explicit Reference House disposition and any consequential house changes or deviations are recorded against their owning Reference House design records.

# D13 — Adversarial closeout

## Mandatory attacks

### A. Reference-House contamination

Could each rule have existed if the Reference House had never been designed?

### B. Palladian / famous-villa overfit

Does the grammar describe a Georgian-derived domestic language, or merely Marble Hill + Danson + a few canonical examples?

### C. Historicism

Have servant systems, class hierarchy or other historical social structures been naturalised into current rules?

### D. Precision theatre

Does any dimension, ratio, tolerance or centreline rule claim more evidence than the sources justify?

### E. Framework contamination

Has any G-01-specific architectural choice leaked into the style-neutral grammar framework or House Kernel doctrine?

### F. Contemporary sterility

Can a house remain clearly inside G-01 while accommodating contemporary occupation and technical requirements, or does the grammar amount to reconstruction cosplay?

## D13 closeout

Record:

- surviving G-01 version;
- rejected/narrowed candidates;
- unresolved research debt;
- Reference House deviations;
- whether grammar-framework corrections are required;
- maturity reassessment with evidence;
- conditions that would reopen G-01.

Do not promote maturity automatically because D8–D13 completed. Maturity follows the strength of the resulting evidence.

# 4. Programme stop rules

Stop and reframe rather than force completion if:

- the evidence only supports morphology-specific dialects rather than one useful G-01 core;
- hold-outs expose that the candidate set is mainly Palladian-villa overfit;
- the only way to make the grammar coherent is to encode obsolete social arrangements;
- the only positive rules available are subjective taste disguised as invariants;
- dimensional preferences remain too weak for defensible ranges;
- the Reference House can pass only by tuning the grammar after D11 freeze.

A legitimate outcome is a narrower name/lineage or a smaller rule set.
