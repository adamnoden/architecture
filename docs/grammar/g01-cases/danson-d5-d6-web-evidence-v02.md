# Danson — Measurement and Coupling Evidence

**Case:** G01-DAN

Danson is unusually useful because its conservation record exposes several different room-proportion families, a shared principal-floor height, canted rooms/elevations, blind windows, structural/opening coordination and a documented change to the original circulation graph.

The evidence therefore tests a Georgian grammar based on relationships rather than one ratio or a simple one-way `plan → elevation → structure` sequence.

## Principal-floor dimensions

| Entity | Role | Width | Length | Height | Plan family |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| DAN-P-ENTRANCE | entrance / principal hall | 20 ft | 26 ft 8 in | 16 ft | ≈4:3 |
| DAN-P-LIBRARY | principal library | 18 ft | 36 ft | 16 ft | 2:1 |
| DAN-P-DINING | principal dining | 18 ft | 36 ft | 16 ft | 2:1 |
| DAN-P-SALOON | dominant garden-front saloon | 26 ft principal measure | 26 ft principal measure | 16 ft | square principal axes / canted-octagonal form |

Additional direct values from the same research family include:

- principal-floor wall thickness: **2 ft 6 in**;
- three long side-bay facets: **9 ft** each;
- internal ellipse of the side bay: approximately **4:3**;
- common principal-floor room height: **16 ft**.

No single room ratio explains the principal floor.

## Construction dimensions and grammar authority

The fabric report also exposes precise construction values, including:

- Library principal floor beam: **320 × 320 mm**;
- Library principal joists: **335 × 75 mm**;
- Library common joists: **215 × 65 mm**;
- Library ceiling joists: **75 × 70 mm**;
- Saloon fireplace opening: **1500 mm wide × 1480 mm high × 430 mm deep**;
- ground-level rusticated ashlar module: approximately **303 × 660 mm (12 × 26 in)**.

Measurability is not grammar authority. A dimension may be:

1. construction evidence;
2. evidence that architecture and structure share geometry;
3. evidence for an architectural grammar only if a cross-case relationship survives and is deliberately made normative.

The Library beam matters primarily because its position is coordinated with window geometry. The ashlar module remains construction/material evidence rather than a G-01 rule.

## Shared sectional datum, plural plan families

Danson gives a clear contrast:

```text
PLAN
Entrance Hall   ≈ 4:3
Library         = 2:1
Dining          = 2:1
Saloon          ≈ 1:1 principal axes / canted

SECTION
all principal rooms = 16 ft height
```

The principal floor therefore achieves unity through a **shared sectional datum while retaining several plan families**.

This supports a grammar capable of role-based dimensional families, common storey/ceiling datums and room hierarchy without requiring one repeated plan ratio.

## Principal-room circuit and later shortcut

The fabric research describes a compact principal-floor circuit around an elliptical top-lit primary stair, with a secondary/service stair immediately west of it.

The four principal rooms are:

- Entrance Hall — north;
- Dining Room — east;
- Saloon — south;
- Library — west.

The Saloon originally had access from the Dining Room and Library. The east-of-stair passage was a closet and did **not** originally provide the later direct Hall/Saloon shortcut.

This is direct evidence that connectivity and architectural sequence are not identical.

### DAN-CPL-00 — principal circuit / later shortcut

- **scope owner:** dwelling;
- **attribute:** route / sequence;
- **original condition:** Hall enters the principal-room circuit without a direct Hall→Saloon bypass;
- **later condition:** a direct shortcut exists;
- **confidence:** high.

The later alteration provides a real historical contrast without making the original social circulation a contemporary invariant.

## Blind-window existence and centreline are separate

Fabric research records blind windows at Danson and an important distinction in how they are located. In relevant conditions they are **centred internally**, putting them out of line with the centre of the external elevation. The report contrasts this with equivalent blind windows at other Robert Taylor villas where the openings are centred on the exterior wall face.

One object can therefore have different coupling states by attribute:

```text
existence coupling   = ELEVATION LEADS
centreline coupling  = PLAN LEADS
result               = CONTROLLED EXTERIOR COMPROMISE
```

### DAN-CPL-02 — blind-window existence

- **scope owner:** dwelling / elevation;
- **attribute:** existence;
- **spatial opening function:** absent;
- **façade representation:** present;
- **coupling:** elevation leads;
- **confidence:** high.

### DAN-CPL-03 — blind-window centreline

- **scope owner:** room / dwelling;
- **attribute:** centreline;
- **interior relation:** internally centred;
- **exterior consequence:** not centred on exterior elevation;
- **coupling:** plan leads into a controlled conflict/compromise;
- **confidence:** high.

A generic `opening.aligned = true` model cannot represent this honestly.

## Window geometry and floor structure share a coordinate

The fabric report records the Library floor frame in detail. Its main north–south beam spans **between the jambs of the windows in the north and south walls**.

### DAN-CPL-04 — Library jamb / floor-frame coupling

- **scope owner:** room / component;
- **subject:** DAN-P-LIBRARY;
- **architectural variable:** north/south window-jamb geometry;
- **structural variable:** principal floor-beam support/alignment;
- **beam:** approximately 320 × 320 mm;
- **coupling:** shared/coordinated geometry;
- **confidence:** high direct fabric evidence.

```text
WINDOW / JAMB POSITION
       ├── belongs to elevation/opening composition
       ├── belongs to room geometry
       └── participates directly in structural floor framing
```

The architectural grammar does not prove structural adequacy, but the semantic model must be able to recognise that one coordinate participates in both architectural and structural obligations.

## Canted rooms and exterior bays are co-geometry

The Library, Dining Room and Saloon conditions use canted geometry that is present in both room form and external bay form.

### DAN-CPL-01 — canted room / exterior bay

- **scope owner:** room / dwelling;
- **attribute:** projection / room form;
- **plan:** principal room incorporates canted geometry;
- **elevation:** corresponding external bay;
- **coupling:** mutual;
- **confidence:** high at broad geometric level.

This is a stronger example of plan/elevation co-evolution than merely centring a rectangular window on a room.

## Section coordinates differing plans

### DAN-CPL-05 — common principal-floor datum

- **scope owner:** storey / dwelling;
- **attribute:** room height / storey hierarchy;
- **plan:** several different room geometries and aspect ratios;
- **section:** common 16 ft principal-room height;
- **architectural consequence:** the rooms read as one principal hierarchical level despite plan variation;
- **confidence:** high.

This complements Marble Hill rather than reproducing it. Marble Hill uses exceptional height to privilege one room; Danson uses a common height to unify several differently shaped principal rooms. A vertical grammar therefore needs both exception and shared datum.

## Historical design image and executed building

The conservation research records discrepancies between Malton’s published plan/view and fabric evidence and suggests that Malton may have worked from architect’s drawings rather than recording the executed building exactly.

That reinforces a general evidence rule:

> **Historical design/publication geometry and executed fabric are separate evidence classes.**

Both can be useful. Their disagreement may reveal design negotiation rather than source error.

## Evidential boundary

Danson is strong enough to support several modelling and architectural conclusions:

1. **one universal room ratio is not defensible;**
2. **a shared sectional datum can coordinate several plan families;**
3. **coupling authority belongs to an attribute, not an object;**
4. **controlled plan/elevation disagreement can be legitimate architecture;**
5. **architectural coordinates can participate in structural obligations without the grammar becoming structural proof;**
6. **route connectivity and intended architectural sequence are separate facts.**

The evidence does not by itself establish positive universal dimensional bands or make Danson’s circulation sequence a contemporary rule. Those normative decisions remain subject to cross-case comparison and the bounded [G-01 v0.1 specification](../g01-v0.1.md).
