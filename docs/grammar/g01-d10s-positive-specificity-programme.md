## 1. Trigger

D10 found that the surviving candidates are defensible but too style-neutral to define membership in a Georgian-derived language.

D10-S exists to answer one narrow question:

> **What positive architectural relationships distinguish G-01 from another coherent architectural grammar without collapsing into a moulding catalogue, a fixed historical plan or ratio folklore?**

This is not a new broad Georgian research programme.

## 3. Target domains

Investigate only these missing membership discriminators:

### S1 — primary body / base / roof termination

Look for relationships between:

- a legible principal architectural body;
- subordinate projections/wings/secondary masses;
- base or lower-storey treatment;
- cornice/eaves/parapet/roof termination;
- roof visibility and its relation to façade order.

Do not require one footprint or roof family unless evidence earns it.

### S2 — bay and opening field

Look for relationships between:

- wall field and openings;
- repeated bay rhythm;
- centre/flank or other ranked bay conditions;
- solids/piers at edges;
- local departures that preserve the larger order.

Do not equate Georgian order with odd bay count or exact bilateral symmetry.

### S3 — entrance within frontage order

Look for how the entrance becomes architecturally legible under:

- centred villa conditions;
- off-centre urban conditions;
- several competing façades/fronts.

The likely object is **ranked entrance relation**, not “door must be centred”.

### S4 — vertical opening family

Look for recurring relations between:

- opening families by storey;
- principal versus subordinate storey;
- sill/head rhythm;
- sash/opening type;
- wall field and storey datum.

Do not infer one numeric size ratio from descriptive evidence.

### S5 — classical element hierarchy

Look for high-order element relationships only:

- plinth/base;
- string/band where present;
- doorcase/portico;
- cornice/eaves/parapet;
- pediment/order only where they organise the whole.

Profiles, moulding dimensions and archaeological order reconstruction are outside D10-S.

### S6 — room enclosure / axial elements

Admit this domain only if the evidence shows a recurrent relationship between defined rooms and architectural elements such as:

- door axes;
- fireplaces/chimney breasts;
- windows/bays;
- room centre or end condition.

Do not invent an axial-room rule because classical interiors often look ordered.

## 4. Candidate admission test

A positive-specificity candidate enters the register only if it can answer all of these:

1. **Specificity:** does it distinguish G-01 from generic “good architectural order”?
2. **Cross-case evidence:** is it supported by more than one derivation source/case?
3. **Counterexample survival:** can known valid Georgian-derived exceptions be described by scope/preference rather than hidden?
4. **Contemporary transfer:** can it survive modern occupation without preserving obsolete social structure?
5. **Non-triviality:** does it constrain architecture rather than merely name a feature?
6. **Evidence honesty:** is its precision no greater than the sources justify?

Failure on any item keeps the proposition out of the positive register.

## 5. Candidate authority classes

D10-S may propose only:

- `CORE RELATION` — likely membership-bearing relationship;
- `LANGUAGE PREFERENCE` — characteristic but legitimately violable;
- `MORPHOLOGY PROFILE` — applies only to a named villa/townhouse/etc. family;
- `ELEMENT FAMILY` — optional but recognisably language-specific when selected;
- `HUMAN JUDGEMENT` — cannot honestly be automated or reduced further.

Do not create hard invariants merely because the historical corpus is repetitive.

## 6. Validation protocol

Only after the positive register is frozen:

1. inspect VAL-S1 Ridgehanger;
2. inspect VAL-S2 20 Castle Street;
3. inspect VAL-S3 Basildon Park;
4. test each candidate as `PASS`, `COUNTEREXAMPLE`, `NARROW`, `NOT APPLICABLE`, or `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE`;
5. do not rewrite the candidate and re-run the same validation case as if it were still held out;
6. if a material narrowing is required, record validation as having forced it and require a new independent challenge before treating the narrowed rule as validated.

## 7. Stop rule

D10-S stops when either:

### Release path

A small positive set survives with enough specificity that a materially different grammar would not satisfy it accidentally.

or:

### Withhold path

The available evidence still supports only general classical/architectural order, isolated motifs or morphology-specific dialects.

If the withhold path wins, D11 records `WITHHOLD` rather than inventing Georgian essence.
