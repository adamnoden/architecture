# Owner project brief

**Issue:** 29 October 2026, reconciled pre-site brief for design development. This records established direction and outstanding decisions; it does not imply owner acceptance of newly proposed choices, a selected site or construction readiness.

## Purpose and authority

Design and ultimately build the owner's own home on a later-selected London plot. Use the book to examine the architecture and communicate earned conclusions; use computation to keep information dependable. The [owner-house charter](../../project/owner-house-charter.md) owns this purpose and priority. The [delivery route](../../project/del/owner-house-delivery-plan.md) owns execution stages.

The immediate brief is to develop a desirable, coherent and credible home whose ordinary occupation is as carefully considered as its maintenance. Rooms, light, privacy, garden relationships and architectural character must earn their value independently of the technical strategy. Provision for future intervention must justify its present space, material, cost and complexity.

## Reading the decisions

- **Confirmed direction:** explicit owner instruction or governing charter.
- **Selected study:** current project design choice; change through the owning design decision, not silent optimisation.
- **Preference:** seek to satisfy, with explicit reasons for any proposed departure.
- **Open:** decision or evidence still needed. An inherited model value is not an owner answer.

Architectural geometry remains in its existing sources. This brief owns project priorities and decision needs; it does not reproduce a dimensional schedule or create a second model.

## Reconciliation of older and current sources

The [RH-00 ledger](../reference-house/brief-and-constraint-ledger.md) remains useful for principles, preference classification and questions. Its old fixture dimensions, open morphology options, stair positions and historical room assumptions are not authority to undo the later accepted re-plan. The ledger's current-owner-constraints section and the current re-plan identify that later direction.

The pre-site house remains a specific study. An actual site can require explicit adaptation or rejection; a nominal orientation or assumed utility approach is not a site fact. Current engineering, environmental and product assumptions must retain their limitations. HRS-BASELINE and HRS-TECHNICAL own the wider identity/applicability repair; this brief does not declare it complete.

The desire to investigate elaborate replaceable interiors does not impose them everywhere. HAD's wall/room composition, IAS proportionality and OAS interface studies determine where such systems earn a place. Similarly, a drawn plant envelope does not select or size equipment.
