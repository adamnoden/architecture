# Delivery

`docs/delivery/` translates sufficiently mature Ovid’s House propositions into **requirements, responsibilities and stage decisions for a real commission**.

Delivery material is downstream of architectural and evidential work. It does not make an unresolved research proposition true merely by turning it into a requirement.

The live internal programme owner is `project/del/delivery-programme.md`. The current upstream book/Reference-House resolution sequence is recorded internally in `project/book-resolution-programme.md`: IAS/OAS/HAD author selected architecture, BCR classifies construction-resolution gaps and routes genuine delivery obligations here, and HRS absorbs material consequences back into the final house.

## What this area owns

Delivery records may define what an appointed design team must resolve, demonstrate, coordinate or hand over, including stage-appropriate verification obligations.

Examples include:

- the [RIBA Implementation Brief Template](riba-implementation-brief-template.md);
- [External Maintenance Access Requirements](external-maintenance-access-requirements.md);
- [Human-Factors Verification Requirements](human-factors-verification-requirements.md).

For selected IAS/OAS/HAD assemblies, delivery may also own concrete obligations such as fabrication responsibility, tolerance ownership, shop-drawing requirements, substitution/equivalence criteria, first-article acceptance, commissioning and reinstatement evidence—but only when BCR/owning design records have established the assembly and the obligation is actually earned.

These documents should state the required outcome and proof obligation without pretending that one implementation is universal where the upstream architecture has not established that.

## What this area does not own

Delivery does not own:

- the general architectural proposition — see [`../manuscript/`](../manuscript/);
- evidential validity — see [`../research/`](../research/) and [`../prototypes/`](../prototypes/);
- Reference House project choices — see [`../reference-house/`](../reference-house/);
- IAS/OAS/HAD architectural authorship or HRS final synthesis;
- BCR construction-resolution classification;
- target, jurisdiction or real-site facts merely because they affect a commission;
- present project-wide status — that lives in `STATUS.md`.

The completed conditional deployment work under `project/dpl/` records the target/site/deployment boundary but does not establish a real commission. Delivery and any future site-specific deployment exercise may exchange constraints; they remain distinct authority layers.

## Rule

**Delivery material states what a real project must resolve; it does not convert an unresolved proposition into proof.**

Where later engineering, physical evidence, site evidence, regulation or professional judgement changes an upstream proposition, update the owning source first and reconcile the delivery requirement downstream. DEL must not solve an architectural conflict by quietly turning it into a contractor requirement.

The wider documentation ownership contract is `docs/README.md`; the repository-wide public map is the root [`README.md`](../../README.md).
