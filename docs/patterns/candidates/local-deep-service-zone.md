# Candidate — Local Deep Service Zone

**Status:** held; no HSA pattern ID  
**Evidence:** Proposed / credible coordination tactic  
**Primary unresolved gate:** demonstrate a recurring invariant beyond “make more space locally”

## Candidate proposition

Where one bulky or gravity-dependent service would otherwise force an entire floor/ceiling zone to deepen, concentrate the additional depth locally around the real route or crossing.

The possible architectural value is **localising service depth rather than universalising it**.

## Why it remains held

The move is sensible, but the Phase-6 audit found insufficient evidence that it is yet more than good coordination practice. A pattern must resolve a repeatable relationship with stable forces and meaningful variants; “provide more depth where needed” is too close to a generic tactic.

## Admission gate

Promote only after several worked conditions show a stable repeatable relationship, for example across:

- gravity drainage;
- major extract/ventilation ductwork;
- transfer zones around structure;
- local plant/terminal conditions.

The worked cases must show:

1. what defines the local zone geometrically;
2. why whole-storey depth is materially worse;
3. how the zone composes with structure, fire/acoustic boundaries and architecture;
4. when the extra local complexity is not worth it;
5. at least two materially different implementation families.

## Risks

- creating awkward soffits merely to save nominal floor depth;
- multiplying boundary interfaces and fire/acoustic details;
- moving complexity into prominent rooms;
- designing around an oversized service that could have been rerouted or reduced;
- turning every coordination clash into a “zone” rather than rewinding an upstream plan/service decision.

## Evidence / provenance

The idea appears repeatedly in the project's service-depth discussions and in Phase-6 targeted evidence around housing service zones, but the audit deliberately retained it as a candidate/design tactic.

See [`../../research/pattern-language-phase6-targeted-evidence.md`](../../research/pattern-language-phase6-targeted-evidence.md).

## Does not prove

This candidate does not justify local soffits/deep zones whenever coordination becomes difficult. Downstream depth pressure may instead be evidence that the route, room arrangement or system choice should change.
