## 4. Why external review no longer blocks the paper fixture

The external-review pack exists but has not been reviewed.

That must remain visible.

However, waiting for external practitioners before H1-PAPER would confuse two questions:

1. **Is the internal model coherent enough to compose a complete bounded house and expose its proof gaps?**
2. **Are the technical/regulatory claims actually correct enough for competent professional reliance?**

H1-PAPER tests question 1.

External review tests question 2.

The fixture should therefore proceed while deliberately failing release on absent external evidence.

## 5. H1-PAPER-01 rules

### Boundedness

The source must stay inside existing H1 families wherever possible.

Use:

- detached two-storey masonry dwelling;
- simple rectangular/square-ish massing;
- simple duo-pitched roof;
- ordinary masonry window/door conditions;
- private stair;
- ordinary two-storey fire route;
- clustered wet/service core;
- hybrid passive-stack ventilation research family;
- ASHP + low-temperature radiators;
- ordinary wet-room family;
- controlled wall/roof service penetrations.

Do not introduce:

- basement;
- flat roof;
- major cantilever;
- transfer structure;
- complex split levels;
- double-height atrium;
- attached garage;
- arbitrary curtain wall;
- Category 2/3 accessibility;
- elaborate smart-home systems;
- PV/battery/EV merely to make the house feel complete.

The fixture is a falsification instrument, not a portfolio design.

### One source

The source authors architectural facts once.

It must not manually reproduce:

- structural model;
- ventilation checklist;
- fire checklist;
- Part-M checklist;
- waterproofing checklist;
- evidence matrix

as parallel user inputs.

Those are compiler consequences.

### No invented proof

Where competent calculations/evidence are absent, the run must say so.

The successful research outcome may therefore be:

**SOURCE COMPOSES / RESEARCH MODEL COHERENT / BUILDING RELEASE FAILS.**

## 6. Whole-house stresses H1-PAPER must create

The frozen source should deliberately make families interact.

At minimum:

### Architecture ↔ structure

- entrance/stair axis;
- ordered front elevation;
- floor openings;
- window/door openings;
- roof support/load path.

### Architecture ↔ services

- wet core located subordinately rather than taking over principal sequence;
- service riser close to bathrooms/kitchen/utility;
- heating distribution follows accessible geography;
- ventilation stacks reach roof without corrupting principal rooms.

### Environment ↔ envelope

- every outdoor-air inlet / extract terminal is an envelope occurrence;
- roof stacks consume PEN-ENV-01;
- airtightness and ventilation remain separate;
- Part-O purge openings are roles on openings, not a duplicate window system.

### Wet services ↔ structure ↔ waterproofing

- upper wet room above/near core;
- drain/soil route passes through floor in planned service zone;
- WZ-BSM-01 consumes floor falls/drain interface;
- floor opening adequacy remains external.

### Fire ↔ circulation ↔ windows

- final exit and stair route;
- upper sleeping-room escape-window roles under selected family;
- architectural window order can conflict with fire/openable requirements and must compose.

### Evidence ↔ change

Mutations must stale only what actually depends on the changed fact.

## 8. Required mutations

The H1 fixture should include at least one mutation in each class:

- **architectural:** break entrance/stair sequence or opening hierarchy while retaining technical validity;
- **structural:** move opening/stair/core into a support route;
- **environmental:** change inlet/stack/terminal geometry or site context;
- **wet:** move drain/wet room away from core or break membrane penetration detail;
- **fire:** remove escape role while leaving the physical window;
- **accessibility:** obstruct entrance/route;
- **evidence:** substitute a product/component without carrying evidence;
- **re-solvable:** make a small change that is probably repairable within the family but is not currently valid.

The point is to test invalidation topology, not accumulate errors.
