# H1-PAPER-01 — Complete Bounded-House Research Brief

**Status:** frozen research brief  
**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Purpose:** perform the final major internal paper-compilation test before any implementation architecture is selected.  
**Release posture:** a successful research compile is expected to remain **NOT RELEASE-READY** while declared external evidence is absent.

## 1. Central question

> **Does the compiler abstraction remain coherent when one complete ordinary two-storey house must compose architecture, structure, envelope, fire, access, environmental strategy, heating, wet services, waterproofing, penetrations, maintenance and evidence at the same time?**

S0 proved one wall bay.

S1 proved one complete room.

S2 proved a connected two-storey cluster and the separation of architectural from technical validity.

H1-PAPER-01 is deliberately the point at which the abstraction must survive the whole.

## 2. Why this is the stopping-point test

The project now has bounded research families for the ordinary conditions required by the selected house.

Adding further pre-emptive ontology before this test would weaken the programme: the project would be designing a catalogue rather than testing a compiler.

H1-PAPER-01 therefore has two possible useful outcomes.

### Outcome A — abstraction survives

The house composes from one semantic source; unresolved technical proof remains explicit; mutations invalidate the correct scopes; authoring remains architectural rather than checklist-driven.

Then the internal paper phase should stop.

### Outcome B — abstraction breaks

Whole-house composition requires duplicate discipline models, uncontrolled family proliferation, hidden external judgement or author-facing metadata bureaucracy.

Then the compiler framing should be weakened before software is built.

Both outcomes are valuable.

## 4. One-source rule

The source package authors each architectural fact once.

Examples:

- a room exists once;
- a door/window exists once;
- a stair exists once;
- the wet core exists once;
- route rank is declared relationally once;
- a penetration exists once;
- a selected external product/evidence item is referenced once.

From those source facts, the paper compiler derives obligations for:

- structure;
- boundaries;
- ventilation;
- heating;
- water/drainage;
- fire;
- accessibility;
- architecture;
- maintenance;
- evidence;
- target applicability.

The author must not manually maintain separate fire, ventilation, structural, waterproofing or evidence copies of the building.

## 5. Required whole-house interactions

The source is only valid as a research fixture if it creates all of these interactions.

### Architecture ↔ structure

- principal entrance and stair share a scoped axis;
- front openings participate in an architectural hierarchy;
- the central spine supplies ordinary support geography;
- the stair creates a real upper-floor opening/trimmer obligation;
- no arbitrary transfer is introduced merely to improve the plan.

### Architecture ↔ services

- the wet/service core remains subordinate to the principal arrival sequence;
- the vertical riser is shared semantically by relevant services without collapsing those networks into one object;
- heating and electrical/data routes follow accessible service geography;
- environmental routes reach the exterior without becoming dominant architectural features.

### Environment ↔ envelope

- purpose-provided outdoor-air inlets are envelope occurrences;
- passive/hybrid stacks terminate through PEN-ROOF-TERM-01;
- kitchen source capture crosses the wall through a controlled penetration but remains a separate technical obligation;
- airtightness is maintained independently of intentional ventilation openings;
- purge/overheating roles are carried by the same physical windows used by architecture and fire where applicable.

### Wet services ↔ structure ↔ waterproofing

- the upper bathroom is stacked over/adjacent to the wet core;
- soil/waste and water routes use a planned floor/service crossing;
- WZ-BSM-01 owns the waterproof boundary and drain transition;
- structural adequacy of floor openings remains external;
- dry-side service access is retained.

### Fire ↔ circulation ↔ windows

- all upper bedrooms participate in the selected escape-window route;
- ground habitable rooms retain a route to the hall/final exit;
- the kitchen does not create an unsupported open-plan stair condition;
- the entrance is simultaneously architectural arrival, access threshold and final-exit contributor.

### Evidence ↔ change

Mutations must stale only dependent evidence.

A technically unrelated edit must not turn the whole building red merely because the compiler cannot scope consequences.

## 7. Success criteria

The **research compile** passes only if:

- the source is internally coherent;
- no unsupported geometry was accidentally introduced;
- selected families compose without duplicate source models;
- every known absent technical proof appears as an explicit scoped obligation;
- architectural and technical validity remain separate;
- evidence dependencies remain legible;
- author-facing errors can be grouped by meaningful building consequence;
- the source remains understandable as architecture.

The **building release** passes only if all release-grade evidence exists.

That is not expected in this paper run.

## 8. Falsification conditions

Treat the whole-house abstraction as materially weakened if any of the following occurs:

- the same room/opening/service route must be re-authored for several disciplines;
- new family types are required merely because the source became larger;
- ordinary house conditions cannot be represented without unsupported catch-all metadata;
- evidence invalidation becomes building-wide by default;
- architectural edits cannot be distinguished from regulatory/technical failures;
- the source requires the author to input specialist calculation internals rather than architectural facts;
- external judgement is silently converted into a compiler PASS;
- the family catalogue becomes more important than the house.

## 9. Mutation requirement

After the baseline run, perform deliberate mutations spanning:

- architectural hierarchy;
- structural support;
- stair geometry/family re-solving;
- fire escape;
- passive/hybrid ventilation;
- envelope penetration;
- wet-zone evidence;
- maintenance access;
- service-core geography;
- target version/evidence invalidation.

At least one mutation must create each of these outcomes:

- technically invalid but architecturally coherent;
- technically coherent but architecturally wrong;
- outside supported domain;
- current occurrence invalid but plausibly resolvable within the selected family;
- evidence stale while geometry remains valid.

## 10. Stop rule

After baseline compilation, mutations and final red-team:

> **do not create H2-PAPER, S3 or another ontology-hardening round merely because more paper work is possible.**

The next meaningful evidence should come from:

- competent external professional attack;
- selected physical/product prototype work;
- or a minimal executable compiler vertical slice.

H1-PAPER-01 is the intended logical end of the pre-implementation internal research phase.
