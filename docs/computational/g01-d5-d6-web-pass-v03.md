## 1. Executive result

The research has moved beyond the original three-case seed without earning a Georgian rulebook.

### D5

**PARTIAL PASS — evidence model and negative conclusions are strong; positive dimensional-rule promotion remains blocked.**

D5 now has credible evidence at several scales:

- room/volume;
- opening/façade;
- dwelling/plot;
- row/block;
- urban field;
- construction element.

It strongly rejects a universal Georgian room ratio and shows that dimensional relationships need scope, role, absolute size, phase and uncertainty.

It does **not** yet justify positive G-01 dimensional bands or preferred ratio families.

### D6

**MODELLING GATE PASS — architectural-rule gate remains open.**

The evidence is now strong enough to promote several style-neutral modelling corrections:

- connectivity, route role and sequence are distinct;
- coupling authority belongs to an attribute;
- coupling has architectural scope/owner;
- section is first-class;
- shared architectural variables can generate technical obligations without merging proof authorities;
- design intent and executed fabric are separate evidence states.

The first real mutation, Danson’s principal-room shortcut, confirms the connectivity/sequence distinction.

No Georgian-specific hard rule is promoted by this gate.

## 3. Source discipline now governing D5/D6

Publicly inspectable web evidence is the hard boundary for this phase.

Source access states:

- **W-I** — directly inspectable;
- **W-R** — inspectable reproduction with retained provenance;
- **W-L** — located but not inspectable at useful quality;
- **W-U** — unavailable/unresolved.

A high-authority archive catalogue entry can be `W-L`. It does not become a measurement merely because its metadata says “measured plan”.

Every future metric extraction must state:

- source;
- phase;
- scale/scope;
- measurement method;
- access state;
- uncertainty;
- derivation.

## 5. D5 findings that have survived the expanded evidence

### D5-F01 — no universal room ratio

Strongly supported.

The direct villa evidence already contains 1:1, 4:3, 2:1 and canted/polygonal families under different sectional conditions.

### D5-F02 — ratio must travel with absolute size and role

Strongly supported conceptually.

An aspect ratio detached from width, length, height, use, furniture, hierarchy and morphology is not a sufficient architectural variable.

### D5-F03 — dimensional systems are scope-specific

Promote as modelling correction.

Examples now include:

These observations should not be averaged into one “Georgian proportion”.

### D5-F04 — shared sectional datum can unify plural plan families

Supported by Danson.

Potential G-01 architectural significance remains to be tested beyond the seed cases.

### D5-F05 — positive dimensional preferences remain unearned

Still true.

No hard/soft G-01 ratio band is promoted.

## 7. D6 evidence matrix

### Marble Hill — section as architectural authority

The Great Room is a 24 ft cube and its upper part occupies the corresponding second-floor position.

Result:

> first-floor hierarchy rewrites upper-floor topology.

This is direct evidence that section cannot be reduced to a late height property of plan geometry.

### Danson — attribute-specific coupling

Blind-window evidence shows:

- simulated-window **existence** can be driven by exterior composition;
- its **centreline** can be driven by the interior and consequently sit off the exterior centre.

Result:

> coupling authority belongs to an attribute, not an object.

### Danson — shared architectural/technical variable

The Library principal floor beam spans between window-jamb conditions.

Result:

> one architectural coordinate can participate in both compositional and structural obligations without the grammar proving structural adequacy.

### Danson — sequence versus connectivity

The original principal rooms formed a circuit around the stair. A later direct Hall–Saloon opening added a shortcut.

Mutation Run 01 shows:

- Hall→Saloon depth **2 → 1**;
- graph connectivity increases;
- Dining/Library remain connected but cease to be required intermediate spaces on the shortest Hall–Saloon route.

Result:

> connectivity can improve while architectural sequence changes.

### Bedford Square — scope-specific coupling

The aggregate terrace composition can have a centrepiece spanning two dwellings, with the compositional centre at their division.

Result:

> coupling needs architectural scope/owner; a row centre is not necessarily a dwelling centre.

### Dean Street — scoped order under urban constraint

A four-bay ordered front with off-centre entrance remains a valuable qualitative counterexample to whole-house centred-entrance rules.

Result:

> symmetry/alignment must remain explicitly scoped.

## 13. Stop rule

Do not keep accumulating Georgian examples because the corpus is interesting.

Acquire another case only when it can answer a named unresolved question better than the current evidence.

The research objective is now **falsification and promotion control**, not corpus growth.

## 14. Current judgement

D5 has done enough to make simplistic proportion programming indefensible, but not enough to replace it with positive numerical rules.

D6 has done more: it has produced a small set of general grammar-modelling capabilities that survive very different Georgian conditions without becoming Georgian taste.

That is the correct asymmetry.

> **The first thing G-01 has successfully generated is not a Georgian rule. It is a better model of what an architectural rule would need to mean.**
