# G-01 — Georgian-Derived Domestic Grammar Charter

**Status:** canonical scope and authority statement for G-01  
**Research programme:** [G-01 Research Brief](g01-research-brief.md)

## Identity

**G-01 is a Georgian-derived domestic architectural grammar.**

It is the first selected architectural language being researched through the House Systems Architecture computational framework because the Reference House already has a Georgian-derived architectural temperament.

G-01 is not an HSA doctrine profile, construction system or definition of good architecture in general.

Its job is narrower:

> **Describe a coherent contemporary domestic language derived from selected English Georgian/classical precedent strongly enough that membership, preference and explicit deviation can be discussed and eventually represented.**

## Authority boundary

Keep these axes independent:

```text
HSA DOCTRINE
  long-life architectural / technical propositions

G-01 GRAMMAR
  Georgian-derived spatial / compositional language

SUPPORTED TECHNICAL DOMAIN
  systems and parameter ranges the compiler can reason about

REFERENCE HOUSE PROJECT
  site, programme, courtyard morphology, selected dialects and implementations
```

A house can therefore produce any of these meaningful states:

```text
HSA doctrine     PASS
G-01             FAIL
```

```text
HSA doctrine     FAIL
G-01             PASS
```

```text
HSA doctrine     PASS
G-01             PASS
H1 domain        UNSUPPORTED
```

No result acquires the authority of another axis.

## What G-01 may own

Subject to evidence and derivation, G-01 may become opinionated about:

- spatial hierarchy;
- topology and sequence;
- axes, bays and ordering relationships;
- dimensional and proportional families;
- storey and opening hierarchy;
- plan / section / elevation coupling;
- element families and compositional relationships;
- historically grounded preferences and explicit deviations.

These rules define membership in G-01. They are not HSA requirements.

## What G-01 does not own

G-01 does not, merely by being the first grammar, own:

- maintenance geography;
- lifespan separation;
- failure tolerance;
- passive-first environmental strategy;
- technical service topology;
- cavity masonry or any other supported-domain family;
- the Reference House courtyard;
- the courtyard's slight Andalusian influence;
- the Reference House brass/bronze tectonic dialect;
- every historical Georgian construction or social arrangement.

Those belong to HSA, technical scope, project morphology, local dialect, implementation or historical evidence as appropriate.

## Contemporary adaptation

Historical description and present normative grammar are different operations.

G-01 research may discover recurrent Georgian relationships and then choose to preserve, modify or reject them for contemporary domestic use. Accessibility, environmental performance, contemporary occupation, structure and servicing can constrain the resulting design without becoming part of the definition of Georgian architecture itself.

Where HSA creates an additional project requirement, record the interaction as a compatibility condition between grammar and doctrine rather than silently rewriting G-01 around HSA.

## Relationship to the existing research brief

The existing research brief was written while the project still used **Long-Life House** terminology and its title/opening wording therefore couples G-01 more closely to that project identity than the current architecture permits.

This charter supersedes that wording on **identity and authority only**. The brief remains the active corpus, annotation, hypothesis, mutation and falsification programme. Its later sections already establish the correct separation between:

- Georgian core grammar;
- courtyard project morphology;
- local Andalusian courtyard dialect;
- HSA doctrine;
- Reference House tectonic dialect.

When the research brief next receives a substantive editorial revision, rename it to **G-01 — Georgian-Derived Domestic Grammar Research Brief** and align its opening definition with this charter. Do not create a rewrite merely to erase the historical project name.

## Governing test

A materially different future grammar should be able to use the same HSA and computational framework without inheriting G-01 rules.

Conversely, G-01 should remain recognisably and unapologetically Georgian-derived even if the HSA doctrine were removed from the comparison.
