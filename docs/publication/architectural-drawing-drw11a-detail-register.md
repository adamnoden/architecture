## Purpose

Re-audit the small-detail queue after completion of the Reference House architectural drawing and analytical families, and admit only details whose present technical/prototype authority is strong enough for architectural detail grammar.

This is an **authority gate**, not a quota. A useful doctrine, a selected pattern occurrence, a prototype brief or an attractive interface does not by itself earn a 1:10 / 1:5 construction-style drawing.

DRW-02C remains the controlling baseline: assembly-selection authority is the minimum threshold for a detail to look selected rather than hypothetical, and unresolved performance closure must not be visually completed by the illustrator.

## Evidence change since DRW-02C

The project has advanced substantially at whole-building and coordination scale:

- DRW-05 hardened the principal section while explicitly retaining frame/reveal/threshold construction as absent technical authority;
- RH-09 selected `OW-01` as a representative permanent-opening / replaceable-window occurrence and selected W0/W1 occupied-surface logic;
- RH-10 selected a credible masonry cavity-wall **coordination family** and a 400–425 mm normal / 450 mm stress-test wall allowance;
- DRW-08 established `SV-01`, service capacity classes and dry-side access at architectural section scale;
- DRW-10B proved service / maintenance geography on both plans without inventing routes or product packing;
- prototype work has produced briefs, rigs, build packs and test protocols, but no relevant measured acceptance result that promotes an unresolved interface into tested construction authority.

These are meaningful advances. They do **not** silently convert coordination propositions into resolved technical details.

## Candidate decisions

### 1. Permanent opening / replaceable frame — still the highest-value deferred detail

Authority has improved since DRW-02C, but in a very specific way.

RH-09 now selects the project occurrence and sequence:

`permanent masonry opening → durable mounting / tolerance zone → replaceable frame → removable internal trim`.

It also selects important behavioural rules: mechanical accessible fixings; no brittle plaster bridge to the moving frame; decorative trim must not be the primary air/water/thermal boundary; complete frame replacement must be an actual operation.

RH-10 adds a credible deep masonry envelope family and therefore makes the problem more realistic.

But RH-09 simultaneously records the jamb/head/sill mock-up as **technically unvalidated**, and RH-10 explicitly refuses to select the frame's thermal position. No window mock-up result exists in the prototype result set.

A drawn jamb, head or sill would therefore have to invent at least one of the very facts the mock-up is meant to determine. Relationship authority has become stronger; assembly-selection and performance authority have not crossed the detail threshold.

**Disposition: `DEFERRED / ABSENT_TECHNICAL`.**

DRW-11B remains an authorised *decision packet*, not an authorised drawing packet. Unless new evidence lands before it executes, its correct outcome is `NOT_REQUIRED — authority threshold not met`.

### 2. Service wall / riser — publication need already satisfied at the owned scale

The service family has advanced further than it had at DRW-02C, but the advance is spatial rather than constructional.

DRW-08A/B and DRW-10B now give the reader:

- the `SV-01` reservation;
- vertical relationship between plant, upper bathroom and dry circulation;
- service capacity classes;
- dry-side access intent;
- ground/first host-space geography;
- explicit absence of invented branch routes and packing.

That is the architectural claim. A smaller-scale riser/wall detail would add apparent exactness rather than new authorised information because exact packing, enclosure, closures, structural penetration and product clearances remain absent.

**Disposition: `NOT_REQUIRED AS DETAIL`.**

DRW-11C should therefore close without drawing unless genuinely new technical source appears before execution.

### 3. Third-detail gate — no candidate currently earns DRW-11D

P03 is the strongest remaining candidate because the floor edge is a real project dependency, not decorative interface research. It still fails the admission gate explicitly: the P03 handoff says the prototype is not yet built or engineer-reviewed and forbids selecting a connection before engineering comparison.

`WCJ-01`, W1/W2 and `TH-01` likewise remain prototype/specialist questions. Roof-edge detail would largely repeat already-successful roof/water drawings while inventing technical build-up.

**Disposition: no third detail is admitted.**

DRW-11D remains a decision packet and should close `NOT_REQUIRED` unless authority materially changes before execution.

## Representation ceiling after DRW-11A

### Authorised

- the already-frozen 1:100 / 1:200 architectural and analytical drawings;
- abstract relationship/interface diagrams where their abstraction is explicit;
- project occurrences, zones, access relationships and replacement sequences stated without construction completion;
- prototype/build-pack drawings clearly confined to evidence-generation work rather than publication construction authority.

### Not authorised as resolved publication detail

- window jamb/head/sill construction;
- exact service-wall/riser packing or enclosure construction;
- structural floor-edge connection;
- wall/ceiling movement construction;
- W1/W2 attachment/lining assembly as a selected house detail;
- external threshold construction;
- roof/parapet/cornice technical build-up;
- any closure that would visually imply resolved structural, fire, acoustic, thermal, air, water, drainage or workmanship performance where current source marks it unresolved.

## Re-entry rule

A later detail may re-enter only if the owning packet can identify **new authority that changed this register**. At minimum it must state:

1. the exact reader question;
2. the selected assembly family and source-owned controlling geometry;
3. what fixing/release/tolerance logic is actually selected;
4. which relevant performance boundaries are resolved enough not to be visually falsified;
5. the result of any prototype/test on which a non-standard physical claim depends;
6. why the information is not already clearer and more honest in plan, section, overlay or prose.

A prototype brief, rig or protocol is not a result. A coordination allowance is not a construction build-up. A pattern occurrence is not a tested junction.
