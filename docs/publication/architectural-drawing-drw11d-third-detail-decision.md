## Decision

Admit **no third construction-style detail** to the present publication.

DRW-11A froze the complete candidate field. DRW-11B and DRW-11C then independently confirmed that the two most obvious detail candidates must not be manufactured from incomplete authority. No new technical or prototype evidence has appeared that changes the remaining candidates' status.

The correct result is therefore `NOT_REQUIRED`, not selection of a third detail to satisfy an assumed quota.

## Strongest challenger — P03 structural floor edge

P03 is the only remaining candidate with enough Reference House significance to deserve a fresh challenge. It is a real project dependency rather than a decorative interface proposition:

- representative side-wing span is approximately 4.2 m;
- engineered timber I-joists are the coordination baseline comparator;
- the architectural floor zone is approximately 350 mm;
- permanent masonry support, wall restraint, diaphragm transfer and boundary closure are real duties;
- the prototype programme explicitly compares conventional restraint-type hangers with seated-bearing challengers and any other engineer-credible family.

That strength does **not** cross the detail-authority threshold.

The P03 handoff is explicit that the prototype is **not yet built or engineer-reviewed**. Its stop rule prohibits selecting a Reference House connection until a structural engineer has compared the viable families against the actual span/load assumptions.

A publication detail today would therefore have to choose or imply:

- support and restraint family;
- bearing/hanger geometry;
- reactions and member adequacy;
- diaphragm/shear-transfer mechanism;
- wall-restraint mechanism;
- fire/acoustic floor-edge closure;
- relationship to the permanent air-control layer;
- anchor/fixing geometry;
- movement assumptions.

Those are the questions P03 exists to resolve. Drawing them first would reverse the evidence order.

**P03 disposition: `DEFERRED / ABSENT_TECHNICAL`.**

## Remaining candidates

### `WCJ-01` wall/ceiling movement junction

The problem and architectural location are selected, but the mechanism is not. P04 exists to compare a conventional junction, concealed-slip cornice and restrained metal alternative. Actual movement demand, boundary closure and cycling/reinstatement evidence remain unresolved.

**Disposition:** deferred; no construction detail.

### W1 controlled attachment / W2 replaceable lining

W1 is a selectively used architectural datum, not a frozen universal assembly. Anchor/load classes and specialist effects remain unresolved. W2 remains an experimental challenger whose build pack and test protocol are evidence-generation instruments rather than a successful result.

**Disposition:** no publication construction detail.

### `TH-01` external threshold

The interface duties and substantially flush architectural intent are selected, but level-access geometry, drainage/weathering, doorset relationship and thermal/air/water closure remain technical-design work.

**Disposition:** deferred; no construction detail.

### Roof/parapet/cornice water edge

DRW-07 and DRW-10C already explain the authorised roof topology, collection and outward-failure logic. Exact parapet/coping/insulation/waterproofing construction remains unresolved.

**Disposition:** no extra detail; low incremental reader value and high false-authority risk.

### Selective floor access / grating

No current Reference House maintenance task selects this research idea. A visually compelling detail would promote an unselected occurrence.

**Disposition:** not selected; no detail.

## Why zero details is a valid DRW-11 result

DRW-11 was authorised to determine whether a small number of detail drawings had become supportable after the main architectural family was complete. It was not authorised to manufacture construction resolution so the book would resemble a technical-detail catalogue.

The audit has produced a useful negative result:

- the publication can now show the Reference House rigorously at whole-building and analytical scales;
- several interfaces have strong design intent and clear future technical questions;
- none currently has enough selected assembly/performance/test authority to support ordinary construction-detail grammar without overclaiming.

That is a more professional result than polished speculative details.

## Re-entry rule

Any later detail must identify the evidence that changed DRW-11A–D. Prototype briefs, rigs and protocols are not results. A coordination zone is not a construction assembly. An interface invariant is not a selected junction.

## Handoff

DRW-11B–D therefore close the small-detail substream with **zero admitted construction-style details**.

Proceed to DRW-11E: audit the existing Parts I–III figures under the architectural-drawing doctrine and freeze `RETAIN / REDRAW / DELETE` decisions. That audit is independent of the refused detail quota and may still admit early-book explanatory figures where they earn their place.

DRW-11D is complete when this record passes repository validation and merges under an exact-main lease.
