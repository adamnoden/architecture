# DRW-11B — Permanent-opening / replaceable-frame detail decision

**Programme:** DRW — Architectural Drawing Programme  
**Packet:** DRW-11B  
**Issue:** #154  
**Status:** `NOT_REQUIRED — DEFERRED / ABSENT_TECHNICAL`  
**Starting lease:** `39c7a68e1ea32a0b17538d5a5a32afde19b3a8dc`

## Decision

Do **not** produce a construction-style jamb, head or sill detail for `OW-01` at the current project authority.

DRW-11A re-audited the full small-detail field immediately before this packet and froze `OW-01` as the highest-value deferred detail, but below the threshold at which normal architectural-detail grammar would be honest. No source or prototype evidence has changed between the DRW-11A merge and this packet.

This packet therefore closes `NOT_REQUIRED` rather than turning a well-developed relationship into fictional technical resolution.

## Authority that is genuinely owned

The Reference House now owns a strong architectural invariant:

`permanent masonry opening → durable mounting / tolerance zone → replaceable frame → removable internal trim`.

RH-09 further selects these project behaviours:

- the masonry reveal and sill belong to the long-lived building;
- frame fixings should be mechanical and accessible rather than buried behind irreversible wet finish;
- internal reveal / architrave trim is removable;
- brittle finish must not bridge directly from permanent wall to moving frame;
- decorative trim must not become the primary air, water or thermal boundary;
- full-frame replacement must be treated as an actual operation rather than inferred from glazing replacement.

RH-10 adds a credible deep masonry envelope coordination family and requires any future window interface to work within the 400–450 mm wall family.

That is sufficient to state the architectural relationship clearly. It is not sufficient to draw a selected construction junction.

## Authority still absent

A jamb/head/sill detail would have to choose or visually imply facts the project explicitly leaves unresolved, including:

- exact frame position through the wall depth;
- jamb, head and sill construction geometry;
- fixing type, spacing, edge distances and release sequence;
- dimensional tolerance / adjustment mechanism;
- air-control continuity;
- water-management and sill/drainage geometry;
- thermal continuity and thermal-bridge strategy;
- movement allowance and seal family;
- relationship to cavity closure / lintel / structural opening;
- product-family applicability and replacement envelope;
- validated workmanship sequence.

RH-10 explicitly declines to select whether the frame aligns toward the inner leaf, cavity or another thermal position. The RH-09 handoff explicitly records the window jamb/head/sill mock-up as **technical detail unvalidated**.

The prototype repository contains no completed window mock-up result that changes those statements.

## Why an “indicative” detail is still rejected

At 1:10 or 1:5, linework is not neutral. A competent reader reasonably reads layers, fixings, seals, falls and closure geometry as selected construction information. Labelling invented geometry “indicative” would not undo that implication.

Nor would a generic subframe diagram solve the problem: selecting the subframe material, continuity, fixing direction, seals, drainage and thermal position is precisely the unresolved technical work.

The correct current representation is therefore the already-frozen architectural relationship plus the whole-building opening geometry—not a pseudo-detail.

## Re-entry condition

`OW-01` remains valuable and should re-enter later if the project acquires enough new authority to answer, without illustrator completion:

1. where the frame sits in the wall family;
2. how jamb/head/sill air-water-thermal continuity is achieved;
3. how the frame is mechanically fixed, adjusted and withdrawn;
4. how the long-lived opening survives replacement without destructive edge work;
5. what movement/tolerance zone is selected;
6. what the jamb/head/sill mock-up or competent technical review actually demonstrated.

A future successful detail should cite that changed authority directly.

## DRW consequence

- no SVG is produced;
- no renderer work is required;
- no publication plate or figure slot is reserved;
- no source geometry is hardened merely to satisfy the drawing programme;
- `OW-01` remains a deferred technical obligation, not a missing DRW deliverable.
