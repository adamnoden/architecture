## Purpose

DRW-08B turns the frozen DRW-08A authority contract into one retained architectural section and proves it through the publication pipeline.

The section is intentionally not a riser detail. Its reader task is to show that the house has a bounded, accessible and vertically coherent service geography without implying an MEP design that does not exist.

## Graphic contract

- whole-house west→east section at true `1:100`;
- controlled shell / courtyard / gallery / level datums;
- light contextual fields for `G-PLANT-01`, `U-BATH-01` and dry-side galleries;
- provisional `SV-01` clear reservation and `PEN-SV-01` engineering-starting envelope;
- qualitative dry-side access and roof-service continuation;
- current service classes stated as capacity notation rather than drawn pipe/duct packing;
- no exact penetration geometry, wall build-up, plant product outline, branch centreline, trimmer, firestop or roof distribution geometry.

## Physical proof

The plate uses the established blank → verso furniture → recto drawing grammar. The SVG remains literal `166 × 112 mm`, with no fit scaling, zoom or page-space distortion. A4 and conservative B5 outputs must retain the 12 mm fold exclusion, 5 mm B5 outer safe edge and browser-safe physical drawing typography.

## Stop rule

A drawing or annotation that does not fit at true scale must be corrected in the renderer. Page CSS may not shrink the architectural geometry or typography to conceal the defect.
