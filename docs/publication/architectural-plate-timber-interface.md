# Timber floor and entrance articulation

Owner feedback requires the floor to read as a separate material assembly from
masonry, with a legible door opening and coherent elevation details. This
successor holds the floor plan and inward/outward roof geometry.

`georgian/section-exemplar.ts` now owns the current candidate. The previous
joined-solid candidate is retained in `section-exemplar-01.ts` and PLT09.
Weather and inner leaves have distinct shared grey fills; the cavity stays white.
The floor is no longer unioned into the masonry fill. Nominal bearing pockets
are subtracted from the inner leaf only and receive the separately drawn timber
zone; the cavity and weather leaf remain uninterrupted by floor structure.

The existing 350 mm floor zone is allocated as 20 mm finish, 22 mm deck, 238 mm
nominal joist, 55 mm service void and 15 mm ceiling. These are explicit drawing
assumptions, not selected products, sized joists, span verification or acoustic/
fire design. The principal section runs longitudinally along the represented
member, so it shows its profile rather than inventing repeated end-grain cuts.
Spacing, restraints, bearings, hangers, moisture separation, penetrations and
removable access remain technical obligations. End marks identify timber without
decorative wood grain. A dashed line distinguishes the service reserve.

The front wall is absent at this cut because it passes through the paired door.
A section frame, closed-leaf convention, separate fanlight and threshold now make
that opening legible. The section does not prove an accessible external approach.

Front elevation leaves have consistent stiles/rails and six panels per leaf,
with a separate rectangular fanlight and frame. Window heads and sills are closed
nominal profiles attached to the aperture rather than isolated strokes. These
choices select an architectural convention, not joinery fabrication, a lintel
product, flashing, water shedding or a tested threshold assembly.

PLT10 supplies principal section, roof, collection and front/courtyard consequence
views at BOOK B5/A4 1:100 and WALL A2 1:50. Historical packs remain reproducible.
Source tests and finished-PDF size/scale/font/Unicode checks accompany rendered
inspection. Physical acceptance, final book integration and technical release
remain open. Apply the accepted material distinction to remaining section views
before whole-set release; do not mix old fused floor regions into a current set.
