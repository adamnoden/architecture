# Principal/service sections and pitched roof candidates

This packet advances PLT-06 and PLT-07 against the accepted ground/first plan
and simplified exterior. It does not reopen room layout. The executable source

## Section construction

A locator fixes section A at X 6000 and service section B at X 9900, both looking
west with south at the left. Wall thicknesses, floor runs, partition intersections
and aperture cuts derive from the held source. A crosses the front entrance,
open courtyard and north courtyard entrance; it does not invent an opening in
the solid middle of the south courtyard wall. B crosses the WC/plant/pantry/
kitchen and bedroom/bathroom/en-suite/principal sequence, including the riser
reserve. Dashed riser geography is a spatial reservation, not pipework or a
resolved slab penetration. Gallery-side access remains a plan relationship.

Cut wall and floor regions use the shared faint fill and cut stroke. Roof profile,
nominal underside and ceiling lines remain secondary to the cut. The reserved
350 mm principal floor zone and 300 mm roof zone are coordination assumptions;
no joist, member size, reinforcement, vapour layer or selected junction is invented.

The low storage floor cannot extend through the shallow eaves. Its nominal edge
is now set back 1000 mm from the outer faces, leaving the reserved roof zone above
FFL 6900. The ceiling line continues to the inner wall face. This is a drawing
coordination choice, not proof of platform support, insulation continuity,
withdrawal capacity or safe hatch access. The former upper stair is omitted.
Ground-to-first stairs lie west of both cuts and are not falsely drawn on them.

The five-sheet proof uses complete single-sheet sections at BOOK 1:100 and WALL
1:50. It proves source/cut relationships, not the final principal BOOK spread.
The historical register's spread and fold-safe composition must be resolved at
book integration rather than inferred from this inspection sheet.

## Roof and water reading

The roof plan has four trapezoidal fields surrounding the open square court.
Their total projected area equals shell minus court. Corner ridges connect the
outer and courtyard corners. Height is eaves plus the nearest outer-edge distance
multiplied by tan(25 degrees); the four field falls therefore run outward.
The service section's middle roof trace is horizontal because that plane falls
perpendicular to the cut, not because the roof is flat.

The paired water reading adds symbolic external collection, four provisional
outlet positions and outward spill arrows. These positions are authored drawing
assumptions requiring coordination with elevations and external access. Gutter
profile/size, outlet/downpipe capacity, below-ground connection, discharge consent
and flood consequence are not established. The drawing deliberately leaves
courtyard surface drainage unresolved. It does not imply that roof water passes
through the courtyard or that an unspecified soakaway can accept it.

Roof-work access, safe platforms/anchors, covering and junction details remain
open. Do not reintroduce dormers, roof windows or a flat access roof to hide them.
