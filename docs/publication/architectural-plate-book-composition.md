## Physical composition

B5 is 176 × 250 mm; A4 is 210 × 297 mm. Ordinary drawings remain exact 1:100.
Page frames are mirrored. The full external plant-door swing now fits clear of
the binding: the previous small source canvas could crop its outer extent.
Recovering the full symbol is a paper-space correction, not a wider doorway.

Pairs start on even pages: plans 2–3, section 4–5, services 6–7, roof 8–9.
The section is one canonical 1:50 paper-space scene emitted as two physical
viewport crops. No vector, type or stroke is resized to fit. Its small locator
uses an explicit 1:500 geometry scale and unchanged physical type. The 12 mm
critical-exclusion band on each side of the fold is retained. Room labels, the rehomed open-courtyard label,
datums, locator letters, scale ratio, scale bar and notes sit wholly outside it.
Only repetitive courtyard window/ground geometry crosses the band; no unique
route event or sole dimension/marker depends on the fold.

A third PDF presents B5 pages in actual facing positions: blank/1, 2/3, 4/5,
and so on, with the final verso facing blank. It uses the same page markup and
physical SVG crops. It is a viewing aid, not printer imposition or a paper/binding
selection. The A2 family remains available from PLT16; no unchanged WALL pack is
reissued merely to inflate the output count.

`publication-drawing-v2/composition.ts` supplies source-agnostic paper translation
and deterministic viewport rendering. An independent asymmetric scene tests
lines, paths, rectangles, text, unchanged physical dimensions/styles and rejected
invalid offsets/crops. House choices, page selection and margin decisions stay
outside the generic renderer. Existing renderer defaults/bytes are unchanged.
