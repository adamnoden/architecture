Physical review exposed one real defect on the first-floor substrate: **`East gallery` collided with the `Courtyard` label** on the canonical first-floor plan.

Because the substrate is deliberately reused, the same defect propagated to exactly three controlled publication artefacts:

- `docs/reference-house/publication-first-floor-plan.svg`;
- `docs/reference-house/publication-first-service-maintenance-overlay.svg`;
- `docs/reference-house/publication-structural-span-overlay.svg`.

The owning correction is intentionally narrow. `U-GAL-02` annotation and leader moved by **1000 model mm = 10 mm in paper space at 1:100**. No wall, opening, room, circulation, structural, service or other architectural geometry changed. No type size, line role or drawing style changed.

The corrected paper-space position is regression-locked in `publication-first-floor-plan.test.ts`.
