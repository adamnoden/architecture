# Coherent section grammar

This revision addresses the section as one assembly drawing while holding the
plan and twin-pitch roof. The owner requests a legible courtyard doorway,
roof-space support/floor reading, restrained cavity strokes, clear timber support
interfaces, grounded building and fully articulated windows beyond the cut.

This historical proof is retained in `georgian/section-exemplar-05.ts`; the
[current successor](architectural-plate-section-assemblies.md) owns new work.
PLT10 retains the prior renderer
in `section-exemplar-02.ts`. New proof is PLT11 (a specimen identity, not completion
of programme PLT-11 physical acceptance).

## Material and support reading

Leaf boundaries use primary rather than cut-weight strokes, avoiding four heavy
black borders in the 417.5 mm envelope. Differentiated leaf fills and the white
cavity remain. Timber end diagonals and masonry pockets are removed. The nominal
longitudinal joist ends 25 mm inside each finished wall face and rests on a
separate indicative 100 mm projection/40 mm deep seat convention. No proprietary
hanger, fastener, anchor, structural bearing or moisture detail is selected.
These dimensions describe the drawing assumption, not a buildable support design.
The timber does not extend into masonry or across the cavity. Structural support,
restraint, fire/acoustic continuity and connection design remain unresolved.

The 350 mm principal floor zone remains split into finish/deck/joist/service void/
ceiling. Thin source boundaries are retained rather than multiplied into thick
black bands. Support seats remain visually separate from the wall and timber.

## Roof space

A continuous nominal ceiling member zone sits over the upper lining. It is
125 mm deep over a 15 mm lining and stops 25 mm inside wall faces, clearing the
nominal roof underside. Limited 22 mm storage decking sits directly on that zone
between the held setbacks. Former dashed FFL 6900 storage lines are removed;
the indicated storage surface is now nominal +6712 mm. This is a light roof-space
ceiling/deck proposition, not a usable attic storey or a checked storage-load
floor. Support seats, spans, loading, hatch access and usable storage limits
remain provisional. Upper partitions meet the ceiling lining without a drawn gap.

## Apertures and ground

The family/dining left edge is the grand courtyard doorway through the cut.
It now has a frame/closed-leaf/threshold convention, as does the entrance.
The windows beyond the cut retain the front's nominal frame, sill and head
profiles, at secondary weight; cut material remains primary.

The house is no longer drawn on a single unsupported line. A 300 mm ground-floor
build-up reservation and external/courtyard grade relationship are shown. Its
faint fill denotes a spatial reserve, not concrete or a selected floor assembly.
Broken support-continuation marks extend below the wall cuts. They are schematic
indicators of unresolved foundation work: no footing width, depth, bearing stratum,
foundation type, ground investigation result, insulation/membrane specification
or ground-floor load path is invented. Their drawn extent must not be measured
as a foundation depth. Door thresholds do not establish accessible approach or
waterproofing performance.

This is an architectural section candidate, not a structural or foundation
