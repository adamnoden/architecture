# Simplified roof and facade successor

The owner accepts a single consistent modest roof pitch, no dormers or roof
windows, six-over-six sashes, and restrained architectural articulation. Floor
plans remain held. This supersedes the exterior of the earlier mansard candidate.

`compiler/reference-house/georgian/facade-source.ts` owns the exterior successor,
sharing ground/first room, partition and opening geography with the accepted
source. A 25-degree outward pitch runs uninterrupted from the courtyard high
edge to external eaves. No flat or very-low-slope substitute is admitted.
The high edge falls from 10878 mm to approximately 8759 mm. Gutter, hip junction,
covering selection and external roof-work access remain technical obligations.
There are no roof openings.

This change withdraws the usable attic and full-height first-to-attic stair
claim: after the nominal 300 mm roof zone, even the highest inner edge gives
less than 2100 mm above attic floor level. The upper stair must not be carried
into new sections. Low storage and an access hatch remain provisional; hatch
position, safe access and usable storage extent need coordination. Ground-to-
first stair and the two principal floor layouts stay held. Earlier three-level
plans/sections are historical and must not be combined with this roof as a
current coordinated set.

Facade choices are nominal architectural selections, not construction details:
60 mm visible frame faces, six-over-six sash bars, expressed sill/head lines,
180 mm eaves/cornice zone, 150 mm plinth and entrance surround, paired panelled
front doors and a 550 mm fanlight zone within the existing entrance aperture.
External ground is provisionally 150 mm below floor level, with an entrance
landing at threshold level. The elevation does not prove the accessible approach,
drainage, damp separation, thermal junction or material specification.
Plinth strokes stop at door apertures. The threshold remains at floor level;
external ground and door panels make that relationship legible.

`publication/specimens/plt07/` supplies five elevation sheets in B5/A4 1:100 and
A2 1:50. PLT06 imports the retained `elevations-01.ts` renderer, preserving its
historical appearance; PLT05 retains the mansard coordination source. The source
policy keeps the historical 30-degree candidate value separately from the new
25-degree project choice. No historical technical proof is retroactively changed.

Next sections must use this exterior successor and explicitly omit the former
attic stair. Physical print acceptance and final drawing release remain open.
