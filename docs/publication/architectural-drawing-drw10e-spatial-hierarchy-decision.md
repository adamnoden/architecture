# DRW-10E — spatial / sectional-hierarchy drawing decision

**Status:** NOT_REQUIRED — decision recorded  
**Programme:** DRW  
**Parent:** #154

## Question

Does a separate spatial / sectional-hierarchy drawing still earn a publication view after the canonical plans, principal longitudinal section and synthesis axonometric have been completed?

## Decision

**No. Do not create another drawing.**

The reader task already has stronger, source-controlled successors. A new spatial-hierarchy diagram would repeat an argument that the publication can now make through ordinary architecture.

This is a `NOT_REQUIRED` packet outcome under the execution plan, not a missing deliverable.

## Evidence

### DRW-02A already assigned the work

The Part IV claim audit deliberately maps the relevant claims to canonical orthographics:

- court-centred movement and front → entrance/stair → court → garden sequence: **ground plan + principal longitudinal section**;
- persistent type identity: **paired plans + principal section + front elevation + service overlay**;
- sectional hierarchy / ground-floor primacy: **principal section**;
- arrival and recentering: **ground plan + principal section**.

The audit explicitly says that a separate circulation/sequence diagram is unnecessary if those orthographics communicate the sequence.

### DRW-02D froze the successor mapping

The final view register maps the inherited schematic reader task:

> **Spatial / sectional order → paired floor plans + principal longitudinal section**

and gives the inherited schematic the disposition:

> **DELETE WHEN SUCCESSOR LIVE**

DRW-03/04 and DRW-05 now provide those controlled successors. Their existence removes rather than creates a need for a parallel hierarchy diagram.

### DRW-09 does not create another proof burden

The admitted sectional axonometric is a synthesis instrument. It may improve comprehension after the orthographic facts are established, but no unique claim depends on it. It therefore does not justify a second diagrammatic restatement of spatial hierarchy.

## Publication consequence

No renderer, source or SVG work is authorised by DRW-10E.

The inherited `Spatial / sectional order` schematic is **not manually deleted here**. DRW-00D requires atomic publication replacement; the old active path remains until the controlled successor set is placed/captioned/validated and the publication inputs are switched deliberately in DRW-12.

The decision is therefore:

- **new spatial / sectional-hierarchy drawing:** `NOT_REQUIRED`;
- **canonical proving views:** paired floor plans + principal longitudinal section;
- **optional synthesis:** already handled by the admitted DRW-09 sectional axonometric;
- **legacy schematic:** delete only at the later atomic replacement step.
