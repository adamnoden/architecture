## Representation and source limits

Shared section wall/floor and aperture functions are exercised by the new
service section and all five elevations. Window grids, nominal frame face and
profiles remain house inputs. Door frames/paired leaves are schematic joinery,
not product specifications. The service floor's full run is an assembly-depth
reservation, **not an 11 m timber member**. It deliberately omits the principal
cut's indicative seats because support direction and intermediate support have
not been established. No new structural capacity or bearing role is assigned
to the internal partitions. Riser space is source-owned; floor crossings,
fire-stopping, routing, plant selection and service performance remain unresolved.
Foundations, lintels, ground packages and envelope closures remain provisional.

Repeated opening names on different storeys are qualified in the new section
adapter. A shared plan option qualifies region IDs by assembly, addressing the
collision between independently numbered envelope and partition regions. The
legacy default retains old specimen fingerprints; the current family explicitly
opts into qualified IDs. This changes identity only, not wall geometry. A
non-Reference-House two-assembly regression proves this boundary. Face-on aperture
notation gains a caller-owned outline role so outer apertures can be primary
while frame/grid/profile lines remain secondary; existing defaults are unchanged.
