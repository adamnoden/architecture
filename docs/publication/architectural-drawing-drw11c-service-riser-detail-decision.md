# DRW-11C — Service-wall / riser detail decision

**Programme:** DRW — Architectural Drawing Programme  
**Packet:** DRW-11C  
**Issue:** #154  
**Status:** `NOT_REQUIRED AS DETAIL`  
**Starting lease:** `049633440d24014fab9e3221c6c89d75baca6030`

## Decision

Do **not** produce a construction-style service-wall / riser detail at current authority.

The service architecture is already represented at the scale the project genuinely owns. DRW-08 established the architectural service section, `SV-01` reservation, capacity classes and dry-side access relationships. DRW-10B added ground/first service-maintenance geography while explicitly refusing invented routes or product packing.

A smaller-scale detail would therefore add apparent technical certainty rather than a new authorised architectural claim.

## Authority already published honestly

The current drawing family can state:

- `SV-01` as an approximately 900 × 700 mm architectural reservation;
- the plant → riser → upper bathroom / dry-circulation relationship;
- the engineering starting envelope around the principal penetration where explicitly recorded;
- service capacity classes rather than exact contents;
- dry-side access intent;
- ground- and first-floor host-space geography;
- protected primary spaces and service concentration;
- explicit absence of remote en-suite infrastructure where source-controlled.

Those are meaningful architectural decisions. They belong in plan, section and analytical overlay, and those instruments now exist.

## Authority still absent

A 1:10 / 1:5 riser or service-wall drawing would visually imply decisions the source deliberately leaves unresolved, including:

- exact pipe, duct and cable packing;
- branch centrelines and route coordinates;
- exact plant/product selection and clearances;
- final structural opening / trimmer / member design;
- riser enclosure build-up;
- access-panel construction and hardware;
- fire and acoustic closure;
- waterproofing / bathroom-side closure;
- penetration sleeves, collars and seals;
- maintenance withdrawal envelopes tied to selected products;
- final opening and closure details at floor/ceiling boundaries.

None of those facts has been promoted by the completion of DRW-08 or DRW-10. Their explicit absence is part of those packets' authority discipline.

## Why no extra “diagrammatic detail” is needed

The project already has the correct diagrammatic instruments:

- service-side architectural section for vertical capacity and access relationships;
- paired service-maintenance plan overlays for horizontal host geography;
- prose for the unresolved technical duties.

A new figure labelled “diagrammatic” would repeat this information at a smaller scale without resolving a distinct reader question. It would also risk teaching the reader to mistake a reserved volume for a designed MEP assembly.

## Re-entry condition

A service-wall/riser construction detail should re-enter only if technical design produces a selected assembly with enough source authority to state, without illustrator invention:

1. actual riser contents and packing constraints;
2. selected enclosure/access construction;
3. structural penetration and support geometry;
4. applicable fire/acoustic/water closures;
5. selected product access and replacement clearances;
6. the exact reader question not already answered by the existing plan/section family.

## DRW consequence

- no SVG is produced;
- no renderer work is required;
- no publication slot is reserved;
- DRW-08 and DRW-10B remain the complete current architectural representation of this service claim;
- unresolved MEP and enclosure design stays downstream of DRW rather than being invented to fill a detail register.

DRW-11C is complete when this decision record passes repository validation and merges under an exact-main lease.
