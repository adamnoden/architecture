# TYP-03C — body-text physical review protocol

Status: **active TYP-03 review protocol; amended by TYP-03D value frontier**  
Programme authority: GitHub issue **#425 — TYP — Publication Typography Programme**  
Depends on: TYP-03A specimen harness + TYP-03B licence-safe evaluation boundary + TYP-03D affordability/value frontier

## 1. Purpose

TYP-03 must choose a body reading system by **reading actual Ovid’s House pages**, not by looking at foundry specimens, alphabets, isolated glyphs or enlarged PDFs.

This protocol freezes how that judgement is made so the current candidate hypotheses do not become self-fulfilling.

The five admitted systems are:

1. Lyon Text + Atlas Grotesk;
2. Tiempos Text + Söhne;
3. Scala Pro + Scala Sans Pro;
4. Source Serif 4 + Source Sans 3;
5. Newsreader + Source Sans 3.

Georgia / Arial remains the before-state, not a finalist.

TYP-03D admits Newsreader/Source as the single free aesthetic challenger and adds a value rule after the blind review. It does not otherwise reopen the candidate search.

---

## 2. What TYP-03 is deciding

TYP-03 owns the **body reading system**.

It must freeze, together:

- body serif;
- body size;
- leading;
- practical measure;
- paragraph spacing versus first-line indentation;
- ragged-right versus justified composition;
- hyphenation policy;
- widow/orphan policy where typography materially affects reading;
- roman/italic relationship;
- sustained page colour.

TYP-03 may eliminate a sans pairing if the serif/sans relationship is clearly wrong on ordinary pages, but detailed hierarchy/navigation and drawing-interface decisions remain later TYP packets.

---

## 3. The aesthetic question is explicit

A candidate is not aesthetically successful merely because its letters are individually attractive.

For Ovid’s House, aesthetic quality means the accumulated physical character of pages and spreads:

- **serious** without institutional stiffness;
- **literary** without preciousness;
- **precise** without clinical coldness;
- **quiet** enough that sustained reading becomes effortless;
- **slightly strange** enough that the object has its own presence;
- **durable** rather than fashionable;
- unmistakably a book rather than a website, report, architecture office document or luxury-brand object.

A technically excellent but aesthetically dead candidate may be rejected.

A beautiful candidate that becomes tiring, muddy, brittle or operationally impossible may also be rejected.

---

## 4. Proof conditions

### Round 1 — common envelope

All candidates see the same semantic specimen and common composition settings.

Primary physical comparison:

- conservative B5 territory;
- black only;
- 100% physical scale;
- same printer;
- same print-quality setting;
- same paper batch;
- same ambient reading conditions where practical;
- no candidate-specific tuning beyond what is required to achieve a genuinely comparable perceived body size.

A4 is retained as the regression/before-state surface but is **not** the primary aesthetic decision territory.

The first comparison should begin with the two body-rhythm alternatives that TYP-01 deliberately reopened:

1. spaced paragraphs;
2. first-line-indented continuous prose.

Within the better rhythm, compare ragged-right against justified/hyphenated composition.

Do not explode this into a full factorial beauty contest. Eliminate obviously weaker combinations as soon as the physical pages make the answer clear.

### Round 2 — bounded optimisation

Only surviving candidates receive candidate-specific adjustment.

Permitted adjustments:

- body point size;
- leading;
- measure within the target page territory;
- paragraph indent/space magnitude;
- bounded hyphenation/composition tuning.

Not permitted:

- candidate-specific decorative hierarchy;
- different manuscript samples;
- hiding a weak italic by choosing pages without italics;
- giving one candidate more whitespace merely because it needs rescue;
- changing the book’s intended character to suit the face.

The purpose is to let each viable face become **itself at its best**, not to art-direct five different books.

---

## 5. Bias control

The candidate ranking recorded in TYP-02 is a hypothesis, not privileged evidence.

For the physical review:

1. remove or cover candidate names on the printed sheets;
2. label the physical sets **A / B / C / D / E / BASELINE** only;
3. shuffle the order before each sustained-reading pass;
4. judge the pages before revealing the mapping;
5. do not consult foundry marketing copy during the review;
6. do not compare prices or licence complexity until the aesthetic/reading judgement for the round has been recorded.

The operator who prepares the specimens may know the mapping. The **reading notes should not be written while looking at that mapping**.

---

## 6. Reading procedure

For each anonymous set:

1. Read the entire fixed sustained-prose specimen normally rather than inspecting letters.
2. Re-read the shorter aphoristic sample.
3. Read the quotation/italic sample aloud once and silently once.
4. Put the set down and record the immediate whole-page impression before zooming into details.
5. Only then inspect specific problems: awkward colour, spacing, punctuation, italics, line endings, hyphenation, heading relationship.

After all sets have been read once, repeat them in a different order.

A candidate should not survive because of one exceptionally beautiful paragraph if it becomes annoying after several pages.

---

## 7. Review record

Do **not** create a weighted numerical score. The qualities are not commensurable enough for a fake precision total.

For each anonymous set record:

### Reading

- **effort:** effortless / noticeable / tiring;
- **tracking:** whether the eye moves naturally from word to word and line to line;
- **measure:** too short / calm / too long;
- **leading:** cramped / calm / loose;
- **page colour:** pale / balanced / dark / uneven;
- **paragraph rhythm:** whether continuous reading feels natural;
- **italic voice:** integrated / weak / theatrical / distracting;
- **punctuation:** quiet / conspicuous / muddy;
- **hyphenation/composition:** benign / noticeable / disruptive.

### Character

Record a short free-text judgement against each target tension:

- serious ↔ institutional;
- literary ↔ precious;
- precise ↔ clinical;
- quiet ↔ bland;
- strange ↔ theatrical;
- durable ↔ fashionable;
- book ↔ report/website/brand object.

### Verdict

Exactly one:

- **KEEP** — deserves bounded optimisation;
- **DOUBT** — viable but currently weaker; name the defect that would need to disappear;
- **REJECT** — state the decisive reason.

A rejection must name a real observed failure. “I like another one more” is not enough until the field has reached the final two.

---

## 8. Final-two decision and value rule

Once only two systems remain, the question changes.

Both finalists should already be good enough technically. The decision should therefore become deliberately aesthetic and architectural:

> **Which system makes Ovid’s House feel most like the physical book it ought to have been all along?**

At this stage it is legitimate to prefer one because its page colour, italic voice, proportions or serif/sans temperature are simply more beautiful and more native to the project.

The choice does **not** need to be reduced to a technical tie-break.

After that aesthetic judgement is written and candidate identities are revealed, apply TYP-03D’s affordability rule:

> **If a zero-cost/open finalist is physically equivalent or only marginally weaker than a paid finalist, choose the zero-cost/open system. A paid system wins only when its advantage is clear, repeatable and materially important to the book.**

Cost therefore cannot rescue a weaker free font during blind review, and prestige cannot justify paying for an imperceptible improvement after reveal.

Before freeze, the winning system must still survive:

- conservative B5 reproduction;
- black-only printing;
- the actual difficult glyph/numeral corpus;
- embedding/licensing/build feasibility;
- a page-count consequence check.

---

## 9. Evidence that may enter the repository

Durable TYP records may store:

- candidate identity after reveal;
- font file SHA-256 values where permitted/appropriate;
- composition settings;
- page-count/line-count measurements;
- written reading observations;
- anonymous A/B/C/D/E verdicts and later mapping;
- physical-print method/paper/printer facts;
- final decision and rejected-candidate reasons;
- cost/licence consequence after the blind reading verdict is frozen.

Do **not** commit restricted font binaries.

Do not commit proprietary trial-font specimens if the applicable licence does not permit their distribution.

Where foundry terms prohibit external/AI-service use, the AI workflow consumes the **written review result**, not the restricted font software or restricted specimen itself.

---

## 10. TYP-03 close condition

TYP-03 closes only when:

- the finalist has been read at physical target territory;
- body face/size/leading/measure are frozen;
- paragraph rhythm is frozen;
- alignment/hyphenation strategy is frozen;
- the winning face is aesthetically preferred, not merely operationally convenient;
- the losing finalists have explicit observed rejection reasons;
- the value decision has applied the TYP-03D free/minimal-cost preference after blind review;
- the winning system has a credible licensing/build route for later integration;
- the body decision does not pre-empt TYP-04 hierarchy or TYP-06 drawing-specific typography.

Until then, there is **no chosen Ovid’s House typeface**.
