## Visual findings

The wall-topology family is now visually settled enough for whole-house use: corners and junctions read as continuous construction rather than overlapping rectangles. The layered cavity family remains intentionally restrained at 1:100 and becomes more legible at 1:50 without changing paper-space tokens. This is preferable to making the book-scale drawing artificially technical.

The opening family is materially improved from the failed PLT-03 seam. Exact, approximate, ranged, centre-only and host-only states no longer collapse into variants of dashed jambs. The C13 review was the useful adversarial case: its first host-wide line with terminal ticks was semantically defensible but visually resembled an unlabelled dimension. That convention was rejected. The replacement is a 0.9 mm constant-paper faint possibility band outside the wall, bounded to the host and incapable of masquerading as physical construction.

The partition pair now reads correctly as two different ontological states. C15 is planning information only. C16 is actual cut matter, but its proof thickness remains explicitly representative inside the upstream range rather than a hidden architectural decision. C18 keeps connectivity requirements separate from door geometry.

C17 closes a capability gap discovered during atlas assembly: the semantic IR already contained dimensions, but the compiler had no composed dimension renderer. ADE-06 therefore earned that missing object rather than faking it in the specimen.
