# Current-house theoretical setting and external obligations

## Question and authority

After the coordinated layout and book-composition candidates, the remaining
setting drawings still described the historical house. This packet asks whether
the current 12 m house and 3.5 m courtyard can be placed in the already documented
theoretical width without importing historical technical conclusions or reopening
the owner's closed RSI programme.

`compiler/reference-house/georgian/setting-family.ts` owns the two candidate
scenes. Current house geometry comes from `layout-revision.ts`; the approximately
15.5 m surrogate width comes from the retained gross-clearance statement in
[external access and maintenance](../reference-house/external-access-maintenance-plan.md).
The resulting 1.75 m on each side is arithmetic, not an equipment working-width
acceptance. This packet does not adopt that older document's superseded roof,
plant, PV, tower or drainage implementation.

## Drawing decisions

- Keep open-ended lateral stress lines, without inventing a front or rear plot
  boundary, legal boundary, road, neighbour, geographic north or ground level.
- Derive the court from current coordinates, not the historical court.
- Keep the setting pair on one exact substrate. The second reading locates the
  current plant replacement opening and names court/outer-face obligations.
- Show no scaffold/tower footprint, ladder, vehicle route, outlet or discharge
  path. Residual space alone cannot establish any of those.
- Use exact 1:200 for B5/A4 and 1:100 for A2. Typography and line widths retain
  the candidate tokens. No fit scaling or release-token change occurs.

This supplies current candidates for the two setting roles. It does not change
the frozen publication register or imply their final admission. B5/A4 ordinary
house drawings remain 1:100; contextual setting views have their separately
labelled conventional scale.

## Evidence and limits

`publication/specimens/plt18` prepares two pages per physical format. Source
checks cover the current court, retained theoretical width, gross residuals,
plant aperture extent and identical setting substrates. Finished-PDF checks
cover six pages, exact physical dimensions, scale-bar endpoint distances,
embedded Source Sans 3 / Unicode, source hashes and unclipped text. Final PDFs
also require rendered inspection before admission.

The accepted source is a virtual house with a theoretical setting. Real-site
access, ground support, handling, drainage, fire and construction adequacy remain
unverified. Actual paper/binding and three-distance review remain with PLT-11.

## Programme position

PLT17 book composition is merged through PR #939. Current setting representation
can advance independently of final house-design resolution. The newer HRS #945
programme explicitly hands the final resolved house back to PLT for definitive
drawings after BCR #944. Current candidate progress must therefore not masquerade
as the final drawing-publication-v2 freeze.

The remaining internal drawing question is a useful current-source sectional
synthesis; the earlier transparent anatomy remains withheld. Final view admission,
manuscript/source reconciliation, actual physical review and atomic publication
integration also remain open. Technical design belongs to its owning streams.
