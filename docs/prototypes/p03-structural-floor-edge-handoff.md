# P03 Structural Floor Edge — RH-05 Handoff

**Status:** Reference House geometry handoff; prototype not yet built or engineer-reviewed  
**Source gate:** [RH-05 Structural Coordination](../reference-house/structural-coordination.md)  
**Programme:** [Tectonic Prototype and Engineering Programme](../development/tectonic-prototype-programme.md)

RH-05 gives P03 a real Reference House condition rather than a generic masonry/floor junction.

## Representative geometry

The first P03 Reference House specimen should represent a **side-wing floor edge**:

- gross floor span to coordinate: approximately **4.2 m**;
- primary floor family: engineered timber I-joist as the baseline comparator;
- permanent support: loadbearing dense-masonry inner leaf within a cavity-wall family;
- floor zone available from RH-04: approximately **350 mm** from ground clear ceiling to first FFL;
- structural deck remains the permanent diaphragm;
- removable/selective occupied-floor layers, if later selected, remain non-structural;
- routine horizontal services are not assumed to occupy the joist field.

The specimen should also represent the actual architectural edge obligations already carried by the vertical-bay work:

- masonry weather/thermal/air-boundary layers remain independent of routine room-side removal;
- floor support, wall restraint, diaphragm transfer, fire/acoustic closure and finish movement are separate duties;
- wall lining and floor finish must terminate without becoming part of the primary connection.

## First comparison

P03 should compare at least:

1. **certified restraint-type masonry hanger** — current conventional baseline;
2. **direct/seated bearing with separate restraint** — challenger only if the engineer judges it credible for this wall/floor family;
3. protected steel angle/shoe only if it answers a specific failure of the first two, not as an aesthetic preference.

Do not build a bespoke connector merely because House Kernel values designed interfaces.

## Additional RH-05 condition

The house also contains four short edge-beam conditions, approximately **3.4 m** long, continuing the courtyard support lines through the front/rear wings. These are not the first P03 specimen.

If engineer review shows that their bearing/restraint detail is materially different from the ordinary masonry/joist edge, create a second bounded P03 specimen rather than overloading the first prototype.

## Questions sharpened by RH-05

- Can the selected floor family fit the 350 mm architectural zone without reducing the controlled 2.95 m ground clear height?
- What is the best conventional support/restraint solution for a roughly 4.2 m side-wing span?
- Does any seated-bearing challenger improve inspection or future repair enough to justify extra interfaces?
- How is diaphragm force transferred independently of the removable room-side layers?
- How is wall restraint maintained if finishes and lining systems are later removed?
- What is the fire/acoustic closure at the floor edge?
- What anchor/hanger geometry is compatible with the permanent air-control layer?
- Which parts of the junction must be protected as no-drill/no-chase structure?

## Stop rule

P03 does not select a Reference House connection until a structural engineer has compared the conventional and challenger families against the actual 4.2 m span/load assumptions.

A prototype that feels elegant but depends on an unproved structural member or connection fails the gate.
