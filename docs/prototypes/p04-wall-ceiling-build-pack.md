# P04 — Wall / Ceiling Movement Junction Build Pack

**Status:** build-ready prototype pack at architectural evidence scope  
**Date:** 2026-10-12  
**Programme:** [Tectonic Prototype and Engineering Programme](../development/tectonic-prototype-programme.md)  
**Reference House occurrence:** `RH-P008-01 / WCJ-01`  
**Architectural authority:** [RH-09 Tectonic and Occupied-Surface Integration](../reference-house/tectonic-integration-rh09.md)  
**Companion protocol:** [P04 Test Protocol](p04-wall-ceiling-test-protocol.md)

P04 tests one proposition:

> **Can the wall and ceiling finish systems terminate independently so expected relative movement does not create a brittle crack, while the resulting junction still reads as ordinary architecture?**

The prototype does **not** exist to prove that a brass line is beautiful or that a cornice must be demountable. The conventional quiet junction is the control and may win.

---

## 1. Prototype families

Build/test three families against the same background condition.

### P04-A — excellent conventional control

A high-quality conventional wall/ceiling junction appropriate to the selected finish systems.

Purpose:

- establish the visual, labour and tactile benchmark;
- reveal whether the project is inventing complexity for a problem already handled well conventionally.

The control must be competently executed. A poor control invalidates the comparison.

### P04-B — concealed-slip cornice — **primary House Kernel challenger**

Working hypothesis:

```text
wall finish terminates
+ bounded movement gap
+ ceiling finish terminates independently
+ mechanically fixed cornice / cover attached to one controlling assembly only
+ overlap conceals the other assembly's movement
```

The cover must not become the air/fire/acoustic boundary by accident.

### P04-C — restrained metal slip/protection line — **secondary challenger**

Use brass/bronze or another durable metal only if the piece performs a genuine function such as:

- protects a vulnerable moving edge;
- provides a durable sliding/locating surface;
- acts as a genuinely removable cover;
- gives a precise sacrificial datum.

A metal strip that merely decorates the joint is a failed hypothesis even if visually attractive.

---

## 2. Representative Reference House geometry

Build a **full-height room-corner specimen** using the controlled principal-ground-floor ceiling datum.

Target rig:

- floor to ceiling: **2950 mm**;
- representative wall width: **minimum 1200 mm**;
- representative ceiling projection from wall: **minimum 1000 mm**;
- enough adjacent width to judge cornice/junction at normal room distance rather than as a detail coupon;
- rigid primary wall background representing `W0` direct-finished masonry or a dimensionally credible rigid analogue;
- ceiling frame/lining mounted so its edge can be displaced vertically relative to the wall in a controlled, measurable way;
- removable access to the back of the junction for inspection between cycles.

The rig may be reusable for A/B/C sequentially if previous fixings/finishes do not contaminate the next result. Otherwise build separate edge modules on the same datum.

---

## 3. Background and finish

### Wall

Baseline:

- rigid masonry/board analogue with realistic stiffness;
- directly applied plaster/mineral finish or a convincing mock-up of the actual finish thickness/edge behaviour;
- ordinary decoration.

Do **not** use W2 removable panels for the primary P04 test. The Reference House baseline is W0/W1 and P04 must remain useful even if W2 is rejected forever.

### Ceiling

Use a representative plasterboard/mineral ceiling build-up sufficient to reproduce:

- lining edge;
- finishing/plaster layer;
- normal decoration;
- movement relative to the wall.

P04 is not a ceiling structural test.

---

## 4. Movement rig

The ceiling edge must be adjustable relative to the wall without dismantling the visible junction.

Required rig capability:

- repeatable vertical displacement in both directions where practicable;
- measurement to at least **0.5 mm resolution**;
- total adjustment capability of at least **±5 mm** so the rig can explore beyond the eventual design envelope;
- no uncontrolled racking of the wall itself;
- ability to return to zero datum repeatedly.

The rig capacity is **not** the design movement allowance.

Before the prototype is treated as technical evidence, the expected project movement envelope must be informed by the actual floor/ceiling structure and specialist advice. If that envelope is unavailable, the exploratory sequence in the test protocol may be run, but the result remains exploratory rather than proof of adequacy.

---

## 5. P04-B concealed-slip construction rules

The primary challenger should satisfy these rules visibly in the build record:

1. wall and ceiling finishes do not rely on one brittle filler bridge;
2. one bounded gap exists behind the cover;
3. the cornice/cover is mechanically fixed to **one controlling assembly only**;
4. the opposite assembly can move behind the overlap without dragging the cover;
5. the cover can be locally removed without destructive wall/ceiling cutting;
6. reinstatement returns it to the same datum;
7. ordinary decoration cannot easily glue both moving assemblies together;
8. the detail has a deliberate response to out-of-range movement rather than concealing unlimited displacement.

### Fixing side

Test the more architecturally/technically credible fixing side first; do not assume wall-fixed or ceiling-fixed universally.

The build record must state:

- controlling assembly;
- fixing type and spacing;
- overlap depth;
- nominal hidden movement gap;
- release sequence;
- whether decoration crosses any intended slip face.

---

## 6. P04-C metal challenger rules

Metal is allowed only after a sentence can be written that begins:

> **This piece of metal is necessary because it…**

If the sentence is aesthetic rather than functional, do not build P04-C as a serious candidate.

Where built:

- use ordinary mechanically replaceable fixings where possible;
- avoid adhesive dependence if removal is part of the claim;
- provide safe finished edges;
- prevent dissimilar-material contact/corrosion issues as appropriate;
- avoid a width/profile that reads as a continuous technical rail around the room unless the architecture independently justifies it.

---

## 7. Boundary independence

P04 must preserve the distinction between visible trim and primary building performance.

The specimen record must show conceptually how, in the real building:

- fire resistance is maintained independently of the decorative cornice/cover;
- acoustic continuity does not rely on the cover;
- air-control continuity does not rely on the cover;
- the movement gap does not accidentally become an uncontrolled service/cavity route.

The prototype does not certify those performances. It must merely avoid a detail whose logic makes them impossible.

---

## 8. Build sequence to record

For each family photograph and time:

1. background inspection / datum measurement;
2. wall finish termination;
3. ceiling finish termination;
4. movement gap formation where used;
5. permanent boundary/closure treatment represented where relevant;
6. grounds / fixings;
7. cover or cornice installation;
8. decoration;
9. first visual assessment;
10. opening/removal sequence for B/C;
11. reinstatement.

Record every improvised filler, packer, adhesive or extra fixing. An undocumented improvisation is evidence about the detail.

---

## 9. Conventional control quality

P04-A should be executed to a standard a good plasterer/joiner would be willing to defend as finished work.

The challengers do not win because the control cracks after intentionally poor preparation.

The comparison should include:

- appearance at 0.5 m / 2 m / normal-room distance;
- visual straightness;
- shadow-line consistency;
- labour/sequence;
- sensitivity to substrate tolerance;
- repair after deliberate movement;
- effect of decoration.

---

## 10. Build completion gate

The build pack is complete only when the following are frozen before testing:

- family A/B/C geometry actually built;
- controlling assembly for B/C;
- fixing schedule;
- finish/decorative system;
- movement-rig zero datum;
- any project-derived expected movement envelope available at that date;
- explicit deviations from this pack.

Do not redesign the detail during the movement test without recording the original as a failed/qualified specimen.
