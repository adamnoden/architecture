# Reference House — RH-10 Envelope Coordination

**Status:** RH-10 corrective envelope baseline / pre-building-physics design  
**Date:** 2026-10-12  
**Triggered by:** whole-house audit of the inherited 350 mm planning wall allowance  
**Architectural authorities:** [RH-02 Ground Floor](ground-floor-development.md), [RH-04 Plan / Section / Elevation](plan-section-elevation-gate.md), [RH-09 Tectonic Integration](tectonic-integration-rh09.md)

RH-02 used **350 mm** as a conservative-enough temporary external/courtyard-wall allowance for furniture-scale planning. That was legitimate at RH-02, but RH-10 cannot call the house coordinated while the clear room dimensions depend on a wall depth that has never been stress-tested against a credible high-performance masonry envelope.

The result is a controlled correction in **planning allowance**, not a shell enlargement.

---

## 1. Regulatory performance anchor

The 2026 Approved Document L Volume 1 gives **0.26 W/(m²·K)** as the limiting area-weighted wall U-value for new dwellings and explicitly notes that meeting the dwelling performance targets will normally require some elements to perform significantly better than the limiting standard.

Canonical source: GOV.UK, *Approved Document L: Energy and greenhouse gas emissions — Volume 1: Dwellings (2026 edition)* — https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/approved-document-l-2026

RH-10 therefore does **not** size a wall merely to scrape the 0.26 limiting value. Exact U-value, thermal bridges, moisture risk and material selection remain building-physics work.

---

## 2. Selected envelope family at coordination level

Retain a conventional, intelligible masonry cavity-wall family as the first Reference House baseline:

```text
real facing masonry / brick outer weathering leaf
→ drained / insulated cavity
→ substantial loadbearing masonry inner leaf
→ continuous internal air / finish boundary
→ W0 direct mineral/plaster finish by default
   or sparse W1 architectural fixing/service datums where justified
```

The exact insulation family is not selected.

A plausible **geometric family**, not a construction specification, is:

- outer masonry leaf: about **102.5 mm**;
- insulated / drained cavity: about **150–175 mm** class;
- structural inner masonry leaf: about **140 mm** class as the first robust engineering comparator;
- direct finish / tolerance: about **15 mm** class before any local W1 build-out.

That places the principal wall family broadly in the **approximately 410–435 mm** range before local exceptional details.

This does not prove thermal or structural performance. It proves that the architectural plan is no longer relying on a 350 mm fiction.

---

## 3. Controlled planning allowances

From RH-10 onward use:

- **400–425 mm** as the normal external/courtyard-wall planning family;
- **450 mm** as the whole-house geometry stress test;
- anything materially above **450 mm** as a **rewind trigger** rather than a silent room-size deduction.

The 350 mm RH-02 allowance remains historical provenance only.

### Why 450 mm matters

With the current geometry:

- side wings = **4.2 m gross** between outer and courtyard envelopes;
- front/rear wings = **3.4 m gross**.

At two 450 mm envelope faces:

- side-wing clear depth remains approximately **3.30 m** before local internal finishes/tolerance;
- front/rear clear depth remains approximately **2.50 m**.

That is tighter than RH-02 but still coherent with the selected use:

- principal west rooms remain long 3.3 m-wide domestic rooms rather than collapsing into corridor-like spaces;
- east study/service rooms remain viable because only one side is a full external envelope and the inner gallery partition is lighter;
- garden dining/hall remains a shallow cross-room by design;
- RH-03's stair reservation of roughly **2.4–2.5 m square** sits at the lower edge of viability rather than being disproved.

The stair therefore becomes the **envelope-depth canary**: a future technical wall materially thicker than 450 mm must reopen RH-03 / shell width rather than stealing stair clearances.

---

## 4. Structure and permanence

The inner masonry leaf remains the first loadbearing comparator from RH-05. RH-10 does not prove that 140 mm is structurally necessary or sufficient; structural engineering owns that decision.

The architectural requirement is:

- outer weathering leaf is not falsely presented as the primary gravity structure if it is not;
- inner loadbearing leaf has clear bearing / opening / lintel logic;
- cavity ties, shelf/support conditions and movement are technically explicit later;
- the wall remains understandable as layered masonry construction rather than a simulated solid wall.

If an engineer demonstrates that a 100 mm-class inner leaf is preferable and robust, the saved depth is a benefit, not something the plan depends upon.

---

## 5. Air, moisture and removable layers

RH-09's occupied-surface hierarchy remains intact.

`W0` direct-finished walls are compatible with a continuous parge/plaster air-control approach on the inner masonry face where building-physics design selects it.

`W1` controlled attachment zones and any future `W2` lining must not become the primary weather, moisture or airtight boundary merely because they are removable.

The slow envelope must remain complete when faster-changing trim is removed.

This is particularly important at:

- window jamb/head/sill interfaces;
- plant/service penetrations;
- courtyard thresholds;
- roof/parapet junction;
- floor edge;
- any W1 fixing or local service datum.

---

## 6. Window consequence

`RH-P007-01 / OW-01` now inherits a more realistic deep masonry opening.

The replaceable-window sequence remains:

**permanent masonry opening → mounting/tolerance zone → mechanically fixed replaceable frame → removable internal trim**.

RH-10 does not select whether the frame sits aligned toward the inner leaf, cavity or another thermal position. The jamb/head/sill mock-up must prove:

- thermal continuity;
- water management;
- air continuity;
- fixing / withdrawal;
- reveal proportion at the 400–450 mm wall family;
- no brittle finish bridge across expected frame/wall movement.

A window detail that only works at the obsolete 350 mm sketch wall fails the gate.

---

## 7. Roof/parapet consequence

RH-08's low-slope roof puts greater responsibility on the outer wall / cornice / parapet junction.

Technical design must show:

- roof insulation and wall insulation continuity;
- drainage falls remaining independent of the thermal/air boundary;
- parapet/coping weathering that fails outward rather than into the cavity;
- PV mounting loads and roof-edge access without crushing or bypassing insulation;
- no decorative cornice piece becoming the sole water seal.

The low roof is allowed to be visually recessive. Its edge still has to be a real envelope detail.

---

## 8. Rewind conditions

Reopen RH-02/RH-03/RH-04 geometry if competent technical design shows any of the following:

1. required wall depth materially exceeds **450 mm** across the house;
2. thermal-bridge resolution requires projecting structure that damages opening order or maintenance access;
3. the courtyard wall build-up materially reduces daylight/opening geometry beyond the RH-07 assumptions;
4. the stair cannot retain its selected clear width/headroom after the real envelope and finishes are applied;
5. window replacement / air-water continuity cannot be achieved within the selected wall family without destructive permanent-fabric work.

---

# RH-10 envelope result

**PASS at architectural / pre-building-physics coordination level.**

The 12.0 × 10.8 m shell survives a **450 mm wall-depth stress test** without a morphology rewind. The Reference House no longer depends on the original 350 mm planning allowance.

The selected project family is therefore:

**real masonry outer leaf + insulated/drained cavity + substantial loadbearing masonry inner leaf + direct quiet internal finish by default**,

with exact structure, insulation, U-value, moisture and junction performance explicitly reserved for engineering/building-physics review.
