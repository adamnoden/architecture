# Reference House — Independent Review Questions

These questions concern **RH-LAYOUT-REVISION-02 / RH-LAYOUT-FAMILY-02**, the accepted 12 × 12 m pre-site house. [Current House Basis](current-house-basis.md) identifies its source owners and inherited evidence limits. This page prepares questions; it does not establish specialist review readiness, commissioned advice or acceptance.

## Structural engineering

Can masonry support and engineered-timber framing carry the current ring, entrance void, bridge, stair and service interruptions within the fully allocated 350 mm floor package? The 4.25 m gross surrounding band is not a verified clear span. Resolve bearing, restraint, diaphragm continuity, vibration, fire/acoustic depth and any transfers. No fictitious bearing may be assigned to the unsupported void edge. Review opening piers/lintels and the pitched roof as actual load paths. Foundations and ground movement remain site-dependent.

## Fire, escape and inclusive use

Test the open entrance volume, bridge and stair as one smoke/escape problem. Do actual window opening geometry, hardware and external landing conditions support the proposed bedroom escape route? Does the stair/hall require enclosure or another architectural change? Treat any guidance correspondence from the old fixture as historical; establish the applicable project/jurisdiction basis at review.

Test finished routes, doors, thresholds, guards, handrails, carrying and sanitary use as complete actions. The 1015 mm gallery and 1100 mm stair flights are drawn dimensions, not finished access or stair-lift certification. The current family/dining arrangement and ground WC need a credible entrance-storey sleeping/washing adaptation study; the old family-room conversion is not automatically available.

## Services, acoustics and wet rooms

Test equipment packing and replacement in the actual plant room, the 550 × 700 mm riser reservation, lining depths, fire/acoustic boundaries and access panels. The small-en-suite panel conflict and unfitted sanitary plan require a current design response. Prove gravity-drainage routes/falls and wet-floor build-up before drawing a successful connection. Do not interpret an access reservation as free internal volume.

Resolve ventilation from the owner's passive-first priorities and current indoor-air/moisture needs. Any mechanical system needs actual routes, duty, acoustics, cleaning and commissioning provisions. Separate cooker capture and replacement air must be coordinated with the whole-house pressure regime. Historical MVHR/heat-pump studies offer questions and candidate systems, not current performance results.

## Water, envelope and environmental performance

Trace both roof slopes, inner/outer collection, blockage and exceedance through to a safe receiving route. The courtyard is not limited to direct rainfall until roof collection and failures are resolved. Test thresholds, wet-room escape paths, ground levels, drying and accessible reinstatement. Confirm actual roof assembly, window/door interfaces, thermal bridges and vapour/air boundaries.

Re-run daylight, overheating, heat loss, ventilation and rainfall calculations against identified current geometry, products, orientation and applicable weather data. Historical solar screening and PV area do not certify the changed court and roof. A real site supplies ground, drainage destination, neighbours, access and planning facts.
