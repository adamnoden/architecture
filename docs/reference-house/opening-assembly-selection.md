# Reference House — opening assembly selection

## Selection and exact claim boundary

The Reference House selects the **conventional renewable frame/doorset family**
for the current nominal opening schedule, with independently releasable room-side
trim and deliberately renewed perimeter interfaces on full-frame exchange.

This is a bounded architectural family selection for triad/BCR development,
not fabrication sizing, product approval or a construction release. The selected
object is the component/maintenance/boundary topology below. Nominal source
geometry remains unchanged. Actual product-dependent dimensions and performance
are not described as already coordinated.

| Condition | Selected architectural family | Explicit limits |
|---|---|---|
| Outer and court windows | OAS E0: mechanically fixed replaceable frame, serviceable sash/glazing/hardware, independent room trim | Operating mechanism, actual frame size/position, support and complete head/jamb/sill remain product/technical-resolution inputs |
| Front entrance | Conventional paired doorset with separate fanlight intent and ranked independent surround | Actual clear passage, fanlight support, leaf mass/hardware and threshold level section unresolved |
| Garden and two court pairs | Conventional drained external doorset/threshold, low-access objective where the actual approach permits | No flush/level-access or weather-performance pass; actual paving/collector/overflow must be resolved |
| Plant replacement opening | Conventional service doorset, local release and deliberate threshold | Nominal 1400 is not equipment clearance; actual equipment/route still controls |
| Ordinary internal doors | OAS I0: supported mechanically fixed frame, serviceable leaf/hardware, independent architrave | Actual leaf/overpanel, frame fit and practical passage unresolved; consequential rated duties can require a complete different assembly |
| Wet/service internal doors | I0 only where compatible with actual wet/boundary duty | Trim is not waterproofing; no generic rated-doorset claim |

E1 sparse mounting interfaces remain eligible **local implementations** of this
family when their actual task earns them. E2 continuous retained cassettes are
not selected as the whole-house answer. E3 buried first release and I2 integrated
door cassette are rejected defaults. Future evidence can defeat the selected
implementation without discarding the life-separation proposition.

## What is fixed, what is conditional

Fixed at architectural-family scope:

- structural support of the opening/partition is independent of ordinary
  frame renewal;
- component support is not supplied by decorative trim or a removable wall face;
- routine hardware/sash/leaf maintenance is distinct from complete frame renewal;
- first release is reachable through local trim/cover, not permanent skim;
- full-frame renewal deliberately replaces interrupted seals/transitions;
- no additional house-wide subframe/cassette is presumed;
- nominal opening identity is level + host + id, with current source apertures
  and datums retained;
- room-side geometry and visible expression follow the completed shared junctions.

Conditional and **not fabrication-frozen**:

- mounting position, aperture/installation allowance, frame outside and section;
- anchor/support sizes, tolerances, loads and actual assembly performance;
- outer/court threshold levels, collection, blockage/overflow and exterior falls;
- practical passage, sash/leaf movement, carrying/handling and maintenance safety;
- actual frame/finish/hardware material choice coordinated with HAD;
- whole-house fire, security, acoustic, energy and moisture applicability.

The candidate g=15 window and g=10 internal-door examples remain sensitivity
points. They are not adopted installation tolerances. Likewise the 220–360
frame-location trial is not a selected installation position.

## Canonical assembly and maintenance set

The canonical scope consists of the current source opening schedule; the
[external](../../project/oas/oas-01e-external-candidates.md) and
[internal](../../project/oas/oas-01i-internal-candidates.md) dimensional/boundary
records; [IAS/OAS](../../project/oas/ias-oas-junction.md) and
[OAS/HAD](../../project/oas/oas-had-junction.md) junctions; and the
[replacement/topology drawing](../../project/oas/studies/selected-opening-renewal.svg).

The subsequent [OAS-NOMINAL-01 studies](opening-interface-studies.md) supply the
representative dimensioned architectural interfaces at 1:5 / 1:2. Their new
local study assumptions are distinct from whole-house fabrication adoption.
OAS-DETAIL explicitly gates triad on that missing original obligation.

These are architectural interface/maintenance drawings. They do **not** reinstate
the previously withheld OW-01 1:5 construction detail. A detailed construction
section requires actual new support, frame and environmental-junction authority;
the old engine's ability to draw it is not that authority.

The following task records are mandatory parts of the selected family:

| Task | First release and support | Boundary state | Reinstatement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hardware | Reach fitting, support leaf/sash as required | Actual applicable operating/seal duty identified | Fitting operation, capture and closure checked |
| Sash/glazing | Retained sound frame; support actual pane/sash mass | Opening temporarily unprotected as declared | Renew disturbed gaskets/seals; verify operation/drainage |
| Internal leaf | Support real leaf before hinge release | Any required boundary temporarily unavailable | Hinge/closure/latch and applicable assembly duty checked |
| Complete window/external frame | Local trim first; support frame independently; expose fixings/transitions | Weather/air/thermal interfaces interrupted deliberately; temporary protection required | Renew actual perimeter transitions; verify support, operation and water/air duties |
| Internal frame | Local trim and fixings; partition head remains independently supported | Named acoustic/fire/privacy interfaces deliberately interrupted | Restore the actual complete assembly duty, then trim |
| Threshold | Doorset-supported planned intervention, actual handling | Water/wear/access interface temporarily unavailable | Restore levels, support, collection/overflow and seals together |
| Retained mounting part, if used | Separate inspection/replacement task | Do not assume it lasts forever | Replace when unsound; no supplier-specific immortal adapter |

No task inherits a successful carrying path from a gross aperture. Universal
inside whole-frame removal is not selected. Where the actual frame cannot pass
inside safely, the maintenance plan needs a credible exterior operation rather
than a false claim that the whole house is internally demountable.

## Concrete resolution register

| ID | Question to discharge | Primary route / consequence |
|---|---|---|
| OS-01 | Actual mounting/support/anchor, frame position and tolerance relationship | OAS detail through BCR/EPR/product evidence; explicit source rewind if finished face/aperture must change |
| OS-02 | Complete head/jamb/sill primary, failed-seal and blocked-drain paths | OAS then MRA/BCR; no blanket conventional-water pass |
| OS-03 | Each external threshold's levels, collector, safe overflow and approach | OAS/house/HAD then MRA/BCR; actual outfall/site facts remain DPL |
| OS-04 | Actual clear passage, leaf sweep and frame/equipment handling | OAS/house; actual products and working geography |
| OS-05 | Rated/fire/acoustic/security/energy applicability | Competent BCR/EPR/product evidence; ordinary I0 may be rejected locally |
| OS-06 | Frame/material/sightline/shade/privacy and final appearance | HAD-02M/03 with OAS; retain source width/order or explicitly rewind |
| OS-07 | Actual trim/release/wet-floor/lining interfaces | IAS/OAS selected details; carry IAS wet-depth and I00-C1 obligations, never imply solved |
| OS-08 | Manufacture, installation, inspection and renewal acceptance | DEL after actual details; physical tests only where the selected proposition earns them |

OS-01/02/03 are substantive detail obligations. They prevent any claim that this
record is a completed engineered opening design. TRIAD-GATE must explicitly
accept or reject the bounded architectural scope and the unresolved obligations;
it must not quietly turn this family selection into an installed-detail pass.

## Effect on the house and downstream work

No opening is resized, no facade/window/room geometry changes and no nominal
threshold mark is reinterpreted as a physical step. No product performance,
statutory acceptance, physical test or maturity gain is implied.

OAS-03 establishes the selected architectural family and its replacement set.
OAS #942 remains open until the shared triad integration gate. IAS and HAD
selection must consume the same schedule and the resolution register.
MRA attacks the selected physical routes when sufficient details exist; BCR
must resolve or explicitly bound the technical obligations before HRS can
publish one apparently settled final house.
