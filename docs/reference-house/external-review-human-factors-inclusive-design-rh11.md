## Decision to resolve

Can people enter, use, clean, adapt and renew this house through complete ordinary actions? Test the actual furnished and finished geometry, including movement while carrying, rather than treating a clear dimension as proof of inhabitation. Establish the household's needs and any applicable access category; do not invent a universal user or treat a 600 mm diagnostic body as a statutory standard.

## Current source and adverse observations

The accepted two-flight stair has 20 rises of 172.5 mm, 300 mm going, nominal 1100 mm flights and a 1635 mm landing. The west gallery is 1015 mm and the bridge 1120 mm. Rails, guards, actual aperture and trimmer soffits remain unselected. The entrance void is 2275 × 2295 mm; no route is accepted merely because a drawn line passes it.

The nominal 1800 mm entrance pair does not establish single-leaf clear width. Threshold 0 and outside/court grade −150 mm are schematic and do not prove level access. Typical windows have nominal sill/head +750/+2850 mm; actual handles, opening arcs, restrictors, cleaning reach and guards need selection.

The [occupation study](../../project/bcr/bcr-occupation-result.md) identifies concrete problems: the accepted Bedroom 3 bed blocks a 600 mm route proxy even before leaves are included; the ground-WC leaf at 90° leaves a 282.5 mm direct gap to the court wall for that tested approach. This does not prove every coupled door/body sequence impossible, but it requires a redesigned or demonstrated complete action. The small eastern en suite is 2020 × 1075 mm clear and remains unfitted. No ground-floor shower or dependable sleeping/washing adaptation has been selected.

Accepted partial Bedroom 4 and wet-section studies remain unadopted alternatives. Their cabinet, tray and visible stack cannot be treated as source furniture or as a resolved accessible sanitary arrangement. A shorter NW shower-waste route introduces a directly wetted west window and its independent assembly/operation duties.

Later accepted [Bedroom 3 reuse](../../project/had/had-03-bedroom3-reuse.md), [kitchen](../../project/had/had-03-kitchen-options.md) and [court-level](../../project/had/had-03-court-levels.md) comparisons are unadopted alternatives. Their positive route states do not supersede adverse findings in current source. Assess the proposed one-sided bed, local gallery narrowing, counter/window reach and raised removable court as complete actions before any selection.

## Ordinary adverse cases

Test a person carrying a laundry basket when another person occupies the landing; a ground-WC user who must close the door before moving past it; a blocked roof receiver during maintenance; and replacement of the largest selected plant item. Use these to discover the first physical obstruction and the smallest architectural correction. Specialist access judgement and actual users' requirements should inform the alternatives, not be simulated by an agent's confidence.
