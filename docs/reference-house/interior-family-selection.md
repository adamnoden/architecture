## Selected family

Select **ordinary quiet finishes with local mechanical release at real tasks**:
V0 direct wall finish, sparse V1 grounds, H0 conventional dry floor, ordinary
skirting and independent opening trim. Select V3 only as a task-specific access
principle, not as an adopted panel occurrence. The house does not select a
universal backplane, removable wall or accessible floor grid.

This is new architectural selection authority for the bounded dry conditions
below. It is not completion of IAS-03: the wet assemblies, actual service-front
section and sanitary fit remain unresolved. The house is not yet a coordinated
selected interior system. No whole-house geometry, product or maturity changes.

Apply the later [whole-house material policy](material-policy.md) and [structural brief](structural-brief.md) to this partial selection. The 125 mm partition and 350 mm floor allocations below are source reservations, not approval of a stud wall, timber primary frame or synthetic constituent. The [located sink-cabinet comparison](../../project/ias/ias-03-sink-cabinet.md) now rejects the earlier resin bowl and conventional polypropylene siphon as defaults; actual connected assembly and maintenance admission remain open.

### A/B — skirting and opening end at 1:2 / 1:5

The section fixes only the face geometry, ordinary fixing access and independent
support principle. Nominal screw axes are at 40/110 above floor. Their heads
are reached through individually replaceable plugs; no continuous filler strip
or paint bridge conceals the first release. Anchor type, spacing along the run,
grounds, fire duty and suitability for the actual wall remain unresolved.

At a door, a 150-long end piece is removed normal to the wall first. It butts
the independently removable architrave; no mitred interlock requires lateral
sliding behind a frame. The end piece is a study length, not a standard module.
The drawing shows the dry face side only; OAS still owns frame mounting and
boundary reinstatement. No cable is attached to the decorative face.

Two 18 projections leave 1015 - 36 = **979** at west-gallery floor level.
Nothing in this selection certifies passage, escape, stair carrying or access.

### C — upper edge at 1:2

Local coordinates use the 3100 ceiling datum. Study wall finish stops at 3080;
the independent ceiling edge is represented at 3100. The optional cover runs
3060–3095, with 20 overlap on wall finish, 15 over the open joint below its
top and a visible 5 top gap. Cover projects 12 from finished wall and fixes
only to wall-side support, with an accessible axis at 3070. It does not bridge
onto the moving ceiling. This is a cover geometry, not an asserted movement
range or an adequate fire/acoustic boundary. An actual separately designed
boundary must remain behind it where required. Omit this cover where the
actual ordinary joint works without it; simplify before borrowing ceiling depth.

Above an aperture head at 2850, a 70 head trim reaches 2920. The optional
cover's bottom at 3060 leaves **140** of quiet wall. The plain default has
180 to the ceiling above the trim. These are appearance dimensions, not frame
tolerances or tool-clearance certification. No 2850-high internal leaf is selected.

### D/E — dry package and rejected wet overlay at 1:5

The section allocates the existing 350 exactly; it does not size members or
prove a duct fits 55. Ordinary dry finish can be renewed from above with local
damage to a first strip if the actual fastening needs it. It is not claimed
that every board lifts intact or gives access through the structural deck.

The previous wet trial remains **rejected for unchanged adoption**:
12.5 carrier + 1 membrane + 3 adhesive + 10 tile = 26.5 above deck, exceeding
20 by 6.5 before falls. The drawing deliberately shows this excess. It does
not shave the structural deck or silently consume the ceiling/service zone.
The trial 24.5 wet-wall build-up is likewise not selected over an assumed
15 allocation. A real wet system needs product support, falls, drain/trap,
floor/door levels and finish/support interfaces, or an explicit source rewind.

### F — access face at 1:5, unadopted occurrence

V3's 700 x 600 gross, 25 frame occupation each edge, 650 x 550 clear and
740 x 640 face remain a dimensioned **study**, not a selected service task.
A 20 face overlap on each gross edge is real; frame capture can reduce useful
clearance further. No component or tool/withdrawal envelope is invented.

The old small-en-suite y3450 reservation is rejected for this face. The y3300
alternative has face 2930–3670 inside returns 2712.5–3787.5: margins
217.5/117.5. It still has no demonstrated section from x9500 to the lining
at x9862.5. Neither the source reservation nor branch is moved. I00-C1 stays
open. A cabinet door or actual front-service fitting may ultimately eliminate
this rear access task; that is not assumed without choosing the actual task.
