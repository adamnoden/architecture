# Reference House — Pattern Occurrence Register

**Status:** canonical Reference House project-state register  
**Purpose:** distinguish patterns selected in principle from actual project occurrences, implementation directions and outstanding evidence obligations.

The Reference House is a worked interpretation of House Kernel. It is **not evidence for the pattern language**.

A House Kernel pattern appears here as a current occurrence only when the active house has enough geometry or information to identify what the occurrence actually is. A pattern may be relevant or selected in principle without yet having an occurrence.

## Current transition state

`RH-01 — Test the gross morphology` removed the F0 room/service geometry. `RH-02 — Resolve ground-floor order` established the new 12.0 × 10.8 m ground-floor baseline. `RH-03 — Resolve stair, upper floor and courtyard circulation` added the four-bedroom upper order, principal stair and main family bathroom directly stacked over the ground service hub. `RH-04` fixed the current sectional/opening order. `RH-05 — Coordinate primary structure` has now established the short-span courtyard-ring structural concept and protected structural zones without creating a new service-penetration occurrence prematurely.

The old F0 identifiers remain suspended provenance. Later gates issue new occurrence identities where the new project object is geometrically distinct.

See [RH-05 Structural Coordination](structural-coordination.md), [RH-03 Stair, Upper Floor and Courtyard Circulation](upper-floor-and-stair-development.md), [RH-02 Ground-Floor Development](ground-floor-development.md), [RH-01 Gross Morphology Options](morphology-options.md) and the [Brief and Constraint Ledger](brief-and-constraint-ledger.md).

## 3. Selected strategies

Strategies are not pattern occurrences and receive no `RH-Pxxx-*` identity.

### Fail-Safe Water Distribution

The house continues to select [Fail-Safe Water Distribution](../patterns/strategies/fail-safe-water-distribution.md) as its water-failure strategy.

The route-class interpretation remains useful as a design brief; detailed routes are not yet re-established:

- `WF-01` plant/manifold geography — accessible joints, isolation, visible/manageable leakage and optional sensing;
- `WF-02` concealed vertical pressurised-water run — joint-minimised route, pipe-in-pipe/withdrawable option, leakage made detectable at an accessible point;
- `WF-03` kitchen/utility branches — short routes with joints/isolation in accessible service geography;
- `WF-04` bathroom branches — dry-side access where practical, joint-minimised concealed finals, waterproofing as a separate boundary;
- `WF-05` soil/waste — accessible stack/base/rodding and short gravity branches.

This selection does **not** mean every child pattern or implementation has been selected. In particular, manifold distribution remains a held candidate and pipe-in-pipe remains an implementation family.

### Structural / floor strategies

[Decompose Structural Interface Functions](../patterns/strategies/decompose-structural-interface-functions.md) and [Separate Structural Floor from Changeable Layers Where Proportionate](../patterns/strategies/separate-structural-floor-changeable-layers.md) now have a worked Reference House direction through RH-05:

- the permanent structural deck carries diaphragm duty;
- any removable/selective floor-access layer remains non-structural;
- bearing, restraint, diaphragm transfer, fire/acoustic closure and finish movement remain separate obligations;
- the selected structural concept uses permanent outer/courtyard masonry boundaries plus short engineered floor spans rather than relying on room partitions or removable linings.

This is a project implementation direction, not validation of either strategy.

## 7. Rejected / rewound moves that remain part of the design record

Earlier whole-house coordination rejected four tempting local solutions. Their **reasons** remain live even though the F0 locations do not:

1. **universal mixed-services horizontal duct** — service-specific routes remain preferred to one oversized mixed-services void;
2. **scattered upper wet rooms without route justification** — the main family bathroom is now deliberately stacked over `G-SVC-02`; `U-ENS-01` remains conditional rather than forcing a second remote service topology;
3. **plant hidden in ordinary kitchen cabinetry** — `G-SVC-02` gives this rule a new geometric home, subject to RH-06 proving it;
4. **blanket leak containment everywhere** — retain route-class-specific failure responses under Fail-Safe Water Distribution.

RH-02 adds two further rejected moves:

5. **courtyard crossing as the only ordinary front-to-rear route** — architectural sequence may use the court, but daily circulation should not depend on weather exposure;
6. **one undifferentiated rear great room as the default solution to contemporary family life** — linked rooms are preferred while they remain functionally convincing and structurally proportionate.

RH-03 adds three further controls:

7. **steepening or winding the principal stair merely to preserve a simpler plan** — the selected three-flight stair protects tread quality instead;
8. **displacing the service hub to make a conventional stair fit** — stair geometry remains subordinate to the whole-house integration result;
9. **full upper gallery around all four courtyard sides** — the selected south/east/north route preserves the west rooms' useful width.

RH-05 adds four structural controls:

10. **whole-house independent frame as the default** — rejected while the permanent masonry envelope/courtyard can carry useful structural work;
11. **long-span floor across the major house dimensions** — rejected in favour of the courtyard-generated 3.4–4.2 m support distances;
12. **making every room partition loadbearing** — rejected because it needlessly freezes subdivision;
13. **using primary beams/courtyard reaction nodes as future service chases** — protected structural zones must be crossed only through explicit later design.

These decisions remain part of the design record unless later evidence justifies reopening them.

## 8. Update rule

Update this register when:

- RH-06 defines enough route geometry to create new utility-entry, horizontal-route, structural-penetration, extract or indexing occurrences;
- an occurrence moves, splits, merges, is suspended or rejected;
- an implementation family is selected or abandoned;
- a strategy changes;
- new evidence invalidates an occurrence;
- a held candidate is promoted/rejected at project or language level;
- a rewind changes upstream architecture.

When an old identifier is re-used, ensure the underlying occurrence is recognisably the same project object; otherwise issue a new occurrence identity and leave the old one as provenance.

Do not update pattern evidence or maturity merely because this house uses a pattern.
