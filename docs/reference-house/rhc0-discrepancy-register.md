## Disposition after RH-C0.2

No item above proves that two **current controlling textual authorities** specify different houses. `RHC0-D01`–`D06` are stage-age/scope problems in manually maintained representations; `RHC0-D07` is an intentionally un-authored coordinate rather than a contradiction.

Accordingly RH-C0.2 does **not** rewrite earlier gate records merely to make their historical drawings look current. RH-C0.6 owns the explicit drawing classification and replacement of comparator views. If later audit finds a current design authority genuinely contradictory, that contradiction must be returned to its owning RH gate before freeze.
