## Decision to resolve

Can the owner's passive-first house be served, maintained and renewed using a proportionate system with actual continuous routes? Compare conventional alternatives before committing to central mechanical ventilation, a heat pump, a single soil stack or elaborate removable construction. None of those selections is established by this brief.

A Q350 comparison body of 570 × 725 × 850 mm needs at least 70 mm more depth than that reserve; a local plan placement and front working field do not establish duty, acoustic treatment, connections or renewal. A 580 × 1804 mm cylinder comparison fits a 700 mm square body reservation but its filled load, valves, expansion, insulation and withdrawal remain unresolved.

The riser is 550 × 700 mm gross. An illustrative 25 mm boundary each side leaves 500 × 650 mm before actual supports, ducts, pipes and insulation; this is body packing only. The IAS study derives a 650 × 550 mm trial clear access aperture from authored frame allowances; the source width reservation and actual installed section remain distinct. A 300 mm gap between the gallery spine face and the lining is unassigned; the trial clear interval y=4425–5075 mm misses the final 125 mm of the riser. No contiguous task-specific passage is demonstrated. A 250 mm lining has no spare gross margin for a 250 mm service envelope.

The floor allocation is 20 + 22 + 238 + 55 + 15 = 350 mm. A 75 mm branch exceeds its 55 mm services zone by 20 mm; a 190 mm connection-scale duct by 135 mm; an authored 250 mm insulated/support comparison by 195 mm. The framing zone cannot be borrowed without a designed crossing. A local 195 mm ceiling drop would reduce the ordinary +3100 mm ceiling to +2905 mm and must be accepted as a changed section.

A trial ground route from the riser travels south to y=3300 mm, west and then toward the western front study at (2500, 2500), about 9.99 m. It crosses room boundaries and the candidate bridge/trimmer region. It is a route to resolve, not a clear continuous corridor. The roof is not a free services plenum.

## Wet-room datum caution

The [wet study](../../project/bcr/bcr-wet-result.md) and accepted partial IAS/HAD studies identify a conditional tray route, not an installed assembly. For a trial 160 mm rim, the waste-axis expression is **85 − δ mm**, where δ is the unknown flange seating offset below the assumed low wet face. A 2685 mm trial run at 18 mm/m gives cabinet-entry axis **36.67 − δ mm**, before internal travel and real fittings. The authored 60 mm fitting envelope is not a measured chosen part. Obtain the matched tray/waste/support installation evidence before approving that route; the withdrawn earlier axis/clash arithmetic must not be reused.

## Ordinary adverse cases and return route

Test a blocked or fouled ventilation path, a failed valve requiring withdrawal of the cylinder, and a drain connection or duct bend that collides with the selected trimmer. Include isolation, safe access, reassembly and restoration of fire/acoustic/wet boundaries. Identify the smallest design change and its spatial/maintenance cost.

Supply the [services study](../../project/bcr/bcr-services-result.md), [wet study](../../project/bcr/bcr-wet-result.md), [proportionality comparison](../../project/bcr/bcr-proportionality-result.md) and [construction-resolution findings](../../project/bcr/construction-resolution-matrix.md). Professional calculations close their supported questions only; BCR retains unresolved route/fit obligations until the owning design and evidence agree.
