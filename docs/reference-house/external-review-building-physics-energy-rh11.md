## Decision to resolve

Can the current courtyard, openings and buildable assemblies provide comfortable, dry and quiet occupation with a proportionate energy strategy? Start with passive measures and the owner's use of the house. Establish actual climate, orientation and site before numerical performance claims; neither the historical solar screen nor the former PV reservation describes this roof.

## Current source and unresolved assemblies

The house is 12 × 12 m with a 3.5 m square court. The represented 417.5 mm envelope is a layer reservation, not a selected thermal/air/moisture system. The upper floor is at +3450 mm; ordinary ground clear height is 3100 mm. Nominal 1000 mm windows have sill/head +750/+2850 mm, but frame, glass, opening fraction, shading and hardware are unselected.

The accepted 25° roof drains both inward and outward from a ring ridge, at an eave datum of +6800 mm. Its 300 mm vertical package equals about 271.9 mm normal to slope for all layers. It cannot be counted as free insulation. Four inner valleys concentrate water at court corners. No thermal envelope line, ventilated airway, vapour strategy or maintainable loft is proved merely by the section.

The [roof comparison](../research/roof-system-candidates.md) considers a current ring with discrete covering and ceiling insulation, a current ring with sheet covering and overstructure insulation, and an outward-only sheet/overstructure challenger. Named product systems constrain those comparisons but none is admitted. At unchanged 25° and eave datum the outward-only high court edge would be about +8781.8 mm, with material courtyard daylight, appearance and wind consequences.

Later accepted HAD [kitchen](../../project/had/had-03-kitchen-options.md) and [court-level](../../project/had/had-03-court-levels.md) comparisons remain unadopted. Raising two north sills from 750 to 1050 mm reduces nominal aperture by 0.60 m², not a computed daylight amount; a raised court walking plane changes splash, wall-base and concealed-water duties. Reassess the actual selected variant and first-vulnerable interfaces rather than carrying current-source results forward.

## Water and thin-layer limits to retain

The nominal roof areas in the water basis are 83.9375 m² outward and 47.8125 m² inward, plus 12.25 m² direct court rainfall. Those are bounded geometry inputs, not an adopted design storm or flow capacity. Collection/overflow must account for concentrated corners, downstream common failures, backwater and the actual vulnerable threshold. The court is not guaranteed to receive only direct rain.

The wet-floor study's 18–23 mm thin tiling comparison is not proof of a matched waterproof wet-duty system. Manufacturer tiling, substrate and waterproofing qualifications have different scopes. Select the actual wet-floor/wall/threshold and any directly wetted window system before analysing its moisture performance. The [moisture register](../../project/mra/mra-00-moisture-constitution-threat-model.md), where applicable to the selected interface, routes obligations; a completed threat definition is not a house performance result.

## Ordinary adverse cases and return route

Test blocked inner collection during rain, an unventilated or obstructed roof drying path, and summer ventilation restricted for noise/security. Identify whether the first failure is geometry, assembly, product duty, site or operation and give a conventional architectural alternative. Recheck the affected calculations after any roof, opening, insulation or services change.

Use the [water study](../../project/bcr/bcr-water-result.md), [wet study](../../project/bcr/bcr-wet-result.md), roof/rainwater studies and [construction-resolution findings](../../project/bcr/construction-resolution-matrix.md). Return source-bound findings through the owning IAS/OAS/HAD/ROF/RWT programmes and BCR to HRS. No internal model, prepared question or regulatory citation establishes whole-house performance.
