# First-floor plan resolution

The square-house first floor retains four bedrooms, an east-side family bathroom,
separate dressing and linen rooms, and the open circulation around three sides
of the courtyard. It uses the same 12 m shell, centred 3.6 m court, 3.1 m clear
ceilings and balanced return stair as the [ground plan](ground-plan-resolution.md).
These are provisional design choices, not construction or performance approval.

## Privacy and circulation

Each bedroom has its own door from a landing or gallery. The former required
connection between the two western bedrooms is superseded by a principal-bedroom
door from the northern landing. Dressing and linen rooms each open independently
from that landing. All seven internal doors have 900 mm nominal apertures and
swing into their destination rooms; none relies on circulation through another
bedroom or an under-flight route.

The front-west bedroom is L-shaped. A short return joins its hall wall to the
courtyard enclosure, closing the previously incomplete corner. The bathroom's
north and south partitions use the same Y 8155 / 3845 mm centre lines as the
corresponding ground-floor service boundaries. Their external room-facing faces
therefore align with the courtyard faces. All are the existing provisional
125 mm nonbearing family; no new structural role is asserted.

## Order and daylight

The western bedroom division moves from Y 5000 to Y 6000 mm, halfway between
courtyard windows at Y 5100 and 6900. This transfers 4.2 m² gross reservation
from the principal bedroom (29.4 → 25.2 m²) to the front-west bedroom
(16.8 → 21.0 m²). These are planning areas before wall deductions, not usable
floor areas. It avoids a partition landing at the jamb of the lower window.

All four west/east courtyard windows are 1200 mm wide, directly above the
selected ground apertures. The north/south courtyard windows are 1400 mm wide,
centred above the ground paired doors. Upper front windows share four equal
2900 mm bay spacings and use 925 mm nominal widths within the inherited range.
The dry-room division moves to X 6000, giving equal 1800 mm gross widths;
separate garden windows centre at X 5100 / 6900. Outer garden bedroom windows
at X 2100 / 9900 complete the symmetrical rear arrangement. East-side windows
serve both bedrooms and the bathroom; the west-side bedrooms also have side
windows. The bathroom's side sill is provisionally 1500 mm above first-floor FFL.

## Evidence and limits

The owning choices and paired source factory are
`compiler/reference-house/first-floor-plan-resolution.ts`. The original B6
source factory remains reproducible. Full-system compilation checks source
room reservations, structure and service topology. The door-swing regression
samples each full arc against its room reservation; it does not establish
finished clear opening, ironmongery, accessibility or furniture fit.

Both ground SVGs are required to remain byte-identical to B6. Historical
Reference House, drawing v1, ADE and B3–B6 proofs remain controls.
`publication/specimens/plt04/` provides paired actual-size plans: ground on
page 1, first floor on page 2, at 1:100 in B5/A4 and 1:50 in A2.

Bed, wardrobe and bathroom equipment fit, stair headroom/guarding/opening
framing, privacy, daylight performance and fire/escape remain technical design
obligations. Side windows are current-house proposals; site constraints are
deferred by owner instruction. This plan does not claim a protected escape
route, real-site fit or adequate bathroom fitout merely because apertures and
partitions are drawn.
