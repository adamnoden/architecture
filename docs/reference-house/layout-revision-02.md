# Layout revision 02 — access, alignment and entry void

## Brief and authority

Starting main `eee7e3be7ca501f3c2fd1fcf83eabbd94144b18e`. The owner reopens
layout with two marked plans: align the stair with the courtyard, improve the
study/cloak frontage, swap WC/utility, compress the service stack and pantry,
centre the garden entrance, reorganise upstairs rooms and consider an entrance
void and southern courtyard door. This is a new architectural candidate, not a
representation-only PLT change. The previous orthographic/analysis family is
retained as historical context; it does not depict this revision.

`georgian/layout-revision.ts` owns the candidate, and `layout-revision-audit.ts`
its bounded geometry/access evidence. The plan adapter accepts source inputs
while its legacy defaults preserve old specimen identities. The shared ADE
plan/opening/stair machinery draws both levels. No duplicated hand-drawn walls.

The owner subsequently accepts this pair as satisfactory for now and authorises
continued PLT work. It is the working basis for the
coordinated successor,
without implying physical or technical acceptance.

## Decisions

- Keep the 12 m square shell. Centre a 3500 mm square courtyard: each surrounding
  band gains 50 mm. This is enough to get the west gallery above 1000 mm; it is
  not represented as a large spatial gain.
- Stair envelope is 2400 × 4335 mm: two 1100 mm flights, 200 mm well, 300 mm
  goings and a 1635 mm intermediate landing. Twenty 172.5 mm rises retain 3450 mm
  floor-to-floor. Both stair ends align with the courtyard's outer wall faces:
  Y 3832.5 and 8167.5. West gallery is 1015 mm clear. South arrival/turn space
  between front-room north faces and stair is 1120 mm. These are project drawing
  dimensions, not a compliance/headroom/carrying certificate.
- The larger study takes both left front windows. Its smaller cloakroom is
  tucked behind the front strip so no partition lands in the second window.
  Study/cloak north partitions align at 2650. Study clear area rises from about
  6.48 to 7.34 m² (~13%). Cloak has back-wall hanging and a separate hall door.
  The hall-facing walls at X 4800 and 7200 register with the upper bedrooms and
  clear atrium edges at X 4862.5 / 7137.5; north guard aligns at Y 2712.5.
- WC moves left of the utility and uses a front window. Front privacy is the
  owner's stated frosted-glazing/shutter intent; no product or final treatment
  is selected or disguised as opaque wall in plan.
- The straight eastern service spine is X 9500, within the front facade pier.
  Utility north is Y 2550; plant north Y 6650; pantry north Y 8105. These clear
  the fixed east-side openings by at least 125 mm from partition face to aperture.
  Utility and plant clear areas are about 4.18 and 8.03 m² (previously about
  4.60 and 9.66). The pantry is about 2.69 m² clear: **slightly larger**, not
  smaller than before, because moving its lower wall clear of the window while
  retaining a straight stack and the flush kitchen edge earns more depth.
  Its north room face Y 8167.5 matches the northern courtyard wall exactly.
  Pantry entry remains from the kitchen. Plant product/carrying design is open.
- One 2400 mm paired garden opening and two 2400 mm courtyard entrances share
  the X 6000 axis. The original 1800 mm front doorway remains the nominal width
  bottleneck. The source gives a direct route, not a ladder-carrying simulation.
  Four north windows flank the ground garden doorway and align with four upper
  windows. The upper centre bay stays blank to admit a straight bedroom division
  in the pier; no partition is allowed through glazing. East/west bay axes stay.
- Four bedrooms remain. Bedroom 4 uses the west gallery with no north landing.
  A straight X 6000 division clears the north courtyard's central pier. The
  principal walk-in wardrobe moves into the suite's south-west corner instead
  of running a dividing wall into the north windows. Its north/east walls are
  entirely internal, with L-shaped storage and a door into the bedroom.
  Rear-room partitions stop at courtyard corners: the cavity wall alone bounds
  the glazed central frontage; no second solid partition blocks those windows.
- Bedroom 3's small en suite lies against the eastern service geography. Its
  northern wall is Y 3850 to clear the next side window, leaving a compact
  shower-room reservation (~2.17 m² clear). Sanitary layout, shower/door fit and
  performance remain unresolved; this is not a certified fitted bathroom.
  The family bathroom and principal en suite retain their eastern alignment.
- An approximately 5.22 m² opening above the entrance hall has a north bridge
  and an explicit guard-edge convention. The facade centre window lights this
  volume. Ground-first stair only; no loft stair or flat roof is restored.

## Proof boundary and downstream work

The access check uses a 600 mm diameter person on a 50 mm grid, with the stair,
courtyard and upper void blocked and authored partition doors admitted. Every
room is reachable from its own floor circulation **without a courtyard shortcut**.
This does not simulate door operation, furniture carrying, accessible use,
evacuation, handrails, guarding, headroom or structural openings.

Checks also cover both stair/court alignments, matching pantry face, source
room overlap, furniture/plant containment and bed/door working geometry. The
entry void needs structural edge/trimmer design, guarding and fire/smoke/escape
review. No technical approval is inferred from its representation. Roof ridge
inset follows the changed court at 2125 mm; current full sections/elevations and
analysis must be regenerated after this plan candidate is reviewed. Do not mix
old views with this plan as though they were coordinated.
