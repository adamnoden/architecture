# Reference House — nominal opening interface studies

**Design case:** OAS-NOMINAL-01.
**Source field:** RH-LAYOUT-REVISION-02 / RH-LAYOUT-FAMILY-02.
**Drawing:** [A3 interface sheet](../../project/oas/studies/nominal-interface-studies.svg).

This record supplies new local architectural geometry for representative
interface studies. It is not a surveyed opening, fabrication schedule,
manufacturer installation tolerance or proven environmental/structural detail.
The whole-house selected topology remains E0/I0. The studies make physical
relationships inspectable before BCR, rather than substituting a renewal
diagram for actual sections.

## Authority and applicability

The 417.5 mm current wall, 125 mm partition, occupied-floor datums and nominal
window aperture remain source inputs. New study dimensions below are authored
**project design assumptions for these cases**. They do not silently resize
current plan/elevation apertures or select every door/threshold occurrence.

The window case uses a 1000 x 2100 nominal opening with sill/head 750/2850
relative to floor. The drawn frame envelope is 970 x 2070 outside and 140 deep,
using a **nominal study gap of 15**. That study gap does not become a selected
manufacturer tolerance for the whole house. Actual product and adjustment
requirements may defeat the case and require an explicit local redesign.

The frame's 60 mm section occupation is a reserved product envelope, not a
fabricated sash/frame profile. Rebate, glazing, drainage channels, seals and
hardware remain actual product inputs. The study therefore does not establish
glazed area, operating aperture, escape dimension or a window's performance.

load, thermal, fire or moisture performance.

Release local trim and any actual frame fixing cover; support/remove sash as
needed to reach fixings; support the frame before unfixing. An architrave alone
need not expose every manufacturer's fixing. No clipping or rail mechanism
depends on sliding a whole room's trim sideways.

## B — head at 1:5

The current nominal head zone 2850–3050 is retained as a **support reserve**.
The drawing does not turn it into a sized lintel. Frame top is 2835 in this
case, leaving the nominal 15 gap to the opening head. Permanent support remains
independent of frame release.

A new nominal cavity-water tray route has outlet elevation 3060, back base
3070 and a 30 upstand to 3100. It falls outward through the outer-leaf outlet
reservation. Outlet width, stops, continuity around corners, construction
tolerance and actual rainfall exposure remain unresolved OS-02 inputs.
The tray is not triangular infill: the drawing shows an open folded route.

Front weather and rear air interfaces at the head remain deliberate
replaceable transitions. Their products, compression/tolerance and continuity
with the jamb are not supplied by a dashed line. The closeness of the tray/
head/ceiling relationships is visible for BCR; it is not a building-physics pass.

## C — sill at 1:5

The visible stone sill bears on the outer-leaf zone in the study, not as an
assumed continuous stone bridge into the inner wall. Its front projection is
80, top rises from 735 at the front to 744.125 at depth 102.5, giving a nominal
1:20 outward fall. The 10 x 10 drip groove is outboard of the weather face.
Material strength, bearing, restraint, weathering and actual fabrication remain
technical inputs.

The formed sill tray runs from depth 370/elevation 760 through depth 262.5/760
to an outlet at depth 100/elevation 747; its back upstand rises 30 to 790.
The outlet is above the local stone surface (744), so the nominal route is
outward onto the sill rather than behind it. Geometry is not spray testing.

At the frame bottom, the nominal gap 750–765 is allocated to a 10 mm support
reserve and a 5 mm setting block. The tray lies over the support reserve and
under the setting block. The tray is not itself assumed load-bearing.
Support, thermal bridge, compression, material compatibility and corner/end
geometry remain OS-01/02 obligations. Do not substitute an arbitrary soft
membrane or insulating offcut for this structural/support duty.

Frame drainage must discharge to the tray/sill path. Do not seal across that
path with an unbroken external cosmetic bead. A blocked product weep or failed
tray still needs the actual failure consequence assessed by MRA; this drawing
does not award the whole opening a single-failure water pass.

## D — front-threshold case at 1:5

This is a front-entry study against the current nominal interior/near-landing
reference of 0, not a selection of courtyard/garden levels.

The threshold/support envelope extends locally to −60 below floor and has a
nominal upper crossing point +15, with an inside bevel returning to 0.
A 44 mm leaf envelope and seal zone are shown for interface inspection, not
doorset manufacture or a rated leaf. The 15 upstand is a **new study choice**,
not reinterpretation of the old drawing's unselected +50 mark and not a legal
or accessible-use claim.

The external collector reserve is 100 wide and 100 deep, immediately outside
the threshold. It is drawn with a removable top and an unselected outlet;
flow capacity, trapped discharge, cleaning and frost/debris consequences still
need actual design. A visible rectangle is not an accepted drain.

The adjoining landing falls away from its near-door reference at a trial 1:80.
The shown 130 mm cropped run therefore drops 1.625. Its proposed normal
surface failure route is away from the door, with an open landing-edge spill
to the lower general field; the current general exterior datum is −150.
The complete landing plan, edge/approach safety and actual discharge are not
shown or approved. The collector cannot be the sole defence against all
inward surface water.

The −60 threshold and −100 collector fit geometrically within a 300 mm
ground-package reserve, but that does not design its support, DPC/DPM, thermal
or structural continuity. No such reserve is borrowed from the intermediate
floor or silently cut through a member.

Court and garden thresholds require their own level/drain/overflow sections.
The tall enclosed court does not inherit this open landing-edge spill.
OS-03 explicitly retains that different water consequence for integrated MRA/
BCR resolution. This representative front detail is not a universal threshold.

## E — ordinary internal-door jamb at 1:2

The 125 mm partition is represented by a local trial 12.5 / 100 / 12.5 build-up.
This is an ordinary **unrated study**, not a fire/acoustic assembly specification.
A 125 deep frame lining occupies 30 laterally, with a 10 nominal installation
gap. A 70-wide, 18-projecting trim releases independently on both sides.

The 44 mm leaf envelope, 3 mm closing-gap trial and 12 x 15 stop make the
closing interface explicit. Hinges, latch, seals, mass and support are actual
product/duty inputs. A fixing axis and packing reserve are shown, never an
anchor size/capacity. At nominal B900 the arithmetic between frame sides is
820; practical passage is still affected by actual leaf/stop/hinge operation.

Support the leaf before releasing hardware; remove the local trim/stop/cover
needed for the actual fixing; preserve independent partition support; renew
interrupted boundary seals and check closure before redecoration.
Any required rated assembly can supersede this ordinary candidate locally.

## Scale, checking and remaining claims

The SVG has physical dimensions 420 x 297 mm and a matching millimetre viewBox.
A/B/C/D use 1:5 model-to-paper geometry; E uses 1:2. Annotation values are model
mm. Geometry/scale checks include the 83.5 paper-mm wall depth, 28 paper-mm
window frame depth, 62.5 paper-mm partition and 22 paper-mm leaf thickness.
The sheet was rendered and inspected; faint linework and unintentionally
closed tray outlines found during inspection were corrected and re-rendered.
No actual physical print acceptance is asserted.

This completes the missing **representative nominal architectural studies**,
not OS-01…08, engineered construction details or certification. MRA/BCR may
reject the local assumptions. The studies give those reviews actual geometry
to challenge; they do not remove uncertainty by drawing it neatly.
