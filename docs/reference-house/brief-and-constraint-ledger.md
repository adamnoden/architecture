## 1. Authority model

Reference House decisions come from different authorities. They must not be collapsed into one undifferentiated list of requirements.

| Authority | Owns | Does not own |
|---|---|---|
| **External requirements / real site facts** | life safety, law, structural integrity, health, environmental performance, actual utilities, boundaries, levels, planning and physical context | architectural taste or House Kernel doctrine |
| **House Kernel doctrine** | long-life architectural propositions: proportional serviceability, lifespan separation, designed interfaces, failure tolerance, maintenance geography, repose, passive-first design, legibility and architectural resolution of technology | Georgian composition, courtyard form, brick, symmetry or a particular technical system |
| **Selected patterns / strategies** | reusable responses selected for use in the project | exact project geometry or proof that an occurrence is technically adequate |
| **G-01 grammar** | Georgian-derived spatial/compositional language: hierarchy, ordering, proportion, opening relationships and element families that survive the G-01 evidence programme | courtyard morphology, service topology, House Kernel doctrine or every historical Georgian convention |
| **Reference House brief / morphology** | site/programme assumptions, compactness, courtyard, room needs, garden relationships and project-specific spatial choices | general House Kernel claims |
| **Reference House tectonic / local dialect** | visible project-specific resolution: joinery, restrained metalwork, courtyard inflection, material expression | universal doctrine or G-01 identity by default |
| **Technical implementation** | selected structural, envelope, floor, services and fabrication families | architectural value merely by being technically convenient |

Where two authorities conflict, record the conflict. Do not silently rewrite one authority to accommodate another.

### Decision-state vocabulary

This ledger uses five states:

- **FIXED** — must be satisfied unless the governing authority itself changes.
- **SELECTED** — part of the current Reference House identity or project brief; may be reopened only by an explicit design decision.
- **PREFERRED** — should normally be preserved, but a materially better integrated solution may defeat it with a recorded reason.
- **OPEN** — a working assumption or unresolved choice with no incumbency advantage.
- **SUPERSEDED** — historical design provenance; must not be treated as current brief.

## 2. The brief in one paragraph

Design a **relatively compact detached family house for a medium London / Zone-4-type plot**, using the **G-01 Georgian-derived grammar** and a **real open courtyard** as project-specific architectural choices. The house should be domestic rather than demonstrative: calm, materially convincing, naturally legible and pleasant in ordinary use. It should embody House Kernel's current long-life propositions without allowing serviceability infrastructure to dominate area, appearance or daily life. The exact site does not yet exist, so the Reference House is a virtual integration project rather than a planning proposal. The current working programme should stay within a **five-bedroom ceiling**, with exact room count and total area earned through design rather than inherited from the fixture.

This paragraph establishes direction, not geometry.

## 3. Fixed constraints — House Kernel and external obligations

These are design constraints on the Reference House regardless of its eventual plan.

### RH-F01 — Safety, compliance and physical performance — **FIXED**

Life safety, legal compliance, structural integrity, health and environmental performance outrank project ideology. Longevity does not override whole-life cost, carbon, building physics or construction risk.

### RH-F02 — Proportional serviceability — **FIXED**

The house is designed for maintenance, repair, renewal and adaptation, but permanent provision must justify itself against frequency, consequence, retrofit difficulty, cost, space and complexity.

The current doctrine **does not** require an absolute ban on penetrations or attachments to permanent fabric. Long-lived fabric should not become the *routine* distribution or attachment zone for shorter-lived systems. Necessary crossings and attachments should be sparse, deliberate, concentrated where practical and documented.

### RH-F03 — Designed interfaces — **FIXED**

Movement, tolerance, fixing, sealing, drainage, fire/acoustic separation, inspection, disassembly and renewal must be designed where they genuinely apply rather than delegated to brittle finish continuity or site improvisation.

### RH-F04 — Failure architecture — **FIXED**

Credible failures, especially water-related failures, should be detectable, containable and locally repairable where proportionate. Failure routes must be considered spatially, not added as sensors after the fact.

### RH-F05 — Maintenance geography — **FIXED**

Maintenance-intensive systems require approach, working space, handling space and credible replacement routes. The same rule applies to façades, roof, rainwater goods, courtyard elevations and external plant.

### RH-F06 — Ordinary parts / understandable construction — **FIXED**

Prefer broadly available components, ordinary tools and intelligible construction. Concentrate invention where arrangement or interfaces genuinely justify it. Avoid dependencies whose future repairability rests on a unique proprietary ecosystem without a strong reason.

### RH-F07 — Architectural permanence where useful — **FIXED**

Adaptability is not the objective everywhere. Rooms, axes, stairs, structure and other defining architectural relationships may be deliberately enduring. The house must not become a generic shell merely to keep everything movable.

### RH-F08 — Repose — **FIXED**

Ordinary occupied spaces should be physically comfortable, spatially comprehensible, capable of privacy and retreat, locally controllable and perceptually settled. Technical infrastructure should not become a persistent demand on attention.

### RH-F09 — Passive first — **FIXED**

Form, envelope, orientation, shading, mass, controllable openings, drainage and gravity do the first work. Mechanical or electronic assistance remains legitimate where performance requires it.

### RH-F10 — Legibility and stewardship — **FIXED**

Future owners and trades must be able to understand, isolate, maintain and alter the important systems. Critical information should survive beyond a single software account or supplier.

### RH-F11 — Technology resolved as architecture — **FIXED**

Necessary technical features encountered by occupants must be resolved through composition, material and craft. Concealment is legitimate; falsification and cosmetic continuity over unresolved construction are not.

Canonical source: [House Kernel — Governing Principles](../manuscript/governing-principles.md).

## 4. Selected Reference House identity

These choices belong to this house. They are not House Kernel doctrine.

### RH-S01 — G-01 Georgian-derived grammar — **SELECTED**

The Reference House uses G-01 as its architectural grammar. G-01 may be strongly opinionated about hierarchy, axes, bays, dimensional families, opening composition, vertical order and element families where the research supports those claims.

Detailed G-01 rules remain subject to their own maturity. Selecting G-01 does **not** freeze an elevation before the grammar research justifies it.

Canonical source: [G-01 Grammar Charter](../grammar/g01-grammar-charter.md).

### RH-S02 — Genuine open courtyard — **SELECTED**

The Reference House is a courtyard house. The courtyard is a project morphology, not a G-01 invariant and not House Kernel doctrine.

What is selected:

- a genuine open-to-sky court forming part of the domestic architecture;
- a court that contributes positively to spatial sequence, light, outlook and/or sheltered outdoor life;
- a court that remains architecture rather than degenerating into a technical yard.

What is **not** selected:

- 3,600 × 3,600 mm dimensions;
- exact centrality;
- a square shape;
- four equal surrounding wings;
- a particular fountain, planting scheme, arcade or paving language.

Any future proposal to remove the courtyard entirely is an explicit brief change, not an ordinary RH-01 optimisation.

### RH-S03 — Relatively compact detached London house — **SELECTED design context**

The current virtual project should behave like a detached house on a medium-sized London / Zone-4-type plot rather than a country-house or estate-house exercise.

This is a **design context**, not a claim that a real site has been selected. RH-01 should penalise unnecessary footprint, perimeter, circulation and gross area.

### RH-S04 — Contemporary family occupation — **SELECTED programme type**

The house is a contemporary family home, not a museum reconstruction of Georgian domestic life. Historical hierarchy may inform G-01, but obsolete social/service arrangements are not binding.

### RH-S05 — Tectonic honesty — **SELECTED project expression consistent with doctrine**

The visible architectural and constructional grammars should coincide. Traditional joinery, mouldings and restrained brass/bronze may do real technical work, but technical necessity does not automatically justify visible technical expression.

Canonical source: [Reference House — Tectonic Architectural Language](tectonic-architectural-language.md).

## 5. Strong owner / project preferences

These should influence design materially, but they are not absolute constraints.

### RH-P01 — Compactness over grandeur — **PREFERRED**

The later project direction supersedes the earliest grand image studies. The Reference House should be compact enough to remain credible on a medium London plot. Area must be earned by domestic or technical value rather than by symmetry, circulation spectacle or maintaining an inherited ring plan.

**Implication for RH-01:** the current fixture's approximately 130.6 m² gross ring per storey / approximately 261 m² across two principal storeys is a benchmark to challenge, not a target.

### RH-P02 — No more than five bedrooms without reopening the brief — **PREFERRED / working ceiling**

Five bedrooms is the current upper bound of the intended programme. The exact count is open. The present four-bedroom arrangement is not a requirement.

### RH-P03 — Strong front → court → garden relationship — **PREFERRED**

A legible relationship from entrance through the inner court toward the garden is strongly preferred. The current perfectly straight north–south centreline is one implementation, not the requirement.

RH-01 may bend, offset, compress or sequence this relationship if the result is materially better, but should not casually lose the sense that the courtyard belongs to the route between public arrival and private garden.

### RH-P04 — Courtyard with a bounded warmer/local dialect — **PREFERRED, weakly binding**

Earlier studies explored a slight Andalusian influence in the courtyard—more arches, restrained green tile/zellige and less literal Greek/classical colonnading. Treat this as a local-dialect direction to test later, not a morphology generator and not a licence to blur G-01 across the whole house.

### RH-P05 — Substantial, legible construction — **PREFERRED**

The house should read as borne, settled and materially credible. Permanent masonry character remains a strong project direction, but the exact structural/envelope family is not frozen by RH-00.

Red brick is a prior material preference and a credible Reference House candidate, not a fixed universal palette.

### RH-P06 — Wet rooms should seek real daylight where proportionate — **PREFERRED**

Bathrooms and other wet rooms should not be pushed automatically into permanently dark technical interiors merely for service efficiency. Natural light should be sought where it can coexist with privacy, service clustering, envelope quality and compactness.

This preference does not justify scattering wet rooms or lengthening high-consequence service routes without limit.

### RH-P07 — Generous, safe stair geometry — **PREFERRED**

The principal stair should favour comfortable going, legibility, safe use and architectural dignity over minimum-space optimisation. Exact rise/going and form remain RH-03 decisions.

### RH-P08 — External maintenance should remain ordinary — **PREFERRED expression of a fixed doctrine constraint**

Avoid geometries that make routine upper façade, window, gutter, roof or courtyard work needlessly specialist. Bays, porticos, planting and projections are allowed, but must earn the access complexity they create.

Canonical project control: [External Access & Maintenance Plan](external-access-maintenance-plan.md).

## 6. Programme: what is actually known

The Reference House programme is intentionally less fixed than the current fixture implies.

### Known / retained at brief level

- detached contemporary family house;
- no more than five bedrooms under the current brief;
- at least one strong shared living room / drawing-room function;
- a less formal family/dining territory should be tested rather than assuming one ceremonial living hierarchy serves all daily life;
- kitchen requires a credible relationship to dining/family life and high-service geography;
- utility/service functions must have real space rather than disappearing into ordinary cabinetry;
- practical WC/bathroom provision for contemporary family use;
- useful work/study capability is desirable;
- courtyard and garden should participate in daily circulation and occupation rather than operate as visual scenery only;
- storage, linen and maintenance access must be allowed spatially rather than treated as residual volume.

### Not fixed by RH-00

- four bedrooms versus another count within the ceiling;
- dedicated guest bedroom versus guest-capable study;
- number of en-suites;
- precise kitchen type or size;
- separate formal dining room versus dining integrated with family/garden space;
- exact drawing-room count/size;
- laundry location;
- ground-floor shower/bath provision;
- home-office count;
- garage, car-port or parking arrangement;
- basement, cellar or habitable roof space;
- dedicated gym, cinema, pantry or other specialist rooms.

Do not invent specialist rooms to fill a symmetrical plan.

## 7. Site facts: none are real yet

There is currently **no actual Reference House site**. Therefore orientation, boundary, utility and planning assumptions in the coordination fixture are test conditions, not facts.

### Known design context

- London / Zone-4-type urban-suburban context;
- medium rather than estate-scale plot;
- detached-house assumption;
- garden is part of the intended domestic condition;
- ordinary side/rear maintenance and replacement logistics should be plausible.

### Unknown until a real site exists

- exact location and planning authority;
- plot width/depth and legal boundaries;
- street orientation;
- north direction relative to entrance/garden;
- neighbouring building positions, overlooking and rights;
- topography and finished levels;
- trees, ecology and mature landscape constraints;
- ground conditions, groundwater and contamination;
- sewer position and invert levels;
- water/electricity/data/gas availability and approach;
- highway crossover, parking and refuse requirements;
- heritage/conservation constraints;
- flood risk and drainage discharge options;
- measured local noise and air-quality conditions.

### RH-01 rule for absent site facts

Gross morphology must not optimise itself around one arbitrary orientation. Use a declared **surrogate site** only to make drawings legible, then stress-test the winning morphology against materially different solar/service-side conditions in RH-07 and RH-06.

The current fixture assumption—street south, garden north, primary service side east—is **OPEN**.

## 9. Selected pattern / strategy intent versus existing occurrences

The [Pattern Occurrence Register](pattern-occurrence-register.md) currently records coordination occurrences for controlled utility entry, plant/service hub, accessible vertical service zone, high-service-room service walls, coherent horizontal routes, designed structural penetrations and a physical service index, plus the selected Fail-Safe Water Distribution strategy.

RH-00 makes a critical distinction:

> **The project may retain the intent while the occurrence geometry moves or disappears.**

RH-01 through RH-06 are therefore permitted to relocate, split, merge or invalidate current `RH-P*` occurrences. The occurrence register must then be updated honestly. Existing identifiers are not reasons to preserve a worse plan.

Current intent to carry forward for testing:

- credible controlled utility transition(s);
- a genuine maintainable service/plant hub where the selected system mix justifies one;
- deliberate vertical service geography rather than accidental chases;
- consolidation of maintainable high-service-room components where proportionate;
- coherent service-specific horizontal routes rather than a universal mixed-services void;
- designed structural penetrations rather than future arbitrary drilling;
- a durable physical service index tied to the building record;
- route-class-specific water failure responses under Fail-Safe Water Distribution.

All exact locations remain subordinate to the redesign.

## 10. Early image-study material that is not the brief

The earliest Reference House image studies were useful for discovering taste. They must not be read as an instruction to reproduce a single rendered house.

The following are **SUPERSEDED as requirements** or remain only weak visual precedent:

- estate-house / mansion scale;
- grandeur for its own sake;
- porticos on every storey or elevation;
- a mandatory circular fountain;
- a mandatory cherry-blossom tree;
- extensive classical columns merely to signal style;
- a predetermined red-brick elevation before plan/section/elevation coupling is resolved;
- courtyard arches or green zellige as mandatory components;
- any earlier area estimate generated from the image studies.

Useful taste signals survive in weaker form: Georgian-derived order, red brick as a candidate, restrained entrance grandeur, a meaningful courtyard, greater depth than a thin suburban plan where justified, and a bounded warmer courtyard dialect. Those are already classified above.

## 13. RH-01 input conditions

`RH-01 — Test the gross morphology` now begins from this brief rather than from the old plan.

The comparison set must:

- include a genuine courtyard in every conforming option;
- support G-01 ordering potential without pretending detailed grammar rules are already proven;
- behave credibly as a compact detached London house;
- expose gross internal area, courtyard area, external perimeter and obvious circulation penalties;

- preserve ordinary external maintenance/logistics possibilities around the building;
- avoid creating rooms merely to complete symmetry;
- treat the existing 12.6 × 11.4 m fixture as a comparator, not an incumbent.

RH-01 should produce a small number of materially distinct options and record why the losers lose.
