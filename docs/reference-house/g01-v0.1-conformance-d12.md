**Status:** D12 PASS at coordinated virtual-design scope  
**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Grammar authority:** [G-01 v0.1](../grammar/g01-v0.1.md), frozen on `main` before this trial  
**Reference House authority:** RH-04 as superseded/extended by RH-08, RH-09 and RH-10  
**Programme:** [G-01 Completion Programme](../grammar/g01-completion-programme.md) / issue #78

## 1. Purpose and anti-tuning rule

This trial asks whether the already-coordinated Reference House conforms to **frozen G-01 v0.1**.

The grammar was merged before D12 began. The house may:

- pass;
- depart from a preference;
- fail and require a house rewind;
- record an explicit deviation;
- expose a condition outside G-01;
- require human review where the evidence is genuinely unresolved.

The grammar text is not changed to make the house pass.

## 2. Result

The Reference House **PASSes G-01 v0.1 at its current coordinated architectural scope**.

No G-01 invariant requires an RH-02→RH-10 rewind.

The important result is not that the house happens to look Georgian. It is that its existing architectural-order decisions can be stated against the independent frozen rules without importing Reference House-specific morphology into the grammar.


# 3. G01-R01 — Ordered classical architectural field

**Disposition:** `PASS`

## Reference House evidence

RH-04 deliberately rejected two opposite failures:

- a centred five-bay façade obtained by distorting the plan merely to achieve abstract symmetry;
- openings following internal partitions independently with no stable façade order.

It instead selected a centred **four-bay principal front field** at X = 1.95 / 4.65 / 7.35 / 10.05 m, with the principal entrance in bay 3.

The elevation is ordered while the door remains off the geometric centre.

RH-10 then completed the other outer elevations as separately scoped fields:

- south/front — four-bay principal composition;
- north/garden — three-axis composition;
- east/service — three broad room/service fields with technical expression concentrated in the middle service field;
- west — quiet two-room-field order;
- courtyard — its own internal order rather than a forced continuation of the front grid.

This is almost a direct demonstration of R01's central distinction:

> **classical order is explicit; symmetry/alignment belong to named architectural domains rather than one whole-building boolean.**

## Why the courtyard does not contaminate G-01

The courtyard is **project morphology**, not a G-01 requirement.

Its elevations nevertheless use explicit local order because the house has selected G-01. That is application of the grammar to a project-specific morphology, not evidence that courtyard houses are intrinsic to the grammar.

## Verdict

No deviation.

# 4. G01-R02 — Related and ranked opening family

**Disposition:** `PASS`

## Reference House evidence

The front elevation uses one four-axis family with deliberate hierarchy rather than unrelated openings:

- ground principal-room windows are larger/taller;
- upper windows are smaller while retaining the same principal vertical axes;
- the stair window remains on the bay axis but changes sill height because the stair/section owns that attribute;
- the entrance is the ranked exception inside the same field.

RH-10 retains the family while assigning two upper front openings additional emergency-egress duties. Performance changes do not dissolve the architectural family.

The garden, service and west elevations similarly use room-owned related opening fields rather than arbitrary penetrations. The service elevation explicitly ranks occupied windows/doors above technical terminals and keeps ventilation/extract/utility penetrations subordinate.

## Sash-derived preference

The architectural opening language is vertically proportioned and derived from the selected G-01 family. RH-09 retains a slim thermally broken aluminium window as a credible comparator specifically because it can support a restrained G-01 expression.

A proprietary frame/product and final egress sash mechanism are **not yet frozen**. That is a technical/product detail residue, not a language departure.

D12 therefore does **not** claim that historical sash construction is selected. R02 does not require it.

## Verdict

Core invariant passes. No recorded preference departure.

Final product/frame selection must preserve the already-coordinated family or reopen the relevant architectural/detail gate.

# 5. G01-R03 — Architecturally ranked principal entrance

**Disposition:** `PASS`

## Reference House evidence

The house explicitly tested and rejected a falsely centred principal entrance.

The selected front field places the entrance in bay 3 because the stair hall's architecture makes that the coherent arrival condition. The entrance is still ranked above an ordinary opening through:

- its role as the principal threshold;
- its dedicated bay condition;
- an approximately 950–1050 mm architectural opening;
- common principal head datum;
- restrained surround / entrance hierarchy;
- the arrival sequence from off-centre front threshold into a hall that re-centres on the courtyard/garden axis.

The entrance is also recorded as `TH-02`, a substantially level/visitable designed threshold. Contemporary accessibility therefore modifies the historic threshold mechanism without erasing entrance rank.

## Important non-result

D12 does not require a portico, columns, grand steps or a centred doorway. Those were explicitly excluded from G-01 v0.1.

## Verdict

Pass. No house change required.

# 6. G01-R04 — Integrated upper termination and roof order

**Disposition:** `PASS`

## Reference House evidence

This is the rule most likely to have exposed a real conflict because RH-04 originally preferred a visible pitched roof while RH-08/RH-10 later selected four outward-draining very-low-slope roof fields.

The later authority resolves the contradiction honestly:

- RH-08 supersedes RH-04 on roof family and roof visibility;
- the roof is selected for materially simpler and safer courtyard rainwater geography, not because technical roofs should disappear;
- the outer cornice/eaves datum remains architecturally controlled at approximately +6250 mm;
- the parapet/cornice edge must be genuine architecture and weathering construction rather than a cosmetic screen hiding unresolved roofing;
- PV reserve, outlets and roof services remain subordinate to the controlled outer order.

This satisfies the **corrected frozen G-01 rule**, whose invariant is integration rather than roof suppression.

The Reference House uses the `recessive / parapet-cornice termination + low-slope roof` family; Ridgehanger's validation showed that a more emphatic roof could also have conformed.

## Verdict

Pass.

The project-specific low-slope roof remains a Reference House water/failure-geography decision. It does not become a G-01 rule merely because it conforms to R04.

# 7. G01-R05 — Relational spatial and sectional hierarchy

**Disposition:** `PASS`

## Spatial hierarchy

The selected ground floor is not an undifferentiated room graph. It has explicit architectural roles:

- drawing room;
- family room;
- entrance / principal stair hall;
- study;
- dining hall;
- kitchen;
- service gallery / plant geography.

The east service geography is deliberately subordinate rather than allowed to become the dominant principal arrival or visual order.

The upper floor similarly distinguishes private rooms, stair hall, courtyard gallery, cross-landing and stacked service/bathroom geography.

## Sectional hierarchy

RH-04 explicitly tested three height families and selected restrained ground-floor primacy:

- ground clear ceiling +2950 mm;
- first-floor FFL +3300 mm;
- upper clear ceiling +6000 mm;
- cornice/eaves control approximately +6250 mm.

The decision gives:

> **principal ground floor > private upper floor**

without turning the compact family house into an exaggerated piano-nobile villa.

Opening heights and entrance scale reinforce that hierarchy.

## Sequence / recentering

The outer front and inner court use different centres intentionally. Arrival moves from the off-centre bay-3 entrance into the stair hall and re-centres on the courtyard/garden axis at X = 6.0 m.

That is a declared architectural sequence rather than a defect hidden by façade symmetry.

## Historical/social boundary

The house uses service geography because current maintainability/programme earns it. It does not reproduce servant circulation as a stylistic requirement.

## Verdict

Pass. No morphology-specific historical mechanism has been promoted into the grammar.

# 8. G01-R06 — Concentrated classical articulation

**Disposition:** `PASS`

R06 is a language preference, not an invariant requiring decorative abundance.

The Reference House's established direction is deliberately restrained:

- entrance hierarchy receives a dedicated surround/threshold condition rather than every opening receiving equal emphasis;
- cornice/eaves forms a primary upper datum;
- substantial skirting, architraves and cornices are used where they already belong to the room and can carry genuine tectonic work;
- window/door trims resolve real replaceable-opening interfaces;
- brass/bronze is restricted to touch, wear, indexing, protection or genuine removable/movement duties rather than used as an ornamental grid;
- direct mineral/plaster walls remain baseline where additional articulation is not earned.

The project therefore concentrates architectural articulation at meaningful thresholds, openings and room datums while leaving quiet surfaces quiet.

That is exactly the restraint R06 was intended to admit.

## Detail residue

RH-04 deliberately left exact entrance surround/fanlight and final moulding profiles downstream. RH-09 also leaves the precise wall/ceiling cornice mechanism to P04 comparison.

Those unresolved details do not constitute a preference departure. They remain subject to the rule: later detail may elaborate the language but must not smear classical articulation uniformly across the house or become fake technical ornament.

## Verdict

Pass at architectural-coordination scope.

# 9. Explicit NOT-G-01 project material

The following Reference House choices are **not evidence for, requirements of, or deviations from G-01 v0.1**:

## Courtyard morphology

`NOT G-01 / PROJECT MORPHOLOGY`

The 3.6 × 4.0 m court belongs to the project. G-01 only governs how the selected language is applied to its elevations/interfaces.

## Andalusian courtyard influence

`NOT G-01 / LOCAL PROJECT DIALECT`

Any warmer/Andalusian court expression remains local. It must negotiate coherently with G-01 at the interface but does not change the frozen grammar.

## Low-slope outward-draining roof

`NOT A G-01 ROOF TYPE`

It is a project/failure-geography decision that **conforms to** R04.

## Red-brick / masonry family

`PROJECT / TECHNICAL FAMILY`

Red brick is a leading project architectural material; cavity/loadbearing masonry is a technical family. Neither is required by G-01 v0.1.

## Brass / bronze tectonic vocabulary

`REFERENCE HOUSE TECTONIC DIALECT`

Sparse functional brass/bronze is a local tectonic choice, not Georgian grammar.

## House Kernel serviceability logic

`OVID'S HOUSE / PATTERN / TECHNICAL AUTHORITY`

Replaceable openings, service routes, failure geography, accessible plant, maintenance access and related patterns remain independent authorities even when the resulting detail is composed inside G-01.
