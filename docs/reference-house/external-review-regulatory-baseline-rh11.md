# Reference House — RH-11 Regulatory Baseline

**Status:** date-stamped review aid; not a legal opinion or substitute for the reviewer confirming applicability  
**Date checked:** 2026-10-12  
**Applies to:** Reference House external review in England

This page prevents the RH-11 review pack from quietly relying on stale regulatory guidance.

The Reference House has no real site, building-control application date or construction start date. Therefore **applicability and transitional provisions are not project facts**. Reviewers should state the regulatory/guidance route they have assumed and should distinguish:

- legislation / functional requirements;
- Approved Document guidance;
- British / European standards and National Annexes;
- product/system evidence;
- professional judgement;
- project-specific House Kernel requirements.

Approved Documents are guidance on ways of satisfying the Building Regulations. They are not themselves the Regulations and are not the only possible compliance route.

## 2. Future Homes / 2027 transition

The Building Regulations etc. (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2026 implement the Future Homes and Buildings Standards.

For ordinary non-HRB work the amendments generally come into force on **24 March 2027**, subject to transitional provisions. Government's circular states that pre-24-March-2027 notices/applications may retain transitional protection where the relevant work commences before **24 March 2028**.

Canonical source:

https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-future-homes-and-buildings-standards-building-circular-012026/the-future-homes-and-buildings-standards-building-circular-012026-letter

The 2026 regime introduces functional requirement **L3** for on-site renewable electricity generation in new dwellings/buildings containing dwellings.

Government's September 2026 Approved Document L FAQ explains that the reference output in paragraph 5.73(a) uses installed peak power equivalent to photovoltaic panels at **0.22 kWp/m² over an area equal to 40% of the dwelling-house ground-floor area**, followed by the approved annual-output methodology.

Canonical source:

https://www.gov.uk/guidance/approved-document-l-energy-and-greenhouse-gas-emissions-frequently-asked-questions

RH-10's approximately **50 m² PV-capable roof reservation** is only a geometric stress test against that known direction. It is not a Future Homes compliance result.

### RH-11 review instruction

The building-physics/energy reviewer should assess both:

1. what can be said about the house under the **known 2026 / Future Homes route**; and
2. what remains impossible to determine without a real site, orientation, application date, fabric specification and approved compliance model.

Do not assume the virtual project's 12 October 2026 date fixes the regulatory route of a later real building.

## 3. Standards and specialist methods

This pack deliberately does **not** freeze every British/European Standard, Eurocode, CIBSE guide, acoustic method, heat-pump standard, SAP/Home Energy Model method or manufacturer system document.

Each competent reviewer should state:

- the standard / method and revision used;
- whether it is normative, guidance, industry method or professional comparator;
- why it applies to this project question;
- where project information is insufficient to apply it.

This matters particularly for:

- Eurocode / UK National Annex structural design;
- masonry and engineered-timber design;
- structural vibration / serviceability;
- fire stopping / cavity barriers / PV fire interfaces;
- MVHR commissioning and acoustic design;
- ASHP acoustic assessment;
- heat-loss / emitter / DHW sizing;
- dynamic thermal / Part O analysis;
- daylight assessment;
- hygrothermal / condensation analysis;
- PV structural/electrical design and safe maintenance.

The reviewer should not infer technical validation from a project document merely because it cites an Approved Document or manufacturer family.

## 4. Version-control rule

Every external response should contain a short **basis of review** section recording:

- review date;
- applicable jurisdiction assumed;
- building-control / transition date assumed, if any;
- Approved Documents relied upon;
- key standards / methods relied upon;
- known forthcoming changes that materially affect the recommendation.

If guidance changes after a review, do not silently treat the old response as current. Reassess only the findings whose applicability or evidence dependencies changed.
